  # Recent Social Policy Developments in Taiwan and Hong Kong: Education Downsizing and Sheltered Workshop Budget Controversies
  
  > East Asia has recently seen a surge in social issues, with Taiwan and Hong Kong facing severe policy challenges in the fields of disability welfare and basic education, respectively. In Taiwan, the legislative budget cuts to sheltered workshops for people with disabilities sparked strong public backlash, leading Kuomintang (KMT) caucus whip Fu Kun-chi to issue a public apology in an attempt to quell the controversy. Meanwhile, in Hong Kong, a structural decline in the school-age population has led to large-scale primary school class reductions and school mergers as the new academic year begins. These events reflect the difficulties both regions face in social welfare and educational resource allocation, while highlighting the political and administrative challenges faced by elected representatives and government agencies when handling sensitive social issues.
  
  ### Taiwan: Sheltered Workshop Budget Controversy and Political Damage Control
  
  On September 1, 2026, Fu Kun-chi, the KMT caucus whip in Taiwan’s Legislative Yuan, led party members in a 90-degree bow of apology in a public setting regarding the "sheltered workshop budget controversy" that has recently drawn significant social attention. This move is viewed as an internal "damage control" strategy by the KMT, aimed at calming the intense public backlash and skepticism triggered by KMT legislator Weng Hsiao-ling’s proposal to cut budgets related to disability welfare during the budget review process.
  
  Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) legislator Wang Shih-chien commented on the issue during an interview on the same day on the CNEWS political talk show "Zhongwu Lai Kai Hui." Wang pointed out that Weng Hsiao-ling’s act of cutting disability welfare budgets not only sparked a collective public backlash but was also seen as a turning point for the DPP in political maneuvering. He even described such controversial behavior as a positive boost for the DPP, jokingly remarking that "God sent KMT Chair Cheng Li-wun and Weng Hsiao-ling to the DPP." Wang believes that the controversial actions of KMT legislators reflect a gap in party talent and policy thinking, and he views these incidents as leverage in political gamesmanship.
  
  The subsequent adjustments to the budget proposal and whether the relevant funding will be fully restored remain subject to further deliberation and confirmation by the Legislative Yuan.
  
  ### Hong Kong: Declining School-Age Population and Educational Structural Adjustment
  
  Meanwhile, the education sector in Hong Kong is facing severe structural social challenges. According to media reports, on the first day of the school year, September 1, 2026, public and aided primary schools across Hong Kong saw a reduction of over 140 Primary 1 classes, affecting more than 120 schools. The Education Bureau stated that this is primarily due to the structural decline in the school-age population. It is currently known that 15 primary schools have been assigned "zero classes," facing the prospect of closure or merger.
  
  There are differing views among parents and school authorities regarding the impact of these policy changes. Some parents believe that the resource integration following a merger could bring "one plus one greater than two" benefits; for instance, the merger case between Kei Tak Primary School in Tseung Kwan O and its sister school in Wong Tai Sin has sparked discussions on resource consolidation. However, some principals have called on the government to provide more detailed "soft landing" strategies. Secretary for Education Christine Choi emphasized that in the face of the structural decline in the school-age population, the government will continue to adopt a gradual adjustment strategy to assist schools in adjusting class sizes or student numbers. For the affected school staff and families, the policy changes still bring significant uncertainty, and how to safeguard students' right to education remains a key point for future observation.
  
  ### Common Challenges and Future Observations in Regional Social Policy
  
  The aforementioned events have had a direct impact on regional social policies. In Taiwan, the controversy over disability welfare budgets highlights the sensitivity of elected representatives regarding the protection of the rights of vulnerable groups during budget reviews. In Hong Kong, the pressure of "school closures" resulting from a shrinking school-age population forces education policy to strike a balance between maintaining educational quality and adjusting class structures. These issues reflect the common challenges faced by governments and legislative bodies in East Asian societies when dealing with low birth rates and the allocation of social welfare resources.
  
  There are significant differences in perspectives from various sectors; Wang Shih-chien views the controversy as leverage for political gamesmanship, while the KMT attempts to frame the event as "damage control" and adjustment through the caucus whip's apology. The public should avoid over-interpreting a single party's apology or a single educational policy change as the ultimate solution to structural social problems. Key points for future observation include: the progress of the Legislative Yuan's subsequent review of the sheltered workshop budget and whether the KMT caucus will further revise its stance on budget cuts; and in Hong Kong, continued attention to how the Education Bureau implements its "soft landing" strategy amid the trend of declining school-age populations, as well as whether transition arrangements during the merger of affected schools can effectively safeguard students' right to education.
  
  The development of these social issues will continue to affect public trust in government policies and social stability in the respective regions.

# 臺灣與香港近期社會政策動態：教育縮編與庇護工場預算爭議

> 近期東亞地區社會議題頻傳，臺灣與香港分別在身心障礙福利與基礎教育領域面臨嚴峻的政策挑戰。臺灣政壇因立委刪減身障庇護工場預算引發社會強烈反彈，國民黨團總召傅崐萁對此公開道歉，試圖平息輿論壓力；香港則因學齡人口結構性下降，導致開學日面臨大規模的小學班級縮編與學校合併壓力。這些事件不僅反映了兩地在社會福利與教育資源分配上的困境，也凸顯了民意代表與政府機關在處理敏感社會議題時，所面臨的政治與行政挑戰。


### 臺灣：庇護工場預算爭議與政治止血策略
2026年9月1日，臺灣立法院國民黨團總召傅崐萁針對近期引發社會高度關注的「身障庇護工場預算爭議」，於公開場合率領黨團成員進行90度鞠躬道歉。此舉被視為國民黨內部針對預算爭議的「止血」策略，旨在平息因國民黨籍立委翁曉玲在審查預算時，針對身障福利相關預算提出刪減而引發的社會各界強烈反彈與質疑。
民進黨立法委員王世堅於同日接受《CNEWS匯流新聞網》政論節目《中午來開匯》專訪時，對此議題發表評論。王世堅指出，翁曉玲刪減身障福利預算的行為，不僅引發社會共同反彈，更被視為民進黨在政治攻防上的轉機。他甚至形容，這類爭議表現反而成為民進黨的正面助力，並戲稱「上帝送了國民黨主席鄭麗文、翁曉玲給民進黨」。王世堅認為，國民黨籍立委的爭議行為反映了政黨人才與政策思維的落差，並將其視為政治博弈的籌碼。
目前各方對於該預算案的後續調整方向，以及是否會完全恢復相關經費，仍有待立法院進一步審議與確認。
### 香港：學齡人口下降與教育結構調整
與此同時，香港教育領域亦面臨嚴峻的社會結構挑戰。根據媒體報導，2026年9月1日開學日當天，全港官津小學共計減少超過140班小一，受影響學校多達120多間。教育局指出，此舉主要受學齡人口結構性下降影響。目前已知有15間小學獲派「0班」，面臨停辦或合併的命運。
對於政策變動帶來的影響，家長與校方之間存在不同看法。部分家長認為合併後的資源整合可能帶來「一加一大於二」的效益，例如將軍澳基德小學與黃大仙姊妹學校的合併案，即引發了關於資源整合的討論。然而，亦有校長呼籲政府應提供更細緻的軟著陸策略。教育局局長蔡若蓮強調，面對學齡人口結構性下降，將持續採取循序漸進的調整策略，協助學校調整班數或人數。對於受到影響的學校教職員與家庭而言，政策的變動仍帶來極大的不確定性，如何保障學生受教權成為後續觀察重點。
### 區域社會政策的共同挑戰與後續觀察
上述事件對區域社會政策產生了直接影響。在臺灣，身障福利預算的爭議凸顯了民意代表在審查預算時，對於弱勢團體權益保障的敏感度問題；而在香港，學齡人口減少帶來的「殺校」壓力，則迫使教育政策必須在維持教育品質與調整班級結構之間取得平衡。這些議題反映了東亞社會在面對少子化與社會福利資源分配時，政府與立法機關所面臨的共同挑戰。
各界觀點存在顯著差異，王世堅將爭議視為政治博弈的籌碼，而國民黨則透過總召道歉試圖將事件定調為「止血」與調整。公眾應避免將單一政黨的道歉或單一教育政策的變動，過度解讀為社會結構問題的終極解決方案。後續觀察重點在於：臺灣立法院針對身障庇護工場預算的後續審查進度，以及國民黨黨團是否會進一步修正其預算刪減立場；香港方面則需持續關注學齡人口下降趨勢下，教育局如何落實「軟著陸」策略，以及受影響學校在合併過程中的過渡安排是否能有效保障學生受教權。
這些社會議題的發展，將持續影響當地民眾對政府政策的信任度與社會穩定。
