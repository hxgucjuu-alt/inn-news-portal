  As a core region for global geopolitics and economic development, Northeast Asia is currently facing multi-dimensional transformation and pressure. From a historical perspective, the region has long been influenced by the entanglements left over from World War II, resulting in complex interdependencies in regional security and diplomatic relations. Simultaneously, with the recovery of the global economy and tourism market in 2026, Northeast Asian nations are actively adjusting their industrial strategies, particularly regarding the transformation of the tourism industry and the restoration of international flight routes. Furthermore, extreme heat caused by climate change has had a significant impact on the region's social infrastructure and the lives of its residents, indicating that while Northeast Asia pursues economic growth, it must confront multiple governance issues such as environmental adaptation and regional stability.
  
  Multiple sources consistently indicate that industrial linkages within the Northeast Asian region are in a phase of reorganization. According to data from the aviation and tourism industries, flight frequency and capacity between Northeast Asian and Southeast Asian markets have rebounded significantly since 2023, signaling that the decoupling of the regional tourism market following the pandemic has come to an end. Southeast Asian countries, such as Vietnam, are actively viewing Northeast Asia as a key engine for tourism industry growth, aiming to achieve annual tourism targets by attracting visitors from the region. At the same time, Northeast Asian countries are committed to developing diverse tourism models, such as "night tourism," in an attempt to tap into consumption potential within a highly differentiated yet closely connected regional market.
  
  However, the development of these economic activities still cannot be entirely decoupled from geopolitical friction within the region, as historical legacies continue to influence the foundation of mutual trust between nations.
  
  Regarding the current state of development in Northeast Asia, different media reports present varying focuses. Some perspectives emphasize the impact of the region's historical baggage on modern diplomacy, noting that the strong reactions from North Korea and China regarding historical issues are often used to justify their military expansion, while pointing fingers at Japan, thereby keeping regional security in a state of long-term tension. Conversely, other reports focus on economic and climate issues, pointing out that Northeast Asia has shown strong performance in the recovery of tourism spending power and aviation capacity, while also facing severe tests brought about by climate change. For instance, during August 2026, the Northeast Asian region experienced abnormal heat, with temperatures soaring above 90 degrees Fahrenheit, demonstrating that environmental pressure has become a common public issue for the region.
  
  These pieces of information indicate that the development of Northeast Asia is not only constrained by politics but is also doubly restricted by the natural environment and global economic trends.
  
  In terms of specific impact, the regional integration and economic performance of Northeast Asia serve as indicators for the global market. The recovery of the tourism industry has not only driven capital investment in the regional aviation sector but has also facilitated the reconstruction of the transnational tourism ecosystem. In particular, the development of the night-time economy has brought new business models to urban governance and the service industry. However, extreme heat triggered by climate change poses a direct threat to power supply, public health, and agricultural production. If countries in the region cannot reach effective cooperation on climate adaptation, extreme weather may exacerbate social inequality and negatively impact cities reliant on the tourism economy, subsequently affecting the overall economic growth momentum of the region.
  
  There are differing interpretations regarding the prospects for Northeast Asia. Some analyses suggest that Northeast Asian countries should move beyond historical entanglements and shift toward more pragmatic regional economic cooperation to address the common challenges brought by globalization. However, other views point out that historical issues are deeply embedded in regional political culture and are difficult to resolve in the short term, meaning that any form of regional integration must be conducted within a highly uncertain political environment. Furthermore, the role of Northeast Asia in international geopolitics is often compared with conflict issues in places like Ukraine, indicating that under the framework of global governance, the stability of Northeast Asia, along with issues such as anti-corruption and the rule of law, is also subject to strict scrutiny by the international community.
  
  Therefore, oversimplifying Northeast Asia as a single economic or political entity risks ignoring its internal, highly differentiated, and complex structural contradictions.

Second, whether the connection between Northeast Asia and Southeast Asia in tourism and the aviation industry can continue to deepen and transform into a more stable model of regional economic integration. Finally, the extent to which historical legacies interfere with regional security dialogues, particularly how countries balance historical sentiment with realistic security needs when facing the North Korean nuclear issue and regional military deployments. These dynamics will determine the regional stability and economic competitiveness of Northeast Asia over the next decade and serve as key indicators for assessing the region's position on the global geopolitical map.

# 東北亞區域發展現況：從歷史糾葛、極端氣候到旅遊經濟的轉型挑戰

> 本文綜整東北亞地區近期在歷史地緣政治、氣候變遷與觀光產業的動態，分析區域內各國如何處理歷史遺緒，並透過旅遊經濟與氣候調適，應對全球化下的區域整合與挑戰。


東北亞作為全球地緣政治與經濟發展的核心區域，近期面臨多重維度的轉型與壓力。從歷史層面觀察，該區域長期受二戰後遺留的歷史糾葛影響，使得區域安全與外交關係呈現複雜的連動性；與此同時，隨著2026年全球經濟與旅遊市場的復甦，東北亞各國正積極調整產業策略，特別是觀光產業的轉型與跨國航線的修復。此外，氣候變遷帶來的極端高溫現象，亦對該區域的社會基礎設施與居民生活造成顯著衝擊，顯示東北亞在追求經濟成長的同時，必須面對環境調適與區域穩定等多重治理課題。
多項來源一致指出，東北亞區域內的產業連結正處於重組階段。根據航空與旅遊產業的資料顯示，自2023年起，東北亞與東南亞市場之間的航班頻率與運能已顯著回升，顯示疫情後區域內旅遊市場的解耦狀態已告終結。越南等東南亞國家正積極將東北亞視為觀光產業成長的關鍵引擎，透過吸引東北亞旅客來訪，以達成年度旅遊目標。與此同時，東北亞各國亦致力於發展「夜間觀光」等多元旅遊型態，試圖在高度差異化且連結緊密的區域市場中，挖掘消費潛力。
然而，這些經濟活動的開展，仍無法完全與區域內的地緣政治摩擦脫鉤，歷史遺緒持續影響著各國間的互信基礎。
關於東北亞的發展現況，不同媒體報導呈現出側重點的差異。部分觀點強調該區域歷史包袱對現代外交的影響，指出北韓與中國對於歷史問題的強烈反應，常被用於正當化其軍事擴張，並將矛頭指向日本，使區域安全域性勢長期處於緊張狀態。然而，另一方面的報導則聚焦於經濟與氣候議題，指出東北亞在旅遊消費力與航空運能的恢復上表現強勁，且正面臨氣候變遷帶來的嚴峻考驗，例如2026年8月期間，東北亞地區出現異常高溫，氣溫飆升至華氏90度以上，顯示環境壓力已成為區域共同面臨的公共議題。
這些資訊顯示，東北亞的發展不僅受限於政治，更受到自然環境與全球經濟趨勢的雙重牽制。
在具體影響層面，東北亞的區域整合與經濟表現對全球市場具有指標性意義。觀光產業的復甦不僅帶動了區域內航空業的資本投入，也促進了跨國旅遊生態系的重新建構，特別是針對夜間經濟的開發，為城市治理與服務業帶來了新的商業模式。然而，氣候變遷引發的極端高溫，則對電力供應、公共衛生及農業生產構成直接威脅。若區域內各國無法針對氣候調適達成有效合作，極端氣候恐將加劇社會不平等，並對依賴旅遊經濟的城市造成負面衝擊，進而影響整體區域的經濟成長動能。
各界對於東北亞的前景存在不同解讀。部分分析認為，東北亞各國應超越歷史糾葛，轉向更務實的區域經濟合作，以應對全球化帶來的共同挑戰；但也有觀點指出，歷史問題已深植於區域政治文化中，短期內難以化解，這使得任何形式的區域整合都必須在高度不確定的政治環境下進行。此外，關於東北亞在國際地緣政治中的角色，亦常與烏克蘭等地的衝突議題進行對照，顯示在全球治理的架構下，東北亞的穩定性與反貪腐、法治等議題同樣受到國際社會的嚴格檢視。
因此，過度簡化東北亞為單一經濟體或政治體，恐將忽略其內部高度差異化與複雜的結構性矛盾。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個面向：首先是東北亞各國在極端氣候頻發的背景下，是否會建立區域性的氣候防災與能源合作機制；其次，觀察東北亞與東南亞在觀光旅遊與航空產業的連結是否能持續深化，並轉化為更穩定的區域經濟整合模式；最後，則是持續關注歷史遺緒對區域安全對話的幹擾程度，特別是各國在面對北韓核武議題與區域軍事部署時，如何平衡歷史情緒與現實安全需求。這些動態將決定東北亞在未來十年內的區域穩定性與經濟競爭力，亦是評估該區域在全球地緣政治版圖中地位的關鍵指標。
