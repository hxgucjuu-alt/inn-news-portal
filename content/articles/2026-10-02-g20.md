  # The U.S. Pushes for Trade System Reform at the G20, While Oceania Island Nations Tread Carefully Amid Geopolitical Rivalries
  
  > U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer called at the G20 for a review of the Most-Favored-Nation (MFN) tariff system to address industrial overcapacity. Meanwhile, South Pacific island nations remain cautious about signing comprehensive cooperation agreements with China, fearing they may become pawns in the geopolitical rivalry between major powers.
  
  The global trade landscape is facing drastic changes. On September 30, 2026, U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer issued a significant policy appeal during the G20 Trade Ministers' Meeting in Milwaukee. Greer argued that, in response to current global supply chain imbalances caused by industrial overcapacity and non-market policies, nations should re-examine the post-WWII tariff system and the core Most-Favored-Nation (MFN) principle of the World Trade Organization (WTO). The U.S. government believes that the unconditional application of identical tariff treatment has limited the ability of countries to respond to market distortions, emphasizing the necessity of revitalizing domestic manufacturing through tariffs and critical supply chain measures.
  
  This position reflects the Trump administration's attempt to reshape global trade rules to alleviate the competitive pressures faced by domestic producers and workers.
  
  As the U.S. pushes for trade system reform, geopolitical trends in the Oceania region have also become a focus of international attention. According to an analysis by the German media outlet *Deutsche Welle* (DW), South Pacific island nations have adopted a highly cautious attitude in handling their relations with China. Although China is actively seeking to expand its influence in the region, most South Pacific island nations have yet to sign comprehensive cooperation agreements with China. Analysts believe the primary concern of these island nations is to avoid becoming pawns in the power struggle between major nations like the U.S. and China, which could jeopardize their own sovereignty and development space.
  
  This vigilance toward great power competition, combined with the European Union's recent shift toward strategic investment in the Persian Gulf to address geopolitical risks, outlines the complex landscape of current global supply chain restructuring and geopolitical tension.
  
  There are clear differences in how various media outlets interpret the current state of global trade. The official U.S. position explicitly points the finger at China's non-market policies, arguing that its production scale far exceeds domestic demand, leading to the dumping of surplus products globally and threatening international market order. However, German media approaches the issue from a geopolitical perspective, highlighting the dilemmas faced by European countries, such as Germany, in the process of decoupling from Chinese interests, and viewing the policy choices of South Pacific island nations as a hedging strategy.
  
  Furthermore, South Korea's export data for September shows that, driven by demand for AI chips, semiconductor exports hit a record high. This indicates that despite the impending reshaping of global trade rules, supply chain demand for high-tech industries remains robust, contrasting with trade frictions in traditional industrial products.
  
  For Oceania and global industries, the aforementioned policy changes will have profound impacts. If the U.S. succeeds in pushing G20 nations to strengthen tariffs on key industries such as steel, it is expected to have a direct impact on manufacturing sectors that rely on global supply chains. For Oceania island nations, this means that in future participation in the global trade framework, they must seek a more nuanced balance between the two major economies of the U.S. and China to ensure they secure resources for energy transition and infrastructure construction without being drawn into intense geopolitical conflicts. The trend of alliances between the EU and the Persian Gulf also provides a reference for Oceania nations: to enhance economic resilience through diversified strategic investments and reduce over-reliance on a single market.
  
  However, there remains disagreement regarding the review of the tariff system proposed by Greer. Some economists worry that if countries compete to abandon the MFN principle, it could lead to the rise of global trade protectionism, triggering a chain reaction of trade retaliation and ultimately harming global economic growth. Additionally, while the argument from German media regarding the decoupling of interests from China resonates within Europe, balancing economic prosperity with human rights issues remains a major challenge for governments in practice. These perspectives remind policymakers that when addressing overcapacity and supply chain security, they should not over-interpret the effectiveness of a single trade tool and must consider the complexity of the global economic structure.
  
  Future observation will focus on the specific responses of G20 member states to U.S. trade proposals and whether the proposed steel tariff measures by 28 nations will be implemented. At the same time, whether South Pacific island nations will further clarify their frameworks and bottom lines for cooperation with major powers in upcoming regional forums will be key to observing the geopolitical direction of Oceania. Furthermore, whether South Korea's strong chip export performance can be sustained, and whether strategic cooperation between the EU and the Persian Gulf can effectively alleviate Europe's energy and technology investment gaps, will all influence the stability of global supply chains and the evolution of future trade rules.

# 美國於G20力推貿易體制改革，大洋洲島國審慎應對地緣博弈

> 美國貿易代表格里爾於G20呼籲檢視最惠國關稅制度以應對產能過剩，與此同時，南太平洋島國因擔憂淪為大國博弈棋子，對與中國簽署全面合作協議保持審慎態度。


近期全球貿易格局面臨劇烈變動，美國貿易代表格里爾（Jamieson Greer）於2026年9月30日在密爾瓦基舉行的G20貿易部長會議中提出重大政策呼籲。格里爾主張，針對當前工業產能過剩及非市場政策導致的全球供應鏈失衡，各國應重新檢視二戰後形成的關稅制度與世界貿易組織（WTO）核心的最惠國待遇原則。美國政府認為，無條件適用相同關稅待遇已限制各國應對市場扭曲的手段，並強調透過關稅與關鍵供應鏈措施振興本國製造業的必要性。
此一立場反映出川普政府試圖重塑全球貿易規則，以緩解國內生產者與勞工面臨的競爭壓力。
在美國推動貿易體制改革的同時，大洋洲地區的地緣政治動向亦成為國際關注焦點。根據德語媒體《法蘭克福匯報》的分析指出，南太平洋島國在處理與中國的關係時採取了高度審慎的態度。儘管中國積極尋求擴大在該區域的影響力，但多數南太平洋島國至今仍未與中國簽署全面合作協議。分析認為，這些島國的主要顧慮在於避免在美中等大國博弈中淪為棋子，從而損害自身的主權與發展空間。
這種對大國競爭的警惕，與歐盟近期為應對地緣風險而尋求波斯灣戰略投資的轉向策略，共同勾勒出當前全球供應鏈重組與地緣政治緊張的複雜圖景。
各方媒體對於全球貿易現狀的解讀存在明顯差異。美國官方立場明確將矛頭指向中國的非市場政策，認為其生產規模遠超國內需求，導致過剩產品傾銷全球，進而威脅國際市場秩序。然而，德語媒體則從地緣政治角度切入，強調歐洲國家如德國在解除與中國利益捆綁過程中的困境，並將南太平洋島國的政策選擇視為一種避險策略。
此外，韓國9月份出口資料顯示，在AI晶片需求帶動下，半導體出口創下歷史新高，這顯示出儘管全球貿易規則面臨重塑，但高科技產業的供應鏈需求依然強勁，與傳統工業產品的貿易摩擦形成對比。
對於大洋洲及全球產業而言，上述政策變動將產生深遠影響。若美國成功推動G20國家對鋼鐵等關鍵產業加強關稅，預計將對依賴全球供應鏈的製造業造成直接衝擊。對於大洋洲島國來說，這意味著在未來參與全球貿易架構時，必須在美中兩大經濟體之間尋求更為細緻的平衡，以確保在能源轉型與基礎設施建設上獲得資源，同時不被捲入激烈的地緣政治衝突。歐盟與波斯灣地區的結盟趨勢，也為大洋洲國家提供了參考，即透過多元化戰略投資來提升經濟韌性，減少對單一市場的過度依賴。
然而，對於格里爾所提出的關稅制度檢視，各界觀點仍存在分歧。部分經濟學家擔憂，若各國競相放棄最惠國待遇原則，可能導致全球貿易保護主義抬頭，進而引發連鎖的貿易報復，最終損害全球經濟成長。此外，德國媒體呼籲解除與中國利益捆綁的論點，雖然在歐洲內部引起共鳴，但在實務上如何兼顧經濟繁榮與人權議題，仍是各國政府面臨的重大挑戰。這些觀點提醒決策者，在處理產能過剩與供應鏈安全問題時，不應過度解讀單一貿易手段的成效，必須考量全球經濟結構的複雜性。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於G20成員國對美國貿易主張的具體回應，以及28國擬議中的鋼鐵關稅措施是否會落實。同時，南太平洋島國在即將到來的區域論壇中，是否會進一步明確其對大國合作的框架與底線，將是觀察大洋洲地緣政治走向的關鍵。此外，韓國晶片出口的強勁表現能否持續，以及歐盟與波斯灣地區的戰略合作是否能有效緩解歐洲的能源與科技投資缺口，均將影響全球供應鏈的穩定性與未來貿易規則的演變。
