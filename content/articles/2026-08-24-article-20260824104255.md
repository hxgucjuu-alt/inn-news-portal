  # North Korea Slams Japan's Record Defense Budget, Accuses It of Preparing for "Aggressive War"
  
  > North Korea's state media strongly condemned Japan's plan to allocate a defense budget of 8.9 trillion yen, accusing it of preparing for an "aggressive war," vowing a "thorough counterattack," and warning that the move would bring "new turmoil" to the region and the world.
  
  North Korea's official media recently issued a strongly worded condemnation regarding the defense budget proposal submitted by Japan's Ministry of Defense for the next fiscal year. According to a commentary released by the Korean Central News Agency (KCNA), North Korean authorities accused Japan of planning to allocate a record 8.9 trillion yen (approximately 60 billion U.S. dollars) defense budget as preparation for launching an "aggressive war," and threatened a "thorough counterattack." This development comes as Japan's Ministry of Defense has formally set its budget request for fiscal year 2027 at a record high of 8.9 trillion yen, with external expectations that the final budget size could further rise to approximately 10 trillion yen.
  
  This move immediately drew attention to the tense security situation in Northeast Asia, with North Korea's reaction becoming a focal point of observation for the international community.
  
  According to multiple foreign wire reports and cross-referencing, the core content of North Korea's condemnation is highly consistent. Yonhap News Agency reported that North Korea's condemnation was issued after Japan's Ministry of Defense set its budget request at 8.9 trillion yen, and threatened a "thorough counterattack." Arab News Japan's report quoted the KCNA statement, saying that Japan's move "clearly marks a dangerous expansion of the war budget, which will bring new turmoil to the region and the world."
  
  In addition, Turkey's Anadolu Agency also confirmed that North Korea criticized the budget through its official media, accusing the move of exacerbating regional security tensions. All three media outlets confirmed North Korea's strong opposition to Japan's defense budget and its core narrative accusing Japan of "preparing for an aggressive war."
  
  Although multiple media outlets reported consistently on the fact of North Korea's condemnation, there are differences in some details and expressions. First, regarding the currency conversion of the budget amount, Yonhap reported approximately 60 billion U.S. dollars, while Anadolu Agency converted it to approximately 56 billion U.S. dollars, showing slight discrepancies due to exchange rate conversion. Second, regarding the description of the budget scale, Yonhap clearly noted that "the final budget is expected to rise to approximately 10 trillion yen," information not explicitly mentioned in the reports of the other two media outlets, representing additional supplementary information from Yonhap.
  
  Furthermore, Arab News Japan's report particularly emphasized the background context that "its U.S. ally is negotiating with Pyongyang," interpreting Japan's defense budget expansion within the framework of U.S.-North Korea dialogue, an aspect less covered in the reports of the other two media outlets. These differences show that while the basic facts of North Korea's condemnation are clear, each media outlet emphasized different backgrounds and interpretations of details in their reporting.
  
  North Korea's strong reaction this time has direct and concrete implications for the regional security landscape in Northeast Asia. If Japan's planned defense budget expansion is ultimately realized, it will remain at historical highs for several consecutive years, which not only changes Japan's own defense policy direction but is also bound to affect the security strategy assessments of neighboring countries. North Korea's condemnation, particularly the rhetoric vowing a "thorough counterattack," intensifies the existing tensions between the Korean Peninsula and Japan. This move could heighten concerns over an arms race in the region and affect the deepening of trilateral U.S.-Japan-South Korea security cooperation and North Korea's response.
  
  At the same time, against the backdrop of the United States seeking dialogue with North Korea, North Korea's strong criticism of Japan's move may also add new variables to related diplomatic mediation, making the handling of regional security issues more complex.
  
  Regarding North Korea's condemnation, the international community and experts may hold different views. Some analyses may argue that North Korea's fierce rhetoric is its customary diplomatic strategy, aimed at strengthening internal unity and gaining leverage in negotiations by highlighting external threats. Therefore, the specific threat of a "thorough counterattack" from North Korea should not be overinterpreted as imminent military action. On the other hand, the Japanese government may argue that its defense budget increase is a necessary measure to respond to changes in the regional security environment, particularly the missile threat from North Korea. However, North Korea's official position clearly characterizes this move as "war preparation," demonstrating a fundamental divergence in perceptions between the two sides.
  
  It must be noted that all current information comes from one-sided statements by North Korea's official media. The specific contents of Japan's budget, the countermeasures North Korea may subsequently take, and the response strategies of the U.S.-Japan-South Korea alliance all remain to be further observed and confirmed.

First, the process of Japan's National Diet deliberating on this budget and the final approved scale, whether it reaches 10 trillion yen as predicted by Yonhap, will be a key indicator for examining the actual direction of Japan's defense policy. Second, whether North Korea will take further concrete actions after issuing this condemnation, such as missile tests or more provocative rhetoric, in response to what it perceives as a threat, deserves close attention. Furthermore, whether the U.S. dialogue process with North Korea at this time will be affected by the Japan budget issue, and how the U.S. plays a coordinating role amid this regional tension, are also important points of observation.

# 北韓抨擊日本創紀錄國防預算，指控為「侵略戰爭」準備

> 北韓官媒強烈譴責日本計劃編列8.9兆日圓的國防預算，指控此舉是「侵略戰爭」的準備，揚言將徹底反擊，並警告此舉將為區域與全球帶來「新動盪」。


北韓官方媒體近日針對日本防衛省提出的下一財政年度國防預算案，發表措辭嚴厲的譴責宣告。根據朝鮮中央通訊社（KCNA）發布的評論，北韓當局指控日本計劃編列創紀錄的8.9兆日圓（約合600億美元）國防預算，是為了準備發動「侵略戰爭」，並威脅將進行「徹底反擊」。此事件發生之際，日本防衛省已正式將2027財政年度的預算要求設定在8.9兆日圓的歷史新高，而外界預期最終預算規模可能進一步攀升至約10兆日圓。
此舉立即引發東北亞區域安全情勢的緊張關注，北韓的反應也成為國際社會觀察焦點。
根據多家外電報導與交叉比對，北韓此次譴責的核心內容具有高度一致性。韓聯社（Yonhap）報導指出，北韓的譴責宣告是在日本防衛省將其預算要求設定於8.9兆日圓之後發布，並威脅將「徹底反擊」。阿拉伯新聞日本版（Arab News Japan）的報導則引述北韓中央通訊社的宣告，指稱日本此舉「清楚地標誌著戰爭預算的危險擴張，將為區域與世界帶來新的動盪」。
此外，土耳其安納杜魯新聞社（Anadolu Agency）的報導也確認，北韓透過其官方媒體批評此預算案，並指責此舉將加劇區域安全緊張局勢。三家媒體均確認了北韓官方對日本國防預算的強烈反對立場，以及其指控日本「準備侵略戰爭」的核心論述。
儘管多家媒體對北韓譴責事實的報導一致，但在部分細節與表述上存在差異。首先，關於預算金額的換算，韓聯社報導約為600億美元，而安納杜魯新聞社則換算為約560億美元，兩者存在匯率換算上的細微出入。其次，在預算規模的表述上，韓聯社明確指出「最終預算預計將升至約10兆日圓」，此資訊在其他兩家媒體的報導中未被明確提及，屬於韓聯社的額外補充資訊。
此外，阿拉伯新聞日本版的報導特別強調了「其美國盟友正與平壤進行談判」的背景脈絡，將日本的國防預算擴張置於美朝對話的框架下解讀，這是其他兩家媒體報導中較少著墨的面向。這些差異顯示，雖然北韓譴責的基本事實明確，但各媒體在報導時側重的背景與細節解讀不盡相同。
此次北韓的強烈反應，對東北亞區域安全格局產生直接且具體的影響。日本計劃中的國防預算擴張，若最終實現，將使其連續多年維持在歷史高位，這不僅改變日本自身的防衛政策走向，也勢必牽動周邊國家的安全戰略評估。北韓的譴責宣告，特別是揚言「徹底反擊」的措辭，加劇了朝鮮半島與日本之間既有的緊張關係。此舉可能導致區域內軍備競賽的擔憂升溫，並影響美日韓三邊安全合作的深化與北韓的反應。
同時，在美國與北韓尋求對話的背景下，北韓對日本此舉的強烈抨擊，也可能為相關外交斡旋增添新的變數，使得區域安全議題的處理更趨複雜。
針對北韓的譴責，國際社會與專家學者可能存在不同觀點。部分分析可能認為，北韓的激烈言辭是其慣用的外交策略，旨在透過對外部威脅的渲染，強化內部團結並在談判中爭取籌碼。因此，對於北韓「徹底反擊」的具體威脅，不宜過度解讀為即將發生的軍事行動。另一方面，日本政府對於其國防預算的增加，則可能辯稱是應對區域安全環境變化，特別是來自北韓飛彈威脅的必要舉措。然而，北韓的官方立場明確將此舉定性為「戰爭準備」，顯示雙方在認知上的根本對立。
必須注意的是，目前所有資訊均來源於北韓官方媒體的單方面表述，關於日本預算的具體內容、北韓後續可能採取的反制措施，以及美日韓同盟的應對策略，都尚待進一步觀察與確認。
後續觀察的重點應聚焦於以下幾個面向。首先，日本國會審議此預算案的過程與最終透過的規模，是否如韓聯社預測般達到10兆日圓，將是檢驗日本防衛政策實際走向的關鍵指標。其次，北韓在發表此次譴責宣告後，是否會採取更進一步的具體行動，例如飛彈試射或更具挑釁性的言論，來回應其所認定的威脅，值得高度關注。再者，美國在此時與北韓的對話程式，是否會因日本預算問題而受到影響，以及美國如何在此一區域緊張局勢中扮演協調角色，亦是重要的觀察點。
最後，南韓與中國對此事態的官方反應，也將提供理解區域國家立場與潛在互動的重要線索。
