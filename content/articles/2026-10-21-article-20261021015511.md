  # Rising Global Cybersecurity Threats: Chinese-made AI Tool Accused of Involvement in Multinational Corporate Cyberattacks
  
  > Tokyo-based cybersecurity firm GMO Cybersecurity reports that a Chinese-made AI tool named "ARTEX" has been exploited by hackers to target multinational entities, including Japanese corporations and South Korean banks, sparking global security concerns.
  
  The global cybersecurity landscape is facing a new form of threat. According to a report released on October 19, 2026, by the Tokyo-based cybersecurity firm GMO Cybersecurity by Ierae Inc, hacker organizations are utilizing "ARTEX," a Chinese-origin AI tool originally designed for testing network defense capabilities, to launch attacks against enterprises and financial institutions across multiple countries. This incident highlights the double-edged nature of artificial intelligence technology in the realm of cybercrime and the urgency of cross-border cybersecurity defense.
  
  Research by Daiki Fukumori, Director of the GMO Cybercrime Countermeasures Center, indicates that the ARTEX tool has been repurposed by hackers as an offensive weapon, recently causing damage to approximately 20 large Japanese corporations. Investigations revealed that these attacks originated from a single server infrastructure and were highly targeted, focusing primarily on Japan. Furthermore, researchers confirmed that the tool’s reach extends beyond Japan, as it is directly linked to multiple bank network intrusions in South Korea last month, demonstrating that the hacking campaign has crossed national borders and poses a significant threat to financial and corporate security in the Asian region.
  
  Although GMO Cybersecurity has confirmed the link between the ARTEX tool and the aforementioned attacks, the specific background of the hacker organizations, the identities of the masterminds, and their ultimate motives remain under investigation. There is currently no clear public information regarding the specific technical details of how the tool was converted from a defensive testing utility into an offensive weapon, nor whether other countries or industries have become potential targets. Additionally, due to varying confidentiality policies and response mechanisms, there is an information asymmetry regarding the extent of losses suffered by the victimized companies and the progress of their security vulnerability remediation.
  
  This incident brings concrete impacts and challenges to global industries and policymakers. First, governments may face pressure to re-evaluate export controls and application regulations for AI technology, particularly concerning cybersecurity tools with "dual-use" characteristics. For enterprises, this means that traditional firewalls and defense systems are no longer sufficient to counter attacks equipped with AI computing capabilities; cybersecurity protection strategies must be upgraded to address more sophisticated and automated attack patterns. Furthermore, cross-border cybersecurity collaboration mechanisms for financial institutions will become a core focus of future policy discussions to prevent localized security vulnerabilities from escalating into global financial risks.
  
  Academia and cybersecurity experts maintain a cautious stance on this incident, urging the public not to over-interpret the threat of a single tool or view all technology originating from a specific region as inherently malicious. Experts emphasize that ARTEX is essentially a defensive tool, and the fact that it has been abused reflects the evolution of hacker techniques rather than a technological monopoly by any single nation. Additionally, relevant authorities remind enterprises to strictly vet the backgrounds of vendors when importing AI security solutions and to establish multi-layered monitoring mechanisms to prevent a single point of technology from becoming a breach in system integrity. They also advise against politicizing such technical issues to avoid hindering the normal exchange and development of global cybersecurity technology.
  
  Future observation will focus on the post-disaster recovery of the victimized companies and whether international cybersecurity organizations will issue a global security alert regarding the ARTEX tool. At the same time, whether regulatory agencies in Japan and South Korea will initiate cross-border judicial assistance to track and crack down on the servers used as attack sources will serve as a benchmark for whether such cross-border cybercrimes can be effectively suppressed. Furthermore, as the share of AI technology in cyberattacks continues to rise, the research and development of "AI vs. AI" defense technologies in the cybersecurity industry is expected to become a key focus for global technology policy and industrial investment.

# 國際資安威脅升溫：中國製AI工具遭指控涉及多國企業駭客攻擊

> 東京資安公司GMO Cybersecurity指出，一款名為ARTEX的中國製AI工具遭駭客利用，攻擊包含日本企業與韓國銀行在內的跨國目標，引發全球資安關注。


近期全球網路安全環境面臨新型態威脅，根據總部位於東京的網路安全公司GMO Cybersecurity by Ierae Inc於2026年10月19日發布的報告，駭客組織正利用一款源自中國、原設計用於測試網路防禦能力的AI工具「ARTEX」，對多國境內的企業與金融機構發動攻擊。此事件凸顯了人工智慧技術在網路犯罪領域的雙面刃特性，以及跨境資安防禦的迫切性。
根據GMO網路犯罪對策中心主任Daiki Fukumori的研究指出，該款名為ARTEX的工具被駭客轉用為攻擊手段，近期已造成約20家日本大型企業受害。調查發現，這些攻擊行動均源自單一伺服器基礎設施，且攻擊目標具有高度針對性，主要集中於日本境內。此外，研究人員進一步證實，該工具不僅限於日本，亦與上個月發生在韓國的多起銀行網路入侵事件有直接關聯，顯示該駭客行動已跨越國界，對亞洲區域的金融與企業安全構成顯著威脅。
儘管GMO Cybersecurity已確認ARTEX工具與上述攻擊事件的關聯性，但目前關於駭客組織的具體背景、幕後主使者身份以及其發動攻擊的最終動機，仍處於調查階段。各界對於該工具是如何從防禦測試用途轉化為攻擊武器的具體技術細節，以及是否還有其他國家或產業成為潛在目標，目前尚未有明確的公開資訊。此外，受害企業的損失程度與資安漏洞修復進度，亦因各企業的保密政策與應對機制不同，呈現出資訊不對稱的情形。
此次事件對全球產業與政策制定者帶來了具體的衝擊與挑戰。首先，針對AI技術的出口管制與應用監管，各國政府可能面臨重新評估的壓力，特別是針對具有「雙重用途」（Dual-use）性質的資安工具。對於企業而言，這意味著傳統的防火牆與防禦系統已不足以應對具備AI運算能力的攻擊手段，資安防護策略必須升級，以應對更為精密且自動化的攻擊模式。此外，跨境金融機構的資安協作機制亦將成為未來政策討論的核心，以防止單一地區的資安漏洞擴大為全球性的金融風險。
學界與資安專家對此事件持有謹慎態度，呼籲各界不應過度解讀單一工具的威脅性，亦不應將所有源自特定地區的技術工具皆視為惡意來源。專家強調，ARTEX本質上是防禦性工具，其遭濫用的事實反映的是駭客技術的演進，而非單一國家的技術壟斷。此外，相關單位提醒，企業在匯入AI資安解決方案時，應嚴格審核供應商背景，並建立多層次的監控機制，避免單一技術點成為系統崩潰的破口，同時應避免將此類技術議題政治化，以免影響全球資安技術的正常交流與研發。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於受害企業的災後復原情況，以及國際資安組織是否會針對ARTEX工具發布全球性的安全警示。同時，日本與韓國相關監管機構是否會啟動跨國司法互助，針對攻擊來源的伺服器進行追蹤與取締，將是衡量此類跨境資安犯罪是否能獲得有效抑制的指標。此外，隨著AI技術在網路攻擊中的佔比持續攀升，未來資安產業對於「AI對抗AI」的防禦技術研發，預計將成為全球科技政策與產業投資的重點方向。
