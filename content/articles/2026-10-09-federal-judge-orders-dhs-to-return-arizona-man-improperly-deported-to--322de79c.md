根據多家媒體報導，美國亞利桑那聯邦地區法院法官約翰·C·欣德雷克（John C. Hinderaker）作出裁決，命令美國國土安全部（DHS）必須協助遭驅逐出境並拘留於非洲赤道幾內亞的男子艾哈邁德·索利曼（Ahmed Soliman）返回美國。

法庭裁定指出，國土安全部在該驅逐程序中違反了美國憲法第五修正案的正當法律程序條款（Due Process Clause）。法官在裁決中要求，美方必須協助索利曼自赤道幾內亞的拘留中獲釋並返美，且後續案件處理必須回復至其未被不當遣送前的正當程序狀態。

報導指出，現年31歲的索利曼原居住於亞利桑那州鳳凰城，為一名同性戀男子。相關資料顯示，他在與赤道幾內亞毫無淵源的情況下於4月遭遣送出境，並在該國遭到拘留長達五個月。此項由川普任命的聯邦法官所作出的裁定，要求政府糾正這起被認定為非法的驅逐行動。

## English edition

A U.S. federal judge has ordered the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) to facilitate the return of Ahmed Soliman, an Arizona man who was improperly deported and subsequently detained in Equatorial Guinea.

Judge John C. Hinderaker of the U.S. District Court for the District of Arizona, a Trump appointee, ruled that the federal government violated the Due Process Clause of the Fifth Amendment. The ruling specifies that DHS must assist in securing Soliman's release from custody in Equatorial Guinea and coordinate his return to the United States, ensuring his immigration case proceeds as it would have prior to the improper removal.

According to media reports, the 31-year-old Phoenix resident and gay man had been deported in April to the Central African nation, a country with which he has no established ties. He has reportedly remained in detention there for five months, prompting the judicial order that requires federal authorities to rectify the unlawful deportation.
