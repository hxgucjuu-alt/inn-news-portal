  # Restructuring of the Northeast Asian Energy Supply Chain: Jet Fuel Exports to Europe Resume for the First Time
  
  > Affected by the Iran war, the Northeast Asian jet fuel supply chain has undergone significant changes. The latest shipping data shows that the region has resumed fuel exports to Europe, easing the pressure of low commercial inventories in Europe.
  
  As a core hub for global energy and industrial production, Northeast Asia is experiencing a chain reaction due to the restructuring of international supply chains caused by recent geopolitical conflicts. Since the outbreak of the Iran war in late February 2026, global energy shipping routes and supply-demand structures have undergone drastic changes. According to monitoring by Reuters and relevant trade data, Northeast Asia has once again become a focus of international attention regarding energy logistics and security issues. At the same time, the regional security situation in Northeast Asia, accompanied by activities such as North Korean missile tests, has left the region facing multiple challenges and adjustments between energy supply and geopolitical security.
  
  According to shipping tracking data and third-party trade sources, Northeast Asia resumed jet fuel exports to Europe in late May 2026, marking the first such shipping record since the outbreak of the Iran war in late February. Data indicates that with increased supply in the Asian region and low commercial inventories in Europe, an east-west arbitrage opportunity has emerged in the market. TradingView cited information indicating that the total volume of jet fuel shipped from Northeast Asia to Europe in June is estimated to reach 465,000 tons, a new high in nearly a year.
  
  This move is seen as a dynamic adjustment by the global energy supply chain to address regional supply-demand imbalances under wartime conditions, reflecting the key role played by the Northeast Asian refining industry in the international energy market.
  
  However, regarding the overall situation in Northeast Asia, information provided by different sources varies in focus. In terms of energy trade, data shows that the supply chain is actively expanding; yet in terms of regional security, information reveals a tense situation. The U.S. government recently publicly condemned North Korea's missile test actions and called on the Pyongyang authorities to exercise restraint regarding behaviors that could escalate tensions in Northeast Asia. Furthermore, while discussions at the U.S.-South Korea summit regarding the Taiwan Strait issue and the alliance's stance against China reflect close cooperation between Washington and Seoul in addressing North Korea's nuclear issue and regional security, relevant analyses point out that such summits still have limitations in achieving substantive breakthroughs.
  
  What market and policy observers need to clarify at present is whether the resumption of energy exports is benefiting from a temporary easing of specific geopolitics, or if it is purely a market behavior driven by commercial interests.
  
  The resumption of jet fuel exports from Northeast Asia to Europe has profound implications for related industries and policies. First, for Northeast Asian refiners, the increased demand from the European market provides a channel to clear excess capacity, helping to maintain refining margins within the region. Second, for Europe, a stable supply from Asia helps alleviate energy anxiety caused by the disruption of the Middle East supply chain. However, this also highlights the degree of reliance of global energy transport on "east-west arbitrage"; once shipping costs rise or geopolitical conflicts expand, the vulnerability of the energy supply chain will be further exposed.
  
  At the policy level, how governments balance ensuring energy security with responding to regional military tensions has become the core of governance for Northeast Asian countries in the coming months.
  
  Although data shows an increase in export volumes, analysts warn against over-interpreting this phenomenon as a comprehensive improvement in the regional situation. First, the resumption of jet fuel exports is primarily driven by market supply-demand gaps, rather than dividends brought by geopolitical stability. Second, North Korea's military provocations in Northeast Asia continue to pose potential risks to regional shipping and energy infrastructure. Furthermore, there remains high uncertainty as to whether the U.S.-South Korea alliance's stance on the issue of countering China will further affect energy trade routes between Northeast Asia and the Middle East or Europe. Therefore, observing energy export data and geopolitical security issues separately is a more objective perspective for assessing the situation in Northeast Asia.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on the following three points: First, whether the volume of jet fuel exports from Northeast Asia to Europe can remain stable, or if it is merely a short-term demand for inventory replenishment; second, whether North Korea's subsequent military actions will affect regional shipping safety and insurance premiums; third, whether further coordination between the U.S. and South Korea on regional security issues will impose additional restrictions on the energy export policies of Northeast Asian countries. As the impact of the Iran war continues to ferment, Northeast Asia's strategic position in the international energy supply system, and how it handles the dual pressures of regional security and economic development, will be the focus of the global market in the future.

# 東北亞能源供應鏈重組：航空燃油首度恢復出口歐洲

> 受伊朗戰爭影響，東北亞航空燃油供應鏈出現顯著變動。最新船運資料顯示，該地區已恢復對歐洲的燃油出口，緩解歐洲商業庫存偏低壓力。


東北亞地區作為全球能源與工業生產的核心地帶，近期因地緣政治衝突導致的國際供應鏈重組，正產生連鎖反應。自2026年2月底伊朗戰爭爆發以來，全球能源運輸航線與供需結構發生劇烈變化。根據路透社與相關貿易資料監測，東北亞地區在能源物流與安全議題上，再次成為國際關注焦點。與此同時，東北亞的安全域性勢亦伴隨北韓飛彈試射等活動，使該區域在能源供應與地緣政治安全之間，面臨多重挑戰與調整。
根據船運追蹤資料及三方貿易來源共同證實，東北亞地區已於2026年5月下旬，恢復向歐洲出口航空燃油，這是自2月底伊朗戰爭爆發以來的首次航運紀錄。資料顯示，隨著亞洲地區供給量提升，加上歐洲商業庫存處於低位，市場出現了東西向套利空間。TradingView引述訊息指出，東北亞在6月份向歐洲運送的航空燃油總量預估達到46.5萬噸，創下近一年來的新高。
此舉被視為全球能源供應鏈在戰時狀態下，針對區域性供需失衡所進行的動態調整，反映出東北亞煉油產業在國際能源市場中扮演的關鍵角色。
然而，針對東北亞地區的整體局勢，不同來源提供的資訊側重各異。在能源貿易方面，資料顯示供應鏈正積極對外擴張；但在區域安全方面，資訊則顯示出緊張態勢。美國政府近期公開譴責北韓的飛彈試射行動，並呼籲平壤當局剋制可能導致東北亞緊張局勢升溫的行為。此外，美韓峰會針對臺海問題及同盟抗中議題的討論，雖然反映了華盛頓與首爾在應對北韓核武問題與區域安全上的緊密合作，但相關分析指出，該類峰會在實質突破上仍有侷限。
目前市場與政策觀察者需釐清的是，能源出口的恢復是否受惠於特定地緣政治的暫時緩解，或是純粹基於商業利益導向的市場行為。
此次東北亞航空燃油恢復出口歐洲，對於相關產業與政策具有深遠影響。首先，對於東北亞的煉油業者而言，歐洲市場的需求增加提供了去化過剩產能的管道，有助於維持區域內的煉油利潤。其次，對於歐洲而言，來自亞洲的穩定供應有助於緩解因中東供應鏈中斷而引發的能源焦慮。然而，這也凸顯了全球能源運輸對「東西向套利」的依賴程度，一旦航運成本上升或地緣衝突擴大，能源供應鏈的脆弱性將進一步顯現。
政策層面上，各國政府如何在保障能源安全與應對區域軍事緊張之間取得平衡，成為東北亞各國未來數月的施政核心。
儘管資料顯示出口量攀升，但分析師提醒，不應過度解讀此現象為區域局勢全面好轉。首先，航空燃油的出口恢復主要受市場供需缺口驅動，而非地緣政治穩定帶來的紅利。其次，北韓在東北亞的軍事挑釁行為，持續為區域航運與能源基礎設施帶來潛在風險。再者，美韓同盟在抗中議題上的立場，是否會進一步影響東北亞與中東或歐洲之間的能源貿易路徑，仍存在高度不確定性。因此，將能源出口資料與地緣政治安全議題脫鉤觀察，是目前評估東北亞局勢較為客觀的視角。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三點：第一，東北亞至歐洲的航空燃油出口量是否能維持穩定，抑或僅是短期內的庫存回補需求；第二，北韓後續的軍事動作是否會影響區域內的航運安全與保險費率；第三，美韓在區域安全議題上的進一步協調，是否會對東北亞國家的能源出口政策產生額外限制。隨著伊朗戰爭影響的持續發酵，東北亞在國際能源供應體系中的戰略地位，以及其如何應對區域安全與經濟發展的雙重壓力，將是未來全球市場關注的焦點。
