  # Trump Expresses No Opposition to Chinese Automakers Establishing Plants in the U.S., Provided They Follow the Japanese Model
  
  > In a recent interview, U.S. President Trump stated that he would not oppose Chinese automakers entering the U.S. market, provided they follow the Japanese model of building factories and hiring local workers. This statement has sparked widespread attention and discussion regarding automotive industry policy.
  
  On September 12, 2026, according to a Reuters report, U.S. President Trump shared his views on the issue of Chinese automakers entering the U.S. market during an interview with Fox News. Despite the prevailing opposition and defensive stance held by U.S. lawmakers and major domestic automakers toward the Chinese automotive industry, Trump explicitly stated that he is open to the idea if Chinese companies are willing to establish manufacturing plants on U.S. soil. This position not only suggests a potential shift in U.S.-China trade policy but also impacts the strategic layout of the global automotive supply chain.
  
  In the interview, Trump emphasized the importance of "localized production," using the historical experience of the Japanese automotive industry in the United States as a comparison. He pointed out that when Japanese automakers entered the U.S. market, they chose to build local plants and hire a large number of American workers; this model created jobs for the U.S., and therefore, he does not oppose Chinese automakers operating under a similar framework. Trump emphasized: "The most important thing is that they must hire our workers." This remark has been interpreted by outsiders to mean that if the Trump administration adopts relevant policies in the future, it may view "job creation" as a core metric for foreign automaker market access, rather than relying solely on trade bans.
  
  There are still variables regarding the information on this issue, primarily due to the gap between Trump's personal stance and the existing positions held by the U.S. Congress and automotive industry unions. Although Trump has expressed a conditional openness, there remain significant concerns within the U.S. automotive industry regarding the security of Chinese electric vehicle technology and its supply chain, data privacy, and fair market competition. It is currently unclear how Trump would handle resistance from Congress if he were to formally push for such policies, or how he would define the specific legal regulations and regulatory details of "following the Japanese model." These details await further clarification in subsequent White House policy documents.
  
  If this policy intention is implemented, it will have a profound impact on the global automotive supply chain. For Chinese electric vehicle giants such as BYD, this could provide a potential path into the North American market, though it would also mean bearing high local construction and labor costs. For the U.S. automotive industry, this could trigger a reshuffling of the industrial structure; on one hand, it may introduce competitive pressure, while on the other, if foreign investment in manufacturing is successfully attracted, it could help strengthen U.S. domestic manufacturing capabilities. Furthermore, this move could prompt international trade partners to re-examine market access fairness, particularly regarding regulations related to electric vehicle subsidies and technology transfers.
  
  However, there are varying interpretations of Trump's remarks. Some observers believe this is merely a bargaining chip proposed by Trump during election campaigns or policy debates based on the "America First" principle, and it should not be over-interpreted as an imminent, comprehensive opening to Chinese automakers. Additionally, automotive industry experts warn that even if Chinese automakers are willing to build plants in the U.S., they would still face extremely strict regulatory scrutiny, including requirements for supply chain transparency and potential national security assessments. These technical restrictions cannot be easily overcome by a single statement from the President. Therefore, industry analysts suggest that all parties should continue to monitor subsequent administrative actions by the U.S. Department of Commerce and relevant regulatory agencies, rather than making investment decisions based solely on the content of public interviews.
  
  Future observation will focus on several key aspects: first, the U.S. Congress's reaction to this issue, particularly whether it will trigger stricter national security review mechanisms for Chinese automakers building plants in the U.S.; second, the substantive response from Chinese automakers regarding such conditions, specifically whether they are willing to sacrifice some supply chain advantages in exchange for U.S. market access; and finally, whether the U.S. government will establish specific regulatory standards for the labor hiring ratios and the degree of technology localization for foreign automakers. These developments will determine whether this policy intention remains merely a verbal statement or becomes a new normal for U.S.-China automotive trade and industrial cooperation.

# 川普表態不反對中國車企赴美設廠 生產模式需比照日本

> 美國總統川普近期受訪表示，若中國車企仿效日本模式在美設廠並聘用當地工人，他不反對其進入市場，此言論引發各界對汽車產業政策的關注與討論。


2026年9月12日，根據《路透社》報導，美國總統川普在接受《福斯新聞》（Fox News）採訪時，針對中國汽車製造商進入美國市場的議題發表了看法。儘管目前美國國會議員及國內大型汽車企業對中國汽車產業普遍持反對與防範態度，川普明確表示，若中國車企願意在美國本土建立生產工廠，他對此持開放態度。此一立場不僅涉及美中貿易政策的潛在轉向，亦牽動全球汽車產業鏈的佈局策略。
川普在訪談中強調了「在地化生產」的重要性，並以日本汽車產業過去在美國的發展經驗作為對照。他指出，日本車企當年進入美國市場時，選擇在當地設廠並大量聘用美國勞工，這種模式為美國創造了就業機會，因此他並不排斥中國車企循此模式運作。川普強調：「最重要的是，他們必須僱用我們的工人。」這番言論被外界解讀為，川普政府未來若採取相關政策，可能將「就業機會」視為衡量外資車企准入的核心指標，而非單純的貿易禁令。
目前關於此議題的資訊仍存在部分變數，主要在於川普的個人表態與美國國會及汽車產業公會的既有立場存在落差。雖然川普本人表達了條件式的開放態度，但美國汽車產業內部對於中國電動車技術及其供應鏈的安全性、資料隱私以及市場競爭公平性仍有極大疑慮。目前尚不清楚川普若正式推動相關政策，將如何處理來自國會的阻力，以及如何定義「比照日本模式」的具體法律規範與監管細節，這些細節仍待後續白宮政策檔案進一步釐清。
此一政策意向若落實，將對全球汽車產業鏈產生深遠影響。對於中國電動車巨頭如比亞迪（BYD）等企業而言，這可能提供了一條進入北美市場的潛在路徑，但也意味著必須承擔高昂的在地建廠成本與勞工成本。對於美國汽車產業而言，這可能引發產業結構的重新洗牌，一方面可能引入競爭壓力，另一方面若能成功吸引外資設廠，則有助於強化美國本土的製造實力。此外，此舉亦可能引發國際貿易夥伴對於市場准入公平性的重新審視，特別是針對電動車補貼與技術轉移的相關規範。
然而，各界對於川普的說法存在不同解讀。部分觀察家認為，這僅是川普在選戰或政策辯論中，針對「美國優先」原則所提出的談判籌碼，不應過度解讀為即將對中國車企全面開放。此外，汽車產業專家提醒，即便中國車企有意願在美設廠，仍需面對極為嚴格的監管審查，包括供應鏈透明度要求及潛在的國安評估，這些技術性限制並非單憑總統的一句表態即可輕易跨越。因此，產業分析師建議各界應持續觀察美國商務部及相關監管機構的後續行政動作，而非僅依據公開訪談內容進行投資決策。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於幾個關鍵層面：首先是美國國會對此議題的反應，特別是針對中國車企在美設廠是否會觸發更嚴格的國家安全審查機制；其次是中國車企對於此類條件的實質回應，是否願意放棄部分供應鏈優勢以換取美國市場准入；最後則是美國政府是否會針對外資車企的勞工聘用比例、技術在地化程度制定具體的法規標準。這些發展將決定這項政策意向是僅止於口頭表態，還是會成為未來美中汽車貿易與產業合作的新常態。
