  # Five EU Nations Join Forces to Promote Trade Protection Policies in Response to Global Unfair Competition Challenges
  
  > Five EU nations have jointly urged the European Union to adopt stricter trade measures to guard against unfair trade practices. Meanwhile, the EU and Beijing have reached an agreement to limit Chinese automobile imports, sparking widespread discussion on the balance between trade protection policies and industrial competitiveness.
  
  Trade defense mechanisms within the European Union have recently become a focal point of attention. According to reports from the *Financial Times* and the *South China Morning Post* cited by the *World Journal*, five countries—Spain, France, Italy, the Netherlands, and Lithuania—have jointly drafted a document explicitly requesting the European Commission to adopt tougher trade defense measures to protect domestic European industries from the increasingly severe impact of "unfair trade practices." This move reflects the high level of anxiety among major European economies regarding shifts in the global trade environment and an attempt to use collective action to push the EU to adjust its current trade policy framework to cope with competitive pressures from external markets.
  
  According to information from multiple media outlets, the EU has already made progress in its trade policy actions. As reported by *Euronews*, the EU and Beijing reached a key agreement last Friday aimed at limiting the import of Chinese-manufactured automobiles into the EU market. This agreement is seen as a major breakthrough for the EU in addressing trade imbalances with China. At the same time, observations from the *Associated Press* regarding the European Commission note that Commission President Ursula von der Leyen emphasized in the State of the Union address that Europe is facing numerous severe challenges, ranging from the development of artificial intelligence technology to wildfires caused by climate change, and that adjustments to trade policy are only one part of the EU's overall response to global changes.
  
  However, there are significant differences in how various sources interpret the impact of these policies. Regarding the specific content of the joint document from the five nations, complete details and an implementation timeline have not yet been disclosed; it is only known that the goal is to strengthen trade protection. Regarding the automobile import agreement reached between the EU and Beijing, *Euronews* specifically mentioned that while this breakthrough has eased some trade frictions, it has also sparked concerns that it might reduce the pressure on the EU to pursue deeper structural reforms, thereby affecting the long-term competitiveness of European industry in the global market. Furthermore, the European Commission's balancing act between trade protectionism and market openness remains a point of contention, with no unified policy consensus yet formed.
  
  This wave of trade policy adjustments is having a direct impact on European industry and society. For the automotive manufacturing sector, import restrictions may protect the market share of domestic European carmakers in the short term and alleviate profit pressures caused by price competition; however, this could also lead to risks such as reduced choices or higher prices for consumers. For policymakers, striking a balance between protecting domestic industry and maintaining the stability of global supply chains has become a significant challenge. Additionally, this trend shows that the EU is attempting to deeply integrate trade policy with industrial policy to ensure that European companies do not lose key advantages during the green and digital transitions due to external competition.
  
  Regarding the aforementioned policies, different perspectives and limiting factors are worth considering. Some economists warn that excessive trade protection measures could trigger retaliatory actions from trade partners, thereby affecting the interests of Europe's export-oriented industries. Furthermore, the economic structures within the EU vary greatly; countries with higher degrees of trade freedom, such as the Netherlands, and countries inclined toward protectionism, such as France, do not share the same level of acceptance for trade restrictions. Therefore, the influence of the five-nation joint document should not be overstated; whether it can ultimately be transformed into a comprehensive EU policy still depends on subsequent intensive negotiations and consultations between the European Commission and member states, a process fraught with uncertainty.
  
  Future observations will focus on how the European Commission responds to the demands of these five nations and the effectiveness of the automobile trade agreement with China. The market will closely watch whether the EU will initiate similar protective investigations or restrictive measures for other industries. Moreover, as global trade protectionism rises, how the EU maintains its image as a defender of the global free trade system while effectively safeguarding its own economic security will be a core issue for EU policy development in the coming months. Various sectors will also continue to track the transparency of relevant trade negotiations and the actual impact of these policies on European consumer prices and the speed of industrial transformation.

# 歐盟五國聯手推動貿易保護政策，應對全球市場不公平競爭挑戰

> 歐盟五國聯合敦促歐盟採取更嚴格貿易措施，以防範不公平貿易行為。同時，歐盟與北京達成限制中國汽車進口協議，引發市場對貿易保護政策與產業競爭力平衡的廣泛討論。


歐盟內部的貿易防禦機制近期成為各界關注焦點。根據《世界日報》引述《金融時報》與《南華早報》的報導，西班牙、法國、義大利、荷蘭與立陶宛五國共同起草一份聯合檔案，明確要求歐盟執委會採取更強硬的貿易防禦手段，以保護歐洲本土工業免受日益嚴峻的「不公平貿易行為」衝擊。此舉反映出歐洲主要經濟體對於全球貿易環境變動的高度焦慮，並試圖藉由集體行動，推動歐盟調整現行的貿易政策框架，以因應來自外部市場的競爭壓力。
綜合多家媒體資訊，歐盟在貿易政策上的行動已有所進展。根據《Euronews》報導，歐盟與北京於上週五達成一項關鍵協議，旨在限制中國製造的汽車進口至歐盟市場。此項協議被視為歐盟在處理對中貿易失衡問題上的重大突破。與此同時，《美聯社》針對歐盟執委會的觀察指出，執委會主席馮德萊恩（Ursula von der Leyen）在歐盟國情諮文中強調，歐洲正面臨從人工智慧技術發展到氣候變遷引發的野火等諸多嚴峻挑戰，貿易政策的調整僅是歐盟整體應對全球變局的一環。
然而，各來源對於政策影響的解讀存在顯著差異。關於五國聯合檔案的具體內容，目前尚未揭露完整的細節與實施時間表，僅知其目標在於強化貿易保護。而針對歐盟與北京達成的汽車進口協議，《Euronews》特別提到，此項突破性進展雖緩解了部分貿易摩擦，但也引發了外界擔憂，認為這可能導致歐盟內部對於推動更深層次結構性改革的壓力減輕，進而影響歐洲工業在全球市場的長期競爭力。此外，歐盟執委會對於貿易保護主義與市場開放之間的拿捏，仍是各界爭論的焦點，尚未形成統一的政策共識。
此波貿易政策調整對歐洲產業與社會產生直接影響。對於汽車製造業而言，限制進口措施短期內可保護歐洲本土車廠的市佔率，並減緩因價格競爭帶來的利潤壓力；然而，這也可能導致消費者面臨選擇減少或價格上漲的風險。對政策制定者而言，如何在保護本土工業與維持全球供應鏈穩定之間取得平衡，成為極大的挑戰。此外，這項趨勢也顯示歐盟正試圖將貿易政策與產業政策深度結合，以確保在綠色轉型與數位轉型過程中，歐洲企業不會因外部競爭而喪失關鍵優勢。
針對上述政策，不同觀點與限制因素值得深思。部分經濟學家提醒，過度的貿易保護措施可能引發貿易夥伴的報復行動，進而影響歐洲出口導向型產業的利益。此外，歐盟內部的經濟體結構差異巨大，如荷蘭等貿易自由度較高的國家，與法國等傾向保護主義的國家，對於貿易限制的接受度並不一致。因此，不應過度解讀五國聯合檔案的影響力，其最終能否轉化為歐盟的全體政策，仍取決於歐盟執委會與各會員國之間後續的密集談判與協商，過程充滿不確定性。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於歐盟執委會如何回應這五國的訴求，以及該項對中汽車貿易協議的執行成效。市場將密切關注歐盟是否會針對更多產業啟動類似的保護性調查或限制措施。此外，隨著全球貿易保護主義抬頭，歐盟如何維持其作為全球自由貿易體系捍衛者的形象，同時又能有效保障自身經濟安全，將是未來數月歐盟政策發展的核心議題。各界亦將持續追蹤相關貿易談判的透明度，以及各項政策對歐洲消費者物價與產業轉型速度的實際影響。
