根據多方報導，知名演員安德魯·加菲爾德（Andrew Garfield）對熱門實境節目《背叛者》（The Traitors）展現出極大熱情，並表達了參與該節目的意願。這部以信任與欺騙為核心的節目，吸引了包括加菲爾德在內的眾多觀眾。

然而，加菲爾德對參與節目設下了一個明確的條件。資料顯示，他渴望以「忠誠者」（Faithful）的身份加入，而非扮演需要進行欺騙的「背叛者」（Traitor）。他坦言，如果被迫擔任「背叛者」角色，他將會「失去理智」。

在接受Digital Spy採訪時，加菲爾德進一步闡述了他的擔憂，指出該節目的高壓性質以及過去參賽者對其難度的描述，讓他認為自己缺乏扮演欺騙性角色所需的「毅力」。其他報導也指出，儘管他對節目充滿熱情，但對於扮演背叛者的心理挑戰感到卻步。

《背叛者》節目以其「不信任任何人」的核心理念聞名，參賽者必須在忠誠與背叛之間做出選擇，並試圖揭露隱藏的背叛者。加菲爾德的立場突顯了即使是經驗豐富的演員，也可能對這種高強度的心理遊戲感到壓力。

## English edition

Renowned actor Andrew Garfield has reportedly expressed a keen interest in joining the popular reality competition series "The Traitors." The show, centered around themes of trust and deception, has captivated a wide audience, including Garfield himself, according to various reports.

However, Garfield has articulated a specific condition for his participation. Reports indicate that while he is eager to be part of the show, he would prefer to play as a "Faithful" rather than taking on the deceptive role of a "Traitor." He candidly stated that the latter role would make him "lose his mind."

In an interview with Digital Spy, Garfield elaborated on his concerns, citing the demanding nature of the show and accounts from past contestants regarding its difficulty. He admitted that he believes he lacks the "grit and..." required for the deceptive role. Other sources, including CinemaBlend and Yahoo, also highlighted his strong desire to participate but his apprehension about the psychological demands of being a Traitor.

"The Traitors" is known for its core premise of "trust no one," where contestants must navigate a complex game of loyalty and betrayal, attempting to identify hidden traitors among them. Garfield's stance underscores the intense psychological pressure the game can exert, even on seasoned performers.
