印度與西印度群島代表隊展開五場制T20I板球系列賽，並迎來第二場對決。根據體育媒體報導，在此次T20I系列賽之前，兩隊已完成單日國際賽（ODI）賽程，最終由印度隊以2比1的戰績勝出。

針對第二場T20I的收視管道，相關報導指出，在印度地區的電視轉播由Star Sports電視網負責。此外，未訂閱該付費頻道的觀眾，亦可透過免費電視頻道DD Sports（DD Free Dish）收看賽事實況轉播。

各家體育媒體在賽前陸續整理並發布了開賽時間、預計先發陣容以及各國電視與線上串流的轉播細節，提供關注兩隊競爭的球迷參考。

## English edition

India and the West Indies are continuing their contest in the shortest cricket format with the second match of a five-match T20I series. According to sports media reports, this series directly follows the ODI leg of the tour, which India won 2-1.

Broadcast details for the second T20I indicate that the match will be telecast live on Star Sports Network channels in India. Additionally, reports note that fans without a subscription to the network can access the live telecast for free via DD Sports on DD Free Dish.

Ahead of the fixture, multiple outlets have released information regarding start times, expected lineups, and international streaming and TV options for audiences following the clash between the two sides.
