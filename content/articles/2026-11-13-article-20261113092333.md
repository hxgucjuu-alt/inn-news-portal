  # Transnational Communication Competitions and Interstellar Scientific Research: A Dual Dialectic of Technological Innovation and Wormhole Theory
  
  > The 25th Mobileheroes communication competition, hosted by the Ministry of Economic Affairs, has concluded with a Singaporean team taking the top prize; meanwhile, the scientific community has raised new questions regarding the wormhole theory depicted in the film "Interstellar," exploring the feasibility of interstellar travel.
  
  The 25th "Mobileheroes" competition finals and awards ceremony concluded successfully on October 28, 2026, at the Taipei Fubon International Convention Center. Hosted by the Industrial Development Administration of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the event focused on the deep integration of next-generation communication technologies and artificial intelligence (AI), aiming to discover top global talent and promote industry-academia collaboration.
  
  Simultaneously, there have been new developments in the theoretical foundations of interstellar space exploration within the global scientific community. In particular, regarding the "wormhole" theory popularized by the science fiction film "Interstellar," recent research suggests that in actual physical operation, it may not be a shortcut to distant galaxies. Such astrophysical topics, combined with innovations in communication technology, constitute the current global landscape for exploring interstellar space and future technology.
  
  According to various media reports, the Mobileheroes competition has become a significant indicator for the global communications industry. The international gold medal was awarded to the Singaporean team SynaXG for their "SynaSpark Rover" project, which demonstrated powerful application potential in next-generation communication environments. The silver and bronze medals were awarded to Taiwan's IOE team and Austria's phine.tech, respectively. Additionally, in the communication antenna system design competition, the "Animal Crossing Islanders" team stood out with their "NTN Satellite Communication Antenna" technology.
  
  These winning projects not only represent technological breakthroughs in the field of communications but also embody how AI plays a key role in hardware infrastructure such as satellite communications and antenna design, injecting innovative momentum into the global communications industry.
  
  However, in the field of interstellar science, theories regarding space travel are facing challenges. A recent research report points out that the depiction of wormholes in the film "Interstellar" as "shortcuts" connecting distant space-time may be physically flawed. Researchers analyzed that if wormholes do exist, their operational mechanisms might result in travel times longer than traditional direct flight paths. This view differs significantly from the public's perception of wormholes in popular culture. Furthermore, discussions on interstellar exploration have extended to the entertainment industry; the recent appearance of the "Interstellar" cast at an event in London has once again sparked public attention regarding the film's scientific accuracy, highlighting the significant cognitive gap that still exists between popular culture and rigorous scientific research.
  
  These developments in technology and theory have had a profound impact on global industries and policies. The results of the communication competition show that Taiwan is actively using policy guidance to strengthen technical connections with international teams, particularly in non-terrestrial networks (NTN) and AI communication integration, where it has already achieved global competitiveness. On the other hand, the scientific revision of wormhole theory serves as a reminder to the aerospace industry and relevant research institutions that when planning future interstellar exploration missions, they must return to rigorous physical calculations rather than relying excessively on concepts from science fiction works. These developments show that whether it is the upgrading of terrestrial communication infrastructure or the re-examination of deep-space physical laws, both are important cornerstones for humanity's move toward an interstellar civilization.
  
  Although the communication competition demonstrated excellent technical strength, there are still differing views on the boundaries between technical implementation and theoretical application. Some experts believe that the innovative solutions in the communication competition are mostly at the application level and are still some distance away from large-scale commercialization; while the research on wormholes challenges existing scientific imagination, it is currently still in the stage of theoretical deduction and lacks observational data to support its existence or path length. Therefore, when facing such news, the public and investors should distinguish the essential differences between "technical applications" and "theoretical hypotheses" to avoid conflating science fiction plots with real scientific progress, so as not to misunderstand the actual progress of interstellar exploration.
  
  Looking to the future, the evolution of communication technology will continue to move toward the integration of AI and next-generation communications, which is expected to drive the next wave of growth in the satellite communication and Internet of Things (IoT) industries. At the level of scientific research, with the advancement of observational technology, human understanding of extreme celestial bodies such as wormholes and black holes will become more precise. Subsequent observation should focus on how these communication innovations can be practically integrated into global satellite networks, and whether the physics community can provide more convincing empirical data on wormhole theory. These two parallel trajectories will jointly determine the heights that humanity can reach in terms of terrestrial communication infrastructure and interstellar exploration over the next decade.

# 跨國通訊競賽與星際科學研究：技術創新與蟲洞理論的雙重辯證

> 經濟部舉辦第 25 屆 Mobileheroes 通訊大賽落幕，新加坡團隊奪冠；與此同時，科學界針對電影《星際效應》中的蟲洞理論提出最新質疑，探討星際航行之可行性。


第 25 屆「Mobileheroes 通訊大賽」決賽暨頒獎典禮於 2026 年 10 月 28 日在臺北富邦國際會議中心圓滿落幕。本屆賽事由經濟部產業發展署主辦，核心議題聚焦於次世代通訊技術與人工智慧（AI）的深度整合，旨在發掘全球頂尖人才並推動產學合作。
與此同時，全球科學界針對星際空間探索的理論基礎亦有新進展，特別是針對科幻電影《星際效應》（Interstellar）中廣為人知的「蟲洞」理論，近期有研究指出其在實際物理運作中，可能並非通往遙遠星系的快捷方式，此類天文物理議題與通訊技術的革新，共同構成了當前全球對於星際空間與未來科技的探索版圖。
根據多方媒體報導，Mobileheroes 通訊大賽已成為全球通訊產業的重要指標。本屆國際賽金獎由新加坡團隊 SynaXG 以「SynaSpark Rover」專案奪得，該方案展現了在次世代通訊環境下的強大應用潛力。銀獎與銅獎則分別由臺灣的 IOE 團隊及奧地利的 phine.tech 獲得。此外，通訊天線系統設計競賽中，由「動森島民」團隊以「NTN 衛星通訊天線」相關技術脫穎而出。
這些獲獎專案不僅代表了通訊領域的技術突破，更體現了 AI 如何在衛星通訊、天線設計等硬體基礎設施中發揮關鍵作用，為全球通訊產業注入創新動能。
然而，在星際科學領域，關於空間旅行的理論卻面臨挑戰。近期一份研究報告指出，電影《星際效應》中將蟲洞描繪為連線遙遠時空的「捷徑」，在物理學層面上可能存在誤區。研究人員分析認為，若蟲洞確實存在，其運作機制可能導致航行時間長於傳統的直線航行路徑。此觀點與大眾文化中對蟲洞的認知存在顯著差異。此外，關於星際探索的討論亦延伸至娛樂產業，近期《星際效應》主演群於倫敦出席活動，再度引發公眾對於該片科學真實性的關注，顯示出流行文化與嚴謹科學研究之間，仍存在巨大的認知落差。
這些技術與理論的發展，對全球相關產業與政策產生了深遠影響。通訊大賽的成果顯示，臺灣正積極透過政策引導，強化與國際團隊的技術連結，特別是在非地面網路（NTN）與 AI 通訊整合方面，已具備全球競爭力。另一方面，關於蟲洞理論的科學修正，則提醒了航太產業與相關研究機構，在規劃未來星際探索任務時，必須回歸嚴謹的物理計算，而非過度依賴科幻作品中的概念。這些發展顯示，無論是地面通訊基礎建設的升級，或是對深空物理定律的重新審視，皆是人類邁向星際文明的重要基石。
儘管通訊大賽展現了卓越的技術實力，但各界對於技術落地與理論應用的界線仍有不同見解。部分專家認為，通訊競賽中的創新方案多偏向應用層面，距離大規模商業化仍有距離；而關於蟲洞的研究，雖挑戰了既有的科學想像，但目前仍處於理論推演階段，尚未有觀測資料支援其存在或路徑長度。因此，公眾與投資者在面對相關新聞時，應區分「技術應用」與「理論假設」的本質差異，避免將科幻電影情節與現實科學進展混為一談，以免對星際探索的實際進度產生誤解。
展望未來，通訊技術的演進將持續朝向 AI 與次世代通訊融合的方向發展，預計將帶動衛星通訊與物聯網產業的下一波成長。而在科學研究層面，隨著觀測技術的進步，人類對於蟲洞、黑洞等極端天體的理解將更為精確。後續觀察重點應放在這些通訊創新方案如何實際整合進全球衛星網路，以及物理學界是否能針對蟲洞理論提出更具說服力的實證資料。這兩條平行發展的軌跡，將共同決定人類在未來十年內，於地球通訊基礎建設與星際探索領域所能達到的高度。
