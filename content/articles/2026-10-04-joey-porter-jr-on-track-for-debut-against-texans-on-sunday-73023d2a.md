達拉斯牛仔隊新簽下的角衛小喬伊·波特（Joey Porter Jr.）有望在即將到來的週日對陣休士頓德州人隊的第四週比賽中首次亮相。多個體育媒體，包括Blogging The Boys和Yahoo Sports，均報導指出波特正朝著上場的方向發展，預計將在比賽中登場。

根據達拉斯牛仔隊官方網站的報導，球隊老闆兼總經理傑瑞·瓊斯（Jerry Jones）和教練布萊恩·肖滕海默（Brian Schottenheimer）更新了小波特的狀態。他們表示，小波特似乎正朝著在週日比賽中上場的正確方向發展。這項消息為球迷帶來了期待，因為這位新角衛的加入將為牛仔隊的防守陣容注入新力量。

然而，肖滕海默教練也指出，小波特需要在週五的訓練中「連續第二天」表現良好，球隊才會對他的上場感到完全放心。儘管如此，目前的資料顯示，小波特極有可能在週日對陣德州人隊的比賽中完成他在牛仔隊的首次亮相。

## English edition

Newly acquired Dallas Cowboys cornerback Joey Porter Jr. is reportedly on track to make his debut in the upcoming Week 4 matchup against the Houston Texans on Sunday. Multiple sports outlets, including Blogging The Boys and Yahoo Sports, have indicated that Porter is trending in the right direction and is expected to play in the game.

According to reports from the Dallas Cowboys' official website, owner/general manager Jerry Jones and coach Brian Schottenheimer provided an update on Porter Jr.'s status. They stated that Porter appears to be moving in the right direction to play in Sunday's game. This news has generated anticipation among fans, as the addition of the new cornerback is expected to bolster the Cowboys' defensive lineup.

However, Coach Schottenheimer also noted that Porter would need to "stack a second day" in practice on Friday for the team to feel completely comfortable with his participation. Despite this condition, current information suggests that Porter Jr. is highly likely to make his debut for the Cowboys against the Texans on Sunday.
