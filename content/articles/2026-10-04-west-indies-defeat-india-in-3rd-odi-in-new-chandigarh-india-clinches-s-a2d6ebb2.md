2026年10月3日，西印度群島在新昌迪加爾舉行的第三場單日國際板球賽（ODI）中，以5個三柱門的優勢擊敗了印度。儘管西印度群島贏得了這場比賽，印度隊仍以2-1的總比分贏得了整個系列賽。

報導指出，西印度群島隊在沙伊·霍普（Shai Hope）的帶領下，成功追逐了352分的目標，霍普個人貢獻了162分未出局的精彩表現。資料顯示，新昌迪加爾的比賽條件對追逐分數的隊伍非常有利，這也是西印度群島在本系列賽中唯一一次獲得追逐機會。

印度隊在贏得第三次擲硬幣後選擇先發球，挑戰自我。比賽前10個回合，印度隊以28分損失2個三柱門開局。然而，隨後的比賽共產生了675分，平均每回合7.64分，顯示了比賽的激烈程度。原始新聞標題提及羅希特·沙瑪（Rohit Sharma）的得分，但具體分數未在佐證資料中詳述。

## English edition

On October 3, 2026, West Indies defeated India by 5 wickets in the third One Day International (ODI) cricket match held in New Chandigarh. Despite this victory for the West Indies, India ultimately secured the series win with a 2-1 overall score.

Reports indicate that the West Indies successfully chased a target of 352 runs, largely thanks to Shai Hope's exceptional unbeaten knock of 162. Data suggests that the conditions in New Chandigarh were particularly favorable for chasing, marking the only instance in the series where West Indies had the opportunity to bat second.

India, after winning their third toss of the series, opted to bat first, seemingly challenging themselves. The initial 10 overs of their innings saw them score 28 runs for the loss of two wickets. However, the remainder of the match proved to be high-scoring, yielding a total of 675 runs at an average of 7.64 runs per over. While the original news title mentioned Rohit Sharma's score, specific details of his performance were not provided in the corroborating sources.
