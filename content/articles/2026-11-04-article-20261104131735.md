  # US Military Equipment Demand Crowds Out Commercial Sales Channels; NCSIST Shifts Focus in Low-Cost Cruise Missile Development
  
  > Due to the impact of the US-Iran conflict, the US Department of Defense has designated the "Barracuda-500" as US military equipment, hindering Taiwan's planned commercial procurement. The National Chung-Shan Institute of Science and Technology (NCSIST) has shifted to negotiating with global startups, aiming to maintain the 18-month production timeline.
  
  Taiwan's National Chung-Shan Institute of Science and Technology (NCSIST) had originally planned to introduce the "Barracuda-500," a low-cost air-launched cruise missile developed by the US defense unicorn Anduril Industries, through commercial sales channels, with intentions to establish a supply chain in Taiwan. This collaboration was publicly unveiled at last year's defense exhibition and was seen as key to enhancing Taiwan's asymmetric warfare capabilities.
  
  However, following the outbreak of the US-Iran conflict, the US Department of Defense, citing combat readiness requirements, has included several low-cost weapons, including the "Barracuda-500," on the US military's priority equipment list. This has caused significant uncertainty for the commercial procurement plan, forcing adjustments to the originally scheduled supply chain development.
  
  According to military and government sources, although the "Barracuda-500" cannot be acquired through the original commercial sales channel, NCSIST has not abandoned its goal of domesticating or introducing low-cost cruise missiles. NCSIST has adjusted its external cooperation strategy and is actively seeking other potential startups globally for negotiations, with the "Singing" series of weapon systems being considered as a potential alternative. Relevant sources emphasized that NCSIST's internal planning for a "target product manufacturing within 18 months" remains unchanged, indicating that the institute is attempting to mitigate the risk of supply chain disruption caused by US military demand by adjusting its supply chain strategy and expanding its international cooperation partners.
  
  Current information indicates that the cooperation model between NCSIST and Anduril Industries has faced substantial obstacles due to US policy adjustments, though details regarding the introduction of the "Singing" system and the specific progress of negotiations remain to be verified. Furthermore, there is no clear official statement on whether the US military's decision to designate the "Barracuda-500" as equipment will have a ripple effect on other international military procurement cases that are already signed or under negotiation. Military experts point out that the resource crowding-out effect caused by international geopolitical conflicts has become a normalized challenge that Taiwan must face when promoting national defense autonomy and international military procurement. Whether NCSIST can successfully transition to new partners in a short period will be a key point for future observation.
  
  This incident has a profound impact on Taiwan's defense industry. First, in terms of building asymmetric combat capabilities, the mass production progress of low-cost cruise missiles is directly linked to defense resilience. If technology transfer or production authorization cannot be successfully obtained, Taiwan will be forced to increase its investment in domestic independent research and development. Second, regarding defense procurement policy, this case highlights the vulnerability of relying on a single foreign supplier or a specific commercial sales model. With the surge in global military demand, the stability of international supply chains has become a critical variable in national security, and future defense procurement strategies may need to be more flexible and incorporate more diverse supply sources to diversify risks.
  
  There are differing views regarding this development. Some commentators believe that NCSIST's shift to negotiating with other manufacturers demonstrates an improvement in its contingency capabilities, moving away from being limited to traditional procurement models. However, others caution that introducing new systems that have not been combat-proven or have different levels of integration with the US military system could increase the complexity of logistics maintenance and tactical integration. Furthermore, the "18-month manufacturing" goal should not be over-interpreted, as there are multiple constraints between negotiation, technology transfer, and actual mass production, such as technical specification adjustments, production line certification, and quality control. Relying solely on a timeline as the only metric for success may overlook the professional thresholds of military industrial development.
  
  Future observation will focus on whether NCSIST can officially announce cooperation agreements with new manufacturers in the near term and whether the "Singing" system can truly meet Taiwan's defense needs. Additionally, the development of the US-Iran conflict will continue to influence US defense export policies. How Taiwan balances the US military's priority rights to weapons and equipment with the fulfillment of its own defense needs will test the negotiation skills and strategic foresight of relevant units. Whether NCSIST will simultaneously adjust the international linkage layout of the entire defense technology industry while promoting the low-cost cruise missile project is also worth tracking.

# 美軍裝備需求排擠商售管道，中科院低成本巡弋飛彈研發轉向

> 受美伊戰爭影響，美國國防部將「梭魚500」列為美軍裝備，導致臺灣商售引進計畫受阻。中科院已轉向與全球新創廠商洽談，目標維持18個月內完成生產的期程。


臺灣國家中山科學研究院（中科院）原定透過商售管道，引進美國國防獨角獸企業「安杜裡爾工業」（Anduril Industries）研發的「梭魚500」（Barracuda-500）低成本空射巡弋飛彈，並計畫在臺建立供應鏈。此項合作曾於去年國防展中公開亮相，被視為提升臺灣不對稱戰力之關鍵。
然而，隨著美伊戰爭爆發，美國國防部基於戰備需求，已將包括「梭魚500」在內的多款低成本武器列入美軍優先裝備清單，導致該項商售引進計畫面臨重大變數，原定的供應鏈建置進度被迫調整。
根據軍政人士透露，儘管「梭魚500」無法透過原定商售管道取得，中科院目前並未放棄低成本巡弋飛彈的國產化或引進目標。中科院已調整對外合作策略，積極在全球範圍內尋求其他具備潛力的新創廠商進行洽談，其中「聖歌」（Singing）系列武器系統被視為潛在的替代方案。相關人士強調，中科院內部對於「18個月內製造出目標產品」的期程規劃並未改變，顯示該院正透過調整供應鏈策略與擴大國際合作物件，試圖緩解因美軍需求排擠所帶來的供應鏈斷鏈風險。
目前資訊顯示，中科院與安杜裡爾工業的合作模式已因美方政策調整而出現實質阻礙，但關於「聖歌」系統的引進細節與具體談判進度，各界說法仍待進一步核實。此外，美軍將「梭魚500」列為裝備的決策，是否會對其他已簽約或洽談中的國際軍事採購案造成連帶影響，目前尚無明確官方說法。軍事專家指出，國際地緣政治衝突導致的資源排擠效應，已成為臺灣在推動國防自主與國際軍購時必須面對的常態化挑戰，中科院能否在短時間內順利轉換合作夥伴，將是後續觀察重點。
此事件對臺灣國防產業的影響深遠。首先，在不對稱戰力建設方面，低成本巡弋飛彈的量產進度直接關聯到防衛韌性。若無法順利取得技術轉移或生產授權，將迫使臺灣必須加大對國內自主研發的投入。其次，在國防採購政策上，此案例凸顯了依賴單一外國供應商或特定商售模式的脆弱性。隨著全球軍事需求激增，國際供應鏈的穩定性已成為國家安全的重要變數，未來國防採購策略恐需更具彈性，並納入更多元的供應來源以分散風險。
針對此項發展，外界觀點存在分歧。部分評論認為，中科院轉向洽談其他廠商顯示其應變能力提升，不再侷限於傳統採購模式；然而，亦有觀點提醒，引進未經實戰驗證或與美軍體系整合度不同的新系統，可能增加後勤維護與戰術整合的複雜度。此外，不應過度解讀「18個月內製造」的目標，因為從洽談、技術轉移到實際量產，中間仍存在技術規格調整、生產線認證及品質控管等多重限制，若單純以時間表作為衡量成敗的唯一指標，恐忽略了軍事工業發展的專業門檻。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於中科院是否能於近期內正式公佈與新廠商的合作協議，以及「聖歌」系統是否確實能符合臺灣國防需求。此外，美伊戰爭的戰況發展將持續影響美國國防出口政策，臺灣如何平衡美軍對武器裝備的優先排程權，與自身國防需求的滿足，將考驗相關單位的談判能力與戰略預判。中科院在推動低成本巡弋飛彈計畫的同時，是否會同步調整整體國防科技產業的國際鏈結佈局，亦值得持續追蹤。
