  # The Global AI Development Debate: Jensen Huang and Mark Zuckerberg Clash on Security and Market Mechanisms
  
  > Regarding artificial intelligence development and security issues, NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang and Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg have recently shared their insights, emphasizing that AI security should be addressed through computational defense and external evaluation mechanisms rather than blindly slowing down research and development.
  
  The rapid evolution of artificial intelligence (AI) technology has recently sparked intense global discussion within the tech community regarding security controls and the pace of research and development. With the widespread deployment of large language models at the application level, balancing innovative development with potential cybersecurity threats has become a core focus for corporate leaders and policymakers.
  
  NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang, speaking at Goldman Sachs' annual global technology conference, offered a different interpretation of the recent industry panic surrounding model "jailbreaking" and hacker threats. Meanwhile, Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg expressed his views on social media, opposing the idea of pausing R&D to address risks and instead advocating for the establishment of an independent external evaluation system.
  
  According to reports from various technology media outlets, although Huang and Zuckerberg addressed different aspects, both remain skeptical about "slowing down AI R&D." Huang pointed out that the current industry panic over cybersecurity jailbreaks is largely driven by the commercial psychology of the cybersecurity industry, which creates market demand by sensationalizing threats. He believes that cybersecurity defense is essentially an arms race of computing power, and that AI Agents will become the most powerful defensive shield in the future.
  
  Zuckerberg further emphasized that the key to AI security lies in "Model Alignment," which he views as a survival threshold for commercial competition. If a product is not secure enough, it will naturally be eliminated by market mechanisms; therefore, he argues for promoting an independent external evaluation ecosystem rather than resorting to passive braking measures.
  
  Regarding information consistency, both tech giants agree that AI security should be addressed through technical means and market competition rather than relying solely on administrative intervention or stagnant R&D. However, their arguments differ in focus: Huang leans toward technical optimism, viewing "computing power as a defensive tool," while also highlighting the marketing tactics of the cybersecurity industry. Zuckerberg focuses on "corporate governance and external auditing," advocating for building trust through third-party assessments.
  
  Furthermore, regarding whether AI will replace human labor, there are signs of market correction. For instance, it was recently reported that Apple, considering the warmth and professionalism of human customer service in handling complex issues, has indefinitely shelved its original layoff plan to replace 5,000 AppleCare customer service representatives with AI agents.
  
  These industry dynamics have had a significant impact on global technology policy and the labor market. Particularly in Europe, as the EU continues to push the AI Act and strengthen regulation of digital services, how companies can utilize the AI defense technologies proposed by Huang or implement the external evaluation mechanisms advocated by Zuckerberg while remaining compliant will become a critical issue. The case of Apple shelving its AI customer service replacement plan also reflects that in the European market, which places high value on consumer rights and service quality, companies must weigh the balance between technical efficiency and human emotional value when implementing AI automation.
  
  However, discussions regarding AI security still face limitations and uncertainties. While the "independent external evaluation" proposed by Zuckerberg has theoretical value for building trust, there is currently no global consensus on how to define "independence" or the uniformity of evaluation standards. Although Huang’s attribution of cybersecurity panic to industry psychology highlights certain commercial operations, over-interpreting his remarks could lead to underestimating the social security risks that technical vulnerabilities might pose in practical applications. Therefore, observers suggest that the statements of these industry leaders should be viewed as references for technological development paths rather than the sole solution for AI risk management.
  
  Future observations will focus on three key points: First, whether companies can effectively defend against cybersecurity threats when promoting AI Agent applications, as Huang expects; second, whether tech giants like Meta can implement credible external evaluation mechanisms that gain regulatory approval; and finally, whether the adjustments made by large enterprises like Apple regarding AI and human resource allocation will trigger other tech companies to follow suit, thereby affecting the global tech industry's employment structure and service models.

# 全球 AI 發展論戰：黃仁勳與祖克柏對安全與市場機制的觀點交鋒

> 針對人工智慧發展與安全議題，輝達執行長黃仁勳與 Meta 執行長祖克柏近期分別提出見解，強調 AI 安全應透過算力防護與外部評估機制解決，而非盲目放緩研發。


人工智慧（AI）技術的快速演進，近期在全球科技界引發了關於安全控管與研發節奏的激烈討論。隨著大型語言模型在應用層面的廣泛部署，如何平衡創新發展與潛在的資安威脅，已成為企業領袖與政策制定者關注的核心。
輝達（NVIDIA）執行長黃仁勳於高盛年度全球科技大會上，針對近期業界對於模型「越獄」與駭客威脅的恐慌情緒，提出了不同的解讀；與此同時，Meta 執行長馬克·祖克柏（Mark Zuckerberg）則在社群平臺發表觀點，反對透過暫停研發來應對風險，主張應建立獨立外部評估體系。
根據多家科技媒體的報導，黃仁勳與祖克柏雖針對不同面向發言，但兩者皆對「放緩 AI 研發」持保留態度。黃仁勳指出，目前業界對於資安越獄的恐慌，很大程度上是由資安產業的商業心理學所驅動，即透過渲染威脅來創造市場需求。他認為，資安攻防本質上是算力軍備競賽，而 AI Agent（人工智慧代理）將成為未來最強大的防護盾。
祖克柏則進一步強調，AI 安全的關鍵在於「模型對齊」（Model Alignment），這已是商業競爭的生存死線，若產品不夠安全，自然會被市場機制淘汰，因此應推動獨立的外部評估生態系，而非採取消極的煞車手段。
在資訊一致性方面，上述兩位科技巨頭皆同意 AI 的安全性應透過技術手段與市場競爭來解決，而非單純仰賴行政幹預或研發停滯。然而，兩者的論述重點存在差異：黃仁勳側重於「算力作為防禦手段」的技術樂觀主義，並點出資安產業的行銷操作；祖克柏則聚焦於「企業治理與外部審計」，主張透過第三方評估來建立信任。
此外，關於 AI 取代人力與否，市場亦出現修正跡象，如蘋果公司近期傳出因考量真人客服在複雜問題處理上的溫度與專業，已無限期擱置原定透過 AI 代理取代 5,000 名 AppleCare 客服的裁員計畫。
這些產業動態對全球科技政策與勞動力市場產生了顯著影響。特別是在歐洲地區，隨著歐盟持續推動 AI 法案（AI Act）並加強對數位服務的監管，企業如何在符合法規的前提下，運用黃仁勳所提的 AI 防護技術，或落實祖克柏主張的外部評估機制，將成為關鍵課題。蘋果擱置 AI 取代客服計畫的案例，亦反映出在高度重視消費者權益與服務品質的歐洲市場，企業在匯入 AI 自動化時，必須權衡技術效率與人類情感價值之間的平衡。
然而，對於 AI 安全的討論仍存在限制與不確定性。祖克柏所提的「獨立外部評估」雖具備理論上的信任價值，但如何定義「獨立性」以及評估標準的統一性，目前尚無全球共識。黃仁勳將資安恐慌歸因於產業心理學，雖點出部分商業操作，但若過度解讀其言論，恐會輕忽技術漏洞在實際應用中可能造成的社會安全風險。因此，觀察家建議，應將這些產業領袖的發言視為技術發展路線的參考，而非對 AI 風險控管的唯一解方。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三點：首先，企業在推動 AI Agent 應用時，是否能如黃仁勳預期般有效抵禦資安威脅；其次，Meta 等科技巨頭是否能落實具公信力的外部評估機制，並獲得監管機構認可；最後，蘋果等大型企業在人工智慧與人力資源配置上的調整，是否會引發其他科技業者效仿，進而影響全球科技產業的就業結構與服務模式。
