  # Canada’s Trade Policy Shift: Prime Minister Carney Seeks EU Alliance to Counter US-Canada Trade War
  
  > Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has recently adopted a dual-track diplomatic and economic strategy to address the severe challenges posed by the US-Canada trade war. The Carney administration is accelerating efforts to forge a transatlantic alliance with Europe, attempting to reduce Canada's over-reliance on the US market—a move described by observers as a "shotgun wedding" style diplomatic pivot.
  
  Canada is currently facing the most severe bilateral trade war in its modern history, as the once-close economic and trade relationship between the US and Canada undergoes intense volatility. According to the Economic Daily News, citing the Wall Street Journal, Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has clearly shifted the nation's economic and trade strategic focus toward Europe, attempting to mitigate the impact of US protectionist policies on domestic Canadian industries by establishing a closer transatlantic alliance. This policy shift is not merely a trade negotiation, but a significant geopolitical adjustment taken by Canada to ensure national economic security after facing restricted access to the US market.
  
  The Carney administration is currently engaging in high-level dialogues with EU member states, hoping to reach meaningful economic and trade agreements in a short period to fill the potential trade gap left by the US market.
  
  Based on aggregated public information and international media reports, the escalation of the US-Canada trade war has become one of the most closely watched public issues in North America. According to cross-referenced reports from the Wall Street Journal and the Economic Daily News, the core of the Canadian government's action lies in "reducing reliance"—that is, diversifying trade partners to decrease the direct impact of US market volatility on Canada's manufacturing, energy, and agricultural sectors. Currently, both the US and Canada remain at a stalemate regarding tariffs, trade barriers, and market access, which has prompted the Carney administration to accelerate the process of forming an alliance with Europe.
  
  Although specific details of the agreement have not yet been fully disclosed, the market generally believes that Canada's move is intended to increase its leverage in North American trade negotiations by expanding market integration with Europe, and to ensure that Canadian companies maintain a competitive advantage in the future restructuring of global supply chains.
  
  However, there remains an information gap and uncertainty regarding the effectiveness of this "shotgun wedding" diplomatic strategy. While the Wall Street Journal reported on Canada's intention to pivot toward Europe, details provided by various media outlets are inconsistent regarding whether the agreement has entered a substantive signing phase or remains in the preliminary stage of intent communication. Furthermore, although the official White House website continues to issue various executive orders and trade policy statements, there has been no clear official response regarding Canada's pursuit of an alliance with Europe. This means that whether the Canadian government's strategy can be implemented as smoothly as expected still depends on the attitudes of EU nations and subsequent US trade retaliation measures.
  
  The market currently remains reserved about whether Canada can complete significant economic and trade integration with Europe in a short period.
  
  This shift in trade policy will have profound impacts on North American industrial and social structures. For a long time, the Canadian economy has been highly dependent on the US market, with the two countries' supply chains deeply integrated. Once the bilateral trade war continues to escalate, Canada's domestic energy exports, automotive manufacturing, and agricultural markets will bear the brunt. If the Carney administration succeeds in shifting its focus to Europe, while it may diversify risks, it could also face challenges such as increased logistics costs, differences in regulatory standards, and high difficulty in industrial chain integration. For Canadian companies, this is a survival battle regarding market reshaping; for the US, this move by Canada may further weaken its dominant position in the North American economic and trade system, and could even trigger a fundamental change in the North American free trade framework.
  
  Different perspectives and analyses point out that over-interpreting this move by Canada as a "complete break from the US" may not be accurate. Some economic commentators believe that the Carney administration's strategy is more like a "negotiating chip," intended to pressure the US government into adopting a more flexible attitude in the trade war. Furthermore, the practice of a transatlantic alliance faces many limitations, including protectionist sentiment within the EU and the high costs of intercontinental trade in transportation and logistics. Therefore, there is still great uncertainty as to whether this policy can produce substantial economic benefits in the short term. The public and investors should closely monitor the subsequent progress of bilateral trade negotiations rather than focusing solely on the shift in diplomatic posturing.

These variables will determine the future direction of the North American economic and trade landscape. As global supply chains continue to restructure, Canada's balancing strategy between the two major economies of the US and Europe will become a key indicator of its national economic resilience. # 加拿大貿易政策轉向：總理卡尼尋求歐盟結盟以應對美加貿易戰

> 加拿大總理卡尼近期採取外交與經貿雙軌策略，因應美加貿易戰帶來的嚴峻挑戰。卡尼政府正加速推動與歐洲的跨大西洋結盟，試圖降低加拿大經濟對美國市場的過度依賴，此舉被外界形容為「閃婚」式外交轉向。


加拿大近期面臨現代史上最嚴峻的雙邊貿易戰，美加兩國過去緊密的經貿關係正經歷劇烈動盪。根據《經濟日報》引述《華爾街日報》的報導，加拿大總理卡尼（Mark Carney）已明確將國家經貿戰略重心轉向歐洲，試圖透過建立更緊密的跨大西洋結盟，緩解美國貿易保護主義政策對加拿大國內產業的衝擊。此一政策轉向並非單純的貿易談判，而是加拿大在面臨美國市場准入受限後，為確保國家經濟安全所採取的重大地緣政治調整。
卡尼政府目前正積極與歐盟成員國進行高層對話，希望在短時間內達成具有實質意義的經貿協議，以填補美國市場可能出現的貿易缺口。
綜合各方公開資訊與國際媒體報導，美加貿易戰的升級已成為北美地區最受關注的公共議題之一。根據《華爾街日報》與《經濟日報》的交叉報導，加拿大政府的行動核心在於「降低依賴」，即透過多元化貿易夥伴，減少美國單一市場波動對加拿大製造業、能源業及農業的直接影響。目前，美加雙方在關稅、貿易壁壘及市場准入問題上仍處於僵局，這促使卡尼政府加速推動與歐洲的結盟程式。
雖然目前具體的協議細節尚未完全公開，但市場普遍認為，加拿大此舉旨在透過擴大與歐洲的市場整合，增加在北美貿易談判中的籌碼，並確保加拿大企業在未來全球供應鏈重組中仍保有競爭優勢。
然而，關於此項「閃婚」式外交策略的成效，各界仍存在資訊落差與待確認之處。雖然《華爾街日報》報導了加拿大轉向歐洲的意圖，但針對該協議是否已進入實質簽署階段，或是仍處於初步意向溝通，目前各媒體提供的細節並不一致。此外，美國白宮官方網站雖持續發布各項行政命令與貿易政策說明，但針對加拿大尋求與歐洲結盟的具體回應，尚未有明確的官方宣告。這意味著，加拿大政府的策略是否能如預期般順利推行，仍需視歐盟各國的態度以及美國後續的貿易報復措施而定。
目前市場對於加拿大能否在短時間內完成與歐洲的重大經貿整合，仍持有保留態度。
此一貿易政策的轉向，對北美產業與社會結構將產生深遠影響。長期以來，加拿大經濟高度依賴美國市場，兩國供應鏈深度融合，一旦雙邊貿易戰持續升級，加拿大國內的能源出口、汽車製造業及農產品市場將首當其衝。若卡尼政府成功將重心轉向歐洲，雖能分散風險，但也可能面臨物流成本增加、法規標準差異及產業鏈整合難度高等挑戰。對於加拿大企業而言，這是一場關於市場重塑的生存戰；對於美國而言，加拿大此舉可能進一步削弱其在北美經貿體系中的主導地位，甚至引發北美自由貿易架構的根本性改變。
不同觀點與分析指出，過度解讀加拿大此舉為「徹底與美國切割」可能並不準確。部分經濟評論家認為，卡尼政府的策略更像是一種「談判籌碼」，意在向美國政府施壓，迫使其在貿易戰中採取更彈性的態度。此外，跨大西洋結盟的實踐面臨諸多限制，包括歐盟內部的保護主義情緒，以及跨洲際貿易在運輸與物流上的高昂成本。因此，這項政策能否在短期內產生實質經濟效益，仍存在極大不確定性。公眾與投資人應密切關注雙邊貿易談判的後續進展，而非僅將焦點放在外交姿態的轉變上。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於三個面向：首先，美加雙方是否能在近期重啟高層貿易談判，並達成暫時性的停火協議；其次，加拿大與歐盟的結盟談判是否能具體化，並提出明確的執行時間表；最後，美國政府對於加拿大轉向歐洲的反應，特別是是否會採取進一步的貿易制裁手段。這些變數將決定北美經貿格局的未來走向。隨著全球供應鏈持續重組，加拿大在美歐兩大經濟體之間的平衡策略，將成為檢驗其國家經濟韌性的關鍵指標。INN新聞將持續追蹤相關政策動態，並為讀者提供即時的分析與報導。
