  # Rising Global Geopolitical Risks: Disruptions to Energy Supply Chains and Cross-Border Security Challenges
  
  > Recent global geopolitical and economic instability, coupled with threats to maritime security in the Middle East, has disrupted energy supplies, leading to long-term uncertainty for international oil prices and natural gas exports. Meanwhile, the Chinese Communist Party’s (CCP) acts of transnational repression have drawn significant attention from the U.S. and Europe, highlighting legal and national security challenges for Taiwan.
  
  Geopolitical risks have recently emerged in multiple regions worldwide, severely impacting energy supplies and cross-border security. In the energy sector, the deterioration of maritime security in the Middle East—marked by frequent attacks on oil tankers in the waters north of Qatar and harassment by the Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps in the Strait of Hormuz, including drone surveillance and blockade threats—has posed a serious threat to global crude oil and liquefied natural gas (LNG) transportation. Simultaneously, hurricanes hitting the U.S. Gulf Coast have led to the suspension of energy facilities and the evacuation of personnel, further exacerbating the strain on global energy supplies.
  
  In the realm of security, the CCP’s transnational repression has alerted the international community. The case of Chinese-American Zhang Wanying, who was allegedly directed to surveil the family of Lai Ching-te’s son, has prompted the U.S. Congress to push for the "Transnational Repression Policy Act," aiming to strengthen countermeasures through more severe criminal penalties.
  
  According to various media reports, the global energy supply chain is showing clear signs of disruption. JKO Asset Management noted that the combined impact of Middle East shipping attacks and U.S. weather factors has kept upward pressure on international oil prices. Data from the U.S. Energy Information Administration shows that U.S. crude oil inventories fell by 3.2 million barrels in the week ending October 2. Regarding natural gas, market analysts indicate that although Qatari LNG tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz saw a brief recovery in September, the high level of shipping security risks has forced most cargo to adopt "dark shipping" modes, making overall flows highly susceptible to geopolitical conflict.
  
  Expectations regarding energy recovery vary across sources. Some analyses suggest that if transit risks in the Strait of Hormuz persist, global LNG export volumes may struggle to recover in the short term, potentially delaying a return to pre-conflict levels until late 2027 or late 2028. On the issue of transnational repression, the U.S. currently prosecutes such acts under the Foreign Agents Registration Act (FARA) and 18 U.S. Code § 951, while the new bill proposed by Congress suggests increasing prison sentences to a maximum of 10 years and imposing heavy fines.
  
  In Taiwan, despite the existence of the Anti-Infiltration Act and the National Security Act, the lack of "dedicated legislation" for transnational repression remains a legal loophole, making it difficult for law enforcement agencies to directly apply existing provisions to acts such as political surveillance and intimidation.
  
  These geopolitical risks have a direct impact on the global economy and regional security. The instability of energy supplies directly drives up logistics costs and market price volatility, posing a challenge to global inflationary pressures. In the technology sector, supply chain positioning is also receiving high attention. For instance, AMD CEO Lisa Su recently visited Seoul not only to secure HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) capacity to address AI chip shortages but also to engage in in-depth discussions with Samsung Electronics regarding foundry cooperation. This demonstrates that in a complex international environment, tech giants are actively strengthening supply chain resilience and competitiveness through cross-border alliances.
  
  However, there remain many limitations and uncertainties regarding legal countermeasures against transnational repression and the evolution of geopolitical conflicts. Experts warn against over-interpreting the impact of single events, noting that the identification and evidence collection of transnational repression are highly complex in legal practice. Countries must strike a balance between national security and legal due process when promoting legislative amendments. Similarly, the recovery of the energy market depends heavily on the subsequent development of the Middle East conflict; any timeline predictions for supply recovery are subject to significant variables, requiring industry players and policymakers to remain highly vigilant.
  
  Future observations will focus on three aspects: First, the legislative progress of the U.S. Congress regarding the "Transnational Repression Policy Act," and whether the Taiwan government will initiate amendments or draft specific legislation concerning transnational repression. Second, whether the maritime security situation in the Strait of Hormuz will further deteriorate, and whether Qatari LNG export volumes can be improved through alternative routes or diplomatic coordination in the coming years. Finally, the progress of cooperation between tech giants like AMD and Samsung in HBM and foundry services will be a key indicator of whether the global AI chip supply chain can effectively diversify geopolitical risks.

# 全球地緣風險升溫：能源供應鏈受阻與跨境安全挑戰

> 近期全球地緣政治與經濟情勢動盪，中東航運安全威脅導致能源供應受阻，國際油價與天然氣出口面臨長期不確定性。同時，中共跨境鎮壓行為引發美歐高度關注，臺灣亦面臨相關國安法治挑戰。


近期全球多地傳出地緣政治風險，嚴重影響能源供應與跨境安全。在能源領域，中東地區航運安全域性勢惡化，卡達北部海域油輪遭襲事件頻傳，伊朗革命衛隊在荷姆茲海峽的騷擾行為，包括無人機監控與封鎖威脅，已對全球原油與液化天然氣（LNG）運輸構成嚴重威脅。與此同時，美國墨西哥灣地區遭遇颶風襲擊，導致能源設施停產與人員撤離，進一步加劇全球能源供應的緊張態勢。
在安全領域，中共跨境鎮壓事件引起國際社會警覺，美籍華裔人士張婉瑩涉嫌受指使跟監賴清德兒子一家，促使美國國會推動「停止跨境鎮壓法草案」，意圖透過更嚴厲的刑責強化反制。
根據多方媒體報導，全球能源供應鏈已呈現明顯受阻跡象。街口投信指出，受中東航運攻擊與美國天候因素疊加影響，國際油價上漲壓力持續增溫。美國能源資訊署資料顯示，截至10月2日當週，美國原油庫存大幅減少320萬桶。在天然氣方面，市場機構分析顯示，儘管9月卡達LNG運輸船經荷姆茲海峽的通行量有短暫回升，但由於航運安全風險居高不下，多數貨物仍被迫採取「暗航」模式，導致整體流量極易受地緣衝突幹擾。
關於能源恢復的預期，各來源間存在不同評估。部分分析指出，若荷姆茲海峽的通行風險持續，全球LNG出口量恐難以在短期內恢復，甚至可能要延宕至2027年底或2028年底，才能回到衝突爆發前的正常水準。在跨境鎮壓議題上，美國現行已透過《外國代理人登記法》（FARA）及《美國法典》第18編第951條進行追訴，而國會提出的新草案則建議將刑期提升至最高10年，並處以高額罰鍰。
臺灣方面，儘管現行有《反滲透法》與《國安法》，但針對跨境鎮壓的「專屬立法」仍是當前法制上的缺口，導致針對政治跟監與恐嚇等行為，執法單位往往難以直接適用現有條文。
上述地緣風險對全球經濟與區域安全產生直接影響。能源供應的不穩定直接推升物流成本與市場價格波動，對全球通膨壓力構成挑戰。在科技產業方面，供應鏈佈局亦受到高度關注，如AMD執行長蘇姿豐近期親赴首爾，不僅是為了爭取HBM（高頻寬記憶體）產能以解決AI晶片缺貨問題，更進一步與三星電子深入探討晶圓代工合作，顯示在複雜的國際情勢下，科技巨頭正積極透過跨國結盟來強化供應鏈韌性與競爭力。
然而，針對跨境鎮壓的法律反制與地緣政治衝突的演變，仍存在諸多限制與不確定性。專家提醒，不應過度解讀單一事件的影響，跨境鎮壓的認定與證據蒐集在法律實務上具有高度複雜性，各國在推動修法時，必須在國家安全與法律程序正義之間取得平衡。同樣地，能源市場的復甦高度取決於中東衝突的後續發展，任何關於供應恢復的時程預測，均存在極大的變數，相關產業與政策制定者需保持高度警覺。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個面向：首先，美國國會針對「停止跨境鎮壓法草案」的立法進度，以及臺灣政府是否會針對跨境鎮壓行為啟動相關法規的修訂或專法研擬；其次，中東地區荷姆茲海峽的航運安全域性勢是否會進一步惡化，以及卡達LNG出口量能否在未來數年內透過替代路徑或外交協調獲得改善；最後，AMD與三星等科技大廠在HBM與晶圓代工領域的合作進展，將是觀察全球AI晶片供應鏈是否能有效分散地緣風險的重要指標。
