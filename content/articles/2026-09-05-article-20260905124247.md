  # Taiwan-US Deepen AI Cooperation Alongside Local International Exchange, Driving New Patterns in Global Industry and Tourism
  
  > On September 4, 2026, Taiwan and the U.S. jointly declared the promotion of an AI revolution at a semiconductor exhibition. Simultaneously, the Nantou County Government convened its International Affairs Committee, focusing on integrating local tourism and cultural resources to actively expand its international exchange layout.
  
  On September 4, 2026, significant progress occurred simultaneously in the fields of global technology and local governance. In the technology sector, the annual landmark event "SEMICON TAIWAN 2026" officially concluded today. The exhibition served not only as a technical exchange platform for the global semiconductor industry but also as a critical arena for geopolitics and technology diplomacy. At the same time, Taiwan's local governments were actively deploying international exchange strategies on the same day, with the Nantou County Government convening the "2026 International and Cross-Strait Affairs Promotion Committee," aiming to enhance the visibility of the region in the global tourism and cultural market through cross-domain resource integration.
  
  These events reflect that Taiwan is simultaneously seeking depth and breadth in aligning with international standards, both in national-level technology strategy and local-level regional governance.
  
  Regarding progress in the technology industry, Eric Frater, Acting Deputy Director of the American Institute in Taiwan (AIT), attended the "Deliberation on Society and Technology (DSET)" forum during SEMICON TAIWAN 2026. According to comprehensive media reports, Frater clearly stated that Taiwan and the U.S. are jointly promoting an AI revolution, demonstrating the close cooperative relationship between the two countries in the semiconductor supply chain and artificial intelligence research and development. This cooperation goes beyond the technical level and is viewed as a key strategic layout to consolidate democratic supply chains and technological security.
  
  Experts at the forum generally believe that Taiwan's advantages in semiconductor manufacturing, combined with the U.S.'s leading position in AI applications and software development, will become the core driving force for the future development of the global technology industry.
  
  Regarding the internationalization process of local governance, the Nantou County Government held a committee meeting on the same day, chaired by Deputy Magistrate Wang Ruei-te, to deliberate on future directions for international and cross-strait exchanges. According to the meeting minutes, the Nantou County Government focused on aspects such as international festivals, traveler marketing, cultural tourism, and specialty industries. Various departments, including the Department of Civil Affairs, the Department of Agriculture, and the Tourism Department, provided business reports for the "Social Exchange Group," "Economic and Industrial Group," "Tourism and Cultural Education Group," and "Administrative Support Group." Notably, specific proposals discussed included increasing international participation in the Nantou Lantern Festival, promoting small-town travel across 13 townships and cities, and establishing a mechanism for sharing the results of international exchanges.
  
  This move indicates that the local government is attempting to systematically integrate fragmented tourism resources to meet the demand of international travelers for in-depth local experiences in the post-pandemic era.
  
  The aforementioned events have had a direct impact on relevant regions and industries. In the technology sector, the deepening of Taiwan-US cooperation is expected to accelerate the R&D and mass production of artificial intelligence chips, thereby influencing the global supply chain layout of the electronics industry and providing a positive boost to the international competitiveness of Taiwan's semiconductor industry chain. In terms of tourism and regional development, the strategic adjustments of the Nantou County Government are directly linked to the transformation of Taiwan's local revitalization and tourism economy. By combining cultural characteristics with international marketing, it is expected to attract more international tourists to travel deep into central and southern Taiwan, alleviate tourism pressure overly concentrated in urban areas, and drive the export and international visibility of local specialty industries.
  
  However, an objective perspective must be maintained regarding these developments. Regarding Taiwan-US AI cooperation, although the intention for cooperation is clear, the specific details of technology transfer, talent cultivation programs, and the implementation progress of relevant policies still depend on the subsequent execution by the administrative departments of both sides and should not be over-interpreted as immediate, comprehensive technology sharing. Regarding the promotion of local tourism, although the Nantou County Government has clear goals for resource integration, challenges remain in execution, such as overcoming international promotion budget constraints, improving the friendliness of local infrastructure for international travelers, and balancing the promotion of international and cross-strait affairs within the complex political atmosphere of cross-strait relations.
  
  Furthermore, global climate change and sustainable development issues are also considered important considerations for future international exchanges, which coincides with the "local action" and "responsibility" emphasized by United Nations COP youth representatives.
  
  Subsequent observation should focus on two major aspects: first, whether the cooperation between Taiwan and the U.S. in the AI field can be transformed from forum declarations into the establishment of specific R&D centers or joint laboratory projects, and its impact on the retention of talent in Taiwan's semiconductor industry; second, whether the Nantou County Government's international exchange strategy can be effectively converted into actual international tourist arrival data, and whether this model can serve as a reference example for other counties and cities in Taiwan to promote the integration of internationalization and local tourism.
  
  In addition, with the continued advancement of regional development plans such as the "Second Five-Year Plan for Economic and Social Development of the Macao Special Administrative Region (2026-2030)," policy trends in surrounding regions may also indirectly affect the strategic space of Taiwan's local governments in cross-strait and international exchanges.

# 臺美深化AI合作與地方國際交流並進，推動全球產業與觀光新格局

> 2026年9月4日，臺美雙方於半導體展共同宣示推動AI革命，同時南投縣政府召開國際事務委員會，聚焦整合在地觀光與文化資源，積極拓展國際交流佈局。


2026年9月4日，全球科技與地方治理領域同步出現重要進展。在科技產業方面，年度指標性盛會「SEMICON TAIWAN 2026國際半導體展」於今日正式落幕，該展覽不僅是全球半導體產業的技術交流平臺，更成為地緣政治與科技外交的關鍵場域。與此同時，臺灣地方政府亦在同一日積極部署國際交流策略，南投縣政府召開「一一五年國際及兩岸事務推動委員會」，旨在透過跨域資源整合，提升地方在全球觀光與文化市場的能見度。
這些事件反映了臺灣在國家層級的科技戰略與地方層級的區域治理上，正同步尋求與國際接軌的深度與廣度。
在科技產業的進展上，美國在臺協會（AIT）代理副處長馮海博（Eric Frater）於SEMICON TAIWAN 2026期間出席「科技、民主與社會研究中心（DSET）」論壇。綜合媒體報導，馮海博明確指出，臺美雙方正共同推動AI革命，顯示兩國在半導體供應鏈及人工智慧研發領域的緊密合作關係。此項合作不僅止於技術層面，更被視為鞏固民主供應鏈與科技安全的重要戰略佈局。
與會專家普遍認為，臺灣在半導體製造的優勢，結合美國在AI應用與軟體開發的領先地位，將成為未來全球科技產業發展的核心驅動力。
關於地方治理的國際化程式，南投縣政府於同日由副縣長王瑞德主持召開委員會，針對未來國際及兩岸交流方向進行研議。根據會議紀錄，南投縣府聚焦於國際節慶、旅客行銷、文化觀光及特色產業等面向。各局處如民政處、農業處、觀光處等，針對「社會交流組」、「經濟產業組」、「觀光文教組」及「行政支援組」進行業務報告。值得注意的是，會中具體提案包含提升南投燈會的國際參與度、推動13鄉鎮市的小鎮旅行，以及建立國際交流成果分享機制。
此舉顯示地方政府正試圖將分散的觀光資源進行系統性整合，以應對後疫情時代國際旅客對深度在地體驗的需求。
上述事件對相關地區與產業產生了直接影響。在科技產業，臺美合作的深化預期將加速人工智慧晶片的研發與量產，進而影響全球電子產業的供應鏈佈局，對臺灣半導體產業鏈的國際競爭力具有正面助益。而在觀光與區域發展方面，南投縣政府的策略調整，直接關聯到臺灣地方創生與觀光經濟的轉型。透過將文化特色與國際行銷結合，預期能吸引更多國際觀光客深入臺灣中南部地區，減緩過度集中於都會區的觀光壓力，並帶動地方特色產業的出口與國際能見度。
然而，針對上述發展仍需保持客觀視角。在臺美AI合作方面，儘管雙方宣示合作意向明確，但具體的技術轉移細節、人才培育計畫以及相關政策的落實進度，仍需視後續雙方行政部門的執行力而定，不應過度解讀為立即性的全面技術共享。在地方觀光推動上，南投縣政府雖有明確的資源整合目標，但如何克服國際宣傳預算限制、提升地方基礎設施對國際旅客的友善度，以及如何在兩岸關係複雜的政治氛圍下，平衡國際與兩岸事務的推動，均是未來執行面臨的挑戰。
此外，全球氣候變遷與永續發展議題，亦被視為未來國際交流的重要考量，這與聯合國COP青年代表所強調的「在地行動」與「責任」不謀而合。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於兩大面向：首先是臺美在AI領域的合作是否能從論壇宣示轉化為具體的研發中心設立或聯合實驗室計畫，以及對臺灣半導體產業人才留任的影響；其次，南投縣政府的國際交流策略是否能有效轉化為實際的國際旅客入境資料，以及該模式是否能成為臺灣其他縣市推動國際化與在地觀光整合的參考範例。
此外，隨著《澳門特別行政區經濟和社會發展第三個五年規劃（2026-2030年）》等區域發展規劃的持續推進，周邊區域的政策動向亦可能間接影響臺灣地方政府在兩岸與國際交流上的戰略空間。
