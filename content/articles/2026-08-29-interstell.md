  # A New Milestone for Japan's Aerospace Industry: Interstellar Selected by JAXA to Lead Satellite Formation Technology
  
  > Japanese space infrastructure company Interstellar Technologies has been selected to join the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA) "Space Strategy Fund" program. The company will lead the research and development of high-precision satellite formation flight technology, while simultaneously advancing IMAP missions and interstellar comet observations, highlighting cross-border and interdisciplinary progress in the global space science sector.
  
  The Japanese space infrastructure industry has recently achieved a significant breakthrough. According to multiple public sources, the Japan-based space technology company Interstellar Technologies (hereinafter referred to as "Interstellar") has been officially selected to participate in the "Space Strategy Fund" program under the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA).
  
  The core of this collaboration lies in advancing "High Accuracy Satellite Formation Flight Technology," marking a shift where the Japanese government is actively integrating private commercial aerospace capabilities to strengthen space infrastructure and vertically integrated rocket-satellite services. The launch of this project aims to accelerate technical innovation in Japan's on-orbit satellite constellations through commissioned research agreements and to enhance its competitiveness in the global space industry chain.
  
  According to official releases from Interstellar and reports from Access Hub, Interstellar has formally signed a commissioned research agreement with JAXA. The primary goal of the project is to develop high-precision satellite formation flight technology, a critical capability for the future deployment of large-scale satellite constellations. Through the precise coordination and synchronization of multiple satellites in orbit, it is possible to not only improve the resolution of observational data but also optimize the operational efficiency of space infrastructure.
  
  As a company dedicated to vertically integrated rocket and satellite services, Interstellar’s core R&D projects include the orbital launch vehicle "ZERO." Its selection for JAXA's strategic fund program is viewed as a major milestone in the company's efforts to increase technical maturity and commercialization.

According to official information from NASA and JAXA, international scientific exploration of interstellar space and comets is also ongoing. For example, NASA’s "Interstellar Mapping and Acceleration Probe" (IMAP) mission is dedicated to mapping the boundary of the heliosphere, while the European Space Agency’s (ESA) XMM-Newton X-ray observatory conducted a 20-hour observation of the interstellar comet "3I/ATLAS" in December 2025.
  
  In current public information, reports have yet to provide detailed quantitative data regarding Interstellar's specific technical indicators, expected completion timelines, or funding allocation ratios within the JAXA program; relevant details await further official disclosure.

For Japan, supporting private enterprises through the "Space Strategy Fund" helps lower the barrier to entry for the space sector and builds a more resilient space industry ecosystem. For the global space market, breakthroughs in high-precision satellite formation technology will directly impact the quality of future meteorological observations, Earth resource monitoring, and deep-space communications.
  
  Furthermore, if Interstellar's technical integration capabilities can complement NASA's IMAP mission or ESA's deep-space observation data, it will help humanity gain a deeper understanding of the interactions between the heliosphere and interstellar space, thereby expanding the scope of space infrastructure from low Earth orbit to more distant interstellar exploration.

# 日本航太產業新里程：Interstellar獲JAXA青睞主導衛星編隊技術

> 日本太空基礎設施企業Interstellar Technologies獲選加入JAXA「太空戰略基金」計畫，將主導高精度衛星編隊飛行技術研發，並同步推進IMAP任務與星際彗星觀測，展現全球太空科學領域的跨國與跨領域進展。


日本太空基礎設施產業近期迎來重大進展。根據多項公開資訊顯示，總部位於日本的太空科技公司Interstellar Technologies（以下簡稱Interstellar），已正式獲選參與日本宇宙航空研究開發機構（JAXA）旗下的「太空戰略基金」（Space Strategy Fund）計畫。
此項合作的核心在於推動「高精度衛星編隊飛行技術」（High Accuracy Satellite Formation Flight Technology），標誌著日本政府在強化太空基礎設施與垂直整合火箭衛星服務方面，正積極與民間商業航太力量深度結合。此項計畫的啟動，旨在透過委託研究協議，加速日本在軌衛星群的技術創新，並提升其在全球太空產業鏈中的競爭力。
綜合Interstellar官方發布與Access Hub的報導，Interstellar已正式與JAXA簽署委託研究協議。該計畫的核心目標在於研發高精度衛星編隊飛行技術，這是一項對於未來大規模衛星群部署至關重要的關鍵技術。透過多顆衛星在軌道的精密協調與同步，不僅能提升觀測資料的解析度，還能最佳化太空基礎設施的運作效率。
Interstellar作為一家致力於垂直整合火箭與衛星服務的企業，其核心研發專案包括軌道運載火箭「ZERO」，此次獲選進入JAXA的戰略基金計畫，被視為該公司在提升技術成熟度與商業化程式上的重大里程碑。
儘管各界對於此項合作寄予厚望，但針對技術細節與執行進度，目前來源間仍存在資訊落差。根據NASA與JAXA的官方資訊，與此同時，國際間對於星際空間與彗星的科學探索亦在持續進行中。例如，NASA的「星際測繪與加速探測器」（IMAP）任務正致力於測繪太陽圈邊界，而歐洲太空總署（ESA）的XMM-牛頓X射線天文臺，則於2025年12月針對星際彗星「3I/ATLAS」進行了長達20小時的觀測。
目前公開資訊中，對於Interstellar在JAXA計畫中的具體技術指標、預計完成時間點以及資金分配比例，各方報導尚未提供詳盡的量化資料，相關細節仍待後續官方進一步披露。
此項政策與技術合作對日本乃至全球太空產業具有深遠影響。對日本而言，透過「太空戰略基金」支援民間企業，有助於降低太空產業的進入門檻，並建立更具韌性的太空產業生態系。對於全球太空市場而言，高精度衛星編隊技術的突破，將直接影響未來氣象觀測、地球資源監控以及深空通訊的品質。
此外，Interstellar的技術整合能力，若能與NASA的IMAP任務或ESA的深空觀測資料形成互補，將有助於人類更深入理解太陽圈與星際空間的互動作用，進而擴大太空基礎設施的應用範疇，從近地軌道延伸至更遙遠的星際探索領域。
然而，學界與產業界專家提醒，儘管衛星編隊技術前景看好，但該技術在極端太空環境下的穩定性與通訊延遲問題，仍是目前面臨的技術挑戰。部分觀點認為，雖然政府資金挹注能加速研發，但過度依賴單一戰略基金可能導致資源分配不均，且商業化應用與基礎科學研究之間的平衡，仍需更長期的觀察。各界不應過度解讀單一合作案對整體太空產業的立即性顛覆，因為太空技術的研發週期長、風險高，從實驗室階段走向實際軌道部署，仍需經歷嚴苛的環境測試與多次飛行驗證，方能確保其任務可靠性。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於Interstellar與JAXA合作協議的執行進度，特別是該公司如何將「ZERO」火箭的運載能力與衛星編隊技術進行整合。此外，隨著全球對於星際物體（如3I/ATLAS）的關注度提升，未來這類高精度衛星編隊技術是否會被應用於深空探測，或是針對近地小行星的防禦監控，將是觀察太空政策走向的關鍵指標。公眾與投資人應持續關注JAXA發布的季度報告，以獲取關於該計畫技術突破、預算執行以及未來衛星群部署規劃的更完整資訊。
