北韓於2026年10月3日進行了一場軍事演習，向日本海方向發射了多枚飛彈。根據Sputnikglobe.com的報導，北韓領導人金正恩的胞妹、勞動黨中央委員會部部長金與正證實了此次發射，並指出這些是中程戰略飛彈。

此次發射活動引發了區域關注。資料顯示，雖然北韓方面透過金與正聲稱發射的是中程戰略飛彈，但南韓和日本的報導則將其描述為彈道飛彈或不明飛行物。路透社報導指出，南韓和日本表示北韓於週六向其東海岸海域發射了一枚彈道飛彈。

這次發射是北韓今年以來最新的一次飛彈活動。儘管佐證資料中提及了其他日期（如2026年1月5日和8月12日）的飛彈發射事件，但本次演習的具體細節與2026年10月3日的報導相符，顯示了平壤持續進行武器測試的趨勢。

## English edition

North Korea conducted a military exercise on October 3, 2026, involving the launch of multiple missiles towards the Sea of Japan. According to Sputnikglobe.com, Kim Yo Jong, sister of North Korean leader Kim Jong Un and a department director at the Central Committee of the Workers’ Party of Korea, confirmed the launch, stating they were medium-range strategic missiles.

The launch has drawn regional attention. While North Korea, through Kim Yo Jong, claimed to have launched medium-range strategic missiles, reports from South Korea and Japan described the projectiles as ballistic missiles or an unidentified projectile. Reuters reported that South Korea and Japan stated North Korea fired a ballistic missile toward the sea off its east coast on Saturday.

This launch marks the latest missile activity by North Korea this year. Although the provided corroborating data mentions other missile launch events on different dates, such as January 5 and August 12, 2026, the specific details of this exercise align with the October 3, 2026 reports, indicating Pyongyang's ongoing trend of weapons testing.
