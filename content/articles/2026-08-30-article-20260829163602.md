  # US-Canada Trade War Escalates: US Imposes 50% Tariffs on Canada, Ottawa Launches Retaliatory Measures
  
  > Trade negotiations between the United States and Canada have collapsed. The US has imposed a 50% tariff on $20 billion worth of Canadian goods, and the Canadian Prime Minister immediately announced equivalent countermeasures, marking a formal escalation in the trade conflict between the two nations.
  
  As the world's largest trading partners, the United States and Canada have seen their bilateral relations grow tense following the failure of trade agreement negotiations. The situation stems from the parties' inability to finalize a trade deal within the agreed-upon timeframe, leading the US to impose punitive tariffs of up to 50% on certain Canadian goods that do not enjoy preferential treatment under the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA). This move not only broke the deadlock at the negotiating table but also triggered severe volatility in North American supply chains and cross-border trade. It is reported that before the tariffs were officially implemented, both sides held intensive consultations in an attempt to reach a consensus at the last minute, yet they ultimately failed to prevent the rise of trade barriers.
  
  According to facts confirmed by multiple media sources, the US government officially implemented a 50% tariff on approximately $20 billion worth of Canadian goods starting on the 22nd of this month. Faced with the US's hardline stance, Canadian Prime Minister Carney responded quickly, explicitly announcing the suspension of bilateral trade negotiations and simultaneously launching equivalent countermeasures, imposing retaliatory tariffs of equal value on imported American goods. This series of actions marks the formal shift of the US-Canada trade dispute from the negotiating table to substantive trade retaliation. Both sides have adopted escalatory measures, turning what was once considered an economic dispute between allies into an open trade conflict.
  
  Regarding the specific details of the negotiation collapse, there is a certain degree of information gap and difference in interpretation among various media reports. Some reports indicated that before the tariffs officially took effect, senior Canadian officials had stated that an agreement was "very close" to being finalized, even revealing that Canadian Minister of Trade Dominic LeBlanc and US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer had held final-stage negotiations in Washington D.C. in an attempt to lower existing tariffs. However, sources such as New Tang Dynasty Television (NTD) focused on the behind-the-scenes scenario of the agreement collapsing rapidly within "72 hours," indicating a major breakdown in communication between the two countries at the final moment.
  
  What remains to be confirmed is which core clause caused the negotiations to collapse at the last minute, and what the specific points of contention were during the process; further official details are still required.
  
  This wave of tariff wars has already had a direct and significant impact on the North American industrial chain. Because the US and Canadian economies are highly integrated, particularly in sectors such as manufacturing, automotive components, and agricultural products, a 50% tariff will undoubtedly significantly increase corporate operating costs. According to business concerns cited by ETtoday, this policy could lead to a survival crisis for small and medium-sized enterprises struggling within the North American free trade zone and could even trigger a large-scale wave of unemployment. For industries reliant on cross-border logistics, this is not just a matter of increased costs, but also involves the risk of supply chain disruption, which, in the long term, will severely weaken the competitiveness of the North American market in the global trade system.
  
  Despite the current tension, perspectives remain divided. Some analysts believe that as long-term strategic allies, neither the US nor Canada wishes to see the trade war expand further, meaning there is still room for maneuver, and a compromise could eventually be reached through diplomatic means. However, this optimistic expectation is limited because the current political atmosphere and protectionist tendencies mean that any compromise must face pressure from domestic public opinion. Experts warn against over-interpreting the current harsh rhetoric from both sides, as in international trade negotiations, public retaliatory measures are often a means to gain leverage and do not necessarily mean that both sides have completely abandoned the path of diplomatic resolution.
  
  The focus for future observation lies in when the two countries will restart negotiations and the actual scope of the retaliatory tariffs. Currently, the market is highly focused on Canada's specific list of targeted American goods and the potential backlash of these measures on the US domestic industry. Furthermore, the subsequent attitude of US President Trump and the political decisions made by the Canadian government under domestic economic pressure will be key to determining the direction of the trade war. The international community will continue to closely monitor whether the US and Canada can resolve the current tense trade situation through a new round of consultations in the short term to avoid deeper negative impacts on the global economy.

# 美加貿易戰升級：美對加徵收50%關稅，加國啟動報復措施

> 美國與加拿大貿易談判破局，美方對價值200億美元的加國商品加徵50%關稅，加拿大總理隨即宣佈採取等額反制，雙方貿易衝突正式升級。


美加兩國作為全球最大的貿易夥伴，近期因貿易協定談判未果，雙邊關係陷入緊張。背景源於雙方未能如期在期限內敲定貿易協議，導致美國針對部分未能享有《美墨加協定》（USMCA）優惠待遇的加拿大商品，祭出高達50%的懲罰性關稅。此舉不僅打破了此前兩國在談判桌上的僵局，更引發了北美供應鏈與邊境貿易的劇烈動盪。據悉，在關稅正式實施前，雙方曾進行多次密集磋商，試圖在最後一刻達成共識，然而最終未能避免貿易壁壘的提升。
根據多個媒體來源共同確認的事實，美國政府於本月22日起，正式對價值約200億美元的加拿大商品實施50%關稅。面對美方的強硬手段，加拿大總理卡尼（Carney）迅速作出回應，明確宣佈暫停雙邊貿易談判，並同步啟動等額反制措施，將對進口的美國商品課徵同等價值的報復性關稅。這一連序列動標誌著美加貿易爭端正式從談判桌轉向實質的貿易報復，雙方皆採取了升級對抗的手段，使得這場原本被視為盟友間的經濟爭議，演變為公開的貿易衝突。
關於談判破局的具體細節，各媒體報導存在一定程度的資訊落差與詮釋差異。部分報導指出，在關稅正式生效前，加拿大高層官員曾對外表示雙方協議已「非常接近」拍板，甚至透露負責對美貿易事務的加國部長多勒布朗（Dominic LeBlanc）與美國貿易代表葛裡爾（Jamieson Greer）在華府進行了最後階段的協商，試圖降低現有關稅。然而，新唐人電視臺等來源則聚焦於協議在「72小時內」迅速破局的幕後情境，顯示兩國在最後關頭的溝通出現了重大斷層。
目前尚待確認的是，究竟是哪項核心條款導致談判在最後時刻崩潰，以及雙方在談判過程中具體的分歧點為何，仍需後續更多官方細節披露。
此波關稅戰對北美產業鏈已產生直接且顯著的衝擊。由於美加經濟高度整合，特別是製造業、汽車零元件及農產品等領域，高達50%的關稅無疑將大幅推升企業營運成本。根據《ETtoday新聞雲》引述的企業界憂慮，這項政策恐將導致北美自由貿易區內苦撐的中小企業面臨生存危機，甚至引發大規模的失業潮。對於依賴跨境物流的產業而言，這不僅是成本增加的問題，更涉及供應鏈中斷的風險，長期來看，將嚴重削弱北美市場在全球貿易體系中的競爭力。
儘管目前局勢緊張，但各界觀點仍存在分歧。部分分析師認為，美加兩國作為長期的戰略盟友，雙方其實都不願見到貿易戰持續擴大，因此仍存在轉圜餘地，最終可能透過外交手段達成妥協方案。然而，這種樂觀預期也受到限制，因為當前的政治氛圍與貿易保護主義傾向，使得任何妥協都必須面對國內輿論的壓力。專家提醒，不應過度解讀雙方目前的強硬言辭，因為在國際貿易談判中，公開的報復措施往往也是爭取談判籌碼的一種手段，並不代表雙方已徹底放棄外交解決的途徑。
後續觀察重點在於兩國何時重啟談判以及報復性關稅的實際執行範圍。目前市場高度關注加拿大針對美國商品的具體清單，以及這些措施對美國國內產業的反作用力。此外，美國總統川普的後續態度，以及加拿大政府在面臨國內經濟壓力下的政治決策，將是決定貿易戰走向的關鍵。國際社會將持續密切關注美加兩國是否能在短期內透過新一輪磋商，緩解目前劍拔弩張的貿易緊張局勢，以避免對全球經濟造成更深層的負面影響。
