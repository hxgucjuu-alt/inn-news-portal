  # Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan Accelerate Digital and Financial Layouts, Central Asia Becomes Strategic Hub for "New Silk Road 2.0"
  
  > Central Asian core nations Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan are actively promoting digital trade and financial cooperation. By deepening the "Belt and Road" Initiative, they are attracting the attention of Hong Kong trade delegations and seeking cross-border upgrades in logistics, innovation, technology, and RMB financing.
  
  The Central Asian region, composed of Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, Kyrgyzstan, and Tajikistan, has long been regarded as a strategic bridge connecting Asia and Europe. According to statistics from the Hong Kong Trade Development Council (HKTDC) Research, the region is rich in natural resources, with a regional Gross Domestic Product (GDP) reaching $468.6 billion in 2023. However, the region's economic growth faces challenges such as geopolitical instability, regional conflicts, and fluctuations in energy commodity prices, with economic growth projected to slow to 5.6% in 2024. Despite macroeconomic uncertainties, Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan are gradually becoming the core drivers of Central Asia's economic transformation, leveraging their demographic dividends and digital ecosystems.
  
  Recently, the strategic status of the Central Asian market has significantly risen, being viewed internationally as a key node of the "New Silk Road 2.0." Hong Kong Chief Executive John Lee led a trade delegation to visit Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan, aiming to deepen bilateral cooperation. According to information from Sing Tao Daily and the Chinese Manufacturers' Association of Hong Kong (CMA), the focus of this visit is to explore trillion-level market opportunities for Hong Kong enterprises in the fields of finance, innovation, technology, and logistics. Kazakhstan is actively promoting digital trade transformation through its advanced "Super App" digital ecosystem, while Uzbekistan, with its vast young population dividend, has become a key development target for cross-border e-commerce and logistics infrastructure construction.
  
  Both countries are currently actively seeking diversified asset allocation and have shown strong interest in RMB trade financing.
  
  Regarding the practical aspects of cross-border e-commerce and logistics, various sources place different emphasis on the details of market development. Analysis from Summit-TD points out that when entering this market, enterprises need to conduct precise assessments of logistics customs clearance timelines and documentation requirements in Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan, while comparing the cost-effectiveness of rail and air freight. However, there is currently a lack of unified execution standards for specific administrative obstacles in customs clearance processes and the subtle differences in national regulations, which remains a major unknown for Hong Kong businesses during their market entry.
  
  In addition, international sources such as Eurasianet and EIN News emphasize that although Central Asia was once considered a geographical periphery, it is re-emerging as a hub for global trade amid the restructuring of global supply chains. However, there are still divergent views on whether this transformation can fully offset the risks brought about by geopolitics.
  
  This wave of strategic layout has had a direct impact on relevant regions and industries. First, in the financial sector, the promotion of RMB trade financing will help reduce exchange rate risks in regional trade and strengthen capital links between Central Asia, mainland China, and Hong Kong. Second, in the logistics industry, with the deepening of cross-border e-commerce layouts, the efficiency of rail and air freight customs clearance will become the key to determining corporate competitiveness. For Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan, introducing Hong Kong's professional services and innovative technologies will help them move away from an economic model reliant on single energy exports toward digitalization and diversification. However, the sources of funding for infrastructure construction and long-term maintenance costs remain major public policy challenges for local governments.
  
  Despite the immense potential of the Central Asian market, analysts and research institutions generally advise against over-interpreting its short-term profit margins. First, geopolitical risk remains the largest variable affecting the economic stability of the region, and any escalation of regional conflict could disrupt trade routes. Second, although the level of market openness in Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan has improved, the transparency of the regulatory environment and administrative efficiency still need improvement. Hong Kong enterprises must consider the degree of alignment between local legal systems and international standards when conducting asset allocation. Furthermore, the promotion of digital trade relies on the penetration of local internet infrastructure, and a digital divide still exists in some remote areas.
  
  Subsequent observation should focus on the implementation progress of specific cooperation agreements following John Lee's visit to Central Asia, particularly the establishment of financial financing platforms and the signing of logistics customs clearance agreements. At the same time, it is necessary to closely monitor the specific policy details of Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan regarding cross-border RMB settlement, as well as how both countries adjust their export-oriented economic strategies in the face of global energy price fluctuations. As the "New Silk Road 2.0" advances, the progress of the five Central Asian countries in regional integration and their ability to maintain balance within the East-West trade system will be important indicators for observing the region's economic development in the coming years.

# 哈薩克與烏茲別克加速數位與金融佈局，中亞成「新絲路2.0」戰略樞紐

> 中亞核心國家哈薩克與烏茲別克正積極推動數位貿易與金融合作，透過深化「一帶一路」倡議，吸引香港商貿代表團關注，並在物流、創科及人民幣融資領域尋求跨境升級。


中亞地區由哈薩克、烏茲別克、土庫曼、吉爾吉斯與塔吉克五國組成，長期以來被視為連線亞洲與歐洲的戰略橋樑。根據香港貿易發展局經貿研究統計，該地區天然資源豐富，2023年地區生產總值（GDP）達到4,686億美元。然而，該地區的經濟成長面臨地緣政局不穩、區域衝突以及能源商品價格波動等挑戰，預計2024年的經濟成長率將放緩至5.6%。儘管存在宏觀經濟的不確定性，哈薩克與烏茲別克兩國憑藉其人口紅利與數位生態系統，正逐漸成為中亞經濟轉型的核心驅動力。
近期，中亞市場的戰略地位顯著提升，被國際視為「新絲綢之路2.0」的關鍵節點。香港行政長官李家超率領商貿代表團訪問哈薩克與烏茲別克，旨在深化雙邊合作。綜合《星島日報》與香港中華廠商聯合會（CHMFIA）的資訊，此次訪問重點在於為香港企業挖掘金融、創科及物流領域的萬億級市場機遇。哈薩克憑藉其先進的「超級App」數位生態，正積極推動數位貿易轉型；而烏茲別克則憑藉龐大的年輕人口紅利，成為跨境電商與物流基礎設施建設的重點開發物件。
兩國目前均積極尋求多元化的資產配置，並對人民幣貿易融資展現出濃厚興趣。
在跨境電商與物流實務層面，各來源對於市場開發的細節存在不同側重。Summit-TD的分析指出，企業進入該市場時，需針對哈薩克與烏茲別克的物流清關時效、檔案要求進行精確評估，並對比鐵路與空運的成本效益。然而，對於清關流程的具體行政障礙與各國法規的細微差異，目前尚缺乏統一的執行標準，這也是港商在佈局時面臨的主要未知數。
此外，Eurasianet與EIN News等國際來源則強調，中亞雖然曾被視為地理上的邊緣地帶，但隨著全球供應鏈重組，該地區正重新成為全球貿易的中樞，但這種轉變是否能完全抵銷地緣政治帶來的風險，各界觀點仍有分歧。
此波戰略佈局對相關地區與產業產生了直接影響。首先，在金融領域，人民幣貿易融資的推廣將有助於降低區域貿易的匯率風險，並強化中亞與中國大陸及香港的資本連結。其次，在物流產業，隨著跨境電商佈局的深入，鐵路與空運物流的清關效率將成為決定企業競爭力的關鍵。對於哈薩克與烏茲別克而言，引入香港的專業服務業與創科技術，有助於其擺脫單一能源出口的經濟模式，轉而走向數位化與多元化。然而，基礎設施建設的資金來源與長期維護成本，仍是當地政府面臨的重大公共政策挑戰。
儘管中亞市場潛力巨大，但分析師與研究機構普遍建議不應過度解讀其短期獲利空間。首先，地緣政治風險依然是影響該地區經濟穩定性的最大變數，任何區域衝突的升級都可能中斷貿易路線。其次，雖然哈薩克與烏茲別克市場開放程度有所提升，但法規環境的透明度與行政效率仍有待改善。香港企業在進行資產配置時，必須考慮到當地法律體系與國際標準的對接程度。此外，數位貿易的推廣依賴於當地網路基礎設施的普及，這在部分偏遠地區仍存在數位落差。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於李家超訪中亞後的具體合作協議落實進度，特別是金融融資平臺的建立與物流清關協議的簽署。同時，需密切關注哈薩克與烏茲別克在人民幣跨境結算上的具體政策細則，以及兩國在面對全球能源價格波動時，如何調整其出口導向的經濟策略。隨著「新絲綢之路2.0」的推進，中亞五國在區域整合上的進展，以及能否在東西方貿易體系中維持平衡，將是未來數年觀察該地區經濟發展的重要指標。
