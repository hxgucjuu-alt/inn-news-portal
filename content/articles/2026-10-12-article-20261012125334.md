  # European Parliament Pushes for Tougher Policy on China: Rising Trade Defense and Geopolitical Risks
  
  > The European Parliament has recently adopted a tougher policy stance toward China, calling for the strengthening of trade defense mechanisms and expressing concern over China's potential support for the Russian military-industrial complex. This move is set to profoundly impact the EU's future external economic, trade, and geopolitical landscape.
  
  The process of European economic integration began in the 1950s with the European Coal and Steel Community and the European Economic Community, aiming to ensure stability and prosperity through regional cooperation. However, with the evolution of globalization and the increasing complexity of geopolitics, EU member states have faced severe economic security challenges in recent years. Recently, the European Parliament has expressed high concern regarding China's trade practices and political influence, demanding that the European Commission adopt more defensive trade policies. The background to this move lies in the concerns of EU officials that China's technology exports, supply of industrial components, and financial dealings may undermine the effectiveness of sanctions imposed by the EU on Russian military production, thereby having a profound negative impact on the situation in Ukraine.
  
  According to reports from various media outlets, the European Parliament has explicitly demanded that the EU adopt a tougher policy toward China. As the EU and China engage in a new round of trade negotiations, European industry associations have also publicly called for the strengthening of trade defense measures against China. Multiple sources have confirmed that the European Parliament is highly skeptical of China's role in the Russia-Ukraine war, believing that the industrial and financial support provided by China directly offsets the pressure of sanctions imposed by Western countries on Russia. Furthermore, industry representative groups within the EU have emphasized that more rigorous procurement reviews and trade protection mechanisms must be implemented to prevent critical supply chains from being manipulated by specific countries, which has become a priority for European public policy and industrial development.
  
  Although there is a high degree of consensus on strengthening trade defense, there are still information gaps and disputes regarding specific implementation. Some media reports indicate that while the European Parliament's attitude toward China policy is trending toward a harder line, it remains to be seen whether the European Commission and individual member states can reach a complete consensus on the details of enforcing sanctions. Additionally, there are subtle differences in assessments among various sources regarding the specific extent of China's impact on the Russian military-industrial complex.
  
  Some analyses suggest that China's technological intervention has caused a substantive threat, but other viewpoints point out that European industries have a high degree of dependence on the Chinese market. How to strike a balance between "de-risking" and "maintaining economic operations" remains a major challenge for EU officials, and relevant data and specific sanction lists have not yet been fully disclosed.
  
  This policy shift has had a direct impact on European industries and the public sector. According to procurement and tender information in Central and Eastern Europe, the European public and private sectors are reassessing the security of their supply chains and seeking more diversified partners to reduce reliance on a single market. This involves not only traditional manufacturing and industrial components but also extends to financial services and high-tech sectors. The tough policy pushed by the European Parliament is expected to force European companies to comply more strictly with EU sanction regulations and review mechanisms when participating in international tenders. For industries in Central and Eastern Europe, this means that the future business environment will face higher compliance thresholds, but it may also drive opportunities for the development of local industries within the region.
  
  Views on the European Parliament's tough stance are divided. Supporters believe this is a necessary means to maintain European strategic autonomy and safeguard security in Ukraine, and that trade defense tools must be used to curb unfair competition and geopolitical threats. However, those who oppose or hold reservations warn that overly aggressive trade defense policies could trigger retaliatory measures from China, causing losses for the EU's export-oriented industries. Furthermore, it must be noted that not all of China's economic activities should be viewed as military support for Russia; a distinction should be made between commercial activities and strategic technology transfers to avoid over-interpretation or the politicization of economic issues, which could lead to further chaos in global supply chains and inflationary pressure.
  
  The focus for subsequent observation lies in how the European Commission will translate the European Parliament's resolutions into specific legal provisions and administrative orders. First, it is necessary to closely monitor whether the EU and China will engage in substantive debates regarding trade defense mechanisms at the upcoming negotiating table. Second, it is important to observe whether European member states will adjust their regulation of investment and technology exports to China in response to this policy. Finally, the actual impact of China on Russian military-industrial production should be continuously monitored, as well as whether the EU will implement more targeted sanctions against specific companies. These dynamics will determine the trajectory of EU-China economic and trade relations in the coming years and have ripple effects on global public health and economic stability.

# 歐洲議會推動對中強硬政策：貿易防禦與地緣政治風險升溫

> 歐洲議會近期針對中國採取更強硬政策立場，呼籲強化貿易防禦機制，並關注中國對俄羅斯軍工產業的潛在支援，此舉將深刻影響歐盟未來的對外經貿與地緣政治佈局。


歐洲的經濟整合程式始於 1950 年代的歐洲煤鋼共同體與歐洲經濟共同體，旨在透過區域合作確保穩定與繁榮。然而，隨著全球化演變與地緣政治複雜化，歐盟成員國近年面臨嚴峻的經濟安全挑戰。近期，歐洲議會針對中國的貿易行為與政治影響力表達高度關注，並要求歐盟執行委員會採取更具防禦性的貿易政策。此舉背景在於，歐盟官員擔憂中國的技術輸出、工業零元件供應以及金融往來，可能削弱歐盟對俄羅斯軍事生產實施的制裁效果，進而對烏克蘭局勢產生深遠的負面影響。
綜合多家媒體報導，歐洲議會已明確要求歐盟採取更強硬的對中政策。在歐盟與中國進行新一輪貿易談判之際，歐洲行業協會亦公開呼籲，必須強化針對中國的貿易防禦手段。多方來源共同確認，歐洲議會對中國在俄烏戰爭中的角色持高度懷疑態度，認為中國提供的工業與金融支援，直接抵銷了西方國家對俄羅斯的制裁壓力。此外，歐盟內部的產業代表團體也強調，必須透過更嚴謹的採購審查與貿易保護機制，防止關鍵供應鏈遭特定國家操縱，這已成為歐洲公共政策與產業發展的優先事項。
儘管各界對加強貿易防禦有高度共識，但在具體執行層面上仍存在資訊落差與爭議。部分媒體報導指出，歐洲議會對於中國政策的態度趨於強硬，但歐盟執委會與各成員國在執行制裁的細節上，是否能達成完全一致的行動，目前仍待觀察。此外，針對中國對俄軍工產業的具體影響程度，不同來源間的評估亦有細微差異。
部分分析認為，中國的技術介入已造成實質威脅，但也有觀點指出，歐洲產業對中國市場的依賴度極高，如何在「去風險」與「維持經濟運作」之間取得平衡，依然是歐盟官員面臨的重大挑戰，相關資料與具體制裁清單尚未完全公開。
此一政策轉向對歐洲產業與公共部門產生了直接影響。根據中歐地區的採購與招標資訊，歐洲公共與私部門正重新評估供應鏈的安全性，並尋求更多元化的合作夥伴以降低對單一市場的依賴。這不僅涉及傳統的製造業與工業零元件，更擴及金融服務與高科技領域。歐洲議會推動的強硬政策，預計將迫使歐洲企業在參與國際標案時，必須更嚴格地遵守歐盟的制裁規定與審查機制。對於中歐地區的產業而言，這意味著未來的商業環境將面臨更高的合規門檻，但也可能帶動區域內本土產業的發展契機。
針對歐洲議會的強硬立場，各界觀點不一。支持者認為，這是維護歐洲戰略自主與保障烏克蘭安全的必要手段，必須透過貿易防禦工具遏止不公平競爭與地緣政治威脅。然而，反對或持保留意見者則提醒，過度激進的貿易防禦政策可能引發中國的報復性措施，導致歐盟出口導向產業蒙受損失。此外，必須注意不應將所有中國的經濟行為均視為對俄軍事支援，應區分商業活動與戰略性技術轉移，避免過度解讀或將經濟問題政治化，以免造成全球供應鏈的進一步混亂與通貨膨脹壓力。
後續觀察重點在於歐盟執委會如何將歐洲議會的決議轉化為具體的法律條文與行政命令。首先，需密切關注歐盟與中國在接下來的談判桌上，是否會針對貿易防禦機制進行實質性辯論。其次，觀察歐洲各成員國是否會因應此政策，調整其對中投資與技術出口的監管力度。最後，應持續監控中國對俄羅斯軍工生產的實際影響，以及歐盟是否會針對特定企業實施更具針對性的制裁，這些動態將決定未來數年歐洲與中國經貿關係的走向，並對全球公共衛生與經濟穩定產生連帶影響。
