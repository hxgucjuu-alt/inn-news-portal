根據體育媒體報導，2027年國際板球理事會（ICC）男子一日本因板球世界盃（ODI World Cup）將由南非、辛巴威與納米比亞共同主辦。此屆賽事預計於2027年10月舉行，也是繼2003年後相隔24年再度回到非洲大陸舉行。

報導指出，在三處主辦國中，僅南非與辛巴威獲得自動晉級名額。作為ICC副會員（Associate Member）的納米比亞，並未直接獲得參賽席次，必須透過後續的全球晉級賽道（global pathway）爭取參賽資格。

資料顯示，主辦國未能直接獲得資格或最終未能晉級並非前所未見。回顧歷史賽事，愛爾蘭與荷蘭在共同主辦1999年板球世界盃時，亦曾發生無法晉級的情況。目前納米比亞仍需在資格賽中力拼佳績，以確保以主辦國身份在本土賽事中登場。

## English edition

According to sports news reports, the 2027 ICC Men's Cricket World Cup (ODI) is set to be co-hosted by South Africa, Zimbabwe, and Namibia. Scheduled for October 2027, the event marks 24 years since the tournament was last hosted in Africa.

Reports indicate that while South Africa and Zimbabwe have been granted direct qualification as hosts, Namibia did not receive an automatic ticket. As an Associate Member of the ICC, Namibia is required to compete through the global pathway system to secure its tournament spot.

Historical precedents show that co-hosts missing out on qualification is not entirely unprecedented, as Ireland and the Netherlands experienced similar challenges during the 1999 World Cup. Namibia now faces the task of navigating upcoming qualifying matches to ensure participation on home soil.
