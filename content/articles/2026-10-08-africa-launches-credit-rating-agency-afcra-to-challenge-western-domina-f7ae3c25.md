非洲聯盟（African Union）推動的「非洲信用評等機構」（AfCRA）已選定將總部設於模里西斯。該機構的設立經過近十年的協商討論，並早在2018年獲得非洲領袖背書，旨在對非洲各國政府、金融機構與企業進行主權及信用評等。

報導指出，AfCRA 的成立被視為非洲抗衡穆迪（Moody's）、標普（S&P）及惠譽（Fitch）等西方三大評等機構主導地位的重大舉措。非洲各國政府長期批評國際評等機構在面臨危機時往往過快調降非洲國家的信用評級，進而加重借貸成本，未能充分考量當地的實際經濟環境。

資料顯示，AfCRA 將仰賴非洲本土的數據與專業知識，提供更具脈絡性的風險評估。此外，該機構計畫為目前未獲西方三大機構評等的23個非洲經濟體進行評估，支持者期盼藉此協助非洲各國取得成本更低的融資管道。

## English edition

The African Union-backed African Credit Rating Agency (AfCRA) is set to be headquartered in Mauritius following nearly a decade of discussions and formal endorsement by African leaders in 2018. The institution is designed to evaluate the creditworthiness of African governments, commercial banks, and corporations.

Reports indicate that AfCRA aims to provide an alternative to the dominant "big three" Western credit-rating firms—Moody's, Standard & Poor's, and Fitch. African governments have frequently argued that major Western agencies are prone to downgrading regional borrowers too quickly during crises, driving up financing costs and failing to reflect local economic resilience.

According to available information, the new agency plans to utilize African data and expertise to deliver context-specific risk evaluations. AfCRA is also expected to assess 23 African economies currently unrated by the major global agencies, with supporters hoping the initiative will help states secure access to more affordable capital.
