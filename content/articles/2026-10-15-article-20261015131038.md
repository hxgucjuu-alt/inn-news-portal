  # Regional Cooperation and Industrial Efficiency in Northeast Asia: A Cross-National Observation from Public Health to Air Transport
  
  > This article focuses on regional dynamics in Northeast Asia, providing a comprehensive analysis of air transport performance and regional public health cooperation mechanisms. It explores the current status of industrial efficiency and cross-national response mechanisms in Japan, South Korea, and surrounding areas, while analyzing their profound impact on regional integration.
  
  As a core hub of global geopolitics and economics, Northeast Asia has long been highly interconnected in terms of historical context, industrial competition, and regional security issues. According to academic research from the Department of History at National Taiwan Normal University, the interaction between Japan and the Korean Peninsula throughout history—mediated through diplomatic etiquette and the concept of "Hua-Yi" (the Sinocentric world order)—laid the foundation for complex bilateral and multilateral relations within the region. This historical legacy not only influences contemporary diplomatic trajectories but also leaves a deep imprint on the framework of transnational cooperation in modern society. As globalization accelerates, how Northeast Asian countries shift from a historical perspective to modern, pragmatic cooperation when facing common challenges has become a key issue for regional stability and development.
  
  Regarding modern industrial performance, the latest statistics from the aviation data analysis firm OAG show that the Northeast Asian region demonstrates world-leading competitiveness in On-Time Performance (OTP) for air transport. Data indicates that among the top ten airlines in the global on-time performance rankings for September, eight are from the Northeast Asian region, and these carriers all achieved an on-time rate exceeding 90%. Among them, Changan Airlines performed particularly well in the region, with an on-time rate as high as 96.03%.
  
  This data collectively confirms that the region has reached a world-class standard in infrastructure management, aviation operational efficiency, and the integration of logistics chains, serving as a vital pillar supporting regional economic operations.
  
  However, there remains a significant disparity in the breadth of regional cooperation. Analysis from South Korea's *The Hankyoreh* points out that experts and scholars strongly advocate for the establishment of a "Northeast Asia Infectious Disease Joint Response Network" covering South Korea, North Korea, China, and Japan to address major public health threats. Despite the excellent performance in technical cooperation within the aviation industry, coordination mechanisms in the public health sector—which involves sovereignty and political sensitivity—remain at the stage of theoretical advocacy and have not yet formed substantive transnational joint action agreements. This reveals a clear implementation gap in Northeast Asia between "industrial technical cooperation" and "political security and health cooperation."
  
  The aforementioned phenomenon has had concrete industrial and policy impacts on the Northeast Asian region. In the aviation industry, high on-time performance not only enhances the efficiency of business exchanges within the region but also strengthens Northeast Asia's position as a global logistics hub, attracting more international capital and technology investment. On the public health policy front, if the regional cooperation mechanism advocated by experts can be implemented, it would significantly reduce the social and economic impact of cross-border infectious diseases. This implies that if Northeast Asian countries can reach a consensus on non-traditional security issues, it will help alleviate regional isolation caused by geopolitical tensions, thereby promoting deeper regional integration.
  
  Despite the impressive industrial data, expectations for regional cooperation must remain cautious. Different perspectives suggest that the divergences among Northeast Asian countries regarding historical memory, ideology, and diplomatic stances remain significant obstacles to promoting regional cooperation mechanisms. Over-interpreting the success of a single industry or the advocacy of the academic community may overlook the high sensitivity countries have toward sovereignty and security in realpolitik. Therefore, when evaluating the prospects for regional integration in Northeast Asia, it is essential to distinguish between the fundamental differences of "technical industrial cooperation" and "strategic public policy," avoiding the direct analogy of excellence in specific fields to a comprehensive thaw in regional political relations.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on whether Northeast Asian countries can transfer successful cooperation models from industries like aviation to more complex public health or environmental issues. Specifically, regarding the transnational network for infectious disease prevention, it remains to be seen whether governments will take substantive diplomatic action or continue to maintain existing bilateral interaction patterns. Furthermore, as global supply chains continue to adjust, whether Northeast Asia can maintain its leading position in aviation and logistics, and how countries will use regional agreements to address potential future public health emergencies, will be key indicators for observing the stability and development of the Northeast Asian region.

# 東北亞區域合作與產業效能：從公共衛生到航空運輸的跨國觀察

> 本文聚焦東北亞區域動態，綜合分析航空運輸績效與區域性公共衛生合作機制。探討日本、韓國與周邊地區在產業效率及跨國應對機制上的現況，並分析其對區域整合的深遠影響。


東北亞作為全球地緣政治與經濟的核心板塊，長期以來在歷史脈絡、產業競爭與區域安全議題上呈現高度關聯。根據國立臺灣師範大學歷史學系的學術研究指出，日本與朝鮮半島在歷史長河中，透過外交禮儀與華夷觀唸的互動，奠定了區域內複雜的雙邊與多邊關係基礎。這種歷史遺緒不僅影響了當代的外交走向，亦在現代社會的跨國合作框架中留下深刻烙印。隨著全球化程式加速，東北亞各國在面對共同挑戰時，如何從歷史的視角轉向現代的務實合作，成為區域穩定與發展的關鍵課題。
在現代產業表現方面，根據航空資料分析機構 OAG 的最新統計，東北亞地區在航空運輸的準點率（On-Time Performance, OTP）表現上展現了全球領先的競爭力。資料顯示，在九月份的全球航空準點率排名中，前十名航空公司中有八家來自東北亞地區，且這些業者的準點率均突破 90%。其中，長安航空（Changan Airlines）在該區域的表現尤為突出，準點率高達 96.03%。
此一資料共同證實了該地區在基礎設施管理、航空營運效率以及物流鏈條的整合上，已達到全球頂尖水準，成為支撐區域經濟運作的重要支柱。
然而，在區域合作的廣度上，各方資訊仍存在顯著差異。韓國《韓民族日報》（The Hankyoreh）的分析指出，針對傳染病等重大公共衛生威脅，專家學者強烈主張建立一個涵蓋南韓、北韓、中國與日本的「東北亞傳染病共同應對網路」。儘管在航空產業的技術性合作上已有卓越表現，但在涉及主權與政治敏感度的公共衛生領域，各國目前的協調機制仍處於理論倡議階段，尚未形成實質的跨國聯合行動協議。這顯示出東北亞在「產業技術合作」與「政治安全與衛生合作」之間，存在著明顯的執行落差。
上述現象對東北亞地區產生了具體的產業與政策影響。在航空產業，高準點率不僅提升了區域內的商務往來效率，也強化了東北亞作為全球物流樞紐的地位，吸引了更多國際資本與技術投入。而在公共衛生政策面，專家所提倡的區域合作機制，若能落實，將能顯著降低跨境傳染病帶來的社會與經濟衝擊。這意味著東北亞各國若能在非傳統安全議題上達成共識，將有助於緩解因地緣政治緊張而造成的區域孤立，進而推動更深層次的區域整合。
儘管產業資料亮眼，但各界對於區域合作的預期仍須保持謹慎。不同觀點認為，東北亞各國在歷史記憶、意識形態及外交立場上的歧異，仍是推動區域性合作機制的巨大阻礙。過度解讀單一產業的成功或學術界的倡議，可能會忽略現實政治中各國對於主權與安全的高度敏感。因此，在評估東北亞區域整合的前景時，必須區分「技術性產業合作」與「戰略性公共政策」之間的本質差異，避免將特定領域的卓越表現，直接類比為區域政治關係的全面破冰。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於東北亞各國是否能將航空等產業的成功合作模式，轉移至更為複雜的公共衛生或環境議題上。特別是針對傳染病防範的跨國網路，各國政府是否會採取實質的對話行動，或是繼續維持現有的雙邊互動模式。此外，隨著全球供應鏈的持續調整，東北亞在航空與物流領域的領先地位是否能持續維持，以及各國如何透過區域性協議來應對未來可能出現的突發性公共衛生風險，將是觀察東北亞區域穩定與發展的關鍵指標。
