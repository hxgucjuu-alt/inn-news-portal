  # The Impact of the Russia-Ukraine War on Central Asian Geopolitics: Defense Industry Autonomy and Mineral Strategic Layout
  
  > As the Russia-Ukraine war triggers international sanctions, Russia's military influence in Central Asia faces challenges. Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan are accelerating the localization of their defense industries, while Central Asian nations are becoming a new focal point of global geopolitical competition due to their abundant uranium and rare earth resources.
  
  Since the collapse of the Soviet Union, the Central Asian region has reached a critical historical milestone marking thirty years of independence. For a long time, the region has maintained a high degree of continuity and structural stability in its political systems. However, since Russia's invasion of Ukraine in 2022, the regional geopolitical landscape has undergone significant shifts. Russia's former monopoly in the Central Asian arms market has been gradually shaken by international sanctions and supply chain disruptions caused by the war, forcing countries like Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan to reassess their national defense and security strategies and accelerate the establishment of localized defense industrial systems to reduce excessive reliance on a single military supplier.
  
  According to various media reports, Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan have already developed the foundational capabilities to maintain Russian-made military equipment. In particular, Kazakhstan's Semipalatinsk Machine-Building Plant JSC (Semey Engineering plant) and Aircraft Repair Plant No. 405 are now capable of performing complex maintenance and upgrade operations on military equipment. This shift is not merely an improvement at the technical level, but symbolizes a substantive leap in the defense autonomy of Central Asian nations.
  
  Although Russia still attempts to maintain its influence through existing military cooperation mechanisms, the trend toward "de-Russification" in the defense industrial chains of Central Asian countries has become an irreversible development trajectory.
  
  Regarding the aforementioned military-industrial transformation, there are differing accounts among various sources concerning the degree and speed of "autonomy." Some perspectives suggest that the current technical capabilities of Central Asian nations remain highly dependent on the infrastructure and spare parts left behind by Russia, making it difficult to completely break away from the Russian military-technical framework in the short term. Furthermore, public information remains unclear as to whether these countries have introduced technical support from Western or other Asian nations while promoting the transformation of their defense industries. There remains a cognitive gap in various circles between "technological upgrading" and "merely possessing maintenance capabilities" regarding the degree of autonomy in Central Asian defense industrial chains, which awaits further industrial data for verification.
  
  At the geo-economic level, the strategic value of the Central Asian region has increased significantly due to its abundant mineral resources. As a global superpower in uranium production, Kazakhstan plays a key role in the global energy supply chain. At the same time, the vast reserves of rare earth metals and battery metals within the Central Asian region are attracting the attention of major global powers. With the rapid development of the global energy transition and the electric vehicle industry, the mineral development potential of Central Asia has gradually shifted from the early geological exploration stage to more concrete industrial development planning. This makes Central Asia not only a buffer zone for military strategy but also a core participant in the global critical mineral supply chain.
  
  However, there are differing interpretations among scholars and think tanks regarding the geopolitical prospects of Central Asia. Some viewpoints emphasize that in the process of pursuing economic and defense autonomy, Central Asian countries must maintain a delicate balance between Russia, China, and Western powers. While this "cooperative geocultural" strategy can bring diversified investment, it also comes with high political risks. One should not over-interpret the ability of Central Asian countries to completely shed the shackles of geopolitics in the short term, as the structural characteristics of their political systems remain deeply tied to the interests of surrounding major powers, and any attempt at autonomy in a single industry could trigger a power reshuffling within the region.
  
  Looking ahead, the development focus of Central Asian nations will center on how to transform abundant natural resources into industrial strength and achieve deeper technological autonomy in the defense industry. Key areas for subsequent observation include: whether Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan will further expand military-technical cooperation with non-Russian-aligned countries, and whether the region can establish more autonomous pricing power and processing chains for critical mineral exports. These issues will be key indicators in determining whether Central Asia will move toward autonomous development or be relegated to a pawn in the power games of major nations over the next decade.

# 俄烏戰局衝擊中亞地緣政治：國防產業自主與礦產戰略佈局

> 隨著俄烏戰爭引發國際制裁，俄羅斯在中亞的軍事影響力面臨挑戰。哈薩克與烏茲別克正加速推動國防產業自主化，同時中亞各國憑藉豐富的鈾礦與稀土資源，成為全球地緣政治競逐的新焦點。


中亞地區自蘇聯解體後，迎來獨立三十週年的關鍵歷史節點。長期以來，該區域在政治體制上保持著高度的連續性與結構穩定。然而，自 2022 年俄羅斯入侵烏克蘭以來，區域地緣政治格局出現顯著轉變。俄羅斯過去在中亞武器市場的獨佔地位，因戰爭帶來的國際制裁與供應鏈中斷而逐漸動搖，迫使哈薩克、烏茲別克等國重新評估其國防安全策略，並加速建立本土化的國防工業體系，以降低對單一軍事供應國的過度依賴。
根據多方媒體報導，哈薩克與烏茲別克已具備維護俄羅斯製軍事裝備的基礎能力。特別是哈薩克的 Semipalatinsk Machine-Building Plant JSC（Semey Engineering plant）以及 Aircraft Repair Plant No 405，已能執行複雜的軍事裝備維修與升級作業。這種轉變不僅是技術層面的提升，更象徵中亞國家在國防自主權上的實質跨越。
儘管俄羅斯仍試圖透過既有的軍事合作機制維持影響力，但中亞國家在國防產業鏈上的「去俄羅斯化」趨勢已成為不可逆的發展方向。
針對上述軍事工業轉型，各來源間對於「自主化」的程度與速度仍存在不同表述。部分觀點認為，中亞國家目前的技術能力仍高度依賴俄羅斯遺留的基礎設施與零件供應，短期內難以完全脫離俄羅斯的軍事技術框架。此外，關於各國在推動國防產業轉型時，是否已引進西方或其他亞洲國家的技術支援，目前公開資訊尚不明確，各界對於中亞國防產業鏈的自主程度，仍存在「技術升級」與「僅具維修能力」之間的認知落差，尚待後續更多產業資料佐證。
在地緣經濟層面，中亞地區的戰略價值正因其豐富的礦產資源而大幅提升。哈薩克作為全球鈾礦生產的超級大國，其在全球能源供應鏈中扮演關鍵角色。同時，中亞區域內蘊藏的大量稀土金屬與電池金屬，正吸引全球主要強權的目光。隨著全球能源轉型與電動車產業的快速發展，中亞的礦產開發潛力已從早期的地質探勘階段，逐漸轉向更為具體的產業開發規劃，這使得中亞不僅是軍事戰略的緩衝區，更成為全球關鍵礦物供應鏈中的核心參與者。
然而，針對中亞的地緣政治前景，學界與智庫存在不同解讀。部分觀點強調，中亞國家在追求經濟與國防自主的過程中，必須在俄羅斯、中國與西方強權之間維持精細的平衡，這種「合作型地緣文化」策略雖然能帶來多元投資，但也伴隨著高度的政治風險。不應過度解讀中亞國家在短期內能完全擺脫地緣政治的束縛，因為其政治體制的結構性特徵，仍與周邊大國的利益深度繫結，任何單一產業的自主化嘗試，都可能引發區域內部的權力重組。
展望未來，中亞國家的發展重點將聚焦於如何將豐富的天然資源轉化為工業實力，並在國防產業上實現更深度的技術自主。後續觀察重點在於：哈薩克與烏茲別克是否會進一步擴大與非俄羅斯體系國家的軍事技術合作？以及該區域在關鍵礦產出口上，是否能建立更具自主性的定價權與加工鏈？這些議題將是決定中亞未來十年在地緣政治中，是走向自主發展，或是淪為大國博弈棋子的關鍵指標。
