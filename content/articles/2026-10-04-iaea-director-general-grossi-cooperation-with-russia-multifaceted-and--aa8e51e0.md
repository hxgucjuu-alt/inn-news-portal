國際原子能機構（IAEA）總幹事拉斐爾·格羅西於2026年10月3日向Sputnik/RIA Novosti表示，國際原子能機構與俄羅斯之間的合作是密集且多面向的，涵蓋了許多敏感議題。格羅西強調，這種合作對於處理全球核能相關的複雜挑戰至關重要。

格羅西進一步指出，國際原子能機構與俄羅斯在「非常敏感的問題」上進行合作，這些問題涉及「區域危機」以及「貴國自身區域的局勢」。這表明雙方合作的範圍廣泛，不僅限於技術層面，更觸及地緣政治和安全領域。

資料顯示，此類合作並非首次被提及。俄羅斯總統弗拉基米爾·普京在2025年9月26日於莫斯科會見格羅西時，也曾表示俄羅斯與國際原子能機構在所有領域都有合作，包括全球核安全、俄羅斯能源發展以及俄羅斯參與海外項目等。克里姆林宮當時的報導指出，普京強調全球核安全是合作的首要任務。

格羅西與普京的這些聲明，共同凸顯了國際原子能機構與俄羅斯之間持續且關鍵的合作關係。在當前全球複雜的政治與安全環境下，雙方在核能安全、區域穩定及能源發展等敏感議題上的協調，對於維護國際和平與安全具有重要意義。

## English edition

International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) Director General Rafael Grossi stated on October 3, 2026, to Sputnik/RIA Novosti that the cooperation between the IAEA and Russia is intensive and multifaceted, spanning many sensitive issues. Grossi emphasized that this collaboration is crucial for addressing complex challenges related to global nuclear energy.

Grossi further indicated that the IAEA has been working with Russia on "very sensitive issues," which include "regional crises" and "the situation in your own region." This highlights the broad scope of their engagement, extending beyond technical aspects to encompass geopolitical and security domains.

Reports suggest that such cooperation has been a consistent theme. On September 26, 2025, Russian President Vladimir Putin, during a meeting with Grossi in Moscow, also affirmed Russia's cooperation with the IAEA across all areas. According to Kremlin reports at the time, Putin specifically mentioned global nuclear safety, the development of Russian energy, and Russia's participation in projects abroad, underscoring global nuclear safety as a primary focus.

These statements from both Grossi and Putin collectively underscore the ongoing and critical cooperative relationship between the IAEA and Russia. In the current complex global political and security landscape, their coordination on sensitive issues such as nuclear safety, regional stability, and energy development holds significant importance for maintaining international peace and security.
