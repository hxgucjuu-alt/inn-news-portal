綜合外媒報導，美國政府針對八家主要科技公司祭出就業型綠卡程序停權措施，暫停受理與處理這些企業的永久勞工認證（PERM）申請。報導點名的企業名單包含微軟（Microsoft）、塔塔諮詢服務（TCS）、Infosys、Wipro、Cognizant、HCL、Adobe 以及 Capgemini 等跨國科技與資訊服務巨頭。

資料顯示，美國勞工部表示將不再接受涉及這些公司的全新申請，亦將暫停審理現有待處理的永久勞工認證案。PERM 認證是美國雇主為外國籍專業人才贊助就業型綠卡過程中的關鍵初期階段，用於確認在當地勞動力市場中無法招募到合適的本國勞工。

報導指出，這項停權措施讓成千上萬依賴此途徑獲得永久居留權的外籍員工陷入困境，其中以印度籍科技專業人才受到的衝擊尤為顯著。相關分析提及，持有 H-1B 簽證的工作者雖然不會立即失去現有工作，但若接近六年簽證期限上限，其在美國的長期移民身分將面臨極大不確定性與潛在延誤。

## English edition

The US administration has suspended eight major technology companies from the employment-based green card process, freezing their Permanent Labor Certification (PERM) applications. According to media reports, the targeted companies include major technology and consulting firms such as Microsoft, Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, HCL, Adobe, and Capgemini.

The US Department of Labor indicated that it will neither accept new applications nor process pending PERM certification cases involving these firms. The PERM process serves as a vital first step for employers seeking to sponsor eligible foreign employees for US employment-based permanent residency, certifying that qualified local workers are unavailable for the positions.

Reports state that this suspension creates significant uncertainty for thousands of foreign professionals, particularly Indian tech workers who heavily rely on the program. While H-1B visa holders will not face immediate job losses, employees approaching the six-year visa limitation may experience prolonged disruptions to their legal immigration pathways.
