  # North American Appliance Repair Trends: Troubleshooting Guide for LG Washer-Dryer Combo Drying Issues
  
  > Recently, in response to common issues regarding poor drying performance in LG washer-dryer combos within the North American market, several appliance repair platforms have released detailed troubleshooting guides, focusing on sensor calibration, load management, and hardware maintenance.
  
  Recently, in the North American appliance repair sector, there has been an increasing number of discussions regarding the poor drying performance of LG washer-dryer combos. Because these appliances combine washing and drying functions, they are widely popular in modern homes with limited space. However, as usage time increases, consumers frequently report that clothes remain damp even after a full cycle. Such issues not only involve the daily maintenance of the device but are also closely related to the technical characteristics of common models in the North American market, such as the WM3998HBA, WM6900HBA, and the WashTower series.
  
  According to various repair information sources, a decline in drying performance is usually related to operating habits, the cleanliness of filters, and the calibration of internal sensors within the device.
  
  Based on information from several professional repair platforms, including Appliance Express TX, Techactiv, and Appliance Code Hub, the common causes for the failure of the drying function in LG washer-dryer combos have been identified. First, overloading is one of the main reasons why clothes cannot be dried thoroughly, as insufficient space inside the drum hinders the circulation of hot air. Second, clogged filters and improper installation of drain hoses also directly affect the machine's dehumidification efficiency.
  
  In addition, Techactiv points out that decoding the "dHE" error code and performing a "Towel Test" to calibrate the humidity sensor are currently recognized by the industry as effective initial diagnostic steps.
  
  Although most repair guides overlap significantly in their handling logic, there are still differences in the depth and details of troubleshooting among various sources. Appliance Code Hub emphasizes potential hardware-level faults, such as a damaged heating element or dirty humidity sensors, which may require further parts replacement; meanwhile, The Tools Trunk focuses on checking the installation configuration of the drain hose, suggesting that incorrect drainage paths can cause the drying cycle to be interrupted.
  
  Furthermore, the troubleshooting advice provided on LG's official support page focuses primarily on systematic maintenance and updates, which may differ from the "manual repair" suggestions provided by third-party repair platforms in terms of operational risk and warranty policy standards.
  
  Such device failures have had a direct impact on the daily lives of North American households, especially for urban residents who rely on washer-dryer combos to save space. When the device fails to achieve the expected drying effect, it not only increases additional power consumption but may also cause clothes to develop odors or mold due to being in a damp state for long periods. From an industry perspective, this reflects an increased demand for transparency in appliance repair information. With the popularity of digital repair guides, the demand for DIY diagnosis has increased, which also prompts appliance manufacturers to provide more detailed error code explanations and maintenance manuals to reduce the pressure on repair services.
  
  However, consumers should remain cautious regarding the aforementioned repair suggestions. There are significant structural differences between various LG washer-dryer models, and not all repair methods for the WM3998HBA are applicable to the entire WashTower series. Furthermore, if the machine is still under the original manufacturer's warranty, disassembling the casing or replacing hardware parts such as the heating element is highly likely to void the warranty. Therefore, suggestions in third-party repair guides should be viewed as "initial troubleshooting" rather than "ultimate solutions." Experts recommend that if the problem persists after performing basic sensor calibration and cleaning the filters, one should prioritize contacting an authorized manufacturer repair service to avoid electrical safety risks caused by improper operation.
  
  The focus of subsequent observation is whether LG will release more precise firmware updates or service bulletins regarding common drying performance issues. As standards for energy-efficient appliances become increasingly stringent in the North American market, the drying efficiency of washer-dryer combos will become a key factor affecting product market share. In the future, consumers should continue to pay attention to official support information provided by manufacturers and combine it with professional repair advice to extend the service life of their appliances. At the same time, whether the industry can establish more unified diagnostic standards for specific error codes across different models will also be an important indicator for improving the quality of after-sales service.

# 北美家電維修趨勢：LG 洗脫烘一體機乾燥功能故障排除指南

> 近期針對北美市場常見的 LG 洗脫烘一體機乾燥效能不彰問題，多個家電維修平臺發布了詳細的故障排除指南，重點在於感測器校準、負載管理及硬體維護。


近期在北美家電維修領域中，針對 LG 洗脫烘一體機（Washer Dryer Combo）乾燥效能不佳的討論日益增加。這類家電因結合洗衣與烘乾功能，在空間有限的現代住宅中廣受歡迎，然而隨著使用時間增加，消費者常回報衣物在完整行程結束後仍呈現潮濕狀態。此類問題不僅涉及裝置的日常保養，更與北美市場常見的型號（如 WM3998HBA、WM6900HBA 及 WashTower 系列）的技術特性密切相關。
根據多方維修資訊顯示，乾燥效能下降通常與操作習慣、過濾器清潔程度以及裝置內部感測器校準有關。
根據 Appliance Express TX、Techactiv 及 Appliance Code Hub 等多個專業維修平臺的資訊，LG 洗脫烘一體機乾燥功能失效的共同原因已獲得確認。首先，過度裝載（Overloading）是導致衣物無法徹底乾燥的主因之一，因為滾筒內空間不足會阻礙熱空氣迴圈。其次，過濾器堵塞與排水管安裝不當，亦會直接影響機器的除濕效率。
此外，Techactiv 指出，針對 dHE 錯誤程式碼的解碼，以及執行「毛巾測試」（Towel Test）來進行濕度感測器校準，是目前業界公認有效的初步診斷步驟。
儘管多數維修指南在處理邏輯上具有高度重疊性，但不同來源對於故障排除的深度與細節仍存在差異。Appliance Code Hub 強調了硬體層面的潛在故障，例如加熱元件（Heating Element）損壞或濕度感測器髒汙，這可能需要進一步的零件更換；而 The Tools Trunk 則將重點放在檢查排水軟管的安裝配置，認為錯誤的排水路徑會導致乾燥迴圈中斷。
此外，LG 官方支援頁面提供的故障排除建議，主要聚焦於系統性的維護與更新，與第三方維修平臺提供的「手動修復」建議在操作風險與保固政策上，可能存在認定標準的落差。
此類裝置故障對北美家庭的日常生活造成了直接影響，特別是對於依賴洗脫烘一體機節省空間的都會區住戶。當裝置無法達成預期的乾燥效果時，不僅增加了額外的電力消耗，亦可能導致衣物因長期處於潮濕狀態而產生異味或黴菌。從產業角度來看，這反映了消費者對於家電維修資訊透明度的需求提升。隨著數位化維修指南的普及，消費者對於自行診斷（DIY）的需求增加，這也促使家電製造商必須提供更為詳盡的錯誤程式碼說明與維護手冊，以減少維修服務的壓力。
然而，針對上述維修建議，消費者仍需保持謹慎。不同型號的 LG 洗脫烘機型在結構上存在顯著差異，並非所有針對 WM3998HBA 的修復手段皆適用於所有 WashTower 系列。此外，若機器仍處於原廠保固期內，自行拆卸機殼或更換加熱元件等硬體零件，極可能導致保固失效。因此，第三方維修指南中的建議應被視為「初步排解」而非「終極解決方案」。專家建議，若在執行基本的感測器校準與清潔過濾器後問題依然存在，應優先聯絡原廠授權維修商，避免因不當操作造成電氣安全風險。
後續觀察重點在於 LG 是否會針對常見的乾燥效能問題，發布更為精確的韌體更新或維修公告。隨著北美市場對於節能家電的標準日益嚴格，洗脫烘一體機的乾燥效率將成為影響產品市佔率的關鍵。未來，消費者應持續關注製造商提供的官方支援資訊，並配合專業的維修建議，以延長家電的使用壽命。同時，針對不同型號的特定故障程式碼，業界是否能建立更統一的診斷標準，也將是提升售後服務品質的重要指標。
