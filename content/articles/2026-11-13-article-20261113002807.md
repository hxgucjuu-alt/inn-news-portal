  # Chiayi City Minzu Elementary School Wins Energy Conservation Education Award and the Global Perspective on Promoting Climate Resilience in Central America
  
  > Chiayi City Minzu Elementary School has won an award in a national energy conservation creative competition, demonstrating the results of foundational energy education; meanwhile, the Central American and Caribbean regions are actively promoting climate change resilience and strengthening disaster management.
  
  Chiayi City Minzu Elementary School recently participated in the "National Primary and Middle School Energy Conservation Creativity Competition" hosted by the Energy Administration of the Ministry of Economic Affairs. Standing out among numerous competing teams nationwide, the school secured third place with their entry, "The Nine Yang Manual." This competition aims to transform abstract concepts of energy conservation and carbon reduction into practical methods that are easy for students to understand through creative drama. Chiayi Mayor Huang Min-hui stated that energy resources are limited and that rooting education is crucial. The city government is committed to implementing the philosophy of parallel economic development, social progress, and environmental protection. Through the efforts of Education Department Director Huang Yuan-ting, the city encourages schools to utilize diverse curricula and innovative teaching to cultivate students' environmental literacy in cherishing energy.
  
  From a global perspective, energy education and climate resilience are not only teaching goals for local schools but also major public issues of international concern. According to public information from Mami Mizutori, the former head of the United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction (UNDRR), the Central American and Caribbean regions are currently actively promoting climate resilience construction. Due to their unique geographical locations, these regions have long faced the threat of climate risks and are committed to reducing the impact of climate change on society and the economy through international cooperation and policy promotion. This aligns at a macro level with the "Protect the Earth" philosophy advocated by Chiayi City Minzu Elementary School, both emphasizing the enhancement of societal adaptability to environmental changes through education and policy intervention.
  
  Although education and resilience building are consistent in their goals, there are significant differences in execution across various sources. The case of Chiayi City Minzu Elementary School focuses on creative education and behavioral change within the campus, falling under the category of micro-level social education; whereas the climate resilience projects in Central America and the Caribbean belong to the realm of macro-level public policy and international disaster management, involving transnational cooperation and infrastructure adjustments.
  
  Furthermore, information from sources such as Central Carolina Technical College (CCTC) focuses on the integration of higher education resources and academic accreditation, as well as personal financial services provided by Credit Central. While these pieces of information fall under the broad category of "social services" alongside the aforementioned educational and social issues, they have no direct connection to climate change or energy conservation education, reflecting the diverse and decentralized operation of social functions within the global information system.
  
  For the relevant regions, the impact of rooting education is profound. The energy conservation education promoted by Chiayi City is expected to extend environmental concepts from the campus to families, subsequently influencing the energy consumption behavior of the community. Meanwhile, the promotion of climate resilience in Central America and the Caribbean is directly related to the safety of life and property and the sustainable development of the local population. Together, these illustrate that whether through grassroots education or regional policy, systematic guidance can effectively enhance societal resilience in the face of environmental challenges. For industries, the promotion of energy-saving technologies and the development of climate disaster prevention-related industries will become important pillars for future social transformation.

While the award won by Chiayi City Minzu Elementary School demonstrates teaching effectiveness, a single award cannot fully represent the comprehensive improvement of the entire educational system, and the continuity and popularity of subsequent curricula remain to be observed. Similarly, although the climate resilience projects in Central America and the Caribbean are mentioned in international reports, their actual implementation effectiveness is still limited by the political stability, economic resources, and the complexity of climate change in each country. One should not over-interpret success stories from a single region or draw inappropriate causal links between information of different natures (such as financial services or higher education administration) and environmental issues.

# 嘉義市民族國小節能教育獲獎與中美洲氣候韌性推動之全球視角

> 嘉義市民族國小於全國節能創意競賽獲獎，展現基礎能源教育成果；與此同時，中美洲及加勒比海地區正積極推動氣候變遷韌性，強化防災管理。


嘉義市民族國小近期參加由經濟部能源署主辦的「國民中小學節約能源創意七十二變－小劇場創作競賽」，在全國眾多競爭隊伍中脫穎而出，以作品《九陽真經》獲得全國第三名。此項賽事旨在透過創意戲劇形式，將節能減碳的抽象概念轉化為學生易於理解的實踐方式。嘉義市長黃敏惠表示，能源資源有限，教育紮根至關重要，市府致力於落實經濟發展、社會進步與環境保護並行的理念，並透過教育處長黃媛楟的推動，鼓勵學校運用多元課程與創新教學，培養學生珍惜能源的環保素養。
在全球視角下，能源教育與氣候韌性不僅是地方學校的教學目標，更是國際關注的重大公共議題。根據聯合國減災署（UNDRR）前負責人水鳥真美（Mami Mizutori）的公開資訊顯示，中美洲與加勒比海地區目前正積極推動氣候韌性建設。該區域因地理位置特殊，長期面臨氣候風險的威脅，透過國際合作與政策推動，致力於降低氣候變遷對社會與經濟的衝擊。這與嘉義市民族國小所倡導的「守護地球」理念在宏觀層面上具有一致性，皆強調透過教育與政策介入，提升社會對環境變化的適應能力。
儘管教育與韌性建設在目標上具有一致性，但各來源資訊在執行層面存在顯著差異。嘉義市民族國小的案例側重於校園內的創意教育與行為改變，屬於微觀的社會教育範疇；而中美洲與加勒比海地區的氣候韌性計畫則屬於宏觀的公共政策與國際防災管理範疇，涉及跨國合作與基礎設施的調整。
此外，部分來源如 Central Carolina Technical College（CCTC）的資訊則聚焦於高等教育資源整合與學術認證，以及 Credit Central 提供的個人金融服務，這些資訊雖與前述教育及社會議題在廣義的「社會服務」範疇內，但與氣候變遷或節能教育並無直接關聯，反映出全球資訊體系中多元且分散的社會功能運作。
對於相關地區而言，教育紮根的影響深遠。嘉義市推動的節能教育，預期能將環保觀念從校園延伸至家庭，進而影響社群的能源消耗行為。而中美洲與加勒比海地區的氣候韌性推動，則直接關係到該區域民眾的生命財產安全與永續發展。這兩者共同說明瞭無論是基層教育還是區域政策，透過系統性的引導，皆能有效提升社會面對環境挑戰時的韌性。對於產業而言，節能技術的推廣與氣候防災相關產業的發展，將成為未來社會轉型的重要支撐。
然而，對於上述資訊的解讀需保持謹慎。嘉義市民族國小的獲獎固然顯示了教學成效，但單一獎項並不能完全代表整體教育體系的全面提升，仍需觀察後續課程的延續性與普及度。同樣地，中美洲與加勒比海地區的氣候韌性計畫，雖然在國際報告中被提及，但實際執行效果仍受限於各國的政治穩定度、經濟資源以及氣候變遷的複雜性。不應過度解讀單一地區的成功案例，或將不同性質的社會服務資訊（如金融服務或高等教育行政）與環境議題進行不當的因果連結。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於：第一，嘉義市民族國小的節能創意教學模式是否能進一步推廣至其他縣市，形成區域性的教育網路；第二，中美洲與加勒比海地區在氣候韌性建設上的國際合作進度，以及這些政策如何具體落實於當地社群的防災教育中；第三，在全球能源轉型與氣候變遷的背景下，教育機構如何與國際組織更有效地對接，將全球性的環境議題轉化為具體的在地行動，以達成真正的永續發展目標。
