  The Middle East is currently facing a severe geopolitical crisis as tensions between the United States and Iran deteriorate sharply. According to reports from The Guardian and NBC News, former US President Donald Trump recently stated publicly that if Iran refuses to engage in negotiations, he will launch attacks on critical infrastructure, such as power plants and bridges, starting next week. This move is viewed as a continuation of the "maximum pressure" campaign against Iran, aimed at forcing the Tehran authorities to make concessions in diplomatic talks.
  
  Simultaneously, the situation in Yemen remains volatile. According to information from Yemen Liveuamap, Yemeni government forces recently launched large-scale airstrikes against Houthi militia positions in the Al-Sarari and Al-Aqroud areas of Taiz Governorate, indicating that conflicts in the region are erupting at multiple points.
  
  Multiple sources have confirmed that there are serious discrepancies in diplomatic communications between the US and Iran. Trump claimed that Iranian officials had proactively proposed a meeting in Washington in an attempt to engage with the US; however, the Iranian government immediately and publicly denied this claim. According to an NBC News report citing a Middle Eastern diplomat, Iran did indeed attempt to establish contact with the Trump administration, but there is clearly a massive gap in how both sides perceive communication channels and the prerequisites for negotiation. Furthermore, following the death of Supreme Leader Khamenei, the power vacuum and social unrest within Iran have not only affected the country's foreign policy decision-making but have also rendered its internal response strategies to US military threats more chaotic and opaque.
  
  Currently, there are differing interpretations and information gaps regarding the true situation inside Iran. According to a report by Middle East Eye, following Khamenei's death, pro-government motorcycle convoys have appeared in cities such as Yazd, patrolling the streets every night and inciting fear among local anti-government citizens. The report notes that the activities of these pro-government armed forces reflect the Iranian ruling class's adoption of hardline stability-maintenance measures in the face of external military pressure and internal discontent.
  
  However, there is still a lack of clear official information regarding the specific chain of command for these armed forces and their connection to the regular Iranian military. Outsiders can only piece together information through sporadic eyewitness reports and social media updates, and it is not yet possible to confirm whether this represents a nationwide mobilization model in Iran.
  
  This geopolitical crisis has profound implications for the Middle East, not only threatening energy transport security in the Persian Gulf but also intensifying military confrontations within the region. If the US follows through on its threat to strike Iranian infrastructure, it could severely impact shipping around the Strait of Hormuz, subsequently triggering fluctuations in international oil prices. Furthermore, the ongoing exchange of fire between Yemeni government forces and the Houthi militia shows that regional proxy wars have not ceased due to US-Iran tensions; rather, they may become more intense due to the escalation of great power competition.
  
  For the region's industries and livelihoods, the ongoing military strikes and threats of blockade are destroying fragile infrastructure and leading to large-scale humanitarian risks and pressures from population displacement.
  
  Regarding the current situation, analysts warn against over-interpreting information from single sources. Trump's negotiation threats may be political rhetoric aimed at securing the initiative in talks rather than a precursor to immediate military action. Similarly, the Iranian government's public denial of a willingness to communicate may be intended to maintain its image before domestic hardliners. A limiting factor is the current lack of reliable third-party channels to verify whether the US and Iran are still engaged in secret consultations behind the scenes. Additionally, whether the social panic triggered by the change in leadership in Iran will evolve into large-scale regime instability or be successfully suppressed by hardliners remains in an observation phase; prematurely concluding the direction of Iran's political situation will lead to misjudgments of the regional security landscape.
  
  Subsequent observations should focus on three aspects: First, whether the US will take actual military action against Iranian infrastructure and the specific countermeasures taken by the Iranian military; second, the power transition process in Iran following Khamenei's death, particularly whether pro-government armed forces will engage in large-scale conflicts with opposition groups; and third, the shifts in stance by other regional countries, such as Saudi Arabia and the UAE, amidst the escalating US-Iran standoff. The international community needs to closely monitor military movements in the Persian Gulf and the chain reaction caused by internal political changes in Iran on regional stability.

# 美伊緊張局勢升溫：川普威脅擴大打擊與伊朗內部政局動盪

> 美國前總統川普威脅將對伊朗發動新一輪針對基礎設施的攻擊，要求其重返談判桌；與此同時，伊朗最高領袖哈米尼逝世後，國內反政府情緒高漲，各地出現支援政府的武裝勢力巡邏，局勢陷入高度不確定性。


中東地區近期面臨嚴峻的地緣政治危機，美國與伊朗之間的緊張關係急劇惡化。根據《衛報》（The Guardian）與《NBC新聞》（NBC News）報導，美國前總統川普近期公開表示，若伊朗拒絕參與談判，將於下週起針對伊朗的發電廠與橋樑等關鍵基礎設施發動攻擊。此舉被視為美國對伊朗施加極限壓力的延續，旨在迫使德黑蘭當局在外交談判中做出讓步。
與此同時，葉門局勢亦不平靜，根據《葉門即時地圖》（Yemen Liveuamap）資訊，葉門政府軍近期在塔伊茲省（Taiz Governorate）的阿薩拉里（Al-Sarari）與阿克魯德（Al-Aqroud）地區，針對胡塞武裝組織（Houthi militia）陣地展開了大規模空襲行動，顯示區域內衝突呈現多點爆發的態勢。
多方來源交叉確認，美伊兩國在外交溝通上存在嚴重分歧。川普聲稱伊朗官員曾主動提議在華盛頓舉行會晤，試圖與美方接觸；然而，伊朗政府隨即公開否認此項說法。根據《NBC新聞》引述一名中東外交官的說法，伊朗確實曾試圖與川普政府建立聯絡，但雙方對於溝通管道與談判前提的認知顯然存在巨大鴻溝。此外，伊朗最高領袖哈米尼（Khamenei）逝世後，伊朗國內政治權力真空與社會動盪加劇，這不僅影響了伊朗的對外決策，也讓該國在面對美國軍事威脅時，內部應對策略顯得更加混亂且不透明。
目前各界對於伊朗內部的真實情況存在不同的解讀與資訊落差。根據《中東之眼》（Middle East Eye）的報導，在哈米尼逝世後，伊朗城市亞茲德（Yazd）等地出現了親政府的摩托車隊，每晚在街頭巡邏，引發當地反政府民眾的恐懼。報導指出，這種親政府武裝勢力的活動，反映了伊朗執政階層在面對外部軍事壓力與內部不滿情緒時，採取了強硬的維穩手段。
然而，關於這些武裝勢力的具體指揮鏈以及其與伊朗正規軍的關聯，目前仍缺乏明確的官方說法，外界僅能透過零星的目擊報告與社群資訊進行拼湊，尚無法確認這是否代表伊朗全國性的動員模式。
這場地緣政治危機對中東地區的影響深遠，不僅威脅到波斯灣地區的能源運輸安全，也加劇了區域內的軍事對峙。若美國兌現對伊朗基礎設施的攻擊威脅，恐將導致霍爾木茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz）周邊的航運受到嚴重衝擊，進而引發國際油價波動。此外，葉門政府軍與胡塞武裝的持續交火，亦顯示出區域代理人戰爭並未因美伊緊張而停歇，反而可能因大國博弈的升級而變得更加激烈。
對於該地區的產業與民生而言，持續的軍事打擊與封鎖威脅，正在摧毀脆弱的基礎設施，並導致大規模的人道主義風險與人口流動壓力。
針對當前局勢，分析人士提醒不應過度解讀單一來源的訊息。川普的談判威脅可能屬於政治修辭，旨在爭取談判主導權，而非立即性的軍事行動預告；同樣地，伊朗政府對外否認溝通意願，亦可能是為了在國內強硬派面前維持形象。限制因素在於，目前缺乏可靠的第三方管道證實美伊雙方是否在檯面下仍有秘密磋商。此外，伊朗內部因領袖更迭引發的社會恐慌，是否會演變成大規模的政權動盪，抑或是被強硬派成功壓制，目前仍處於觀察期，過早斷定伊朗的政局走向將導致誤判區域安全形勢。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於三個面向：第一，美國是否會針對伊朗的基礎設施採取實際軍事行動，以及伊朗軍方對此的具體反制手段；第二，伊朗國內在哈米尼逝世後的權力過渡過程，特別是親政府武裝勢力是否會與反對派發生大規模衝突；第三，區域內其他國家如沙烏地阿拉伯、阿聯酋等，在美伊對峙升級下的立場轉換。國際社會需密切留意波斯灣地區的軍事調動，以及伊朗內部政局變動對區域穩定造成的連鎖效應。
