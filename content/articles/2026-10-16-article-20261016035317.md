  # European Defense and Cybersecurity Challenges: NATO Armament Shortages and the Risks of Digital Information Warfare
  
  > The European region is currently facing severe security challenges. Beyond the significant depletion of military stockpiles due to aid provided to Ukraine, the digital space continues to suffer from foreign interference, with Meta recently dismantling a Russian propaganda network targeting Europe.
  
  European nations are currently under a stress test regarding their dual lines of defense: national defense and information security. According to an assessment report released by the European Defence Agency in September, Europe is facing a critical shortage of armaments following the provision of billions of dollars in weapons and equipment to Ukraine. NATO envoys have pointed out that because U.S. military industrial capacity is prioritizing domestic demand, European countries have been pushed to the back of the U.S. arms supply chain for critical military capabilities, including ammunition, air defense systems, and missile defense. This supply gap in strategic materials not only impacts Europe's overall national defense autonomy but also raises concerns across all sectors regarding regional security and stability.
  
  While physical defense supplies are scarce, Europe's digital public space is also under threat. According to an investigative report by AP News, Meta recently took action to successfully identify and block a vast network of fake accounts. This network was confirmed to have been spreading pro-Russian propaganda targeting the European region for an extended period, attempting to manipulate public opinion to influence European perceptions of the international situation. Such digital intervention tactics interact with physical military threats, demonstrating that in responding to hybrid warfare, Europe must simultaneously address the replenishment of hardware armaments and the upgrading of software information protection.
  
  However, regarding these security challenges, there are differing perspectives on the prioritization of threats and methods of response among various sources. Reports related to NATO emphasize structural issues concerning supply chain scheduling and industrial capacity, arguing that the recovery speed of the European defense industrial base is far slower than the rate of consumption. In contrast, reports in the field of digital security focus on the covert nature and technical disruption of information manipulation. Furthermore, while hardware protection technologies such as "privacy screens" (Lingdun) offer new solutions for personal information security in public spaces, their scope remains limited in terms of national-level information warfare defense and cannot currently resolve the issue of large-scale digital propaganda network infiltration.
  
  These security issues have had a direct impact on Europe's industrial policy and social life. In the defense industry, European governments are being forced to re-examine the ratio of defense budgets to investments in local military industries to reduce reliance on foreign arms supply chains. On a social level, with the increase in remote work and the need to handle confidential documents in public spaces, the development of information security technology has become a focal point. For example, the privacy screen technology recently launched by Huawei, which utilizes OLED pixel-level hardware privacy protection, aims to resolve the risk of "shoulder surfing" in public areas. The proliferation of such technology reflects the urgent need for individuals and enterprises to protect sensitive information in a digitized society.
  
  Although technological progress can enhance personal privacy protection, experts warn against over-interpreting the effectiveness of a single technology. Regarding the Russian propaganda network facing Europe, Meta's blocking action is merely one part of the defense and does not eradicate the root cause of the information warfare threat. At the same time, the armament shortage issue raised by the European Defence Agency cannot be solved by technological innovation alone; it requires long-term planning for multinational military procurement coordination and industrial production lines. Therefore, conflating "hardware privacy technology" with "national security defense" is impractical, as the two play vastly different roles in maintaining information security and regional stability.
  
  Future observations will focus on whether European countries can successfully integrate defense industrial capacity to alleviate the "queue effect" feared by NATO envoys. Additionally, regarding the aftermath of Meta's blocking of the Russian propaganda network, whether European regulatory agencies will further strengthen digital governance of social media platforms, and whether Europe can establish a more resilient information defense system in the face of hybrid warfare threats, will be core indicators for European policy-making and social security maintenance in the coming months.

# 歐洲國防與資訊安全挑戰：北約軍備短缺與數位輿論戰風險

> 歐洲地區近期面臨嚴峻的安全挑戰，除軍備庫存因援助烏克蘭而顯著告急外，數位空間亦持續遭受外國勢力幹預，Meta 近期封鎖了針對歐洲的俄羅斯宣傳網路。


歐洲各國正處於國防與資訊安全雙重防線的壓力測試中。根據歐洲國防局（European Defence Agency）於九月發布的評估報告，歐洲在支援烏克蘭數十億美元的武器裝備後，內部面臨嚴重的軍備短缺問題。北約特使指出，由於美國軍工產能優先供應國內需求，歐洲國家在獲取彈藥、防空系統及導彈防禦等關鍵軍事能力上，已排在美國軍火供應鏈的後端。此一戰略物資的供給缺口，不僅影響歐洲整體的國防自主能力，亦引發各界對於區域安全穩定性的擔憂。
在實體國防物資匱乏的同時，歐洲的數位公共空間亦面臨威脅。根據美聯社（AP News）的調查報導，Meta 公司近日採取行動，成功識別並封鎖了一組龐大的假帳號網路。該網路被證實長期針對歐洲地區散佈親俄羅斯的宣傳內容，試圖透過操作輿論來影響歐洲民眾對於國際局勢的認知。此類數位幹預手段與實體軍事威脅形成互動影響，顯示歐洲在應對混合戰（Hybrid Warfare）時，必須同時兼顧硬體軍備的補充與軟體資訊防護的升級。
然而，針對上述安全挑戰，不同來源對於威脅的優先順序與應對方式存在觀點差異。北約相關報導強調的是供應鏈排程與工業產能的結構性問題，認為歐洲國防工業基地的恢復速度遠不及消耗速度；相較之下，數位安全領域的報導則聚焦於資訊操弄的隱蔽性與技術性阻斷。此外，關於「靈盾防窺屏」等硬體防護技術在公共場所資料保護的應用，雖然提供了個人資訊安全的新解法，但對於國家層級的資訊戰防禦而言，其作用範圍仍屬有限，目前尚無法直接解決大規模數位宣傳網路滲透的問題。
這些安全議題對歐洲的產業政策與社會生活產生了直接影響。在國防產業方面，歐洲各國政府正被迫重新審視國防預算與本土軍工產業的投資比例，以減少對外國軍火供應鏈的依賴。在社會層面，隨著遠端辦公與公共場所處理機密檔案需求增加，資訊安全技術的發展成為焦點。例如華為近期推出的靈盾防窺屏技術，透過 OLED 畫素級硬體防窺，旨在解決公共場所旁觀窺視的風險，這類技術的普及反映了在數位化社會中，個人與企業對於敏感資訊保護的迫切需求。
儘管科技進步能提升個人隱私保障，但專家提醒不應過度解讀單一技術的防護效能。針對歐洲面臨的俄羅斯宣傳網路，Meta 的封鎖行動僅是防禦的一環，並未根除資訊戰的威脅根源。同時，歐洲國防局提出的軍備短缺問題，並非單靠技術創新即可解決，更需仰賴跨國軍事採購協調與工業生產線的長期規劃。因此，將「硬體防窺技術」與「國家安全防禦」混為一談是不切實際的，兩者在維護資訊安全與區域穩定的層次上，扮演的角色截然不同。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於歐洲各國是否能成功整合國防工業產能，以緩解北約特使所擔憂的「排隊效應」。此外，針對 Meta 封鎖俄羅斯宣傳網路的後續影響，歐洲各國監管機構是否會進一步加強對社群媒體平臺的數位治理，以及在面對混合戰威脅時，歐洲是否能建立更具韌性的資訊防禦體系，將是未來數月內歐洲政策制定與社會安全維護的核心指標。
