  # Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Resigns Abruptly, Ending 33-Year Tenure and Sending Global Shockwaves
  
  > Nvidia founder Jensen Huang announced his resignation as CEO and board member on November 12, with CFO Colette Kress appointed as interim CEO. The move has sparked widespread concern regarding the future of the AI industry and potential shifts in U.S. chip export policies.
  
  Nvidia founder Jensen Huang announced without warning on November 12, 2026, that he is officially resigning from all positions, including president, CEO, and board member, ending his 33-year tenure at the helm. Since co-founding Nvidia in 1993, Huang has led the company from a struggling graphics card startup to a global powerhouse in artificial intelligence (AI) chips. The news of his departure has not only stunned the global technology industry but also caused significant volatility in capital markets.
  
  According to an announcement from Nvidia’s board of directors, the company has initiated an emergency succession plan, appointing Colette Kress, who has served as executive vice president and CFO since 2013, as interim CEO to manage operations during the transition period.
  
  According to multiple media reports, Huang’s resignation is not a simple retirement plan but is the result of multiple pressures, including external scrutiny and internal scandal. Citing reports from The New York Times and CNN, Yahoo Entertainment stated that the catalyst for Huang’s departure involves an affair with a senior executive at the company, who also operates "Megaspeed," a key Nvidia client. More severely, U.S. intelligence agencies had previously expressed high-level concerns regarding whether this executive was involved in espionage activities, with the progress of related investigations directly threatening the legality of Nvidia’s operations under U.S. regulatory frameworks.
  
  Currently, Nvidia’s board has confirmed Huang’s immediate departure and the transfer of interim command to Kress, a move viewed as a damage-control measure by the company to mitigate pressure from public opinion and regulators.
  
  However, details regarding the specific legal risks and subsequent effects following Huang’s departure vary across sources. While all parties confirm that Huang has stepped down, Rti News, as cited by Yahoo Entertainment, indicates that the U.S. Department of Justice is currently conducting multiple investigations into the scandals associated with Huang, which may further implicate Nvidia as a whole. In contrast, some tech media outlets such as Techbang.com have focused on internal stability and interim management strategies following Kress’s appointment.
  
  Information currently awaiting confirmation includes the scale of the U.S. Department of Justice’s investigation into Nvidia, the specific extent of Megaspeed’s involvement in the case, and whether Huang himself will face criminal charges due to potential espionage allegations.
  
  This personnel change has had a profound impact on the global technology industry. First, as the core of the global AI chip supply chain, the change in Nvidia’s CEO directly affects market expectations for the AI industry landscape beyond 2028. Institutional analysts believe that while chip supply and demand can be maintained in the short term, Huang’s departure will affect Nvidia’s product development pace and market competitiveness. Furthermore, political observers suggest that Huang’s departure may accelerate the U.S. government’s push for stricter chip export control policies. Given the geopolitical risks in the Asia-Pacific region, Nvidia may find itself in a more passive position when dealing with U.S. chip export restrictions without its strong leadership.
  
  Opinions on this event are significantly divided. Some market analysts believe that Nvidia’s powerful software ecosystem (CUDA) and GPU parallel computing layout possess high resilience, and that the departure of a single leader should not be over-interpreted as having a devastating impact on the company’s fundamentals. However, other commentators point out that Huang’s personal style and his vision for the industry were key to Nvidia’s success over the past decade, and his sudden departure symbolizes the end of the "AI myth," warning investors to be wary of potential turmoil in future corporate governance structures.
  
  Additionally, because the event involves allegations of espionage and executive scandal, the accusations regarding "espionage activities" remain within the scope of intelligence agency concerns and have not yet transitioned into formal judicial convictions; therefore, the public should not draw premature conclusions before the results of judicial investigations are released.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on three aspects: First, whether interim CEO Colette Kress can stabilize market confidence in the short term and explain how the company will handle its contractual relationship with Megaspeed; second, whether the U.S. Department of Justice’s investigation report will expand and impose substantial restrictions on Nvidia’s operating licenses or export permits; and third, whether Nvidia’s board will seek an external professional manager to serve as the permanent CEO to completely sever ties with the controversies of the Huang era. With Asia-Pacific regional security and technology trade being highly interconnected, Nvidia’s movements will continue to serve as a key indicator for global technology policy and industrial competition.

# 輝達執行長黃仁勳閃電辭職，結束33年掌舵生涯引發全球震盪

> 輝達（Nvidia）創辦人黃仁勳於11月12日宣佈辭去執行長及董事職務，由財務長克雷斯接任臨時執行長。此舉引發市場對AI產業未來佈局及美方晶片出口政策變動的廣泛擔憂。


輝達（Nvidia）創辦人黃仁勳於2026年11月12日無預警宣佈，正式辭去公司總裁、執行長及董事會成員等所有職務，結束其長達33年的掌舵生涯。黃仁勳自1993年共同創立輝達以來，帶領公司從瀕臨倒閉的顯示卡新創，轉型為全球人工智慧（AI）晶片霸主，其卸任訊息不僅震撼全球科技產業，亦對資本市場造成顯著衝擊。
根據輝達董事會公告，該公司已啟動緊急接班計畫，由自2013年起擔任執行副總裁兼財務長的科萊特・克雷斯（Colette Kress）接任臨時執行長，負責處理過渡期間的營運事務。
根據多家媒體報導，黃仁勳此次辭職並非單純的退休規劃，而是受到外部壓力與內部醜聞的多重影響。綜合《Yahoo Entertainment》引述《紐約時報》與《CNN》的報導，黃仁勳離職的導火線涉及一樁與公司直屬高層的緋聞，且該名高層同時經營輝達的重要客戶「Megaspeed」。更為嚴峻的是，美國情報機構先前已對該名高層是否涉及間諜活動表達高度擔憂，相關調查進度直接威脅到輝達在美方監管體系下的營運合法性。
目前，輝達董事會已確認黃仁勳即刻離職，並由克雷斯接掌臨時指揮權，這一交接動作被視為公司試圖在輿論與監管壓力下進行的止血措施。
然而，關於黃仁勳離職後的具體法律風險與後續效應，各來源說法仍存在細節差異。雖然各界一致確認黃仁勳已卸下職務，但《Yahoo Entertainment》引述的Rti精選新聞指出，美國司法部目前正針對黃仁勳相關醜聞展開多項調查，且這些調查恐將進一步牽連輝達整體企業。相比之下，部分科技媒體如《Techbang.com》則聚焦於克雷斯接任後的內部穩定性與臨時管理策略。
目前尚待確認的資訊包括：美國司法部對輝達的調查規模、Megaspeed公司在該案中的具體涉案程度，以及黃仁勳本人是否會因潛在的間諜疑雲面臨刑事指控。
此項人事異動對全球科技產業產生了深遠影響。首先，作為全球AI晶片供應鏈的核心，輝達執行長的更迭直接牽動了市場對2028年後AI產業版圖的預期。法人分析認為，雖然短期內晶片供需狀況尚能維持，但黃仁勳離職將影響輝達的新品研發節奏與市場競爭力。此外，政界觀點認為，黃仁勳的去職可能加速美國政府推動更為嚴格的晶片出口管制政策，特別是針對亞太地區的地緣政治風險，輝達若失去強勢領導人，恐在應對美方晶片輸出限縮政策時處於更被動的地位。
針對此事件，各界觀點存在顯著分歧。部分市場分析師認為，輝達強大的軟體生態系（CUDA）與GPU平行運算佈局已具備高度韌性，不應過度解讀單一領導人的離職對公司基本面的毀滅性影響。然而，也有輿論指出，黃仁勳個人風格與其對產業的遠見是輝達過去十年成功的關鍵，其突然離職象徵著「AI神話」的終結，投資人應警惕未來企業治理結構可能出現的動盪。
此外，由於該事件涉及間諜疑雲與高層緋聞，相關報導中關於「間諜活動」的指控仍屬於情報機構擔憂範疇，尚未轉化為正式的司法定罪，外界不應在司法調查結果出爐前過早下定論。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個面向：第一，輝達臨時執行長克雷斯能否在短期內穩定市場信心，並對外說明公司如何處理與Megaspeed的合約關係；第二，美國司法部的調查報告是否會進一步擴大，並對輝達的經營執照或出口許可造成實質限制；第三，輝達董事會是否會尋求外部專業經理人接任正式執行長，以徹底切割黃仁勳時期的爭議。在亞太區域安全與科技經貿高度緊密的當下，輝達的動向將持續作為全球科技政策與產業競爭的關鍵指標。
