  # A Dual Observation: The Timeless Appeal of NES Classic Games and the Geopolitical Situation in East Asia
  
  > This article focuses on the enduring charm of classic NES games from nearly forty years ago, while simultaneously exploring the latest diplomatic friction between Japan and Russia regarding the Northern Territories dispute in East Asian geopolitics, analyzing the impact of both on cultural and policy levels.
  
  In the context of the contemporary video game industry, which is highly reliant on 4K resolution, ray tracing technology, and open-world experiences that often span hundreds of hours, 1980s Nintendo Entertainment System (NES / Famicom) games are often viewed as products of limited hardware performance, and are even labeled by some younger players as "tears of an era" with stiff controls.
  
  However, according to Techbang, which cited the foreign media outlet Polygon, even after nearly forty years of technological iteration, there are still 10 classic NES titles that remain highly playable today due to their pure game design logic and ultimate control feel, enough to make modern players willing to invest the time to complete them from start to finish. This phenomenon reflects the core value of game design: that within the shackles of limited 8-bit computing, precise level configuration and rhythm control can successfully overcome hardware limitations, allowing their charm to transcend generations.
  
  Regarding the enduring phenomenon of NES games, multiple media outlets and industry analyses have confirmed that the reason these classics remain timeless is not merely supported by nostalgia. The core reason lies in the fact that developers in the extremely restricted hardware environment of the time had to condense the essence of game mechanics to the extreme. Unlike modern games that emphasize visual spectacle and grand narratives, these 8-bit classics focus more on the direct interactive feedback between the player and the system. This "purity" allows the games to remain challenging and rewarding even after the visual filters are removed.
  
  This trend shows that while the gaming industry pursues cutting-edge technology, the reproduction of and homage to fundamental design logic have become important pillars for current independent game development and the retro gaming market.
  
  However, there are still differences in subjective perception among various sources regarding the definition of which games possess "modern playability." Some viewpoints suggest that modern players are accustomed to auto-saves and difficulty adjustments, and may face adaptation barriers regarding the high difficulty and punishment mechanisms of the NES era; another perspective emphasizes that it is precisely this uncompromising difficulty design that constitutes the soul of these works. Furthermore, there is currently no unified quantitative standard for whether some games have been overly romanticized due to nostalgia. Such discussions reflect that when evaluating "classics," the game criticism community often needs to strike a balance between historical status and the modern player experience, and this gray area in evaluation criteria is a focal point of ongoing debate in game history research.
  
  On the level of East Asian geopolitics, in contrast to the cultural soft power of the gaming industry, is the tense political situation within the region. According to a report by Fedpress, the Japanese government's recent diplomatic stance on the Northern Territories issue (referred to as the Southern Kuril Islands by Russia) has triggered intense friction with the Russian side. Japan's demands for the return of the territories were interpreted by Russian media as a diplomatic misstep, sparking further political war of words. Such geopolitical events have a direct impact on the stability of the East Asian region, not limited to territorial sovereignty disputes, but also involving energy supplies, regional security architectures, and the strategic game between Japan and Russia in the Asia-Pacific region.
  
  This political stalemate and the trend in the gaming industry of pursuing cross-national cultural exchange present vastly different paths of development.
  
  Regarding the aforementioned phenomena, there are significant differences in viewpoints across various sectors. On the cultural level, game enthusiasts believe that the value of classic works lies in their entertainment value that transcends national borders, serving as an important carrier for cultural export; however, on the political level, observers warn against conflating cultural products with geopolitics. The limitation is that while the globalization of the gaming industry can promote cultural exchange, it cannot directly alleviate territorial disputes within the region. Furthermore, when reading related reports, the public should be careful to avoid over-interpreting the political stance of a single media outlet.
  
  For example, the criticism from Russian media regarding Japan's diplomatic moves reflects its domestic political narrative, while Japan's demands represent its long-term diplomatic policy stance; both have their own strategic considerations in the diplomatic game and should not be simplified into a single judgment of right or wrong.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on two major areas: First, whether the gaming industry will continue to mine the design essence of the 8-bit era and transform it into a standard for modern game development, thereby influencing future game design trends; second, whether the evolution of East Asian geopolitics, particularly the further development of Japan-Russia relations regarding the Northern Territories issue, will create substantial obstacles to regional economic cooperation and security dialogue. For the public, continuing to pay attention to the development of these cross-disciplinary issues helps in understanding the complex interaction between cultural soft power and hard power politics in the context of globalization. In the future, as technology advances and the international situation changes, the influence of these issues will continue to spread, making them worthy of continued tracking by academia and the public.

# 紅白機經典遊戲跨越時空與東亞地緣政治局勢的雙重觀察

> 本文聚焦近四十年前紅白機經典遊戲的長青魅力，並同步探討東亞地緣政治中，日本與俄羅斯關於北方領土爭議的最新外交摩擦，分析兩者在文化與政策層面的影響。


在當代電子遊戲產業高度依賴 4K 畫質、光線追蹤技術以及動輒數百小時開放世界體驗的背景下，1980 年代的任天堂紅白機（NES / Famicom）遊戲，常被視為硬體效能受限下的產物，甚至被部分年輕玩家標籤為操作生硬的「時代眼淚」。
然而，根據《Techbang》引述外媒《Polygon》的評選指出，即便經歷了近四十年的技術迭代，仍有 10 款紅白機經典作品憑藉著純粹的遊戲設計邏輯與極致的操作手感，至今依然具備極高的可玩性，足以讓現代玩家願意投入時間從頭至尾完成通關。這類現象反映了遊戲設計的核心價值，即在有限的 8-bit 運算枷鎖下，透過精密的關卡配置與節奏控制，成功突破了硬體限制，使其魅力得以跨越世代。
針對紅白機遊戲的長青現象，多家媒體與產業分析共同確認的事實在於，這些經典作品之所以能歷久彌新，並非僅靠懷舊情懷支撐。核心原因在於其開發者在當時極端受限的硬體環境中，必須將遊戲機制的精華濃縮至極致。不同於現代遊戲強調視覺震撼與龐大敘事，這些 8-bit 經典作更注重玩家與系統間的直接互動反饋。這種「純粹性」使得遊戲在脫離了畫質濾鏡後，依然具備挑戰性與成就感。
這股趨勢顯示，遊戲產業在追求尖端科技的同時，對於基礎設計邏輯的重現與致敬，已成為當前獨立遊戲開發與復古遊戲市場的重要支柱。
然而，關於哪些遊戲具備「現代可玩性」的定義，各方來源仍存在主觀認知的差異。部分觀點認為，現代玩家習慣了自動存檔與難度調節，對於紅白機時代的高難度與懲罰機制可能產生適應障礙；另一派觀點則強調，正是這種不妥協的難度設計，才構成了這些作品的靈魂。此外，針對部分遊戲是否因情懷而過度美化，目前尚無統一的量化標準。這類討論反映了遊戲評論界在評估「經典」時，往往需要在歷史地位與現代玩家體驗之間取得平衡，而這種評估標準的模糊地帶，正是遊戲史研究中持續爭論的焦點。
在東亞地緣政治層面，與遊戲產業的文化軟實力形成對比的，是區域內緊張的政治局勢。根據《Fedpress》報導，日本政府近期在北方領土（俄方稱南千島群島）議題上的外交表態，引發了與俄羅斯方面的激烈摩擦。日方針對領土歸還的要求，被俄方媒體解讀為外交策略上的失誤，並引發了進一步的政治口水戰。此類地緣政治事件對於東亞區域的穩定具有直接影響，不僅限於領土主權爭議，更牽涉到能源供應、區域安全架構以及日俄兩國在亞太地區的戰略博弈。
這種政治上的僵局與遊戲產業中追求跨國文化交流的趨勢，呈現出截然不同的發展路徑。
針對上述現象，各界觀點存在顯著分歧。在文化層面，遊戲愛好者認為經典作品的價值在於其超越國界的娛樂性，能作為文化輸出的重要載體；但在政治層面，觀察家則警告不應將文化產品與地緣政治混為一談。限制在於，遊戲產業的全球化趨勢雖能促進文化交流，但無法直接緩解區域內的領土糾紛。此外，公眾在閱讀相關報導時，需注意避免過度解讀單一媒體的政治立場。
例如俄方媒體對日方外交舉措的批評，反映了其國內的政治敘事，而日方的訴求則代表了其長期的外交政策立場，兩者在外交博弈中均有各自的戰略考量，不應被簡化為單一的對錯論斷。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於兩大領域：首先，遊戲產業是否會持續挖掘 8-bit 時代的設計精髓，並將其轉化為現代遊戲開發的標準，進而影響未來遊戲的設計趨勢；其次，東亞地緣政治的演變，特別是日俄關係在北方領土議題上的進一步發展，是否會對區域經濟合作與安全對話產生實質性的阻礙。對於公眾而言，持續關注這些跨領域議題的發展，有助於理解全球化背景下，文化軟實力與硬實力政治之間的複雜互動。未來，隨著技術進步與國際局勢的變動，這些議題的影響力將持續擴散，值得學界與大眾持續追蹤。
