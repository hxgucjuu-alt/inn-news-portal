《巨人之行》（March of Giants）是一款全新的4v4 MOBA遊戲，正成為備受期待的作品，早期預覽顯示它可能是十年來該類型中最有潛力的遊戲之一。Windows Central在造訪Ubisoft蒙特婁工作室並進行實機測試後，將該遊戲描述為「真正獨特」，並有能力挑戰業界領先者。來自各遊戲媒體的報導一致強調其擺脫傳統MOBA機制，轉而追求深度和創造性。

儘管包括Dot Esports和GameLoop.gg在內的多份報導均指出Ubisoft是《巨人之行》的發行商和開發商，但Ubisoft自家網站上的一份摘要卻提及這是一款「來自Amazon Games的大膽全新4v4戰爭MOBA」。儘管存在這一差異，但預覽報導的普遍共識指向Ubisoft的重大參與，GameLoop.gg更明確指出「Ubisoft已確認」遊戲的相關細節。該遊戲也因其獨特的柴油龐克美學而受到關注。

渴望體驗《巨人之行》的玩家將有多個機會。據報導，一項封閉Alpha測試已於2025年9月2日開始。最近，Ubisoft已確認將於2026年10月7日至10月18日在Steam上進行先鋒測試。展望未來，根據GameLoop.gg的報導，該遊戲計劃於2027年夏季推出搶先體驗版。這些測試階段預計將在遊戲正式發布前進一步完善。

## English edition

“March of Giants,” a new 4v4 MOBA, is emerging as a highly anticipated title, with early previews suggesting it could be one of the most promising games in its genre in a decade. Windows Central, following a visit to Ubisoft Montreal and a hands-on test, described the game as “genuinely unique” and capable of challenging industry leaders. Reports from various gaming outlets consistently highlight its departure from traditional MOBA mechanics, favoring depth and creativity.

While multiple reports, including those from Dot Esports and GameLoop.gg, identify Ubisoft as the publisher and developer of “March of Giants,” a summary from Ubisoft’s own website mentions “a bold new 4v4 War MOBA from Amazon Games.” Despite this discrepancy, the broader consensus from preview coverage points to Ubisoft’s significant involvement, with GameLoop.gg specifically stating that “Ubisoft has confirmed” details regarding the game. The game is also noted for its distinctive dieselpunk aesthetic.

Players eager to experience “March of Giants” will have several opportunities. A Closed Alpha reportedly began on September 2, 2025. More recently, Ubisoft has confirmed a Pioneer Playtest on Steam, scheduled from October 7 to October 18, 2026. Looking ahead, the game is slated for an Early Access launch in summer 2027, according to GameLoop.gg. These testing phases are expected to further refine the game ahead of its full release.
