  # Fujifilm Expands Tape Storage Production; AI Giants Join Forces to Block Reverse Engineering Tools
  
  > Fujifilm has announced a $2.05 billion investment to expand magnetic tape production capacity to meet the demand for AI cold data, while OpenAI and Anthropic have joined forces to block reverse engineering frameworks to prevent security risks arising from the misuse of generative AI technology.
  
  The global technology industry has recently seen significant developments in both hardware infrastructure and software security governance. On November 5, 2026, Fujifilm officially announced that it would invest $64 million (approximately NT$2.05 billion) to expand its production base in Odawara, Kanagawa Prefecture, Japan, with the goal of increasing its magnetic tape storage production capacity to five times its current level.
  
  Meanwhile, two giants in the artificial intelligence field, OpenAI and Anthropic, took joint action on November 4 to comprehensively block an automated framework known as "Reverse Everything (REA)" and launched large-scale bans on developer accounts utilizing AI for software reverse engineering, aiming to curb security threats where generative AI is misused to crack code and exploit system vulnerabilities.
  
  In terms of hardware storage, the core of Fujifilm's expansion is to address the massive data storage demand brought about by the explosion of generative AI. As global data centers face storage budget pressures, magnetic tape storage—characterized by high density, offline security, and low energy consumption—is once again receiving attention. The latest generation of tape technology developed by Fujifilm has reached a capacity of 100TB per cartridge, becoming a key solution for "cold data" storage. According to public information, the expansion plan will be implemented in phases between fiscal years 2026 and 2028, aiming to resolve infrastructure bottlenecks faced by cloud service providers in the AI era.
  
  The software security sector is experiencing high tension. The joint blocking action by OpenAI and Anthropic stems primarily from the extremely high cracking efficiency demonstrated by AI reverse engineering tools in a short period, such as showing abnormal attack capabilities when uncovering major vulnerabilities in PHP systems that had existed for a decade. Anthropic has launched a real-time security defense classifier in its system specifically to block the REA framework; OpenAI has adopted more direct account management measures, suspending accounts involved in related development activities on the grounds of "network abuse."
  
  In addition, Apple released an iOS developer beta on November 3 that officially includes support for Traditional Chinese Siri AI, indicating progress in AI application language localization and multimodal interaction functions.
  
  The aforementioned events have multiple impacts on the global technology ecosystem. Fujifilm's capacity expansion is expected to alleviate cost pressures on data centers for long-term data storage, further consolidating the position of magnetic tape in the cold storage market. The defensive measures taken by OpenAI and Anthropic mark the AI industry's entry into a new phase of "security governance priority," which will have a profound impact on the tool usage norms for software developers and security researchers. Apple's Traditional Chinese Siri update signals a technological breakthrough in AI personal assistants regarding cross-language context understanding and screen perception capabilities, which will further change the interaction patterns of end-users.
  
  However, there are still different perspectives and limitations regarding these developments. Regarding the ban on AI reverse engineering, some developers worry that overly strict defense mechanisms might limit legitimate security research and vulnerability patching work; how to strike a balance between security defense and technological innovation remains a challenge for the industry. Furthermore, while Fujifilm's tape expansion plan can solve cold data storage issues, it provides no direct benefit for "hot data" storage needs that require real-time access, and the market should not view this expansion as a panacea for all data storage problems.
  
  As for the Traditional Chinese support for Siri AI, it is currently still in the developer testing stage, and the stability and popularity of the final features will depend on the subsequent release of the official version.
  
  Key points for future observation include whether Fujifilm can meet its tape production capacity targets by 2028 as scheduled and effectively reduce operating costs for global data centers; at the same time, whether the blocking actions by OpenAI and Anthropic will trigger a backlash from the open-source community, potentially leading to an underground development trend for AI reverse engineering tools. Additionally, Apple's deployment progress for Traditional Chinese Siri AI and whether it will further open up more context-aware functions in the future are also important indicators for observing the localization and personalized development of AI applications.
  
  As the technology industry pursues high performance and powerful features, balancing the sustainability of infrastructure with the security of the software ecosystem will be a core issue for global technological development in the coming years.

# 富士軟片擴產磁帶儲存與 AI 巨頭聯手封殺逆向工程工具

> 富士軟片宣佈斥資 20 億元擴充磁帶產能以應對 AI 冷資料需求，同時 OpenAI 與 Anthropic 聯手封殺逆向工程框架，以防範生成式 AI 技術濫用帶來的資安風險。


全球科技產業近期在硬體基礎設施與軟體安全治理兩端均出現重大進展。富士軟片（Fujifilm）於 2026 年 11 月 5 日正式宣佈，將投入 6,400 萬美元（約 20.5 億元新臺幣）擴建位於日本神奈川縣小田原市的生產基地，目標將磁帶儲存產能提升至現有水準的五倍。
與此同時，人工智慧領域的兩大巨頭 OpenAI 與 Anthropic 於 11 月 4 日採取聯合行動，全面封鎖名為「萬物反編譯（REA）」的自動化框架，並針對利用 AI 進行軟體逆向工程的開發者帳號進行大規模封禁，旨在遏止生成式 AI 被濫用於破解程式碼與挖掘系統漏洞的資安威脅。
在硬體儲存方面，富士軟片此次擴產的核心在於應對生成式 AI 爆發帶來的海量資料儲存需求。隨著全球資料中心面臨儲存預算壓力，具備高密度、離線安全及低能耗特性的磁帶儲存再次受到重視。富士軟片研發的最新一代磁帶技術，單捲容量已達到 100TB，成為應對「冷資料（Cold Data）」儲存的關鍵解決方案。根據公開資訊，該擴產計畫將於 2026 至 2028 財年期間分階段執行，旨在解決雲端服務商在 AI 時代面臨的基礎設施瓶頸。
軟體安全領域則呈現高度緊張態勢。OpenAI 與 Anthropic 的聯合封殺行動，主要源於 AI 逆向工程工具在短時間內展現出極高的破解效率，例如在挖掘 PHP 系統長達十年的重大漏洞時表現出異常的攻擊能力。Anthropic 已於系統中上線即時資安防禦分類器，專門針對 REA 框架進行阻斷；OpenAI 則採取更為直接的帳號管理手段，以「網路濫用」為由，對涉及相關開發活動的帳號進行處置。
此外，蘋果公司（Apple）也在 11 月 3 日釋出 iOS 開發者測試版，正式納入繁體中文 Siri AI 支援，顯示 AI 應用在語言在地化與多模態互動功能的推進。
上述事件對全球科技生態產生多重影響。富士軟片的產能擴張預計將緩解資料中心在長期資料儲存上的成本壓力，進一步鞏固磁帶在冷儲存市場的地位。而 OpenAI 與 Anthropic 的防禦措施，則標誌著 AI 產業進入「安全治理優先」的新階段，這將對軟體開發者與資安研究人員的工具使用規範產生深遠影響。蘋果的繁體中文 Siri 更新，則預示著 AI 個人助理在跨語言情境理解與螢幕感知能力上的技術突破，將進一步改變終端使用者的互動模式。
然而，各界對此仍有不同觀點與限制。針對 AI 逆向工程的封殺，部分開發者擔心過度嚴格的防禦機制可能限制合法的資安研究與漏洞修補工作，如何在資安防禦與技術創新之間取得平衡，仍是產業面臨的挑戰。此外，富士軟片的磁帶擴產計畫雖然能解決冷資料儲存問題，但對於需要即時讀取的「熱資料」儲存需求並無直接助益，市場不應將其擴產視為解決所有資料儲存問題的萬靈丹。
至於 Siri AI 的繁體中文支援，目前仍處於開發者測試階段，最終功能的穩定性與普及程度仍需視後續正式版本發布而定。
後續觀察重點在於，富士軟片的磁帶產能擴張是否能如期在 2028 年前達成目標，並有效降低全球資料中心的營運成本；同時，OpenAI 與 Anthropic 的封殺行動是否會引發開源社群的反彈，進而導致 AI 逆向工程工具出現地下化的發展趨勢。此外，蘋果在繁體中文 Siri AI 的部署進度，以及未來是否會進一步開放更多情境感知功能，亦是觀察 AI 應用在地化與個人化發展的重要指標。
科技產業在追求高效能與強大功能的同時，如何兼顧基礎設施的永續性與軟體生態的安全性，將是未來數年全球科技發展的核心議題。
