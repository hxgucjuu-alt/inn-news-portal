  # Fact-Checking and Debunking the Phenomenon of Non-Mixing Waters at the Pacific and Atlantic Boundary
  
  > Regarding the widely circulated online imagery of the Pacific and Atlantic oceans "not mixing" at their boundary, the scientific community has confirmed this claim to be a misunderstanding. It is a misinterpretation of oceanographic physical phenomena and is entirely distinct from recent conspiracy theories circulating online.
  
  The scientific community has long been dedicated to clarifying public misconceptions regarding ocean geography. One of the most famous myths concerns whether the waters at the boundary of the Pacific and Atlantic oceans remain "unmixed" due to differences in density and salinity. According to a report by BBC Science Focus Magazine, a video filmed in 2015 has been circulating online, showing two bodies of water with distinctly different colors separated by a sharp boundary. This video has long been mislabeled as the meeting point of the Pacific and Atlantic oceans.
  
  Scientific institutions point out that while this visual effect is real, it does not occur at the boundary of the two major oceans. Instead, it is a physical phenomenon caused by river mouths or glacial meltwater meeting the ocean under specific environmental conditions, and it has nothing to do with the large-scale water mixing mechanisms between oceans.
  
  According to multiple scientific sources, ocean waters do indeed undergo stratification due to differences in temperature, salinity, and density, but the mixing of water between oceans is a continuous and dynamic process. Scientists emphasize that the global ocean system is a fluid entity; water exchange between the Pacific and Atlantic oceans occurs primarily through the Antarctic Circumpolar Current, rather than being "static and unmixed" as the online video suggests.
  
  In a 2020 fact-checking report, BBC Science Focus Magazine explicitly stated that the "boundary line" images seen by the public on social media are mostly visual illusions caused by differences in suspended sediment concentrations when freshwater meets seawater, and are not directly related to oceanic geographical boundaries.
  
  However, in the process of online information dissemination, information regarding geography and government secrets often suffers from confusion and source distortion. In stark contrast to the aforementioned oceanographic discussion is the conspiracy theory information recently circulating on online forums and some media outlets, such as The Gateway Pundit. The report cites a self-proclaimed former housing official claiming that the U.S. government has secretly misappropriated $21 trillion to build underground refuge cities for the elite to cope with a so-called "near-extinction event."
  
  Such information is fundamentally different in nature from oceanographic topics; the latter belongs to the realm of verifiable natural science, while the former consists of allegations lacking concrete evidence. When these two are discussed side-by-side in the online ecosystem, it easily leads to confusion among readers regarding the authenticity of the information.
  
  The mixing of this type of online information has had a tangible impact on public perception. Regarding climate and environmental policy, public misconceptions about marine ecology may lead to misunderstandings of scientific research on how global climate change affects ocean circulation, thereby affecting the level of attention paid to the crises faced by Oceania regions, such as Pacific Island nations, due to rising sea levels. When scientific facts are obscured by sensational visual imagery or conspiracy theories, public trust in accurate climate change data is challenged. Furthermore, the widespread dissemination of such information reflects the current state of the modern digital environment, where scientific communication competes with unverified speculation in the same information stream, posing a certain obstacle to education and the promotion of public policy.
  
  In response to these phenomena, experts offer different perspectives and emphasize limitations. The scientific community urges the public to maintain a cautious attitude toward any natural geographical imagery labeled as a "spectacle" and to prioritize explanations from authoritative scientific institutions, avoiding the over-mystification or politicization of natural phenomena. At the same time, allegations such as "secret government underground cities," which lack verification and concrete evidence, should be treated as unverified rumors and should not be conflated with rigorous scientific topics. Over-interpreting such information not only fails to help in understanding the complexity of global climate change but may instead exacerbate social distrust in public policy.
  
  The limitation of scientific communication lies in the fact that its explanatory process is often relatively mundane and struggles to compete for traffic against sensationalist conspiracy theories, which is a major challenge currently facing science education.

Second, regarding online information verification mechanisms, one should observe how various platforms distinguish between "scientific facts" and "unverified conspiracy theories" to reduce information pollution. Finally, for Oceania and Pacific Island nations, accurate climate science information is crucial. Future observations should focus on how the international community, when addressing climate change issues, transmits data through more credible channels to ensure that policymakers and the public can work together to address the survival challenges brought by climate change based on real scientific evidence.

# 網路流傳太平洋與大西洋交界不融合現象之事實查核與闢謠

> 針對網路上廣泛流傳太平洋與大西洋交界處海水「互不相融」的視覺影像，科學界已證實該說法為誤解，實為海洋物理現象的錯誤解讀，並與近期網路流傳的陰謀論資訊截然不同。


科學界長期以來致力於釐清大眾對於海洋地理的誤解，其中最為著名的迷思之一，即是關於太平洋與大西洋交界處海水是否會因為密度、鹽度差異而呈現「互不相融」的現象。根據 BBC Science Focus Magazine 的報導，網路上流傳一段拍攝於 2015 年的影片，畫面中顯示兩片顏色明顯不同的海水在交界處呈現出涇渭分明的界線，該影片長期以來被錯誤標籤為太平洋與大西洋的交會點。
科學機構指出，這種視覺效果雖然真實存在，但並非發生在兩大洋的交界處，而是由特定環境條件下的河流入海口或冰川融水與海洋交匯所造成的物理現象，與大洋之間大規模的水體混合機制完全無關。
根據多個科學來源的共同確認，海洋水體確實會因為溫度、鹽度與密度的不同而產生分層現象，但大洋之間的水體混合是一個持續且動態的過程。科學家強調，全球海洋系統是一個流動的整體，太平洋與大西洋之間的水體交換主要透過南冰洋的繞極流進行，並非如網路影片所暗示的「靜止不混合」。
BBC Science Focus Magazine 在 2020 年發布的查核報告中明確指出，大眾在社群媒體上看到的「分界線」影像，多半是淡水與海水交匯時，因懸浮沉積物濃度差異所產生的視覺錯覺，與大洋地理邊界無直接關聯。
然而，在網路資訊傳播的過程中，關於地理與政府機密的資訊常出現資訊混淆與來源失真的情況。與上述海洋科學討論形成強烈對比的，是近期在網路論壇與部分媒體如 The Gateway Pundit 上流傳的陰謀論資訊。該報導引述了一名自稱前住房官員的說法，指稱美國政府秘密挪用 21 兆美元，在地下建設供菁英階層使用的避難城市，以應對所謂的「近乎滅絕事件」。
此類資訊與海洋科學議題在性質上完全不同，後者屬於可驗證的自然科學範疇，而前者則屬於缺乏具體證據支援的指控，兩者在網路生態中被並列討論，容易導致閱聽人對資訊真實性的判斷產生混淆。
這類網路資訊的混雜對社會大眾的認知造成了具體影響。在氣候與環境政策方面，大眾對於海洋生態的錯誤認知，可能導致對於全球氣候變遷影響海洋環流的科學研究產生誤解，進而影響對太平洋島國等大洋洲地區因海平面上升所面臨危機的關注度。當科學事實被誇張的視覺影像或陰謀論掩蓋時，公眾對於氣候變遷真實資料的信任度亦會受到挑戰。此外，這類資訊的廣泛傳播，也反映了現代數位環境中，科學傳播與未經證實的臆測資訊在同一資訊流中競爭的現狀，對教育與公共政策的推動構成了一定的阻礙。
針對上述現象，專家提出不同觀點並強調限制。科學界呼籲，對於任何標榜「奇觀」的自然地理影像，應保持審慎態度，並優先查詢權威科學機構的解釋，避免將自然現象過度神秘化或政治化。同時，針對如「政府秘密地下城市」等缺乏查核與具體證據的指控，應將其視為未經證實的傳言，不應與嚴謹的科學議題混為一談。過度解讀這類資訊不僅無助於理解全球氣候變遷的複雜性，反而可能加劇社會對於公共政策的不信任感。
科學傳播的限制在於其解釋過程往往較為平淡，難以與聳動的陰謀論競爭流量，這也是當前科學教育面臨的重大挑戰。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於資訊素養的提升。首先，應持續追蹤科學機構對於海洋物理現象的科普傳播力度，觀察其是否能有效降低公眾對地理迷思的依賴。其次，針對網路資訊的查核機制，應觀察各類平臺如何區分「科學事實」與「未經證實的陰謀論」，以減少資訊汙染。最後，對於大洋洲與太平洋島國而言，正確的氣候科學資訊至關重要，後續應觀察國際社會在處理氣候變遷議題時，如何透過更具公信力的管道傳遞資料，以確保政策制定者與民眾能基於真實的科學依據，共同應對氣候變遷帶來的生存挑戰。
