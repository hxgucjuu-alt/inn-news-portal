  # Escalating Middle East Conflict and Energy Market Volatility: U.S. Military Pressure and Diplomatic Mediation
  
  > U.S. Presidential Advisor Massad Boulos stated that the Trump administration is committed to ending the war in Iran as soon as possible. Meanwhile, the situation in the Middle East has caused shocks to the global energy supply chain, leading to a surge in Russian crude oil prices, while U.S. military deployments in the region face severe pressure.
  
  The conflict in the Middle East has recently reached a critical turning point, involving not only the restructuring of the regional security architecture but also creating a chain reaction affecting global public health and energy stability. According to a report by *The Intercept*, the U.S. military is on the verge of operational collapse under the sustained pressure of the war in Iran. Multiple U.S. officials familiar with Middle East operations have pointed out that U.S. military bases and frontline troops are enduring unprecedented logistical and operational burdens. Meanwhile, Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian publicly condemned U.S. hegemony in mid-September, accusing the United States of using intimidation to hinder other countries from engaging in substantive cooperation with Tehran.
  
  Against this backdrop, the Middle East has become not only a geopolitical arena but also the core region for adjustments in global energy markets and security policies.
  
  Multiple sources have confirmed that the ongoing conflict in the Middle East has had a significant impact on the global energy supply chain. According to *The Moscow Times*, as Middle East supply chains are disrupted by the war, global market demand for alternative energy has surged, causing the price of ESPO crude oil in Russia's Far East to soar, exceeding $120 per barrel. This data reflects that the supply chain shocks triggered by the Middle East conflict have directly increased procurement costs for major energy importers such as China. Furthermore, the U.S. government has begun coordinating with Persian Gulf nations in an attempt to alleviate the dual pressure on military and energy supplies, indicating that energy security in the Middle East is now deeply tied to U.S. military strategy.
  
  On the diplomatic front, there are clear differences regarding the specific path to ending the war. Massad Boulos, a senior advisor to U.S. President Trump, told *Euronews* that the Trump administration is actively taking action to end the months-long war in Iran as soon as possible, emphasizing that Trump is a leader who "delivers on his promises." However, Iranian officials remain highly skeptical of U.S. mediation intentions. President Pezeshkian has publicly criticized the U.S. for bullying other nations, implying that in the current diplomatic climate, even if governments privately lean toward supporting Tehran, they find it difficult to take public action in practice due to fear of U.S. pressure.
  
  Additionally, *The Conversation* points out that European countries, such as Italy, are attempting to use pragmatic diplomacy to politically recalibrate between regional powers like Saudi Arabia and Palestine, seeking a new diplomatic balance point amidst the chaotic situation.
  
  This situation has had a direct impact on public health and social stability in the Middle East. The pressure of military deployments caused by the prolonged war is not limited to the U.S. military but also affects infrastructure and medical resource supplies within the region. With soaring oil prices and supply chain disruptions, the economic pressure on countries in the region has increased dramatically, which may further weaken the ability of these governments to invest in the public health sector. While U.S. military coordination with various countries in the Persian Gulf is intended to maintain regional security, this highly militarized response strategy may also crowd out the allocation of resources for civilian infrastructure, leaving vulnerable groups in the region facing greater survival risks.
  
  Although all parties have signaled diplomatic mediation, analysts warn against over-interpreting current ceasefire intentions. The "collapse" risk currently faced by the U.S. military reflects that the war has exceeded expected logistical loads, which may be one of the drivers pushing the U.S. to seek a diplomatic solution. However, Iran's deep distrust of U.S. hegemony remains a major obstacle at the negotiating table. Furthermore, while the political recalibration strategies of countries like Italy demonstrate European concern for regional stability, it remains to be seen whether their influence can effectively counterbalance the direct confrontation between the U.S. and Iran. All parties should be wary of the secondary inflation risks that may be brought about by energy price fluctuations, as well as the chronic damage caused by the prolonged war to regional public health systems.
  
  Follow-up observations should focus on three areas: first, whether U.S. military deployments in the Persian Gulf will undergo a strategic contraction due to logistical pressure; second, whether Iran and Persian Gulf nations will be forced to adjust their diplomatic stances in the face of soaring energy prices, thereby affecting political recalibration within the region; and finally, whether the international community can propose concrete coordination plans for the energy and humanitarian crises triggered by the Middle East conflict. As oil prices continue to hover at high levels, the stability of the global energy market will become a key indicator of whether the situation in the Middle East is moving toward de-escalation.

# 中東戰局升溫與能源市場動盪：美國軍事壓力與外交斡旋並行

> 美國總統顧問馬薩德·布洛斯表示川普政府致力於儘速結束伊朗戰事，與此同時，中東局勢導致全球能源供應鏈震盪，俄羅斯原油價格飆升，美軍在中東的作戰部署亦面臨嚴峻壓力。


中東地區的戰事近期進入關鍵轉折點，不僅涉及區域安全架構的重組，更對全球公共衛生與能源穩定構成連鎖反應。根據《The Intercept》報導，美國軍方在伊朗戰事的持續壓力下，已處於作戰能力崩潰的邊緣，多名熟悉中東行動的美國官員指出，美軍基地與前線部隊正承受前所未有的後勤與作戰負荷。與此同時，伊朗總統佩澤希齊揚（Masoud Pezeshkian）於九月中旬公開譴責美國的霸權行為，指控美國透過威嚇手段阻礙其他國家與德黑蘭進行實質合作。
此一背景下，中東不僅是地緣政治的角力場，更成為全球能源市場與安全政策調整的核心區域。
多項來源共同確認，中東戰事的持續已對全球能源供應鏈產生顯著衝擊。據《The Moscow Times》報導，由於中東供應鏈受到戰事幹擾，全球市場對替代能源的需求大增，導致俄羅斯遠東地區的 ESPO 原油價格飆升，每桶價格已突破 120 美元。這一資料反映出中東戰事引發的供應鏈震盪，已直接推升了中國等主要能源進口國的採購成本。此外，美國政府已開始與波斯灣國家進行協調，試圖緩解軍事與能源供應上的雙重壓力，這顯示出中東地區的能源安全已與美方的軍事戰略深度繫結。
在外交層面，各方對於結束戰事的具體路徑存在明顯差異。美國總統川普的高階顧問馬薩德·布洛斯（Massad Boulos）向《Euronews》表示，川普政府正積極採取行動，致力於儘速終結這場長達數月的伊朗戰事，並強調川普是一位「說到做到」的領導人。然而，伊朗官方對於美國的斡旋意圖抱持高度懷疑，佩澤希齊揚總統公開批評美國對其他國家進行霸凌，暗示在當前的外交氛圍下，各國政府即便私下傾向支援德黑蘭，在實務上也因畏懼美國壓力而難以採取公開行動。
此外，《The Conversation》指出，義大利等歐洲國家正試圖透過務實外交，在沙烏地阿拉伯與巴勒斯坦等區域勢力間進行政治校準，試圖在混亂的局勢中尋求新的外交平衡點。
此一局勢對中東地區的公共衛生與社會穩定造成了直接影響。長期戰事導致的軍事部署壓力，不僅限於美軍，更波及區域內的基礎設施與醫療資源供應。隨著油價飆升與供應鏈中斷，區域內各國的經濟壓力劇增，可能進一步削弱各國政府在公共衛生領域的投入能力。美軍在波斯灣地區與各國的軍事協調，雖然旨在維持區域安全，但這種高度軍事化的應對策略，亦可能排擠到民生基礎建設的資源分配，導致區域內脆弱群體面臨更大的生存風險。
儘管各方釋出外交斡旋的訊號，但分析家提醒，不應過度解讀當前的停火意圖。目前美國軍方所面臨的「崩潰」風險，反映出戰事已超出預期的後勤負荷，這或許是推動美方尋求外交解決的動力之一，但伊朗方面對於美國霸權的強烈不信任感，仍是談判桌上的巨大障礙。此外，義大利等國的政治校準策略，雖然展現了歐洲國家對於區域穩定的關切，但其影響力能否有效制衡美伊之間的直接對抗，仍有待觀察。各界應警惕能源價格波動可能帶來的二次通膨風險，以及戰事長期化對區域公共衛生體系造成的慢性損害。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於三方面：首先是美軍在波斯灣的軍事部署是否會因後勤壓力而進行戰略性收縮；其次是伊朗與波斯灣國家在面對能源價格飆升時，是否會被迫調整其外交立場，進而影響區域內的政治校準；最後則是國際社會對於中東戰事引發的能源與人道危機，是否能提出具體的協調方案。隨著油價持續在高位徘徊，全球能源市場的穩定性將成為檢驗中東局勢是否走向緩和的關鍵指標。
