根據多家體育與財經媒體報導，南非二十20板球聯賽（SA20）公布的拍賣候選名單中，沒有任何一名巴基斯坦板球球員入列。相關名單的釋出引發了外界對各球團選秀考量以及潛在因素的廣泛討論。

媒體報導指出，SA20聯賽的球隊所有權與印度超級板球聯賽（IPL）特許經營權存在密切關聯，這被外界視為可能影響球員選拔的未公開因素之一。不過，聯賽官方與相關球團並未就具體未入選原因作出排除特定國籍球員的定論。

不同媒體所引用的候選名單數據存在差異。報導指出，部分消息稱在約翰尼斯堡舉行的拍賣名單包含541名球員，而另有報導則指出候選名單規模為156人。針對入選人數與賽季年份的衝突資訊，仍有待官方進一步釐清。

## English edition

According to multiple media reports, not a single Pakistani cricketer was included in the shortlisted auction pool for South Africa's SA20 cricket league. The release of the shortlist has prompted discussions regarding franchise selection strategies and potential underlying factors.

Reports highlighted that the ownership ties between SA20 franchises and Indian Premier League (IPL) teams have drawn scrutiny as an unspoken factor in team selection dynamics. However, neither the league nor the franchises have confirmed that players were excluded specifically due to national background.

There are discrepancies among sources regarding the auction numbers and specific cycles. While some reports indicated a shortlist of 541 players ahead of an auction in Johannesburg, others cited an auction pool of 156 players. The differing figures reflect conflicting reporting across various outlets.
