根據多個報導，由東尼·吉爾羅伊執導、佩德羅·帕斯卡主演的電影《巨獸！》（Behemoth!）近期獲得影評界的廣泛關注。該片在紐約影展（NYFF）上亮相，深入探討了音樂、記憶以及家族傳承的重擔等核心主題。

影評指出，該片以其獨特的方式，呈現了音樂如何喚起記憶的強烈、短暫且幾乎是潛意識的過程，以及它在瞬間所能傳遞的奇異情感距離。報導形容《巨獸！》是一封寫給電影配樂的情書，同時也致敬了那些複雜而充滿熱情的音樂家。

對於影片的評價，資料顯示存在不同觀點。有評論讚揚其將音樂、記憶與家族傳承的重擔轉化為一場優雅的漸強，並指出其結構獨特。然而，也有報導將其描述為一項「蒼白的角色研究」或「無調性戲劇」，認為劇情發展時有停滯。佩德羅·帕斯卡在片中飾演一位來自音樂世家的傑出大提琴家，其表演被認為暗示了深沉的靈魂。

除了佩德羅·帕斯卡，影片中也出現了奧莉維亞·魏爾德（Olivia Wilde）和伊娃·維克托（Eva Victor）等演員。儘管評價不一，但《巨獸！》無疑引發了關於藝術、記憶與個人歷史之間複雜關係的討論。

## English edition

Tony Gilroy's film 'Behemoth!', starring Pedro Pascal, has recently garnered significant attention from film critics, according to multiple reports. Premiering at the New York Film Festival (NYFF), the movie delves into core themes such as music, memory, and the profound burden of family legacy.

Reviews highlight the film's unique approach to portraying how music evokes memory in a powerful, ephemeral, and almost unconscious manner, alongside the strange emotional distance it can traverse instantly. 'Behemoth!' is described as a love letter to movie scores, simultaneously honoring the complex and passionate musicians who bring them to life.

Critical reception for the film, however, shows varying perspectives. Some reviews praise its ability to transform the themes of music, memory, and family legacy into a graceful crescendo, noting its unique structure. Conversely, other reports characterize it as a "wan character study" or an "atonal drama," suggesting that the narrative frequently stalls. Pedro Pascal, who portrays a virtuoso cellist from a musical dynasty, is noted for hinting at soulful depths in his performance.

In addition to Pedro Pascal, the film also features actors such as Olivia Wilde and Eva Victor. Despite the mixed reviews, 'Behemoth!' has undoubtedly sparked discussions surrounding the intricate relationship between art, memory, and personal history.
