前曼聯後衛馬科斯·羅霍據報導拒絕為其現效力球隊競賽俱樂部進行訓練。資料顯示，這位36歲的球員正積極推動轉會，希望重返其前東家學生隊。此舉旨在迫使俱樂部同意其轉會意願。

根據《曼徹斯特晚報》的報導，羅霍的行動明確表達了他希望離開競賽俱樂部並回到學生隊的願望。多個消息來源，包括The Peoples Person和Yahoo Sports，均引述了《曼徹斯特晚報》的說法，指出羅霍正透過拒絕訓練來施壓。

羅霍此番舉動被視為他試圖促成轉會的策略。報導指出，他對重返學生隊的渴望是此次拒絕訓練行為背後的主要驅動力。目前，競賽俱樂部尚未就羅霍的行為或其轉會請求發表公開聲明。

## English edition

Former Manchester United defender Marcos Rojo is reportedly refusing to train with his current club, Racing Club. Data indicates that the 36-year-old player is actively pushing for a transfer back to his former side, Estudiantes. This action is aimed at compelling the club to agree to his transfer wishes.

According to reports from the Manchester Evening News, Rojo's actions clearly express his desire to leave Racing Club and return to Estudiantes. Multiple sources, including The Peoples Person and Yahoo Sports, have cited the Manchester Evening News, stating that Rojo is applying pressure by refusing to train.

Rojo's move is seen as a strategy to facilitate his transfer. Reports suggest that his strong desire to rejoin Estudiantes is the primary driving force behind his refusal to train. As of now, Racing Club has not issued any public statement regarding Rojo's actions or his transfer request.
