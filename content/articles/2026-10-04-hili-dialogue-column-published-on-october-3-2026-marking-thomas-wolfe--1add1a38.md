2026年10月3日，知名部落格「Why Evolution Is True」發布了其定期專欄「Saturday: Hili dialogue」。該部落格由傑瑞·科因撰寫，內容涵蓋演化與生物學，同時也探討政治、文化和貓等多元主題。此次發布恰逢「CaturSaturday」，並被提及為猶太貓的安息日。

根據資料顯示，此次「Hili 對話」的發布日也紀念了美國作家托馬斯·沃爾夫（Thomas Wolfe, 1900-1938）的生日。沃爾夫被部落格作者譽為其最喜愛的作家之一，儘管他常因「過度寫作」而受到文學界的批評。報導指出，沃爾夫在寫作時力求捕捉美國的語氣和色彩，以至於無法自制，但在其早期作品中表現得相當克制。

「Hili 對話」是「Why Evolution Is True」部落格的一個常設特色，過去也曾於週三發布。Pinterest上的相關資訊指出，該對話可能以幽默的方式探討演化議題，並鼓勵讀者參與討論，以有趣且引人入勝的方式探索演化背後的真相，甚至可能包含有趣的圖片和迷因。這表明該專欄不僅限於嚴肅的科學討論，也融入了文化和娛樂元素。

## English edition

On October 3, 2026, the prominent blog "Why Evolution Is True" published its regular column, "Saturday: Hili dialogue." Written by Jerry Coyne, the blog covers evolution and biology, alongside diverse topics such as politics, culture, and cats. This particular installment coincided with "CaturSaturday" and was noted as shabbos for Jewish cats.

According to the available data, the publication date of this "Hili Dialogue" also marked the birthday of American author Thomas Wolfe (1900-1938). Wolfe is celebrated by the blog's author as one of his favorite writers, despite often being denigrated by the literati for "overwriting." Reports indicate that Wolfe was frantic to capture the tone and color of America in his writing, to the extent that he couldn't stop himself, though he was quite restrained in his first works.

"Hili Dialogue" is a recurring feature on the "Why Evolution Is True" blog, having also appeared on Wednesdays in the past. Related information from Pinterest suggests that these dialogues may explore evolutionary topics in a humorous manner, encouraging readers to join the conversation and discover the truth behind evolution in a fun and engaging way, potentially incorporating funny images and memes. This indicates that the column is not limited to serious scientific discourse but also integrates cultural and entertainment elements.
