根據多家外媒報導，阿根廷足球球星梅西（Lionel Messi）宣布退出國家隊，結束代表阿根廷出戰國際賽事的生涯。這項告別消息隨即在國際體壇引發廣泛關注，球迷與各界人士紛紛對其成就表達讚賞。

資料顯示，梅西的長期宿敵、葡萄牙球星C羅（Cristiano Ronaldo）在梅西最新的Instagram發文下留言致敬。C羅留下了「致上我全部的尊重」（All my respect）等感性言詞，並讚許這位長年對手譜寫了「將永遠流傳的故事」。

媒體指出，這兩位當代足壇頂尖球星在過去十多年間持續在最高水平競爭，此次公開致敬被視為展現長年競爭關係中罕見且深厚的相互尊重，標誌著國際足球一個重要時代的落幕。

## English edition

Argentine football star Lionel Messi has announced his farewell to international soccer, concluding his historic tenure representing the Argentina national team. The news of his departure quickly reverberated across the global sporting world, drawing praise from fans and sports commentators alike.

According to media reports, Cristiano Ronaldo, Messi's longtime rival, paid homage to the Argentine following the announcement by leaving a supportive message on Messi's latest Instagram post. Ronaldo commended his peer with the phrase "All my respect" and praised him for writing a "story that will live on forever."

Reports highlighted that after more than a decade of competing at the highest level of world soccer, this public gesture reflects rare mutual respect between the two icons, marking the symbolic conclusion of an era in international football.
