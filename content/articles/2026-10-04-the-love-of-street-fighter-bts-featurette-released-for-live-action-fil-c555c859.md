2026年10月上旬，派拉蒙影業（Paramount Pictures）與傳奇影業（Legendary Entertainment）為其備受期待的真人版《快打旋風》（Street Fighter）電影發布了一部名為「快打旋風之愛」（The Love of Street Fighter）的幕後特輯。該特輯於10月2日或3日開始串流，旨在向全球觀眾展示製作團隊對卡普空（CAPCOM）經典格鬥遊戲系列的深厚情感與承諾。

根據報導，這部幕後特輯的核心訊息是製作團隊對原作的熱愛以及力求完美呈現的決心。資料顯示，電影的行銷策略也大力強調這一點，以確保粉絲們相信這部改編作品將忠於其豐富的遊戲傳承。特輯中包含了演員們對該系列的高度評價和評論，進一步鞏固了這一承諾。

關於電影的劇情，資料顯示，故事將圍繞著經典角色隆（Ryu）和肯（Ken）的重聚展開。他們在春麗（Chun-Li）的招募下，參與了世界格鬥大賽（World Warrior Tournament）。隨著他們面對一場隱藏的陰謀，兩位格鬥家必須正視彼此和他們的過去，否則將面臨毀滅。這暗示了電影將融合經典角色、標誌性賽事以及引人入勝的敘事元素。

《快打旋風》系列作為卡普空旗下最受歡迎的遊戲之一，其真人電影改編一直備受關注。此次幕後特輯的發布，不僅是電影宣傳的重要一環，也反映了電影製作方在面對廣大粉絲群體時，對於IP改編品質的重視。這部電影的成功與否，將對未來更多遊戲改編作品產生示範效應。

## English edition

In early October 2026, Paramount Pictures and Legendary Entertainment released "The Love of Street Fighter," a behind-the-scenes featurette for their highly anticipated live-action *Street Fighter* film. The featurette, which began streaming around October 2nd or 3rd, aims to showcase the production team's profound passion and commitment to CAPCOM's classic fighting game series to a global audience.

Reports indicate that the core message of this behind-the-scenes featurette is the production team's love for the source material and their determination to get the adaptation right. The film's marketing strategy has heavily leaned on this point, aiming to assure fans that the adaptation will be faithful to its rich gaming legacy. The featurette reportedly includes comments from the cast expressing their appreciation for the franchise, further solidifying this commitment.

Regarding the film's plot, information suggests the story will revolve around the reunion of classic characters Ryu and Ken. They are recruited by Chun-Li to participate in the World Warrior Tournament. As they confront a hidden conspiracy, the two fighters must face each other and their past, or risk destruction. This indicates the film will blend iconic characters, signature events, and an engaging narrative.

As one of CAPCOM's most popular game series, the live-action adaptation of *Street Fighter* has garnered significant attention. The release of this behind-the-scenes featurette is not only a crucial part of the film's promotion but also reflects the filmmakers' emphasis on the quality of IP adaptation when engaging with a large fanbase. The success of this film could set a precedent for future video game adaptations.
