  # Global Tech Industry Dynamics: From AI Naming Disputes to Semiconductor Supply Chain Restructuring
  
  > The global technology industry is undergoing significant shifts. The U.S. federal government’s "Superintelligence" naming policy has triggered backlash from California, while semiconductor and optical communication supply chains face geopolitical risks. Meanwhile, international educational collaborations are actively positioning themselves for talent development.
  
  The global technology industry is currently facing multiple governance and supply chain challenges. Recently, U.S. President Trump signed an executive order requiring federal administrative departments to replace the term "Artificial Intelligence" (AI) with "Superintelligence" (SI) in all non-legal official documents. This move immediately triggered a strong counter-response from California Governor Gavin Newsom, who signed a state-level executive order explicitly requiring all agencies within California to continue using the term "AI," refusing to comply with the federal government's naming change.
  
  This naming dispute is not merely a matter of vocabulary; it reflects a fundamental disagreement between the federal government and the state of California—the core of technological innovation—regarding AI governance frameworks, the assessment of regulatory risks, and the authority to define technology.
  
  At the same time, global semiconductor and optical communication supply chains are undergoing structural adjustments. Market reports indicate that U.S. lawmakers and the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) are closely scrutinizing Chinese-made optical transceiver modules, particularly those with 3.2T specifications, amid rumors of potential restrictive orders. This led to significant volatility in U.S. optical communication stocks on October 1. Regarding the hardware supply chain, Micron Technology is optimistic about the humanoid robot market, anticipating that the massive memory requirements for humanoid robots to perform complex AI tasks will become a new blue ocean for the industry.
  
  Furthermore, gallium arsenide foundry Win Semiconductors announced steady revenue performance for September and is actively planning its product roadmap for 2027, indicating that despite challenges such as rising component costs and material shortages, the relevant supply chain continues to seek growth momentum.
  
  In terms of the industrial technology ecosystem, global tech giants are embroiled in a resource battle over "API Layer Access." Major companies have implemented API access restrictions, which have had a direct impact on startups and the developer ecosystem. For instance, Figma CEO Dylan Field recently publicly questioned why Anthropic has not publicly supported the Model Context Protocol (MCP), sparking widespread industry discussion regarding the development of AI agents and open standards.
  
  Currently, there is no consensus among the parties regarding the boundaries between closed and open APIs, and this "agent war" has become a significant variable in the field of software development.
  
  The aforementioned technological governance and industrial changes have also had a direct impact on global education and talent cultivation. Asia University announced the signing of a five-year memorandum of understanding with Arizona Western College (AWC) in the United States, with both parties focusing on cutting-edge fields such as semiconductors, artificial intelligence, and smart manufacturing. This cooperation project includes international study programs, faculty and student exchanges, and internship collaborations, with future plans to welcome American students to study in Taiwan. Against the backdrop of an accelerating restructuring of the tech industry landscape, such international higher education collaborations are seen as a key bridge for youth to connect with global technological trends and address talent shortages.
  
  Although various policies and industrial layouts are being promoted, many uncertainties remain. For example, it remains to be seen whether the opposition between the federal and California governments over AI naming will escalate into conflicts over actual regulatory policies. Furthermore, the restrictive measures regarding Chinese-made optical communication modules currently stem mostly from market rumors and foreign media reports, with no official details of a ban released yet; investors and the supply chain should avoid over-interpreting these reports. At the same time, there are uncertainties regarding the long-term impact of API access restrictions on the overall AI ecosystem, as well as the timing of actual demand surges in the humanoid robot market, both of which depend on technological evolution and market acceptance.
  
  Future observations will focus on three key areas: First, whether the confrontation between the U.S. federal government and the California government over tech governance will affect corporate compliance costs; second, whether U.S. regulatory actions on optical communication modules will escalate, thereby impacting the stability of the global semiconductor supply chain; and third, whether the competition among tech giants over API access permissions will drive the industry toward more open standard protocols or further exacerbate technological monopolies. These developments will profoundly influence the strategic planning and competitive landscape of the global tech industry in the coming years.

# 全球科技產業動態：從AI命名爭議到半導體供應鏈重組

> 近期全球科技產業變動劇烈，美國聯邦政府推動「超級智慧」命名政策引發加州反彈，同時半導體與光通訊供應鏈面臨地緣政治風險，跨國教育合作則積極佈局人才培育。


全球科技產業近期面臨多重治理與供應鏈挑戰。美國聯邦政府近期由總統川普簽署行政命令，要求聯邦行政部門在非法律公文中，全面以「超級智慧」（Superintelligence, SI）取代「人工智慧」（AI）一詞。此舉隨即引發加州州長紐森（Gavin Newsom）的強烈反擊，紐森簽署州級政令，明確要求加州內所有機構必須持續使用「AI」稱謂，拒絕配合聯邦政府的命名變更。
這場命名之爭不僅是詞彙上的差異，更反映出聯邦政府與作為科技創新核心的加州政府，在人工智慧治理架構、監管風險認定及科技定義權上的核心分歧。
與此同時，全球半導體與光通訊供應鏈正經歷結構性調整。市場訊息指出，美國國會議員及聯邦通訊委員會（FCC）正密切審視中國製光收發模組，特別是針對 3.2T 規格的產品，傳出可能祭出禁制令。此舉導致美國光通訊類股於 10 月 1 日出現顯著波動。在硬體供應鏈方面，美光科技（Micron Technology）則看好人形機器人市場的發展，預期未來人形機器人執行複雜 AI 任務所需的龐大記憶體需求，將成為該產業的新藍海。
此外，砷化鎵代工廠宏捷科公佈 9 月營收表現穩健，並積極規劃 2027 年的產品佈局，顯示儘管面臨零元件成本上揚與缺料挑戰，相關供應鏈仍持續尋求成長動能。
在產業技術生態方面，全球科技巨頭正陷入一場「存取層級」（API Layer Access）的資源爭奪戰。各大公司紛紛祭出 API 存取限制，此舉對新創公司與開發者生態造成直接衝擊。例如，Figma 執行長 Dylan Field 近期公開質疑 Anthropic 公司為何未公開支援 Model Context Protocol（MCP），引發業界對於 AI 代理（Agent）發展與開放標準的廣泛討論。
目前，各方對於 API 封閉與開放的界線仍未達成共識，這場「代理戰爭」已成為軟體開發領域的重要變數。
針對上述科技治理與產業變動，對全球教育與人才培育亦產生直接影響。亞洲大學宣佈與美國亞利桑那西部學院（AWC）簽署為期五年的合作備忘錄，雙方將聚焦半導體、人工智慧與智慧製造等前瞻領域。此合作計畫包含跨國研習、師生互訪及實習合作，未來更計畫迎接美籍學子來臺學習。在科技產業版圖加速重組的背景下，此類高等教育的跨國合作，被視為青年接軌全球科技脈動、解決人才缺口的關鍵橋樑。
儘管各項政策與產業佈局陸續推動，但仍存在諸多不確定性。例如，聯邦與加州政府在 AI 命名上的對立，是否會進一步擴大至實際監管政策的衝突，仍待後續觀察。此外，針對中國製光通訊模組的限制措施目前多源自市場傳聞與外電報導，尚未有官方正式發布的禁制令細節，投資人與產業鏈應避免過度解讀。同時，API 存取限制對整體 AI 生態系造成的長期影響，以及人形機器人市場的實際需求爆發時間點，均存在技術演進與市場接受度的不確定因素。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個面向：第一，美國聯邦政府與加州政府在科技治理上的對抗是否會影響企業的合規成本；第二，美國針對光通訊模組的監管動作是否會進一步升級，進而衝擊全球半導體供應鏈的穩定性；第三，科技巨頭在 API 存取許可權上的博弈，是否會促使產業轉向更開放的標準協議，抑或進一步加劇技術壟斷。這些發展將深刻影響全球科技產業在未來數年的佈局策略與競爭格局。
