哈雷戴維森公司已宣布，將在2026賽季結束後，終止其在Mission King of the Baggers錦標賽中的工廠車隊參與和官方支持。此一決定於2026年10月2日公布，讓賽事主辦方MotoAmerica措手不及，並坦承此舉已為賽事未來帶來挑戰。

MotoAmerica對此迅速做出回應，表示他們「正積極探索所有可用選項」，以應對哈雷戴維森退出所帶來的影響。賽事主辦方也指出，他們理解並分享廣大車迷對此消息的失望與沮喪，並承認哈雷戴維森的遲來通知確實造成了問題。

哈雷戴維森作為Mission King of the Baggers錦標賽中的主要參與者之一，其工廠車隊的撤出無疑將對該系列賽的未來產生重大影響。此舉不僅可能改變賽事的競爭格局，也促使MotoAmerica必須尋求新的合作夥伴或調整賽事結構，以確保錦標賽的持續發展。

## English edition

Harley-Davidson has announced its decision to conclude its factory team involvement and official backing in the Mission King of the Baggers Championship after the 2026 season. This move, announced on October 2, 2026, has reportedly left the sanctioning body, MotoAmerica, scrambling and openly acknowledging the problems created by the late notice.

MotoAmerica has swiftly responded to the announcement, stating that it is "actively exploring all available options" to address the implications of Harley-Davidson's departure. The race organizer also indicated that it shares the frustration of fans regarding this news and recognizes the challenges posed by the timing of Harley-Davidson's decision.

As a prominent participant in the Mission King of the Baggers Championship, Harley-Davidson's withdrawal of its factory program is expected to have a significant impact on the series' future. This decision not only alters the competitive landscape but also necessitates MotoAmerica to seek new partnerships or adjust the championship's structure to ensure its continued viability and growth.
