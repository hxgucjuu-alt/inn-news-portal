  # Controversy Over National Think Tank Appointments and the Population Crisis: The Disparity in Public Attention
  
  > The national think tank DSET has recently faced public scrutiny over personnel appointments. Meanwhile, a long-term population projection report released by the National Development Council (NDC) has warned of a severe crisis, yet it has been met with significant apathy from the public, reflecting an imbalance in the current focus on public issues.
  
  Recently, two significant social focal points have emerged in the domestic public sphere: the controversy surrounding personnel appointments at a national think tank, and a grim warning from the National Development Council (NDC) regarding demographic shifts over the next half-century. The Center for Security, Diplomacy and Strategy (DSET), a national think tank established in 2023, has recently become a focus of media attention due to its personnel structure. According to public information, the position of Director of the National Security Division is held by social activist Chen Wei-ting. This information was not proactively released by official channels but was instead inadvertently revealed through documentation related to the Danish anti-drone manufacturer "MyDefence," sparking discussions regarding the transparency and professional background of the think tank's personnel.
  
  While the controversy over the think tank's personnel continues to unfold, the NDC released its latest population projection report for the years 2026 to 2075 on August 28. The report clearly indicates that Taiwan will face an even more drastic trend of population decline and aging. Although this report was featured on the front pages of the three major domestic newspapers on August 29, demonstrating the high importance placed on the demographic crisis by mainstream media, the issue appeared unusually cold among the general public compared to the heated social media discourse surrounding the think tank controversy. There was little follow-up reporting by the media, no significant ripples of discussion on social networks, and the topic quickly faded from public view the following day.
  
  Regarding the controversy over the think tank's appointments, there is a clear cognitive gap in the information presented by various parties. Journalist "Ni Feng De Wu Ya" (Crow Against the Wind) noted on social media that DSET has maintained a low-profile attitude toward the appointment, leading to external questions about whether there was a deliberate concealment of information. However, there has been no official response or explanation regarding the specific scope of responsibilities, hiring criteria, or the decision-making process for this position.
  
  External questioning regarding Chen Wei-ting serving as the Director of the National Security Division primarily focuses on whether the connection between his social movement background and the professionalism required for national security policy is sufficient, as well as what standards of personnel transparency a national research institution should uphold. These matters remain in a stage of interpretation and debate among various sectors.
  
  The development of these social issues has had a profound impact on the current government's administrative logic and public policy formulation. The cold reception of the population crisis reflects a lack of public attention toward long-term, structural problems, which may result in a lack of sufficient public support and oversight pressure when the government promotes relevant response policies. On the other hand, the think tank personnel controversy highlights the issue of public trust in government-commissioned research institutions. When the operations of a national think tank lack transparency, it not only weakens the credibility of policy research but may also, in a polarized political environment, turn a professional appointment into a focus of political maneuvering, thereby affecting the objectivity of policy formulation and the effectiveness of social communication.
  
  Regarding the aforementioned phenomena, different perspectives suggest that a single personnel appointment or a single policy report should not be viewed as an isolated event. Some public opinion holds that when facing major national security issues such as the population crisis, the government should establish more effective communication mechanisms rather than merely releasing projection data. Regarding think tank personnel, some commentators believe that the issue should return to a systemic review to avoid over-linking personal backgrounds with job functions, which leads to unnecessary political labeling.
  
  However, the limitation lies in the current lack of sufficient information disclosure, which prevents the public from fully assessing the reasonableness of the appointments or determining whether population policy has received sufficient resources and attention in actual implementation. Therefore, in the absence of transparency, excessive interpretation or political association may not help resolve substantive issues.
  
  Future observation should focus on two main directions: First, whether the projection report on the population crisis issued by the NDC will be translated into concrete policy adjustments and resource allocation, and how the government will enhance public awareness of the crisis regarding demographic changes. Second, whether national think tanks like DSET will adopt more open methods of explanation when facing external questions about personnel transparency, and whether the government will establish more rigorous information disclosure standards when commissioning think tanks for national security and technology research in the future. These developments will directly affect the effectiveness of government governance and the willingness of the public to participate in public affairs.

# 國家級智庫人事爭議與人口危機：社會公共議題的冷熱反差

> 國家級智庫DSET近期因人事任命引發輿論質疑，與此同時，國發會發布的長期人口推估報告雖示警嚴重危機，卻在社會輿論中呈現極大冷感，反映出當前公共議題關注度的失衡。


近期國內公共領域出現兩項顯著的社會焦點，分別涉及國家級智庫的人事任命爭議，以及國發會針對未來半世紀人口結構變化的嚴峻預警。科技、民主與社會研究中心（DSET）作為2023年成立的國家級智庫，其人事結構近日成為媒體關注焦點。據公開資訊顯示，該機構國家安全組組長一職由社運人士陳為廷擔任，此一訊息並非由官方主動發布，而是因丹麥反制無人機製造商「MyDefence」的相關資訊意外揭露，引發外界對於智庫人事透明度與專業背景的討論。
在智庫人事爭議持續發酵的同時，國發會於8月28日發布了針對2026年至2075年的最新人口推估報告。該報告明確指出，臺灣人口將面臨更為劇烈的減少與老化趨勢。儘管此份報告在8月29日當天被國內三大報列為頭版頭條，顯示出主流媒體對於人口結構危機的高度重視，然而與智庫人事爭議引發的社群熱議相比，人口議題在社會大眾層面卻顯得異常冷清，不僅媒體後續追蹤報導稀少，社群網路亦未見顯著的討論漣漪，相關話題在隔日即迅速淡出公眾視野。
關於智庫人事任命的爭議，目前各方資訊呈現出明顯的認知落差。新聞工作者「逆風的烏鴉」在社群平臺發文指出，DSET對於該職位的人事安排採取低調態度，甚至引發外界質疑該職位是否存在資訊揭露上的刻意隱匿。然而，針對該職位的具體職責範疇、聘任標準以及決策過程，目前尚無來自官方的正式回應或說明。
外界對於陳為廷擔任國安組長一職的質疑，主要集中在社會運動背景與國家安全政策專業性之間的連結是否足夠，以及智庫作為國家級研究機構，其人事透明度應達到何種標準，目前仍處於各界解讀與爭議階段。
這些社會議題的發展，對當前政府的施政邏輯與公共政策制定產生了深遠影響。人口危機的冷處理反映出社會對於長期性、結構性問題的關注度不足，這可能導致政府在推動相關因應政策時，缺乏足夠的民意支援與監督壓力。另一方面，智庫人事爭議則凸顯了民眾對於政府委託研究機構的信任度問題。當國家級智庫的運作缺乏透明度，不僅會削弱政策研究的公信力，更可能在政治極化環境下，將單純的專業職位任命轉化為政治攻防的焦點，進而影響政策制定的客觀性與社會溝通效能。
針對上述現象，不同觀點指出，不應將單一的人事任命或單一的政策報告視為孤立事件。部分輿論認為，政府在面對人口危機等重大國安議題時，應建立更有效的溝通機制，而非僅止於發布推估資料；對於智庫人事，亦有評論認為應回歸制度面檢視，避免過度將個人背景與職位功能掛鉤，造成不必要的政治標籤化。
然而，限制在於目前缺乏足夠的資訊揭露，導致公眾無法完整評估相關任命的合理性，亦無法判斷人口政策在實際執行層面是否已獲得足夠的資源與重視，因此在資訊不透明的前提下，過度解讀或進行政治聯想恐無助於解決實質問題。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於兩大方向：首先，國發會針對人口危機所提出的推估報告，後續是否會轉化為具體的政策調整與資源投入，以及政府如何提升民眾對於人口結構變遷的危機意識；其次，DSET等國家級智庫在面對外界對於人事透明度的質疑時，是否會採取更為公開的說明方式，以及未來政府在委託智庫進行國安與科技研究時，是否會建立更為嚴謹的資訊揭露規範。這些發展將直接影響政府治理的效能與社會大眾對於公共事務的參與意願。
