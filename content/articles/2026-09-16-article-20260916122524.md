  # Global Financial Risks and Industrial Transformation: The Dual Challenges of Stablecoin Application and Geopolitics
  
  > As global geopolitical instability intensifies, armed groups in the Middle East are utilizing cryptocurrency to evade sanctions, bringing financial regulation and digital asset applications into the spotlight. Meanwhile, Taiwan’s financial and cultural industries are actively responding to global economic shifts through digital transformation and international alliances.
  
  In mid-September 2026, the global financial and trade system faces multiple challenges. On one hand, according to a report by Fox News, the Houthi armed group in Yemen has successfully evaded U.S. economic sanctions targeting Iran and its proxies by utilizing a diversified financial network that includes cryptocurrency, oil trading, and port control, generating billions of dollars in annual revenue. This move not only consolidates the group's control over Yemen's Red Sea coast but also threatens the Bab-el-Mandeb Strait, a critical node for global energy transport and maritime trade.
  
  On the other hand, with the proliferation of the digital economy and blockchain technology, Taiwan’s O-Bank officially partnered with BitoGroup on September 15 to launch a "Corporate Stablecoin Cross-Border Payment Service" pilot program. This makes it the first bank in the country to integrate stablecoins into its corporate online banking system, aiming to solve the pain points of cross-border trade collections for enterprises.
  
  Multiple sources confirm that cryptocurrency has become a key variable affecting global geopolitics and business models. In terms of financial regulation, the Houthis leverage the anonymity and decentralized nature of cryptocurrency to bypass monitoring by the traditional banking system, thereby sustaining their military and political operations. In terms of commercial application, Taiwan’s financial industry is attempting to use stablecoins as a legitimate tool to enhance the efficiency of cross-border trade. The collaboration between O-Bank and BitoGroup requires companies to open bank accounts and complete the BitoGroup exchange’s verification process, demonstrating that financial institutions maintain strict compliance and KYC (Know Your Customer) standards when promoting digital asset applications, attempting to strike a balance between innovation and risk management.
  
  However, there are significant differences in perspectives among information sources regarding the use of cryptocurrency. In the realm of international security, cryptocurrency is viewed as a financial tool that undermines sanction mechanisms and fuels regional conflicts, with Washington intensifying efforts to cut off such illicit fund flows. Yet, in the field of industrial development, stablecoins are seen as necessary infrastructure for digital trade. What remains to be confirmed is whether such financial innovation services will face more complex challenges in cross-border regulatory compliance as global regulation of cryptocurrency tightens, and how governments will draw the line between combating illicit finance and encouraging fintech innovation remains a focus of market attention.
  
  Geopolitical instability also has a profound impact on the regional economy. According to an analysis by the Associated Press (AP News), the United States, long mired in Middle Eastern conflicts, has been forced to shift some of its military resources and strategic focus away from the Asia-Pacific region, which has indirectly affected regional stability and the trade environment in East Asia. Against this backdrop, Taiwanese industries have demonstrated resilience in seeking international connections. For instance, the 2026 Taipei Fashion Week kicked off on September 11; this year, it not only collaborated with the Italian Economic, Trade and Cultural Promotion Office to incorporate top-tier Italian craftsmanship but also integrated cross-disciplinary elements of music and art, attempting to enhance Taiwan's international visibility and industrial competitiveness through cultural output amidst the turbulent international situation.
  
  Views on the aforementioned phenomena vary. Some economists warn that while stablecoins can improve trade efficiency, their integration with the traditional financial system remains limited by the digital asset regulations of various countries. Furthermore, the Houthi financial network demonstrates that relying solely on financial sanctions is no longer sufficient to completely curb the activities of armed groups; the international community must engage in cross-border collaboration across multiple dimensions, including energy security, port management, and digital financial regulation. For Taiwanese enterprises, although stablecoin cross-border services offer new payment options, companies must carefully assess potential market volatility risks and compliance costs arising from changes in international regulations when utilizing new technologies, and should not over-interpret the improvement effect of a single technology on the overall trade environment.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on three points: First, whether the U.S. government will adopt more specific restrictive measures on the cross-border flow of cryptocurrency, thereby affecting the development path of global fintech; second, the adoption rate of stablecoin services like those of O-Bank following the pilot program, and whether more financial institutions will follow suit; and finally, how East Asia, against the backdrop of the U.S. strategic shift, can maintain regional trade stability and development momentum through regional economic integration and international alliances in the cultural industry. These developments will continue to influence the global economy and regional security landscape in the second half of 2026.

# 全球金融風險與產業轉型：穩定幣應用與地緣政治的雙重挑戰

> 隨著全球地緣政治動盪加劇，中東武裝勢力透過加密貨幣避開制裁，促使金融監管與數位資產應用成為焦點。同時，臺灣金融業與文化產業正積極透過數位轉型與國際結盟，應對全球經濟變局。


二〇二六年九月中旬，全球金融與貿易體系面臨多重挑戰。一方面，根據外媒《Fox News》報導，葉門胡塞武裝組織透過包含加密貨幣、石油貿易及港口控制在內的多元化金融網路，成功規避美國針對伊朗及其代理人的經濟制裁，每年獲利達數十億美元。此舉不僅鞏固該組織在葉門紅海沿岸的控制權，更威脅到曼德海峽這一全球能源運輸與海運貿易的關鍵節點。
另一方面，隨著數位經濟與區塊鏈技術的普及，臺灣金融機構王道銀行（O-Bank）於九月十五日正式攜手幣託集團（BitoGroup），推出「企業穩定幣跨境收款服務」先導計畫，成為國內首家將穩定幣整合至企業網銀系統的銀行，旨在解決企業跨境貿易的收款痛點。
多項來源共同確認，加密貨幣已成為影響全球地緣政治與商業模式的關鍵變數。在金融監管層面，胡塞武裝利用加密貨幣的匿名性與去中心化特性，繞過傳統銀行體系的監控，維持其軍事與政治運作；而在商業應用層面，臺灣金融業則試圖將穩定幣作為提升跨境貿易效率的合法工具。王道銀行與幣託集團的合作，要求企業必須在銀行開立帳戶並完成幣託交易所的審查程式，顯示出金融機構在推動數位資產應用時，仍維持嚴格的合規與 KYC（認識你的客戶）標準，試圖在創新與風險控管之間取得平衡。
然而，資訊來源間對於加密貨幣用途的觀點存在顯著差異。在國際安全領域，加密貨幣被視為破壞制裁機制、助長區域衝突的金融工具，華府正加強努力切斷此類非法資金流。但在產業發展領域，穩定幣被視為數位貿易的必要基礎設施。目前尚待確認的是，隨著全球對加密貨幣的監管力道趨嚴，此類金融創新服務在跨境法規遵循上是否會面臨更為複雜的挑戰，以及各國政府如何在打擊非法金融與鼓勵金融科技創新之間劃定界線，仍是市場關注的焦點。
地緣政治的動盪亦對區域經濟產生深遠影響。根據《美聯社》（AP News）分析，美國因長期深陷中東戰局，不得不將部分軍事資源與戰略重心從亞太地區轉移，這對東亞地區的區域穩定與貿易環境產生了間接影響。在這種大環境下，臺灣產業展現出尋求國際連結的韌性，例如二〇二六年臺北時裝週於九月十一日正式啟動，今年不僅與義大利經濟貿易文化推廣辦事處合作，匯入頂級義大利工藝，更透過與音樂、藝術的跨界整合，試圖在動盪的國際局勢中，透過文化輸出提升臺灣的國際能見度與產業競爭力。
針對上述現象，各界觀點不一。部分經濟學家提醒，穩定幣雖能提升貿易效率，但其與傳統金融體系的整合程度仍受限於各國的數位資產法規。此外，胡塞武裝的金融網路顯示，單純依靠金融制裁已難以完全遏止武裝勢力的活動，國際社會必須在能源安全、港口管理與數位金融監管等多個面向進行跨國協作。對於臺灣企業而言，雖然穩定幣跨境服務提供了新的收款選項，但企業在利用新技術的同時，亦需審慎評估潛在的市場波動風險與國際法規變動帶來的合規成本，不宜過度解讀單一技術對整體貿易環境的改善效果。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三點：首先，美國政府是否會針對加密貨幣的跨境流動採取更具體的限制措施，進而影響全球金融科技的發展路徑；其次，王道銀行此類穩定幣服務在先導計畫後的普及率，以及是否會有更多金融機構跟進；最後，東亞地區在美國戰略重心轉移的背景下，如何透過區域經濟整合與文化產業的國際結盟，維持區域內的貿易穩定與發展動能。這些發展將持續影響二〇二六年下半年的全球經濟與區域安全格局。
