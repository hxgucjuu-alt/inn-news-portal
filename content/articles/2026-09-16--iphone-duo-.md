  # Apple’s First Foldable Phone "iPhone Duo" Officially Unveiled: Samsung Launches PR War Amid Global Workplace Transformation
  
  > Apple officially launched its first foldable smartphone, the iPhone Duo, at its fall event with a price tag of $1,999. Meanwhile, global enterprises are experiencing a workplace transformation wave toward a four-day workweek, driven by AI-enhanced productivity.
  
  At its fall product launch event on September 15, 2026, Apple officially unveiled the "iPhone Duo," its long-awaited first foldable smartphone. With a starting price of $1,999, the product marks Apple’s formal entry into the large-screen foldable device market. However, this move immediately drew significant attention from Samsung Electronics, a pioneer in the foldable phone sector.
  
  Samsung quickly released a highly dramatic promotional video featuring an actor resembling Apple CEO Tim Cook to market the device, thereby mocking Apple’s late entry into foldable technology and attempting to solidify Samsung’s market leadership and influence in the foldable category.
  
  According to multiple media reports, the release of the iPhone Duo is not merely a public relations battle between brands, but also highlights the deep-seated symbiotic interests within the supply chain. Although Samsung has adopted a tough, mocking stance in its marketing, it is reported that the most expensive key components inside the iPhone Duo are still supplied by Samsung. This "competitor as a supplier" dual-track strategy reflects the complex dynamics between brand competition and global supply chain reliance in the current high-end hardware manufacturing sector. Meanwhile, the global workplace environment is undergoing a qualitative shift due to the widespread application of Artificial Intelligence (AI).
  
  According to reports from BBNTimes and Yahoo Entertainment, the proliferation of generative AI tools and multi-agent automated task systems has significantly boosted corporate productivity. Tasks such as data analysis, code review, and administrative coordination, which previously took 40 hours, can now be completed in under 24 hours. This has prompted global enterprises to re-examine traditional work models, with the four-day workweek becoming an emerging trend in major technology hubs.
  
  Regarding the market positioning of the iPhone Duo and Samsung’s reaction, there are some discrepancies in information across various sectors. Techbang reported that Samsung’s mocking video aims to emphasize its status as a pioneer in foldable phones; however, there is currently a lack of concrete sales data to support whether the device will shake Samsung’s market share in the foldable sector.
  
  Furthermore, regarding the issue of AI driving a four-day workweek, although most reports indicate that productivity gains are the primary driver, the level of adaptation to AI and the data regarding time savings vary across different industries. Some companies remain reserved about fully implementing a four-day workweek, arguing that it involves complex labor regulations and corporate culture adjustments, rather than being a simple matter of efficiency.
  
  From an industry impact perspective, Apple’s entry into the foldable phone market is expected to accelerate the popularization of such products and drive further upgrades in display technology and precision assembly industries. For Samsung, despite facing competition from Apple, its influence in the hardware ecosystem remains solid as a key component supplier. On the other hand, the AI-driven productivity revolution is having a profound impact on the global labor market. If companies can successfully reduce routine working hours through automation, it will help alleviate the stagnation in labor output per hour and technology investment growth that has persisted for the past half-century.
  
  This transformation not only changes the structure of working hours but also compels companies to reallocate human resources toward higher-value innovation and development tasks.
  
  However, market analysts caution against over-interpreting the success of the iPhone Duo. Its high price tag of $1,999 locks it into the premium consumer market, and its market penetration remains limited by the price barrier. Additionally, while the outlook for the AI-driven four-day workweek is promising, this trend is currently concentrated in tech hubs and highly digitized enterprises. For traditional manufacturing or service industries, the challenge of converting AI-driven efficiency gains into actual reductions in working hours remains limited by technology transfer costs and skill gaps among employees; this trend should not be viewed as an inevitable phenomenon occurring simultaneously worldwide.
  
  Future observation will focus on three aspects: first, the actual sales performance of the iPhone Duo after its launch and whether it can drive overall growth in the foldable phone market; second, whether Samsung’s dynamic balance between supply chain and brand marketing will undergo strategic adjustments due to Apple’s entry; and finally, the extent to which global enterprises implement the four-day workweek and the accompanying policy measures after adopting AI. As generative AI agent technology continues to evolve, the shift in future work models will be a key indicator for observing global industrial competitiveness and changes in the labor market.

# 蘋果首款折疊機 iPhone Duo 正式亮相：三星發動公關戰與全球工作模式變革

> 蘋果於秋季發表會正式推出首款折疊手機 iPhone Duo，定價 1,999 美元。與此同時，全球企業受惠於 AI 生產力提升，正掀起一股四天工作制的職場轉型浪潮。


蘋果（Apple）於 2026 年 9 月 15 日的秋季發表會上，正式揭曉了市場期待已久的首款折疊式智慧型手機「iPhone Duo」。該產品起售價定為 1,999 美元，象徵蘋果正式跨入大螢幕折疊裝置市場。然而，此舉隨即引發折疊機先行者三星電子（Samsung）的高度關注。
三星官方迅速發布了一支具有高度戲劇性的宣傳影片，影片中找來外型神似蘋果執行長庫克（Tim Cook）的演員進行推銷，藉此嘲諷蘋果遲來的折疊技術，並試圖鞏固三星在折疊機領域的市場領導地位與話語權。
根據多家媒體報導，iPhone Duo 的發布不僅是品牌間的公關角力，更凸顯了供應鏈深層的利益共生關係。儘管三星在行銷端採取強硬的嘲諷態度，但在技術層面，iPhone Duo 內部最昂貴的關鍵零元件，據悉仍是由三星所供應。此種「競爭對手亦是供應商」的雙軌算盤，反映了當前高階硬體製造領域中，企業在品牌競爭與全球供應鏈依賴之間的複雜動態。與此同時，全球職場環境正因人工智慧（AI）的廣泛應用而發生質變。
根據《BBNTimes》與 Yahoo Entertainment 的報導，生成式 AI 工具及多代理自動化任務系統的普及，使得企業生產力大幅提升，原本耗時 40 小時的資料分析、程式碼審查與行政協調工作，現已能壓縮至 24 小時內完成，這促使全球企業重新審視傳統工作模式，四天工作制正成為科技重鎮的新興趨勢。
關於 iPhone Duo 的市場定位與三星的反應，各界資訊存在部分差異。Techbang 報導指出，三星透過影片開酸，意在強調其作為折疊機先驅的地位；然而，針對該裝置是否能撼動三星在折疊市場的市佔率，目前仍缺乏具體的銷售資料佐證。
此外，關於 AI 帶動四天工作制的議題，雖然多數報導指出生產力提升是主要驅動力，但不同產業對於匯入 AI 的適應程度與節省時間的資料呈現並不一致，部分企業仍對全面實施四天工作制持保留態度，認為這涉及複雜的勞動法規與企業文化調整，並非單純的效率問題。
從產業影響層面來看，蘋果進入折疊機市場預計將加速該類產品的普及化，並帶動螢幕技術與精密組裝產業的進一步升級。對於三星而言，雖然面臨蘋果的競爭，但作為關鍵零元件供應商，其在硬體生態系中的影響力依然穩固。另一方面，AI 驅動的生產力革命正對全球勞動力市場產生深遠影響。若企業能成功透過自動化縮減例行工時，將有助於緩解過去半個世紀以來，勞工每小時產出與科技投資成長停滯的困境。
這種轉型不僅改變了工作時間結構，也促使企業必須重新配置人力資源，轉向更高價值的創新開發任務。
然而，市場分析師提醒，對於 iPhone Duo 的成功不應過度解讀。1,999 美元的高昂定價將其鎖定在頂級消費市場，其市場滲透率仍受限於價格門檻。此外，關於 AI 帶來的四天工作制，雖然前景看好，但這項趨勢目前主要集中在科技重鎮與高數位化程度的企業。對於傳統製造業或服務業而言，如何將 AI 帶來的效率紅利轉化為實際的工時縮減，仍面臨技術轉移成本與員工技能落差的限制，不應將此趨勢視為全球同步發生的必然現象。
後續觀察重點將集中在三個面向：首先是 iPhone Duo 在上市後的實際銷量表現，以及其是否能帶動折疊機市場的整體成長；其次是三星在供應鏈與品牌行銷之間的動態平衡，是否會因為蘋果的加入而產生策略調整；最後則是全球企業在匯入 AI 後，對於四天工作制的落實程度與政策配套。隨著生成式 AI 代理技術的持續演進，未來工作模式的轉變將是觀察全球產業競爭力與勞動市場變遷的重要指標。
