  The world is currently facing the dual challenges of digital technology abuse and geopolitical conflict. In the realm of digital security, Europol and the European Commission's Joint Research Centre (JRC) have conducted an inventory and study of emerging Privacy-Enhancing Technologies (PETs) in response to increasingly severe privacy and fraud threats. Simultaneously, the conflict in the Middle East continues to escalate, with new developments in the war between the Yemeni government forces and the Houthis. Furthermore, the British government has used exceptionally strong diplomatic language regarding Israel's policies in the West Bank, signaling the international community's deep concern over the humanitarian crisis and regional security situation.
  
  According to the joint research by Europol and the European Commission, Deepfake technology has become a primary concern for law enforcement agencies. As the realism of forged images and videos continues to improve, they have become difficult to distinguish from reality with the naked eye, and such technology is being widely used in crimes such as identity theft, fraud, and extortion. Experts point out that AI technology permeates the development of various privacy-enhancing technologies, and encrypted computing is seen as a critical turning point for future law enforcement investigative methods.
  
  Meanwhile, Kaohsiung City Councilor Lin Chih-hung pointed out during a legislative interpellation that, according to statistics from the National Police Agency, there were 179,507 fraud victims nationwide between July 2025 and June 2026. Among them, 13,411 victims were seniors aged 65 and over, indicating that preventing new forms of fraud has become an urgent social issue.
  
  Regarding information consistency, Europol has confirmed that Deepfake technology poses a substantial threat to public safety and emphasized that law enforcement agencies must adjust their methods for analyzing digital evidence. Regarding the situation in the Middle East, both the BBC and Sky News have reported on the tensions in the region. The BBC reported that the British Foreign Secretary used unprecedentedly strong language, such as "ethnic cleansing" and "settler terrorists," regarding Israel's actions in the West Bank, indicating a shift toward a stricter stance by the British government on the local situation.
  
  On the other hand, Sky News cited Al Jazeera reports confirming that the Yemeni government military has launched a counteroffensive against the Houthis, and that the conflict has resulted in civilian casualties, including women and children, confirming the trend of escalating military confrontation in the region.
  
  These events have had concrete impacts on global industries and policies. In the digital industry, law enforcement agencies are accelerating the development of AI detection tools to address the trust crisis brought about by Deepfakes, which will force technology companies to invest more resources in content verification and privacy protection technologies. At the policy level, Kaohsiung City Councilor Lin Chih-hung advocates for the early deployment of AI anti-fraud systems to protect the elderly population. At the international political level, the escalation of conflict in the Middle East not only affects regional stability but also impacts global energy supplies and shipping security, particularly regarding potential risks in the Strait of Hormuz, which has become a core consideration in the diplomatic and defense policies of various countries.
  
  Although there is high attention on AI anti-fraud and the Middle East situation, there are still limitations and differences in interpretation. Regarding AI technology, experts warn that while Deepfake detection technology has improved, criminal methods are evolving simultaneously, and over-reliance on a single defensive technology may lead to security vulnerabilities. On the Middle East issue, although the British Foreign Secretary's remarks represent a shift in official stance, there is still a lack of consensus in the international community on how to effectively mediate the regional conflict. Furthermore, the complexity of the war in Yemen lies in the intervention of multiple forces; reports from a single media outlet may only reflect regional combat conditions and should not be interpreted as a comprehensive turning point in the war.

Second, whether the conflict in the Middle East will further affect shipping security in the Red Sea, and whether the pressure from the UK and the international community on Israeli policy will have a substantive impact on subsequent ceasefire negotiations or the regional diplomatic landscape. All parties should continue to monitor international diplomatic developments and the practical application of digital security technologies to address the ever-changing global situation.

# 中東局勢動盪與全球AI詐騙風險：執法挑戰與區域衝突並行

> 歐洲刑警組織針對AI偽造技術提出警示，與此同時，中東地區葉門政府軍對胡塞武裝發動反擊，英國外交大臣對以色列西岸政策採取強硬立場，顯示全球面臨數位犯罪與地緣政治雙重壓力。


近期全球面臨數位技術濫用與地緣政治衝突的雙重挑戰。在數位安全領域，歐洲刑警組織（Europol）與歐盟執委會聯合研究中心（JRC）針對日益嚴峻的隱私與詐騙威脅，進行了新興隱私強化技術（PETs）的盤點與研究。與此同時，中東地區的衝突局勢持續升溫，葉門政府軍與胡塞武裝（Houthis）之間的戰事出現新進展，且英國政府針對以色列在西岸地區的政策，罕見地使用了極為強烈的外交措辭，顯示國際社會對該區域人道危機與安全域性勢的高度關注。
根據歐洲刑警組織與歐盟執委會的共同研究，Deepfake（深度偽造）技術已成為執法機關最為關注的議題。隨著偽造影像與影片的逼真度不斷提升，肉眼已難以辨識真偽，這類技術被廣泛用於假冒身分、詐騙及勒索等犯罪行為。專家指出，AI技術貫穿了多項隱私強化技術的發展，而加密運算則被視為未來執法調查方式的關鍵轉捩點。
與此同時，臺灣高雄市議員林智鴻亦於議會質詢中指出，根據警政署統計，自2025年7月至2026年6月，全國詐騙被害人高達17萬9,507人，其中65歲以上長者受害人數達1萬3,411人，顯示防範新型態詐騙已成為刻不容緩的社會議題。
在資訊一致性方面，歐洲刑警組織確認Deepfake技術對公共安全構成實質威脅，並強調執法機關必須調整數位證據的分析方法。而在中東局勢方面，BBC與Sky News兩家媒體均報導了該地區的緊張情勢。BBC報導指出，英國外交大臣針對以色列在約旦河西岸的行動，使用了「種族清洗」與「定居者恐怖分子」等前所未有的強硬措辭，顯示英國政府對當地局勢的立場轉趨嚴厲。
另一方面，Sky News則引述半島電視臺（Al Jazeera）報導，證實葉門政府軍已對胡塞武裝發動反擊，且衝突中造成包括婦女與兒童在內的平民受傷，確認了該區域軍事對抗的升級趨勢。
這些事件對全球產業與政策產生了具體影響。在數位產業方面，執法機關正加速開發AI偵測工具，以應對Deepfake帶來的信任危機，這將迫使科技公司投入更多資源於內容驗證與隱私保護技術。在政策層面，高雄市議員林智鴻主張應提前部署AI防詐系統，以保護高齡族群；而在國際政治層面，中東地區的衝突升級不僅影響區域穩定，更牽動全球能源供應與航運安全，特別是涉及荷姆茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz）的潛在風險，已成為各國外交與國防政策的核心考量。
儘管各界對AI防詐與中東局勢高度關注，但仍存在限制與解讀差異。針對AI技術，專家提醒，雖然Deepfake偵測技術有所進步，但犯罪手法亦在同步演進，過度依賴單一技術防護可能導致防禦漏洞。而在中東議題上，英國外交大臣的言論雖代表了官方立場的轉變，但國際社會對於如何有效調停該區域衝突，仍缺乏共識。此外，葉門戰事的複雜性在於多方勢力的介入，單一媒體的報導可能僅反映區域性戰況，不應將特定戰術行動直接解讀為戰爭的全面轉折。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下兩方面：首先，歐洲刑警組織是否能成功推動跨國合作，建立一套標準化的AI偵測與數位證據處理機制，以及臺灣地方政府在AI防詐政策上的落實程度與成效。其次，中東地區的衝突是否會進一步波及紅海航運安全，以及英國與國際社會對以色列政策的施壓，是否會對後續的停火談判或區域外交格局產生實質影響。各界應持續關注後續的國際外交動態與數位安全技術的實戰應用，以應對不斷變化的全球局勢。
