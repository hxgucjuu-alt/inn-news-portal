  # OpenAI Announces Termination of AI Model Supply to Cursor, Targeting Musk’s SpaceX
  
  > On August 29, 2026, OpenAI officially announced that it would cease providing AI model services to the software development tool company Cursor. The official statement indicates that this decision stems from the company's acquisition by Elon Musk’s SpaceX; citing past contractual disputes, OpenAI has expressed concerns regarding the security of its technology usage.
  
  On August 29, 2026, OpenAI issued a major announcement via its official blog, formally declaring the termination of its technical partnership with the AI software development tool company Cursor and stating it would cease supplying its AI models to the firm. The core context of this incident lies in the change of ownership of Cursor, which has now been fully acquired by Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SpaceX), founded by Elon Musk.
  
  Given the long-standing legal disputes and commercial rivalry between Elon Musk and OpenAI, this termination of partnership is viewed by outsiders as a further escalation of tensions between the two parties. It also reflects the challenges facing the global AI industry regarding technology licensing, data security, and compliance following corporate mergers and acquisitions.
  
  According to OpenAI’s public explanation, the key reason for terminating the partnership is a "lack of trust." In its statement, OpenAI explicitly noted that based on past experiences collaborating with companies under Musk, it could not be confident that SpaceX would strictly adhere to the terms of service when using its technology following the acquisition of Cursor. OpenAI further alleged that companies under Musk have had specific records of contract violations in past commercial collaborations. Therefore, to protect the security and compliance of its core technology, the company’s board of directors decided to take decisive measures to cut off model supply to Cursor, preventing its technology from being diverted to unauthorized uses.
  
  However, there are significant discrepancies in information regarding OpenAI’s allegations. While OpenAI’s statement emphasizes a history of "contractual violations," as of now, SpaceX has not issued an official response to the accusations, nor has it clarified how it will handle existing user rights and technical architecture adjustments for Cursor. Furthermore, industry concerns regarding whether this incident involves antitrust issues, as well as the potential technical disruptions that the original Cursor developer community may face following a model transition, remain to be clarified.
  
  Whether OpenAI will take similar actions against other projects associated with Elon Musk remains unknown.
  
  This incident has had an immediate impact on the global software development industry and the AI technology supply chain. As a widely popular AI-assisted programming tool, the disruption of model supply to Cursor directly affects the enterprises and individual users who rely on the platform for software development. If Cursor cannot find an alternative model provider in a short period or develop its own controllable AI model, its market competitiveness may decline significantly.
  
  Furthermore, this event highlights the contradiction between "technological neutrality" and "corporate loyalty" in the global AI industry. In the future, tech giants will inevitably incorporate the backgrounds and compliance histories of acquiring parties into more rigorous scrutiny when licensing technology.
  
  Although OpenAI has framed this move as a matter of compliance and security, external perspectives remain divided. Some market observers believe this is not merely a simple contractual dispute, but a strategic move by OpenAI to block its competitor, Elon Musk, from expanding his footprint in the AI field. However, legal experts have cautioned that in the absence of court rulings confirming a breach of contract, one should not over-interpret OpenAI’s unilateral allegations, so as not to overlook the complexities of technical integration following commercial mergers. Additionally, whether linking technology supply to corporate ownership will negatively impact the openness and innovation environment of the global AI industry remains a major focus of industry concern.
  
  Future observations will focus on several aspects: first, whether SpaceX will take legal action or issue a clarifying statement regarding OpenAI’s public allegations; second, what technical countermeasures Cursor will adopt after losing support from OpenAI’s models, and whether it will pivot to open-source models or services from other competitors; and finally, whether this incident will trigger investigations by regulatory agencies into AI technology licensing monopolies or exclusionary competitive practices. As competition in the AI industry intensifies, similar supply chain disruptions caused by corporate mergers may become a significant issue in future global tech industry governance.

# OpenAI 宣佈停止供應 Cursor AI 模型 劍指馬斯克旗下 SpaceX

> OpenAI 於 2026 年 8 月 29 日正式宣佈，將停止向程式開發工具公司 Cursor 提供 AI 模型服務。官方宣告指出，此舉源於該公司已被馬斯克旗下的 SpaceX 收購，基於過往合約爭議，OpenAI 對其技術使用安全性存疑。


OpenAI 於 2026 年 8 月 29 日透過官方部落格發布重大宣告，正式宣佈終止與 AI 程式開發工具公司 Cursor 的技術合作，並將停止向其供應旗下的 AI 模型。此次事件的背景核心在於 Cursor 公司的所有權變更，該公司目前已被馬斯克（Elon Musk）創辦的太空探索科技公司（SpaceX）全資收購。
由於馬斯克與 OpenAI 之間長期存在法律訴訟與商業競爭關係，此次合作終止被外界視為雙方矛盾的進一步升溫，亦反映出全球 AI 產業在技術授權、資料安全與企業併購後的合規性挑戰。
根據 OpenAI 的公開說明，決定終止合作的關鍵理由在於「信任缺失」。OpenAI 在宣告中明確指出，根據過往與馬斯克旗下企業的合作經驗，該公司無法確信 SpaceX 在收購 Cursor 後，仍能嚴格遵守服務條款使用其技術。OpenAI 進一步指控馬斯克旗下企業在過往的商業合作中，曾出現違反合約的具體紀錄，因此為了保護核心技術的安全性與合規性，公司董事會決議採取斷然措施，切斷對 Cursor 的模型供應，以防止其技術被轉用於未經授權的用途。
然而，針對 OpenAI 的指控，目前各方資訊仍存在顯著差異。OpenAI 的說法強調了「合約違規」的歷史紀錄，但截至目前為止，SpaceX 官方尚未針對 OpenAI 的指控做出正式回應，亦未說明未來將如何處理 Cursor 現有的使用者權益與技術架構調整。此外，業界對於此次事件是否涉及反壟斷法的疑慮，以及 Cursor 原有開發者社群在模型更換後可能面臨的技術中斷問題，目前皆尚待進一步釐清。
OpenAI 是否會針對其他與馬斯克相關的合作專案採取類似行動，目前也仍屬未知數。
此次事件對全球軟體開發產業與 AI 技術供應鏈產生了直接影響。Cursor 作為一款廣受開發者歡迎的 AI 輔助程式設計工具，其模型供應的中斷，直接衝擊了依賴該平臺進行軟體開發的企業與個人使用者。若 Cursor 無法在短時間內尋找到替代的模型供應商，或無法開發出自主可控的 AI 模型，其市場競爭力恐將大幅下滑。
此外，此事件亦凸顯了全球 AI 產業在「技術中立性」與「企業忠誠度」之間的矛盾，未來科技巨頭在進行技術授權時，勢必會將收購方背景與合規歷史納入更嚴格的審查範疇。
儘管 OpenAI 將此舉定調為合規性與安全性問題，但外界觀點仍存在分歧。部分市場觀察家認為，這不僅是單純的合約爭議，更是 OpenAI 為了防堵競爭對手馬斯克在 AI 領域擴張版圖的策略性手段。然而，亦有法律專家提醒，在缺乏法院判決證實違約的情況下，不應過度解讀 OpenAI 的單方面指控，以免忽略了商業併購後技術整合的複雜性。此外，將技術供應與企業所有權掛鉤，是否會對全球 AI 產業的開放性與創新環境造成負面影響，仍是產業界高度關注的焦點。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於幾個方面：首先是 SpaceX 是否會針對 OpenAI 的公開指控採取法律行動或發表澄清宣告；其次，Cursor 公司在失去 OpenAI 模型支援後，將採取何種技術應對措施，是否會轉向開源模型或其他競爭對手的服務；最後，此事件是否會引發監管機構對於 AI 技術授權壟斷或排他性競爭行為的調查。隨著 AI 產業競爭白熱化，類似因企業併購引發的技術供應鏈斷鏈事件，恐將成為未來全球科技產業治理的重要課題。
