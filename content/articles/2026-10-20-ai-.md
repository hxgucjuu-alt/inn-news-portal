  # Rising Tensions in the Middle East and Global Economic Transformation: The Interplay of AI Impact and Regional Conflict
  
  > Recent global focus has centered on escalating conflicts in the Middle East, accompanied by the profound impact of artificial intelligence (AI) on economic structures. Federal Reserve Governor Kevin Warsh has highlighted AI as a key transformation, while the Middle East faces severe challenges, including airstrikes in Yemen and diplomatic tensions with Iran.
  
  In mid-October 2026, the global geopolitical and economic landscape experienced significant volatility. In the Middle East, conflict within Yemen escalated; according to the Associated Press, an airstrike resulted in at least nine deaths. The area is controlled by the Iranian-backed Houthi militant group, which continues to engage in hostilities with forces supported by Saudi Arabia. Meanwhile, the Iranian Foreign Ministry summoned the German ambassador to protest allegations made by the German side, which the official Iranian news agency Tasnim described as "baseless." These events underscore the fragile stability of the Middle East under the influence of transnational power struggles, and as the scope of the conflict expands, regional security risks are becoming a focal point for the international community.
  
  In the realms of economy and technology, there is a high degree of consensus regarding the influence of artificial intelligence. U.S. Federal Reserve Governor Kevin Warsh, speaking at the annual meetings of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the World Bank in Bangkok, explicitly stated that artificial intelligence is the "most consequential economic shift" he has seen in his lifetime. Warsh predicted that while AI will bring positive economic benefits, its development process may be accompanied by a "bumpy" adjustment period.
  
  This view echoes research data from the technology sector. Peter McCrory, head of economic research at Anthropic, recently published an analysis noting that despite the two-year explosion of generative AI technology, the U.S. unemployment rate has not risen as a result. Data suggests that AI currently tends to function as a "labor-augmenting technology," assisting humans in filling weak links in complex tasks.
  
  However, there remains divergence and room for observation regarding the long-term impact of AI on the labor market. Although Anthropic's research emphasizes that unemployment in highly exposed occupations has not yet been impacted, such studies largely focus on data analysis from developed countries. There is no consensus yet on whether the proliferation of AI technology and labor market adaptability will yield different results in regions like the Middle East, which are currently in the midst of conflict, turmoil, or industrial transformation. Furthermore, whether the "bumpy" outlook mentioned by Warsh implies that future financial policies will undergo significant adjustments in response to the productivity shifts brought about by AI remains to be verified by subsequent financial markets.
  
  As for the specific casualty details of the Middle East conflict and the subsequent impact of diplomatic disputes, sources from all sides remain limited to initial announcements following the events, with no signs of de-escalation appearing yet.
  
  These events have had a direct impact on global policy and industrial development. For the Middle East, ongoing airstrikes and diplomatic tensions not only threaten regional security but may also disrupt the stability of global energy supplies and trade routes, thereby affecting the pace of global economic recovery. For the industry, Warsh's remarks indicate that global financial regulators are closely monitoring how AI is reshaping economic structures, which may prompt countries to formulate more proactive technology investment and talent training policies in the future.
  
  At the same time, smart city transformations promoted by local governments, such as Yunlin County, demonstrate that under the AI wave, governments at all levels are attempting to enhance industrial competitiveness through smart governance, transforming traditional industries into technology-oriented sustainable cities, which aligns with the global trend of pursuing technological upgrades.
  
  Although the development of AI brings optimistic economic expectations, analysts warn against over-interpreting single research data points. While the Anthropic report points out that unemployment has not risen due to AI, this does not mean that the impact of AI on all industries is positive. Furthermore, the conflict in the Middle East is highly complex, involving proxy wars and great power competition; the escalation of a single event is often accompanied by deep-seated political structural issues and should not be simplified as a mere military conflict. For investors and policymakers, balancing the technological dividends brought by AI with the risks posed by geopolitics will be the greatest challenge in the coming years.
  
  The focus for subsequent observation lies in whether the Middle East conflict will further expand, particularly whether the diplomatic tug-of-war between Iran and Western nations will evolve into broader sanctions or confrontation. Simultaneously, the tracking of global economic data regarding the labor substitution effect of AI will continue, especially as AI technology moves from generative applications into deeper integration with physical industries, to see if its impact on the job market will undergo a structural shift. Additionally, how the Federal Reserve's view on the productivity gains brought by AI will be reflected in future monetary policy will also be a key indicator for observing the stability of global financial markets. The international community will continue to monitor how these transnational events intertwine to affect the global political and economic landscape.

# 中東局勢升溫與全球經濟變革：AI 影響與區域衝突交織

> 近期全球焦點集中於中東地區衝突加劇，伴隨人工智慧（AI）對經濟結構的深遠影響。聯準會主席華許強調 AI 為關鍵變革，而中東地區則面臨葉門空襲與伊朗外交緊張等嚴峻挑戰。


二零二六年十月中旬，全球地緣政治與經濟局勢出現顯著波動。在中東地區，葉門境內衝突升溫，據美聯社報導，一場空襲造成至少九人死亡，該區域由伊朗支援的胡塞武裝組織控制，並與沙烏地阿拉伯支援的勢力持續交火。與此同時，伊朗外交部召見德國大使，針對德方提出的指控進行抗議，伊朗官方媒體塔斯尼姆通訊社稱該指控為「毫無根據」。這些事件凸顯了中東地區在跨國勢力角力下的脆弱穩定性，隨著衝突範圍擴大，區域內的安全風險正成為國際社會關注的焦點。
在經濟與科技領域，全球對於人工智慧的影響力達成高度共識。美國聯準會主席華許（Kevin Warsh）於曼谷召開的國際貨幣基金（IMF）與世界銀行年度會議中明確指出，人工智慧是他一生中所見過對全球經濟「影響最深遠的變革」。華許預測，儘管 AI 將帶來積極的經濟效益，但其發展過程可能伴隨「崎嶇坎坷」的調整期。
此觀點與科技界的研究資料形成呼應，Anthropic 經濟研究負責人彼得·麥克羅裡（Peter McCrory）近期發表分析指出，儘管生成式 AI 技術爆發已達兩年，但美國的失業率並未因此上升，資料顯示 AI 目前更傾向於作為「勞動力增強技術」，協助人類補足複雜任務中的弱環節。
然而，關於 AI 對勞動力市場的長期影響，各界仍存在分歧與觀察空間。雖然 Anthropic 的研究強調高暴露職位的失業率目前未受衝擊，但這類研究多集中於已開發國家的資料分析，對於中東等正處於衝突動盪或產業轉型期的地區，AI 技術的普及與勞動力市場的適應性是否會產生不同結果，目前尚無定論。此外，華許提到的「崎嶇坎坷」前景，是否暗示未來金融政策將因應 AI 帶來的生產力變革進行大幅調整，仍有待後續金融市場的實證。
至於中東衝突的具體傷亡細節與外交糾紛的後續影響，各方來源目前僅停留在事件發生後的初步宣告，尚未出現緩解跡象。
這些事件對全球政策與產業發展產生了直接影響。對中東而言，持續的空襲與外交緊張不僅威脅區域安全，更可能幹擾全球能源供應與貿易路線的穩定，進而影響全球經濟復甦的步調。對產業而言，華許的談話顯示，全球金融監管機構正密切關注 AI 如何重塑經濟結構，這可能促使各國在未來制定更為積極的科技投資與人才培訓政策。
與此同時，如雲林縣等地方政府推動的智慧城市轉型，顯示出在 AI 浪潮下，各級政府正試圖透過智慧治理提升產業競爭力，將傳統產業轉型為科技導向的永續城市，這與全球追求技術升級的趨勢不謀而合。
儘管 AI 的發展帶來樂觀的經濟預期，但分析人士提醒，不應過度解讀單一研究資料。Anthropic 的報告雖指出失業率未因 AI 上升，但這並不代表 AI 對所有產業的衝擊均為正面。此外，中東地區的衝突具有高度複雜性，涉及代理人戰爭與大國博弈，單一事件的升溫往往伴隨著深層的政治結構問題，不應將其簡化為單純的軍事衝突。對於投資者與政策制定者而言，如何在 AI 帶來的技術紅利與地緣政治帶來的風險之間取得平衡，將是未來數年內最大的挑戰。
後續觀察重點在於中東衝突是否會進一步擴大，特別是伊朗與西方國家的外交角力是否會演變為更廣泛的制裁或對抗。同時，全球經濟資料對於 AI 勞動力替代效應的追蹤將持續進行，特別是當 AI 技術從生成式應用進入更深層的實體產業整合時，其對就業市場的影響是否會發生結構性轉變。此外，聯準會對於 AI 帶來的生產力提升如何反映在未來的貨幣政策中，亦將是觀察全球金融市場穩定性的關鍵指標。國際社會將持續關注這些跨國事件如何交織影響全球的政經格局。
