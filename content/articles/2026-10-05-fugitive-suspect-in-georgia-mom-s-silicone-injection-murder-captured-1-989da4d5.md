喬治亞州一名涉嫌謀殺的逃犯Tamaira Angell Smith，在逃亡長達17年後，終於被警方逮捕。資料顯示，她的逃亡生涯因一次駕照續期申請中的失誤而告終，當時她使用了竊取的身份。警方證實了這次逮捕，為這場漫長的追捕行動畫上了句號。

Smith被通緝的原因，是與喬治亞州格威內特縣（Gwinnett County）一名四個孩子的母親之死有關。報導指出，這名受害者在接受矽膠注射後不幸身亡，警方稱這些注射是由Smith所施打。這起案件懸而未決多年，受害者的家人近二十年來一直在等待正義的伸張。

這次逮捕的突破點，發生在Smith試圖使用竊取身份續期駕照時。這次「DMV失誤」引起了當局的注意，最終導致了她的落網。資料顯示，她的被捕預計將為受害者的孩子和家人帶來遲來的慰藉，他們在17年的不確定中度過。這起案件的成功偵破，也彰顯了執法部門即使在多年之後，仍堅持不懈追捕逃犯的決心。

## English edition

Tamaira Angell Smith, a fugitive wanted for felony murder in Georgia, has finally been apprehended after 17 years on the run. Her lengthy evasion reportedly came to an end due to a slipup during a driver's license renewal request, where she used a stolen identity. Police confirmed the arrest, bringing a conclusion to a prolonged manhunt.

Smith was sought in connection with the death of a Loganville mother of four in Gwinnett County. Reports indicate the victim tragically died following silicone injections, which authorities allege were administered by Smith. This case has remained unresolved for years, with the victim's family awaiting justice for nearly two decades.

The breakthrough in the case occurred when Smith attempted to renew a driver's license using a stolen identity. This "DMV slipup" alerted authorities, ultimately leading to her capture. Her arrest is expected to provide long-awaited closure to the victim's children and family, who have endured 17 years of uncertainty. This successful apprehension underscores law enforcement's persistence in pursuing fugitives, even after many years.
