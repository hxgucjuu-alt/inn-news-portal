  # Rising Tensions in the Middle East Impact Global Energy Markets; Iran Threatens to Expand Scope of Retaliation
  
  > Frequent shipping attacks in the Persian Gulf have led to a rise in global crude oil prices. Meanwhile, Iran has warned that it will take retaliatory actions beyond regional limits if the United States launches a military strike, sparking significant international concern.
  
  Tensions in the Middle East have recently returned to the center of global attention. According to reports from Arab News and Asharq Al-Awsat, frequent shipping attacks in the Persian Gulf and around the Strait of Hormuz have intensified market concerns over supply chain disruptions, directly driving up international oil prices. Brent crude oil futures rose by $2.28 to reach $102.28 per barrel, while U.S. West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude also climbed by $1.66 to reach $89.94 per barrel.
  
  Beyond geopolitical influences, this wave of oil price volatility has been exacerbated by uncertainty in energy supplies caused by reduced offshore production in the U.S. due to hurricane threats.
  
  On the military and diplomatic fronts, the situation in the Middle East faces the risk of further deterioration. As reported by Ajker Patrika, citing Reuters, Iran is currently assessing potential U.S. military strikes and is planning broader and more robust countermeasures. Sources indicate that Iran’s retaliation would not be limited to the region; if the U.S. launches a major military attack, Iran may expand its targets to include countries supporting U.S. operations, potentially extending the scope of attacks beyond the Middle East. This narrative aligns with recent reports from The Jerusalem Post regarding the activities of senior U.S. Department of Defense officials, indicating that the strategic standoff between the U.S. and Iran in the region is in a highly sensitive phase.
  
  Although media outlets generally agree on the correlation between rising oil prices and Iranian threats, there remains an information gap regarding the specific details of a potential military conflict. Iran's retaliation plans are currently based only on "anonymous sources" and have not been officially confirmed; furthermore, the specific nature of its "beyond the Middle East" targets remains unknown. Additionally, Asharq Al-Awsat specifically noted that the decline in U.S. domestic production due to hurricanes, occurring simultaneously with Middle East supply chain risks, complicates the analysis of oil price increases, making it difficult to attribute the rise solely to geopolitics.
  
  Such geopolitical crises have direct and profound impacts on the global energy industry, shipping security, and international policy. For countries reliant on Middle Eastern energy supplies, the security of shipping in the Strait of Hormuz is directly linked to national energy security and price stability. If Iran expands its retaliation to a global scale, it will force nations to re-evaluate their energy import routes and defense-diplomatic strategies. For multinational corporations, supply chain stability will face unprecedented challenges, especially against the backdrop of surging demand for electricity and energy due to the expansion of global data centers, where sharp fluctuations in energy prices will directly impact the operating costs of the technology industry.
  
  However, regarding current developments, analysts and international observers urge against over-interpreting a single military threat. Iran's hardline rhetoric has often been viewed in past geopolitical conflicts as a bargaining chip for diplomatic negotiations rather than an inevitable military action. At the same time, oil price fluctuations are influenced by multiple factors, including U.S. climate impacts, global demand forecasts, and policy adjustments by OPEC. Attributing all market volatility solely to military threats in the Middle East may overlook the inherent fragility of the global energy market's supply and demand structure, and excessive panic could lead to inaccurate market reactions.
  
  Future observation will focus on three key areas: first, the U.S. government's specific response to Iranian threats and whether it will take further military or diplomatic sanctions; second, the frequency and intensity of shipping attacks in the Persian Gulf, which will directly reflect the effectiveness of regional security defense mechanisms; and finally, the latest assessment reports on global crude oil inventories and supply chains from the International Energy Agency (IEA) and related energy institutions, which will be key indicators in determining whether oil prices will continue to climb. The international community will continue to monitor whether the U.S. and Iran, beyond public rhetoric, are engaging in secret diplomatic communications through third countries to prevent the conflict from spiraling out of control.

# 中東局勢升溫牽動全球能源市場，伊朗威脅擴大反擊範圍

> 中東地區因波斯灣航運攻擊事件頻傳，導致全球原油價格上漲。同時，伊朗警告若美國發動軍事打擊，將採取超越區域限制的報復行動，引發國際高度關注。


中東地區的緊張局勢近期再度成為全球焦點。根據《Arab News》與《Asharq Al-Awsat》報導，由於波斯灣與荷姆茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz）周邊的航運攻擊事件頻發，市場對供應鏈中斷的擔憂情緒加劇，直接推升了國際油價。布蘭特原油期貨價格上漲 2.28 美元，達到每桶 102.28 美元；美國西德州中級原油（WTI）亦上漲 1.66 美元，來到每桶 89.94 美元。
此波油價波動除受地緣政治影響外，美國因颶風威脅導致離岸生產減少，亦加劇了能源供給的不確定性。
在軍事與外交層面，中東局勢面臨進一步惡化的風險。據《Ajker Patrika》引述路透社報導，伊朗正針對美國可能發動的軍事打擊進行評估，並計畫採取更廣泛且強硬的應對措施。訊息來源指出，伊朗的報復手段不僅限於區域內，若美國發動重大軍事攻擊，伊朗可能將目標擴大至支援美國行動的國家，甚至將攻擊範圍延伸至中東地區之外。此一威脅論述與《The Jerusalem Post》近期報導的美國國防部高層動態相互呼應，顯示美伊兩國在區域內的戰略對峙正處於高度敏感期。
儘管各方媒體對於油價上漲與伊朗威脅的關聯性有高度共識，但針對具體的軍事衝突細節，目前仍存在資訊落差。伊朗方面的報復計畫目前僅止於「匿名來源」透露，尚未獲得官方正式證實，且其所謂「超越中東地區」的攻擊目標具體為何，目前仍屬未知資訊。此外，《Asharq Al-Awsat》特別提到美國國內因颶風導致的產能下降，這與中東供應鏈風險同時發生，使得油價上漲的歸因分析變得複雜，難以單一歸咎於地緣政治。
此類地緣政治危機對全球能源產業、航運安全及國際政策具有直接且深遠的影響。對於仰賴中東能源供應的國家而言，荷姆茲海峽的航運安全直接關乎國家能源安全與物價穩定。若伊朗將反擊範圍擴大至全球，將迫使各國重新評估能源進口路徑與國防外交策略。對於跨國企業而言，供應鏈的穩定性將面臨前所未有的挑戰，特別是在全球資料中心擴建、對電力與能源需求激增的背景下，能源價格的劇烈波動將直接衝擊科技產業的營運成本。
然而，針對目前的局勢發展，分析師與國際觀察家呼籲不應過度解讀單一軍事威脅。伊朗的強硬言論在過去多次地緣衝突中常被視為外交談判的籌碼，而非必然發生的軍事行動。同時，油價的漲跌受到多重因素影響，包括美國的氣候影響、全球需求預期以及石油輸出國組織的政策調整。將所有市場波動完全歸因於中東的軍事威脅，可能忽略了全球能源市場供需結構本身的脆弱性，過度恐慌可能導致市場反應失準。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個面向：首先是美國政府針對伊朗威脅的具體回應，以及是否會採取進一步的軍事或外交制裁；其次是波斯灣航運攻擊事件的頻率與強度，這將直接反映區域安全防禦機制的有效性；最後，國際能源署（IEA）及相關能源機構對全球原油庫存與供應鏈的最新評估報告，將是判斷油價是否會持續攀升的關鍵指標。國際社會將持續關注美伊雙方在公開喊話之外，是否透過第三國進行秘密外交溝通，以避免衝突進一步失控。
