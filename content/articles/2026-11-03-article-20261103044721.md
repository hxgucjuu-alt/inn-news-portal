  # Cyberattack on Carrefour Belgium Causes Cold Chain System Malfunctions and Operational Disruptions
  
  > Carrefour Belgium recently suffered a cyberattack that caused temperature anomalies in its in-store refrigeration and freezer units. Officials confirmed the incident was a deliberate act by hackers and are currently working on repairs, emphasizing that the overall impact on operations remains limited.
  
  In recent years, the global retail industry has faced increasingly severe cybersecurity threats, with digital infrastructure becoming a primary target for hackers. Carrefour Belgium recently experienced a serious cyberattack that caused malfunctions in the refrigeration and freezer units at several of its supermarkets, leading to abnormal temperature spikes. According to reports, the incident occurred between Sunday night and Monday morning, affecting not only the monitoring systems of the cold chain equipment but also posing a potential threat to the storage safety of fresh food in the stores.
  
  This incident once again highlights that Internet of Things (IoT) devices in commercial environments can easily become entry points for cyberattacks if they lack sufficient security protections, thereby impacting the stability of essential public infrastructure.
  
  According to multiple media reports, Carrefour Belgium spokesperson Mich Vergauwen has publicly confirmed that the equipment malfunction was indeed caused by a cyberattack, stating clearly that there was "no other cause." Currently, technical teams are working at full capacity to repair the systems and restore the normal operation of the refrigeration units. Although the attack caused fluctuations in equipment temperatures, Carrefour officials emphasized that the impact on the company's overall operations is "very limited." The company is currently working with cybersecurity experts to trace the source of the attack and strengthen system defenses to prevent similar incidents from recurring.
  
  Although Carrefour officials confirmed the nature of the attack, key information regarding the specific identity of the hackers, the methods used, and whether ransomware was involved remains under investigation, with no further public disclosures at this time. Furthermore, details such as the specific number of affected stores, the scale of damaged food, and whether customer data was compromised remain subject to follow-up investigation reports. Media descriptions of the exact timing and scope of the incident are currently based only on the spokesperson's preliminary statements, and it is not yet possible to fully assess the long-term cybersecurity ripple effects of this attack on the Belgian retail market.
  
  The impact of this incident on the retail industry should not be underestimated, especially for the cold chain logistics sector, which relies on automated monitoring systems; this serves as a serious warning. As retailers accelerate the integration of smart connected devices to improve operational efficiency, these devices—if not subjected to rigorous security testing—will become springboards for hackers to infiltrate corporate internal networks. For the local Belgian retail market, this not only affects the consumer shopping experience but also forces operators to re-evaluate the vulnerabilities of their digital infrastructure and invest more resources in cybersecurity and disaster recovery plans to ensure food safety and supply chain stability.
  
  There are differing perspectives regarding this attack. Some cybersecurity experts believe that such attacks on IoT devices reflect a tendency in corporate digital transformation to prioritize convenience while neglecting the construction of a foundational security architecture. However, other viewpoints caution that until official investigations are fully disclosed, one should not over-interpret the motives and impact of the attack to avoid inciting unnecessary panic. For the general public, the primary concern remains food safety; however, Carrefour has stated that the impact is limited and has not issued large-scale food recall notices, suggesting that the extent of the damage may still be within a manageable range.
  
  Future observation will focus on whether Carrefour Belgium releases a detailed analysis report of the attack and whether relevant regulatory agencies intervene to investigate potential lapses in the company's cybersecurity defenses. Additionally, whether this incident triggers a re-examination of IoT security standards within the European retail industry is also a point of concern for the sector. In the future, how companies balance smart operations with cybersecurity will become a key indicator of the success or failure of digital transformation in the retail industry. Relevant authorities have also suggested that all companies relying on automated systems should immediately inspect their network interfaces and update security software to cope with an increasingly complex cybersecurity environment.

# 比利時家樂福遭網路攻擊導致冷鏈系統異常與營運受影響

> 比利時家樂福近期遭受網路攻擊，導致店內冷藏與冷凍裝置溫度異常。官方證實此為人為駭客行為，目前正進行修復，並強調對整體營運影響有限。


近年來，全球零售業面臨日益嚴峻的網路安全威脅，數位化基礎設施已成為駭客攻擊的主要目標。比利時家樂福（Carrefour Belgium）於近期發生一起嚴重的網路攻擊事件，導致該公司旗下多家超市的冷藏與冷凍裝置運作異常，溫度出現異常飆升。根據相關報導指出，此事件發生於週日晚間至週一上午之間，不僅影響了冷鏈裝置的監控系統，更對店內生鮮食品的儲存安全構成潛在威脅。
此次事件再次凸顯了物聯網（IoT）裝置在商業環境中若缺乏足夠的安全防護，極易成為網路攻擊的突破口，進而影響民生基礎設施的穩定性。
綜合多家媒體報導，比利時家樂福發言人米奇·維爾高文（Mich Vergauwen）已公開證實，此次裝置異常確實是由於網路攻擊所致，並明確表示「沒有其他原因」。目前，相關技術人員正全力進行系統修復，以恢復冷藏裝置的正常運作。雖然攻擊造成了裝置溫度的波動，但家樂福官方強調，此次事件對公司整體的營運影響屬於「非常有限」的範圍。該公司目前正與資安專家合作，追查攻擊來源並強化系統防禦能力，以防止類似事件再次發生。
儘管家樂福官方確認了攻擊性質，但關於駭客的具體身份、攻擊手法以及是否涉及勒索軟體等關鍵資訊，目前仍處於調查階段，尚未有進一步的公開說明。此外，受影響的具體門市數量、受損食品的規模，以及是否涉及客戶資料外洩等細節，仍有待後續調查報告釐清。媒體報導中對於事件發生的確切時間點與影響範圍的描述，目前僅能根據發言人的初步說法進行確認，尚無法全面評估該次攻擊對比利時零售市場造成的長期資安連鎖反應。
此次事件對零售產業的影響不容小覷，特別是對於依賴自動化監控系統的冷鏈物流產業而言，這是一次嚴重的警訊。隨著零售商加速匯入智慧聯網裝置以提升營運效率，這些裝置若未經嚴格的資安檢測，將成為駭客入侵企業內網的跳板。對於比利時當地的零售市場而言，這不僅影響了消費者的購物體驗，也迫使業者必須重新評估其數位基礎設施的脆弱性，並投入更多資源於網路防禦與災難復原計畫，以保障食品安全與供應鏈穩定。
針對此次攻擊事件，外界觀點存在一定程度的分歧。部分資安專家認為，這類針對物聯網裝置的攻擊反映了企業在數位轉型過程中，往往過度追求便利性而忽略了基礎安全架構的建置。然而，也有觀點提醒，在官方調查尚未完全公開之前，不應過度解讀此次攻擊的動機與影響力，避免引發不必要的恐慌。對於一般消費者而言，最關切的仍是食品安全問題，但目前家樂福方面已表示影響有限，並未發布大規模的食品回收通知，顯示受影響程度可能仍在控管範圍之內。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於比利時家樂福是否會公佈詳細的攻擊分析報告，以及相關監管機構是否會介入調查，以釐清該公司在資安防護上的疏失。此外，該事件是否會引發歐洲零售業對於物聯網資安標準的重新審視，也是產業關注的焦點。未來，企業如何平衡智慧化營運與資安防護，將成為零售業數位轉型成敗的關鍵指標。相關單位也建議，所有依賴自動化系統的企業應立即檢查其網路介面，並更新防護軟體，以應對日益複雜的網路威脅環境。
