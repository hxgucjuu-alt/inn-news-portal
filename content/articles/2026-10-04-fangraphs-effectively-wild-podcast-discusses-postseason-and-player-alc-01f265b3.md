FanGraphs於2026年10月3日發布了其廣受歡迎的播客節目《Effectively Wild》的最新一集，編號為2539，題為「BAC in Action」。由Ben Lindbergh和Meg Rowley共同主持，本集提供付費內容的免費預覽，並邀請聽眾前往Patreon平台收聽完整內容。該集涵蓋了多個棒球相關的熱門話題，引發了廣泛關注。

節目中，兩位主持人首先預告了他們即將推出的首場季後賽直播，並就聽眾提出關於如何稱呼跨越多個月的季後賽的建議進行了輕鬆的討論。此外，他們還探討了體育播報員Jason Benetti與Liberty Biberty之間的關係，甚至分享了實時的文字訊息交流，為聽眾帶來了獨特的幕後視角。

本集的一個核心討論點聚焦於球員在俱樂部慶祝活動中，特別是奪冠慶祝時的真實酒精濃度（BAC）。資料顯示，節目中特別提到了日本球星村上宗隆及其他球員的酒精攝入情況，並提出了「BAC Blast?」的疑問，引發了對運動員健康與慶祝文化的思考。此外，主持人也探討了美國職棒大聯盟（MLB）是否應安排重疊的季後賽賽程。

除了上述話題，播客還回顧了外卡輪賽事的結束，並對未來的發展方向發表了看法。本集內容豐富，從賽事安排到球員文化，提供了深入的分析與討論。欲收聽更多精彩內容，聽眾可透過FanGraphs網站、iHeart.com及player.fm等平台獲取更多資訊，並前往Patreon支持節目。

## English edition

FanGraphs released the latest installment of its popular podcast, "Effectively Wild," episode 2539, titled "BAC in Action," on October 3, 2026. Hosted by Ben Lindbergh and Meg Rowley, this episode serves as a free preview of paid content, encouraging listeners to visit their Patreon for the full experience. The episode covers a range of topical baseball discussions, drawing significant attention.

During the program, the hosts teased their inaugural playoff livestream and engaged in a lighthearted discussion about a listener's suggestion for how to refer to a postseason that spans multiple months. Furthermore, they explored the relationship between sports announcer Jason Benetti and Liberty Biberty, even sharing a real-time text exchange, offering listeners a unique behind-the-scenes perspective.

A central point of discussion in this episode focused on the true alcohol intoxication levels (BAC) of players during clubhouse clinching celebrations. Reports indicate that the podcast specifically addressed the alcohol consumption of Japanese star Munetaka Murakami and other players, posing the question "BAC Blast?", which prompts reflection on athlete health and celebration culture. Additionally, the hosts debated whether Major League Baseball (MLB) should schedule overlapping playoff games.

Beyond these topics, the podcast also recapped the conclusion of the wild card round and offered opinions on future developments. This episode provides a rich array of content, from game scheduling to player culture, offering in-depth analysis and discussion. For more content, listeners can find information via FanGraphs, iHeart.com, and player.fm, and support the show on Patreon.
