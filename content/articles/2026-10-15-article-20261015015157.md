  # China's Steel Exports Exceed 10 Million Tons for Five Consecutive Months, Impacting Global Steel Markets and Trade Order
  
  > China's steel exports reached 10.5 million tons in September, surpassing the 10-million-ton mark for the fifth consecutive month. This phenomenon reflects the price competitiveness of Chinese steel products, but it has also intensified market pressure on global trade partners and raised concerns regarding China's debt structure and economic growth model.
  
  The Chinese steel industry has recently demonstrated strong export momentum in the global market. According to the latest customs data, China's steel exports reached 10.5 million tons in September 2026, an increase of 0.3% compared to the same period last year. Notably, this marks the fifth consecutive month that China's steel exports have exceeded 10 million tons, setting a historical record since such data began to be tracked. Although the cumulative export volume for the first nine months of this year is down 2.6% compared to the same period last year, current export levels remain at historical highs, second only to the peak of 11.3 million tons recorded in December 2025.
  
  This trend indicates that the Chinese steel industry has maintained high export resilience despite pressure from import restrictions imposed by some trade partners, continuing to supply steel production capacity to the global market.
  
  Multiple sources confirm that the strong performance of China's steel exports is closely related to market price differentials. Macquarie analyst Florence Sun pointed out that the significant price gap between China's domestic market and overseas markets gives Chinese steel strong price competitiveness internationally. This price advantage has become the core driver keeping export volumes consistently high. Meanwhile, according to a report by The Wall Street Journal, China is currently mired in a vicious cycle of debt expansion, with total non-financial sector credit exceeding 300% of its Gross Domestic Product (GDP).
  
  This economic model, which relies on large-scale investment to drive technological and military power, has not only caused a sharp rise in debt burdens but has also further forced the Chinese steel industry to export its excess capacity, thereby creating spillover impacts on the economic stability of global trade partners.
  
  However, there are still differing views regarding the long-term impact of and market reactions to China's steel exports. While export data shows the strong position of Chinese steel in the international market, some trade partners have begun to implement import restrictions on Chinese steel to protect their domestic industries. It remains to be confirmed whether these trade protection measures will escalate further, and whether the Chinese government will adjust its subsidies or export policies for the steel industry in response to domestic debt pressure. Furthermore, the long-term impact of China's debt issues on global supply chains is still being closely monitored by economists and international financial institutions, with no definitive conclusion yet.
  
  For Oceania and the global steel industry, the continuous dumping of low-priced Chinese steel has a direct negative impact. As steel-consuming nations and trade participants in the region, Australia and New Zealand are facing intense competitive pressure from cheap Chinese steel on their domestic manufacturers. When China impacts the international market with monthly export volumes exceeding 10 million tons, global steel prices are bound to face downward pressure, which not only affects the profitability of steel companies but may also lead to a reduction in employment and investment within related industrial chains. In the current global economic environment, this trade imbalance has become a public issue that governments must address when formulating trade policies.
  
  Regarding this phenomenon, different perspectives suggest that the global trade situation should not be interpreted solely from a single set of export data. Some experts believe that the surge in China's steel exports is a result of global supply chain restructuring and changes in demand, rather than simple malicious dumping. However, other views emphasize that China's debt-driven economic model is highly uncertain; if domestic demand in China remains weak, its steel exports to the global market could further exacerbate trade frictions. Therefore, various parties have called for a comprehensive consideration of factors such as debt risk, industrial structural adjustment, and international geopolitics when assessing China's economic impact, so as to avoid oversimplification or one-sided interpretation.
  
  Future observation should focus on whether China's steel export volume will reach a turning point due to increased trade barriers, and whether the Chinese government will take further economic stimulus or industrial reform measures in the face of debt pressure. In addition, how Oceania countries such as Australia, New Zealand, and Pacific Island nations balance protecting their own economic interests while maintaining a free trade system will be key to future regional economic and trade policies. The continued tracking of China's debt risks by the Bank for International Settlements (BIS) and other international financial organizations will also be an important indicator for judging global economic trends.

# 中國鋼材出口量連五月破千萬噸，衝擊全球鋼鐵市場與貿易秩序

> 中國9月鋼材出口量達1,050萬噸，連續五個月突破千萬噸大關。此現象反映中國產鋼材的價格競爭優勢，但也加劇了全球貿易夥伴的市場壓力，並引發對中國債務結構與經濟成長模式的疑慮。


中國鋼鐵產業近期在全球市場展現了強勁的出口動能。根據最新海關資料顯示，2026年9月份中國鋼材出口量達到1,050萬噸，較去年同期成長0.3%。值得關注的是，這已是中國鋼材出口量連續第五個月突破1,000萬噸，創下自相關資料追蹤以來的歷史紀錄。儘管今年前九個月的累計出口量較去年同期略降2.6%，但目前的出口水準仍維持在歷史高位，僅次於2025年12月創下的1,130萬噸歷史峰值。
此一趨勢顯示，中國鋼鐵產業在面對部分貿易夥伴實施進口限制的壓力下，依然維持了極高的出口韌性，持續向全球市場輸出鋼材產能。
多項來源共同確認，中國鋼材出口的強勁表現與市場價差密切相關。麥格理分析師Florence Sun指出，由於中國國內市場與海外市場之間存在顯著的鋼材價差，使得中國產鋼材在國際市場上具備強大的價格競爭力。這種價格優勢成為推動出口量持續居高不下的核心動力。與此同時，根據《華爾街日報》的報導，中國目前正深陷債務擴張的惡性迴圈，非金融部門信貸總額已超過國內生產毛額（GDP）的300%。
這種以大規模投資推動科技與軍事強權的經濟模式，不僅導致債務負擔急遽攀升，也進一步迫使中國鋼鐵產業必須透過出口來消化過剩產能，從而對全球貿易夥伴的經濟穩定性產生了外溢衝擊。
然而，關於中國鋼鐵出口的長期影響與市場反應，各界觀點仍存在差異。雖然出口資料顯示中國鋼材在國際市場上的強勢地位，但部分貿易夥伴已開始針對中國鋼材採取進口限制措施，以保護本國鋼鐵產業。目前尚待確認的是，這些貿易保護措施是否會進一步升級，以及中國政府是否會因應國內債務壓力，調整其對鋼鐵產業的補貼或出口政策。此外，中國債務問題對全球供應鏈的長遠影響，目前仍處於各方經濟學家與國際金融機構密切觀察的階段，尚未有定論。
對於大洋洲及全球鋼鐵產業而言，中國鋼材的持續低價傾銷具備直接的負面影響。澳洲與紐西蘭作為區域內的鋼鐵需求國與貿易參與者，其國內鋼鐵製造商正面臨來自中國廉價鋼材的劇烈競爭壓力。當中國以高於千萬噸的月出口量衝擊國際市場時，全球鋼鐵價格勢必面臨下行壓力，這不僅影響鋼鐵企業的獲利能力，也可能導致相關產業鏈的就業與投資縮減。在當前全球經濟環境下，這種貿易失衡現象已成為各國政府在制定貿易政策時必須正視的公共議題。
針對此一現象，不同觀點指出，不應僅從單一的出口資料解讀全球貿易局勢。部分專家認為，中國鋼材出口量大增是全球供應鏈重組與需求變化的結果，而非單純的惡意傾銷。然而，也有觀點強調，中國債務驅動的經濟模式具有高度不確定性，若中國國內需求持續疲軟，其對全球市場的鋼材輸出恐將進一步惡化貿易摩擦。因此，各界呼籲在評估中國經濟影響時，必須綜合考量其債務風險、產業結構調整以及國際地緣政治等多重因素，避免過度簡化或單一解讀。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於中國鋼材出口量是否會因貿易壁壘的增加而出現轉折，以及中國政府在面對債務壓力下，是否會採取進一步的經濟刺激或產業改革措施。此外，大洋洲國家如澳洲、紐西蘭與太平洋島國，如何在保障自身經濟利益與維持自由貿易體系之間取得平衡，將是未來區域經貿政策的關鍵。國際清算銀行（BIS）及其他國際金融組織對中國債務風險的持續追蹤，亦將是判斷全球經濟走勢的重要指標。
