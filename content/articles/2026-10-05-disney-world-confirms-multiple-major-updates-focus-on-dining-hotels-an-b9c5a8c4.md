根據disneyfoodblog.com於2026年10月4日的報導，迪士尼世界確認了三項主要更新，重點聚焦於新的餐飲選擇、新餐廳以及新的酒店細節。這些消息在本週成為園區內的熱門話題，為遊客帶來了對未來體驗的期待。

這些具體更新與稍早於2026年8月舉行的D23：終極迪士尼粉絲活動中揭示的更廣泛計畫相互關聯。insidethemagic.net和disneydining.com的報導指出，D23活動確認了華特迪士尼世界度假區將進行大規模擴建和重大變革，涵蓋其佛羅里達州的所有四個主題公園。

D23活動由尼爾·派屈克·哈里斯主持，呈現了一系列歷史性的公告，預計將從根本上改變華特迪士尼世界度假區的景觀。資料顯示，這些計畫包括針對魔法王國（Magic Kingdom）的「反派之地」（Villains Land）等主題更新，以及其他園區的廣泛發展。

綜合來看，從新的美食體驗到酒店住宿的提升，迪士尼世界正準備迎接一個轉型時期。這些更新不僅為遊客提供了更多選擇，也反映了迪士尼在主題公園領域持續投資和創新的承諾。

## English edition

According to disneyfoodblog.com on October 4, 2026, Disney World confirmed three major updates, focusing on new food options, new restaurants, and new hotel details. These announcements were the talk of the town this week, building anticipation for future guest experiences.

These specific updates are linked to broader plans revealed earlier at D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event in August 2026. Reports from insidethemagic.net and disneydining.com indicate that the D23 event confirmed massive expansions and significant changes coming to the Walt Disney World Resort, encompassing all four of its Florida theme parks.

Hosted by Neil Patrick Harris, the D23 event delivered a historic lineup of announcements expected to fundamentally transform the landscape of Walt Disney World Resort. Data suggests these plans include themed updates such as "Villains Land" for Magic Kingdom, alongside extensive developments across other parks.

Collectively, from new culinary experiences to enhanced hotel accommodations, Disney World is preparing for a period of transformation. These updates not only offer more choices for visitors but also reflect Disney's ongoing commitment to investment and innovation in the theme park sector.
