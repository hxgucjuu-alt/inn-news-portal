  A child protection case that has drawn significant public attention recently occurred in Taipei, involving a 10-year-old girl who was subjected to long-term sexual molestation by her mother's sex work clients. Despite the relevant authorities receiving reports, the girl was not placed in a safe environment for 240 days. This case not only exposed potential loopholes in the execution of child protection mechanisms but also sparked intense debate among politicians and the public regarding whether the city government's handling procedures were inappropriate. Addressing external questioning, Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an publicly explained during a city council inquiry on August 28, 2026, that the city government had immediately intervened through inter-departmental units, including the Domestic Violence Prevention Center and the Police Department, to conduct safety assessments on the case.
  
  According to Chiang Wan-an’s explanation at the council, the city’s social work team followed professional standards to conduct a "nine major risk factors" assessment of the girl's situation. The results showed that eight of these indicators were negative, leading to the determination that the case did not meet the criteria for immediate placement at that time. Chiang emphasized that the city government feels deep pain and regret regarding such cases and is not inactive, but must follow professional assessment results in its administrative procedures. Meanwhile, DPP Taipei mayoral candidate Shen Po-chen issued a stern criticism of the city government's attitude. He argued that the city should not shift responsibility onto frontline social workers, but should instead establish a more robust system. He warned that if the city government stubbornly refuses to review its practices, it will cause "internal hemorrhaging" within the administrative system.
  
  Currently, there is a significant gap in how various parties perceive this case, with the main point of contention being the disconnect between "professional assessment" and "actual risk." Media reports indicate that the city government insists the social workers' assessment results serve as the professional basis for their decision that the threshold for placement was not met. However, critics question whether the assessment mechanism is too rigid, resulting in the victimized girl remaining in a high-risk environment for 240 days. Furthermore, the scope of authority and accountability of social workers during the assessment process remains to be clarified. Whether the flaw lies in the assessment indicators themselves or in errors of judgment at the execution level remains inconclusive, and all parties are awaiting further investigation reports for clarification.
  
  This incident has had a direct impact on Taipei City's social welfare policies and child protection system. First, this case highlights that the current Domestic Violence Prevention Center’s definition of "placement standards" for sexual assault cases may differ from public expectations. Second, for social workers, the challenge of accurately executing risk factor assessments under heavy caseloads while balancing institutional requirements with practical realities has become a focal point for policy review. This concerns not only the handling of a single case but also affects the credibility of the city's entire child protection work and whether the domestic violence prevention mechanism requires structural adjustments and optimization in the future.
  
  Regarding the subsequent development of this case, opinions remain divided. Some public discourse suggests that the professional judgment of social workers should not be entirely dismissed due to the regret surrounding a single case, as excessive criticism could lead to the loss of social workers, thereby affecting overall service quality. Conversely, others emphasize that the original intent of the system is to protect the vulnerable, and if the assessment mechanism leads to an eight-month window of vulnerability, there is clearly room for review. Limitations and unknowns persist, as the public can only understand partial information through public inquiries, and complete case details involve the privacy of the minor and cannot be fully disclosed. This creates an information asymmetry that limits the public's ability to assess the city government's responsibility, and one should avoid over-interpreting any single narrative.
  
  Future observations will focus on whether the Taipei City Government will conduct a comprehensive review of the "child protection placement assessment mechanism." Beyond the internal administrative investigation by the city government, whether the city council will establish a special task force for oversight, and whether the Department of Social Welfare will adjust current assessment indicators to address potential loopholes, will be key points to watch. Furthermore, as the election approaches, whether this case will evolve into a focal point for policy-based political attacks, and whether the city government can propose concrete improvement measures to rebuild public confidence, both deserve continued attention.

# 臺北市女童遭性猥褻未安置案引發爭議，沈伯洋批評市府制度缺失

> 臺北市一名10歲女童遭長期性猥褻，通報240天後未獲安置。市長蔣萬安強調經社工評估未達標準，民進黨參選人沈伯洋則批評市府應檢討制度，勿將責任推卸給第一線社工。


臺北市近期發生一起引發社會高度關注的兒少保護案件，一名10歲女童長期遭到母親的性交易物件性猥褻，然而在相關單位接獲通報後的240天內，女童始終未被安置於安全環境。此案不僅揭露了兒少保護機制在執行層面的潛在漏洞，更引發了政治人物與社會大眾對於市府處理程式是否失當的激烈討論。針對外界質疑，臺北市長蔣萬安於2026年8月28日在議會備詢時公開說明，市府在接獲通報後，已由家防中心及警察局等跨局處單位立即介入，並針對個案進行安全評估。
根據蔣萬安在議會的說明，市府社工團隊在處理此案時，依照專業標準對女童處境進行了「九大危險因子」評估，結果顯示其中八項指標均為「否」，因此判定該個案當時未達到立即安置的標準。蔣萬安強調，市府對於此類案件深感心痛與不捨，並非毫無作為，但在行政程式上必須依循專業評估結果辦理。與此同時，民進黨臺北市長參選人沈伯洋則對市府的處理態度提出嚴厲批評，他認為市府不應將責任轉嫁給第一線社工，而是應當建立更為完善的制度，若市府一味拒絕檢討，將會造成行政體系的「內出血」。
目前各界對於此案的認知存在顯著落差，主要爭點在於「專業評估」與「實際風險」之間的認知鴻溝。媒體報導指出，市府堅持社工評估結果為專業依據，認為未達安置門檻；然而，批評者則質疑該評估機制是否過於僵化，導致在長達240天的期間內，受害女童仍處於高風險環境中。此外，關於社工在評估過程中的許可權與責任歸屬，目前仍有待進一步釐清。究竟是評估指標本身存在缺陷，還是執行層面的判斷失誤，目前尚無定論，各方說法仍需等待後續調查報告的釐清。
此事件對臺北市的社會福利政策及兒少保護體系產生了直接影響。首先，這起案件凸顯了現行家防中心在處理性侵害案件時，對於「安置標準」的認定可能與社會期待存在落差。其次，對於社工人員而言，如何在繁重的個案負擔下，精確執行危險因子評估，並在制度與實務之間取得平衡，已成為政策檢討的重點。這不僅關乎單一個案的處置，更影響到全市兒少保護工作的公信力，以及未來家防機制是否需要進行結構性的調整與最佳化。
針對此案的後續發展，各界觀點不盡相同。部分輿論認為，不應因單一個案的遺憾就全面否定社工的專業判斷，過度苛責可能導致社工流失，進而影響整體服務品質；但另一方觀點則強調，制度的設計初衷應是為了保護弱勢，若評估機制導致長達八個月的保護空窗期，則顯然存在檢討空間。限制與未知之處在於，目前大眾僅能透過公開質詢內容瞭解部分資訊，完整案情細節涉及兒少隱私，無法完全公開，這也使得外界在評估市府責任時存在資訊不對稱的限制，不應過度解讀單一說法。
後續觀察重點將集中在臺北市政府是否會針對「兒少保護安置評估機制」進行全面檢討。除了市府內部的行政調查外，議會是否會成立專案小組進行監督，以及社會局是否會調整現行的評估指標，以彌補可能存在的漏洞，將是未來觀察的重點。此外，隨著選舉將近，此案是否會進一步演變為政策攻防的焦點，以及市府能否提出具體的改善措施以重建公眾信心，均值得持續關注。
