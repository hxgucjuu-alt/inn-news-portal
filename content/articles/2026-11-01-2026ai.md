  # 2026 Global Asset and Technology Trends: AI-Driven Transformation in Finance and Healthcare
  
  > In 2026, the world faces geopolitical shifts and AI technological revolutions. The financial industry is addressing the "new normal" through international cooperation, while the healthcare and retail sectors are actively integrating Agentic AI technology to resolve labor shortages and efficiency challenges.
  
  By the end of October 2026, the global economy and technology industry reached a critical turning point. With intensifying geopolitical risks, supply chain restructuring, and the rapid iteration of artificial intelligence (AI) technology, companies worldwide are re-evaluating their asset allocation and operational strategies. In the financial sector, Cathay Financial Holdings hosted the "2026 Cathay Asset Management Summit" on October 30, bringing together six major international investment banks—BlackRock, PIMCO, J.P. Morgan, Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, and Neuberger Berman—to conduct in-depth discussions on the new normal of the global economy.
  
  Meanwhile, Cathay Life Insurance announced its membership in the Global Asia Insurance Partnership (GAIP). This initiative aims to foster cross-border collaboration among industry, government, and academia to jointly research topics such as AI applications, climate change, and retirement security, demonstrating that large financial institutions are actively strengthening their ability to manage complex risks through international cooperation.
  
  In terms of industrial application, AI technology has shifted from simple data analysis to "Agentic AI," which possesses autonomous decision-making capabilities. Lin Hung-jung, Superintendent of Chi Mei Medical Center, noted at a recent hospital information management conference in Taiwan that the hospital has advanced its AI development path to the fourth stage: "AI-First" and "Agentic AI" applications. Since entering the "AI Together" phase in 2023, Chi Mei Medical Center has successfully created the "A+ Ward Round Alliance" and the "A+ HIS Copilot" assistant, effectively assisting medical staff in organizing medical records and synchronizing patient information.
  
  The deployment of such technology not only reduces the administrative burden on medical personnel but also improves the precision of clinical decision-making, setting a new benchmark for smart healthcare.
  
  Simultaneously, the retail and payment industries are facing a global labor shortage crisis, with AI becoming a key driver in alleviating human resource gaps. Jackel Shen, Vice President of PAX Technology, analyzed that smart payment terminals like the Square Kiosk, empowered by AI, have upgraded payment points into "automated clerks." This system effectively optimizes human resource allocation in the physical food and beverage industry and enhances operational efficiency through automated processes.
  
  Although the AI application scenarios in finance, healthcare, and retail differ, there is a consensus that AI has evolved from a mere auxiliary tool into a critical infrastructure for core corporate competitiveness. It demonstrates significant potential for substitution and efficiency gains, particularly in handling highly repetitive tasks and complex data analysis.
  
  However, there are still varying priorities and limitations regarding the application of AI technology across different sectors. The financial industry is currently more focused on establishing standardized and security protocols through international organizational frameworks to address long-term risks posed by climate change and population aging. In contrast, the healthcare and retail sectors are more focused on resolving immediate labor shortages and automating operational workflows. Furthermore, despite the continuous increase in corporate investment in AI, ethical standards for Agentic AI in practical applications, data privacy protection, and technical bottlenecks in system integration remain challenges that all sectors must cautiously address.
  
  Regarding industrial observations in South America and global emerging markets, while this summit and industry forum primarily focused on Asia and the global macroeconomy, the shift in global asset management trends is highly relevant to capital market flows in the Andean region and the Southern Cone of South America. As global supply chains restructure, if the South American region can combine its rich resource advantages with the demand for digital transformation and participate in international financial cooperation organizations, it will help attract more international capital for local infrastructure and technology industries. However, investors should also be aware that geopolitical uncertainty under the global economic "new normal" could cause volatility in emerging market exchange rates and asset valuations; therefore, the short-term benefits brought by a single technology should not be interpreted with excessive optimism.
  
  Looking ahead, future observations will focus on three key aspects: First, whether large financial institutions can produce concrete research results in AI risk control and product design after joining international organizations like GAIP. Second, whether the penetration rate of Agentic AI in the healthcare industry can effectively reduce burnout among medical staff and improve patient safety. Finally, whether the automated payment ecosystem in the retail industry can be successfully expanded to more service sectors, thereby influencing wage structures and the job market in the global service industry. These developments will determine whether AI technology will act as an engine for economic growth or a potential factor in exacerbating the digital divide in the coming years.

# 2026全球資產與科技趨勢：AI驅動金融與醫療產業轉型

> 2026年全球面臨地緣政治與AI科技變革，金融業透過國際合作應對新常態，醫療與零售產業則積極匯入AI代理人技術，以解決缺工與效率挑戰。


2026年10月底，全球經濟與科技產業迎來關鍵轉折點。隨著地緣政治風險加劇、供應鏈重組及人工智慧（AI）技術的快速迭代，各國企業正重新審視其資產配置與營運策略。在金融領域，國泰金控於10月30日舉辦「2026國泰資產管理高峰會」，集結貝萊德、PIMCO、摩根大通、摩根士丹利、高盛及路博邁等六大國際投行，針對全球經濟新常態進行深入探討。
與此同時，國泰人壽亦宣佈加入「全球亞洲保險合作夥伴組織」（GAIP），旨在透過跨國產官學交流，共同研究AI應用、氣候變遷與退休保障等議題，顯示出大型金融機構正積極透過國際合作，強化對複雜風險的應對能力。
在產業應用層面，AI技術已從單純的資料分析轉向具備自主決策能力的「代理型AI」（Agentic AI）。奇美醫院院長林宏榮於近期臺灣醫院資訊主管會議中指出，該院已將AI發展路徑推進至第四階段，即「AI優先」與「代理型AI」應用。自2023年進入「AI伴行」（AI Together）階段，奇美醫院已成功打造A+巡房大聯盟及A+ HIS Copilot助手，有效協助醫護人員整理病歷與同步病人資訊。
此類技術的部署，不僅減輕了醫療人員的行政負擔，更提升了臨床決策的精準度，為智慧醫療樹立了新的標竿。
與此同時，零售與支付產業亦面臨全球性缺工危機，AI成為緩解人力缺口的關鍵推手。同亨科技副總經理沈宗鑫（Jackel）分析指出，Square Kiosk等智慧支付終端透過AI賦能，已將支付端升級為「自動化店員」。這種系統能有效最佳化實體餐飲業的人力配置，並透過自動化流程提升營運效率。
儘管金融、醫療與零售業在AI應用場景上有所不同，但各界均一致認同，AI已從過去的輔助工具，逐漸演變為企業核心競爭力的關鍵基礎設施，特別是在處理高重複性任務與複雜資料分析方面，展現出顯著的替代與增效潛力。
然而，目前各界對於AI技術的應用仍存在不同的發展側重點與限制。金融業目前更關注於如何透過國際組織的合作架構，建立標準化與安全性規範，以應對氣候變遷與人口高齡化帶來的長期風險；而醫療與零售業則更聚焦於解決當前的勞動力短缺與作業流程自動化問題。此外，儘管企業對AI的投入力度持續攀升，但關於代理型AI在實際應用中的倫理規範、資料隱私保護以及系統整合的技術瓶頸，仍是各界在推進過程中必須審慎面對的挑戰。
針對南美洲及全球新興市場的產業觀察，雖然本次高峰會與產業論壇多聚焦於亞洲及全球總體經濟，但全球資產管理趨勢的轉變，對於南美安地斯地區及南錐體國家的資本市場流動具有高度相關性。隨著全球供應鏈重組，南美地區若能結合其豐富的資源優勢與數位轉型需求，透過參與國際金融合作組織，將有助於吸引更多國際資金投入在地基礎建設與科技產業。然而，投資者亦需留意，全球經濟「新常態」下的地緣政治不確定性，恐對新興市場的匯率與資產估值造成波動，不應過度樂觀解讀單一技術帶來的短期效益。
展望未來，後續觀察重點將聚焦於三大面向：首先是大型金融機構在加入GAIP等國際組織後，能否在AI風險控管與商品設計上產出具體的研究成果；其次，醫療產業中代理型AI的普及率，是否能有效降低醫護人員的過勞現象並提升病人安全；最後，零售業的自動化支付生態系，是否能成功擴充套件至更多服務業態，進而影響全球服務業的薪資結構與就業市場。這些發展將決定AI技術在未來幾年內，是成為推動經濟成長的引擎，還是加劇數位落差的潛在因素。
