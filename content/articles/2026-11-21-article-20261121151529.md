  # The Global AI Investment Boom and Industrial Transformation: From Infrastructure Expansion to Commercial Subscription Challenges
  
  > Global tech giants are seeing a surge in AI infrastructure investment, raising concerns about a potential bubble. Meanwhile, AI agent technology and subscription-based business models are reshaping distribution channels and end-user markets.
  
  The rapid evolution of artificial intelligence (AI) technology is profoundly impacting the global tech industry's landscape. According to recent market trends, global tech giants continue to expand their investments in AI infrastructure. It is projected that by 2026, the combined investment from the five major giants—Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and Oracle—will exceed $800 billion, representing a 94% increase compared to 2025.
  
  Meanwhile, global distribution leader Ingram Micro announced in Colorado, USA, that it will fully integrate AI agents into its channel ecosystem. By utilizing server architectures that support the Model Context Protocol, AI assistants are taking over tedious processes such as procurement, quoting, and order processing, which has reportedly helped increase channel performance by nearly fourfold.
  
  Regarding the transformation of technical applications and business models, it is reported that Samsung Electronics will launch a "Galaxy AI Premium" subscription service. Expected to be priced at $4.99 per month (approximately NT$162), the service aims to provide users with greater computing quotas and transition some high-power AI features into paid-only offerings. This move reflects how tech companies are actively exploring profitable paths for AI commercialization.
  
  Furthermore, in terms of international cooperation and standard setting, Taiwan has launched its first 6G experimental network (6G-FORMOSA). This network connects with experimental networks in the EU and the UK, as well as international satellite operators, aiming to capture business opportunities from the 6G standards to be set by 3GPP in 2029, while emphasizing Taiwan's critical position in international testing bases.
  
  However, warning signs have emerged regarding the over-expansion of the AI industry. Economist Lamont pointed out that the probability of an AI bubble has risen to 70%, identifying Anthropic's market debut as a key indicator. Although Anthropic closed up 17% on its first day, AI-related stocks in both the US and Asian markets saw significant pullbacks, indicating investor anxiety over the gap between massive capital expenditures and actual profitability. Additionally, against the backdrop of the US-China tech war, the formulation of AI safety rules has become a focal point. Exchanges between the Anyuan AI delegation and OpenAI reveal the complex interaction between US-China technical blockades and safety dialogues, highlighting the sensitivity of the struggle for rule-making authority.
  
  These changes have had a concrete impact on the global industry. For the supply chain, the integration of AI agent technology has significantly improved operational efficiency, but it has also altered the operational logic of traditional distribution models. For end consumers, the gradual shift toward paid AI features means that users will likely face additional subscription costs when using high-end devices like smartphones in the future. For policymakers and industry leaders, how to promote AI infrastructure while guarding against bubble risks and participating in the formulation of international safety rules has become an unavoidable challenge.
  
  Although various data indicate that the AI industry is in a period of high-speed growth, analysts warn against over-interpreting the performance of individual companies. Current market concerns regarding AI investment are primarily focused on the risk of "circular financing," where tech giants pour money into AI infrastructure, which in turn supports the development of AI startups, creating a closed loop of capital. Furthermore, the attribution of the right to define AI safety remains in a stage of contention among nations; as a hub for chip manufacturing, Taiwan's risk of absence in rule-making is a limitation that must be addressed in future industrial development.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on three aspects: First, whether AI paid subscription models from terminal device manufacturers like Samsung can be widely accepted by the market and become an industry norm. Second, whether the $800 billion in infrastructure investment by the five major tech giants in 2026 can be converted into substantial revenue growth within the next two years to alleviate market concerns about a bubble. Finally, as the 6G standard-setting process advances, how international safety standards and cooperation mechanisms regarding the integration of AI and communication technologies will affect the restructuring of the global tech supply chain.

# 全球AI投資熱潮與產業變革：從基礎設施擴張到商業化訂閱挑戰

> 全球科技巨頭AI基礎設施投資額飆升，產業面臨泡沫化隱憂，同時AI代理技術與訂閱制商業模式正重塑經銷與終端應用市場。


人工智慧（AI）技術的快速演進正深刻影響全球科技產業佈局。根據近期市場動態，全球科技巨頭在AI基礎設施上的投入規模持續擴大，預計2026年Alphabet、亞馬遜、Meta、微軟與甲骨文五大巨頭的投入金額將超過8,000億美元，較2025年大幅成長94%。
與此同時，全球科技經銷龍頭英邁國際（Ingram Micro）於美國科羅拉多州宣佈，將AI代理（AI Agent）全面匯入通路生態系統，透過支援模型上下文協議（Model Context Protocol）的伺服器架構，讓AI助理接手採購、報價與訂單處理等繁瑣流程，據稱已使通路業績成長近四倍。
在技術應用與商業模式的轉型上，三星電子傳出將推出「Galaxy AI Premium」訂閱服務，預計以每月4.99美元（約新臺幣162元）的價格，提供使用者更強大的運算額度，並將部分高耗能AI功能轉為付費專屬。此舉反映出科技業者正積極探索AI商業化的獲利路徑。
此外，在國際合作與標準制定方面，臺灣已啟用首座6G實驗網（6G-FORMOSA），串聯歐盟、英國實驗網及國際衛星營運商，旨在搶佔2029年3GPP制定的6G標準商機，並強調臺灣在國際驗測基地中的關鍵地位。
然而，市場對於AI產業的過度擴張也出現了警訊。經濟學家拉蒙特（Lamont）指出，AI泡沫化機率已升至70%，並將Anthropic的上市表現視為關鍵指標。儘管Anthropic首日收漲17%，但美股與亞股的AI類股卻出現顯著回檔，顯示投資人對於巨額資本支出與實際獲利之間的落差感到擔憂。此外，美中科技戰背景下，AI安全規則的制定亦成為焦點，安遠AI代表團與OpenAI的交流，揭示了美中在技術封鎖與安全對話之間的複雜互動，凸顯出規則制定權爭奪的敏感性。
這些變革對全球產業產生了具體影響。對供應鏈而言，AI代理技術的匯入大幅提升了營運效率，但也改變了傳統經銷模式的運作邏輯；對終端消費者而言，AI功能逐漸走向付費化，意味著未來使用高階智慧型手機等裝置時，需承擔額外的訂閱成本。對於政策制定者與產業領袖，如何在推動AI基礎建設的同時，防範泡沫化風險並參與國際安全規則的制定，已成為無法迴避的課題。
儘管多方資料顯示AI產業正處於高速成長期，但分析師提醒，不應過度解讀單一企業的業績表現。目前市場對AI投資的擔憂主要集中在「迴圈融資」風險，即科技巨頭將資金投入AI基礎設施，而這些設施又反過來支撐AI新創公司的發展，形成封閉的資金迴圈。此外，AI安全定義權的歸屬仍處於各國角力階段，臺灣作為晶片製造重鎮，在規則制定上的缺席風險，亦是未來產業發展必須正視的限制。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個面向：首先，三星等終端裝置商的AI付費訂閱制是否能被市場廣泛接受，並成為產業常態；其次，五大科技巨頭在2026年投入的8,000億美元基礎建設，能否在未來兩年內轉化為實質的營收增長，以緩解市場對泡沫化的疑慮；最後，隨著6G標準制定程式推進，國際間在AI與通訊技術整合上的安全標準與合作機制，將如何影響全球科技供應鏈的重組。
