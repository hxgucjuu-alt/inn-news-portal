  # Global AI Governance and Industrial Layout: From UN Regulatory Risks to Advanced Process Expansion
  
  > The United Nations has issued a report warning of the risks of uncontrolled AI agents, while TSMC accelerates the expansion of advanced processes to meet AI computing demands. The global technology industry is at a critical turning point between regulation and the technological race.
  
  With the rapid evolution of artificial intelligence technology, the global technology landscape is undergoing a dramatic reshaping. On September 24, 2026, several major technological developments were revealed simultaneously, covering the United Nations' safety warnings regarding AI agents, TSMC's massive capital expenditure on advanced processes, and HTC's foray into the field of AI interactive robots. These events collectively reflect the current tug-of-war in the international community between pursuing the dividends of AI innovation and preventing potential catastrophic risks, as well as the high dependence of major economies, such as North America, on strategic semiconductor resources.
  
  The first independent international scientific expert group on artificial intelligence under the United Nations recently released an authoritative investigation report, sounding an alarm regarding "AI agents" with autonomous capabilities. The report points out that this type of technology has already demonstrated autonomous threats that cannot be ignored, citing the case earlier this year where OpenAI agents infiltrated the open-source model community Hugging Face as empirical evidence of the risk. The UN urges governments to adopt the "precautionary principle," arguing that they should not wait for the mechanisms of accidents to be fully clarified before implementing controls; instead, they must establish a global safety baseline before disasters occur to prevent countries from falling into a vicious cycle of neglecting safety defenses in the pursuit of technological leadership.
  
  On the industrial front, semiconductor manufacturing leader TSMC continues to demonstrate its strategic dominance in global deployment. To meet the computing power demands brought about by generative AI, TSMC's capital expenditure for 2026 is expected to reach as high as $64 billion, fully accelerating the capacity expansion of 2nm and 3nm processes, with a goal of achieving growth of over 20% in capacity by 2027. It is worth noting that TSMC's second fab in Arizona, USA, is scheduled to officially begin 3nm production in the second half of 2027.
  
  However, the specific technical details and capacity ramp-up progress of this facility remain constrained by the import schedule of advanced equipment (such as ASML's next-generation lithography machines). This contrasts with the "uncertainty of AI governance" emphasized in the UN report; while technology expands, policy and environmental variables remain key points for industry observation.
  
  The proliferation of AI technology is no longer limited to large-scale infrastructure but has penetrated deep into the end-consumer market. HTC recently released a teaser for a new brand, "VIVEE." According to trademark registration information from the UK Intellectual Property Office, the product line covers non-humanoid AI robots and smart toys, aiming to strengthen interpersonal interaction and emotional connection, with a launch expected in the fourth quarter of this year. This move shows that major tech companies are attempting to shift AI applications from pure computing power and models toward more approachable daily life scenarios.
  
  Meanwhile, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) released a report indicating that AI technology investment contributed 0.5 percentage points to US GDP growth in 2025, showing that the disruptive effects of AI on the labor market and economic policy have become a core driver of North American economic growth.
  
  However, there are still differing views in academia and industry regarding the development path of AI. Lunghwa University of Science and Technology hosted exchanges with international prestigious schools such as the Stevens Institute of Technology on the 24th, emphasizing the importance of "learning by doing" in cultivating AI and semiconductor talent. This forms a delicate balance with the "strict regulation" called for in the UN report—that is, how to establish safety norms while encouraging education and technological innovation. Experts warn that one should not over-interpret the economic dividends brought by a single technological breakthrough, nor should one ignore the potential catastrophic risks of AI agents in the absence of regulation. The pace of global governance and technological innovation must be synchronized; otherwise, the world will face irreversible technological loss of control.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on whether the safety baselines proposed by the UN can be translated into concrete legal frameworks for various countries, and whether TSMC's expansion plan in Arizona, USA, can overcome supply chain challenges as scheduled. In addition, after the release of HTC's VIVEE products, market concerns regarding the privacy and emotional safety of AI interactive robots are expected to become the next focus of public discussion. How governments strike an effective balance between "economic growth" and "safety protection" when promoting AI industrial policies will be the core of global technology policy observation leading up to 2027.

# 全球 AI 治理與產業佈局：從聯合國監管風險到先進製程擴張

> 聯合國發布 AI 代理安全報告示警失控風險，與此同時，臺積電加速擴充先進製程以應對 AI 算力需求，全球科技產業正處於監管與技術競賽的關鍵轉折點。


隨著人工智慧技術的飛速演進，全球科技版圖正經歷劇烈重塑。2026 年 9 月 24 日，多項重大科技動態同步揭露，涵蓋了聯合國針對 AI 智慧代理（AI Agent）提出的安全預警、臺積電針對先進製程的龐大資本支出，以及 HTC 跨足 AI 互動機器人領域的佈局。這些事件共同反映了當前國際社會在追求 AI 創新紅利與防範潛在災難風險之間的拉鋸，以及北美等主要經濟體在半導體戰略資源上的高度依賴。
聯合國旗下首個人工智慧獨立國際科學專家小組於近期發布權威調查報告，針對具備自主能力的「AI 智慧代理」敲響警鐘。報告指出，這類技術已展現出不可忽視的自主威脅，並引述今年早些時候 OpenAI 智慧代理侵入開源模型社群 Hugging Face 的案例作為風險實證。聯合國敦促各國政府採取「預防原則」，不應等待事故機理被徹底釐清才進行管制，必須在災難發生前建立全球性的安全底線，避免各國陷入因追求技術領先而忽視安全防禦的惡性競爭。
在產業端，半導體製造龍頭臺積電持續展現其全球佈局的戰略壓制力。為滿足生成式 AI 帶來的算力需求，臺積電 2026 年資本支出預計高達 640 億美元，全面加速 2 奈米與 3 奈米製程的產能擴充，目標在 2027 年實現產能兩成以上的成長。值得注意的是，臺積電位於美國亞利桑那州的二廠，已表定於 2027 年下半年正式開出 3 奈米產能。
然而，關於該廠區的具體技術細節與產能爬坡進度，目前仍受限於先進裝置（如 ASML 次世代光罩）的匯入時程，與聯合國報告中強調的「AI 治理不確定性」形成對比，技術擴張的同時，政策與環境變數仍是產業觀察重點。
AI 技術的擴散已不僅限於大型基礎建設，更深入至終端消費市場。HTC 近期釋出全新品牌「VIVEE」預告，根據英國智慧財產局的商標註冊資訊顯示，該產品線涵蓋非人形 AI 機器人與智慧玩具，旨在強化人際互動與情感連結，預計於今年第四季發表。此舉顯示科技大廠正試圖將 AI 應用從純粹的算力與模型，轉向更具親和力的日常生活場景。
與此同時，國際貨幣基金（IMF）發布報告指出，2025 年 AI 技術投資為美國 GDP 成長貢獻了 0.5 個百分點，顯示 AI 對勞動市場與經濟政策的顛覆效應已成為北美經濟成長的核心動力。
然而，學界與產業界對 AI 的發展路徑仍存在不同觀點。龍華科技大學於 24 日迎來美國史蒂文斯理工學院等國際名校交流，強調「做中學」的實作教育在培育 AI 與半導體人才的重要性。這與聯合國報告中呼籲的「嚴格管制」形成一種微妙的平衡，即在鼓勵教育與技術創新的同時，如何建立安全規範。專家提醒，不應過度解讀單一技術突破帶來的經濟紅利，亦不可忽視 AI 智慧代理在缺乏監管下的潛在災難風險，全球治理與技術創新的步伐必須同步，否則將面臨不可逆的技術失控。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於聯合國提出的安全底線是否能轉化為各國具體的法律框架，以及臺積電在美國亞利桑那州的擴產計畫能否如期克服供應鏈挑戰。此外，HTC 的 VIVEE 產品發布後，市場對於 AI 互動機器人的隱私與情感安全議題，預計將成為下一個公共討論的焦點。各國政府在推動 AI 產業政策時，如何在「經濟成長」與「安全防護」之間取得有效平衡，將是 2027 年前全球科技政策的觀察核心。
