  The European Commission has recently proposed a new set of regulations aimed at controlling short-term rental platforms such as Airbnb to address the housing shortage in tourist hotspots across Europe. The core objective of this proposal is to provide member states with a unified framework for housing management to tackle the social issues caused by the over-expansion of the short-term rental market, which has led to insufficient housing supply and skyrocketing rents for local residents. This policy involves not only the transformation of the tourism industry but also touches upon the balance between tourism development and the residential rights of citizens in major European cities. According to reports, the proposal is currently in the deliberation stage and must receive formal approval from EU member states and the European Parliament in the coming months to complete the legislative process and become legally binding.
  
  Regarding another major policy issue within the EU, new developments have emerged concerning sanctions against Russian oligarchs. According to Euronews, France and Slovakia have recently taken a joint stance, requesting the removal of specific Russian oligarchs from the sanctions list. This move has triggered widespread discussion within the EU. French officials responded by stating that Paris supports the overall policy of continuing sanctions against Russia, but declined to provide specific details regarding the delisting of individual oligarchs, citing confidentiality principles. This reflects the complex challenges the EU faces in implementing sanctions against Russia, involving not only political considerations but also legal procedures and the diplomatic strategies of individual member states.
  
  Regarding the two issues mentioned above, there are still some discrepancies and areas requiring confirmation. In terms of short-term rental regulations, although the EU has proposed a common framework, it remains undecided whether member states will adjust the execution details and the strictness of these regulations based on their local dependence on tourism; the differing positions of various countries during the parliamentary deliberation stage will be a key variable for future policy implementation. As for the issue of sanctions against Russian oligarchs, the specific names and reasons for the delisting requests from France and Slovakia remain highly confidential. Observers can only speculate on the underlying economic or diplomatic motives through internal EU diplomatic maneuvering, and relevant information awaits further official announcements from the EU.
  
  These policy changes have had a profound impact on the European region. If the new short-term rental regulations are successfully passed, they will directly impact the business models of platforms like Airbnb in Europe, forcing hosts and platform operators to operate under stricter transparency requirements. For cities in Southern and Western Europe that rely on tourism revenue, this will represent a significant market restructuring. On the other hand, the controversy over sanctions against Russian oligarchs demonstrates that Europe holds differing views on the boundaries and sustainability of economic sanctions when facing geopolitical conflicts. This not only affects the long-term economic and trade relations between Europe and Russia but also tests the consistency and cohesion of the EU's foreign policy.

Similarly, regarding the delisting requests for Russian oligarchs, the outside world should not over-interpret this as a comprehensive shift in the EU's policy toward Russia. # 歐盟推動短租管制新規與俄羅斯寡頭制裁爭議之進展

> 歐盟近期針對觀光熱點住房短缺問題擬定Airbnb短租管制新規，同時法國與斯洛伐克針對俄羅斯寡頭制裁名單持有不同意見，引發歐洲政經界高度關注。


歐盟執委會近期針對歐洲境內觀光熱點住房短缺問題，提出了一項旨在管制Airbnb等短租平臺的全新規範。該提案的核心目標，在於為各成員國提供一套統一的住房管理框架，以應對長期以來因短租市場過度擴張，導致在地居民住房供給不足、租金飆升的社會問題。此項政策不僅涉及旅遊產業的轉型，更觸及歐洲各大城市在觀光發展與居民居住權益之間的平衡。根據相關報導，該提案目前正處於審議階段，後續數月內仍須獲得歐盟成員國及歐洲議會的正式同意，方能完成立法程式並轉化為具備法律效力的規範。
在歐盟的另一項重大政策議題上，針對俄羅斯寡頭的制裁措施亦出現了新的動態。根據歐洲新聞臺（Euronews）報導，法國與斯洛伐克近期採取了共同立場，要求將特定俄羅斯寡頭從制裁名單中移除。這一舉動在歐盟內部引發了廣泛討論。法國官方對此回應，表示巴黎當局支援延續對俄製裁的整體政策，但針對個別寡頭的除名討論，則以保密原則為由拒絕提供具體細節。此舉反映出歐盟在執行對俄製裁時，除了政治考量外，亦面臨法律程式與個別成員國外交策略的複雜挑戰。
針對上述兩項議題，目前資訊仍存在部分差異與待確認之處。在短租管制方面，雖然歐盟已提出共同框架，但各成員國對於該規範的執行細節與寬嚴程度，是否會因應在地觀光依賴度而有所調整，目前尚無定論；各國在議會審議階段的立場分歧，將是未來政策落實的關鍵變數。而在俄羅斯寡頭制裁議題上，法國與斯洛伐克要求除名的具體名單與理由，目前仍處於高度保密狀態，外界僅能從歐盟內部的外交角力中推測其背後的經濟或外交動機，相關資訊仍有待歐盟官方進一步公告。
這些政策變動對歐洲地區產生了深遠的影響。短租管制新規若順利透過，將直接衝擊Airbnb等平臺在歐洲的營運模式，迫使房東與平臺業者必須在更嚴格的透明度要求下運作，這對於依賴觀光收益的南歐及西歐城市而言，將是一次重大的市場重組。另一方面，關於俄羅斯寡頭的制裁爭議，則顯示出歐洲在面對地緣政治衝突時，內部對於經濟制裁的邊界與持續性存在不同見解。這不僅影響歐洲與俄羅斯的長期經貿關係，也考驗著歐盟在對外政策上的一致性與凝聚力。
然而，針對上述事件，各界觀點仍存在顯著差異。部分支援短租管制的論者認為，此舉能有效遏止房價過熱，保障在地居民的居住正義；但也有產業分析師擔憂，過度嚴苛的監管可能導致觀光產業萎縮，進而衝擊後疫情時代的經濟復甦。同樣地，關於俄羅斯寡頭的除名請求，外界不應過度解讀為歐盟對俄政策的全面轉向。多數分析認為，這可能僅是針對特定法律訴訟或外交談判的技術性調整，若將其視為制裁體系的崩解，恐會誤判當前歐洲政經局勢的複雜性與制裁機制的韌性。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於兩方面：首先，歐盟議會針對短租管制新規的投票結果，以及各成員國如何將此框架轉化為地方法規，將直接決定該政策的實際執行力；其次，法國與斯洛伐克在俄羅斯寡頭制裁上的後續動作，以及歐盟執委會是否會針對此類除名請求發布統一的審核標準，將是觀察歐盟對俄製裁是否會出現「破口」的關鍵指標。INN新聞將持續追蹤這些政策在歐盟內部的立法程式與外交博弈，並為讀者提供最即時的後續報導。
