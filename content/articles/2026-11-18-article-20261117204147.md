Both sides engaged in in-depth consultations on issues including tariff adjustments, reciprocal tariff reduction plans of $30 billion each, and agricultural trade, aiming to establish a balanced bilateral relationship.
  
  On November 13, 2026, the two major economies of the US and China held the inaugural meeting of the "Board of Trade" under the "US-China Economic and Trade Consultation Mechanism" via video conference. The meeting was rooted in a prior bilateral consensus reached by the leaders of both nations, aimed at addressing long-standing economic and trade differences through institutionalized communication channels. The Chinese Ministry of Commerce released a statement on November 14, confirming that both sides had conducted substantive exchanges regarding tariff structures, agricultural trade, and other economic and trade issues of mutual concern.
  
  The Office of the United States Trade Representative (USTR) subsequently confirmed that the meeting had been held successfully, emphasizing that the Trump administration will continue to maintain contact with the Chinese side with the goal of building a fair, balanced, and stable bilateral trade framework.
  
  According to publicly available information, both the US and China confirmed progress on several core issues during the meeting. First, both sides have placed "reciprocal tariff reductions" at the center of the agenda, specifically involving tariff adjustment plans of $30 billion each. This move is seen as a key signal to ease bilateral trade tensions, indicating that both governments are attempting to inject stability into the global trade environment through concrete economic concessions. Furthermore, agricultural trade was a highlight of the consultations, involving not only the interests of US agricultural exporters but also China's domestic food security and market demand.
  
  Through the institutionalized Board of Trade, both sides hope to transform past fragmented trade disputes into predictable policy dialogues, marking a shift in US-China economic and trade relations from mere friction and confrontation to a more structural negotiation model.
  
  Although both sides hold a positive attitude toward the progress of the meeting, there remain different interpretations and areas requiring clarification. The Chinese Ministry of Commerce emphasized in its announcement that this meeting was a continuation of implementing previous consultation outcomes, focusing on the concretization of consensus. However, the USTR's announcement was relatively concise, emphasizing the principles of "continued engagement" and "fair trade," without making further commitments regarding a specific implementation timeline or supporting conditions for the $30 billion tariff reduction plan. This narrative discrepancy reflects the different negotiation strategies of the two countries: the Chinese side tends to emphasize the implementation and results of institutionalization, while the US side places greater value on the fairness and reciprocity of trade terms.
  
  Additionally, regarding whether the tariff reduction plan includes exemptions for specific industries or is accompanied by political conditions in non-trade areas, there are currently no detailed official documents available for verification. Market analysts generally suggest that subsequent implementation details should be closely monitored.
  
  The convening of this Board of Trade has profound implications for the global economy and trade landscape. As the world's two largest economies, any change in US-China trade policy directly affects the reconfiguration of global supply chains. If the reciprocal tariff reduction plan can be successfully implemented, it will help reduce import costs for North American companies and alleviate inflationary pressures caused by tariff barriers. For relevant industries, particularly agriculture, manufacturing, and cross-border logistics, the results of this meeting will serve as an important indicator for trade strategy adjustments over the next six months.
  
  At the same time, this provides a window for policymakers in North America to assess how to strike a balance between protecting domestic industries and maintaining global trade openness, avoiding systemic risks caused by over-reliance on a single market.
  
  However, experts caution against over-interpreting the immediate effectiveness of this meeting. Although the establishment of the Board of Trade represents the creation of a communication mechanism, significant differences remain between the US and China on deep-seated structural issues such as technology transfer, intellectual property protection, and digital trade. This meeting focused on traditional trade items like agricultural products and tariffs, which are areas where consensus is relatively easier to reach, and does not mean that all core contradictions have been resolved. Furthermore, the political atmosphere within the US and upcoming policy changes may also have unpredictable impacts on the negotiation process.

All parties will closely monitor the performance of both sides regarding specific trade data and policy implementation in the coming months to determine whether US-China economic and trade relations have truly entered a track of stable development.

# 中美貿易理事會召開首次正式會議，聚焦對等降稅及農產品貿易

> 中美經貿磋商機制項下貿易理事會於本月13日召開首次正式會議，雙方針對關稅調整、各300億美元對等降稅計畫及農產品貿易等議題展開深入磋商，致力建立平衡雙邊關係。


中美兩大經濟體於2026年11月13日透過視訊會議形式，正式召開「中美經貿磋商機制」項下的貿易理事會（Board of Trade）首次會議。此次會議背景源於中美元首先前達成的雙邊共識，旨在透過制度化的溝通管道，處理長期以來懸而未決的經貿分歧。中國商務部於11月14日對外發布訊息，確認雙方已針對關稅結構、農產品貿易以及其他共同關切的經貿議題進行實質性交流。
美國貿易代表辦公室（USTR）隨後亦證實會議已順利舉行，並強調川普政府將持續與中方保持接觸，目標在於構建一個公平、平衡且具備穩定性的雙邊貿易框架。
根據目前公開的資訊，中美雙方在會議中共同確認了多項核心議題的進展。首先，雙方已將「對等降稅」納入議程重點，具體涉及各300億美元規模的關稅調整方案。此舉被視為緩解雙邊貿易緊張關係的關鍵訊號，顯示兩國政府正試圖透過具體的經濟讓步，為全球貿易環境注入穩定因子。此外，農產品貿易亦是此次磋商的重頭戲，這不僅涉及美國農業出口商的利益，亦關乎中國國內糧食安全與市場需求。
透過制度化的貿易理事會，雙方期望能將過去零散的貿易爭端，轉化為可預測的政策對話，這標誌著中美經貿關係從單純的摩擦對抗，轉向更為結構性的談判模式。
儘管雙方均對會議進展持正面態度，但各界對於具體細節仍存在不同解讀與尚待釐清之處。中國商務部在宣告中強調，此次會議是落實前期經貿磋商成果的延續，側重於共識的具體化；然而，美國貿易代表辦公室的宣告則相對簡潔，強調「持續接觸」與「公平貿易」的原則，並未對300億美元降稅方案的具體執行時間表或配套條件做出進一步承諾。這種敘事上的差異反映出兩國在談判策略上的不同：中方傾向於強調變度化的落實與成果，美方則更看重貿易條件的公平性與對等性。
此外，關於降稅方案是否包含特定產業的豁免，或是否附帶非貿易領域的政治條件，目前尚無更細部的官方檔案可供查證，市場分析師普遍建議需持續觀察後續的執行細節。
此次貿易理事會的召開，對於全球經濟與貿易格局具有深遠影響。作為全球前兩大經濟體，中美貿易政策的任何變動，均會直接牽動全球供應鏈的重新配置。若對等降稅方案能順利實施，將有助於降低北美企業的進口成本，並減緩因關稅壁壘導致的通貨膨脹壓力。對於相關產業而言，特別是農業、製造業及跨境物流業，這場會議的結果將成為未來半年內貿易策略調整的重要指標。
同時，這也為北美地區的政策制定者提供了一個觀察視窗，評估如何在保護本土產業與維持全球貿易開放之間取得平衡，避免因過度依賴單一市場而產生系統性風險。
然而，專家提醒不應過度解讀此次會議的即時成效。儘管貿易理事會的成立代表了溝通機制的建立，但中美之間在技術轉移、智慧財產權保護及數位貿易等深層次結構性問題上，仍存在顯著分歧。此次會議聚焦於農產品與關稅等傳統貿易專案，屬於相對容易達成共識的領域，並不代表雙方已解決所有核心矛盾。此外，美國內部的政治氛圍與即將到來的政策變動，亦可能對談判程式產生不可預測的影響。
因此，將此次會議視為「全面貿易戰結束」的訊號恐過於樂觀，更應將其定位為「風險控管與機制對話」的開端，而非最終解決方案。
後續觀察重點將集中於以下三方面：第一，雙方是否會針對300億美元的降稅清單發布具體實施細則，以及這些清單是否包含敏感技術產品；第二，農產品貿易的採購量是否達到預期目標，這將直接影響美國農業州的經濟表現與政治支援度；第三，貿易理事會是否會定期召開，並形成具備約束力的決策機制，而非僅止於形式上的溝通。各界將密切關注未來幾個月內，雙方在具體貿易資料與政策執行面上的表現，以判斷中美經貿關係是否真正進入了穩定發展的軌道。
