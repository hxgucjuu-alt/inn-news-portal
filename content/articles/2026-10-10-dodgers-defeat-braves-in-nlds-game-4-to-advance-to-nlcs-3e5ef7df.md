根據體育媒體報導，美國職棒大聯盟洛杉磯道奇隊於 2026 年 10 月 7 日在喬治亞州 Truist Park 舉行的國家聯盟分區系列賽（NLDS）第四戰中，以 4 比 1 戰勝亞特蘭大勇士隊，成功淘汰對手並挺進國家聯盟冠軍賽（NLCS）。

報導指出，這場關鍵客場勝利得益於道奇隊投手群的壓制力以及適時的進攻表現。比賽進行至第七局時，道奇隊外野手安迪·佩吉斯（Andy Pages）擊出一支帶有 2 分打點的一壘安打，為球隊奠定勝基；一壘手佛雷迪·弗里曼（Freddie Freeman）等主力球員亦隨隊迎來勝利時刻。

媒體分析指出，道奇隊此役雖面對嚴苛挑戰，但仍憑藉扎實的戰術執行力過關。隨著該系列賽結束，道奇隊距離世界大賽再邁進一步，將繼續在國聯冠軍賽中爭奪更高的季後賽榮譽。

## English edition

According to sports media reports, the Los Angeles Dodgers defeated the Atlanta Braves 4-1 in Game 4 of the National League Division Series (NLDS) at Truist Park in Georgia on October 7, 2026, officially eliminating the Braves and advancing to the National League Championship Series (NLCS).

Reports indicate that the crucial road victory relied on another dominant performance from the Dodgers' pitching staff combined with timely offensive execution. In the seventh inning, Dodgers outfielder Andy Pages delivered a critical two-RBI single to help secure the lead, as key players including first baseman Freddie Freeman contributed to closing out the contest.

Media analyses highlighted that while the victory was not effortless, Los Angeles relied on its proven championship formula to navigate the series. With the NLDS victory in hand, the Dodgers move one step closer to the World Series as they prepare for the upcoming League Championship Series.
