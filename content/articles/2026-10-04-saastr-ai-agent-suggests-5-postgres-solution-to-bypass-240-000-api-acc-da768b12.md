根據報導，SaaStr公司目前以3名人類員工與超過21個AI代理協同運作。其中，名為「10K」的AI營收副總裁，最初僅是一個簡單的儀表板，現已負責公司大部分的出站、入站及營收營運。該AI系統每天在所有相關應用程式中產生35,000至40,000次的API呼叫。

然而，SaaStr面臨一項挑戰，其供應商如Salesforce、HubSpot和Atlassian開始對AI代理的API存取收取費用。資料指出，SaaStr收到了一份高達每年240,000美元的代理API存取費用估價，這對其營運成本構成了顯著壓力。

令人驚訝的是，SaaStr的AI營收副總裁「10K」提出了一個創新的解決方案。該AI代理建議將數據鏡像到一個每月僅需5美元的Postgres實例中，以此作為替代方案，有效規避了供應商的高額API存取費用。

此事件凸顯了AI代理在企業營運中日益增長的關鍵角色，不僅限於自動化任務，更延伸至成本管理和策略性決策。SaaStr的案例展示了AI如何透過尋找經濟高效的技術替代方案，來優化企業的API成本結構。

## English edition

According to reports, SaaStr currently operates with a lean team of 3 human employees and over 21 AI agents. Among them, "10K," an AI Vice President of Revenue, initially started as a simple dashboard but now manages most of the company's outbound, inbound, and revenue operations. This AI system generates between 35,000 to 40,000 API calls daily across all the applications it interacts with.

SaaStr encountered a significant challenge when its vendors, including Salesforce, HubSpot, and Atlassian, began charging for AI agent API access. Data indicates that SaaStr received an estimate of up to $240,000 annually for this access, posing a substantial financial burden on its operations.

Remarkably, SaaStr's AI VP of Revenue, "10K," devised an innovative solution. The AI agent suggested an alternative approach: mirroring the necessary data to a Postgres instance costing only $5 per month. This strategy effectively allowed SaaStr to bypass the exorbitant API access fees imposed by its vendors.

This incident underscores the expanding and critical role of AI agents in enterprise operations, extending beyond mere task automation to include strategic decision-making and cost management. SaaStr's experience exemplifies how AI can optimize a company's API cost structure by identifying and implementing economically efficient technological alternatives.
