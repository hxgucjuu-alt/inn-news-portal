  # Capital Securities Partners with Amundi to Deepen International Financial Cooperation
  
  > Capital Securities and Amundi hosted a summit during "2026 Taiwan Week," leveraging AI-driven services and global asset allocation strategies to help investors navigate market trends and strengthen Taiwan's competitiveness as an Asian asset management hub.
  
  On November 4, 2026, Capital Securities (6005) officially announced a strategic partnership with Amundi Taiwan, a subsidiary of the global asset management giant Amundi. The collaboration stems from "2026 Taiwan Week," an annual international financial event promoted by the Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC). The core vision of this event is to drive Taiwan's transformation into an "Asian Asset Management Hub" and to foster deep integration between the domestic capital market and international systems.
  
  As a key implementation of this vision, Capital Securities hosted an exclusive investment summit in Taipei, aimed at providing domestic investors with broader and deeper asset allocation solutions by introducing top-tier international asset management perspectives, while showcasing the application of financial technology in the wealth management sector.
  
  According to information released by both parties, the progress of this partnership focuses on two main areas. First is the introduction of professional investment strategies. As Europe's largest asset manager and one of the top ten globally, with assets under management exceeding 2.6 trillion euros, Amundi possesses extensive experience in global market research and operations. Through this summit, Amundi’s expert team and Capital Securities jointly analyzed risks and opportunities in the current global economic environment to assist clients in adjusting their investment portfolios.
  
  Second, Capital Securities has integrated an intelligent service system called "AI Ask," which is designed to provide real-time, convenient investment decision support through AI technology, transforming complex market data into easy-to-understand financial advice to enhance investors' flexibility and efficiency in responding to market volatility.
  
  Although Capital Securities and Amundi reached a consensus at the summit to promote diversified asset allocation, there are currently no further public details regarding specific investment product details, the technical iteration path for future AI services, or the allocation of rights and responsibilities in the long-term partnership. Furthermore, while "2026 Taiwan Week" emphasized the vision of an asset management hub, there remain varying interpretations regarding the progress toward this goal and the associated regulatory supporting measures.
  
  Some market observers believe that while introducing international giants can enhance market professionalism, the key issues that need to be clarified include how to ensure these international resources can be effectively converted into tangible profits for local investors, and how to achieve deeper integration with the existing domestic financial ecosystem.
  
  The specific impact of this partnership on Taiwan's financial industry is primarily reflected in a dual upgrade of "professionalism" and "technological advancement." At the policy level, this demonstrates the intention of Taiwan's financial sector to actively respond to FSC policies, which helps increase Taiwan's visibility in the international financial landscape. In terms of industrial application, by combining financial technology with traditional securities services through the "AI Ask" system, Capital Securities has not only lowered the threshold for investors to access professional market analysis but also symbolizes the acceleration of Taiwan's securities industry's transformation from a simple trading channel into a consultant-type financial institution with data analysis capabilities.
  
  For investors, this means that when facing the challenges of globalized investment, they can obtain more professional and real-time technical support, thereby optimizing their personal asset allocation strategies.
  
  However, investors and market participants should remain objective and cautious when interpreting such news. First, although AI intelligent services can provide real-time assistance, any investment decision must still consider market uncertainty and individual risk tolerance; one should not over-rely on the advice of a single system. Second, while the entry of international asset management companies can bring advanced investment thinking, their strategies are often based on a global perspective and may not be entirely applicable to the needs of all local investors in Taiwan.
  
  In addition, there is often a time lag between policy visions and actual implementation in financial markets. The effectiveness of related cooperation projects requires long-term market verification rather than being determined by a single summit. One should avoid mythologizing such strategic partnerships as a guarantee of market profit.
  
  Subsequent observations should focus on three aspects: First, the accuracy and customer satisfaction of Capital Securities' "AI Ask" system in practical application, and whether the system will be expanded to more diverse financial product lines. Second, whether Amundi will launch more customized, localized investment products for the Taiwan market following its partnership with Capital Securities. Third, the follow-up policy promotion by the FSC regarding "2026 Taiwan Week," and whether more financial institutions will follow suit to create a cluster effect, thereby driving the overall scale and professional standards of Taiwan's asset management market. These dynamics will be important indicators for assessing whether Taiwan can steadily move toward becoming an Asian asset management hub.

# 群益證攜手東方匯理資產管理，深化國際金融合作佈局

> 群益金鼎證券與東方匯理資產管理於「2026臺灣週」舉辦高峰會，透過AI智慧服務與全球資產配置策略，協助投資人掌握市場趨勢，強化臺灣作為亞洲資產管理中心之競爭力。


2026年11月4日，群益金鼎證券（6005）正式宣佈與全球資產管理巨擘東方匯理資產管理（Amundi）旗下的在臺子公司東方匯理投信展開戰略合作。此次合作背景源於金融監督管理委員會（金管會）所推動的年度國際金融盛事「2026臺灣週」。該活動的核心願景在於推動臺灣轉型為「亞洲資產管理中心」，並促進國內資本市場與國際體系的深度接軌。
作為此願景的重要實踐，群益金鼎證券在臺北舉辦了尊榮投資高峰會，旨在透過引入國際頂尖資產管理觀點，為國內投資人提供更具廣度與深度的資產配置方案，並展示金融科技在財富管理領域的應用。
根據雙方公開的資訊，此次合作的進展主要聚焦於兩大面向：首先是專業投資策略的引進，東方匯理作為歐洲規模最大、全球排名前十大的資產管理公司，其管理資產規模已突破2.6兆歐元，擁有極為豐富的全球市場調研與操作經驗。透過此次高峰會，東方匯理的專家團隊與群益金鼎證券共同剖析了當前全球經濟環境下的風險與機會，協助客戶調整投資組合。
其次，群益金鼎證券匯入了名為「AI問問」的智慧服務系統，該系統旨在透過AI技術提供即時、便利的投資決策輔助，將複雜的市場資料轉化為易於理解的理財建議，提升投資人應對市場波動的靈活性與效率。
儘管群益金鼎證券與東方匯理投信在高峰會上達成了推廣多元資產配置的共識，但針對具體的投資產品細節、後續AI服務的技術迭代路徑，以及雙方在未來長期合作中的權責分配，目前尚未有進一步的公開細節。此外，雖然「2026臺灣週」強調了資產管理中心的願景，但各界對於該目標的達成進度與監管配套措施仍存在不同解讀。
部分市場觀察家認為，引入國際巨頭固然能提升市場專業度，但如何確保這些國際資源能有效轉化為在地投資人的實質獲利，以及如何與國內現有金融生態系統進行更深層次的整合，仍是後續需要持續釐清的關鍵議題。
本次合作對臺灣金融產業的具體影響，主要體現在「專業化」與「科技化」的雙重升級。在政策層面上，這展現了臺灣金融業積極響應金管會政策的意圖，有助於提升臺灣在國際金融版圖中的能見度。在產業應用方面，群益證券透過「AI問問」系統，將金融科技與傳統證券服務結合，這不僅降低了投資人獲取專業市場分析的門檻，也象徵著臺灣證券業正加速從單純的交易通路，轉型為具備資料分析能力的顧問型金融機構。
對於投資人而言，這意味著在面對全球化投資挑戰時，能獲得更為專業且即時的技術支援，進而最佳化個人的資產配置策略。
然而，投資人與市場參與者在解讀此類新聞時，應保持客觀與審慎。首先，雖然AI智慧服務能提供即時輔助，但任何投資決策仍需考量市場的不確定性與個人風險承受能力，不應過度依賴單一系統的建議。其次，國際資產管理公司的進駐雖能帶來先進的投資思維，但其策略多基於全球視角，未必完全適用於所有臺灣在地投資人的需求。
此外，金融市場的政策願景與實際執行之間往往存在時間差，相關合作案的成效需經由長期的市場驗證，而非單一高峰會的舉辦即能定論，應避免將此類戰略合作過度神話為市場獲利的保證。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於三個面向：第一，群益金鼎證券的「AI問問」系統在實際應用中的精準度與客戶滿意度，以及該系統是否會擴大應用於更多元的金融產品線。第二，東方匯理投信在與群益合作後，是否會針對臺灣市場推出更多客製化的在地化投資產品。第三，金管會針對「2026臺灣週」後續的政策推動情形，以及是否有更多金融機構跟進，形成群聚效應，進而推動臺灣資產管理市場的整體規模與專業水準提升。這些動態將是評估臺灣是否能穩步邁向亞洲資產管理中心的重要指標。
