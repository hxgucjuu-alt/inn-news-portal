  # Geopolitical Security in Northeast Asia and the Development of AI Agent Technology: The Dual Challenges of Regional Stability and Digital Defense
  
  > This article explores the historical legacy and current geopolitical tensions in the Northeast Asian region, while contrasting them with global trends in AI agent security defense to analyze the needs for human security and corporate cybersecurity in the digital age.
  
  The Northeast Asia region has long been considered one of the most sensitive geopolitical sectors in the world. According to historical reviews and regional analyses by the Associated Press (AP), despite more than 75 years having passed since the end of World War II, Northeast Asian nations remain deeply constrained by the past when addressing historical legacies and current regional security issues. This state of being "anchored by history" keeps territorial disputes, military deployments, and diplomatic relations within the region in a state of constant instability. Meanwhile, as digital transformation permeates all industries, awareness regarding the protection of "personal safety" and "information security" is rising globally.
  
  Taking Taiwan as an example, the Zhongli Precinct of the Taoyuan City Police Department recently collaborated with the Rakuten Monkeys baseball team to conduct anti-sexual harassment and anti-stalking awareness campaigns for cheerleaders who are frequently in the public eye. This demonstrates that in contemporary social structures, human security protection for specific professional groups has become a core public issue.
  
  In the field of digital security, technological progress and risk control are also highly correlated. According to a report by iThome, AI agent technology is currently in a period of explosive growth. Gartner predicts that the number of AI agents used by Fortune 500 companies globally will surge from fewer than 15 in 2025 to over 150,000 by 2028. In response to this trend, Reco, a company focused on AI agent security, announced the completion of a $55 million funding round, bringing its total funding to $140 million.
  
  Since launching its "Reco Agent Security" product this June, the company has been actively positioning itself in enterprise access permission management, aiming to address cybersecurity vulnerabilities and permission control issues that may arise during the automated decision-making processes of AI agents. This technological advancement echoes the release of Google’s "Gemini 4 Argon" model, indicating that the global technology industry is committed to using more powerful models and defense mechanisms to cope with an increasingly complex digital environment.
  
  However, at the intersection of regional security and technological development, significant information discrepancies and uncertainties remain. Taking the geopolitical status quo of Northeast Asia as an example, although international media such as ABC4 emphasize the profound impact of historical factors on regional stability, Indian media outlets like The Telegraph India and NENOW focus on border security issues in North East India.
  
  Reports indicate that Indian government official Kiren Rijiju has expressed concern regarding military activities along the China-India border in Arunachal Pradesh, particularly China's military movements in the Upper Subansiri district. Such information shows that there is a gap in the geographical scope covered by the term "Northeast" in different contexts, and that interpretations of border security and geopolitical expansion vary by country. Furthermore, while the development of AI security has clear funding data and predictive models, whether AI agents can fully prevent malicious attacks in practical applications remains a technical challenge within the industry.
  
  The aforementioned events have had a profound impact on the relevant regions and industries. At the social security level, harassment prevention for public figures and high-exposure professions has become a focus of collaboration between police agencies and civil society groups, reflecting modern society's high regard for personal privacy and bodily autonomy. At the industrial level, the widespread adoption of AI agents forces companies to re-examine their Identity and Access Management (IAM) mechanisms; this is not merely a technical upgrade, but a key to corporate operational resilience. For the Northeast Asian region, the entanglement of historical legacies and military activities makes economic cooperation and political dialogue extremely challenging, and any change in border situations could have a ripple effect on global supply chains and regional stability.
  
  Although attention to security issues continues to rise across all sectors, over-interpretation should be avoided. For example, directly linking the technical defense of AI agents with the military defense of geopolitics may overlook the essential differences between the two in terms of logic and execution. AI security primarily addresses software vulnerabilities and data leakage risks, while geopolitics involves sovereignty, historical identity, and military strategy. While both fall under the "security" umbrella, their paths to resolution are vastly different. Furthermore, while the awareness campaign for the Rakuten Girls reflects the police's protection of specific groups, such actions should be viewed as part of a social safety net rather than a specialized treatment for a single industry.
  
  When interpreting such news, the public should distinguish between the different dimensions of "digital defense" and "physical security" to avoid conflating technical risks with social risks.
  
  Future observations will focus on the following three directions: First, the actual deployment effectiveness of AI agent security solutions from firms like Reco in the market following their funding, and whether they can effectively reduce cybersecurity risks brought about by AI automation in enterprises. Second, the official responses of countries in the Northeast Asian region to territorial disputes and military activities, particularly subsequent diplomatic developments regarding the China-India border and the situation on the Korean Peninsula. Finally, whether the awareness models for preventing sexual harassment and stalking used by Taiwanese police and civil groups can be further expanded to other high-risk professional groups and form a standardized social security protection mechanism.
  
  The evolution of these issues will directly influence the future direction of regional stability and the development of the digital ecosystem.

# 東北亞地緣安全與AI代理技術發展：區域穩定與數位防禦的雙重挑戰

> 本文探討東北亞區域安全域性勢的歷史遺緒與當前地緣緊張，並對照全球AI代理技術的安全防禦趨勢，分析數位時代下的人道安全與企業資安防護需求。


東北亞地區長期以來被視為全球地緣政治最為敏感的板塊之一。根據美聯社（AP）的歷史回顧與區域分析，儘管二次大戰結束已逾75年，但東北亞國家在處理歷史遺緒與當前區域安全問題時，仍深受過去歷史的羈絆。這種「拖著歷史錨鏈」的狀態，使得區域內的領土爭議、軍事部署與外交關係，始終處於高度不穩定狀態。與此同時，隨著數位化轉型深入各行各業，針對「人身安全」與「資訊安全」的防護意識也在全球範圍內同步提升。
以臺灣為例，桃園市政府警察局中壢分局近期與樂天桃猿球團合作，針對長期暴露於公眾視野下的啦啦隊成員，進行性騷擾與跟蹤騷擾防治宣導，顯示出在當代社會結構中，針對特定職業群體的人道安全防護已成為公共議題的核心。
在數位安全領域，技術進展與風險控制同樣呈現高度關聯。根據《iThome》的報導，AI代理（AI Agent）技術正處於爆發性成長期。Gartner預測，全球《財星》500大企業所使用的AI代理數量，將從2025年的不到15個，激增至2028年的超過15萬個。面對此一趨勢，專注於AI代理安全的業者Reco宣佈完成5,500萬美元的募資，累計募資額達1.4億美元。
該公司自今年6月推出「Reco Agent Security」產品後，積極佈局企業存取許可權管理，旨在應對AI代理在自動化決策過程中可能產生的資安漏洞與許可權失控問題。此一技術進展與Google發布的「Gemini 4 Argon」模型相呼應，顯示全球科技產業正致力於透過更強大的模型與防禦機制，來應對日益複雜的數位環境。
然而，在區域安全與技術發展的交匯點上，仍存在顯著的資訊差異與不確定性。以東北亞的地緣現狀為例，儘管國際媒體如《ABC4》強調歷史因素對區域穩定的深遠影響，但印度媒體《Telegraph India》與《NENOW》則將焦點置於印度東北部（North East India）的邊境安全問題。
報導指出，印度政府官員Rijiju針對中印邊境阿魯納恰爾邦（Arunachal Pradesh）的軍事活動表示關切，特別是中國在蘇班西里（Upper Subansiri）地區的軍事動態。此類資訊顯示「東北亞」一詞在不同語境下可能涵蓋的地理範圍存在落差，且各國對於邊境安全與地緣擴張的解讀亦不盡相同。此外，AI安全領域的發展雖有明確的募資資料與預測模型，但AI代理在實際應用中是否能完全防堵惡意攻擊，仍是產業內部的技術挑戰。
上述事件對相關地區與產業產生了深遠影響。在社會安全層面，針對公眾人物與高曝光度職業的騷擾防治，已成為警政機關與民間團體協作的重點，這反映了現代社會對於個人隱私與身體自主權的高度重視。在產業層面，AI代理的普及化迫使企業必須重新檢視存取許可權管理（IAM）機制，這不僅是技術升級，更是企業營運韌性的關鍵。對於東北亞區域而言，歷史遺緒與軍事活動的相互糾葛，使得區域內的經濟合作與政治對話面臨極大挑戰，任何邊境局勢的變動，皆可能對全球供應鏈與區域穩定造成連鎖效應。
儘管各界對於安全議題的關注度持續提升，但仍需避免過度解讀。例如，將AI代理的技術防禦與地緣政治的軍事防禦直接掛鉤，可能忽略了兩者在邏輯與執行層面的本質差異。AI安全主要應對的是軟體漏洞與資料洩漏風險，而地緣政治則涉及主權、歷史認同與軍事戰略，兩者雖皆屬「安全」範疇，但解決路徑截然不同。此外，針對樂天女孩的宣導活動，雖然體現了警政機關對特定群體的保護，但此類行動應被視為社會安全網的一環，而非單一產業的特殊化處理。
公眾在解讀此類新聞時，應區分「數位防禦」與「實體安全」的不同維度，避免將技術性風險與社會性風險混為一談。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個方向：首先，Reco等資安業者在獲得資金挹注後，其AI代理安全解決方案在市場上的實際部署成效，以及是否能有效降低企業因AI自動化帶來的資安風險；其次，東北亞區域內各國對於領土爭議與軍事活動的官方回應，特別是針對中印邊境與朝鮮半島局勢的後續外交動態；最後，臺灣警政單位與民間團體在預防性騷擾與跟蹤騷擾方面的宣導模式，是否能進一步擴充套件至其他高風險職業群體，並形成一套標準化的社會安全防護機制。
這些議題的演變，將直接影響未來區域穩定與數位生態的發展走向。
