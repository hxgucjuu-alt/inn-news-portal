  # Silicon Valley Tech Giants Face Physical Security Crisis: California Security Union Issues Strike Ultimatum
  
  > ### Summary
  
  Frontline security personnel at major California tech companies—including OpenAI, Google, and Meta—have officially submitted strike notices. Should labor negotiations fail, Silicon Valley campuses could face a physical security vacuum, forcing companies to adjust their operational models. This event highlights the public issue of ensuring labor rights for infrastructure maintenance staff while the tech industry pursues leadership in artificial intelligence.
  
  ---
  
  The Silicon Valley tech industry has long been committed to an artificial intelligence arms race, yet it now faces a significant challenge to its internal physical security perimeter. According to a report by Techbang, frontline security personnel responsible for maintaining the safety of tech campuses in California—including those of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, and NVIDIA—officially submitted a 72-hour strike notice on the morning of September 15, 2026 (local time). This move implies that physical security staff at these tech giants' headquarters could withdraw from their posts in the coming days, directly impacting daily operational security and personnel access control mechanisms at the campuses.
  
  Information confirmed from various sources indicates that this strike action was initiated by the California security union, primarily focusing on demands regarding labor conditions and compensation. The union has issued a clear ultimatum to the aforementioned tech giants; if labor and management cannot reach an agreement within the specified timeframe, a full-scale strike will commence as early as this Friday. This event has drawn significant attention from the tech industry, as a large-scale withdrawal of security personnel would leave the office buildings of core Silicon Valley firms with severe physical security vulnerabilities, forcing companies to develop emergency response measures or potentially revert to fully remote work models to mitigate risks.
  
  Currently, there are some discrepancies among sources regarding the specific details of the strike and the progress of negotiations. Although the Techbang report indicates that the strike notice has been formally submitted and covers multiple industry leaders, there is no unified account regarding specific internal contingency plans or the companies' official responses. Furthermore, while the union has issued a clear 72-hour ultimatum, it remains to be seen whether labor and management will reach a compromise at the last minute or if the strike will proceed as scheduled. Some tech media outlets speculate that if the strike continues, it will not only hinder physical access but also place unprecedented pressure on the protection of confidential data and the maintenance of hardware facilities.
  
  The impact of this event on the North American tech industry is profound. As a global hub for technological innovation, the security of Silicon Valley campuses involves not only the protection of corporate assets but also the stability of global artificial intelligence research and development programs. If security systems are suspended, companies will be forced to invest additional resources into seeking alternative security solutions or adopting closed-management practices, which will directly impact daily operational efficiency, employee commuting, and interactions with external partners. Moreover, this underscores that while tech giants pursue digital transformation and AI leadership, the protection of labor rights for infrastructure maintenance personnel has become an unavoidable public issue.
  
  Perspectives on this strike vary. Supporters of the union argue that as the first line of defense for tech campuses, the wages and benefits of security personnel should be commensurate with the high profitability of the tech industry. However, some industry insiders worry that a prolonged security vacuum resulting from the strike could cause irreparable damage to the protection of corporate secrets. It should be noted that while the threat of a strike is imminent, the public should not over-interpret the event as having a devastating impact on the long-term operations of these companies, as large tech firms typically have redundant contingency mechanisms in place. Currently, it is particularly important to monitor whether management will intervene through legal channels or if the union will further expand the strike to other related service supply chains.
  
  The key focus for subsequent observation will be the situation following this Friday: first, whether the tech giants issue formal announcements explaining their security contingency plans; second, whether labor and management reach a last-minute reconciliation to avoid a large-scale withdrawal of security staff; and finally, whether this event triggers a chain reaction among security personnel at other California tech campuses, potentially evolving into a larger labor dispute in the Silicon Valley region, which will be a key indicator for assessing the stability of the industry.
  
  Against the macro backdrop of North American public safety and the industrial environment, such labor disputes also contrast with current regional security issues. For example, according to information released by the U.S. Department of War on September 17, 2026, the North American Aerospace Defense Command (NORAD) is conducting "Operation Arctic Vanguard," deploying F-16 fighter jets to Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada, to address defense missions in the Arctic region.
  
  Although this military deployment and the Silicon Valley security strike belong to different domains, both reflect the high sensitivity of North America to resource scheduling and risk management when facing internal labor rights issues and external national defense security challenges.
  
  Furthermore, as the highest authority for maintaining public safety and legal order, the U.S. Department of Justice is responsible for ensuring public security amidst domestic and foreign threats. Although the current strike is a labor dispute, should it evolve into a situation affecting the security of critical infrastructure or involving illegal acts, the Department of Justice's stance on law enforcement and the maintenance of public order will become a potential variable of concern. Combining the sources above, the Silicon Valley security strike is not merely a labor issue for a single company, but a complex governance challenge that North America must face at the intersection of the digital economy and physical security.

# 矽谷科技巨頭面臨實體維安危機：加州保全工會發出罷工通牒

> ### 摘要


加州大型科技企業（如 OpenAI、Google、Meta 等）的基層保全人員已正式提交罷工通知，若勞資協商未果，矽谷園區恐面臨實體維安真空，迫使企業調整辦公模式。此事件凸顯了科技產業在追求人工智慧領先地位的同時，對於基礎設施維護人員勞動權益保障的公共議題挑戰。
---
矽谷科技產業長期以來致力於人工智慧的軍備競賽，然而近期卻面臨內部實體安全防線的重大挑戰。根據 Techbang 報導，負責維護 OpenAI、Anthropic、Google、Meta 及輝達（NVIDIA）等位於加州科技園區安全工作的基層保全人員，已於當地時間 2026 年 9 月 15 日上午正式提交 72 小時的罷工通知。此舉意味著這些科技巨頭總部大樓的實體維安人員可能於近日全面撤哨，直接衝擊園區的日常營運安全與人員管制機制。
綜合各方資訊確認，本次罷工行動由加州保全工會發起，主要針對勞動條件與薪資待遇進行訴求。工會已明確向上述科技巨頭發出最後通牒，若勞資雙方無法在時限內達成協議，最快將於本週五啟動全面罷工。此事件已引起科技產業高度關注，因為一旦保全人員大規模撤離，將導致矽谷核心企業的辦公大樓面臨嚴重的實體安全漏洞，企業被迫研擬緊急應變措施，甚至可能重啟全遠距辦公模式以規避風險。
目前關於罷工的具體細節與談判進度，各來源間仍存在部分差異。儘管 Techbang 報導指出罷工通知已正式提交且影響範圍涵蓋多家龍頭企業，但針對企業內部的具體應變方案以及資方的具體回應，目前尚無統一說法。此外，雖然工會已發出明確的 72 小時通牒，但勞資雙方是否在最後關頭達成妥協，或罷工行動是否會如期全面爆發，仍有待後續觀察。部分科技媒體推測，若罷工持續，不僅是實體門禁受阻，機密資料保護與硬體設施維護亦將面臨前所未有的考驗。
此事件對北美科技產業的影響深遠。矽谷作為全球科技創新中心，其園區安全不僅涉及企業資產保護，更關乎全球人工智慧研發程式的穩定性。若保全系統停擺，企業將不得不投入額外資源尋求替代維安方案，或採取封閉式管理，這將對企業的日常運作效率、員工通勤以及與外部合作夥伴的互動產生直接衝擊。此外，這也凸顯了科技巨頭在追求數位轉型與 AI 領先地位的同時，對於基礎設施維護人員的勞動權益保障，已成為不可忽略的公共議題。
針對此次罷工事件，各界觀點不一。支援工會的人士認為，保全人員作為科技園區安全的第一線，其薪資與福利應與科技產業的高獲利能力相匹配；然而，部分產業界人士擔憂，若罷工行動導致長期維安真空，將對企業機密保護造成無法挽回的損失。在此提醒，儘管罷工威脅迫在眉睫，但外界不應過度解讀該事件對企業長期營運的毀滅性影響，畢竟大型科技公司通常設有冗餘的應變機制。目前需特別留意的是，資方是否會採取法律途徑介入，或工會是否會進一步擴大罷工範圍至其他相關服務供應鏈。
後續觀察重點在於本週五後的實際情況：首先，觀察科技巨頭是否會發布正式宣告，說明其安全應變計畫；其次，關注勞資雙方是否在最後一刻達成和解，避免大規模撤哨發生；最後，此事件是否會引發加州其他科技園區保全人員的連鎖效應，進而演變成矽谷地區更大規模的勞資爭議，將是後續評估該產業穩定性的關鍵指標。
在北美公共安全與產業環境的宏觀背景下，此類勞資糾紛亦與當前區域性安全議題形成對比。例如，根據美國國防部（U.S. Department of War）於 2026 年 9 月 17 日發布的資訊，北美防空司令部（NORAD）正執行「北極先鋒行動」（Operation Arctic Vanguard），將 F-16 戰機部署至加拿大紐芬蘭與拉布拉多省，以應對北極地區的防禦任務。
雖然此軍事部署與矽谷保全罷工屬於不同範疇，但兩者皆反映了北美在面對內部勞動權益與外部國防安全挑戰時，對於資源排程與風險控管的高度敏感性。
此外，美國司法部（U.S. Department of Justice）作為維護公共安全與法律秩序的最高機關，其職責在於確保國內外威脅下的公共安全。儘管目前罷工事件屬於勞資糾紛範疇，但若未來演變為影響關鍵基礎設施安全或涉及非法行為，司法部對於法律執行與公共秩序的維護立場，將成為各界關注的潛在變數。綜合上述來源，矽谷保全罷工不僅是單一企業的勞資問題，更是北美在數位經濟與實體安全交織下，必須面對的複雜治理挑戰。
