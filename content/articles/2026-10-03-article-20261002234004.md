  # Global AI Arms Race Intensifies: White House Security Summit and Advanced Process Layout Dynamics
  
  > Global tech giants gathered at the White House to discuss AI safety agreements, though Apple’s absence has drawn attention. Meanwhile, TSMC is accelerating A14 process R&D, Micron reported stellar earnings, and Temasek continues to increase its investments in AI infrastructure.
  
  Recent global artificial intelligence (AI) industry developments have been frequent. In October 2026, U.S. President Trump convened an AI luncheon at the White House, inviting tech leaders including OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, Microsoft, Google, and NVIDIA, with the aim of pushing companies to sign a series of AI safety agreements. However, Apple Chairman Tim Cook and the new CEO, Kevan Turner, were both absent, sparking widespread speculation regarding Apple’s stance on AI policy and its industry partnerships.
  
  Meanwhile, the global technology supply chain continues to make strategic adjustments in response to AI computing demands. TSMC has officially announced the acceleration of its next-generation "A14" process R&D, with trial production in Taiwan expected to begin as early as the first quarter of 2027, highlighting the extreme demand from global chip designers for advanced process capacity.
  
  In terms of financial performance, the strong demand driven by AI infrastructure construction has translated into substantial profits. Micron Technology released its financial report for the fourth quarter and the full year of 2026, showing a gross margin that surged to 86.8%, with both quarterly and annual results hitting record highs. Additionally, the Singaporean investment firm Temasek expressed optimism regarding the continued investment in AI infrastructure by major cloud providers. Jane Atherton, the firm's head of North America, noted that Temasek plans to increase its AI-related investment allocation to 15% over the next five years, reflecting the confidence of large institutional investors in long-term AI returns and industry balance sheets.
  
  HSBC Global Asset Management analyzed that, benefiting from the global AI arms race, the profit growth rate of Taiwanese companies is expected to reach a historic peak of 46%, indicating that the AI industry chain has become a core engine of global economic growth.
  
  Despite the promising industry outlook, there remain differing perspectives and unknown variables regarding AI development. Regarding the AI safety luncheon convened by Trump, the reason for Apple's absence has not been officially explained, which contrasts with the proactive participation of other tech giants in the safety agreements. Furthermore, although Temasek holds a positive view on massive AI spending, some market analysts question whether major cloud providers can maintain such high-intensity capital expenditures in the long term, given the volatility in global bond markets and a high-interest-rate environment where monetary policy may tighten further.
  
  At the same time, adjustments by the International Monetary Fund (IMF) to the global economic outlook, particularly regarding changes in economic growth forecasts for the South American region, will also indirectly affect global capital flows and the macroeconomic layout of the technology industry.
  
  For South America, while the explosion of the global AI industry and the race for advanced processes are primarily concentrated in the tech hubs of the Northern Hemisphere, the resulting global economic growth expectations and capital flows remain closely linked to the South American economy. According to the latest forecasts from the International Monetary Fund (IMF), despite the lucrative profits in the global tech industry, while South America's economic growth estimate was raised for 2025, the forecast for 2026 was downgraded. This shift in the macroeconomic environment means that South American nations must seek a balance between attracting foreign investment and stabilizing their internal economies as they face the global technological transformation.
  
  As the demand for AI infrastructure expands, how to link global computing dividends with regional economic development will be an issue that policymakers in South American countries must continue to monitor.
  
  Currently, there are limitations to how the AI industry is interpreted. Although the progress of TSMC's A14 process provides a clear technical path for the market, the advancement of Moore's Law at physical boundaries still faces significant challenges, and the actual return on investment for the widespread adoption of AI applications still requires time to be verified. Furthermore, the actual execution and regulatory effectiveness of companies after signing AI safety agreements are still in the observation stage; one should not over-interpret the absence of a single meeting or a single company as representing an industry split. All parties should continue to monitor the actual execution of capital expenditures by major cloud providers, as well as the potential crowding-out effect of the global high-interest-rate environment on R&D investment in the tech industry.
  
  Key areas for follow-up observation include: first, Apple's specific response regarding future AI policies and safety agreements; second, whether TSMC's A14 process trial production proceeds as scheduled and its impact on the global chip supply chain; third, whether major cloud providers adjust the scale of their AI capital expenditures in the coming quarters in response to changes in the macroeconomic environment; and fourth, how the South American region adjusts its industrial policies to meet the challenges brought by global technological transformation in the face of expectations of a global economic slowdown.

# 全球AI軍備競賽加劇：白宮安全會議與先進製程佈局動態

> 全球科技巨頭齊聚白宮商討AI安全協議，蘋果卻缺席引發關注。與此同時，臺積電加速A14製程研發，美光財報亮眼，淡馬錫亦持續加碼AI基礎設施投資。


近期全球人工智慧（AI）產業動態頻繁，美國總統川普於2026年10月於白宮召開AI午餐會，邀集OpenAI、Anthropic、Meta、微軟、Google及輝達等科技龍頭，旨在推動企業簽署一系列AI安全協議。然而，蘋果公司董事長庫克與新任執行長特納斯均未出席，此舉引發外界對於蘋果在AI政策立場與產業合作關係的諸多揣測。
與此同時，全球科技供應鏈持續因應AI算力需求進行戰略調整，臺積電正式宣佈加速次世代「A14」製程研發，預計最快於2027年第一季在臺灣啟動試產，顯示出全球晶片設計商對於先進製程產能的極度渴求。
在產業財務表現方面，AI基礎設施建設帶動的強勁需求已轉化為實質獲利。美光科技公佈2026年第四季及全年財務報告，其毛利率衝上86.8%，單季與全年業績均創下歷史新高。此外，新加坡淡馬錫投資機構亦對大型雲端業者持續投入AI基礎設施的行為表示樂觀，該機構北美主管珍．艾瑟頓指出，淡馬錫計畫在未來五年內將AI相關投資佔比提高至15%，顯示大型機構投資人對於AI長期報酬與產業資產負債表的信心。
匯豐投信則分析，受惠於全球AI軍備競賽，臺灣企業獲利成長率預期將衝上46%的歷史高峰，顯示AI產業鏈已成為全球經濟成長的核心引擎。
儘管產業前景看好，各界對於AI發展仍存在不同觀點與未知變數。針對川普召開的AI安全午餐會，蘋果公司的缺席原因尚未獲得官方明確解釋，這與其他科技巨頭積極參與安全協議的態度形成對比。此外，儘管淡馬錫對AI巨額支出持正面態度，但市場上仍有分析師質疑，在全球債市波動及貨幣政策可能進一步緊縮的高利率環境下，大型雲端業者能否長期維持如此高強度的資本支出。
同時，國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）針對全球經濟展望的調整，特別是針對南美地區經濟成長預估的變動，亦將間接影響全球資本流動與科技產業的宏觀佈局環境。
對南美地區而言，全球AI產業的爆發與先進製程的競逐，雖主要集中在北半球科技重鎮，但其帶動的全球經濟成長預期與資本流動，仍與南美經濟息息相關。根據國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）的最新預測，儘管全球科技產業獲利豐厚，但南美地區的經濟成長預估在2025年獲得上調後，2026年的預測卻遭到下調。這種宏觀經濟環境的變動，意味著南美各國在面對全球科技產業轉型時，需在吸引外資與穩定內部經濟之間尋求平衡。
隨著AI基礎設施需求擴張，如何將全球算力紅利與區域經濟發展連結，將是南美各國政策制定者需持續關注的課題。
目前各方對於AI產業的解讀仍存在限制。雖然臺積電A14製程的進度為市場提供了明確的技術路徑，但摩爾定律在物理邊界上的推進仍面臨極大挑戰，且AI應用普及的實際報酬率仍需時間驗證。此外，企業簽署AI安全協議後的實際執行力與監管成效，目前仍處於觀察階段，不應過度解讀單一會議或單一企業的缺席即代表產業分裂。各界應持續關注大型雲端業者在資本支出上的實際執行狀況，以及全球高利率環境對科技產業研發投入的潛在排擠效應。
後續觀察重點包括：第一，蘋果公司在未來AI政策與安全協議上的具體回應；第二，臺積電A14製程試產進度是否如期推進，以及其對全球晶片供應鏈的影響；第三，大型雲端業者在未來幾個季度中，是否因應總體經濟環境變化而調整AI資本支出規模；第四，南美地區在面對全球經濟成長放緩預期下，如何調整其產業政策以因應全球科技轉型帶來的挑戰。
