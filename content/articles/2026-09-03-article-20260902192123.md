  > A Taipei City councilor revealed that a young girl was sexually molested but not placed in protective custody, sparking controversy over the city government's handling procedures. On the same day, the freezing of the Presidential Office's state affairs budget impacted orders for a sheltered workshop, which subsequently reached its target through public support.
  
  A recent case involving the sexual exploitation of a minor in Taipei City has drawn significant public attention. During a city council interpellation, Taipei City Councilor Miao Po-ya revealed that a 10-year-old girl had been sexually molested multiple times at home. Despite the family reporting the case, 240 days passed without the relevant city government units taking proactive placement measures. Miao reported the case on the spot, demanding that Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an intervene in the investigation. However, the attitude displayed by Yao Shu-wen, Commissioner of the Department of Social Welfare, in response to the councilor's questioning has sparked controversy, with critics questioning the professionalism and proactiveness of her handling of the case.
  
  This case not only involves the failure of child protection mechanisms but has also triggered an intense debate over whether administrative agencies deliberately disclosed the personal information of a "whistleblower" during the background investigation of the potential placement guardians.
  
  According to media reports, the girl's godfather and godmother volunteered to provide assistance. However, during the assessment process, social welfare authorities disclosed that the couple had past criminal records involving drugs, theft, and kidnapping for ransom. The girl's godfather admitted to his past mistakes but emphasized that in the critical moment when the girl was being harmed, he could not stand by and watch. Political commentator Wen Lang-dong publicly criticized the city government's disclosure of their criminal history, comparing the act to that of the "Jinyiwei" (Imperial Secret Police). He questioned why the city government failed to prioritize the protection of the girl, instead focusing on exposing the history of those offering help, which could discourage others from stepping forward to protect vulnerable children in the future and result in the loss of the basic conscience and protective functions expected of social welfare agencies.
  
  Regarding the details of the case and the determination of administrative responsibility, there are significant discrepancies in the accounts provided by various parties. The city government’s assessment criteria for placement procedures, and why no effective action was taken 240 days after the report, remain to be clarified by further investigation reports. On the other hand, the causal relationship between the godfather's admission of his criminal record and the city government's use of that background as a reason to refuse placement has become a focal point of public debate. Information currently awaiting confirmation includes the specific internal assessment records of the city government during the handling of the case and whether the Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) for reporting sexual exploitation were effectively implemented.
  
  Furthermore, whether the statements made by Commissioner Yao Shu-wen represent the consistent position of the city government, and where legal responsibility lies in this case, remain to be clarified by supervisory or judicial authorities.
  
  The mishandling of such child protection cases has had a tangible impact on Taipei City's social welfare system and public trust. If administrative agencies fail to establish clear and empathetic placement mechanisms when handling sexual exploitation cases, it will directly affect the protection of victims' rights and may lead to gaps in the social safety net. Additionally, if government departments use the background information of whistleblowers or helpers as a tool for public opinion manipulation, it will create a chilling effect on future social participation, weaken the willingness of private groups and individuals to assist vulnerable groups, and negatively affect the promotion of overall social welfare policies.
  
  Views on this incident are polarized. Those supporting the city government argue that social welfare units must strictly vet the backgrounds of potential guardians to ensure the girl grows up in a safe environment and to prevent potential harm from the guardians' past records. Opposing views argue that in cases of urgent protective needs, the victim's psychological needs and familiarity with the guardians should be prioritized rather than using past criminal records as the sole criterion, questioning whether administrative procedures are too rigid and lack flexibility. The public should not over-interpret the remarks of a single official but should refocus on the structural reform of child protection mechanisms, avoiding the simplification of complex social cases into political struggles to ensure that victimized children receive substantive and timely assistance.

Furthermore, whether the City Council will establish a special task force to demand an explanation from the Department of Social Welfare regarding the specific reasons for the lack of placement within the 240-day period will be a key indicator of the city government's determination to reform. At the same time, the public should continue to monitor the girl's subsequent placement status to ensure her physical and mental development is no longer compromised. Regarding the implementation of child protection laws, how to balance the "safety of the placement environment" with the "immediacy of emergency placement" will be a focal point for future policy adjustments.

# 臺北市女童性剝削案引爭議 與總統府國務預算刪凍引發社會效應

> 臺北市議員揭露女童遭性猥褻卻未獲安置，引發市府處理程式爭議；同日，總統府國務預算遭凍結導致庇護工場訂單受影響，獲社會各界認購達標。


臺北市近期發生一起涉及未成年女童的性剝削案件，引發社會高度關注。根據臺北市議員苗博雅在議會質詢時揭露，一名10歲女童在家中多次遭受性猥褻，家屬報案時間長達240天，期間市府相關單位卻未採取積極的安置措施。苗博雅當場通報此案，要求臺北市長蔣萬安介入調查。然而，針對議員的質疑，臺北市社會局長姚淑文在回應時的態度引發爭議，遭外界質疑其處理案件的專業性與積極度。
此案不僅涉及兒少保護機制的失靈，更因涉及女童安置物件的背景調查，導致後續輿論對於行政機關是否刻意針對「吹哨者」進行個資公開產生激烈辯論。
根據媒體披露，該名女童的乾爸、乾媽自願提供協助，但社政單位在評估過程中，揭露了該對夫妻過去曾有毒品、竊盜及擄人勒贖等前科紀錄。女童乾爸對此坦承過去確曾犯錯，但強調在女童遭受侵害的危急時刻，不能見死不救。針對市府將其前科背景公開的行為，政治評論員溫朗東公開批評此舉猶如「錦衣衛」行徑，質疑市府未能將保護女童視為首要任務，反而將重心放在揭露協助者的歷史紀錄，恐導致未來無人敢挺身而出保護弱勢兒童，喪失了社政單位應有的基本良知與保護功能。
在案件細節與行政責任的認定上，目前各界說法存在顯著差異。市府方面對於安置程式的評估標準，以及為何在報案長達240天後仍未完成有效處置，尚待進一步的調查報告釐清。另一方面，女童乾爸對於自身前科的坦承，與市府將其背景作為拒絕安置理由的因果關係，亦成為輿論攻防焦點。目前尚待確認的資訊包括：市府在處理該案件時的具體內部評估紀錄，以及針對性剝削通報後的標準作業程式（SOP）是否確實落實。
此外，社會局長姚淑文的發言是否代表市府的一貫立場，以及該案在法律層面上的責任歸屬，仍有待監察機關或司法單位介入釐清。
此類兒少保護案件的處理失當，對臺北市的社政體系與社會信任度造成具體衝擊。若行政機關在處理性剝削案件時，未能建立明確且具備同理心的安置機制，將直接影響受害兒童的權益保障，並可能導致社會安全網出現破洞。此外，政府部門若將吹哨者或協助者的背景資料作為輿論操作工具，將對未來的社會公益參與產生寒蟬效應，削弱民間團體與個人協助弱勢群體的意願，對整體社會福利政策的推動產生負面影響。
針對此事件，各界觀點呈現兩極。支援市府的一方認為，社政單位必須嚴格審核安置物件的背景，以確保女童在安全環境下成長，避免安置物件的不良紀錄對女童造成二次傷害；反對意見則認為，在緊急保護需求下，應優先考量受害者的心理需求與熟悉度，而非僅以過去前科作為唯一審核標準，質疑行政程式過於僵化且缺乏彈性。外界不應過度解讀單一行政官員的言論，應將焦點回歸至兒少保護機制的結構性改革，避免將複雜的社會案件簡化為政治鬥爭，確保受害女童能獲得實質且即時的協助。
後續觀察重點在於臺北市政府是否會針對此案啟動內部調查，並對社政單位的安置流程進行檢討。此外，市議會是否會針對該案成立專案小組，要求社會局說明報案240天內未安置的具體原因，將是判斷市府是否具備改革決心的關鍵。同時，社會大眾應持續關注該名女童的後續安置狀況，確保其身心發展不再受損。在兒少保護法規的落實上，如何平衡「保護物件的安全性」與「緊急安置的即時性」，將是未來相關政策調整的觀察重點。
