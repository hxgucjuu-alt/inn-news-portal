  > Interstellar Technologies has recently entered the flight demonstration phase, while the U.S. government's registration of the Alien.gov domain has sparked widespread public attention, representing technological breakthroughs in the private aerospace industry and policy deployments regarding interstellar issues, respectively.
  
  Interstellar Technologies has recently achieved significant progress in the international space industry. According to an official statement released by the company on March 31, 2026, the enterprise successfully passed the third phase of review in a multinational aerospace selection program. The program initially screened four participating companies, with only three remaining after the first phase of review in September 2024. In the latest review cycle, Interstellar Technologies and one other competitor stood out, officially advancing to the final stage, which includes a flight demonstration.
  
  This program marks a transition for the company from theoretical design to practical flight verification in the development of interstellar-grade aerospace technology, laying the foundation for future deep-space exploration.
  
  Regarding industrial technology, a study from the University of Cambridge published in the *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* (PNAS) has provided deeper scientific exploration into the theoretical foundations and physical limitations of interstellar travel. The Interstellar Research Group (IRG) also updated its database on September 25, 2026, integrating the latest advancements and technical parameters concerning interstellar exploration.
  
  These academic and industrial data collectively confirm that global interest in "interstellar-grade" missions is rising significantly, and that the relevant technical thresholds are gradually shifting from academic research to flight programs with commercial and scientific value through rigorous, phased review mechanisms.
  
  However, there remains an information gap among various sources regarding the details of Interstellar Technologies' flight demonstration. While the company has officially confirmed its entry into the final stage, public information does not yet detail the specific flight schedule, launch vehicle specifications, or the specific funding utilization behind the project. Simultaneously, the U.S. federal government registered the "Alien.gov" and "Aliens.gov" domains on March 20, 2026, a move that has triggered widespread speculation among the public and the media.
  
  According to a report by *USA Today*, although these domains were registered by U.S. federal agencies, the government has yet to provide a clear official explanation regarding the specific purpose of these websites, whether they involve extraterrestrial life research, or if they are merely a component of national security policy. This creates a subtle contrast with the technological progress of private aerospace companies.
  
  Such developments have had a direct impact on the global aerospace industry and policy formulation. The progress of Interstellar Technologies indicates that the participation and success rates of private enterprises in deep-space exploration are increasing significantly, forcing governments to re-examine the priorities of space governance and resource allocation. The U.S. government's registration of these domains, despite the unclear motivation, reflects that the level of importance placed on "interstellar issues" at the government level has risen from simple research subsidies to the level of national security and information disclosure. For the relevant industries, this means that future space exploration will not only be a matter of technological competition but will also be accompanied by complex government regulation and public opinion pressure.
  
  Although there is much anticipation, experts warn against over-interpreting the available information. First, Interstellar Technologies' flight demonstration is still in the testing phase, and there remains a significant technical gap before true interstellar travel can be achieved. Second, the government's registration of a domain does not equate to the discovery of extraterrestrial life or the possession of related technology. Current information is limited by the commercial secrets of private companies and the national security considerations of the government, making it difficult for the public to obtain a complete picture of the projects. Therefore, linking these independent events to the inference that "the government has obtained extraterrestrial evidence" currently lacks any empirical support and belongs only to the public's reasonable curiosity and speculation regarding unknown fields.

# 星際技術公司透過第三階段審查與美國政府啟動 Alien.gov 網域之進展

> 近期星際技術公司成功進入太空飛行示範階段，同時美國政府註冊 Alien.gov 網域引發社會廣泛關注，兩者分別代表民間航太產業的技術突破與政府對跨星際議題的政策部署。


星際技術公司（Interstellar Technologies）近期在國際太空產業發展中取得重大進展。根據該公司於 2026 年 3 月 31 日發布的官方訊息，該企業在參與的一項跨國航太評選計畫中，成功透過第三階段審查。此計畫最初篩選四家參與企業，隨後在 2024 年 9 月進入第一階段審查時，僅剩三家企業過關。到了最新的審查環節，星際技術公司與另一家競爭對手脫穎而出，正式進入包含飛行示範（flight demonstration）在內的最終階段。
此一程式標誌著該公司在星際級航太技術開發上，已從理論設計轉向實質的飛行驗證，為未來深空探索奠定基礎。
在產業技術層面，根據《美國國家科學院院刊》（PNAS）刊登的劍橋大學研究，關於星際探索的理論基礎與物理限制已有更深入的科學探討。星際研究小組（Interstellar Research Group, IRG）亦於 2026 年 9 月 25 日更新其資料庫，整合了多項關於星際探索的最新進展與技術引數。
這些學術與產業資料共同證實了全球航太領域對於「星際級」任務的關注度正顯著提升，且相關技術門檻正透過嚴格的分階段審查機制，逐步由學術研究轉化為具備商業與科學價值的飛行計畫。
然而，目前關於星際技術公司的飛行示範細節，各來源間仍存在資訊落差。雖然官方確認已進入最終階段，但對於具體的飛行時間表、發射載具規格，以及該計畫背後的具體資金運用細節，目前公開資訊並未詳述。與此同時，美國聯邦政府於 2026 年 3 月 20 日註冊了「Alien.gov」與「Aliens.gov」網域，此舉引發了公眾與媒體的廣泛猜測。
根據《今日美國》（USA Today）報導，儘管這些網域由美國聯邦機構註冊，但政府對於設立這些網站的具體目的、是否涉及外星生命研究或僅為國家安全政策的一環，目前尚未給出明確的官方解釋，這與民間航太企業的技術進展形成了微妙的對照。
此類發展對全球航太產業與政策制定產生了直接影響。星際技術公司的進展顯示，民間企業在深空探索領域的參與度與成功率正大幅提高，這迫使各國政府必須重新審視太空治理與資源分配的優先順序。美國政府對相關網域的註冊，儘管動機不明，卻反映出政府層面對於「星際議題」的重視程度已從單純的科研補助，上升至國家安全與資訊公開的層級。對於相關產業而言，這意味著未來的太空探索將不僅是技術競爭，更將伴隨著複雜的政府監管與公眾輿論壓力。
儘管各界對此充滿期待，但專家提醒不應過度解讀現有資訊。首先，星際技術公司的飛行示範仍處於測試階段，距離真正的星際航行仍有極大技術鴻溝；其次，政府註冊網域並不等同於發現外星生命或具備相關技術。目前的資訊限制在於，民間企業的商業機密與政府的國安考量，使得公眾難以獲取完整的計畫全貌。因此，將這些獨立事件連結為「政府已掌握外星證據」的推論，目前缺乏任何實證支援，僅屬於公眾對未知領域的合理好奇與猜測。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於星際技術公司是否能如期完成飛行示範，以及該示範任務的具體科學目標為何。此外，美國政府何時會正式啟用 Alien.gov 網域，以及該網站所承載的內容是否涉及實質的太空探索政策，將是衡量政府對星際議題態度轉變的關鍵指標。隨著航太技術與國際政策的互動影響，未來數月內相關領域的資訊透明度與產業競爭態勢，將成為全球太空科學界關注的核心。
