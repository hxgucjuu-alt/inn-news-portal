  # Tainan City Partners with Southern Taiwan University of Science and Technology to Promote Food Safety System, Announces 10-Year Results and List of Excellent Vendors
  
  > The Tainan City Government’s collaboration with the Southern Taiwan University of Science and Technology on a food safety and hygiene management project has reached its tenth year. During the 2026 results presentation, 225 excellent catering vendors were announced, aiming to strengthen the food safety net of the "Capital of Gastronomy" and provide residents and tourists with more secure dining options.
  
  As a renowned "Capital of Gastronomy" in Taiwan, Tainan City’s catering industry encompasses a diverse range of formats, including street food, large-scale restaurants, school lunches, and home-style cooking; consequently, food safety management has always been a core priority of municipal governance. Since 2016, the Tainan City Government has engaged in a long-term partnership with the Southern Taiwan University of Science and Technology (STUST) to jointly promote the "Food Safety and Hygiene Management System Project."
  
  This project aims to build a comprehensive food safety monitoring and management network through the professional guidance of academic institutions and the administrative execution of the city government. On November 13, 2026, the two parties held a results presentation for the "2026 Tainan City Food Safety and Hygiene Management System Project," marking the tenth year of their collaboration and highlighting the long-term commitment of industry-government-academia cooperation in the field of public health.
  
  The most notable development at this results presentation was the city government’s official announcement of a list of 225 "excellent vendors." Tainan Mayor Huang Wei-che attended the event to deliver remarks, emphasizing that food safety should be prioritized in the development of the culinary sector. Through this project, the city government not only provides strict hygiene management guidance to catering operators but also translates food safety information into a reference index for consumers through a transparent selection mechanism for excellent vendors.
  
  These selected vendors represent high standards in sanitary environments, ingredient management, and operational processes. This not only enhances the competitiveness of the local catering industry but also provides clear consumption guidance for tourists visiting Tainan, ensuring that the public can enjoy better health protections while savoring local delicacies.
  
  Although this results presentation clearly demonstrated the concrete achievements of the collaboration between the city government and academia, there remains ongoing discussion regarding the project's coverage across different types of catering establishments, as well as the depth and breadth of guidance provided to small-scale street vendors. Some perspectives suggest that while the announcement of 225 excellent vendors is significant, the challenge of extending food safety standards to grassroots food stalls—given the vast scale of Tainan's catering industry—remains a hurdle to be overcome. Furthermore, details regarding the dynamic update mechanism for the list of excellent vendors and the follow-up guidance for non-selected vendors are currently lacking in public information, awaiting further clarification in future policy white papers or related execution reports.
  
  The promotion of this food safety system project has a profound impact on public health and the tourism industry in the Tainan region. At the policy level, this is not merely about hygiene inspections, but a "soft" management model that uses "guidance instead of punishment," which helps improve the self-management awareness of catering operators. At the industry level, the certification label for excellent vendors can effectively build brand credibility, thereby boosting confidence in local tourism consumption. For the general public, the establishment of this network reduces information asymmetry regarding food safety risks, allowing consumers to have a more credible basis for choosing where to dine, ultimately driving the overall catering environment toward a safer and more hygienic standard.
  
  However, the public should remain rational and objective when interpreting such results. First, the list of excellent vendors reflects hygiene performance within a specific evaluation cycle and does not guarantee that the operator will maintain zero risk indefinitely. Second, food safety management involves complex links in the supply chain; while the promotion of a single project can improve the quality of terminal catering, it must be coordinated with upstream ingredient source management and cold chain logistics to achieve comprehensive public health protection. Therefore, this list should not be viewed as the sole guarantee of food safety. Consumers should still maintain basic hygiene observations and judgment when choosing where to eat and continue to monitor inspection updates released by the city government, avoiding over-reliance on a single certification label.
  
  Looking to the future, the partnership between the Tainan City Government and STUST will enter its next decade. The focus will shift toward the integration of digital management tools, such as whether mobile applications or real-time updated online maps can be used to make food safety information more accessible to the public. At the same time, with the rise of global public health awareness, how to align such localized food safety experiences with international standards—especially in maintaining the resilience of the food safety net in the face of climate change or fluctuations in international supply chains—will be a key indicator of the project's continued influence.
  
  Additionally, whether the city government will design a more refined grading system for guidance tailored to different types of catering operators will also be an important direction for observing the evolution of Tainan's food safety policy.

# 臺南市攜手南臺科大推動食安體系，十年成果發表並公佈優良廠商名單

> 臺南市政府與南臺科大合作食品安全衛生管理計畫邁入第十年，於115年度成果發表會中公佈225家優良餐飲廠商，旨在強化美食之都的食安防護網，提供民眾與遊客更安心的飲食選擇。


臺南市作為臺灣著名的「美食之都」，其餐飲產業涵蓋街邊小吃、大型餐廳、學校營養午餐及家庭料理等多元型態，食品安全管理向來是市政建設的核心重點。自2016年起，臺南市政府便與南臺科技大學展開長期合作，共同推動「食品安全衛生管理體系計畫」。
該計畫旨在透過學術單位的專業輔導與市府的行政執行力，建構一套完整的食安監測與管理網路。2026年11月13日，雙方共同舉辦「115年度臺南市食品安全衛生管理體系計畫」成果發表會，正式邁入合作的第十年，標誌著產官學合作在公共衛生領域的長期深耕。
在本次成果發表會中，最受矚目的進展為市府正式公佈了225家「優良廠商」名單。臺南市長黃偉哲親臨現場致詞，強調食品安全應被置於美食發展的優先位置。透過此項計畫，市府不僅對餐飲業者進行嚴格的衛生管理輔導，更透過公開透明的優良廠商評選機制，將食安資訊轉化為消費者可查詢的參考指標。
這些獲選廠商代表著在衛生環境、食材管理及作業流程上均符合高標準要求，不僅提升了在地餐飲業的競爭力，也為造訪臺南的遊客提供了明確的消費指引，確保民眾在品嚐在地美食時，能享有更為完善的健康保障。
儘管本次成果發表會明確展示了市府與學界合作的具體成效，但針對該計畫在不同餐飲型別間的覆蓋率，以及對於小型攤商的輔導深度與廣度，各界仍有不同討論。部分觀點認為，雖然225家優良廠商的公佈具有指標意義，但對於臺南市龐大的餐飲業規模而言，如何將食安標準進一步普及至基層小吃攤，仍是未來需要持續克服的挑戰。此外，關於優良廠商名單的動態更新機制，以及後續如何對未入選廠商進行持續性輔導，目前公開資訊中尚缺乏細節說明，仍有待後續政策白皮書或相關執行報告的進一步釐清。
此項食安體系計畫的推動，對臺南地區的公共衛生與觀光產業具有深遠影響。在政策層面，這不僅是單純的衛生稽查，更是一種透過「輔導代替處罰」的軟性管理模式，有助於改善餐飲業者的衛生自主管理意識。在產業層面，優良廠商的標章認證能有效建立品牌信譽，進而帶動在地觀光消費的信心。對於社會大眾而言，這套網路的建立降低了食安風險的資訊不對稱，讓消費者在選擇餐飲時能有更具公信力的參考依據，進而推動整體餐飲環境向更安全、衛生的方向升級。
然而，公眾在解讀此類成果時，亦應保持理性與客觀。首先，優良廠商名單反映的是特定評選週期內的衛生表現，並不代表該業者永久維持零風險。其次，食安管理涉及供應鏈的複雜環節，單一計畫的推動雖能改善終端餐飲品質，但仍需與上游食材源頭管理、物流冷鏈等環節相互配合，方能達到全面的公共衛生防護。因此，不應將此名單視為唯一的食安保證，消費者在選擇餐飲時，仍應維持基本的衛生觀察與判斷，並持續關注市府後續公佈的稽查動態，避免過度依賴單一認證標章。
展望未來，臺南市政府與南臺科大的合作將進入下一個十年。觀察重點將聚焦於數位化管理工具的匯入，例如是否能透過手機應用程式或即時更新的線上地圖，讓民眾更便利地查詢食安資訊。同時，隨著全球公共衛生意識的提升，如何將此類在地化的食安經驗與國際標準接軌，特別是在面對氣候變遷或國際供應鏈波動時，如何維持食品安全防護網的韌性，將是未來該計畫能否持續發揮影響力的關鍵指標。
此外，市府是否會針對不同型別的餐飲業者，設計更為精細的輔導分級制度，亦將是觀察臺南市食安政策演進的重要方向。
