  # Global Space Race Heats Up: China, Russia, the US, and Europe Conduct Intensive Launches Within 24 Hours
  
  > Over the past 24 hours, China, Russia, the United States, and Europe have conducted a rare series of consecutive space launches. Notably, China successfully launched the Shenzhou-24 manned spacecraft, which carries a Pakistani payload specialist for the first time, marking a significant milestone in international cooperation for China's manned space program.
  
  On November 26, 2026, the global space exploration sector experienced a rare window of intensive launch activity. According to reports from Technews citing The Paper, major global space powers—China, Russia, the United States, and Europe—conducted space launch missions one after another within the past 24 hours. This wave of intensive launches not only demonstrates the competition and capabilities of these nations in aerospace technology but also highlights a significant increase in the frequency of international space activities. Among these, China's mission progress has drawn particular attention; at 8:18 a.m. that morning, the Long March 2F Y24 carrier rocket, carrying the Shenzhou-24 manned spacecraft, was successfully launched from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center.
  
  Approximately 10 minutes after ignition, the rocket successfully delivered the spacecraft into its intended orbit.
  
  The most notable focus of the Shenzhou-24 mission lies in the depth of its international cooperation. Cross-referenced information from Technews confirms that the mission carries a payload specialist from Pakistan, who is primarily responsible for conducting scientific experiments in orbit. This marks the first time since the inception of China's manned space program that Chinese and foreign astronauts have flown together in the same cabin. This move is viewed as a key strategy in China's space diplomacy and technology outreach, aimed at strengthening scientific research ties with partner countries in the Asia-Pacific region through the manned space platform.
  
  Meanwhile, the launch missions conducted by the United States, Russia, and the European Space Agency within the same time window, while varying in detail, all indicate that global space infrastructure deployment and scientific exploration activities are currently at a peak.
  
  Although media outlets have reached a consensus on the fact of the "intensive global launches within 24 hours," there remains an information gap regarding the specific objectives and technical details of each nation's mission. According to Technews, China has clearly stated that the core of the Shenzhou-24 mission is manned spaceflight and international scientific cooperation; however, public information regarding the specific payloads, orbital parameters, and mission purposes of the launches conducted by Russia, the United States, and Europe during the same period remains relatively limited.
  
  Furthermore, compared to the incident in July of this year when China's long-range ballistic missile test in the Pacific triggered strong reactions from neighboring countries, the civilian and scientific nature of the Shenzhou-24 mission is more clearly defined. Nevertheless, various sectors continue to maintain a high level of concern regarding the ambiguity of the "dual-use" nature of China's space activities.
  
  This wave of intensive launches has had a profound impact on the technological and security landscape of the Asia-Pacific region. As space activity becomes a key arena for national competition, Asia-Pacific nations are increasingly reliant on space assets. According to relevant reports from Defense News, Japan is currently actively strengthening its long-range missile training and plans to utilize testing ranges in India for exercises, indicating that the Asia-Pacific region is responding to the increasingly complex geopolitical situation through transnational cooperation in defense technology and regional security.
  
  The enhancement of space launch capabilities is not only a symbol of scientific research strength but is also directly linked to navigation, communication, and intelligence-gathering capabilities within the region, making space policy an indispensable part of the diplomatic and defense strategies of Asia-Pacific nations.
  
  However, experts and observers have raised differing perspectives and concerns regarding the rapid expansion of global space activities. On one hand, international cooperation such as the joint flight of Shenzhou-24 is seen as a positive step toward scientific progress; on the other hand, the trend toward the militarization of space by various nations, as well as the lack of consensus on space traffic management rules, remain potential sources of risk. As discussed by The Daily Star regarding demographic shifts in the Asia-Pacific region, future societies will face significant challenges from aging populations, and resource allocation will become extremely sensitive. If the space race evolves into an endless arms race, it risks crowding out investments in public welfare and social infrastructure.
  
  Therefore, various sectors have called for avoiding the over-interpretation of the military intentions behind a single launch event, suggesting instead a greater focus on the potential for space activities to promote regional technological prosperity and peaceful use.

Within the Asia-Pacific region, the further integration of Japan and India in missile defense and space technology will also be an important indicator for observing changes in the regional strategic balance. As the frequency of space launches becomes normalized, establishing a set of transparent and predictable space operation norms will become a major challenge that Asia-Pacific nations and global aerospace powers must face in the coming years.

# 全球太空競賽升溫：中俄美歐於24小時內密集執行發射任務

> 在過去24小時內，中國、俄羅斯、美國與歐洲罕見地接連執行太空發射任務。其中，中國神舟24號載人太空船成功發射，並首次搭載巴基斯坦載荷專家，標誌著中國載人航天工程在國際合作上的重大里程碑。


2026年11月26日，全球太空探索領域迎來罕見的密集發射視窗。根據《Technews科技新報》引述澎湃新聞的報導，在過去24小時內，全球主要太空強權——中國、俄羅斯、美國與歐洲，先後執行了太空發射任務。此波密集發射潮不僅展現了各國在航太技術上的競爭與實力展示，更凸顯了國際太空活動頻率的顯著提升。其中，中國的任務進展備受矚目，其於當日上午8時18分，在酒泉衛星發射中心成功發射搭載神舟24號載人太空船的長徵2號F遙24運載火箭。
該火箭在點火後約10分鐘，順利將太空船送入預定軌道。
此次神舟24號任務最受關注的焦點，在於其國際合作的深度。根據《Technews科技新報》的交叉資訊確認，該任務搭載了一名來自巴基斯坦的載荷專家，主要負責在軌道上執行科學實驗。這是中國載人航天工程自實施以來，首次實現中外太空人同艙在軌聯合飛行。此舉被視為中國在太空外交與技術輸出上的重要策略，旨在透過載人航天平臺，加強與亞太地區夥伴國家的科研連結。
與此同時，美國、俄羅斯與歐洲太空總署在同一時間視窗內執行的發射任務，雖細節各異，但均顯示出全球太空基礎設施部署與科學探測活動的活躍度正處於高峰。
儘管各媒體對於「24小時內全球密集發射」的事實達成共識，但各國任務的具體目標與技術細節仍存在資訊落差。根據《Technews科技新報》報導，中國方面明確指出神舟24號的任務核心為載人航天與國際科研合作；然而，關於俄羅斯、美國與歐洲在同一時間段發射的具體載荷內容、軌道引數以及任務目的，目前公開資訊仍相對有限。
此外，與今年7月中國在太平洋進行長程彈道飛彈試射引發周邊國家強烈反應的事件相比，此次神舟24號的民用與科研性質較為明確，但各界對於中國太空活動在「軍民兩用」界線上的模糊性，仍保持高度關注。
此波密集發射潮對亞太地區的科技與安全格局產生了深遠影響。隨著太空活動成為各國競爭的關鍵領域，亞太國家對於太空資產的依賴度日益增加。根據《Defense News》的相關報導，日本近期正積極強化其長程飛彈訓練，並計畫利用印度的測試場域進行演練，顯示出亞太地區在國防科技與區域安全合作上，正透過跨國協作來應對日益複雜的地緣政治局勢。
太空發射能力的提升，不僅是科研實力的象徵，更直接關聯到區域內的導航、通訊與情報偵蒐能力，這使得太空政策已成為亞太各國外交與國防戰略中不可或缺的一環。
然而，針對全球太空活動的快速擴張，專家與觀察家也提出了不同觀點與限制。一方面，國際合作如神舟24號的聯合飛行，被視為推動科學進步的正面舉措；另一方面，各國在太空領域的軍事化趨勢，以及對於太空交通管理規則的缺乏共識，仍是潛在的風險來源。正如《The Daily Star》針對亞太地區人口結構變遷的討論，未來社會面臨高度老齡化挑戰，資源分配將變得極為敏感，若太空競賽演變成無止境的軍備競賽，恐將排擠民生與社會基礎建設的資源投入。
因此，各界呼籲不應過度解讀單一發射事件的軍事意圖，應更關注太空活動在促進區域科技共榮與和平利用上的潛力。
後續觀察重點將集中於神舟24號任務的在軌科學實驗成果，以及巴基斯坦載荷專家參與後的技術轉移效應。此外，國際社會將持續關注美國與歐洲在後續太空任務中，是否會針對中國的國際航太合作模式採取相應的外交或技術制衡措施。在亞太區域內，日本與印度在飛彈防禦與太空技術上的進一步整合，亦將是觀察區域戰略平衡變化的重要指標。隨著太空發射頻率的常態化，如何建立一套透明、可預測的太空運作規範，將成為亞太各國乃至全球航太強權在未來數年內必須面對的重大課題。
