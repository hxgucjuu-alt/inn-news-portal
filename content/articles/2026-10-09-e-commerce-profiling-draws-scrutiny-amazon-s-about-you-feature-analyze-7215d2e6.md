綜合多家媒體報導，電商龍頭亞馬遜平台上的「關於您」（About You）顧客檔案功能近日引發消費者與社群網路的廣泛關注。該功能主要透過分析使用者的歷史購買紀錄，進而歸納並列出系統對消費者的多項推測與標籤。

根據報導指出，這些資料檔案涵蓋的推論範圍相當具體，不僅涉及用戶喜愛的品牌和個人愛好，甚至包含對體型等身體特徵的直接推測。相關內容在網路上曝光後迅速傳播，不少消費者對演算法所呈現的高度個人化資訊感到驚訝。

此外，數位廣告與科技產業媒體在行業動態中亦提及此項數據追蹤現象，並對大型零售商的個人數據運用與廣告策略展開關注。各界報導顯示，這類由演算法生成的消費者檔案，再次凸顯出科技平台如何利用日常消費行為建立極為詳盡的用戶畫像。

## English edition

Recent media reports have brought widespread attention to Amazon's 'About You' customer profile feature, which has sparked discussions across the internet. The system utilizes shoppers' purchase histories to generate a series of assumptions and detailed profiles regarding individual consumers.

Reports indicate that these dossiers cover an unexpectedly specific range of characteristics, including preferred brands, personal hobbies, and even direct assumptions about shoppers' physical body traits. As users began discovering and sharing these profiles, the blunt and detailed nature of the algorithmic conclusions quickly went viral.

Concurrently, digital advertising and media outlets have noted this data-tracking phenomenon as part of broader shifts in retail media and consumer profiling. Coverage shows that such algorithmically generated user dossiers underscore the extent to which major tech platforms synthesize routine purchasing habits into deeply personalized consumer portraits.
