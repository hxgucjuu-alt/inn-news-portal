在一場歷史性且普遍出乎意料的事件中，樞機主教羅伯特·普雷沃斯特（Robert Prevost）在2025年的教宗選舉會議（conclave）中獲選為教宗良（Pope Leo），成為首位美國籍教宗。據報導，此次選舉發生在2025年5月，打破了外界普遍認為美國人不會擔任天主教會最高職位的預期。

資料顯示，當教宗選舉會議開始時，外界普遍未將美國籍教宗的可能性納入考量。然而，會議結束時，普雷沃斯特樞機主教已晉升為教宗。他曾被一些人稱為「鮑勃」（Bob）或「鮑勃神父」（Father Bob），其獲選為教宗良在全球範圍內引起了廣泛關注。

教宗良的當選對梵蒂岡和全球天主教社群而言，代表著一個關鍵時刻。包括NPR在內的媒體指出，有一篇「內幕故事」詳細描述了普雷沃斯特樞機主教如何被選中的幕後事件。這篇報導探討了這位美國樞機主教意外登上教宗寶座的歷程。

## English edition

In a historic and largely unexpected turn of events, Cardinal Robert Prevost was elected Pope Leo during the 2025 conclave, becoming the first American pontiff. The election, which reportedly took place in May 2025, defied widespread expectations that an American would never hold the highest office in the Catholic Church.

Sources indicate that when the conclave commenced, the prospect of an American pope was not widely considered. However, by its conclusion, Cardinal Prevost had ascended to the papacy. Known to some as "Bob" or "Father Bob," his elevation to Pope Leo has drawn significant attention globally.

The election of Pope Leo represents a pivotal moment for the Vatican and the global Catholic community. NPR, among other outlets, has highlighted that an "inside story" details the behind-the-scenes events of how Cardinal Prevost came to be chosen. This narrative explores the unexpected path that led to an American cardinal assuming the role of Pope.
