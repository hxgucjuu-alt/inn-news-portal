  # Europe Faces Dual Challenges of Geopolitics and Economics: From Brexit Legacies to Current Security Landscapes
  
  > Europe is currently facing multiple pressures. Beyond the border disputes left over from Brexit negotiations, the threat of Russian hybrid warfare and global economic volatility are impacting EU stability, with nations seeking to address these changes through retreat meetings and policy adjustments.
  
  The European region is currently in a period of complex transformation and turbulence. Looking back at the historical context, the issue of the border between Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland long remained the core of the deadlock in Brexit negotiations, not only delaying the Brexit timeline but also causing profound damage to political trust between the EU and the UK. At the same time, Europe is facing even more severe geopolitical challenges. According to warnings from Western officials, Russia is using "hybrid warfare" tactics—including cyberattacks, the spread of disinformation, and deliberate sabotage—to attempt to weaken public trust in European political systems and to divide support for Ukraine within the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) through economic pressure.
  
  These background factors collectively constitute the decision-making environment for current European public health, economic security, and defense policies.
  
  Regarding recent developments, European Council President Antonio Costa recently convened a special retreat meeting aimed at conducting in-depth public discussions on defense policy. Multiple sources have consistently pointed out that the meeting was not intended to produce formal declarations or concrete decisions, but rather to provide an informal platform for dialogue among member state leaders to address rising regional security risks. Meanwhile, European financial markets have also reflected the uncertainty of the external environment; the pan-European STOXX 600 index recently experienced a pullback, indicating that rising oil prices and climbing bond yields have pressured investor confidence, exacerbating economic anxiety within the European region.
  
  However, there remain differences in how the European situation is interpreted. Regarding the Brexit border issue, some reports indicated that the negotiation process could lead to a further delay of Brexit until January 2020, but such predictions have a temporal gap with the EU's current prioritization of geopolitical crises. Furthermore, regarding the specific impact of Russian hybrid warfare on Europe, while intelligence agencies and media outlets in various countries agree that the threat is rising, there is currently no consensus on what specific countermeasures governments should take, or how to balance economic sanctions with energy supply security.
  
  In addition, some commercial information regarding market entry and media promotion reflects the reality that companies are still seeking exposure in the European market amidst the turbulent environment.
  
  The aforementioned situation has had a direct impact on European industries and policies. At the defense policy level, EU member states have been forced to reassess their military budgets and strategic autonomy to cope with the long-term security threats posed by Russia. Economically, energy price volatility and inflationary pressures have forced the European Central Bank and national fiscal departments to seek a balance between monetary tightening and economic stimulus, especially against the backdrop of rising bond yields, which has increased corporate financing costs and subsequently affected overall market performance. Furthermore, political divisions within the region may also affect the EU's coordination efficiency in handling public health and humanitarian affairs, such as the Ukraine issue and refugee resettlement.
  
  Regarding the current situation, there are significant differences in perspectives, which limits policy effectiveness. Some observers believe that the EU's current retreat meeting model is too soft to provide a decisive response to Russia's hybrid warfare threats; however, another school of thought emphasizes that in a situation where member states are divided, maintaining open channels of dialogue is crucial to preventing the escalation of regional conflicts. Additionally, analyses regarding the ripple effects of external conflicts (such as the situation in Iran) on the European economy vary; one should not directly attribute single market fluctuations or isolated military events to the failure of European policy, as over-interpretation may lead to market panic and weaken Europe's resilience in addressing long-term challenges.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on the progress of concrete coordination in EU defense policy, particularly whether the EU can establish a more unified defense mechanism in the face of Russian hybrid warfare tactics. At the same time, the performance of European stock markets under the volatility of bond yields, and how various countries use fiscal measures to alleviate the pressure on livelihoods caused by rising oil prices, will be key indicators for assessing European economic stability. Furthermore, whether the legacy of the Northern Ireland border issue will continue to disrupt UK-EU relations, and whether the attractiveness of the European market to external investment will undergo structural changes due to geopolitical risks, all require continued monitoring of subsequent policy trends and market data.

# 歐洲面臨地緣政治與經濟雙重挑戰：從脫歐遺緒到當前安全域性勢

> 歐洲近期面臨多重壓力，除脫歐談判遺留的邊界爭議外，俄羅斯混合戰威脅與全球經濟波動亦影響歐盟穩定，各國正尋求透過避靜會議與政策調整應對變局。


歐洲地區當前正處於複雜的轉型與動盪期。回顧歷史脈絡，英國脫歐程式中，北愛爾蘭與愛爾蘭邊界問題長期成為談判僵局的核心，不僅拖延了脫歐時程，更對歐盟與英國的政治互信造成深遠影響。與此同時，當前歐洲面臨更為嚴峻的地緣政治挑戰，根據西方官員示警，俄羅斯正透過網路攻擊、假訊息散佈及蓄意破壞等「混合戰」手段，試圖削弱歐洲公眾對政治體制的信任，並藉由經濟壓力分化北大西洋公約組織（NATO）內部對烏克蘭的支援。
這些背景因素共同構成了當前歐洲公共衛生、經濟安全與國防政策的決策環境。
在近期進展方面，歐盟理事會主席柯斯塔（Antonio Costa）近期召集了一場特別避靜會議，旨在針對國防政策進行深入的公開討論。多個來源一致指出，該會議並非為了產出正式宣言或具體決策，而是提供成員國領導人一個非正式的對話平臺，以應對日益升高的區域安全風險。與此同時，歐洲金融市場亦反映了外部環境的不確定性，泛歐 STOXX 600 指數近期出現回撥，顯示油價上漲及債券殖利率攀升，已對投資人信心構成壓力，加劇了歐洲區域內部的經濟焦慮感。
然而，各界對於歐洲局勢的解讀仍存在差異。關於脫歐邊界問題，部分報導指出談判程式可能導致脫歐進一步延後至 2020 年 1 月，但此類預測與當前歐盟處理地緣政治危機的優先順序存在時間落差。此外，關於俄羅斯混合戰對歐洲的具體影響，各國情報機構與媒體觀點雖一致認為威脅正在上升，但對於各國政府應採取何種具體反制措施，以及如何平衡經濟制裁與能源供應安全，目前尚未達成一致共識。
此外，部分關於市場進入與媒體宣傳的商業資訊，則反映了企業在動盪環境中仍試圖尋求歐洲市場曝光的現實需求。
上述局勢對歐洲產業與政策產生了直接影響。在國防政策層面，歐盟成員國被迫重新評估其軍事預算與戰略自主性，以應對俄羅斯帶來的長期安全威脅。在經濟層面，能源價格波動與通膨壓力迫使歐洲央行與各國財政部門必須在貨幣緊縮與刺激經濟之間尋求平衡，特別是在債券殖利率上升的背景下，企業融資成本增加，進而影響了市場的整體表現。此外，區域內部的政治分歧亦可能影響歐盟在處理烏克蘭議題及難民安置等公共衛生與人道事務上的協調效率。
針對目前的局勢，各界觀點存在顯著差異，限制了政策的有效性。部分觀察家認為，歐盟目前的避靜會議模式過於軟性，難以對俄羅斯的混合戰威脅做出果斷回應；然而，另一派觀點則強調，在成員國意見分歧的情況下，維持對話管道的暢通對於避免區域衝突升級至關重要。此外，對於外部衝突（如伊朗局勢）對歐洲經濟的連帶影響，各界分析亦呈現不一，不應將單一市場波動或單一軍事事件直接歸因於歐洲政策的失敗，過度解讀可能導致市場恐慌，反而削弱了歐洲應對長期挑戰的韌性。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於歐盟在國防政策上的具體協調進度，特別是面對俄羅斯混合戰手段時，歐盟是否能建立更為統一的防禦機制。同時，歐洲股市在債券殖利率波動下的表現，以及各國如何透過財政手段緩解油價上漲帶來的民生壓力，將是評估歐洲經濟穩定性的關鍵指標。此外，北愛邊界問題的遺緒是否會持續幹擾英歐關係，以及歐洲市場對於外部投資的吸引力是否會因地緣政治風險而發生結構性改變，皆需持續關注後續的政策動向與市場資料。
