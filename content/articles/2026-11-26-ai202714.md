  # Global AI Capital Expenditure Projected to Reach $1.4 Trillion by 2027, Market Warns of Investment Risks and Bubble Concerns
  
  > UBS forecasts that global AI capital expenditure will rise to $1.4 trillion by 2027; however, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) has pointed to high levels of future revenue commitments, sparking a debate over whether AI investment is a growth engine or a bubble.
  
  The global artificial intelligence (AI) industry is experiencing an unprecedented frenzy of capital investment, with the scale of hardware infrastructure construction continuing to expand. According to the latest 2027 outlook report released by UBS, global AI-related capital expenditure is expected to climb significantly from $1 trillion in 2026 to $1.4 trillion in 2027, with a further projection of reaching $1.5 trillion in 2028. This massive expenditure covers hardware infrastructure such as servers, high-end chips, and power and cooling systems required for building data centers.
  
  Meanwhile, tech giant NVIDIA saw a significant rebound in its stock price following CEO Jensen Huang's return to his position, with its market capitalization increasing by approximately $953 billion in a single day, indicating that market confidence in the AI industry's development roadmap remains strong.
  
  Multiple sources have confirmed that current capital expenditure in the global tech industry has reached a staggering scale and is highly concentrated among a few tech giants. The UBS report notes that due to the high concentration risk in the AI industry, institutional investors are advised to adjust their portfolios by diversifying funds into hyperscale cloud service providers and shifting toward broader supply chain targets such as wafer foundries and equipment manufacturers. Furthermore, NVIDIA’s market capitalization climbing to approximately $5.8 trillion, and TSMC’s record-high revenue in the third quarter of this year (approximately 7.27 trillion yen), both confirm that the AI hardware supply chain is highly profitable amidst the current capital frenzy, serving as the primary driver for global tech stock performance.
  
  However, there is significant disagreement regarding the nature of this investment boom. Data compiled by the International Monetary Fund (IMF) shows that there are currently 14 AI-related "circular financing" deals, involving equity and debt scales of approximately $950 billion, accompanied by future revenue commitments totaling as much as $1.4 trillion. Some economists and market analysts warn that these "commitments" are not equivalent to actual revenue, and if the funding sources lack support from substantive commercial return logic, it could trigger bubble risks.
  
  In addition, market observers such as Hsieh Chin-ho have also cautioned that although Jensen Huang's return has boosted confidence, the global tech industry still faces potential variables such as tariff events, and investors should be prudent regarding the sustainability of profits for high-margin enterprises.
  
  This expansion in AI capital expenditure has had a profound impact on the global economy and industrial chain. For Taiwan, as the core of the AI hardware supply chain, the order status and revenue performance of companies like TSMC and SK Hynix directly reflect the intensity of global data center construction. However, the IMF report warns that the growth in orders for the Taiwanese supply chain ultimately depends on the source and stability of client funding. If AI investment is based solely on power struggles or overly optimistic market expectations rather than concrete terminal commercial application profits, any future slowdown in capital expenditure could impact export-oriented economies that are highly dependent on AI hardware orders.
  
  Different perspectives suggest that AI investment should not be simplified into a binary opposition between a bubble and a growth engine. The asset allocation strategy suggested by UBS shows that professional institutions are attempting to cope with the industry's high concentration by diversifying risks; meanwhile, economists emphasize that the current frenzy of hardware procurement and infrastructure construction may have moved away from simple commercial return logic toward strategic power struggles. Therefore, market observers urge investors to avoid over-interpreting the market capitalization fluctuations of individual tech giants and to closely monitor the cash flow structure behind capital expenditures, as well as the potential interference of global trade policies (such as tariffs) on supply chain stability.
  
  The key focus for future observation is whether the projected $1.4 trillion in capital expenditure by 2027 can be translated into concrete productivity gains and corporate profits. If future revenue commitments cannot be met as expected, the market may face pressure for capital expenditure corrections. Furthermore, as AI hardware construction enters a mature phase, competition in power supply, cooling technology, and chip manufacturing processes will continue to intensify. Investors should continue to track the actual operational data of tech giants following data center construction, as well as changes in regulation and tax policies regarding the AI industry in major global economies; these factors will determine whether this AI capital frenzy is a long-term industrial upgrade or a short-lived market bubble.

# 全球AI資本支出預計2027年達1.4兆美元，市場警示投資風險與泡沫疑慮

> 瑞銀預測全球AI資本支出將於2027年增至1.4兆美元，惟國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）指出存在高額未來營收承諾，引發市場對AI投資是成長引擎或泡沫的辯論。


全球人工智慧（AI）產業正經歷前所未有的資本投入狂潮，硬體基礎設施建設規模持續擴張。根據瑞銀（UBS）最新發布的2027年展望報告顯示，全球AI相關的資本支出預計將從2026年的1兆美元，大幅攀升至2027年的1.4兆美元，並預估在2028年進一步達到1.5兆美元。這項龐大的支出涵蓋了建置資料中心所需的伺服器、高階晶片，以及電力與冷卻系統等硬體基礎設施。
與此同時，科技巨頭輝達（NVIDIA）在執行長黃仁勳重返職位後，股價出現顯著回升，單日市值曾增加約9,530億美元，顯示市場對於AI產業發展藍圖的信心依然強勁。
多方來源共同確認，當前全球科技產業的資本支出已達到驚人規模，且高度集中於少數科技巨頭。瑞銀報告指出，由於AI產業存在高度集中風險，建議機構投資人應調整投資組合，將資金分散配置至超大規模雲端服務商，並轉向晶圓代工與裝置製造商等更廣泛的供應鏈標的。此外，輝達市值攀升至約5.8兆美元，以及臺積電在今年第三季營收創下歷史新高（約7.27兆日圓），均證實了AI硬體供應鏈在當前資本狂潮中獲利豐厚，成為推動全球科技股行情的主要動力。
然而，市場對於這波投資熱潮的本質存在顯著分歧。國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）整理的資料顯示，目前市場存在14筆AI相關的「迴圈融資」交易，涉及股權與債務規模約9,500億美元，且伴隨高達1.4兆美元的未來營收承諾。部分經濟學家與市場分析師警示，這些「承諾」並不等同於實際收入，若資金來源缺乏實質商業回報邏輯支援，可能引發泡沫風險。
此外，謝金河等市場觀察家亦提醒，儘管黃仁勳的回歸帶動了信心，但全球科技產業仍面臨關稅事件等潛在變數，投資人應審慎看待高毛利企業的獲利持續性。
這波AI資本支出的擴張，對全球經濟與產業鏈產生了深遠影響。對於臺灣而言，作為AI硬體供應鏈的核心，臺積電、SK海力士等企業的接單狀況與營收表現，直接反映了全球資料中心建置的強度。但IMF的報告提醒，臺灣供應鏈的接單成長，最終仍取決於客戶資金的來源與穩定性。若AI投資僅是基於權力爭奪或過度樂觀的市場預期，而非基於具體的終端商業應用獲利，未來一旦資本支出放緩，將可能對高度依賴AI硬體訂單的出口導向經濟體造成衝擊。
不同觀點認為，AI投資不應被簡化為泡沫或成長引擎的二元對立。瑞銀建議的資產配置策略，顯示出專業機構正試圖透過分散風險來應對產業的高度集中化；而經濟學家則強調，當前硬體採購與基礎建設的狂潮，可能已脫離單純的商業回報邏輯，轉向戰略性的權力爭奪。因此，市場觀察家呼籲，投資人應避免過度解讀單一科技巨頭的市值波動，並應密切關注資本支出背後的現金流結構，以及全球貿易政策（如關稅）對供應鏈穩定性的潛在幹擾。
後續觀察重點在於，2027年預期的1.4兆美元資本支出能否轉化為具體的生產力提升與企業獲利。若未來營收承諾無法如期兌現，市場恐將面臨資本支出修正的壓力。此外，隨著AI硬體建設進入成熟期，電力供應、冷卻技術與晶片製程的競爭將持續加劇。投資人應持續追蹤科技巨頭在資料中心建置後的實際營運資料，以及全球主要經濟體對於AI產業的監管與稅務政策變化，這些因素將決定這波AI資本狂潮是長期的產業升級，還是短暫的市場泡沫。
