  # Observations on China’s Deepening Digital Economy Cooperation and Regional Strategic Layout with Mekong Countries
  
  > The 11th Lancang-Mekong Cooperation (LMC) Foreign Ministers' Meeting recently concluded, focusing on green innovation and the digital economy. Meanwhile, external observers are paying close attention to China's military infrastructure footprint in Southeast Asia, particularly the potential impact of airports in Laos on South China Sea strategy.
  
  The 11th Lancang-Mekong Cooperation (LMC) Foreign Ministers' Meeting was held successfully. This mechanism, involving China and five countries in the Mekong River basin (Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar, Thailand, and Vietnam), aims to promote regional economic and social development as well as connectivity. The meeting focused on fostering momentum for green innovation and explicitly proposed expanding cooperation in emerging fields such as the digital economy and artificial intelligence (AI). China also publicly welcomed more Mekong countries to join the "World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization." This move indicates that China is attempting to further deepen its strategic ties with Southeast Asian nations through technology exports and standard-setting, extending its economic influence into the digital and high-tech sectors.
  
  According to various public information and official announcements, the LMC mechanism has become a key platform for China to promote its policies in Southeast Asia. During this Foreign Ministers' Meeting, all parties reaffirmed their willingness to strengthen multilateral cooperation, reaching a consensus particularly on digital infrastructure and innovation-driven development. At the same time, the international community holds varying perspectives on China's substantive influence in Southeast Asia. A BBC Vietnamese report noted that China's infrastructure construction in Southeast Asia is not limited to civilian projects; its layout of military airport facilities in places like Laos has also sparked discussions about whether China intends to use these outposts to strengthen its strategic projection capabilities in the South China Sea.
  
  The dual-use nature of these infrastructure projects has become a central focus in observing the security cooperation relationship between China and Southeast Asian nations.
  
  Regarding the assessment of China's military airport layout in Laos and its impact on regional security, there are currently significant differences in information from various parties. Official channels largely emphasize the economic development and mutually beneficial nature of the LMC, focusing on the digital economy and green transition; however, security analysts have expressed concerns about the strategic intent behind China's infrastructure construction. Information yet to be confirmed includes the actual military planning for these airports in the event of future regional conflicts, and how the Laotian government balances its autonomy in regional security issues while maintaining economic cooperation with China.
  
  Furthermore, the specific implementation progress and technical standard compatibility of the China-promoted "World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization" in Mekong countries are also key variables in observing the direction of digital governance in this region.
  
  For the Southeast Asian region, such strategic layouts by China have profound economic and security implications. Economically, by participating in China-led digital economy cooperation, Mekong countries may accelerate their domestic industrial upgrading and digitalization processes; however, this could simultaneously lead to a higher dependency on China regarding technical standards and supply chains. In terms of security, if China continues to strengthen its military logistics and infrastructure layout in inland Mekong countries, it could alter the strategic balance around the South China Sea, forcing surrounding nations to adopt more cautious diplomatic strategies between the two major powers of the U.S. and China to safeguard their sovereignty and regional stability.
  
  However, these developments should not be over-interpreted or attributed to a single cause. First, as a regional multilateral mechanism, the core goal of the LMC remains economic development; linking all infrastructure construction directly to military strategy may oversimplify complex bilateral relations. Second, when facing China's digital and infrastructure investments, Southeast Asian countries generally adopt diversified foreign policies, attempting to disperse risks by introducing investments from multiple sources rather than simply leaning toward one power. Therefore, China's expansion of influence is constrained by the geopolitical complexity of the region, and the defensive mechanisms of various countries regarding digital sovereignty and national security will also limit the exercise of unilateral Chinese influence.
  
  Future observations should focus on two main directions: First, whether the digital economy agreements under the LMC framework will translate into concrete technical standards and market access rules, and how these rules will affect the region's digital trade relations with other global economies (such as the U.S., EU, and Japan). Second, whether China's construction of military and paramilitary facilities in Southeast Asia will shift from existing logistics support to more substantive military cooperation as geopolitical tensions rise, which would directly affect the long-term stability of the South China Sea. All parties should continue to monitor the changing roles of these facilities in future regional military exercises or contingencies to assess the true intent and actual efficacy of China's strategic layout in Southeast Asia.

# 中國與湄公河國家深化數位經濟合作及區域戰略佈局觀察

> 瀾湄合作第十一次外長會近期落幕，聚焦綠色創新與數位經濟。與此同時，外界關注中國在東南亞的軍事基礎設施佈局，特別是寮國機場對南海戰略的潛在影響。


瀾滄江—湄公河合作（簡稱「瀾湄合作」）第十一次外長會近期順利舉行。該機制由中國與湄公河流域的五個國家（柬埔寨、寮國、緬甸、泰國、越南）共同參與，旨在推動區域內的經濟社會發展與互聯互通。本次會議重點討論了培育綠色創新動能，並明確提出拓展數位經濟、人工智慧（AI）等新興領域的合作，中方並公開歡迎更多湄公河國家加入「世界人工智慧合作組織」。此舉顯示中國正試圖透過技術輸出與標準制定，進一步深化與東南亞國家的戰略連結，將其經濟影響力擴充套件至數位與高科技產業領域。
根據多方公開資訊與官方宣告，瀾湄合作機制已成為中國在東南亞推動政策的重要平臺。在本次外長會中，各方共同確認了加強多邊合作的意願，特別是在數位基礎設施與創新驅動發展方面達成共識。與此同時，國際社會對於中國在東南亞的實質影響力存有不同視角。BBC越南語報導指出，中國在東南亞的基礎設施建設不僅限於民用專案，其在寮國等地的軍用機場設施佈局，亦引發外界對於中國是否意圖利用這些據點，強化其在南海地區戰略投射能力的討論。
這些基礎設施的雙重用途特性，成為觀察中國與東南亞國家安全合作關係的核心焦點。
關於中國在寮國的軍用機場佈局及其對區域安全影響的評估，目前各方資訊存在顯著差異。官方層面多強調瀾湄合作的經濟發展與互利共贏性質，聚焦於數位經濟與綠色轉型；然而，安全分析人士則對中國在基礎設施建設背後的戰略意圖表示關切。目前尚待確認的資訊包括：這些機場在未來區域衝突中的實際軍事用途規劃，以及寮國政府如何在維持與中國經濟合作的同時，平衡其在區域安全議題上的自主性。
此外，中國推動的「世界人工智慧合作組織」在湄公河國家的具體落地進度與技術標準相容性，亦是觀察此區域數位治理走向的關鍵變數。
對於東南亞地區而言，中國此類戰略佈局具有深遠的經濟與安全影響。在經濟層面，湄公河國家透過參與中國主導的數位經濟合作，有望加速國內產業升級與數位化程式；但同時，這也可能導致區域內國家在技術標準與供應鏈上對中國產生更高的依賴。在安全層面，若中國持續強化在湄公河內陸國家的軍事物流與基礎設施佈局，將可能改變南海周邊的戰略平衡，迫使周邊國家在美中兩大強權之間採取更為謹慎的外交策略，以維護自身的主權與地區穩定。
然而，對於上述發展不應過度解讀或進行單一面向的歸因。首先，瀾湄合作作為區域性多邊機制，其核心目標仍以經濟開發為主，將所有基礎設施建設直接連結至軍事戰略可能過於簡化複雜的雙邊關係。其次，東南亞國家在面對中國的數位與基礎設施投資時，普遍採取多元化的對外政策，試圖透過引入多方投資來分散風險，而非單純倒向某一強權。因此，中國的影響力擴張面臨著區域內地緣政治複雜性的制約，各國在數位主權與國家安全上的防禦機制，亦將限制中國單方面影響力的發揮。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於兩大方向：第一，瀾湄合作框架下的數位經濟協議是否會轉化為具體的技術標準與市場准入規則，以及這些規則如何影響該區域與全球其他經濟體（如美國、歐盟、日本）的數位貿易關係。第二，中國在東南亞的軍事與準軍事設施建設，是否會隨著地緣政治緊張局勢的升溫，而從現有的物流支援轉向更具實質性的軍事合作，這將直接影響南海地區的長遠穩定。各界應持續關注這些基礎設施在未來區域性軍事演習或突發事件中的角色變化，以評估中國在東南亞戰略佈局的真實意圖與實際效能。
