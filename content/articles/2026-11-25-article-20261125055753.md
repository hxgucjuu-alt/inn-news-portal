  # Analysis of Semiconductor Education Roots and Global Computing Power Regulation Trends in Taiwan
  
  > In response to recent developments in the technology industry, KidZania Taipei has introduced a TSMC wafer fab experience to promote popular science education. Meanwhile, the U.S. Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) has issued new regulations extending export controls to remote computing power, impacting the governance models of Taiwan's supply chain.
  
  KidZania Taipei officially launched its trial operations on November 24, 2026. Located on the 5th floor of LaLaport in Nangang, Taipei, it is the world's first children's occupational experience city built entirely in Mandarin. The most notable highlight of this launch is the cross-industry collaboration between TSMC and KidZania, which brings a professional semiconductor wafer fabrication scene into the park. During the experience, children must wear cleanroom suits to act as engineers and personally operate interactive semiconductor manufacturing teaching tools.
  
  This initiative is not only a concrete action for companies to practice Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles, but it also transforms a highly complex technology industry chain into a practical field for STEAM education, attempting to establish foundational awareness of the semiconductor industry at the childhood stage.
  
  At the same time, Taiwan's technology industry is facing significant changes in the international geopolitical and regulatory environment. According to the latest regulations issued by the U.S. Department of Commerce's Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS), the scope of export controls is no longer limited to physical chips but has been extended to users of "remote computing power." This means that if Taiwanese companies provide large-scale remote computing services to restricted entities, they may also need to apply for export licenses. This policy shift marks a new phase in global computing power governance; the business model previously held by Taiwanese operators—that "product delivery completes the responsibility"—is no longer applicable. They must re-examine the compliance of their supply chains and computing services to cope with increasingly stringent cross-border regulatory requirements.
  
  In terms of industrial development, Portwell Inc. (7562), as a representative player in the Industrial PC (IPC) sector, has an operational strategy closely linked to the implementation of global edge AI applications. Benefiting from the steady growth of smart transportation and automation projects, Portwell achieved an annual revenue of NT$750 million last year, with earnings per share (EPS) of NT$3.35. In the first three quarters of this year, after-tax profit reached NT$51 million, with an EPS of NT$2.24. The company expects to transfer to a listing on the Taipei Exchange in mid-January 2026 and held an investor conference prior to the listing on November 25.
  
  Portwell currently focuses on industrial motherboards, fanless embedded systems, and industrial-grade edge AI computing platforms. Its operating performance and expectations for double-digit growth in the future reflect the technical strength and market competitiveness of Taiwanese technology companies in the field of edge computing.
  
  The aforementioned events have had multiple impacts on Taiwan's technology industry. First, in terms of education and talent cultivation, KidZania's immersive experience helps improve public understanding of the semiconductor industry, which contributes to the establishment of a talent pool in the long run. Second, regarding policy compliance, the new U.S. BIS regulations will force Taiwanese companies to transform from simple device suppliers into "governance partners" with a high level of compliance awareness. This requires companies not only to remain cautious in hardware exports but also to establish sound risk control mechanisms for the cross-border flow of software and computing services to ensure a stable competitive position in the global supply chain.
  
  However, there are still differences and limitations in the interpretation of these trends. Regarding the semiconductor experience field, it is generally believed to have value for popular science enlightenment, but the gap between its educational effectiveness and industrial practice still needs to be evaluated through long-term observation; one should not over-mythologize the immediate impact of a single experience venue on the industry's talent structure. Furthermore, regarding the U.S. regulation of remote computing power, although the policy direction is clear, the specific execution details and the degree of impact on various types of enterprises still depend on the subsequent interpretation of legal details and international coordination. When facing global computing power regulations, Taiwanese companies need to carefully assess whether their own businesses touch upon regulatory red lines to avoid operational risks caused by information asymmetry.
  
  Looking ahead, Taiwan's technology industry will continue to advance simultaneously on the two paths of "educational roots" and "international compliance." The trial operation of KidZania Taipei will continue until December 6, and subsequent market reactions will be key to observing whether this popular science education model can be successfully promoted. In terms of industrial regulation, Portwell's transfer to the Taipei Exchange and its performance in the edge AI field will become indicators of how Taiwan's IPC industry responds to global computing power governance policies.
  
  Industry and academia should continue to pay attention to the subsequent developments of the U.S. BIS and strengthen supply chain transparency and compliance governance to ensure that Taiwan can continue to play a key and reliable partner role in the changing global technology landscape.

# 臺灣半導體教育紮根與全球算力管制趨勢分析

> 針對近期科技產業動態，KidZania Taipei 引入臺積電晶圓廠體驗以推動科普教育，同時美國 BIS 發布新規，將出口管制範圍擴大至遠端算力，影響臺灣供應鏈治理模式。


KidZania Taipei 奇薩尼亞臺北於 2026 年 11 月 24 日正式啟動試營運，該場域位於臺北南港 LaLaport 5 樓，為全球首座以全中文體驗語言打造的兒童職業體驗城。本次最受矚目的亮點，在於臺積電與 KidZania 的跨界合作，將半導體製造晶圓廠的專業場景搬進園區。孩童在體驗過程中，需穿上無塵衣化身工程師，親自操作半導體製程互動教具。
此舉不僅是企業實踐環境、社會與公司治理（ESG）的具體行動，更將高度複雜的科技產業鏈轉化為 STEAM 科普教育的實踐場域，試圖在兒童階段即建立對半導體產業的基礎認知。
與此同時，臺灣科技產業亦面臨國際地緣政治與監管環境的重大變革。根據美國商務部工業與安全域性（BIS）發布的最新規範，出口管制的範疇已不再侷限於實體晶片，而是進一步延伸至「遠端算力」的使用者。這意味著，若臺灣業者向受限物件提供大規模的遠端算力服務，亦可能需要申請出口許可。此項政策變動標誌著全球算力治理進入新階段，臺灣業者過去認為「產品交付即完成責任」的商業模式已不適用，必須重新檢視供應鏈與算力服務的合規性，以應對日益嚴格的跨國監管要求。
在產業發展層面，博來科技（7562）作為工業電腦（IPC）領域的代表業者，其營運策略與全球邊緣 AI 應用的落地程式緊密相連。博來科技受惠於智慧交通與自動化專案的穩健發酵，去年度營收達 7.5 億元，每股盈餘（EPS）為 3.35 元；今年前三季稅後盈餘達 0.51 億元，EPS 為 2.24 元。該公司預計於 2026 年 1 月中旬轉上櫃掛牌，並於 11 月 25 日舉辦上櫃前業績發表會。
博來科技目前主攻工業主機板、無風扇嵌入式系統及工業級邊緣 AI 運算平臺，其經營績效與未來雙位數成長的預期，反映出臺灣科技業者在邊緣運算領域的技術實力與市場競爭力。
上述事件對臺灣科技產業產生了多重影響。首先，在教育與人才培養方面，KidZania 的沉浸式體驗有助於提升大眾對半導體產業的理解，長遠來看有助於人才庫的建立。其次，在政策合規方面，美國 BIS 的新規將迫使臺灣企業轉型，從單純的裝置供應商轉變為具備高度合規意識的「治理夥伴」。這不僅要求企業在硬體出口上保持謹慎，更需在軟體與算力服務的跨境流動中，建立完善的風險控管機制，以確保在全球供應鏈中維持穩定的競爭地位。
然而，各界對於上述趨勢的解讀仍存在差異與限制。針對半導體體驗場域，外界普遍認為其具有科普啟蒙價值，但其教育成效與產業實務的落差仍需透過長期觀察評估，不應過度神話單一體驗場域對產業人才結構的立即影響。此外，關於美國對遠端算力的管制，雖然政策方向明確，但具體的執行細節與對各型別企業的衝擊程度，仍取決於後續法令的細則解釋與國際間的協調。臺灣企業在面對全球算力管制時，需謹慎評估自身業務是否觸及管制紅線，避免因資訊不對稱而造成營運風險。
展望未來，臺灣科技產業將持續在「教育紮根」與「國際合規」兩條路徑上同步前進。KidZania Taipei 的試營運將持續至 12 月 6 日，後續市場反應將是觀察科普教育模式是否能成功推廣的關鍵。而在產業監管方面，博來科技的轉上櫃程式與其在邊緣 AI 領域的表現，將成為觀察臺灣 IPC 產業如何應對全球算力治理政策的指標。
產業界與學界應持續關注美國 BIS 的後續動態，並強化供應鏈的透明度與合規治理，以確保在變動的全球科技局勢中，臺灣能持續扮演關鍵且可靠的合作夥伴角色。
