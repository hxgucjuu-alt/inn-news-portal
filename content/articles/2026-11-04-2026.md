  # 2026 Taiwan Week Focuses on Financial Internationalization: President Lai Ching-te Announces Plan to Build an Asian Asset Management Center
  
  > 2026 Taiwan Week officially opened this month. President Lai Ching-te emphasized that by internationalizing the financial market and leveraging the advantages of the AI industry, Taiwan will be promoted as an Asian asset management center to strengthen the nation's overall economic competitiveness.
  
  2026 Taiwan Week officially kicked off recently. This annual financial event is led by the Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC) and co-organized by various securities and futures-related institutions. The event aims to showcase Taiwan's development momentum in the financial industry and asset management sector to the global capital market. It brought together over 400 distinguished guests, including leaders of international exchanges, representatives from global asset management institutions, foreign institutional investors, senior executives from listed companies, and domestic financial industry professionals. This grand event serves not only as an important platform for Taiwan's financial industry to integrate with the international community but is also seen as a key indicator for assessing how Taiwan's capital market can transform and enhance its global influence in the post-pandemic era and the wave of AI technology.
  
  In his opening remarks, President Lai Ching-te clearly pointed out that Taiwan has demonstrated excellent economic resilience through its strong industrial strength amidst the current global AI technology wave. However, Lai emphasized that the enhancement of national competitiveness cannot rely solely on hardware manufacturing and the technology industry; it requires a robust, open, and internationally competitive financial market as a solid foundation. According to public data, based on the sum of national market categories, the Taiwan stock market has officially risen to become the fourth-largest in the world, with the scale of the capital market showing a continuous expansion trend.
  
  To sustain this growth momentum, the government has set a clear policy direction, committing to promoting the internationalization of the financial market, actively building Taiwan into an Asian asset management center, and continuously enhancing the innovation capabilities of the capital market.
  
  Regarding the details of this policy initiative, the focus of various sectors lies in how the government will implement the specific plans for an "Asian Asset Management Center" and how it will balance financial regulation with market openness. Currently, officials have confirmed that they will proceed in three major directions: internationalizing the financial market and enhancing capital market innovation, aiming to attract more international capital inflows and optimize the domestic asset management environment.
  
  However, regarding the specific implementation timeline, supporting measures for tax incentives, and how to specifically address the potential impact of international financial volatility on the Taiwan stock market, relevant departments have not yet released details. Market analysts point out that these details will be the key to whether the policy can be successfully implemented in the future, and further planning from the FSC and relevant financial ministries is still awaited.
  
  This policy will have a profound impact on Taiwan's industrial structure and financial ecosystem. First, if an Asian asset management center can be successfully established, it will help Taiwan's financial service industry transform from traditional deposit and lending businesses into a high-value-added wealth management and asset allocation center, thereby driving the upgrading of financial talent. Second, for listed companies, the internationalization of the capital market means they will have the opportunity to obtain broader international capital support, which is conducive to cross-border mergers and acquisitions or technological research and development. In addition, for long-term investors, the expansion of market scale and the internationalization of systems are expected to enhance the liquidity and transparency of the Taiwan stock market, further attracting long-term allocations from global institutional investors.
  
  Although the government has shown high ambition for this goal, outside observers have also raised relevant limitations and reminders. Some market observers believe that the degree of deregulation in Taiwan's financial laws, the flexibility of foreign exchange control policies, and the degree of alignment with international tax standards remain challenges that must be overcome in the process of promoting internationalization. Furthermore, global geopolitical risks and regional economic competition cannot be ignored; over-interpreting the short-term stimulus effect of a single policy on the market is inappropriate. The sound development of the financial market requires not only policy guidance but also long-term institutional construction and the support of market participants' confidence. Therefore, the manifestation of policy effects requires time to ferment, and should not be measured solely by short-term index fluctuations as the only indicator of success.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on whether the government will propose specific draft amendments to laws and regulations regarding the "Asian Asset Management Center" in the coming months, and how financial regulatory agencies will interface with international supervisory bodies. In addition, with the changes in the global AI industry supply chain, how Taiwan's financial market can assist the technology industry in hedging and capital scheduling through innovative financial products will be another indicator of Taiwan's financial resilience. All sectors will closely monitor the FSC's subsequent policy briefings and the progress of relevant financial peripheral units to assess changes in Taiwan's positioning in the Asian financial landscape.

# 2026臺灣週聚焦金融國際化：賴清德宣示打造亞洲資產管理中心

> 2026臺灣週於本月正式開幕，總統賴清德強調將透過金融市場國際化，並結合AI產業優勢，推動臺灣成為亞洲資產管理中心，強化國家整體經濟競爭力。


2026臺灣週於日前正式揭幕，這場年度金融盛會由金融監督管理委員會主導，並聯合證券期貨周邊單位共同舉辦。活動旨在向全球資本市場展示臺灣在金融產業與資產管理領域的發展動能，現場匯集了國際交易所領袖、全球資產管理機構代表、國外機構法人、上市櫃公司高層以及國內金融業者等超過400位貴賓。此次盛會不僅是臺灣金融業與國際接軌的重要平臺，更被視為檢視臺灣資本市場在後疫情時代及AI科技浪潮下，如何轉型並提升全球影響力的關鍵指標。
在開幕致詞中，總統賴清德明確指出，臺灣在當前全球AI科技浪潮中，憑藉強勁的產業實力展現了極佳的經濟韌性。然而，賴清德強調，國家競爭力的提升不能僅僅依賴硬體製造與科技產業，更需要一個穩健、開放且具備國際競爭力的金融市場作為堅實後盾。根據公開資料顯示，以國家市場類別總和計算，臺股目前已正式躍升為全球第四大股市，資本市場規模呈現持續擴大趨勢。
為延續此一成長動能，政府已訂定明確政策方向，將致力於推動金融市場國際化，並積極打造臺灣成為亞洲資產管理中心，同時持續提升資本市場的創新能力。
針對此次政策推動的細節，各界關注的焦點在於政府如何落實「亞洲資產管理中心」的具體規劃，以及如何平衡金融監管與市場開放之間的關係。目前，官方已確認將從金融市場國際化、提升資本市場創新等三大方向著手，旨在吸引更多國際資金流入，並最佳化國內資產管理環境。
然而，針對具體的執行時程表、稅務優惠政策的配套措施，以及如何具體應對國際金融波動對臺股的潛在衝擊，目前相關部門尚未公佈細節，市場分析師指出，這些細節將是未來政策能否順利落實的關鍵，後續仍有待金管會與相關財經部會進一步釋出規劃。
這項政策對臺灣的產業結構與金融生態將產生深遠影響。首先，若能成功打造亞洲資產管理中心，將有助於臺灣金融服務業從傳統的存款與放貸業務，轉型為高附加價值的財富管理與資產配置中心，進而帶動金融人才的升級。其次，對於上市櫃公司而言，資本市場的國際化意味著將有機會獲得更廣泛的國際資金支援，有利於企業進行跨國併購或技術研發。此外，對於長期投資人而言，市場規模的擴大與制度的國際化，預期將提升臺股的流動性與透明度，進一步吸引全球機構法人的長期配置。
儘管政府對此目標展現高度企圖心，但外界亦提出相關限制與提醒。部分市場觀察家認為，臺灣在金融法規的鬆綁程度、外匯管制政策的彈性，以及與國際稅務標準的接軌程度，仍是未來推動國際化過程中必須克服的挑戰。此外，全球地緣政治風險與區域經濟競爭亦不容忽視，過度解讀單一政策對市場的短期激勵效果並不妥當。金融市場的健全發展不僅需要政策引導，更需要長期的制度建設與市場參與者的信心支撐，因此，政策效果的顯現需要時間發酵，不應僅以短期的指數漲跌作為衡量成功的唯一指標。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於政府在未來數月內是否會針對「亞洲資產管理中心」提出具體的法規修正草案，以及金融監管機構如何與國際監理單位進行對接。此外，隨著全球AI產業供應鏈的變動，臺灣金融市場如何透過創新金融商品，協助科技產業進行避險與資金排程，將是觀察臺灣金融韌性的另一指標。各界將密切關注金管會後續的政策說明會，以及相關金融周邊單位的執行進度，以評估臺灣在亞洲金融版圖中的定位變化。
