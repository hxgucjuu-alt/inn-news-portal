  > Former U.S. President Donald Trump recently publicly demanded that Ukraine replace its president. This move has sparked significant international concern regarding the stability of U.S. diplomatic commitments and has prompted nations worldwide to reassess their risk management strategies within geopolitical and economic landscapes.
  
  The global geopolitical landscape has recently experienced significant turbulence. Former U.S. President Donald Trump made strong public remarks regarding the situation in Ukraine, arguing that Ukraine should elect a leader better capable of reaching an agreement with Russia. These comments not only directly targeted the political future of Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy but were also viewed as a signal of a potential major shift in U.S. foreign policy.
  
  According to a report by Yahoo Entertainment, U.S.-based professor Weng Li-chung pointed out that Trump’s move is redefining the global perception of "U.S. support" by linking American international commitments to realistic interests. Such changes have become a variable that cannot be ignored in international governance, especially for regions reliant on U.S. security guarantees, where interpreting such political signals has become a core subject of current diplomatic and strategic research.
  
  Through cross-media observation, the international community has resonated with the uncertainty surrounding U.S. foreign policy. Beyond the Ukraine issue, the South Korean ambassador to Kyiv was recalled to Seoul due to a dispute over prisoners of war with Ukraine, illustrating that in complex international conflicts, bilateral diplomatic relations are highly susceptible to geopolitical friction. A report by DW (Deutsche Welle) confirmed that the tension between South Korea and Ukraine has led to a cooling of diplomatic levels, reflecting the tug-of-war between sovereign interests and international alliances that countries face when addressing regional conflicts within the current global governance system.
  
  These events collectively outline a trend: under the current international framework, traditional security alliance relationships are under unprecedented pressure, and countries must seek more flexible strategic positioning in a volatile international environment.
  
  However, there remains significant disagreement regarding the interpretation and subsequent impact of Trump’s remarks. In media reports, some perspectives view this as a "pragmatic shift" in U.S. policy, suggesting that the U.S. is attempting to end long-term conflicts by adjusting leadership; another perspective, however, fears that this move will severely undermine the credibility of the U.S. as a leader of the global democratic camp. Furthermore, there is no consensus on whether these remarks represent the specific future policy direction of a potential U.S. administration. Professor Weng Li-chung’s analysis emphasizes that Taiwan should view this as a "major warning" and consider how to enhance its autonomous defense capabilities under the premise that U.S. interests come first.
  
  The differences in these viewpoints reflect the lack of a unified assessment model for the potential spillover effects of an "America First" policy across different regions.
  
  Such geopolitical shifts also have a direct impact on global industry and economic governance. The Asset Management Summit to be held by Cathay Financial Holdings on October 30 lists "geopolitics" and "tariff policies" as key factors influencing investment layouts. This demonstrates the financial market's high sensitivity to political risk, as investors attempt to find stable investment paradigms under a "new normal" amidst the turbulent international situation. When the remarks of political leaders are enough to shake international market confidence, enterprises and governments must place political stability at the highest weight in risk assessment when allocating cross-border resources; this may also have indirect chain reactions on international capital flows in emerging markets such as South America.
  
  Although discussions on geopolitical risks are becoming increasingly heated, analysts remind that one should not over-interpret the remarks of a single political figure. While Trump’s public stance is influential, the U.S. diplomatic decision-making system remains constrained by the long-term strategic planning of Congress, the military, and the executive branch. Directly linking individual events to regional security collapse may overlook the complex coordination mechanisms in international politics. Furthermore, the international community’s current solutions for the war in Ukraine remain at a stalemate; any assumptions regarding "replacing the president" or "reaching an agreement" are speculative in the absence of concrete diplomatic progress and should not be treated as imminent facts.

At the same time, the aftermath of the diplomatic dispute between South Korea and Ukraine, and how major global economies maintain financial market stability through multilateral mechanisms (such as the topics to be discussed at the upcoming international asset management summit) when facing geopolitical risks, will be important indicators for international governance in the coming months. Whether governments and international organizations will shift toward more diverse regional cooperation models when dealing with the uncertainty of "U.S. support" is also worth continuous tracking.

# 國際地緣政治變局：川普對烏政策與全球安全治理的連鎖效應

> 美國前總統川普近期公開要求烏克蘭更換總統，此舉引發國際社會對美國外交承諾穩定性的高度關注，並促使全球各國重新評估在地緣政治與經濟佈局中的風險管理策略。


全球地緣政治局勢近期出現顯著震盪，美國前總統川普針對烏克蘭局勢公開發表強硬言論，主張烏克蘭應選出一位更能與俄羅斯達成協議的領導人。此番言論不僅直接針對烏克蘭總統澤倫斯基的政治去留，更被視為美國外交政策可能發生重大轉向的訊號。
根據 Yahoo Entertainment 的報導，旅美教授翁履中指出，川普此舉正在重新定義全球對「美國支援」的認知，將美國的國際承諾與現實利益掛鉤，這類變動已成為國際治理中不可忽視的變數，特別是對於依賴美國安全保障的地區而言，如何解讀此類政治訊號已成為當前外交與戰略研究的核心課題。
在多方媒體的交叉觀察中，國際社會對於美國外交政策的不確定性已產生共鳴。除了烏克蘭議題，南韓駐基輔大使因與烏克蘭間的戰俘爭議撤回首爾，顯示出在複雜的國際衝突中，雙邊外交關係極易受到地緣政治摩擦的影響。DW（德國之聲）的報導證實，南韓與烏克蘭之間的緊張關係已導致外交層級的降溫，反映出當前全球治理體系在應對區域衝突時，各國在主權利益與國際聯盟之間的拉鋸。
這些事件共同勾勒出一個趨勢：在當前的國際架構下，傳統的安全同盟關係正承受前所未有的壓力，各國必須在多變的國際環境中尋求更具彈性的戰略定位。
然而，針對川普言論的解讀與後續影響，各界仍存在顯著分歧。媒體報導中，部分觀點將此視為美國政策的「務實轉向」，認為美國正試圖透過調整領導層來終結長期衝突；但另一派觀點則擔憂，此舉將嚴重削弱美國作為全球民主陣營領導者的公信力。此外，關於川普此言論是否代表未來美國政府的具體施政方針，目前尚無定論。翁履中教授的分析強調，臺灣應將此視為「最大警訊」，思考在美國利益優先的前提下，如何提升自主防衛能力。
這些觀點的差異，反映出國際社會對於「美國優先」政策在不同區域可能產生的外溢效應，仍缺乏統一的評估模型。
此類地緣政治的變動對全球產業與經濟治理亦產生直接影響。國泰金控將於 10 月 30 日舉辦的資產管理高峰會，便將「地緣政治」與「關稅政策」列為影響投資佈局的關鍵因子。這顯示出金融市場對於政治風險的高度敏感，投資者正試圖在動盪的國際局勢中，尋找「新常態」下的穩健投資典範。當政治領袖的言論足以動搖國際市場信心，企業與政府在進行跨國資源配置時，必須將政治穩定性納入風險評估的最高權重，這對於南美洲等新興市場的國際資本流動，亦可能產生間接的連鎖效應。
儘管各界對地緣政治風險的討論日益熱烈，但分析人士提醒，不應過度解讀單一政治人物的言論。川普的公開表態雖具影響力，但美國的外交決策體系仍受制於國會、軍方及行政部門的長期戰略規劃。將個別事件直接連結至區域安全崩潰，可能會忽略國際政治中複雜的協調機制。此外，目前國際社會對於烏克蘭戰事的解決方案仍處於僵局，任何關於「更換總統」或「達成協議」的假設，在缺乏具體外交談判進展前，均屬於推測性質，不應被視為即將發生的事實。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於美國大選後的實際政策走向，以及烏克蘭在面對國際壓力下的戰略調整。同時，南韓與烏克蘭的外交爭議後續，以及全球主要經濟體在面對地緣政治風險時，如何透過多邊機制（如即將召開的國際資產管理高峰會所探討的議題）來維持金融市場的穩定，將是未來數月國際治理的重要觀察指標。各國政府與國際組織在應對「美國支援」的不確定性時，是否會轉向更為多元的區域合作模式，亦值得持續追蹤。
