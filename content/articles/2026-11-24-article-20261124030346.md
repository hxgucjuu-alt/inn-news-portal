  # U.S. Monetary Policy Sparks Global Economic Concerns: Trump Criticizes the Fed as Markets Call for Higher Inflation Targets
  
  > U.S. President Trump has reiterated that the U.S. should have the lowest interest rates in the world and criticized the Federal Reserve's decision-making as politically motivated. Meanwhile, hedge fund manager Bill Ackman has warned that if the Fed continues to raise interest rates, it could push the global economy into a recession.
  
  U.S. monetary policy has recently become the focus of global financial markets. With U.S. President Trump publicly criticizing the Federal Reserve's (Fed) interest rate policy and growing market concerns that monetary tightening could trigger a recession, the stability of global economic governance has once again come under widespread scrutiny. On November 22, 2026, Trump reiterated that the United States should have the lowest interest rates in the world, pointing out that the Fed's current decision-making process is politically colored and does not truly align with the actual needs of the U.S. economy.
  
  At the same time, Bill Ackman, founder of the hedge fund Pershing Square, questioned the Fed's inflation target on the same day, arguing that the current 2% goal is outdated. He strongly warned that if the Fed further raises interest rates at its December 9 policy meeting, it could lead to a global economic recession.
  
  According to various media reports, there is a significant divergence between the Fed's current monetary policy stance and that of the White House. In his remarks, Trump made it clear that while he was not personally attacking current Fed Chair Kevin Warsh, he believes there is structural political interference in the Fed's monetary policy stance, resulting in interest rates that are too high to effectively support U.S. economic development. This position stands in stark contrast to the hawkish signals recently released by Fed officials, some of whom believe that further adjustments to the current policy rate are necessary to effectively curb inflation. This has once again made the independence of central bank decision-making a core issue for international financial markets and political observers.
  
  Regarding information integration and cross-referencing, there is a clear divide over whether the Fed should continue to raise interest rates. Citing the debt financing rate recently obtained by SpaceX, Ackman pointed out that the company's secured debt rate has reached as high as 8.259%, far exceeding previous financing levels, and emphasized that the recent slump in the stock market has significantly pushed up the cost of equity financing for companies. Ackman argues that the Fed should raise its inflation target to 3% to align with the current economic environment and avoid a broader economic crisis caused by policy errors at a time when financial conditions have already tightened significantly.
  
  However, hawkish officials within the Federal Reserve tend to favor maintaining a tightening policy to ensure inflation returns to the target range. This divergence in policy paths constitutes a primary source of uncertainty in the current global financial market.
  
  Such drastic shifts in monetary policy have profound implications for the global economy, particularly for emerging markets such as those in South America. Economies in the Andes and Southern Cone regions have long been highly dependent on international capital flows and U.S. dollar interest rate levels. If the U.S. Federal Reserve continues to maintain high interest rates, it will lead to a sharp rise in financing costs for emerging markets, exacerbate debt pressures on various countries, and potentially trigger capital flight, thereby affecting infrastructure investment and industrial development in South American nations. Furthermore, if U.S. policy errors lead to a global recession, South America, as a major hub for raw material exports, could face the dual blow of shrinking export demand and currency depreciation, posing severe challenges to public policy and social stability within the region.
  
  Although the market is highly sensitive to the Fed's policy direction, analysts caution against over-interpreting Trump's political rhetoric and the views of individual investors. The Fed's decision-making mechanism is designed to maintain a degree of independence to navigate long-term economic cycles, rather than simply responding to short-term political pressure or the demands of specific market participants. Currently, whether the Fed will adjust its inflation target or how it will strike a balance between "curbing inflation" and "maintaining economic growth" remains to be seen following the official announcement after the December 9 meeting. Market participants should distinguish between political rhetoric and economic data when interpreting relevant information to avoid making overly pessimistic predictions about the prospects of global economic governance based on a single perspective.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on several key areas: first, whether there will be any softening in the stance of Fed officials in their public remarks before the December meeting; second, whether domestic U.S. inflation data will force the Fed to reassess its policy path; and finally, whether regions dependent on foreign capital, such as South America, will take preemptive monetary policy adjustments or capital control measures in response to changes in U.S. interest rate policy to cope with potential financial shocks. As uncertainty in the global financial environment increases, the need for international coordination on monetary policy will become increasingly urgent, and the subsequent actions of central banks and international governance institutions will be key indicators for observing whether the global economy can achieve a soft landing.

# 美國貨幣政策引發全球經濟隱憂：川普抨擊聯準會與市場籲調高通膨目標

> 美國總統川普重申美國利率應為全球最低，並批評聯準會決策具政治色彩；同時，避險基金經理人阿克曼警告，若聯準會持續升息，恐導致全球經濟陷入衰退。


美國貨幣政策近期成為全球金融市場焦點，隨著美國總統川普公開抨擊聯準會（Fed）的利率政策，以及市場對於貨幣緊縮可能引發衰退的擔憂加劇，全球經濟治理的穩定性再次受到廣泛關注。川普於2026年11月22日重申，美國理應擁有全球最低的利率水準，並直指聯準會目前的決策過程帶有政治色彩，未能真正符合美國經濟的實際需求。
與此同時，避險基金潘興廣場資本（Pershing Square）創辦人比爾‧阿克曼（Bill Ackman）則於同日對聯準會的通膨目標提出質疑，認為現行的2%目標已不合時宜，並強烈警告若聯準會於12月9日的利率決策會議中進一步升息，將可能導致全球經濟陷入衰退。
根據多家媒體報導，聯準會目前的貨幣政策立場與白宮方面存在顯著分歧。川普在談話中明確表示，雖然他並非直接針對現任聯準會主席華許（Kevin Warsh）進行個人抨擊，但他認為聯準會的貨幣政策立場存在結構性的政治幹預，導致利率水準過高，無法有效支援美國的經濟發展。這一立場與近期聯準會官員接連釋出的鷹派訊號形成強烈對比，部分聯準會官員認為，為了有效抑制通膨，現行的政策利率仍有進一步調整的必要，這使得央行決策的獨立性再次成為國際金融市場與政治觀察家關注的核心議題。
在資訊整合與交叉比對方面，各界對於聯準會是否應繼續升息存在明顯分歧。阿克曼以SpaceX近期取得的債務融資利率為例，指出該公司擔保債務利率已高達8.259%，遠高於過去的融資水準，並強調近期股市的重挫已顯著推高企業的權益融資成本。阿克曼主張，聯準會應將通膨目標調高至3%，以符合當前的經濟環境，並避免在金融條件已經大幅收緊的情況下，因政策失誤而引發更廣泛的經濟危機。
然而，聯準會內部的鷹派官員則傾向於維持緊縮政策以確保通膨回落至目標區間，這種政策路徑的差異，構成了當前全球金融市場不確定性的主要來源。
此類貨幣政策的劇烈變動對全球經濟，特別是南美等新興市場地區具有深遠影響。南美各國在安地斯山脈與南錐體區域的經濟體，長期以來高度依賴國際資本流動與美元利率水準。若美國聯準會持續維持高利率，將導致新興市場的融資成本大幅上升，加重各國的債務壓力，並可能引發資本外流，進而影響南美各國的基礎建設投資與產業發展。此外，若美國政策失誤導致全球經濟衰退，作為原物料出口重鎮的南美地區，恐將面臨出口需求萎縮與貨幣貶值的雙重打擊，對區域內的公共政策與社會穩定構成嚴峻挑戰。
儘管市場對聯準會的政策走向高度敏感，但分析人士提醒，不應過度解讀川普的政治言論與個別投資人的觀點。聯準會的決策機制設計旨在維持一定程度的獨立性，以應對長期的經濟週期，而非單純回應短期政治壓力或特定市場參與者的訴求。目前，聯準會是否會調整其通膨目標，或是如何在「抑制通膨」與「維持經濟成長」之間取得平衡，仍需觀察12月9日會議後的正式宣告。市場參與者在解讀相關資訊時，應區分政治修辭與經濟資料之間的落差，避免因單一觀點而對全球經濟治理的前景做出過於悲觀的預判。
後續觀察重點將集中於以下幾個面向：首先，聯準會官員在12月會議前的公開發言是否會出現立場鬆動；其次，美國國內的通膨資料是否會迫使聯準會重新評估其政策路徑；最後，南美等依賴外資的地區，是否會針對美國利率政策的變動，提前採取貨幣政策調整或資本管制措施以應對潛在的金融衝擊。隨著全球金融環境的不確定性增加，國際間對於貨幣政策協調的需求將日益迫切，各國央行與國際治理機構的後續動作，將是觀察全球經濟能否軟著陸的關鍵指標。
