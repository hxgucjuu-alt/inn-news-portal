根據《告示牌》（Billboard）與外媒報導，流行歌手泰勒絲（Taylor Swift）收錄於豪華版專輯《The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore》的新單曲《Patient Zero》，在告示牌百大單曲榜（Hot 100）空降奪冠。這首歌曲成為泰勒絲生涯第16首百大單曲榜冠軍作品。

這項紀錄使泰勒絲在告示牌單曲榜歷史奪冠總數上名列第三，僅次於擁有20首冠軍單曲的披頭四樂團（The Beatles）以及擁有19首的瑪麗亞凱莉（Mariah Carey）。此外，報導指出《Patient Zero》也是她第10首直接空降冠軍的單曲，追平了德瑞克（Drake）所保持的紀錄。

《Patient Zero》的登頂終結了艾拉·蘭利（Ella Langley）單曲《Choosin' Texas》的榜首紀錄，後者降至第二位。業界報導亦指出，隨著豪華版發行，專輯內歌曲在當期單曲榜前五名中佔據四席，而《The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore》也同時重回告示牌200大專輯榜（Billboard 200）榜首。

## English edition

Pop artist Taylor Swift's track 'Patient Zero,' featured on her deluxe album 'The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore,' debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 chart. The achievement marks her 16th chart-topping single on the Hot 100, according to reports from Billboard and entertainment outlets.

With this milestone, Swift holds the third-most No. 1 singles in Hot 100 history, trailing only The Beatles with 20 and Mariah Carey with 19. Media reports also noted that 'Patient Zero' is Swift's 10th song to debut directly at the top spot, tying rapper Drake for the record.

The single's arrival displaced Ella Langley's 'Choosin' Texas' from the peak position, pushing it to No. 2. Music coverage further highlighted that tracks from the deluxe release occupied four of the top five positions on the Hot 100, while 'The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore' concurrently returned to No. 1 on the Billboard 200 albums chart.
