根據《紐約時報》報導，美國前總統川普在其第二任期內，已決定於今年十二月在邁阿密的私人高爾夫俱樂部舉辦二十國集團（G20）年度峰會。此一決定引發了外界對政治道德界線變化的關注，特別是與他第一任期內類似嘗試的對比。

資料顯示，川普在第一任期時曾計劃在同一地點舉辦七國集團（G7）峰會。當時，這種「自我交易」的行為引發了共和黨內部成員的強烈反對，導致該計劃最終被迫取消。這一事件當時被視為對既有政治規範的嚴重挑戰。

然而，七年後，當川普在第二任期再次提出在私人俱樂部舉辦G20峰會的計劃時，報導指出，這次的安排幾乎沒有引起任何抗議。這種態度的轉變，被多個消息來源解讀為政治環境中道德標準和預期已發生顯著變化的跡象。

評論指出，此類事件是川普及其家族和友人自他重返總統職位以來「前所未有的自我致富攻勢」的一部分。批評者進一步指出，川普在第二任期內已累積了高達22億美元的財富，這使得關於公職人員利用職位謀取私利的討論再次浮上檯面。

## English edition

According to The New York Times, former U.S. President Trump has decided to host the annual Group of 20 (G20) summit at his private golf club in Miami this December during his second term. This decision has drawn attention to the shifting landscape of political ethical lines, particularly when compared to a similar attempt made during his first term.

Data indicates that during his first term, Trump had planned to host the Group of 7 (G7) summit at the same location. At that time, this "act of self-dealing" provoked a significant uproar among fellow Republicans, ultimately forcing him to scrap the idea. The incident was then seen as a serious challenge to established political norms.

However, seven years later, as Trump revives the plan to host the G20 summit at his private club during his second term, reports indicate that this arrangement has generated practically no protest. This shift in reaction is interpreted by multiple sources as a sign of significant changes in ethical standards and expectations within the political landscape.

Commentators suggest that such incidents are part of an "unprecedented blitz of self-enrichment" by Trump, his family, and friends since his return to the presidency. Critics further point to an alleged $2.2 billion in second-term enrichment, bringing discussions about public officials using their positions for private gain back to the forefront.
