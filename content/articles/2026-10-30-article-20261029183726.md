  # EU Trade Policy and the Energy Landscape: Trump’s Tariff Threats and Russian Energy Negotiation Trends
  
  > The European Union is simultaneously facing tariff challenges from U.S. trade protectionism and pressure from Russia, which is attempting to resume gas exports to Europe through energy deals. EU trade official Maros Sefcovic has emphasized addressing industrial overcapacity, while negotiations between the U.S. and Russia regarding the restart of the Nord Stream pipeline have sparked concerns over European energy security.
  
  Europe’s current political and economic stability is facing a dual test from both internal and external fronts. Internally, the EU continues to strengthen digital governance and modern slavery regulations to ensure fair market competition; externally, it must navigate the threat of tariffs stemming from the Trump administration's trade protectionism while addressing industrial overcapacity. Meanwhile, subtle shifts in international geopolitics have emerged, with the U.S. and Russia recently engaging in contact regarding energy transactions. This includes the potential restart of the Nord Stream natural gas pipeline, a move that directly touches a sensitive nerve in EU energy policy and has raised high levels of vigilance among Northern and Eastern European member states.
  
  According to reports from Deutsche Welle (DW) and Liveuamap, EU Trade Commissioner Maros Sefcovic recently stated publicly that the EU is willing to cooperate with the U.S. to jointly address the structural issue of global industrial overcapacity. This stance is viewed as a strategy by the EU to seek a diplomatic buffer under the pressure of trade investigations by the Trump administration. At the same time, Liveuamap noted that U.S. and Russian officials have begun discussions on a series of energy and commercial agreements, covering topics such as Russian diesel sales, the disposal of Lukoil’s overseas assets, and the possibility of restarting the Nord Stream natural gas pipeline.
  
  Such negotiations are taking place against the backdrop of a lengthy phone call between Russian President Vladimir Putin and the Finnish leadership, indicating that energy negotiations have become a significant avenue for Russia to attempt to bypass European sanctions.
  
  However, there remain significant discrepancies and uncertainties regarding the details of these energy negotiations. While U.S. officials have disclosed a list of items under discussion, EU officials have not confirmed participation in or agreement to any deal regarding the restart of the Nord Stream pipeline. In fact, the Nord Stream pipeline has become a core symbol of European energy security and sanctions policy throughout the geopolitical conflicts of the past few years. Source information indicates that there is currently a lack of public official documentation to confirm whether bilateral consultations between the U.S. and Russia will bypass EU decision-making mechanisms, or whether these agreements would conflict with the EU’s existing energy transition policies. Furthermore, data from various parties regarding the specific scale of Russian diesel sales and their impact on the European market has yet to reach a consensus and remains subject to further observation.
  
  These geopolitical and trade shifts have a direct impact on European industries and society. First, if the U.S. implements trade tariffs against the EU, it will severely impact Europe’s manufacturing and export-oriented economies, particularly the automotive and steel industries. Second, the volatility of energy policy not only affects the energy expenditures of European households but also poses a challenge to the green transition promoted by the EU. Digital platforms such as Agoda are also subject to strict regulation under the Digital Services Act while operating within the EU, demonstrating that the EU continues to insist on using legal frameworks to maintain digital sovereignty and consumer rights in the single market when facing external trade pressure.
  
  There is a clear gap in perspectives among the various parties. Some U.S. officials tend to favor easing energy supply pressures through commercial negotiations, but within the EU, particularly among Northern and Eastern European member states, there is deep skepticism toward any form of "energy compromise." Some analysts believe that over-interpreting the progress of U.S.-Russia negotiations could lead to market panic, as the EU has already established a relatively diversified energy supply chain, and its dependence on Russian natural gas has been significantly reduced compared to pre-war levels. Therefore, viewing a single instance of diplomatic contact as a shift in energy policy may overlook the defensive mechanisms the EU currently has in place at the legal and diplomatic levels.
  
  Future observation will focus on three key areas: first, the results of the U.S. trade investigation into EU industrial capacity and whether the EU will adopt retaliatory tariff measures; second, the European Commission’s formal response to the Russian energy restart negotiations, particularly whether its stance on the Nord Stream pipeline has softened; and third, how Nordic countries coordinate their internal security policies with the EU’s overall energy strategy when facing geopolitical pressure. While major events like the UEFA European Championship bring cultural cohesion to Europe, the EU must still seek a new balance between economic autonomy and geopolitical security in the face of complex international governance challenges.

# 歐盟貿易政策與能源局勢：川普關稅威脅與俄國能源談判動向

> 歐盟正同時面臨美國貿易保護主義的關稅挑戰，以及俄羅斯試圖透過能源交易重啟對歐輸氣的壓力。歐盟貿易官員謝夫喬維奇強調將應對產能過剩，而美俄間關於北溪管道重啟的談判則引發歐洲能源安全疑慮。


歐洲當前的政治與經濟穩定正面臨內外雙重考驗。對內，歐盟持續強化數位治理與現代奴役法規，以確保市場公平競爭；對外，則需在川普政府的貿易保護主義下，處理工業產能過剩帶來的關稅威脅。與此同時，國際地緣政治出現微妙變化，美俄雙方近期針對能源交易進行接觸，其中涉及北溪天然氣管道（Nord Stream）重啟的可能性，此舉直接觸動歐盟能源政策的敏感神經，並引起北歐及東歐成員國的高度警覺。
根據德國之聲（DW）與 Liveuamap 的報導，歐盟貿易執委謝夫喬維奇（Maros Sefcovic）近期公開表示，歐盟願意與美國合作，共同解決全球工業產能過剩的結構性問題。這一表態被視為歐盟試圖在川普政府的貿易調查壓力下，尋求外交緩衝的策略。與此同時，Liveuamap 指出，美俄官員已就一系列能源及商業協議展開討論，內容涵蓋俄國柴油銷售、盧克石油（Lukoil）海外資產處置，以及重啟北溪天然氣管道輸歐的可能性。
此類談判發生在俄羅斯總統普丁與芬蘭領導人進行長時間通話的背景之下，顯示能源談判已成為俄國試圖突破歐洲制裁的重要途徑。
然而，目前關於能源談判的細節仍存在顯著差異與不確定性。雖然美方官員透露了談判清單，但歐盟官方並未證實已參與或同意任何關於北溪管道重啟的協議。事實上，北溪管道在過去幾年的地緣衝突中已成為歐洲能源安全與制裁政策的核心象徵。來源資訊顯示，美國與俄羅斯之間的雙邊磋商是否會繞過歐盟決策機制，以及這些協議是否會與歐盟現行的能源轉型政策產生衝突，目前仍缺乏公開的官方檔案佐證。此外，關於俄國柴油銷售的具體規模與對歐市場的影響，各方資料尚未達成一致，仍待後續觀察。
這些地緣政治與貿易變動，對歐洲產業與社會帶來直接影響。首先，若美國落實針對歐盟的貿易關稅，將嚴重衝擊歐洲的製造業與出口導向經濟，特別是汽車與鋼鐵產業。其次，能源政策的搖擺不定，不僅影響歐洲家庭的能源支出，也對歐盟推動的綠色轉型構成挑戰。Agoda 等數位平臺在歐盟境內運作時，亦受到數位服務法（Digital Services Act）的嚴格監管，顯示歐盟在面對外部貿易壓力時，仍堅持透過法制化手段維護單一市場的數位主權與消費者權益。
各方觀點存在明顯落差。美方部分官員傾向透過商業談判緩解能源供應壓力，但歐盟內部，特別是北歐與東歐成員國，對任何形式的「能源妥協」持高度懷疑態度。部分分析認為，過度解讀美俄談判的進展可能導致市場恐慌，因為歐盟目前已建立相對多元的能源供應鏈，對俄國天然氣的依賴程度已較戰前大幅降低。因此，將單一的談判接觸視為能源政策的轉向，可能忽略了歐盟目前在法律與外交層面的防禦機制。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個面向：第一，美國針對歐盟工業產能的貿易調查結果，以及歐盟是否會採取報復性關稅措施；第二，歐盟執委會針對俄國能源重啟談判的正式回應，特別是針對北溪管道的態度是否出現鬆動；第三，北歐國家在面對地緣政治壓力時，如何協調內部安全政策與歐盟整體的能源戰略。歐洲足球錦標賽等大型賽事雖為歐洲帶來文化凝聚力，但面對複雜的國際治理挑戰，歐盟仍需在經濟自主與地緣安全之間尋求新的平衡點。
