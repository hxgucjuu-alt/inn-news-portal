  # Challenges in Technology and Energy Security: Wi-Fi Vulnerabilities, Shifts in Hacker Communities, and Northeast Asian Energy Supply Risks
  
  > Northeast Asia is currently facing multiple challenges, including privacy concerns arising from the misuse of Wi-Fi beamforming technology, the disintegration of the PS5 hacker community due to the intervention of AI tools, and sharp fluctuations in regional Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) prices caused by the war in Iran.
  
  Northeast Asia has recently encountered significant challenges in information security and energy supply chains. In the field of information security, a German research team revealed an attack technique dubbed "BFId" at the ACM Conference on Computer and Communications Security (ACM CCS) held in Taipei in October 2026. This technique exploits feedback information from Wi-Fi beamforming protocols to precisely identify individuals without the need for cameras, sparking public concern regarding wireless communication privacy.
  
  Simultaneously, major changes have occurred in the field of game console security. Legendary hacker Andy Nguyen (TheFlow), who previously developed cracking tools for the PS Vita, officially announced his withdrawal from the PS5 Linux kernel development project on September 16, 2026, and halted the originally planned support for the PS5 Pro in 2027.
  
  Furthermore, influenced by the war in Iran, the Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) supply chain in Northeast Asia has shown signs of instability. The ICIS East Asia Index (EAX) and TTF prices fluctuated violently within two weeks, breaking the $20 per million British thermal units (MMBtu) mark, reaching a new high since the winter of 2022–2023.
  
  Multiple sources have confirmed the development of these events. Regarding Wi-Fi privacy and security, research indicates that by analyzing radio wave disturbances in beamforming feedback information, attackers can establish unique "radio fingerprints," making identity recognition possible without detection; this finding has been verified in the international cybersecurity academic community. Regarding the hacker community, Andy Nguyen’s withdrawal announcement explicitly pointed out that low-quality code generated by Large Language Models (LLMs) is severely polluting the technical community. Furthermore, novices are using AI tools to discover vulnerabilities and privately claim bounties from vendors, leading to the collapse of a development environment previously based on trust and technical exchange.
  
  In terms of energy, ICIS data shows that the Northeast Asian energy market is extremely sensitive to geopolitics. Supply chain concerns triggered by the war in Iran have directly led to a surge in natural gas prices, forcing countries in the region to re-examine their energy contingency plans.
  
  However, there are still discrepancies in details and information awaiting confirmation across various reports. Regarding the Wi-Fi vulnerability, although the research team has proven its theoretical feasibility, the threshold for large-scale application in real-world environments and the corresponding defense mechanisms are still under discussion. Regarding the shifts in the hacker community, while Andy Nguyen pointed the finger at the misuse and commercialization of LLMs, it remains to be seen whether this will lead to a total shutdown of other mainstream hacker projects. Some community participants still hold differing views on the definition of "hacker spirit" and the impact of AI intervention.
  
  Furthermore, whether the volatility in energy prices will evolve into a long-term supply crisis depends on the subsequent development of the war in Iran and the strategic reserve response capabilities of various Northeast Asian countries. Market analysis currently remains in a state of high uncertainty.
  
  These events have had concrete impacts on the industries and society of Northeast Asia. The exposure of the Wi-Fi vulnerability may force the communications industry to accelerate the updating of wireless protocol standards to guard against radio fingerprint tracking. The suspension of the PS5 Linux development project demonstrates the double-edged sword effect of AI tools in software development, which has not only impacted the collaborative model of the open-source community but also affected the progress of game console reverse engineering and security research.
  
  The surge in energy prices has directly increased the economic pressure on Northeast Asian countries, forcing governments to seek a balance between energy security and inflation control. This has also prompted increased cooperation among cities in the region; for instance, the experience sharing and crisis response mechanisms previously emphasized by the Northeast Asia Mayors Forum appear even more critical under the current situation.
  
  There are divergent views across various sectors regarding the aforementioned events. Supporters believe that the hacker community should adapt to the changes of the AI era, while opponents worry that lowering the technical threshold will lead to the collapse of cybersecurity defense systems. Regarding the Wi-Fi privacy threat, some experts remind the public not to panic excessively, as the attack technique still requires specific hardware and environmental conditions to function and is not a ubiquitous threat. Similarly, regarding the volatility in the energy market, analysts point out that while the price surge reflects geopolitical anxiety, the market still possesses a certain degree of adjustment capability in the short term, and price fluctuations should not be directly equated with a conclusion of energy shortages.
  
  Key points for follow-up observation include whether the academic community can propose effective software patches for the Wi-Fi beamforming vulnerability, and whether cybersecurity regulations will be enacted to govern radio fingerprint technology. In the hacker community, it remains to be seen whether new leaders or organizations can establish new trust mechanisms to fill the technical vacuum left by Andy Nguyen’s departure. Regarding energy, the emergency reserve policies of Northeast Asian countries in response to the situation in Iran, as well as subsequent changes in natural gas procurement costs, will be key indicators determining regional economic stability. Additionally, it is worth continuing to monitor whether transnational city cooperation forums will include energy security and digital privacy as discussion topics.

# 科技與能源安全挑戰：Wi-Fi 漏洞、駭客社群變遷及東北亞能源供應風險

> 近期東北亞面臨多重挑戰，包括 Wi-Fi 波束成形技術遭濫用引發隱私疑慮、PS5 駭客社群因 AI 工具介入而瓦解，以及伊朗戰爭導致區域液化天然氣（LNG）價格劇烈波動。


近期東北亞地區在科技安全與能源供應鏈上遭遇顯著挑戰。在資訊安全領域，德國研究團隊於 2026 年 10 月在臺北舉辦的 ACM 計算機與通訊安全會議（ACM CCS）中，揭露了一項名為「BFId」的攻擊技術，該技術能利用 Wi-Fi 協議中的波束成形（Beamforming）反饋資訊，在無須攝影機的情況下精準識別個人身分，引發大眾對無線通訊隱私的擔憂。
同時，遊戲主機安全領域亦發生重大變動，曾開發 PS Vita 破解工具的傳奇駭客 Andy Nguyen（TheFlow）於 2026 年 9 月 16 日正式宣佈退出 PS5 Linux 核心開發專案，並中止原定 2027 年針對 PS5 Pro 的支援計畫。
此外，受伊朗戰爭影響，東北亞地區的液化天然氣供應鏈出現不穩定跡象，ICIS 東亞指數（EAX）與 TTF 價格在兩週內劇烈震盪，突破每百萬英熱單位（MMBtu）20 美元大關，創下 2022 至 2023 年冬季以來的新高。
多個來源共同確認了上述事件的發展。關於 Wi-Fi 隱私安全，研究指出攻擊者透過分析波束成形反饋資訊中的電波擾動，能建立獨特的「無線電指紋」，使身分辨識在無形中成為可能，這項發現已在國際資安學術界獲得證實。在駭客社群方面，Andy Nguyen 的退出宣告明確指出，大型語言模型（LLM）產出的劣質程式碼正嚴重汙染技術社群，且有新手利用 AI 工具挖掘漏洞後，私自向廠商領取賞金，導致原本基於信任與技術交流的開發環境崩解。
能源方面，ICIS 的資料顯示，東北亞能源市場對地緣政治極為敏感，伊朗戰爭引發的供應鏈擔憂，直接導致天然氣價格飆升，迫使區域內國家重新審視能源應急計畫。
然而，各來源報導中仍存在細節差異與待確認資訊。針對 Wi-Fi 漏洞，研究團隊雖證明瞭理論上的可行性，但該技術在現實環境中大規模應用的門檻與防禦機制尚在討論階段。在駭客社群變遷的部分，雖然 Andy Nguyen 將矛頭指向 LLM 的濫用與商業化行為，但此舉是否會導致其他主流駭客專案全面停擺，目前尚無定論，部分社群參與者對「駭客精神」的定義與 AI 介入的影響仍有不同看法。
此外，能源價格的波動是否會演變成長期的供應危機，取決於伊朗戰爭的後續發展及東北亞各國的戰略儲備因應能力，目前市場分析仍處於高度不確定狀態。
這些事件對東北亞的產業與社會產生了具體影響。Wi-Fi 漏洞的曝光可能迫使通訊產業加速更新無線協議標準，以防範無線電指紋追蹤。PS5 Linux 開發專案的停擺，則顯示了 AI 工具在軟體開發領域帶來的雙面刃效應，不僅衝擊了開源社群的協作模式，也影響了遊戲主機逆向工程與安全研究的程式。
能源價格的飆升則直接加重了東北亞各國的經濟壓力，迫使政府在能源安全與通膨控制之間尋求平衡，這也促使區域內城市間加強合作，例如過去東北亞市長論壇曾強調的城市間經驗分享與危機應對機制，在當前局勢下顯得更為關鍵。
各界對於上述事件的觀點存在分歧。支持者認為駭客社群應適應 AI 時代的變革，反對者則擔憂技術門檻降低將導致資安防禦體系崩潰。對於 Wi-Fi 隱私威脅，部分專家提醒大眾不應過度恐慌，因為該攻擊技術仍需特定的硬體與環境條件配合，並非隨處可見的威脅。同樣地，針對能源市場的波動，分析師指出價格飆升雖反映了地緣政治焦慮，但短期內市場仍具備一定的調節能力，不應將價格波動直接等同於能源短缺的定論。
後續觀察重點在於，學術界能否針對 Wi-Fi 波束成形漏洞提出有效的軟體修補方案，以及資安法規是否會針對無線電指紋技術進行規範。在駭客社群方面，需觀察是否有新的領軍人物或組織能建立新的信任機制，以填補 Andy Nguyen 退出後留下的技術真空。能源方面，東北亞各國針對伊朗局勢的應急儲備政策，以及後續的天然氣採購成本變化，將是決定區域經濟穩定性的關鍵指標。此外，跨國城市間的合作論壇是否會將能源安全與數位隱私納入討論議題，亦值得持續關注。
