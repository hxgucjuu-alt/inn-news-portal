遊戲發行商 Infini Fun 與開發團隊 Raven Studio 近期對外揭露最新 PC 新作《Cats in the Shell》相關資訊。這款融合模組化機甲建造、Roguelike 機制與自動戰鬥特色的遊戲，已公開首部宣傳影像，並於 Steam 平台開放玩家加入願望清單。

資料顯示，該作品設定由貓咪擔任大型機械戰士的駕駛員。玩家在遊戲中能指揮並組裝具備模組化升級特性的客製化戰鬥機甲，將策略組裝與自動化戰鬥流程相互結合，展開一系列機甲戰鬥對決。

根據發布消息，Raven Studio 的研發成員包含來自騰訊（Tencent）與網易（NetEase）的業界資深從業者。該作預計於 2027 年 1 月 20 日以搶先體驗（Early Access）形式登上 PC Steam 平台，並在正式發行前於 Steam Next Fest 提供試玩版本供玩家體驗。

## English edition

Game publisher Infini Fun and development team Raven Studio have revealed details regarding their upcoming PC title, "Cats in the Shell." Featuring a blend of modular mech construction, roguelike mechanics, and auto-battler systems, the project was officially announced alongside a debut trailer, and its Steam store page is currently open for wishlisting.

According to available reports, the game features a unique premise where cats serve as pilots for heavy combat war machines. Players are tasked with designing and commanding customizable mechs, utilizing modular components and upgrades to optimize automated combat encounters.

Press materials state that Raven Studio is comprised of industry veterans from Tencent and NetEase. "Cats in the Shell" is slated to arrive on PC via Steam Early Access on January 20, 2027, with a demo made accessible during the Steam Next Fest ahead of the official early access launch.
