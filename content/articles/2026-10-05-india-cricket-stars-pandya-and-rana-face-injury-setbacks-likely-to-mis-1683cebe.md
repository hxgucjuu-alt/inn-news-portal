印度板球界兩位重要球員哈迪克·潘迪亞（Hardik Pandya）和哈爾希特·拉納（Harshit Rana）的傷勢狀況近日引發關注。根據多方報導，兩位明星球員的傷勢出現新的挫折，導致他們重返競技板球賽場的時間將會延遲。資料顯示，這對印度雙人組很可能因此缺席即將到來的紐西蘭系列賽。

報導指出，全能型選手哈迪克·潘迪亞的傷勢遭遇了「新的挫折」，進一步推遲了他的復出。資料顯示，潘迪亞上一次代表印度國家隊出戰國際賽事是在今年三月對陣紐西蘭的比賽中。此次傷勢復發對他的職業生涯無疑是一大打擊，也讓球迷們對他的歸期充滿擔憂。

與此同時，哈爾希特·拉納的情況也同樣不樂觀。資料顯示，拉納「不太可能」參加對陣紐西蘭的系列賽。他與潘迪亞一樣，已經缺席賽場一段時間，此次傷勢更新意味著他將繼續在場邊休養。

目前，潘迪亞和拉納都在板球管理委員會的監督下進行康復治療。儘管他們正積極努力恢復，但最新的傷勢發展表明，他們需要更多時間才能完全康復並重返高強度比賽。這對印度隊在即將到來的國際賽事中無疑是一個挑戰。

## English edition

The injury status of two key Indian cricket players, Hardik Pandya and Harshit Rana, has recently drawn significant attention. According to multiple reports, both star players have experienced new setbacks in their recovery, which will delay their return to competitive cricket. Data suggests that this Indian duo is likely to miss the upcoming New Zealand series.

Reports indicate that all-rounder Hardik Pandya has suffered "another setback" in his injury recovery, further postponing his comeback. Data shows that Pandya last played an international match for India against New Zealand in March earlier this year. This latest injury recurrence is undoubtedly a blow to his career and raises concerns among fans about his return date.

Concurrently, Harshit Rana's situation is similarly concerning. Data shows that Rana is "unlikely" to feature in the series against New Zealand. Like Pandya, he has been out of action for some time, and this injury update means he will continue his recovery on the sidelines.

Currently, both Pandya and Rana are undergoing rehabilitation under the supervision of the cricket board. While they are actively working towards recovery, the latest developments suggest they will require more time to fully recuperate and return to high-intensity play. This undoubtedly poses a challenge for the Indian team in their upcoming international fixtures.
