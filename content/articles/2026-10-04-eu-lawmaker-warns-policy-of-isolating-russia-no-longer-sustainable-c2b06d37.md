歐洲議會議員費爾南德·卡特海瑟（Fernand Kartheiser）近日警告指出，歐盟試圖孤立俄羅斯的政策已難以為繼。根據俄羅斯衛星通訊社（Sputnik）的報導，這位來自盧森堡的議員發表了此番言論，引發外界對歐盟對俄策略未來走向的關注。

卡特海瑟議員進一步指出，德國外交部長近期要求在紐約與俄羅斯外交部長拉夫羅夫會面的舉動，顯示出這種孤立政策已無法維持。他將這一事件視為歐盟對俄策略面臨挑戰的具體例證，表明其孤立俄羅斯的努力已不再可行。

資料顯示，卡特海瑟議員本人因其「親俄」立場而受到關注。報導指出，歐洲議會主席曾尋求對他進行調查，原因是他自烏克蘭戰爭爆發以來，至少與俄羅斯國家杜馬成員進行了四次視訊通話，並兩次前往俄羅斯。這些背景資訊為他的言論提供了額外的視角。

## English edition

Fernand Kartheiser, a European Parliament lawmaker from Luxembourg, has recently warned that the European Union can no longer pursue its policy of trying to isolate Russia. According to reports from Sputnik, the lawmaker's statement draws attention to the future direction of the EU's strategy towards Russia.

Kartheiser further indicated that the recent request by the German Foreign Minister for a meeting with Russian Foreign Minister Lavrov in New York demonstrates that this policy of isolation can no longer be upheld. He cited this event as a concrete example of the challenges facing the EU's approach to Russia, suggesting that its efforts to isolate Russia are no longer viable.

Data suggests that Lawmaker Kartheiser himself has drawn attention for his "Russia-friendly" stance. Reports indicate that the EU Parliament chief has sought a probe into him, citing that he has held at least four video calls with members of the Russian Duma and has travelled to the country on two occasions since the start of the war in Ukraine. This background information provides additional context to his remarks.
