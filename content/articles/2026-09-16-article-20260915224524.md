  # Germany Rejects Pause in AI Development, Plans to Promote International Governance Coordination via G7 Framework
  
  > The German Ministry for Digital Affairs recently made a clear statement rejecting a pause in artificial intelligence (AI) research and development, emphasizing that it will continue to promote technological innovation to safeguard European digital sovereignty. The ministry also plans to leverage the G7 mechanism to encourage the participation of the U.S. and China in global AI governance.
  
  In mid-September 2026, the German Ministry for Digital Affairs officially responded to recent calls from the tech industry to "slow down AI development." Addressing a recent long-form article by Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, which argued that AI companies should slow the pace of model capability advancement and strengthen internal independent evaluation mechanisms to reduce the risk of models spiraling out of control, the German government has taken a definitive stance. German officials pointed out that halting artificial intelligence development is not a viable option for Europe at this time, emphasizing that strengthening European digital sovereignty must rely on continuous R&D investment and technological innovation.
  
  This move indicates that the German government, when faced with the choice between self-regulation suggestions from tech giants and global competitive pressures, has chosen to prioritize industrial development and strategic autonomy.
  
  According to reports from Reuters and Yahoo Entertainment, while the German government has rejected the proposal to "pause R&D," it has not ignored issues of technical safety. A spokesperson for the German Ministry for Digital Affairs emphasized that the government is closely monitoring global AI development trends and is taking the public warnings issued by major developers seriously. The core of the German government's current strategy lies in using the G7 international platform to push major AI powers, including the United States and China, to jointly participate in and establish a set of binding international governance frameworks. This shows that Germany is attempting to elevate AI risk management to the level of global governance through diplomatic and policy means, without sacrificing technological progress.
  
  However, there remain significant differences regarding the pace and path of AI governance. Although the German government advocates for continued R&D, industry leaders like Amodei have called from a technical and practical perspective for the establishment of stricter safety nets before AI breaks through the limitations of existing testing environments. Currently, the German government and private enterprises are experiencing a tug-of-war between policy goals and safety considerations regarding the core issue of "whether to slow down." Furthermore, whether the G7 governance framework that Germany plans to promote can effectively incorporate the two major AI powers of the U.S. and China, and how consensus can be reached amidst conflicting national interests, remains a political challenge with no specific execution details or timetable yet confirmed.
  
  This policy stance has profound implications for Europe's digital industry and social development. As the core economy of the European Union, Germany's refusal to pause AI R&D will directly influence the overall direction of EU digital policy. If Europe continues to increase its investment in AI R&D, it will help narrow the gap with the U.S. and China in terms of computing power and model scale, thereby consolidating Europe's competitiveness in key technological fields. However, this also means that Europe must face the potential social impacts and security risks of advanced models on its own in the absence of globally unified regulatory standards, which places high governance demands on regulatory bodies within the EU.
  
  Although the German government has demonstrated a proactive attitude toward R&D, experts caution against over-interpreting this as "laissez-faire development." The German Ministry for Digital Affairs' emphasis on "taking public warnings seriously" suggests that while the government promotes innovation, it still reserves the possibility of implementing mandatory regulations or establishing independent evaluation mechanisms in the future. Furthermore, while linking AI governance to the G7 framework demonstrates Germany's influence in international politics, given the complexity of geopolitics, there remains a high degree of uncertainty as to whether the U.S. and China will be willing to cooperate with European governance proposals under the G7 structure. Therefore, it is premature to interpret this policy as "Europe will fully lead the formulation of global AI rules."
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on how Germany implements its specific plans to promote G7 participation in governance, and whether the European Commission will follow Germany's stance to adjust its existing AI regulatory framework. Additionally, as AI technology evolves over the coming years, if major safety incidents or technological breakthroughs occur, whether the German government will be forced to adjust its bottom line of "no pause in R&D" will be a focal point for the global tech community and policymakers. Meanwhile, the balance between digital sovereignty and AI safety within the EU is expected to continue to ferment in upcoming parliamentary discussions.

# 德國拒絕暫停人工智慧研發，擬透過G7架構推動國際治理協調

> 德國數位事務部近期明確表態，拒絕暫停人工智慧（AI）研發，強調將持續推動技術創新以維護歐洲數位主權，並計畫透過G7機制推動美中參與全球AI治理。


德國數位事務部於2026年9月中旬正式回應近期科技界關於「放緩AI發展」的呼籲。針對Anthropic執行長阿莫迪（Dario Amodei）日前發表的長文，主張AI企業應放慢模型能力提升速度並加強內部獨立評估機制，以降低模型失控風險，德國政府採取了明確的立場。德國官方指出，停止發展人工智慧並非歐洲當前可行的選擇，強調強化歐洲的數位主權，必須仰賴持續的研發投入與技術創新。
此舉顯示德國政府在面對科技巨擘的自我監管建議與全球競爭壓力之間，選擇了優先保障產業發展與戰略自主。
根據路透社與Yahoo Entertainment的報導，德國政府雖然拒絕了「暫停研發」的提議，但並未忽視技術安全議題。德國數位事務部發言人強調，政府正密切關注全球AI發展動態，並嚴肅看待主要開發商提出的公開警告。德國政府目前的策略核心，在於透過七大工業國組織（G7）的國際平臺，推動包括美國與中國在內的主要AI強權，共同參與並建立一套具備約束力的國際治理框架。這顯示德國試圖在不犧牲技術進步的前提下，透過外交與政策手段，將AI的風險控管提升至全球治理層次。
然而，各界對於AI治理的節奏與路徑仍存在顯著差異。雖然德國政府主張持續研發，但業界領袖如阿莫迪則從技術實務層面，呼籲必須在AI突破現有測試環境限制前，建立更嚴格的安全防護網。目前，德國政府與民間企業在「是否應放慢腳步」這一核心議題上，呈現出政策目標與安全考量之間的拉鋸。此外，德國計畫推動的G7治理框架，是否能有效納入美中兩大AI強權，以及如何在各國利益衝突下達成共識，目前仍屬於尚待確認的政治挑戰，尚未有具體的執行細節或時間表。
此項政策立場對歐洲的數位產業與社會發展具有深遠影響。德國作為歐盟的核心經濟體，其拒絕暫停AI研發的態度，將直接影響歐盟整體的數位政策走向。若歐洲持續加大對AI研發的投入，將有助於縮小與美中在算力與模型規模上的差距，進而鞏固歐洲在關鍵技術領域的競爭力。然而，這也意味著歐洲必須在缺乏全球統一監管標準的情況下，獨自面對先進模型可能帶來的社會衝擊與安全風險，這對歐盟內部的監管機構提出了極高的治理要求。
儘管德國政府展現了積極的研發態度，但專家提醒，不應過度解讀此舉為「放任發展」。德國數位事務部強調的「嚴肅看待公開警告」，暗示了政府在推動創新之餘，仍保留了後續進行強制性監管或設定獨立評估機制的可能性。此外，將AI治理議題掛鉤G7框架，雖然展現了德國在國際政治中的影響力，但考慮到地緣政治的複雜性，美中兩國是否願意在G7架構下配合歐方的治理主張，仍存在高度不確定性。因此，將此政策解讀為「歐洲將全面主導全球AI規則制定」尚言之過早。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於德國如何落實其推動G7參與治理的具體方案，以及歐盟委員會是否會跟進德國的立場，調整其現有的AI監管法規。此外，隨著AI技術在未來數年內的演進，若出現重大安全事故或技術突破，德國政府是否會被迫調整其「不暫停研發」的底線，將是全球科技界與政策制定者持續關注的焦點。同時，歐盟內部對於數位主權與AI安全之間的平衡，預計也將在接下來的議會討論中持續發酵。
