  # French President Macron Visits the UK to Strengthen EU Role in Ukraine-Russia Peace Talks
  
  > French President Emmanuel Macron has embarked on a state visit to the United Kingdom, aiming to repair bilateral relations following Brexit. Simultaneously, the European Union is actively seeking to expand its influence in Ukraine-Russia peace negotiations, attempting to play a pivotal role in the formulation of U.S. policy.
  
  French President Emmanuel Macron officially began a three-day state visit to the United Kingdom in July 2025. This marks the first such high-level visit by a French head of state to the UK since 2008, and the first time the British government has hosted an EU head of state for a state visit since Brexit. The context of this trip not only symbolizes a new phase in post-Brexit bilateral relations between France and the UK but also highlights the urgent need for both sides to deeply coordinate their defense and foreign policies amidst the current turbulent international landscape. At the same time, the EU is actively adjusting its diplomatic strategy, attempting to secure a more substantive voice in the peace negotiation process regarding the Ukraine-Russia conflict.
  
  With the shifting international geopolitical landscape, the EU leadership is committed to incorporating the European perspective into U.S. policy considerations regarding Ukraine, ensuring that European interests are not marginalized in the future peace framework.
  
  According to current public information, during President Macron's visit, France and the UK reaffirmed their willingness to strengthen bilateral cooperation, particularly in the areas of defending the international order and security defense. Throughout the visit, Macron repeatedly called for France and the UK to work together to address regional security challenges. At the EU level, regarding participation in Ukraine-Russia peace talks, multiple sources indicate that the EU is attempting to expand its role. In recent rounds of negotiations held in Miami and Oslo, Bjoern Seibert, Chief of Staff to European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen, has participated on behalf of the EU.
  
  This development confirms that the EU is utilizing the direct participation of high-level staff to secure a more influential seat at the negotiating table in the power dynamics between the U.S., Ukraine, and Russia, thereby attempting to influence subsequent U.S. policy directions.
  
  Although the EU attempts to project diplomatic consistency, significant gaps remain in internal coordination. According to a report by Euronews, while Bjoern Seibert, Chief of Staff to European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen, was invited to participate in recent negotiations involving Ukraine, Europe, and the U.S., Pedro Lourtie, Chief of Staff to European Council President António Costa, was not. This detail reveals divisions within the EU regarding external representation and decision-making mechanisms; such opacity in "power distribution" may lead to concerns about the EU speaking with multiple voices during external negotiations.
  
  Furthermore, regarding the specific details of agreements from Macron's visit to the UK, and the extent of the substantive influence the EU can exert on the U.S. in Ukraine-Russia negotiations, there is currently a lack of supporting documentation, which remains to be verified by further diplomatic activities.
  
  These two diplomatic developments have profound implications for European industry and policy. First, the repair of Anglo-French relations facilitates the integration of the defense industrial chain under the NATO framework, particularly in addressing the disconnect in military technology research and defense supply chains between the UK and EU nations post-Brexit, which may be alleviated through bilateral agreements following this state visit. Second, if the EU successfully expands its influence in Ukraine-Russia peace talks, it will directly impact Europe's future energy security policy and geopolitical stability. If the EU can successfully integrate its own interests into U.S. policy on Ukraine, it will help Europe occupy a leading position in post-war reconstruction and security guarantee systems, rather than merely acting as a bystander to U.S.-Russia negotiations.
  
  However, there are diverging views regarding these diplomatic developments. Some analysts point out that while Macron's visit holds high symbolic significance, post-Brexit Anglo-French relations remain constrained by structural issues such as trade barriers and immigration policies, and it should not be over-interpreted as a full-scale restart of deep integration. At the same time, the EU's role in Ukraine-Russia negotiations is limited primarily by its lack of unified military projection capabilities and internal differences regarding the degree of toughness in policy toward Russia. Therefore, the EU's efforts to influence U.S. decision-making are highly likely to face challenges from U.S. domestic political priorities; viewing these diplomatic contacts as evidence that the EU has achieved the ability to lead negotiations would be overly optimistic and ignore the realities of geopolitical constraints.
  
  Subsequent observation should focus on two areas: first, whether France and the UK will sign specific defense and technology cooperation agreements following Macron's visit, which will serve as an indicator of whether Anglo-French relations have substantively improved. Second, whether the EU's participation mechanism in Ukraine-Russia peace talks will be adjusted, particularly how the European Council and the European Commission will integrate their negotiating representatives to avoid an image of diplomatic fragmentation. Furthermore, as variables in U.S. policy increase, whether the EU can translate its staff-level participation into more binding policy influence in the coming months will be key to determining what role Europe plays in the endgame of the Ukraine-Russia conflict.

# 法國總統馬克龍訪英並強化歐盟在烏俄和平談判角色

> 法國總統馬克龍近日展開對英國的國是訪問，旨在修復脫歐後的雙邊關係。與此同時，歐盟正積極尋求擴大在烏俄和平談判中的影響力，試圖在美國政策制定過程中發揮關鍵作用。


法國總統馬克龍（Emmanuel Macron）於2025年7月正式展開為期三天的英國國是訪問，這是自2008年以來法國元首首次對英國進行此類最高規格的訪問，亦是英國脫歐後，英國政府首度以國是訪問規格接待歐盟國家元首。此行背景不僅象徵英法兩國在脫歐後的雙邊關係邁向新階段，更凸顯雙方在當前動盪的國際局勢下，亟需針對防衛與外交政策進行深度協調。與此同時，歐盟正積極調整其外交戰略，試圖在烏俄衝突的和平談判程式中取得更具實質性的話語權。
隨著國際地緣政治格局的變動，歐盟領導層正致力於將歐洲的聲音納入美國對烏政策的考量範疇，以確保在未來的和平架構中，歐洲的利益不會被邊緣化。
根據目前的公開資訊，英法兩國在馬克龍訪問期間，共同確認了加強雙邊合作的意願，特別是在捍衛國際秩序與安全防衛領域。馬克龍在訪問中多次呼籲法英兩國應攜手合作，共同應對區域安全挑戰。而在歐盟層面，針對烏俄和平談判的參與度，多個來源指出歐盟正試圖擴大其角色。近期在邁阿密與奧斯陸舉行的多輪談判中，歐盟委員會主席馮德萊恩（Ursula von der Leyen）的幕僚長塞伯特（Bjoern Seibert）已代表歐盟參與其中。
這一進展證實了歐盟正透過高層幕僚的直接參與，試圖在美、烏、俄三方的博弈中，為歐洲爭取更具影響力的談判席位，並藉此影響美國後續的政策走向。
儘管歐盟試圖在外交上展現一致性，但內部協調仍存在明顯落差。根據《歐洲新聞臺》（Euronews）的報導，在近期涉及烏克蘭、歐洲與美國的談判中，歐盟委員會主席馮德萊恩的幕僚長塞伯特獲邀參與，然而歐洲理事會主席科斯塔（António Costa）的幕僚長盧爾蒂（Pedro Lourtie）卻未獲邀請。此一細節揭示了歐盟內部在對外談判代表權與決策機制上的分歧，這種「權力分配」的不透明性，可能導致歐盟在對外談判時出現多頭馬車的疑慮。
此外，關於馬克龍訪英的具體協議細節，以及歐盟在烏俄談判中究竟能對美國施加多少實質影響力，目前仍缺乏具體檔案支援，尚待後續外交活動的進一步驗證。
這兩項外交動態對歐洲產業與政策具有深遠影響。首先，英法關係的修復有助於北約（NATO）體系下的防衛產業鏈整合，特別是在脫歐後，英國與歐盟國家在軍工技術研發與國防供應鏈的脫節問題，可能透過此次國是訪問後的雙邊協議獲得緩解。其次，歐盟若能成功擴大在烏俄和平談判中的影響力，將直接影響歐洲未來的能源安全政策與地緣政治穩定。若歐盟能將自身利益成功融入美國對烏政策，將有助於歐洲在戰後重建與安全保障體系中佔據主導地位，而非僅僅作為美俄談判的旁觀者。
然而，針對上述外交進展，各界觀點仍存在分歧。部分分析指出，馬克龍的訪問雖具有高度象徵意義，但脫歐後的英法關係仍受限於貿易壁壘與移民政策等結構性問題，不應過度解讀為兩國將全面重啟深度整合。同時，歐盟在烏俄談判中的角色受限，主要在於其缺乏統一的軍事投射能力，且內部對於對俄政策的強硬程度仍有不同意見。因此，歐盟試圖影響美國決策的努力，極可能面臨來自美國國內政治優先順序的挑戰，若將這些外交接觸視為歐盟已具備主導談判能力，恐過於樂觀且忽略了地緣政治的現實限制。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於兩方面：首先是馬克龍訪英後，雙方是否會簽署具體的國防與科技合作協議，這將是檢視英法關係是否實質改善的指標。其次，歐盟在烏俄和平談判中的參與機制是否會進行調整，特別是歐洲理事會與歐盟委員會之間如何整合談判代表，以避免外交上的分裂形象。此外，隨著美國政策動向的變數增加，歐盟能否在未來幾個月內，將其幕僚層級的參與轉化為更具約束力的政策影響力，將是決定歐洲在烏俄衝突終局中扮演何種角色的關鍵。
