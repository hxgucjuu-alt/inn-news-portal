湯姆克魯斯主演的電影《挖掘者》在首映週末遭遇票房慘敗，根據資料顯示，該片在美國和加拿大地區僅獲得750萬至800萬美元的票房收入。這部華納兄弟公司的電影在票房榜上僅排名第五，成為近年來該製片廠最昂貴的失敗之一，同時也標誌著湯姆克魯斯職業生涯中最差的首映表現之一。

《挖掘者》的製作預算高達1.6億至1.8億美元，與其微薄的開畫票房形成鮮明對比。報導指出，華納兄弟公司預計在與派拉蒙合併之前，將因這部電影面臨高達1.5億美元的巨額虧損。這部電影的失利，對於即將與派拉蒙合併的華納兄弟來說，無疑是一個苦澀的結局。

此次票房失利並非華納兄弟近期唯一的挫折。資料顯示，該公司此前也曾經歷過《實用魔法2》和《橡樹街的盡頭》等影片的票房不佳。湯姆克魯斯主演的《挖掘者》的慘敗，進一步加劇了華納兄弟在合併前所面臨的財務壓力，並被描述為該公司作為獨立製片廠的一個「苦澀的最終音符」。

## English edition

Tom Cruise's latest film, "Digger," suffered a significant box office collapse during its opening weekend, with reports indicating it grossed only $7.5 million to $8 million in the U.S. and Canada. The Warner Bros. production landed in fifth place at the box office, marking one of the studio's costliest flops in years and one of the worst debuts of Tom Cruise's career.

The film's meager opening weekend performance stands in stark contrast to its substantial production budget, which ranged between $160 million and $180 million. Data suggests that Warner Bros. is now bracing for potential losses of up to $150 million from "Digger" before its impending merger with Paramount. This failure serves as a bitter final note for Warner Bros. as a standalone studio.

This box office disappointment is not an isolated incident for Warner Bros., which has reportedly faced a "losing streak" with other recent releases such as "Practical Magic 2" and "The End of Oak Street." The poor performance of "Digger" further exacerbates the financial pressures on Warner Bros. ahead of its merger, highlighting a challenging period for the studio.
