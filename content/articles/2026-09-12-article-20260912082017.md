  # Executive Yuan Countersigns Drone Ordinance; Hsiao Bi-khim Visits Italy Amid Domestic Policy Developments
  
  > Premier Cho Jung-tai has officially countersigned the "Strengthening National Defense Autonomy and Unmanned Vehicle Industry Development Ordinance," sparking debate between the ruling and opposition parties over constitutional responsibilities. Meanwhile, Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim visited Italy to participate in a democracy forum, marking a diplomatic breakthrough.
  
  The Executive Yuan announced on September 11, 2026, that Premier Cho Jung-tai has officially countersigned the "Strengthening National Defense Autonomy and Unmanned Vehicle Industry Development Ordinance," which was passed by the Legislative Yuan after three readings. Since the bill was proposed, the positions of the executive and legislative branches have differed significantly. Executive Yuan Secretary-General Chang Tun-han explained that the Executive Yuan had originally proposed the "Special Ordinance on the Procurement of National Defense Autonomous Unmanned Vehicles" on June 18, aiming to plan based on national defense strategic requirements; however, the version ultimately passed by the Legislative Yuan was not the Executive Yuan's version. This countersignature represents the Executive Yuan fulfilling its procedural responsibilities under the constitutional system regarding legislation passed by the Legislative Yuan.
  
  Regarding the progress of the bill's countersignature, there is a clear gap in how the ruling and opposition parties interpret the move. Kuomintang Chairperson Cheng Li-wun responded publicly, arguing that the Executive Yuan's action was merely a "return to the constitutional track." She emphasized that this was neither a concession by the Executive Yuan to the legislature nor a gesture of goodwill, but rather a constitutional obligation that the Premier was required to fulfill, stating bluntly that the Executive Yuan "has no room to claim credit." The Executive Yuan maintained a cautious stance, with Chang Tun-han emphasizing that although the countersignature process for the bill is complete, the concerns held by various sectors of society regarding the content of the bill will not disappear, and the challenges in the subsequent implementation phase remain to be seen.
  
  The current focus of attention is the divergence between the Executive Yuan and the Legislative Yuan regarding the path to national defense autonomy during the process from legislation to countersignature. The Executive Yuan advocated starting from national defense procurement needs, while the version passed by the Legislative Yuan differs in the weighting assigned to industrial development versus national defense autonomy. What remains to be confirmed is how the Executive Yuan will reconcile its original version's concepts with the provisions of the bill passed by the Legislative Yuan when implementing the ordinance in the future, and whether administrative resistance will arise due to the differences in the bill's versions regarding budget allocation and execution efficiency.
  
  In terms of diplomacy and social policy, Taiwan has recently seen several major developments. Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim, accompanied by Minister of Foreign Affairs Lin Chia-lung, officially arrived at the island of Ventotene, Italy, to participate in the second "Ventotene Conference on Democracy and Freedom." European Parliament Vice-President Pina Picierno publicly emphasized at the meeting that Beijing has no right to interfere in the international exchanges of Taiwanese officials. Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Legislator Wu Pei-yi expressed gratitude for this, believing that this proves Taiwan is not alone on the path of pursuing democracy and freedom.
  
  In addition, DPP Taipei mayoral candidate Shen Po-yang also unveiled social welfare policies on the same day, proposing several benefits for the elderly, children, and people with disabilities, including free enterovirus vaccinations. This indicates that the focus of domestic politics is simultaneously expanding toward diplomatic breakthroughs and local livelihood issues.
  
  Regarding the aforementioned events, perspectives from various sectors are diverse. On the national defense bill, legal experts and political commentators have cautioned against viewing the countersignature as a singular political victory, suggesting instead that focus should be placed on the substantive national security benefits after the bill is implemented. On diplomatic issues, although Hsiao Bi-khim's visit received strong support from European Parliament officials, the international geopolitical environment is complex—for instance, the upcoming BRICS summit and the continuing influence of the "Trump factor" on regional stability mean that Taiwan must maintain strategic composure in expanding its international space. Furthermore, regarding discussions on Taiwan's history and democratic development, some scholars have called for a re-examination of Taiwan's democratic story from the perspective of "decolonialism," avoiding the simplification of complex historical issues into a single political narrative.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on three areas: first, the implementation details and budget review progress of procurement and industrial support plans following the Executive Yuan's countersignature of the "Strengthening National Defense Autonomy and Unmanned Vehicle Industry Development Ordinance"; second, the substantive benefits of Hsiao Bi-khim's trip to Italy for Taiwan-EU trade or technological cooperation, with the support of the European Parliament; and finally, how the social welfare policies proposed by candidates like Shen Po-yang will be balanced with fiscal discipline in local elections and municipal planning. These issues will continue to influence Taiwan's political and social atmosphere in the second half of the year, and all sectors need to closely monitor the specific implementation of these policies.

# 行政院副署無人機條例、蕭美琴出訪義大利及國內政策動態

> 行政院長卓榮泰正式副署「強化國防自主暨無人載具產業發展條例」，引發朝野對憲政責任的論辯；同時，副總統蕭美琴出訪義大利參與民主論壇，展現外交突破。


行政院於今（2026）年9月11日宣佈，行政院長卓榮泰已正式副署由立法院三讀透過的「強化國防自主暨無人載具產業發展條例」。此法案自提出以來，行政與立法兩院立場顯著不同。行政院秘書長張惇涵說明，行政院原於6月18日提出「國防自主無人載具採購特別條例」，旨在從國防戰略需求出發進行規劃，然而立法院最終透過的版本並非行政院版本。此次副署行動，代表行政院在憲政體制下，針對立法院透過之法案履行程式責任。
在法案副署的相關進展中，朝野雙方對於此舉的解讀存在明顯落差。國民黨主席鄭麗文公開回應，認為行政院此舉僅是「回歸憲政軌道」，強調這並非行政院對國會的讓步，亦非行政院釋出善意，而是行政院長本就應履行的憲政義務，並直言行政院「沒有任何邀功的餘地」。行政院方面則維持謹慎態度，張惇涵強調，儘管法案已完成副署程式，但社會各界針對該法案內容的疑慮，並不會因此而消失，後續執行層面的挑戰仍待觀察。
目前各界關注的焦點，在於該法案從立法到副署的過程中，行政院與立法院在國防自主路徑上的分歧。行政院主張從國防採購需求出發，而立法院透過的版本則在產業發展與國防自主的權重分配上有所不同。目前尚待確認之處，在於行政院未來在執行該條例時，如何調和行政院原先的版本構想與立法院透過的法案條文，以及在預算分配與執行效率上，是否會因法案版本差異而產生行政阻力。
在外交與社會政策方面，臺灣近期亦有多項重大進展。副總統蕭美琴在外交部長林佳龍陪同下，正式抵達義大利文託泰內島（Ventotene），參與第二屆「文託泰內民主與自由會議」。歐洲議會副議長皮齊耶諾（Pina Picierno）在會中公開強調，北京無權幹預臺灣官員的國際交流。民進黨立委吳沛憶對此表示感謝，認為此舉證明臺灣在追求民主自由的道路上並不孤單。
此外，民進黨臺北市長參選人沈伯洋亦於同日端出社福政策，針對長者、幼兒及身障族群提出包括腸病毒疫苗免費接種在內的多項福利牛肉，顯示國內政治重心正同步向外交突破與地方民生議題擴充套件。
針對上述事件，各界觀點呈現多元化。在國防法案上，法律界與政治評論者提醒，不應將副署視為單一政治勝利，而應關注法案落實後的國家安全實質效益。在外交議題上，雖然蕭美琴出訪獲得歐洲議會官員力挺，但國際地緣政治環境複雜，如金磚國家（BRICS）峰會即將召開，且川普因素對區域局勢的影響力持續發酵，臺灣在國際空間的拓展仍需保持戰略定力。此外，關於臺灣歷史與民主發展的討論，亦有學者呼籲應從「脫殖民主義」角度重新審視臺灣民主故事，避免將複雜的歷史議題簡化為單一的政治敘事。
後續觀察重點將集中於三方面：首先是「強化國防自主暨無人載具產業發展條例」在行政院副署後，相關採購與產業扶植計畫的執行細節與預算審查進度；其次是蕭美琴此次義大利之行，在歐洲議會支援下，對臺灣與歐盟後續經貿或科技合作的實質助益；最後則是沈伯洋等參選人提出的社會福利政策，在地方選舉與市政規劃中將如何與財政紀律進行平衡。這些議題均將持續影響臺灣下半年的政治與社會氛圍，各界需密切關注相關政策的具體落實情況。
