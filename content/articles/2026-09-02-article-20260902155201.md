  # Taipei Social Welfare Department Commissioner Yao Shu-wen’s Appointment to Fubon Financial Board Sparks Controversy; City Government Clarifies Salary Status
  
  > Taipei City Social Welfare Department Commissioner Yao Shu-wen has faced scrutiny over a child placement case, followed by revelations of her appointment as a government-appointed director at Fubon Financial Holding. This has triggered accusations from city councilors regarding her professional background and potential political patronage, prompting the Taipei City Government to clarify the mechanism for remitting her director’s salary to the public treasury.
  
  A major social case recently occurred in Taipei City involving a 10-year-old girl who was sexually molested by an individual involved in sex work with her mother. The process from the initial report to the actual placement of the child took 240 days, sparking significant public concern and questioning regarding the city government's administrative efficiency. As the case escalated, the handling and public statements of Taipei City Social Welfare Department Commissioner Yao Shu-wen were criticized by various sectors. Under public pressure, Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Taipei City Councilor Lin Yen-feng revealed via social media on September 1, 2026, that in addition to her role as Commissioner, Yao had been appointed as a government-appointed director at Fubon Financial Holding. This revelation immediately ignited a fierce debate in political circles regarding "political patronage" and "professional suitability."
  
  According to various media reports, Lin pointed out that, based on past practices of the Taipei City Government, government-appointed directors at Fubon Financial Holding are typically required to possess deep expertise in finance or law, and the positions have historically been held by officials such as the Director-General of Budget, Accounting and Statistics, the Commissioner of the Department of Legal Affairs, or the Commissioner of the Department of Finance. However, in her director profile, Yao only checked the "risk management" box, leaving the "industry experience" section blank. Lin questioned how Yao, lacking experience in the financial industry, could obtain this position, suggesting it might be a "special reward" from Mayor Chiang Wan-an for her "protection of her superiors" during the child placement case, and demanded that the city government explain her professional suitability.
  
  In response to the external criticism, the Taipei City Government and Yao herself have issued statements. Yao stated that when the Social Welfare Department promotes public welfare, it is crucial to fully integrate social resources. She added that as a large enterprise, if Fubon Financial Holding can exert its influence to invest in public welfare, it will have a positive impact on society, and she will do her best to assist in promoting related matters. Regarding the salary allegations raised by the councilor, the city government clarified that the remuneration Yao receives as a Fubon Financial director is not the 150,000 NTD per month rumored by the public; the actual amount is only 8,500 NTD per month, and this sum has been paid in full to the city treasury in accordance with the law, rather than entering her personal pocket.
  
  This incident has had a concrete impact on the Taipei City Government's personnel policy and the operations of the Social Welfare Department. Regarding social welfare policy, whether department heads should concurrently serve as directors of private enterprises to facilitate the integration of public and private resources has become a focal point of public governance discussions. Supporters argue that having department heads serve as directors helps promote Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) and directs resources toward assisting the vulnerable. Opponents, however, argue that this not only blurs the line between politics and business but may also weaken the professional neutrality of the Social Welfare Department in handling child protection cases due to excessive politicization, especially under the trust crisis triggered by the girl's case, making such personnel arrangements more susceptible to scrutiny.
  
  Currently, there are significant differences in perspectives regarding this matter, and some information remains to be clarified. Lin Yen-feng’s questioning focuses on the "breaking of precedent" and "lack of professional alignment," arguing that the city government's hiring lacks transparency. Conversely, the city government's defense focuses on "public welfare connections" and the legality of "remitting salaries to the treasury." However, there is currently no concrete data or record to substantiate what specific public welfare functions Yao has performed on the Fubon Financial board, or how her professional background can provide substantive advice on financial risk management. The public should not over-interpret the situation based solely on the term "patronage," and should instead examine future board meeting minutes and the actual effectiveness of the Social Welfare Department's public welfare initiatives.

# 臺北市社會局長姚淑文任富邦金董事惹議，北市府澄清薪資流向

> 臺北市社會局長姚淑文因女童安置案遭質疑，隨後被爆出擔任富邦金控公股董事，引發議員質疑其專業背景與酬庸爭議，北市府對此澄清薪資繳庫機制。


臺北市近期發生一起10歲女童遭母親性交易物件性猥褻之重大社會案件，該案自通報至實際安置過程長達240天，引發社會輿論高度關注與對市府行政效率的質疑。隨案件延燒，臺北市社會局長姚淑文的處置方式與公開發言遭到各界抨擊。在輿論壓力下，民進黨籍臺北市議員林延鳳於2026年9月1日透過社群媒體揭露，姚淑文除擔任社會局長外，亦受指派擔任富邦金控公股董事，此舉隨即引發政壇對於「酬庸」及「專業適任性」的激烈辯論。
根據多方媒體報導，林延鳳指出，依據臺北市政府過去的慣例，派任至富邦金控的官派董事通常需具備深厚的財經、法律專業背景，過往多由主計處長、法務局長或財政局長等具備相關職能背景的官員出任。然而，姚淑文在董事履歷中，僅勾選「風險控管」一項，其餘「產業經驗」欄位則空白。林延鳳質疑，姚淑文缺乏金融產業相關經驗，卻能獲得此職位，是否與其在女童案中「護主有功」，因而獲得市長蔣萬安的特別賞賜有關，並要求市府說明其專業適任性。
針對外界質疑，臺北市政府與姚淑文字人已做出回應。姚淑文表示，社會局在推動公益事項時，能充分結合社會資源至關重要，而富邦金控作為大型企業，若能發揮影響力投入公益福利，將對社會有正面助益，她將盡其所能協助推動相關事務。針對議員指控的薪資問題，北市府澄清，姚淑文擔任富邦金董事所領取的報酬並非外界傳聞的每月15萬元，實際金額僅為每月8,500元，且該筆款項已依法全數繳入市庫，並非進入個人口袋。
此事件對臺北市府的用人政策與社會局的運作產生了具體影響。在社會福利政策層面，公部門與私人企業資源的結合是否應由局處首長直接兼任董事，已成為公共治理的討論焦點。支持者認為，局處首長兼任董事有助於推動企業社會責任（CSR），將資源導向弱勢救助；反對者則認為，這不僅模糊了政商界線，更可能因過度政治化而削弱了社會局在處理兒少保護案件時的專業中立性，特別是在女童案引發的信任危機下，此類人事安排更易被放大檢視。
目前關於此事的觀點存在顯著分歧，且部分資訊仍有待釐清。林延鳳的質疑重點在於「慣例打破」與「專業不符」，認為市府用人缺乏透明度；而市府的辯護則聚焦於「公益連結」與「薪資繳庫」的合法性。然而，針對姚淑文在富邦金董事會中具體發揮了何種公益功能，以及其專業背景如何能在金融風險控管上提供實質建議，目前尚無具體資料或紀錄佐證。外界不應僅憑「酬庸」一詞過度解讀，仍需檢視後續董事會的會議紀錄與社會局公益推動的實際成效。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於兩個面向：首先是臺北市議會是否會針對此人事任用案進行正式質詢，並要求市府公開相關派任標準；其次是社會局在女童安置案後的檢討報告與改善措施，是否能有效回應公眾對於兒少保護體系失靈的擔憂。此外，姚淑文在富邦金控董事會中的表現，以及其推動的公益計畫能否具體落實，將是檢驗其任職適當性的關鍵指標。INN新聞將持續追蹤相關後續發展，並關注北市府在處理重大社會案件與人事安排上的透明度。
