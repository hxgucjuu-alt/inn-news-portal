This service aims to provide the Asia-Pacific market with precise financial data analysis tools, assisting financial institutions and investors in the region to grasp more competitive market information.
  
  At the same time, through the precious images left behind by its photojournalists, Reuters has documented important historical turning points in the Asia-Pacific region over the past few decades. These images are not merely news materials but have become an essential component of regional historical memory.

In the field of financial data, the introduction of DataScope Select has strengthened the transparency of Asia-Pacific financial markets, enabling professionals in the region to process complex market data more effectively. On the level of news reporting, Reuters and other international news agencies, such as the Associated Press (AP), have long conducted in-depth interviews in the Asia-Pacific region, recording historical moments including major political events. These reports not only provided real-time information delivery at the time but also preserved historical truth through imagery, having a profound impact on subsequent public discussion and historical education in the regional society.

同時，路透社亦透過其攝影記者留下的珍貴影像，記錄了亞太地區近數十年來的重要歷史轉折，這些影像不僅是新聞素材，更成為了區域歷史記憶的重要組成部分。
根據多方公開資訊確認，路透社在亞太地區的業務拓展採取了高度專業化的策略。在金融資料領域，DataScope Select 的引進強化了亞太金融市場的透明度，使區域內的專業人士能更有效地處理複雜的市場資料。而在新聞報導層面，路透社與美聯社（AP）等國際通訊社長期在亞太地區進行深度採訪，記錄了包括重大政治事件在內的歷史時刻。這些報導不僅在當時提供了即時的資訊傳遞，更透過影像儲存了歷史真相，對於後續區域社會的公共討論與歷史教育產生了深遠影響。
企業內部的成長策略亦顯示，透過高層的戰略規劃與人才培育，國際媒體機構正持續深化其在亞太地區的影響力。
