根據美媒 Fox16 等體育報導，美國阿肯色州哈丁大學（Harding University）美式足球跑衛安德魯·米勒（Andrew Miller）除了在球場上具備出色得分能力外，私底下還有一項特殊的野生動物愛好。當他不在球場上爭取達陣時，經常會前往野外尋找蛇類、烏龜等爬行動物。

資料顯示，米勒擁有亮眼的運動經歷，曾代表哈丁學院奪得三次州冠軍，並在 2023 年隨哈丁大學獲得全國冠軍。談及野外抓捕經歷時，米勒表示自己基本上「什麼都抓」，雖然也曾抓過海狸或負鼠等動物，但主要仍以爬行動物為主；相關體育報導亦提及他曾與親屬一同進入有蛇出沒的水域進行捕捉。

對於這項獨特愛好是否對運動場上的表現有所助益，體育記者傑克·艾倫（Jack Allen）在訪談中指出，米勒認為這確實帶來了正向影響。米勒表示，除了在面對野生生物時能學習應對與克服恐懼之外，野外捕獵過程還有助於強化某些較細小的肌群，進而在激烈的碰撞運動中達到預防受傷的效果。

## English edition

Andrew Miller, a football star at Harding University in Arkansas, has gained attention for his unconventional passion off the gridiron: tracking and catching snakes, turtles, and other wildlife, according to reports by Fox16 and sports outlets.

Miller has compiled a notable athletic track record, having won three state championships at Harding Academy and captured a national championship with Harding University in 2023. Commenting on his outdoor adventures, Miller stated that he would catch almost anything—including beavers and possums on occasion—though his primary focus remains on reptiles. Sports reporters also noted accounts of him venturing into snake-inhabited waters alongside family members.

When asked about whether his wildlife pursuits benefit his athletic performance, sports reporter Jack Allen noted that Miller sees practical value in the hobby. Miller explained that beyond learning to manage and conquer fear, handling wild animals and traversing natural terrain helps him strengthen smaller muscle groups, ultimately aiding in injury prevention during high-impact football competitions.
