  # Global Public Health Governance and Digital Transformation: Challenges of Transnational Cooperation and Technological Resilience
  
  > Amid accelerating global public health threats and digital transformation, Taoyuan General Hospital in Taiwan hosted an international symposium to explore smart healthcare resilience, echoing the necessity of transnational public health cooperation emphasized by former European Commission President José Manuel Barroso.
  
  In late October 2026, the global public health and technology sectors saw several major developments, highlighting not only the urgency of transnational health governance but also the critical role of digital infrastructure in regional stability. On October 21, 2026, the Ministry of Health and Welfare’s Taoyuan General Hospital hosted the "15th International Symposium on Holistic Integrated Care and the Healthy Taiwan Deepening Project," focusing on the practical applications of smart medicine, precision medicine, and resilient healthcare.
  
  Meanwhile, former European Commission President José Manuel Barroso publicly called for global action during his visit to Taiwan, noting that public health issues are highly interconnected. He urged nations to establish common pandemic response mechanisms, emphasizing that no one can remain unaffected in the face of transnational health crises—a perspective that resonates with the diplomatic and security challenges currently facing the Southeast Asian region.
  
  According to various public sources, the international community's definition of "resilience" has expanded from the scope of public health alone to include digital security and hardware supply chains. Taoyuan General Hospital's symposium explicitly pointed out that in the face of global medical labor shortages and environmental sustainability challenges, digital transformation is a core pathway to improving medical quality.
  
  On the diplomatic front, U.S. Secretary of State Antony Blinken has previously strengthened ties with ASEAN member states through frequent virtual meetings, signaling that the U.S. views Southeast Asia as a regional strategic priority. This aligns with the "we are all in the same boat" philosophy advocated by Barroso, suggesting that open channels for transnational communication are crucial for regional security and health governance.
  
  However, technical security risks have emerged alongside the push for digitalization and international cooperation. According to technology media reports, a vulnerability was recently discovered in the Node.js Brotli decoder, which could lead to Remote Code Execution (RCE) risks, posing a potential threat to medical and administrative systems that rely on global digital infrastructure. Currently, there is no unified defense standard among governments and research institutions on how to balance the "medical efficiency gains from digital transformation" with the "cybersecurity risks posed by software vulnerabilities."
  
  Furthermore, while former European Commission President Barroso’s remarks emphasized cooperation, significant differences remain among countries regarding regulations and implementation—such as vaccine distribution and privacy permissions for medical data sharing—which still require further coordination by international organizations.
  
  For emerging markets like Southeast Asia, the impact of these public health and technological issues is profound. As wearable devices such as Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses enter the Asian market, personal data collection and health monitoring functions will become more prevalent. This not only drives industrial innovation but also places higher demands on personal privacy protection and digital governance within the region. Moreover, if regional medical information systems are attacked due to software vulnerabilities, it will directly impact public health resilience. Therefore, the "holistic integrated care" advocated by Taoyuan General Hospital could serve as a reference model for regional medical digital transformation if combined with cybersecurity protections and international standards.
  
  Although there is a consensus on the willingness to strengthen cooperation, it must be noted that the implementation of international public health policies is often limited by geopolitical and economic disparities. For example, Southeast Asian countries face significant gaps in response capabilities compared to the EU or Taiwan due to limitations in basic medical resources and the digital divide. Therefore, over-interpreting "globalized" medical mechanisms may overlook the inequality in resource allocation among nations. Experts remind us that while promoting smart healthcare, it is essential to ensure the inclusivity of technology transfers to avoid widening medical inequalities caused by the digital divide; this is the key to achieving a "Healthy Taiwan" and "global common good."
  
  Looking ahead, the international community will continue to focus on cybersecurity resilience in medical digital transformation, particularly regarding the defense capabilities of critical infrastructure. The results of the Taoyuan General Hospital symposium are expected to provide practical recommendations for future medical policies, while the alignment of public health and digital policies in Southeast Asia will depend on how countries balance digital sovereignty with international cooperation. Governments should closely monitor the progress of patches for underlying software vulnerabilities like those in Node.js and continue to promote the standardization of transnational health information sharing platforms to ensure that the international community possesses stronger coordination resilience and response capabilities when the next global health crisis arrives.

# 全球公衛治理與數位轉型：跨國合作與科技韌性之挑戰

> 隨全球公衛威脅與數位轉型加速，臺灣桃園醫院舉辦國際研討會探討智慧醫療韌性，並呼應前歐盟主席巴羅佐強調之跨國公衛合作必要性。


2026年10月下旬，全球公衛與科技領域迎來多項重大進展，不僅凸顯了跨國衛生治理的迫切性，亦揭示了數位基礎設施在區域穩定中的關鍵角色。衛生福利部桃園醫院於2026年10月21日主辦「115年第十五屆全人醫療整合服務暨健康臺灣深耕計畫國際研討會」，聚焦於智慧醫療、精準醫療及韌性醫療的實務應用。
與此同時，前歐盟執行委員會主席何塞·曼努埃爾·巴羅佐（José Manuel Barroso）於訪臺期間公開呼籲，全球公衛議題具有高度連動性，各國必須建立共同的疫情應變機制，強調在跨國衛生危機面前，無人能置身事外，此觀點與東南亞區域目前面臨的外交與安全挑戰形成呼應。
根據多方公開資訊確認，國際社會對於「韌性」的定義已從單純的公共衛生範疇，擴充套件至數位安全與硬體供應鏈。桃園醫院的研討會明確指出，面對全球醫療人力短缺與環境永續挑戰，數位轉型是提升醫療品質的核心路徑。
而在外交層面，美國國務卿布林肯（Antony Blinken）過去曾透過頻繁的線上會議，強化與東南亞國協（ASEAN）成員國的聯絡，顯示美國將東南亞視為區域戰略優先事項，這與巴羅佐所提倡的「同舟共濟」理念一致，即在區域安全與衛生治理上，跨國溝通管道的暢通至關重要。
然而，在推動數位化與國際合作的過程中，技術安全風險亦同步浮現。根據科技媒體報導，Node.js的Brotli解碼器近期被發現存在程式漏洞，可能導致遠端程式碼執行（Remote Code Execution）風險，這對全球依賴數位基礎設施的醫療與行政系統構成潛在威脅。目前，各國政府與學研機構對於如何平衡「數位轉型帶來的醫療效率提升」與「軟體漏洞帶來的資安風險」尚無統一的防禦標準。
此外，前歐盟主席巴羅佐的言論雖強調合作，但對於具體機制如何落實（例如疫苗分配、醫療資料共享的隱私許可權），各國在法規與執行層面仍存在顯著差異，尚待國際組織進一步協調。
對於東南亞等新興市場而言，這些公共衛生與技術議題的影響深遠。隨著科技產品如Ray-Ban Meta智慧眼鏡等穿戴式裝置進入亞洲市場，個人資料收集與健康監測功能將更趨普及，這不僅帶來產業創新，也對區域內的個人隱私保障與數位治理提出更高要求。此外，若區域內的醫療資訊系統因軟體漏洞遭受攻擊，將直接衝擊公共衛生韌性。因此，臺灣桃園醫院所提倡的「全人醫療整合服務」，若能結合資安防護與國際標準，將成為區域內醫療數位轉型的參考典範。
儘管各界對於加強合作的意願一致，但必須注意，國際公衛政策的實踐常受限於地緣政治與經濟條件的差異。例如，東南亞國家在面對跨國衛生危機時，受限於基礎醫療資源與數位落差，其應變能力與歐盟或臺灣存在顯著差距。因此，過度解讀「全球一體化」的醫療機制可能忽略了各國在資源分配上的不平等。專家提醒，在推動智慧醫療的同時，必須確保技術轉移的包容性，避免因數位鴻溝導致的醫療不平等擴大，這才是實現「健康臺灣」與「全球共好」的關鍵。
展望後續發展，國際社會將持續關注醫療數位轉型中的資安防護韌性，特別是針對關鍵基礎設施的防禦能力。桃園醫院的研討會成果預計將為未來醫療政策提供實務建議，而東南亞區域的公衛與數位政策對接，則取決於各國在數位主權與國際合作間的平衡。各國政府應密切關注Node.js等底層軟體漏洞的修復進度，並持續推動跨國衛生資訊共享平臺的標準化，以確保在下一次全球性衛生危機來臨時，國際社會能具備更強的協調韌性與應變能力。
