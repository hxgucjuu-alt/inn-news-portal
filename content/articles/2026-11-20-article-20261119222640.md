The risk of regional conflict continues to escalate alongside "Operation Epic Fury."
  
  As a strategic artery for global energy transport, the Strait of Hormuz has long been at the core of geopolitical power struggles in the Middle East. Recently, the security situation in the region has deteriorated once again, with the Iranian Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) Navy issuing a statement alleging that two oil tankers exploded in the waters of the strait. This incident occurs within the framework of a military operation dubbed "Operation Epic Fury," which has resulted in 23 U.S. military personnel deaths since its inception this February, signaling that the military confrontation in the Persian Gulf has evolved from obscure proxy conflicts into a direct armed standoff.
  
  According to cross-referenced information from various media outlets and real-time mapping platforms, Iran has adopted a high-profile stance regarding the explosions in the strait, attempting to demonstrate its dominance over Persian Gulf shipping lanes and its counter-strike capabilities. Meanwhile, U.S. officials continue to push back against Iranian military propaganda. U.S. defense officials have explicitly stated that although Tehran frequently claims to have launched successful attacks on U.S. military facilities, these strikes have caused no substantive damage. The U.S. emphasizes that its local defensive installations remain fully operational and have not been affected by the "precision strikes" claimed by Iran.
  
  However, there remains a significant information gap regarding the details of the tanker explosions. Iranian state media and the Revolutionary Guard reports focus on the fact that the explosions occurred, but there is no consensus on the specific causes, the nationalities of the damaged tankers, crew casualties, or whether external attacks were involved. U.S. sources remain highly skeptical of the Iranian narrative, suggesting that Iran may be exaggerating the severity of regional conflicts to mask the military setbacks it has suffered during "Operation Epic Fury." Furthermore, the international shipping industry remains in a state of high uncertainty regarding the actual threat level and navigational safety in the area, as there is a lack of imagery or data from neutral third parties to verify the explosion sites.
  
  This incident has had a direct and profound impact on the energy industry and international policy in the Persian Gulf. The Strait of Hormuz is a choke point for global oil supply; any report of tanker explosions, even if unverified, immediately triggers volatility in global crude oil futures markets. For the nations within the Persian Gulf, this is not only a security challenge but a direct threat to the stability of their energy exports. The U.S. military presence in the region is currently under the pressure of "Operation Epic Fury." If Iran continues to expand its range of attacks against Persian Gulf neighbors, it may force the U.S. to adjust its Middle East strategy, or even increase its military intervention in the region, further compressing the space for diplomatic mediation.
  
  It must be noted that one should not over-interpret military propaganda from a single source. Iran is currently under strategic pressure, and its release of information regarding "tanker explosions" may carry elements of psychological warfare intended to divert domestic and international attention from U.S. counter-strikes. On the other hand, the U.S. claim of "zero damage" may also involve strategic downplaying, aimed at maintaining the confidence of regional allies and preventing market panic. Therefore, in the absence of independent investigation reports, any view that treats this incident as a precursor to full-scale war may overlook the precise calculations currently employed by all parties to maintain a "low-intensity conflict" threshold. The limiting factor is that information transparency in the region is currently extremely low, and military intelligence is often used as a bargaining chip in diplomatic negotiations.

# 荷姆茲海峽油輪爆炸事件引發波斯灣地緣政治緊張局勢升溫

> 伊朗伊斯蘭革命衛隊海軍宣稱兩艘油輪在荷姆茲海峽發生爆炸，美方則駁斥近期伊朗對美軍設施的攻擊成效，區域衝突風險隨「史詩憤怒行動」持續升級。


荷姆茲海峽作為全球能源運輸的戰略要道，長期以來是中東地緣政治的角力核心。近期，該區域的安全情勢再度惡化，伊朗伊斯蘭革命衛隊（IRGC）海軍對外發布訊息，指控有兩艘油輪在該海峽水域發生爆炸事件。此一事件背景發生在名為「史詩憤怒行動」（Operation Epic Fury）的軍事框架下，該行動自今年二月啟動以來，已造成美軍在該地區累積二十三名人員陣亡，顯示波斯灣地區的軍事對抗已從隱晦的代理人衝突，逐漸演變為直接的武裝對峙。
根據多家媒體與即時地圖平臺的交叉資訊顯示，伊朗方面對於海峽內的爆炸事件採取了高調宣示的態度，試圖透過此舉展現其對波斯灣航道的主導權與反制能力。與此同時，美國官方則持續針對伊朗的軍事宣傳進行反擊，美國國防官員明確指出，儘管德黑蘭方面近期頻繁宣稱對美軍設施發動攻擊並取得戰果，但實際上這些攻擊對美軍設施並未造成實質性的損害，美方強調其在當地的防禦設施維持高度運作，並未受到伊朗宣稱的「精準打擊」影響。
然而，目前關於油輪爆炸事件的細節仍存在顯著的資訊落差。伊朗官方媒體與革命衛隊的報導側重於爆炸的發生事實，但對於爆炸的具體原因、受損油輪的國籍、船員傷亡情況以及是否涉及外部攻擊，各方說法尚未達成一致。美方訊息來源則對伊朗的敘事保持高度懷疑，並暗示伊朗可能透過誇大區域衝突的嚴重性，以掩蓋其在「史詩憤怒行動」中遭受的軍事挫敗。此外，國際航運界對於該海域的實際威脅程度與航行安全，目前仍處於高度不確定的觀察階段，缺乏中立第三方驗證爆炸現場的影像或資料。
此一事件對波斯灣地區的能源產業與國際政策產生了直接且深遠的影響。荷姆茲海峽是全球石油供應的咽喉，任何關於油輪爆炸的報導，即便未經證實，都會立即導致全球原油期貨市場的波動。對於區域內的波斯灣國家而言，這不僅是安全層面的挑戰，更直接威脅到其能源出口的穩定性。美國在該地區的軍事部署正處於「史詩憤怒行動」的壓力之下，若伊朗持續擴大對波斯灣鄰國的攻擊範圍，恐將迫使美國調整其中東戰略，甚至加大在該區域的軍事介入力度，進一步壓縮外交斡旋的空間。
必須注意的是，外界不應過度解讀單一來源的軍事宣傳。伊朗目前正處於戰略壓力期，其發布的「油輪爆炸」訊息可能帶有心理戰色彩，意在轉移國內外對於美軍反擊行動的關注。另一方面，美方對於「零損害」的說法亦可能存在戰略性淡化，旨在維持區域盟友的信心與防止市場恐慌。因此，在缺乏獨立調查報告的情況下，任何將此事件視為全面戰爭爆發前兆的觀點，都可能忽略了當前各方在維持「低強度衝突」邊緣的精確計算。限制因素在於，目前該區域的資訊透明度極低，且軍事情報往往被用作外交談判的籌碼。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於三方面：首先是國際航運保險公司是否針對荷姆茲海峽調升風險等級，這將是評估局勢真實危險性的重要指標；其次是美國海軍是否會針對此次油輪事件採取護航行動或進一步的軍事回應，這將決定衝突是否會從區域性摩擦升級為區域性海戰；最後則是伊朗是否會針對波斯灣其他能源基礎設施採取進一步行動，以及國際社會（特別是聯合國與區域組織）是否能介入調解，以防止「史詩憤怒行動」引發更大規模的人道與經濟災難。
