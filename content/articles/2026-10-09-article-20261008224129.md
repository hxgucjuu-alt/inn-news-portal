  # Global Economic Outlook: Analysis of AI Supply Chain Risks and RMB Exchange Rate Trends
  
  > The global economy is currently positioned between AI-driven growth and potential risks of imbalance. While the World Bank has raised its GDP forecast for East Asia, Capital Economics and AMRO have issued warnings that the undervaluation of the RMB and a potential reversal of the AI boom could trigger chain reactions across regional economies.
  
  The recent global economic and trade environment presents a complex dynamic equilibrium. According to a report released by the World Bank in early October 2026, economic growth expectations for the East Asia and Pacific region have been revised upward, with the 2026 growth rate projected to reach 4.5%, an increase of 0.3 percentage points from the April forecast. This growth momentum is primarily driven by the strong performance of AI-related exports, with Vietnam's economic growth forecast seeing a significant upward revision of 1.1 percentage points.
  
  Meanwhile, the global textile industry is demonstrating momentum for transformation at the Taipei Innovative Textile Application Show (TITAS). The event brought together 410 manufacturers from 12 countries, including Malaysia, Vietnam, and mainland China, highlighting the deep integration and trend of cross-border cooperation within the regional industrial chain regarding sustainable circularity.
  
  Under the joint observation of several international institutions, the over-concentration of the AI industrial chain has become a potential concern for the regional economy. The "2026 Financial Stability Report" released by the ASEAN+3 Macroeconomic Research Office (AMRO) clearly points out that because Southeast Asia, China, Japan, and South Korea are at the core of the global AI supply chain, and financial markets are increasingly linked to AI-related assets, the region would face chain reactions—such as a decline in tech product exports, capital outflows, and refinancing pressures for highly leveraged companies—should the AI boom undergo a disorderly correction. The World Bank concurs, warning that the region's high dependence on the AI boom makes it extremely vulnerable to fluctuations in global technology spending.
  
  Regarding currency markets and macroeconomic imbalances, the latest research from Capital Economics offers a different perspective. The firm estimates that the RMB is currently undervalued by as much as 25%; if China's gold imports are viewed as financial capital flows rather than trade flows, the degree of undervaluation could potentially widen further. However, Capital Economics emphasizes that a significant revaluation of the RMB is unlikely, with the baseline scenario remaining a continued, modest appreciation over the coming years.
  
  This view differs from some market expectations regarding RMB exchange rate policy. Furthermore, Capital Economics notes that a moderate appreciation alone is unlikely to eliminate China's external imbalances; a significant rise in the RMB is only likely if the U.S. economy slows down significantly, accompanied by substantial interest rate cuts by the Federal Reserve.
  
  These economic dynamics have a direct impact on industries and policy-making in South Asia and East Asia. With the reconfiguration of global supply chains, economies reliant on AI manufacturing are facing the dual challenge of "growth and vulnerability." For relevant manufacturing exporters, how to maintain export competitiveness while guarding against financial shocks caused by a pullback in tech spending has become a core issue for policy-making. Additionally, traditional manufacturing sectors, such as textiles, are seeking new growth engines through cross-border exhibitions and technical exchanges while facing pressure from net-zero carbon emissions and green transformation. This indicates that regional industries are attempting to diversify operational risks through sustainable development in the face of digital transformation risks.
  
  Although there is a consensus on the economic dividends and risks brought by AI, analysts caution against over-interpreting any single indicator. For example, Capital Economics' estimation model for RMB undervaluation relies heavily on assumptions regarding the current account balance and the application of elasticity parameters, and the results are subject to the limitations of the model itself. Similarly, while the warnings regarding East Asia's dependence on AI reflect the high level of supply chain integration, differences in industrial structure, fiscal resilience, and domestic demand markets among various countries will lead to varying degrees of impact. Investors and policymakers should consider the complex interaction between global tech spending cycles and regional financial stability mechanisms when referencing such reports, avoiding the treatment of any single institution's forecast as an inevitable conclusion.
  
  Future observation will focus on the actual flow of global technology spending and the substantive impact of Federal Reserve monetary policy on Asian currency exchange rates. Furthermore, the financial regulatory measures adopted by countries in the East and South Asian regions to address AI supply chain risks, as well as the speed of industrial adjustment under net-zero transition policies, will determine whether the region can effectively mitigate potential economic shocks. The market will continue to monitor export data, capital flow conditions, and subsequent assessments of global external imbalances by the International Monetary Fund (IMF) to clarify the true resilience of the regional economy after the AI boom subsides.

# 全球經濟展望：AI供應鏈風險與人民幣匯率走勢分析

> 全球經濟正處於AI驅動的成長與潛在失衡風險之間。世界銀行上調東亞GDP預測，但凱投宏觀與AMRO示警，人民幣匯率低估及AI熱潮逆轉可能對區域經濟帶來連鎖衝擊。


近期全球經濟與貿易環境呈現複雜的動態平衡。根據世界銀行於2026年10月初發布的報告，東亞及太平洋地區的經濟成長預期獲得上調，2026年成長率預計達到4.5%，較4月份的預測提升0.3個百分點。此增長動力主要源於人工智慧（AI）相關出口的強勁表現，其中越南的經濟成長預期更大幅上調了1.1個百分點。
與此同時，全球紡織產業亦在臺北紡織展（TITAS）中展現出轉型動能，匯集了包含馬來西亞、越南及中國大陸等12國共410家廠商，顯示區域產業鏈在永續迴圈議題上的深度連結與跨國合作趨勢。
在多個國際機構的共同觀測下，AI產業鏈的過度集中已成為區域經濟的潛在隱憂。東協（ASEAN）與中日韓宏觀經濟研究辦公室（AMRO）發布的《2026年金融穩定報告》明確指出，由於東南亞及中日韓地區處於全球AI供應鏈的核心，且金融市場與AI相關資產的連結度日益緊密，一旦AI熱潮出現失序式修正，該區域將面臨科技產品出口下滑、資本外流及高槓桿企業再融資壓力等連鎖衝擊。世界銀行亦對此表示認同，警告該地區經濟對AI熱潮的高度依賴，使其極易受到全球科技支出波動的影響。
針對貨幣市場與宏觀經濟失衡議題，凱投宏觀（Capital Economics）的最新研究提出了不同視角。該機構估計人民幣匯率目前被低估幅度高達25%，若將中國黃金進口視為金融資金流動而非貿易流動，低估幅度甚至可能進一步擴大。然而，凱投宏觀強調，人民幣大幅重估的可能性並不高，基準情境仍為未來幾年持續小幅升值。
此說法與部分市場對於人民幣匯率政策的預期存在差異，且凱投宏觀指出，僅靠溫和升值恐難以消除中國的外部失衡，除非美國經濟出現明顯放緩並伴隨聯準會（Fed）大幅降息，人民幣才可能出現顯著升勢。
上述經濟動態對南亞及東亞地區的產業與政策制定產生直接影響。隨著全球供應鏈重新配置，依賴AI製造業的經濟體正面臨「成長與脆弱性」並存的挑戰。對於相關製造業出口國而言，如何在保持出口競爭力的同時，防範科技支出回落所帶來的金融震盪，已成為政策制定的核心課題。此外，紡織等傳統製造業在面臨淨零碳排與綠色轉型壓力下，正透過跨國展會與技術交流尋求新的成長引擎，這顯示出區域產業在面對數位化轉型風險時，仍試圖透過永續發展來分散營運風險。
儘管各界對AI帶來的經濟紅利與風險已有共識，但分析師提醒，不應過度解讀單一指標。例如，凱投宏觀對於人民幣低估的估算模型，高度依賴於對經常帳餘額的假設及彈性引數的應用，其結果存在模型本身的限制。同樣地，關於東亞地區對AI依賴度的示警，雖反映了供應鏈的高度整合，但各國在產業結構、財政韌性及內需市場的差異，亦會導致受衝擊程度不一。投資人與決策者在參考相關報告時，需考量全球科技支出週期與區域金融穩定機制之間的複雜互動，避免將單一機構的預測視為必然發生的定論。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於全球科技支出的實際流向，以及聯準會貨幣政策對亞幣匯率的實質影響。此外，東亞及南亞區域內各國針對AI供應鏈風險所採取的金融監理措施，以及在淨零轉型政策下的產業調整速度，將決定該地區能否有效緩解潛在的經濟震盪。市場將持續關注各國出口資料、資本流動狀況，以及國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）後續對全球外部失衡的評估報告，以釐清區域經濟在AI熱潮退去後的真實韌性。
