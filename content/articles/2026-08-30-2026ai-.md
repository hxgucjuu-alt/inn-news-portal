  # Qualcomm Launches 2026 APAC AI Innovation Program, Focusing on Startup Development in Japan, Singapore, and South Korea
  
  > Qualcomm has officially launched the "Qualcomm AI Program for Innovators 2026 - APAC," aimed at providing technical resources to assist startups in Japan, Singapore, and South Korea in deepening their AI capabilities, while driving regional industrial digital transformation and technological upgrades.
  
  Qualcomm recently announced the official launch of the "Qualcomm AI Program for Innovators 2026 - APAC," an initiative designed to integrate resources and provide technical empowerment for the technology startup ecosystem in the Asia-Pacific region. According to public information, the strategic core of the program focuses on three key markets: Japan, Singapore, and South Korea. It aims to leverage Qualcomm's technological advantages in mobile communications and edge computing to assist local startups in achieving breakthroughs in AI research, development, and application deployment.
  
  The promotion of this program is not merely a corporate-level technical collaboration; it is also viewed as a significant indicator of the Asia-Pacific region's efforts to deeply integrate high-tech industrial chains and enhance global competitiveness in the post-pandemic era.
  
  According to reports from various media outlets, Qualcomm's program has garnered significant attention from industry and government officials across the Asia-Pacific region. As reported by *Travel Daily News Asia*, the program has attracted widespread participation since its inception, with a focus on lowering the barriers for startups to enter the high-end AI R&D field by providing AI technical frameworks and hardware support.
  
  Meanwhile, the academic community is also actively participating in related cross-border collaborations. Dr. Brenda Jensen, Dean of the College of Natural and Computational Sciences at Hawaii Pacific University, publicly stated that the university is honored to participate in such interdisciplinary collaborative projects. This demonstrates that the Asia-Pacific region has formed a close "industry-government-academia" cooperation model to drive technological innovation, aiming to accelerate the commercialization of AI technology within the region through cross-border resource sharing.
  
  However, there are still some discrepancies in the currently available public information regarding the specific execution details and eligibility criteria of the program. While media outlets have confirmed the official launch of the program, there is currently no unified official statement regarding the proportions of startup support for each country, the specific pathways for technology transfer, or how regulatory and legal differences between nations will be coordinated.
  
  Furthermore, regarding the potential impact of the public health environment in the Asia-Pacific region on cross-border business dealings, some historical and background data indicate that the region previously implemented strict temperature checks and preventive quarantine measures in response to sudden public health events. Whether these past epidemic prevention experiences will become variables for future cross-border technical exchanges and personnel movement remains to be clarified by relevant policies. While the public maintains a positive attitude toward the expected benefits of the program, the technical details of implementation and the adaptability of regulations for cross-border cooperation remain in an observation phase.
  
  This program will have a profound impact on the industries of the Asia-Pacific region. First, in terms of the technology industrial chain, Qualcomm's involvement will help strengthen the advantages of Japan, Singapore, and South Korea in AI hardware and software integration, thereby consolidating the Asia-Pacific region's position in the global AI supply chain. Second, at the policy level, the program may prompt governments to re-examine their support policies for AI startups and further promote digital economy agreements within the region.
  
  For the travel and aviation industries, with the infusion of AI technology, more innovative solutions are expected to emerge in areas such as smart airport management, passenger flow optimization, and predictive maintenance. This aligns with the long-term industry digitalization trends monitored by *Travel Daily News Asia*, indicating that AI technology is gradually penetrating various livelihood and infrastructure industries beyond pure R&D.
  
  Although the market has high expectations for Qualcomm's AI program, analysts caution against over-interpreting the immediate stimulus effect of a single corporate program on the overall regional economy. First, the development of AI technology is highly dependent on local talent pools and data privacy regulations in each country; Japan, Singapore, and South Korea each have their own legal frameworks and market characteristics, and a single program cannot fully overcome the institutional differences between these nations. Second, global economic fluctuations and geopolitical restrictions on high-tech industries may affect the progress of the program's execution.
  
  Therefore, when evaluating the success probability of this program, one must consider the cyclical nature of technological R&D and market acceptance. It should not be viewed as a panacea for all industrial dilemmas, but rather as an important catalyst for driving regional digital transformation, rather than a single decisive factor.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on the specific results of the program over the next six months, particularly the list of the first batch of supported startups and their R&D achievements. Furthermore, as the program deepens, whether Qualcomm will expand its scope of cooperation to other countries in the Asia-Pacific region, and how governments will coordinate cross-border ethics and safety standards for AI technology, will be key indicators for assessing the long-term influence of the program. Media and industry should continue to monitor the transparency of related technology transfers and how this program interacts with existing Asia-Pacific regional economic and trade frameworks (such as the digital trade chapters under RCEP or CPTPP), which will determine whether Qualcomm's move can truly achieve the goal of promoting the sustainable development of the Asia-Pacific AI ecosystem.

# 高通啟動2026亞太AI創新計畫 聚焦日星韓新創產業發展

> 高通正式啟動「2026年亞太區AI創新者計畫」，旨在透過技術資源挹注，協助日本、新加坡與南韓的新創企業深耕人工智慧領域，並推動區域內產業數位轉型與技術升級。


高通（Qualcomm）於近期正式宣佈啟動「2026年亞太區AI創新者計畫」（Qualcomm AI Program for Innovators 2026 - APAC），此項計畫旨在針對亞太地區的科技新創生態系進行資源整合與技術賦能。根據公開資訊顯示，該計畫的戰略核心聚焦於日本、新加坡與南韓等三個關鍵市場，期望透過高通在行動通訊與邊緣運算領域的技術優勢，協助當地新創公司在AI技術研發與應用落地方面取得突破。
此項計畫的推動不僅是企業層級的技術合作，更被視為亞太地區在後疫情時代，針對高科技產業鏈進行深度整合與全球競爭力提升的重要指標。
綜合多家媒體報導，高通此項計畫已獲得亞太地區產業界與政府官員的高度關注。根據《Travel Daily News Asia》的報導，該計畫自啟動以來，已吸引多方參與，重點在於透過提供AI技術框架與硬體支援，降低新創企業進入高階AI研發領域的門檻。
與此同時，學術界亦積極參與相關的跨國合作，夏威夷太平洋大學自然與計算科學學院院長布蘭達·詹森（Dr. Brenda Jensen）公開表示，該校對於能參與此類跨領域的合作計畫深感榮幸，這顯示出亞太地區在科技創新推動上，已形成「產、官、學」三方緊密連結的合作模式，旨在透過跨國界的資源共享，加速區域內AI技術的商業化程式。
然而，在計畫具體執行細節與參與資格的認定上，目前的公開資訊仍存在部分差異。雖然各方媒體均確認計畫已正式啟動，但關於各國在新創扶持比例、技術轉移的具體路徑，以及不同國家間的監管法律差異如何協調，目前尚無統一的官方說明。
此外，針對亞太地區公共衛生環境對跨國商務往來的潛在影響，部分歷史與背景資料顯示，過去亞太地區在應對突發性公共衛生事件時，曾採取嚴格的體溫檢測與預防性隔離措施，這些過往的防疫經驗是否會成為未來跨國技術交流與人員流動的變數，仍待相關政策進一步明朗化。目前各界對於該計畫的預期效益雖保持正面態度，但對於具體執行層面的技術細節與跨國合作的法規適應性，仍處於觀察階段。
此項計畫對亞太地區的產業影響深遠。首先，在科技產業鏈方面，高通的介入將有助於強化日本、新加坡與南韓在AI硬體與軟體整合上的優勢，進而鞏固亞太地區在全球AI供應鏈中的地位。其次，在政策層面，該計畫可能促使各國政府重新審視其對於AI新創企業的扶持政策，並進一步推動區域內的數位經濟協定。
對於旅遊與航空產業而言，隨著AI技術的匯入，未來在智慧機場管理、旅客動線最佳化及預測性維護等方面，將有更多創新的解決方案出現，這也與《Travel Daily News Asia》長期關注的產業數位化趨勢不謀而合，顯示出AI技術正在從單純的研發層面，逐步滲透至各類民生與基礎設施產業。
儘管市場對於高通的AI計畫寄予厚望，但分析人士提醒，不應過度解讀單一企業計畫對整體區域經濟的立即性拉動效果。首先，AI技術的發展高度依賴於各國在地的人才儲備與資料隱私法規，日本、新加坡與南韓在這些領域各有其法律架構與市場特性，單一計畫難以完全克服各國間的制度差異。其次，全球經濟環境的波動以及地緣政治對於高科技產業的限制，均可能對計畫的執行進度造成影響。
因此，在評估此計畫的成功機率時，必須考量到技術研發的週期性與市場接受度，避免將其視為解決所有產業困境的萬靈丹，應將其定位為推動區域數位轉型的重要催化劑，而非單一的決定性因素。
後續觀察重點將集中於該計畫在未來六個月內的具體成效，特別是首批獲得扶持的新創企業名單及其研發成果。此外，隨著計畫深入，高通是否會將合作範圍擴充套件至亞太其他國家，以及各國政府如何針對AI技術的倫理與安全規範進行跨國對接，將是評估該計畫長期影響力的關鍵指標。媒體與產業界應持續關注相關技術轉移的透明度，以及該計畫如何與現有的亞太區域經貿架構（如RCEP或CPTPP框架下的數位貿易章節）產生聯動，這將決定高通此舉是否能真正達成促進亞太AI生態系永續發展的目標。
