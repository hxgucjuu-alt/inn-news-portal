  # NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang’s Sudden Resignation Sparks Internal Turmoil and Global Tech Industry Concern
  
  > NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang’s sudden resignation has triggered a strong backlash from internal staff, with over a thousand engineers signing a petition demanding that the board of directors conduct a public investigation. This event not only threatens the stability of the semiconductor industry but also stands in stark contrast to the performative culture observed at the climate summit.
  
  NVIDIA co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang officially resigned from all positions on November 12, 2026. This decision, which shocked the tech world, immediately triggered unprecedented turmoil within the company. According to reports, Huang’s sudden departure was not announced in advance; rather, internal staff sensed something was wrong hours before the board’s official announcement. Subsequently, senior engineers launched a petition in the company’s internal suggestion box, demanding that the board release a complete and independent investigation report and include the genuine opinions of all employees regarding whether the CEO should remain in his post.
  
  As of now, the petition system has accumulated over 1,600 comments, reflecting the dissatisfaction and anxiety of the rank-and-file staff as they strive to stabilize the morale of this global semiconductor leader.
  
  According to multiple media reports, the internal protest at NVIDIA continues to escalate. Employees are not only demanding a transparent investigation process but are also explicitly calling for the "return of our CEO." Such large-scale internal collective petitioning is rare in major multinational tech corporations, reflecting that Huang is viewed not merely as management, but as the soul and core of NVIDIA. Currently, the board of directors has yet to provide a formal response to the employees' collective petition, nor have they publicly explained the specific investigation details that led to Huang’s sudden resignation.
  
  This "Battle for NVIDIA" has evolved beyond an internal management crisis into a major event closely watched by investors and partners throughout the semiconductor supply chain. Its subsequent impact on NVIDIA’s stock price and technical development processes is becoming a focal point for the market.
  
  However, there remain significant discrepancies in the information regarding the specific reasons for Huang’s resignation and the details of the investigation. Media reports indicate that internal employees strongly question the legitimacy of the board’s handling of the process and are demanding the disclosure of an independent investigation report. To date, the board has only issued a resignation announcement and has provided no public explanation regarding the specific motives behind the resignation, or whether it involved internal misconduct or strategic disagreements. This information asymmetry has led to various speculations in the market, including rumors of management strategy disputes, health factors, or external pressures; however, none of these claims have been officially confirmed, leaving them in a gray area awaiting clarification.
  
  This event has had a direct and profound impact on the global semiconductor industry and the tech market. As the absolute leader in the AI chip sector, the personnel change of NVIDIA’s CEO directly affects the development pace of the global AI industry. If the internal turmoil continues to expand, it threatens to affect the stability of NVIDIA’s GPU supply chain, subsequently impacting capital expenditure and technical development progress across the global tech industry.
  
  Meanwhile, the focus of global public discourse has recently shifted to the COP31 climate summit. However, the event has been characterized by a "photo-op over agenda" performative culture. This phenomenon, which reduces climate action to mere formality, stands in stark contrast to the pragmatic engineering culture within NVIDIA, prompting deep reflection on whether the global elite can translate slogans into concrete policies.
  
  Opinions on this event remain significantly divided. Some analysts believe that the NVIDIA employees' petition demonstrates a defense of corporate culture and leadership, serving as an embodiment of corporate democratization. Others, however, caution against over-interpreting personnel changes at a single company, lest one ignore the complexities of the global macroeconomy and industrial structure. Furthermore, observations regarding COP31 suggest that personal branding under the spotlight often masks a lack of local policy implementation and engineering execution in climate action.
  
  These perspectives remind observers that whether it concerns the internal governance of tech giants or global climate governance, the focus must return to substantive data and transparent decision-making mechanisms, rather than relying on emotional petitions or superficial performances to solve problems.

Furthermore, NVIDIA’s succession plan and future strategic layout following Huang’s departure will be key to determining the stability of the global semiconductor market. Regarding climate issues, the focus will be on whether COP31 can move beyond its noisy performative culture to truly produce binding local policies. Although these two events differ in nature, both reflect an urgent need for transparency and substantive performance in the fields of corporate governance and public policy. Over the coming weeks, the tech world and the international community will closely monitor the trajectory of these two major issues.

# 輝達執行長黃仁勳閃辭引爆內部動盪與全球科技界高度關注

> 輝達執行長黃仁勳閃電辭職引發內部基層強烈反彈，逾千名工程師連署要求董事會公開調查，此事件不僅衝擊半導體產業穩定，亦與氣候大會的展演文化形成強烈對比。


輝達（NVIDIA）共同創辦人兼執行長黃仁勳於 2026 年 11 月 12 日正式辭去所有職務，此一震撼科技界的決策，隨即在公司內部引發前所未有的動盪。根據相關報導，黃仁勳的閃電離職並非早有預告，而是在董事會公告發布前數小時，內部基層便已察覺異狀。隨後，資深工程師於公司內部建議箱發起請願，要求董事會必須公開完整的獨立調查報告，並納入全體員工對於執行長留任與否的真實意見。
截至目前，該請願系統已累積超過 1,600 則留言，顯示基層對於此項倉促決策的不滿與焦慮，力求穩定這家全球半導體龍頭的軍心。
綜合多家媒體報導，輝達內部的抗議行動正持續升溫，員工不僅要求透明化的調查程式，更直接表達了「還我執行長」的訴求。這種大規模的內部集體請願，在大型跨國科技企業中實屬罕見，反映出黃仁勳在輝達內部不僅是管理層，更被視為公司的靈魂核心。目前，董事會方面尚未針對員工的集體請願做出正式回應，亦未公開說明導致黃仁勳閃辭的具體調查內容。
這場「輝達保衛戰」目前已不僅是公司內部的管理危機，更演變為半導體產業鏈中，投資人與合作夥伴高度關注的重大事件，其後續對於輝達股價與技術研發程式的影響，正成為市場焦點。
然而，關於黃仁勳辭職的具體原因與調查細節，目前各方資訊仍存在顯著差異。媒體報導指出，內部員工強烈質疑董事會處理程式的正當性，並要求公開獨立調查報告，但截至目前，董事會僅發布了辭職公告，對於辭職背後的具體動機、是否涉及內部違規或戰略分歧，均未給予公開說明。這種資訊的不對稱，導致市場上出現多種臆測，包括經營策略之爭、健康因素或外部壓力等傳言，但這些說法均未獲得官方證實，屬於尚待釐清的灰色地帶。
此事件對全球半導體產業與科技市場產生了直接且深遠的影響。輝達作為人工智慧晶片領域的絕對霸主，其執行長的人事變動直接牽動全球 AI 產業的發展節奏。若內部動盪持續擴大，恐將影響輝達在 GPU 供應鏈的穩定性，進而波及全球科技產業的資本支出與技術開發進度。
與此同時，全球公共議題的焦點亦在近期轉向 COP31 氣候大會，然而與會現場卻出現了「重拍照、輕議程」的展演文化，這種將氣候行動流於形式的現象，與輝達內部務實的工程師文化形成了強烈對比，引發外界對於全球精英階層是否能將口號落實為具體政策的深刻反思。
針對此事件，各界觀點存在顯著分歧。部分分析認為，輝達員工的請願行動展現了對企業文化與領導力的捍衛，是企業民主化的體現；但亦有觀點提醒，不應過度解讀單一企業的人事變動，以免忽略全球宏觀經濟與產業結構的複雜性。此外，針對 COP31 的觀察則指出，鎂光燈下的個人品牌經營，往往掩蓋了氣候行動在在地政策與工程落實上的匱乏。
這些觀點提醒觀察者，無論是科技巨頭的內部治理，還是全球性的氣候治理，皆需回歸實質的資料與透明的決策機制，而非僅靠情緒性的請願或表面化的展演來解決問題。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於輝達董事會是否會回應員工訴求，以及是否會公開相關調查報告以平息內部怒火。此外，黃仁勳離職後，輝達的接班計畫與未來戰略佈局，將是決定全球半導體市場穩定性的關鍵。在氣候議題方面，觀察重點則在於 COP31 是否能從喧嘩的展演文化中脫離，真正產出具有約束力的在地政策。這兩起事件雖然性質不同，但皆反映了當前全球在企業治理與公共政策領域，對於透明度與實質績效的迫切需求。未來幾週，科技界與國際社會將密切關注這兩大議題的發展走向。
