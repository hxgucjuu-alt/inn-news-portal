  # Global Tech Industry Shift: The Dual Challenges of AI Development Slowdown and Interactive Commerce Technology
  
  > The global technology industry is facing a significant pivot. On one hand, AI giants are calling for a slowdown in development to mitigate risks, impacting the outlook for market IPOs. On the other hand, the application of interactive commerce technology continues to deepen, with Globant and TCI Entertainment announcing a strategic partnership.
  
  The global technology and artificial intelligence industries reached a critical turning point in mid-September 2026. As the core hub of global technological development, the United States is currently facing both adjustments in regulatory policy and technological innovation in business models. In the field of artificial intelligence, industry leaders including OpenAI and Anthropic have recently publicly called for a slowdown in AI development programs, aiming to address potential catastrophic risks and comply with stricter regulatory frameworks. This move is not only an adjustment of technical strategy but also has a ripple effect on global capital markets, directly impacting the performance of tech stocks and corporate initial public offering (IPO) plans.
  
  At the same time, the digital commerce technology sector is showing sustained momentum. Global technology services giant Globant officially announced on September 16 that it has signed a Master Services Agreement (MSA) with TCI Entertainment, indicating that the industry remains actively committed to the deepening and application of interactive commerce technology despite facing macroeconomic uncertainty.
  
  According to various media reports, the slowdown in the "AI race" within the tech industry has become a market focus. Washington policy analyst Ed Mills pointed out that tech giants are proactively requesting a slowdown in development speed to balance industry growth with social safety risks; however, this move has sparked investor concerns about whether the AI industry boom will peak prematurely. Meanwhile, in the digital experience sector, the collaboration between Globant and TCI Entertainment has established a clear commercial path. The two parties will leverage TCI Entertainment's patented interactive commerce technology to seamlessly integrate commercial transaction mechanisms into games, virtual environments, and various digital experiences.
  
  The core objective of this partnership is to provide new transaction scenarios for brands, retailers, and game developers, reflecting the tech industry's continued pursuit of commercial monetization breakthroughs in application layers while shifting its AI R&D strategy.
  
  Although the aforementioned events both occurred in the technology sector, there are differences in information transparency and the certainty of future developments. Regarding the issue of the AI development slowdown, there is currently no unified standard for the "extent of the slowdown" or "regulatory details." There is a gap between the views of Washington policy analysts and the panic among market investors, and the specific content of regulatory bills remains in a state of dynamic adjustment. In contrast, the cooperation agreement between Globant and TCI Entertainment has already been signed as a Master Services Agreement, providing a clear commercial contractual basis; however, the actual conversion rate and market acceptance of this technology in virtual environments will still depend on the subsequent deployment progress of digital experience platforms.
  
  Currently, the market holds differing opinions on whether the AI slowdown will lead to long-term weakness in tech stocks and whether interactive commerce can become a new engine for revenue growth.
  
  These trends have had a profound impact on the North American technology industry and policy environment. The call for an AI industry slowdown may force the U.S. government to re-examine relevant technology policies, particularly the trade-off between national security and industrial competitiveness. If the pace of AI development slows, it will directly impact tech startups that rely on high-growth expectations, leading to a cooling of the IPO market and subsequently affecting the venture capital ecosystem in Silicon Valley and North America as a whole. On the other hand, the popularization of interactive commerce technology may change the digital transformation path of the North American retail and entertainment industries, prompting companies to invest more resources into infrastructure for virtual spaces to meet the growing consumer demand for immersive shopping experiences.
  
  The intertwining of these two forces indicates that the North American tech sector is in a transitional phase, moving from "pursuing extreme speed" to "pursuing depth of application and security."
  
  However, there are significant differences in perspectives regarding these industry changes. Some market observers believe that the AI development slowdown is a necessary process for the industry to mature, effectively avoiding the risk of a bubble, and should not be over-interpreted as an industry recession. But other commentators have pointed out that if regulations are too stringent, it may lead to the outflow of technological innovation to regions with more lenient regulatory environments. Regarding interactive commerce technology, although its development prospects are promising, consumer transaction privacy and security in virtual environments will be major challenges in the future. If these technological applications lack appropriate legal norms, they may trigger new digital governance issues.
  
  Therefore, market analysts remind investors to distinguish between "long-term value brought by technological innovation" and "market volatility caused by short-term policy adjustments," avoiding overly pessimistic judgments about the overall tech industry outlook based on single events.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on three aspects: first, the specific policy direction of U.S. regulatory agencies following the AI development slowdown, particularly whether Congress will propose mandatory legislation regarding AI risk management; second, whether tech giants will shift their R&D focus to application layers such as interactive commerce to maintain revenue growth after slowing down development; and finally, the actual outcomes of the Globant and TCI Entertainment partnership, including user penetration and transaction conversion data for their technology in virtual environments.
  
  These indicators will serve as key references for assessing whether the North American tech industry can successfully achieve business model transformation under regulatory pressure, and will also be an important weather vane for observing the trend of the global tech industry in the second half of the year.

# 全球科技產業變局：AI開發放緩與互動商務技術的雙重挑戰

> 全球科技產業近期面臨顯著轉向，一方面AI巨擘呼籲放緩開發以降低風險，衝擊市場IPO前景；另一方面，互動式商務技術應用持續深化，Globant與TCI Entertainment宣佈建立策略合作。


全球科技與人工智慧產業在2026年9月中旬迎來關鍵轉折。美國作為全球科技發展的核心場域，近期同時面臨監管政策的調整與商業模式的技術革新。以人工智慧領域為例，包括OpenAI與Anthropic在內的業界領袖，近期公開呼籲應放緩AI開發程式，旨在應對潛在的災難性風險並配合更嚴格的監管框架。此舉不僅是技術策略的調整，更對全球資本市場產生連鎖效應，直接影響科技股表現及企業首次公開發行（IPO）的規劃。
與此同時，數位商業技術領域則展現出持續擴張的動能，全球科技服務巨擘Globant於9月16日正式宣佈與TCI Entertainment簽署主服務協議（MSA），顯示業界在面臨宏觀經濟不確定性時，仍積極投入互動式商務技術的深化與應用。
根據多方媒體報導，科技產業的「AI競賽」放緩已成為市場焦點。華盛頓政策分析師Ed Mills指出，科技巨頭主動要求放緩開發速度，是為了平衡產業發展與社會安全風險，但此舉引發了投資人對於AI產業榮景是否將提前觸頂的擔憂。與此同時，在數位體驗領域，Globant與TCI Entertainment的合作則確立了明確的商業路徑。雙方將透過TCI Entertainment擁有的專利互動式商務技術，將商業交易機制無縫整合至遊戲、虛擬環境及各類數位體驗中。
這項合作的核心目標在於為品牌商、零售商及遊戲開發商提供新的交易場景，反映出科技產業在AI研發策略轉向的同時，仍持續在應用端尋求商業變現的技術突破。
儘管上述事件均發生於科技領域，但在資訊透明度與未來發展的確定性上仍存在差異。關於AI開發放緩的議題，目前各界對於「放緩幅度」與「監管細節」尚無統一標準，華盛頓政策分析師的看法與市場投資人的恐慌情緒之間存在落差，且具體的監管法案內容仍處於動態調整中。相對地，Globant與TCI Entertainment的合作協議已簽署主服務協議，具備明確的商業合約基礎，但該技術在虛擬環境中的實際轉化率與市場接受度，仍需視後續數位體驗平臺的部署進度而定。
目前市場對於AI放緩是否會導致科技股長期疲軟，與互動式商務能否成為新的營收成長引擎，仍持有不同意見。
這些趨勢對北美科技產業與政策環境產生了深遠影響。AI產業的放緩呼籲，可能迫使美國政府重新審視相關科技政策，特別是在國家安全與產業競爭力之間的權衡。若AI開發速度減緩，將直接衝擊依賴高成長預期的科技新創企業，導致IPO市場冷卻，進而影響矽谷及北美整體的創投生態。另一方面，互動式商務技術的普及，則可能改變北美零售與娛樂產業的數位轉型路徑，促使企業將更多資源投入虛擬空間的基礎建設，以因應消費者對於沈浸式購物體驗日益增長的需求。
這兩股力量交織，顯示出北美科技界正處於從「追求極致速度」轉向「追求應用深度與安全」的過渡階段。
然而，針對上述產業變動，各界觀點存在顯著分歧。部分市場觀察家認為，AI開發放緩是產業走向成熟的必要過程，能有效避免泡沫化風險，不應過度解讀為產業衰退。但亦有評論指出，若監管過於嚴苛，可能導致技術創新外流至監管環境較為寬鬆的地區。針對互動式商務技術，雖然其發展前景看好，但消費者在虛擬環境中的交易隱私與安全保障，將是未來面臨的重大挑戰。這些技術應用若缺乏適當的法律規範，恐引發新的數位治理問題。
因此，市場分析師提醒，投資人應區分「技術創新帶來的長期價值」與「短期政策調整造成的市場震盪」，避免因單一事件而對整體科技產業前景做出過於悲觀的判斷。
後續觀察重點將集中於三個面向：首先是美國監管機構針對AI開發放緩後的具體政策走向，特別是國會是否會針對AI風險管理提出強制性法案；其次是科技巨頭在放緩開發後，是否會將研發重心轉移至互動式商務等應用層面，以維持營收成長；最後則是Globant與TCI Entertainment合作案的實際落地成果，包含其技術在虛擬環境中的使用者滲透率與交易轉換資料。
這些指標將成為評估北美科技產業能否在監管壓力下，成功實現商業模式轉型的關鍵參考，亦是觀察全球科技產業下半年走勢的重要風向球。
