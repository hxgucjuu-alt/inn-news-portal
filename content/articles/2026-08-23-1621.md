  # Lisbon Glória Funicular Fatal Accident: 16 Dead, 21 Injured, Maintenance Records Become Focus of Investigation
  
  > A derailment and collision with a building involving Lisbon's Glória funicular on September 3 has left at least 16 people dead and 21 injured. Authorities have launched an investigation, with maintenance records and safety inspection procedures drawing widespread attention.
  
  A serious public transport accident occurred in Portugal's capital Lisbon on September 3, 2025, when a carriage on the famous tourist route "Glória Funicular" (Elevador da Glória) suddenly derailed on a section near Avenida da Liberdade, slid out of control downhill, and ultimately crashed into a building beside the track, killing at least 16 people and injuring 21 others. According to a report by the British Broadcasting Corporation (BBC), the incident took place in the afternoon of that day. The route is one of the city's most iconic tourist attractions and normally carries large numbers of visitors and commuters.
  
  The Irish Independent (Independent.ie) noted that following the accident, Lisbon immediately entered a period of mourning. The Portuguese government declared national mourning and pledged a thorough investigation into the cause of the incident.
  
  According to multiple international media reports, the accident occurred on Wednesday, September 3, when a funicular carriage suddenly left the track while in operation, hurtled down the steep slope, and only came to a stop after slamming into a building. The Guardian reported 16 deaths and 21 injuries, with several of the injured in serious condition and taken to local hospitals in Lisbon. Reuters, citing Portuguese authorities, confirmed at least 16 fatalities and 22 injuries, a figure slightly different from other media outlets, indicating that casualty statistics were still being updated.
  
  The BBC reported that firefighters, police, and multiple ambulances arrived at the scene promptly after the incident. Surrounding streets were temporarily sealed off, and rescue workers searched the wreckage for trapped passengers.
  
  Regarding the cause of the accident, reports from various media outlets currently differ, and official investigation findings are still pending. The Irish Independent reported that questions have been mounting since the accident, with attention focused on whether routine maintenance and safety inspections on the funicular line were properly carried out. The report cited sources claiming that the route had not undergone daily routine maintenance since late last month. If true, this claim would directly implicate the operator's safety management responsibilities. Reuters, however, focused on the technical investigation, noting that Portuguese prosecutors and the traffic accident investigation committee had become involved and would conduct a comprehensive examination of the vehicles' mechanical structure, track conditions, braking systems, and operating procedures.
  
  The BBC reported that the operator said it would fully cooperate with the investigation but had not yet responded to specific details regarding maintenance records.
  
  The accident has dealt a major blow to Lisbon's local community and tourism industry. The Glória Funicular, in operation since 1885, is one of Lisbon's oldest funicular lines and an important transport link connecting the Baixa and Bairro Alto districts, carrying a considerable number of passengers daily. Following the accident, the Lisbon city government announced an indefinite suspension of the line's operations and ordered emergency safety inspections of other funiculars and mountain railways of the same type within the city. The Guardian reported that the accident has not only sparked domestic debate in Portugal over budgets for upgrading and maintaining aging public transport infrastructure, but may also affect foreign tourists' confidence in travel safety in Lisbon.
  
  The Portuguese Tourism Association issued a statement expressing condolences over the accident and emphasizing its commitment to working with the government to strengthen safety oversight of tourism facilities.
  
  Regarding the cause of the accident and the attribution of responsibility, accounts currently vary, and outside observers should not jump to conclusions prematurely. Some media outlets and union representatives have questioned whether the operator may have cut maintenance staffing and frequency to save costs, creating gaps in safety inspections. However, engineering experts have also pointed out that funicular systems are highly mechanized modes of transport, and a single component failure or operational error could cause an accident; it will be necessary to wait for the investigation report to determine whether systemic negligence was involved. In addition, casualty figures vary across media reports, ranging from 16 to at least 16 dead, with injured counts differing between 21 and 22, reflecting the confusion of information in the immediate aftermath and the absence of final confirmed figures from the authorities.
  
  It is worth noting that none of the reports so far have mentioned whether any foreign nationals were among the casualties; this information still awaits further clarification from Portuguese authorities.

In addition, the ongoing treatment of injured passengers, compensation and bereavement arrangements for the families of the deceased, and when the Glória Funicular may resume operations are all matters of continued public attention.

# 里斯本榮耀纜車致命事故：16死21傷，維修紀錄成調查焦點

> 葡萄牙里斯本榮耀纜車於9月3日發生出軌撞樓事故，造成至少16人死亡、21人受傷，當局已展開調查，維修保養紀錄與安全檢查程式成為各界關注焦點。

葡萄牙首都里斯本於2025年9月3日發生嚴重公共運輸事故，一列行駛於著名觀光路線「榮耀纜車」（Elevador da Glória）的車廂，在鄰近自由大道（Avenida da Liberdade）的路段突然出軌並失控向下滑行，最終撞上路旁建築物，造成至少16人死亡、21人受傷。根據英國廣播公司（BBC）報導，事發時間為當天下午，該路線是當地最具代表性的觀光景點之一，平日乘載大量遊客與通勤民眾。
愛爾蘭獨立報（Independent.ie）指出，事故發生後，里斯本隨即進入哀悼狀態，葡萄牙政府宣佈全國哀悼，並承諾徹查事故原因。
綜合多家國際媒體報導，事故發生於9月3日星期三，一列纜車車廂在行駛過程中突然脫離軌道，沿著陡峭坡道向下衝撞，最終撞上建築物才停下。英國《衛報》（The Guardian）報導，事故造成16人死亡、21人受傷，傷者中多人傷勢嚴重，已送往里斯本當地醫院救治。路透社（Reuters）則引述葡萄牙當局說法，確認死亡人數至少16人，另有22人受傷，數字與其他媒體略有出入，顯示事故傷亡統計仍在更新中。
BBC報導指出，事發後消防隊、警察與多輛救護車迅速抵達現場，周邊街道一度全面封鎖，救援人員在殘骸中搜尋受困乘客。
關於事故原因，目前各家媒體報導仍存在差異，尚待官方調查確認。愛爾蘭獨立報報導，事故發生後，外界質疑聲浪逐漸升高，焦點集中在纜車路線的日常維護與安全檢查是否落實。報導引述訊息人士說法，稱該路線自上個月底以來，就沒有進行每日例行維護，這項說法若屬實，將直接衝擊營運單位的安全管理責任。然而，路透社報導則側重於技術層面的調查，指出葡萄牙檢方與交通事故調查委員會已介入，將針對車輛機械結構、軌道狀況、煞車系統以及操作程式進行全面檢驗。
BBC則報導，營運單位表示將全力配合調查，但尚未對維護紀錄的具體細節做出回應。
這起事故對里斯本當地社會與觀光產業造成重大衝擊。榮耀纜車自1885年啟用，是里斯本歷史最悠久的纜車路線之一，也是連線龐巴爾下城（Baixa）與上城（Bairro Alto）的重要交通工具，每日載客量相當可觀。事故發生後，里斯本市政府宣佈無限期暫停該路線營運，並對市內其他同型別纜車與登山鐵路進行緊急安全檢查。《衛報》報導指出，這起事故不僅引發葡萄牙國內對於老舊公共運輸設施更新與維護預算的討論，也可能影響外國觀光客對里斯本旅遊安全的信心。
葡萄牙旅遊業協會發表宣告，對事故表達哀悼，同時強調將配合政府強化旅遊設施的安全監管。
對於事故原因與責任歸屬，目前各方說法不一，外界不宜過早下定論。部分媒體與工會代表質疑，營運單位可能為了節省成本而縮減維護人力與頻率，導致安全檢查出現漏洞；但也有工程專家指出，纜車系統屬於高度機械化的運輸工具，單一零件故障或操作失誤都可能釀成意外，必須等待調查報告出爐，才能確認是否涉及系統性疏失。此外，關於死亡人數的統計，不同媒體報導從16人到至少16人不等，傷者人數也有21人與22人的差異，顯示事故發生初期資訊混亂，官方尚未公佈最終確認數字。
值得注意的是，目前所有報導均未提及是否有外國籍乘客傷亡，這部分資訊仍有待葡萄牙當局進一步說明。
後續觀察重點包括：葡萄牙官方調查報告的公佈時間與內容，特別是纜車的煞車系統、軌道狀況與維護紀錄是否成為事故關鍵因素；里斯本市政府是否會對全市老舊運輸設施進行全面體檢，並提出具體改善方案；以及這起事故是否會引發葡萄牙國內對於公共運輸投資不足的檢討，進而影響相關預算分配與安全法規修訂。此外，受傷乘客的後續救治狀況、罹難者家屬的賠償與撫卹安排，以及榮耀纜車何時能夠恢復營運，都是外界持續關注的焦點。
