  # Global AI Industry Dynamics: New Regulations for Chinese Enterprises Going Global and Trends in Transnational Tech Cooperation
  
  > With the globalization of artificial intelligence technology, Chinese companies such as DeepSeek and MiniMax have been approved for international expansion, subject to revisions in the catalog of technologies restricted for export. Meanwhile, the global insurance industry and transnational tech diplomacy are actively integrating AI to strengthen risk management and supply chain resilience.
  
  Artificial intelligence (AI) technology is currently in a critical phase of global strategic deployment, not only transforming the operational models of traditional industries but also becoming a core issue in international diplomacy and economic cooperation. Recently, the scope of AI applications has extended from simple software development to insurance risk management, personal assistant services, and national-level technology supply chain cooperation. In the Asia-Pacific region, as AI technology iterates, relevant regulatory frameworks and market access standards are being adjusted accordingly, exerting an influence that extends beyond individual nations and profoundly impacts the cross-border flow of the global technology industry.
  
  According to multiple media reports, Chinese artificial intelligence companies DeepSeek and MiniMax have been officially approved to operate in the global market and have completed the necessary registration and authorization arrangements. This development is considered to be closely related to the recent revision process of the "Catalog of Technologies Prohibited or Restricted from Export" by China's Ministry of Commerce and Ministry of Science and Technology. The draft revision covers 23 key AI technologies, including large models, intelligent agents, computing clusters, and information processing.
  
  At the same time, Chinese AI startup Today Ai has received joint investment from Tencent and Ant Group, with total funding exceeding $200 million. This highlights the intense competition among Chinese tech giants in the personal assistant AI product sector and their attempts to push such products into the global market.
  
  However, the specific details regarding the regulations for AI technology exports remain to be seen. Although state media reported that these companies have been approved for international expansion, the revision of the "Catalog of Technologies Prohibited or Restricted from Export" is still in the public comment phase, and the specific export restriction thresholds and review mechanisms for relevant technologies have not yet been finalized. Furthermore, although Today Ai has launched testing and downloads for both its global and Chinese versions, its data privacy standards and compliance in different markets remain a focus of international attention.
  
  On the other hand, while the benefits of integrating AI into the insurance industry have become apparent in risk assessment and proactive prevention, its transnational operations still face challenges regarding technical standards and cross-border data transmission regulations.
  
  In terms of industry impact, the application of AI has spread from the consumer end to the professional services sector. For example, companies such as Liberty Mutual are using AI to provide early warnings for construction risks, shifting insurance services from passive claims processing to proactive prevention, thereby improving overall operational efficiency. This trend is consistent with the development path of global trade technology, where companies need a more comprehensive understanding of the roles of AI infrastructure, data centers, and automated devices in international business.
  
  For Taiwan, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs has also recently emphasized that it will combine its semiconductor industry advantages to deepen cooperation with international partners such as the UK in fields including quantum technology, artificial intelligence, and aerospace, aiming to jointly build a more resilient global technology supply chain.
  
  Despite the rapid development of the AI industry, there remains a cautious attitude toward over-reliance on technology and potential risks. Experts remind us that while the long-term benefits of AI are significant, companies must still consider regional consumer preferences and legal regulations when dealing with complex geopolitical and economic situations. Furthermore, investment decisions in technology should not be viewed merely as simple software upgrades but should be incorporated into a framework of global business environment analysis. The pursuit of emerging AI products should also avoid over-interpreting their immediate impact on the market landscape, as the maturity of technology and the implementation of regulations still require time to be verified.
  
  Key points for future observation include the execution details following the official release of China's revised "Catalog of Technologies Prohibited or Restricted from Export," as well as the compliance performance of these AI companies in overseas markets. At the same time, the progress of cooperation between Taiwan and the international community in the fields of AI and semiconductors, as well as the effectiveness of risk management in the insurance industry following the integration of AI technology, will be important indicators for observing the resilience of the global technology supply chain and industrial transformation. How governments balance national security and market openness while promoting technological innovation will continue to influence the development trajectory of the global AI industry.

# 全球 AI 產業動態：中國企業出海新規與跨國科技合作趨勢

> 隨著人工智慧技術全球化發展，中國企業如 DeepSeek 與 MiniMax 獲準出海，並受相關出口技術目錄修訂影響；同時，全球保險業與跨國科技外交正積極匯入 AI 以強化風險管理與供應鏈韌性。


人工智慧（AI）技術正處於全球戰略佈局的關鍵期，不僅改變了傳統產業的營運模式，亦成為各國外交與經貿合作的核心議題。近期，AI 應用範疇已從單純的軟體開發延伸至保險風險管理、個人助理服務，以及國家級的科技供應鏈合作。在亞太地區，隨著 AI 技術的迭代，相關監管法規與市場准入標準亦隨之調整，影響力不僅限於單一國家，更對全球科技產業的跨境流動產生深遠影響。
根據多家媒體報導，中國人工智慧企業 DeepSeek 與 MiniMax 已正式獲準在全球市場營運，並已完成相關登記與授權安排。此項進展被認為與中國商務部及科技部近期針對《中國禁止出口限制出口技術目錄》的修訂程式密切相關。該修訂草案涵蓋了包括大模型、智慧體、算力叢集及資訊處理等 23 項關鍵 AI 技術。
與此同時，中國 AI 新創公司 Today Ai 亦在資本市場獲得騰訊與螞蟻集團的共同注資，累計募資金額突破 2 億美元，顯示出中國科技巨頭在個人助理 AI 產品領域的激烈競爭，並試圖將此類產品推向全球市場。
然而，目前關於 AI 技術出口的具體規範細節仍有待觀察。雖然官方媒體報導了企業獲準出海的訊息，但針對《中國禁止出口限制出口技術目錄》的修訂仍處於公開徵求意見階段，相關技術的具體出口限制門檻與審查機制尚未完全定案。此外，雖然 Today Ai 已在全球版與中國版分別啟動測試與下載，但其在不同市場的資料隱私規範與合規性，仍是國際市場關注的焦點。
另一方面，保險業匯入 AI 的效益雖在風險評估與主動預防方面有所顯現，但其跨國運作的技術標準與資料跨境傳輸，仍面臨不同區域法規的挑戰。
在產業影響層面，AI 的應用已從消費端擴散至專業服務領域。例如，利寶互助保險（Liberty Mutual）等業者正透過 AI 進行建築風險的早期預警，將保險服務從被動理賠轉向主動預防，提升整體營運效率。此趨勢與全球貿易科技的發展路徑一致，即企業需更全面地理解 AI 基礎設施、資料中心及自動化裝置在國際商務中的角色。
對於臺灣而言，外交部近期亦強調將結合半導體產業優勢，與英國等國際夥伴在量子科技、人工智慧及航太等領域深化合作，旨在共同打造更具韌性的全球科技供應鏈。
儘管 AI 產業發展迅速，但各界對於技術過度依賴與潛在風險仍持謹慎態度。專家提醒，AI 的長期效益雖然顯著，但在處理複雜的地緣政治與經濟情勢時，企業仍需考量各地區的消費者偏好與法律規範。此外，科技投資的決策不應僅視為單純的軟體升級，更應納入全球商業環境分析的框架中。對於新興 AI 產品的追捧，亦應避免過度解讀其對市場格局的立即影響，畢竟技術的成熟與法規的落地仍需要時間驗證。
後續觀察重點在於中國修訂後的《中國禁止出口限制出口技術目錄》正式發布後的執行細節，以及這些 AI 企業在海外市場的合規表現。同時，臺灣與國際間在 AI 與半導體領域的合作程式，以及保險業在匯入 AI 技術後的風險控管成效，將是未來觀察全球科技供應鏈韌性與產業轉型的重要指標。各國政府在推動科技創新的同時，如何平衡國家安全與市場開放，將持續牽動全球 AI 產業的發展走向。
