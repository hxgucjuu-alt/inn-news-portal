  # The EU Faces Internal and External Geopolitical Challenges: Escalating Security Threats and Policy Divergence
  
  > The European Union is at a critical juncture defined by intersecting crises. Beyond addressing the security shocks caused by Russia's hybrid warfare, internal disagreements over migration policy and external trade strategies are testing European political unity and border stability.
  
  Europe is currently under dual pressure from geopolitical and public security challenges. As the situation in Russia and Ukraine persists, the stability of the European continent faces a severe test. According to various observations, the hybrid tactics employed by Russia against Europe—including cyberattacks, the dissemination of disinformation, and sabotage targeting critical infrastructure—have significantly eroded public trust and political cohesion across European nations. Simultaneously, there remain persistent differences within the EU regarding how to respond to external threats, manage border migration pressures, and adjust trade policies toward China. These factors collectively form the core context of current European public policy and security.
  
  Multiple media sources have confirmed that Europe is currently on high alert. Through hybrid warfare, Russia intends to weaken European support for Ukraine and exploit internal economic pressures to divide the positions of NATO member states. At the diplomatic and security levels, European governments are frequently conducting high-level consultations, attempting to strike a balance between countering external hybrid threats and maintaining the operation of the internal market. Furthermore, the EU is engaged in discussions regarding whether to adopt a tougher stance on external trade policy to protect its own industrial chains, reflecting the complex game Europe plays between pursuing strategic autonomy and maintaining global trade connectivity.
  
  However, there are significant differences in opinion regarding the specific strategies the EU should adopt. Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orbán has repeatedly emphasized that Europe's border security is under severe threat from large-scale migration waves, arguing that the EU must implement more unified and stringent border control measures—a view that contrasts with the positions of EU member states advocating for openness and humanitarianism.
  
  Moreover, regarding whether the EU has the sufficient resolve to adopt tougher trade and diplomatic policies toward Beijing, media analysis indicates that there is no consensus within the EU on the assessment of economic dependence versus geopolitical risk. Some countries fear that overly aggressive policies will cause irreversible damage to their domestic export industries, while others argue that collective action is necessary to counter potential economic coercion. The actual scope of these policy divergences and the final decision-making paths remain in a state of high uncertainty.
  
  The aforementioned challenges have had a direct impact on Europe's public health, social stability, and industrial development. In terms of public health and social resilience, the spread of disinformation through hybrid warfare is no longer just a political issue; it has affected public trust in government pandemic responses, social welfare, and public policy, indirectly weakening society's ability to respond to sudden crises. At the industrial level, European countries are attempting to strengthen the defense capabilities of critical infrastructure through procurement and tendering processes, while seeking to reduce reliance on single external markets for supply chains. However, whether this trend toward protectionism or regionalization will lead to increased operating costs within the European internal market and impact small and medium-sized enterprises remains a focus of industry concern.
  
  Regarding these complex issues, there are clear limitations and differences in interpretation among various parties. Some analyses suggest that European political unity is not as fragile as outsiders expect, and that cooperation on security issues is continuing to deepen. However, other perspectives point out that if migration issues and internal economic inequality cannot be effectively resolved, political polarization in Europe will further deteriorate. Experts caution against viewing the policy stance of a single country as the collective will of the EU, and warn against overstating the destructive power of any single threat to the overall European structure. Currently, EU policy-making remains constrained by the voter structures and economic interests of its member states, and any major policy shift requires a lengthy coordination process.
  
  Future observation will focus on whether the EU can propose a more concrete defense framework against the threat of Russian hybrid warfare during upcoming diplomatic and security meetings in the coming months, and whether EU member states can reach a substantive consensus on migration quotas and border control. Additionally, in response to changes in the global trade environment, how the EU adjusts its procurement policies to implement strategic autonomy and how it handles friction with major trading partners will directly affect Europe's future economic development path. The public and the industrial sector should continue to monitor official EU policy white papers and the subsequent procurement trends of various governments to grasp the latest shifts in European public policy.

# 歐盟面臨內外地緣政治挑戰：安全威脅與政策分歧加劇

> 歐盟正處於多重危機交織的關鍵時刻，不僅需應對俄羅斯混合戰帶來的安全衝擊，內部對於移民政策與對外經貿戰略的分歧，亦考驗著歐洲政治團結與邊境穩定。


歐洲當前正處於地緣政治與公共安全的雙重壓力之下，隨著俄烏局勢持續，歐洲大陸的穩定性面臨嚴峻考驗。根據多方觀察，俄羅斯針對歐洲發動的混合戰術，包括網路攻擊、假訊息散佈以及針對關鍵基礎設施的破壞行動，已對歐洲各國的公眾信任度與政治凝聚力造成顯著侵蝕。與此同時，歐盟內部對於如何應對外部威脅、如何管理邊境移民壓力，以及如何調整對華經貿政策，始終存在不同的聲音，這些因素共同構成了當前歐洲公共政策與安全領域的核心背景。
多個媒體來源共同確認，歐洲目前正處於高度戒備狀態。俄羅斯透過混合戰手段，意圖削弱歐洲各國對烏克蘭的支援，並試圖利用歐洲內部的經濟壓力來分化北約成員國的立場。在外交與安全層面，歐洲各國政府正頻繁進行高層磋商，試圖在應對外部混合威脅與維持內部市場運作之間尋求平衡。此外，歐盟在對外經貿政策上，亦面臨是否應採取更為強硬的立場以保護自身產業鏈的討論，這反映了歐洲在追求戰略自主與維持全球貿易連結之間的複雜博弈。
然而，關於歐盟應採取何種具體策略，各界說法存在顯著差異。匈牙利總理奧爾班（Viktor Orbán）曾多次強調，歐洲的邊境安全正受到大規模移民潮的嚴重威脅，認為歐盟必須採取更為統一且強硬的邊境管控措施，此觀點與部分主張開放與人道主義的歐盟成員國立場相左。
此外，針對歐盟是否具備足夠的底氣對北京採取更強硬的經貿與外交政策，媒體分析指出，歐盟內部對於經濟依賴程度與地緣政治風險的評估並不一致，部分國家擔憂過度強硬的政策將對本國出口產業造成不可逆的損害，而另一些國家則主張必須透過集體行動來應對潛在的經濟脅迫。這些政策分歧的實際影響範圍與最終決策路徑，目前仍處於高度不確定狀態。
上述挑戰對歐洲的公共衛生、社會穩定及產業發展產生了直接影響。在公共衛生與社會韌性方面，混合戰帶來的假訊息傳播已不僅是政治問題，更影響了公眾對政府防疫、社會福利及公共政策的信任，間接削弱了社會面對突發危機的應變能力。在產業層面，歐洲各國正透過採購與招標程式，試圖強化關鍵基礎設施的防禦能力，並尋求在供應鏈上減少對單一外部市場的依賴。然而，這種轉向保護主義或區域化佈局的趨勢，是否會導致歐洲內部市場的運作成本上升，並對中小企業造成衝擊，仍是產業界關注的焦點。
對於這些複雜議題，各方觀點存在明顯的限制與解讀差異。部分分析認為，歐洲的政治團結並未如外界預期般脆弱，各國在安全議題上的合作正持續深化；但也有觀點指出，若無法有效解決移民問題及內部經濟不平等，歐洲的政治極化現象將進一步惡化。專家提醒，不應將單一國家的政策立場視為歐盟的集體意志，亦不應過度誇大單一威脅對歐洲整體架構的破壞力。目前，歐盟的政策制定仍深受各成員國選民結構與經濟利益的牽制，任何重大政策的轉向都需要漫長的協調過程。
後續觀察重點在於歐盟在未來數月的外交與安全會議中，能否針對俄羅斯混合戰威脅提出更具體的防禦框架，以及歐盟各國能否在移民配額與邊境管控議題上達成實質共識。此外，針對全球經貿環境的變動，歐盟如何調整其採購政策以落實戰略自主，以及如何處理與主要貿易夥伴之間的摩擦，將直接影響歐洲未來的經濟發展路徑。公眾與產業界應持續關注歐盟官方發布的政策白皮書及各國政府的後續採購動向，以掌握歐洲公共政策的最新風向。
