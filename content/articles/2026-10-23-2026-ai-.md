  # 2026 Eurasia Tech and Industry Summit: AI-Driven Cybersecurity, Smart Transportation, and Global Governance Challenges
  
  > In late October 2026, global multinational industries are focusing on the Eurasian region. From the CISO Summit in Taipei and the ITS World Congress in Gangneung, South Korea, to the AIBC Eurasia Summit in Dubai, stakeholders are actively addressing the challenges of digital transformation, supply chain security, and transnational transportation governance in the AI era.
  
  In October 2026, industry summits held across the globe marked a high level of interaction in digital governance and infrastructure within the Eurasian region.
  
  In Taiwan, the "HITCON CISO Summit 2026," hosted by the Taiwan Hacker Association, kicked off on October 21 at the Taipei Marriott Hotel. It brought together cybersecurity decision-makers from 14 countries and regions to explore AI-driven cyber defense and semiconductor supply chain security. Simultaneously, at the "2026 ITS World Congress" in Gangneung, South Korea, the focus was on smart transportation and data governance. Taiwan’s Secom Group subsidiary, GoSmart, officially showcased its "Smart Parking Management Data Center" to the international community, symbolizing the export of Taiwan's AIoT software and hardware integration capabilities.
  
  Additionally, the AIBC Eurasia Summit was held in Dubai, aiming to connect technology and blockchain leaders in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region, highlighting the active state of transnational technical exchange and industrial collaboration across Eurasia.
  
  According to public information from various parties, multiple summits have listed "AI empowerment" as a core topic. The HITCON CISO Summit confirmed that CISOs from the world's top 15 industries are collectively facing the evolution of threats brought by AI, particularly regarding defense strategies for the semiconductor supply chain. In the field of smart transportation, Deputy Minister of Transportation and Communications Chen Yen-po visited South Korea to confirm that Taiwan's smart parking technology has achieved international competitiveness and has realized the digital management of roadside parking through a data center model.
  
  Meanwhile, workplace survey reports indicate that while enterprises pursue efficiency during the integration of AI, nearly 60% of employees feel mentally exhausted due to AI. This has made "psychological safety" an indispensable key to corporate transformation, showing the close connection between technological innovation and human resource management.
  
  Although several technological advancements have been confirmed, there remain different interpretations and information gaps regarding the specific paths for "global governance" among various sources. For example, HITCON focuses on enterprise-level cybersecurity practices and technical defense, while the ITS World Congress emphasizes infrastructure collaboration between governments and private enterprises. Furthermore, regarding the regional political interaction between Iran and the United States, some sources indicate that both sides have reached a consensus on certain conditions, yet clear explanations on key issues remain lacking. This contrasts with the rapid expansion of the tech industry, indicating that a high degree of uncertainty remains regarding geopolitical stability in Eurasia.
  
  Moreover, regarding the specific policy impact of the AIBC Eurasia Summit, most discussions remain at the level of industrial networking and business growth, with no clear transnational regulatory standards having emerged yet.
  
  These industrial activities have profound implications for the Eurasian region. First, in terms of supply chain security, as a semiconductor hub, the enhancement of Taiwan's cybersecurity defense capabilities directly affects the stability of the global tech industry. Second, in the field of smart transportation, Taiwan's data governance model provides a reference case for digital transformation in other Eurasian cities. However, if enterprises ignore employee psychological safety and workplace equity (diversity and inclusion) while pursuing AI transformation, it may lead to a decline in transformation efficiency. Therefore, the integration of technology and the reshaping of corporate culture have become a dual-track strategy for enterprise transformation in the Eurasian region.
  
  Regarding different perspectives and limitations, it is necessary to note the gap between the "optimistic outlook" presented at technology exhibitions and the reality of geopolitics. Although industry summits demonstrate powerful technological energy, as mentioned in the sources, geopolitical tensions and the progress of international negotiations may still hinder technical exchange and infrastructure promotion. Furthermore, reports released by various conferences are mostly provided by organizers or related enterprises; their data reflects the perspectives of specific industries and should not be viewed as comprehensive conclusions on global governance. Regarding the issue of workplace exhaustion caused by AI, enterprises must adopt more inclusive preventive measures when promoting digital transformation, rather than relying solely on technological upgrades.
  
  Subsequent observation points should focus on: First, the follow-up implementation of semiconductor supply chain cybersecurity standards by Taiwanese enterprises after the HITCON summit; second, whether GoSmart can further secure substantive technology export contracts in the Eurasian region following the international exhibition; and third, whether there will be concrete progress in subsequent negotiations between Iran and the United States on pending issues, which will directly affect energy and logistics stability in the Eurasian region. All parties should continue to monitor the policy implementation and business cooperation details following these transnational summits to assess their actual contribution to global governance.

# 2026 歐亞科技與產業高峰會：AI 驅動資安、智慧運輸與全球治理挑戰

> 2026年10月下旬，全球跨國產業聚焦歐亞地區。從臺北的資安長高峰會、韓國江陵的智慧運輸大會，到杜拜的 AIBC 歐亞峰會，各界正積極應對 AI 時代下的數位轉型、供應鏈安全與跨國交通治理挑戰。


2026年10月，全球各地的產業高峰會密集召開，標誌著歐亞地區在數位治理與基礎建設上的高度互動。
在臺灣，由臺灣駭客協會主辦的「HITCON CISO Summit 2026」於10月21日在臺北萬豪酒店登場，匯聚了來自14個國家與地區的資安決策者，探討 AI 驅動下的網路攻防與半導體供應鏈安全；與此同時，在南韓江陵舉行的「2026江陵 ITS 世界大會」，則聚焦於智慧交通與資料治理，中保科技集團旗下的國雲智慧停車，正式向國際展示其「智慧停車管理資料中心」，象徵臺灣在 AIoT 軟硬體整合實力的輸出。
此外，AIBC 歐亞峰會亦在杜拜舉行，旨在連結中東與北非（MENA）地區的科技與區塊鏈領袖，顯示歐亞大陸在跨國技術交流與產業協作上的活躍態勢。
根據各方公開資訊，多個高峰會均將「AI 賦能」列為核心議題。HITCON CISO Summit 確認了全球15大產業的資安長正共同面對 AI 帶來的威脅演變，特別是針對半導體供應鏈的防禦策略。在智慧運輸領域，交通部政務次長陳彥伯親自赴韓參訪，確認了臺灣智慧停車技術已具備國際競爭力，並透過資料中心模式實現路邊停車的數位化管理。
同時，職場調查報告指出，企業在匯入 AI 的過程中，雖然追求效率，但近六成的員工因 AI 感到心理消耗，這促使「心理安全感」成為企業轉型不可或缺的賦能關鍵，顯示技術革新與人力資源管理之間的緊密關聯。
儘管多項技術進展獲得確認，但各來源間對於「全球治理」的具體路徑仍存在不同層面的詮釋與資訊缺口。例如，HITCON 聚焦於企業層級的資安實務與技術防禦，而 ITS 世界大會則側重於政府與民間企業的基礎建設協作。此外，針對伊朗與美國在區域政治上的互動，部分來源顯示雙方在部分條件上達成共識，但對於關鍵議題仍缺乏明確說明，這與科技產業的快速擴張形成對比，顯示在歐亞地緣政治穩定度上，仍存在高度不確定性。
此外，關於 AIBC 歐亞峰會的具體政策影響，目前多停留在產業網路連結與商業增長層面，尚未有明確的跨國監管標準出爐。
這些產業活動對歐亞地區的具體影響深遠。首先，在供應鏈安全方面，臺灣作為半導體重鎮，其資安防禦能力的提升直接影響全球科技產業的穩定；其次，在智慧運輸領域，臺灣的資料治理模式為歐亞其他城市提供了數位轉型的參考案例。然而，企業在追求 AI 轉型時，若忽略了員工的心理安全與職場公平性（多元共融），可能會導致轉型效率下降。因此，技術的匯入與企業文化的重塑已成為歐亞地區企業轉型的雙軌策略。
不同觀點與限制方面，需注意技術展會所呈現的「樂觀前景」與現實地緣政治之間的落差。雖然產業峰會展現了強大的技術能量，但如來源中所述，地緣政治的緊張局勢與國際談判的進度，仍可能成為技術交流與基礎建設推廣的阻礙。此外，各項會議所發布的報告多由主辦單位或相關企業提供，其資料反映的是特定產業的觀點，不應將其視為全球治理的全面性結論。對於 AI 帶來的職場消耗問題，企業在推動數位轉型時，必須採取更具包容性的防治措施，而非僅依賴技術升級。
後續觀察重點應放在：第一，HITCON 高峰會後，臺灣企業在半導體供應鏈資安標準上的後續落實情況；第二，國雲智慧停車在國際展會後，是否能進一步取得歐亞地區的實質技術輸出合約；第三，伊朗與美國在後續談判中，針對尚待釐清的議題是否會有具體進展，這將直接影響歐亞地區的能源與物流穩定。各界應持續關注這些跨國峰會後的政策落地與商業合作細節，以評估其對全球治理的實際貢獻。
