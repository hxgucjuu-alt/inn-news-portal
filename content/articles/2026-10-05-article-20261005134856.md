  # Taiwan-UK Economic and Trade Dialogue Deepens Supply Chain Cooperation; Global Semiconductor Demand Boosts Economic Momentum
  
  > Vice Minister of Economic Affairs Jiang Wen-ruo chaired the Taiwan-UK Economic and Trade Dialogue in London, focusing on supply chain resilience and cooperation in key industries. Meanwhile, a surge in global AI chip demand has driven South Korea's semiconductor exports to record highs.
  
  On October 3, 2026, Vice Minister of Economic Affairs Jiang Wen-ruo led a delegation to London, UK, to co-chair the "2026 Taiwan-UK Economic and Trade Dialogue" with Lord Anas Sarwar, the UK Minister for Trade at the Department for Business and Trade (DBT). The meeting was framed against the backdrop of current global geopolitical shifts and the risks associated with highly concentrated supply chains for critical materials. The two sides aimed to engage in in-depth exchanges on economic and trade policy, economic security, advanced manufacturing, clean energy, and life sciences through institutionalized dialogue mechanisms.
  
  Vice Minister Jiang emphasized that the Taiwan government’s current core economic development strategy is to "base itself in Taiwan, deploy globally, and market to the world." The government is committed to assisting domestic enterprises in upgrading and transforming, and to strengthening supply chain resilience through close cooperation with trusted international partners to address the volatile international economic competitive environment.
  
  Regarding specific progress in cooperation, Taiwan and the UK have confirmed that they will leverage their respective strengths in scientific innovation and industrial development. The UK possesses a deep foundation in basic research and artificial intelligence (AI), while Taiwan holds global competitiveness in advanced manufacturing and supply chain integration. Both sides agreed that by combining their strengths in these areas, they can effectively reduce the risks associated with the over-concentration of critical materials. At the same time, the momentum of the global semiconductor industry is showing strong growth. According to the latest data released by South Korea's Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy, benefiting from the global AI investment boom, data centers and major cloud service providers continue to expand capital expenditures, directly driving a surge in chip demand.
  
  South Korea's total exports in September reached $120.94 billion, a year-on-year increase of 83.5%, setting a new monthly record. Semiconductor exports exceeded the $60 billion mark for the first time, with a year-on-year growth rate of 262.8%, accounting for approximately 50% of South Korea's total exports. This indicates that semiconductors have become the core driving force accelerating global economic exports.
  
  Regarding the aforementioned economic and trade dynamics, reports from various sources differ in their data details and thematic focus. Coverage of the Taiwan-UK Economic and Trade Dialogue focuses on institutional cooperation at the policy level and resilience planning amidst geopolitics; whereas the South Korean export data focuses on specific economic indicators driven by market demand. It is worth noting that while both involve semiconductors and high-tech industries, the Taiwan-UK dialogue emphasizes "long-term security and strategic cooperation in industrial chains," while the South Korean data reflects "export dividends brought about by the short-term explosion in AI chip demand."
  
  Information currently awaiting confirmation includes the specific investment amounts and technology transfer details of the Taiwan-UK industrial cooperation projects, as well as whether the high growth rate of South Korean semiconductor exports will face risks of supply-demand imbalances in future quarters due to global economic fluctuations.
  
  For relevant regions and industries, these developments have profound implications. For Taiwan, deepening cooperation with the UK helps diversify market risks and gain a head start in emerging fields such as clean energy and life sciences. For Oceania and the global supply chain, the strong performance of South Korean semiconductor exports indicates that the construction of AI hardware infrastructure is at its peak, which will directly affect the speed and cost of global digital transformation. Furthermore, the enhancement of supply chain resilience has become a priority for national policy formulation, and it is expected that more cross-border cooperation projects will be planned for critical raw materials and advanced manufacturing processes to reduce reliance on single markets.
  
  However, market analysts and policy observers caution against over-interpreting single-month export data or the results of a single economic and trade meeting. Although South Korean semiconductor exports have hit record highs, they are highly dependent on AI demand; if global cloud providers adjust their capital expenditure plans in the future, it could impact export momentum. Similarly, while the Taiwan-UK Economic and Trade Dialogue has established a direction for cooperation, whether it can be implemented into specific commercial contracts and technical cooperation still depends on the willingness of enterprises from both sides and their market competitiveness. In addition, the global economy still faces multiple constraints such as inflation, geopolitical conflicts, and energy price fluctuations, all of which may affect the long-term stability of the aforementioned industrial development.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on three aspects: first, whether the specific cooperation projects between Taiwan and the UK in clean energy and life sciences can be launched as scheduled within the next year; second, whether the strong momentum of South Korean semiconductor exports can continue into the fourth quarter, and its impact on global semiconductor prices and inventory cycles; and finally, whether the global AI hardware investment wave will see diminishing marginal effects, thereby affecting the long-term profit performance of related industrial chains. INN News will continue to track the implementation of relevant policies and the dynamic adjustments of the global supply chain.

# 臺英經貿對話深化供應鏈合作，全球半導體需求推升經濟動能

> 經濟部政務次長江文若於倫敦主持臺英經貿對話，聚焦供應鏈韌性與關鍵產業合作；與此同時，全球AI晶片需求激增，帶動韓國半導體出口創下歷史新高紀錄。


2026年10月3日，臺灣經濟部政務次長江文若率團前往英國倫敦，與英國商業、創新、科學及貿易部（BIST）主管貿易之副部長Anas Sarwar勳爵共同主持「二○二六年年臺英經貿對話會議」。此次會議背景建立在當前全球地緣政治變局及關鍵物資供應鏈高度集中的風險之下，雙方旨在透過制度化的對話機制，針對經貿政策、經濟安全、先進製造、潔淨能源及生命科學等領域進行深度交流。
江文若次長強調，臺灣政府現階段以「立足臺灣、佈局全球、行銷全世界」為核心經濟發展策略，致力於協助國內企業升級轉型，並透過與國際可信賴夥伴的緊密合作，共同強化供應鏈韌性，以因應未來多變的國際經濟競爭環境。
在具體的合作進展方面，臺英雙方已確認將在科研創新及產業發展領域發揮各自優勢。英國在基礎研究與人工智慧（AI）領域具備深厚底蘊，而臺灣則在先進製造與供應鏈整合方面擁有全球競爭力。雙方一致認為，透過結合兩國在上述領域的強項，能有效降低關鍵物資過度集中的風險。與此同時，全球半導體產業的動能亦呈現強勁成長。根據韓國產業通商部最新公佈的資料顯示，受惠於全球AI投資熱潮，資料中心與大型雲端業者持續擴大資本支出，直接帶動晶片需求激增。
韓國9月出口總額達到1,209.4億美元，年增率高達83.5%，創下單月歷史新高，其中半導體出口額首度突破600億美元大關，年增率達262.8%，佔韓國整體出口比重約五成，顯示半導體已成為支撐全球經濟出口加速的核心推力。
針對上述經貿動態，各來源報導在資料細節與側重議題上存在差異。臺英經貿對話會議的報導重點在於政策層面的制度性合作與地緣政治下的韌性佈局；而韓國出口資料則聚焦於市場需求驅動的具體經濟指標。值得注意的是，雖然兩者皆涉及半導體與高科技產業，但臺英對話側重於「產業鏈的長期安全與策略合作」，而韓國資料則反映了「短期內AI晶片需求爆發帶來的出口紅利」。
目前尚待確認的資訊包括，臺英雙方在具體產業合作專案中的投資金額與技術轉移細節，以及韓國半導體出口的高成長幅度，在未來季度是否因全球經濟波動而面臨供需失衡的風險。
對相關地區與產業而言，這些動態具有深遠影響。對於臺灣而言，深化與英國的合作有助於分散市場風險，並在潔淨能源與生命科學等新興領域取得先機。對於大洋洲及全球供應鏈而言，韓國半導體出口的強勁表現，預示著AI硬體基礎設施的建設正處於高峰期，這將直接影響全球數位轉型速度與成本。此外，供應鏈韌性的提升已成為各國政策制定的優先選項，預期未來將有更多跨國合作計畫針對關鍵原物料與先進製程進行佈局，以降低單一市場依賴度。
然而，市場分析師與政策觀察家提醒，不應過度解讀單一月份的出口資料或單次經貿會議成果。韓國半導體出口雖創紀錄，但其高度依賴AI需求，若未來全球雲端業者調整資本支出計畫，可能對出口動能造成衝擊。同樣地，臺英經貿對話雖確立了合作方向，但後續能否落實為具體的商業合約與技術合作，仍取決於雙方企業的參與意願及市場競爭力。此外，全球經濟仍面臨通膨、地緣政治衝突及能源價格波動等多重限制，這些變數均可能影響上述產業發展的長期穩定性。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個面向：首先，臺英雙方在潔淨能源與生命科學領域的具體合作專案進度，是否能如期在未來一年內啟動；其次，韓國半導體出口的強勁勢頭能否在第四季延續，以及其對全球半導體價格與庫存週期的影響；最後，全球AI硬體投資潮是否會出現邊際效應遞減，進而影響相關產業鏈的長期獲利表現。INN新聞將持續追蹤相關政策落實情形與全球供應鏈的動態調整。
