  # Eurasian Economic Expansion and Multiple Focal Points of Public Opinion in Taiwan
  
  > This article summarizes the international trend of the Eurasian Economic Union (EEU) promoting transnational free trade zones, and contrasts it with major public events in Taiwan, including controversies surrounding the Chiang family, corporate legal litigation, and local election bribery, to present a multifaceted view of social and geopolitical dynamics.
  
  The Eurasian region has recently seen significant progress in geopolitical and economic integration. According to relevant international observations, the Eurasian Economic Union (EEU) is actively promoting regional currency integration and attempting to expand free trade zone agreements with Israel and over 30 other governments or regimes worldwide. This move is viewed as a strategic layout in energy, infrastructure, and logistics within the framework of globalization. At the same time, several public events in Taiwan have recently drawn high attention, including the livestream controversy involving Chiang Yu-ching, a family member of Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an, on a Chinese social media platform; the massive compensation lawsuit filed by Taiwan Family Mart (泰山企業) regarding the Zhonglian Oil incident; and a vote-buying case involving daily necessities by a neighborhood warden candidate in Xinzhuang District, New Taipei City.
  
  These events involve the influence of political families, conflicts of corporate interest, and the integrity of local elections, respectively, reflecting the current situation in Taiwan as it faces complex social transformations and legal challenges.
  
  Regarding domestic social news in Taiwan, Chiang Yu-ching, the fourth-generation descendant of the Chiang family, publicly discussed controversies surrounding Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an and his family members during a Douyin livestream in China in September 2026. These included allegations regarding Chiang Te-li’s school admission and claims of privilege, as well as questions regarding Chiang Wan-an’s bloodline connection to the Chiang family. During the livestream, Chiang Yu-ching revealed that he was restricted by a "gag order" from his mother, Chiang Fang Chih-yi, before the broadcast signal was interrupted, sparking speculation about whether the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) intervened to control remarks related to the Chiang family. Political science professor Fan Shih-ping analyzed this, noting that the CCP highly values the historical influence of the Chiang family in Taiwan's political arena and therefore adopts protective measures to intervene in the dissemination of such remarks to maintain their strategic value in cross-strait political narratives.
  
  Information sources and details regarding the aforementioned events vary in focus across media reports. In the Chiang Yu-ching livestream incident, media focused on his revelations about internal conflicts within Chiang Wan-an’s family and the technical suspiciousness of the broadcast interruption; however, claims that the "CCP directly ordered the ban" remain largely expert speculation and lack official confirmation. In the Taiwan Family Mart litigation case, media consistently confirmed that the company has retained the Yuli Law Firm to formally sue Zhonglian Oil, Fushou, Fumaow, and Nanchiao for 1 billion NTD in damages, citing that the Zhonglian Oil incident led to product recalls and inventory destruction, causing significant operational losses.
  
  As for the neighborhood warden bribery case investigated by the New Taipei District Prosecutors Office, media collectively confirmed that a candidate surnamed Yeh used daily necessities such as soy sauce, masks, and plastic wrap for vote-buying. Although the prosecution requested detention and a ban on communication, the court ultimately ruled for release on 300,000 NTD bail with restricted residence.
  
  These events have had a direct impact on Taiwan's society and industries. Taiwan Family Mart’s legal action signals an increased emphasis on supply chain quality and legal liability within the food industry; the 1 billion NTD compensation claim will impact the finances and market reputation of the involved enterprises. The Xinzhuang District bribery case once again highlights that the risk of using daily necessities for improper interest exchange persists in grassroots organizations during local elections in Taiwan. This not only challenges the fairness of elections but also tests the enforcement intensity of prosecutorial and investigative agencies during election periods. Furthermore, the controversy surrounding the Chiang family member's remarks highlights the risks of speech by family members of political figures in the digital age, as well as the high sensitivity of political symbols in the cross-strait public opinion sphere.

Some public opinion suggests that Chiang Yu-ching’s remarks should be viewed as personal behavior and should not be over-interpreted as an inevitable result of cross-strait political maneuvering. Regarding corporate litigation, legal experts remind that the determination of compensation amounts requires rigorous judicial procedures, and it is inappropriate to prejudge the verdict. Similarly, regarding economic integration in the Eurasian region, international analysts point out that although the EEU is actively expanding, free trade agreements involving multiple countries often face long negotiation periods. Furthermore, given the rapid changes in the global geopolitical environment, the implementation of a regional currency remains highly uncertain and should not be viewed as a settled conclusion for the global economic landscape.

Second, the subsequent investigation and verdict by the New Taipei District Prosecutors Office regarding the local bribery case will reflect the judicial system's ability to curb grassroots election fraud. Finally, the trade negotiation processes between the Eurasian Economic Union and countries like Israel, as well as the dynamics of remarks made by Chiang family members on cross-strait online platforms, will continue to influence the direction of geopolitics and political opinion in Taiwan. All parties should continue to monitor the progress of relevant judicial rulings and the signing of international economic and trade agreements to grasp the latest pulses of society and the regional economy.

# 歐亞經貿擴張與臺灣內部社會輿論之多重焦點觀察

> 本文匯整近期歐亞經濟聯盟（EEU）推動跨國自由貿易區之國際趨勢，並對照臺灣社會近期發生的蔣家爭議、企業法律訴訟及地方選舉賄選等重大公共事件，呈現多面向的社會與地緣政治動態。


歐亞地區近期在地緣政治與經貿整合上出現顯著進展，根據相關國際觀察，歐亞經濟聯盟（EEU）正積極推動區域貨幣整合，並試圖擴大與以色列及全球超過30個政府或政權簽署自由貿易區協議。此舉被視為全球化架構下，歐亞版圖在能源、基建與物流領域的戰略佈局。與此同時，臺灣社會近期發生多起引發高度關注的公共事件，包括臺北市長蔣萬安家族成員蔣友青在中國社群平臺的直播爭議、泰山企業針對中聯油脂事件發起的巨額求償訴訟，以及新北市新莊區裡長候選人涉及生活用品賄選案。
這些事件分別涉及政治家族影響力、企業利益衝突與地方選舉誠信，反映出臺灣在面對複雜社會轉型與法律挑戰時的現況。
在臺灣內部的社會新聞方面，蔣家第四代蔣友青於2026年9月在中國抖音直播中，公開談論臺北市長蔣萬安及其家族成員的相關爭議，包括蔣得立的入學與特權疑雲，以及蔣萬安與蔣家血緣關係的質疑。直播過程中，蔣友青透露受到母親蔣方智怡的「封口令」限制，隨後直播訊號中斷，引發外界對中共是否介入管控蔣家相關言論的揣測。政治學教授範世平對此分析指出，中共極為看重蔣家在臺灣政壇的歷史影響力，因此採取保護性手段介入相關言論傳播，以維護其在兩岸政治敘事中的戰略價值。
關於上述事件的資訊來源與細節，各家媒體報導存在不同側重。在蔣友青直播事件中，媒體重點在於其對蔣萬安家族內部矛盾的爆料，以及直播被中斷的技術性疑點；然而，對於「中共是否直接下令封禁」的說法，目前多為專家推論，尚缺乏官方證實。在泰山企業訴訟案中，媒體一致確認泰山已委任友理法律事務所，正式向中聯油脂、福壽、福懋及南僑等企業提告求償10億元，理由為中聯油脂事件導致產品回收與庫存銷毀，造成重大營運損失。
至於新北檢偵辦的裡長賄選案，媒體共同確認葉姓候選人以醬油、口罩、保鮮膜等生活用品進行賄選，檢方雖聲請羈押禁見，但法院最終裁定以30萬元交保並限制住居。
這些事件對臺灣社會與產業產生了直接影響。泰山企業的法律行動預示著食品產業對於供應鏈品質與法律責任的重視程度提升，10億元的求償金額將對相關涉案企業的財務與市場信譽造成衝擊。而新莊區的賄選案則再次凸顯臺灣地方選舉在基層組織中，仍存在利用民生用品進行不正當利益交換的風險，這不僅挑戰了選舉的公平性，亦考驗檢調機關在選舉期間的執法強度。此外，蔣家成員的言論爭議則凸顯了政治人物家庭成員在數位時代的言論風險，以及兩岸輿論場域對於政治符號的高度敏感性。
針對上述現象，各界觀點存在顯著差異。部分輿論認為，蔣友青的言論應視為個人行為，不應過度解讀為兩岸政治博弈的必然結果；而針對企業訴訟，法律界人士提醒，求償金額的認定需經過嚴謹的司法程式，不宜過早預判判決結果。同樣地，對於歐亞地區的經貿整合，國際分析師指出，雖然EEU積極擴張，但涉及多國利益的自由貿易協定往往面臨漫長的談判期，且全球地緣政治環境變動迅速，區域貨幣的推行仍存在極大不確定性，不應將其視為全球經濟格局的定論。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於三個層面：首先，泰山企業的訴訟進展將成為臺灣食品安全與供應鏈賠償責任的指標性案例；其次，新北市地檢署對於地方賄選案的後續偵查與判決，將反映司法體系對基層選舉舞弊的遏止力度；最後，歐亞經濟聯盟與以色列等國的貿易談判程式，以及蔣家成員在兩岸網路平臺的言論動態，將持續影響地緣政治與臺灣政治輿論的走向。各界應持續關注相關司法判決與國際經貿協議的簽署進度，以掌握社會與區域經濟的最新脈動。
