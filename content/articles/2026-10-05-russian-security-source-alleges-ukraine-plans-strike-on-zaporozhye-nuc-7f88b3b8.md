根據俄羅斯安全消息來源向Sputnik通訊社透露，烏克蘭武裝分子正計劃對札波羅熱核電廠（ZNPP）附近與其相關的設施發動一次「自導自演」的襲擊。此一報導指出，這次行動的目的是為了合理化烏克蘭對俄羅斯境內核電廠的潛在打擊。此消息於2026年10月4日發布。

札波羅熱核電廠是歐洲最大的核電設施，資料顯示該廠已由俄羅斯控制超過四年。俄羅斯國家核能機構的報導指出，在過去的四個半月裡，札波羅熱核電廠及其所在的埃涅爾戈達爾市（Energodar）已遭受烏克蘭無人機和火砲超過750次的襲擊。

最近的一次事件發生在2026年5月31日，當時俄羅斯聲稱烏克蘭無人機襲擊了札波羅熱核電廠，但烏克蘭否認了這項指控。國際原子能機構（IAEA）隨後要求進入該設施進行檢查，以評估情況。這些相互矛盾的說法凸顯了圍繞該核電廠安全局勢的緊張與不確定性。

## English edition

A Russian security source informed Sputnik on October 4, 2026, that Ukrainian militants are allegedly planning to stage an attack on facilities linked with the Zaporozhye Nuclear Power Plant (ZNPP) near the contact line. This report suggests the purpose of such an action would be to justify Ukraine's own potential strikes on nuclear power plants within Russia.

The Zaporozhye Nuclear Power Plant, Europe's largest nuclear facility, has reportedly been under Russian control for more than four years. Russian state nuclear sources, as cited by globalsecurity.org, indicate that the ZNPP and the city of Energodar have been subjected to over 750 strikes by Ukrainian drones and artillery over the past 4.5 months.

More recently, on May 31, 2026, Russia claimed that a Ukrainian drone struck the Zaporozhye nuclear plant, an accusation that Ukraine denied. Following this reported incident, the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) sought access to examine the facility. These conflicting reports underscore the tension and uncertainty surrounding the security situation at the nuclear plant.
