  # S&P Upgrades Kazakhstan's Sovereign Rating to BBB, First Time Since 2016
  
  > International credit rating agency S&P Global Ratings upgraded Kazakhstan's sovereign credit rating from "BBB-" to "BBB" on August 21, 2026, with a "stable" outlook, marking the first return to this rating level since 2016.
  
  International credit rating agency S&P Global Ratings announced on August 21, 2026, that it has upgraded the Republic of Kazakhstan's long-term and short-term sovereign credit ratings from "BBB-/A-3" to "BBB/A-2," while maintaining a "stable" outlook. This rating adjustment marks Kazakhstan's first return to the "BBB" investment-grade level since 2016, reflecting the international credit market's reassessment of the country's overall macroeconomic fundamentals.
  
  According to multiple media reports, this upgrade is not attributable to a single factor but rather reflects a comprehensive assessment of Kazakhstan's economic development resilience, progress in industrial diversification, and the accumulation of external and fiscal buffer space across multiple dimensions.
  
  Based on aggregated reporting from various sources, S&P's primary justifications for the upgrade lie in Kazakhstan's "robust economic growth prospects" and "ample accumulated external and fiscal buffers." Specifically, S&P projects Kazakhstan's economic growth rate to reach 5.1% in 2026, a figure higher than most Central Asian neighbors, indicating that its economy maintains relatively strong expansion momentum amid international energy price volatility and geopolitical pressures. Additionally, S&P's report noted that the economic diversification strategy promoted by the Kazakh government in recent years has gradually shown results, reducing excessive dependence on the single energy export sector and helping to strengthen its capacity to withstand external shocks.
  
  Multiple media outlets have confirmed that the effective date of this rating adjustment is August 21, 2026, and the outlook is "stable," implying that the probability of a further upgrade or downgrade in the near term is limited.
  
  Although various media outlets report consistently on the core fact of the rating upgrade, discrepancies exist in certain details that remain to be further confirmed. For example, Kazakhstan's local media outlet apgazeta.kz only briefly stated that the rating was upgraded from "BBB-" to "BBB" without explicitly mentioning the specific change in the short-term rating; whereas astanatimes.com and qazinform.com clearly indicated that the long-term and short-term ratings were upgraded from "BBB-/A-3" to "BBB/A-2," providing a more complete context of the rating levels.
  
  Furthermore, regarding economic growth rate projections, astanatimes.com explicitly cited S&P's expectation of 5.1% GDP growth for 2026, while other sources such as inform.kz and apgazeta.kz did not provide specific growth rate figures, merely describing "robust economic development prospects" in general terms. This discrepancy may stem from different media outlets excerpting different sections of S&P's original report; readers requiring precise data should refer to the official documents published by S&P.
  
  The direct impact of this rating upgrade on Kazakhstan is first reflected in the financing costs and investment attractiveness within international capital markets. Achieving a "BBB" investment-grade rating means that Kazakhstan's sovereign bonds will be included in the investable universe of more international institutional investors, particularly for pension funds, insurance companies, and sovereign wealth funds that require minimum credit rating thresholds—this upgrade will significantly expand their pool of eligible investment targets. Secondly, the rating upgrade also helps reduce the interest rate costs for the Kazakh government and state-owned enterprises when issuing bonds in international markets, alleviating fiscal burdens.
  
  For foreign direct investment, an upgrade in sovereign credit rating is often perceived as a signal of improvement in a country's overall investment environment, helping to attract more foreign investment in manufacturing, the digital economy, and green energy sectors, further supporting the Kazakh government's economic diversification objectives.
  
  However, there are also cautious perspectives in the market regarding the interpretation of this rating upgrade, and excessive optimism should be avoided. Some analysts point out that while S&P's "stable" outlook rules out the immediate risk of a downgrade in the short term, Kazakhstan's economy remains highly dependent on energy exports, and global energy transition policies and long-term trends in international oil prices continue to pose structural challenges to its fiscal revenues and balance of payments. Additionally, infrastructure aging, administrative inefficiency, and the international perception of the rule of law environment within Kazakhstan are not incorporated into the core parameters of the sovereign rating model, yet these factors may influence actual investors' long-term investment decisions.
  
  Therefore, while this rating upgrade is certainly a positive signal, it should not be viewed as conclusive evidence that Kazakhstan's economic structural problems have been fundamentally resolved, but rather understood as an affirmation by the international credit rating agency of the country's short-to-medium-term macroeconomic stability.
  
  Regarding key points for subsequent observation, the market will closely monitor whether S&P further adjusts the outlook from "stable" to "positive" within the next 12 months, which will depend on whether the Kazakh government can consistently implement fiscal discipline, accelerate structural reforms, and whether global energy markets remain relatively stable. Additionally, whether other major rating agencies Moody's and Fitch will follow suit in upgrading Kazakhstan's rating will also be an important indicator of whether international credit market confidence in Kazakhstan is comprehensively recovering.
  
  At the same time, how the Kazakh government leverages the market confidence brought by this rating upgrade to advance reform agendas such as state asset privatization, financial market deepening, and SME development will be key to determining whether the country can truly attract long-term capital and achieve economic transformation. International investors will also monitor the subsequent monetary policy direction of the National Bank of Kazakhstan, as well as whether the government will seize this opportunity to expand international bond issuance to raise funds needed for infrastructure and green transition projects.

# S&P調升哈薩克主權信評至BBB 2016年來首見

> 國際信評機構S&P Global Ratings於2026年8月21日將哈薩克主權信用評等自「BBB-」調升至「BBB」，展望為「穩定」，為2016年以來首次重返該評級水準。

國際信評機構標準普爾（S&P Global Ratings）於2026年8月21日宣佈，將哈薩克共和國長期及短期主權信用評等由「BBB-/A-3」調升至「BBB/A-2」，同時維持「穩定」展望。此一評級調整標誌著哈薩克自2016年以來，首次重新達到「BBB」等級的投資級評等，顯示國際信用市場對該國總體經濟體質的重新評價。
根據多家媒體報導，此次調升並非單一因素所致，而是反映哈薩克在經濟發展韌性、產業多元化進展以及外部與財政緩衝空間累積等多重面向的綜合評估結果。
綜合各來源報導，S&P此次調升評等的主要依據，在於哈薩克具備「穩健的經濟成長前景」以及「充足的累積外部與財政緩衝」。具體而言，S&P預期哈薩克2026年經濟成長率可達5.1%，此一數字高於多數中亞鄰國，顯示其經濟在國際能源價格波動與地緣政治壓力下仍保有相對強勁的擴張動能。此外，S&P的報告亦指出，哈薩克政府近年來推動的經濟多元化策略已逐步顯現成效，降低對單一能源出口部門的過度依賴，有助於強化其抵禦外部衝擊的能力。
多家媒體均確認，此次評等調整的生效日期為2026年8月21日，且展望均為「穩定」，意味著短期內信評再獲調升或遭調降的機率均屬有限。
儘管各媒體對評等調升的核心事實報導一致，但在部分細節上仍存在差異，尚待進一步確認。例如，哈薩克當地媒體《apgazeta.kz》僅簡述評等由「BBB-」調升至「BBB」，並未明確提及短期評等的具體變動；而《astanatimes.com》與《qazinform.com》則明確指出長期與短期評等分別由「BBB-/A-3」調升至「BBB/A-2」，提供更完整的評等等級脈絡。
此外，關於經濟成長率預測，《astanatimes.com》明確引述S&P預期2026年GDP成長率為5.1%，但其他來源如《inform.kz》與《apgazeta.kz》則未提供具體成長率數字，僅以「穩健的經濟發展前景」概括描述。此一差異可能源於各家媒體擷取S&P原始報告的不同段落，讀者若需精確資料，仍應以S&P官方發布檔案為準。
此次信評調升對哈薩克的直接影響，首先反映在國際資本市場的融資成本與投資吸引力層面。獲得「BBB」投資級評等，意味著哈薩克主權債券將納入更多國際機構投資人的可投資範圍，尤其對於要求最低信評門檻的退休基金、保險公司與主權財富基金而言，此一調升將顯著擴大其投資標的池。其次，評等調升亦有助於降低哈薩克政府及國營企業在國際市場發行債券的利率成本，減輕財政負擔。
對外資直接投資而言，主權信評的提升往往被視為國家整體投資環境改善的訊號，有助於吸引更多製造業、數位經濟與綠色能源領域的外資進駐，進一步支援哈薩克政府推動的經濟多元化目標。
然而，對於此次評等調升的解讀，市場上亦存在審慎觀點，不應過度樂觀。部分分析師指出，S&P的「穩定」展望雖然排除了短期內降評的立即風險，但哈薩克經濟仍高度依賴能源出口，全球能源轉型政策與國際油價長期走勢，仍對其財政收入與國際收支構成結構性挑戰。此外，哈薩克境內的基礎設施老化、行政效率低落以及法治環境的國際評價，均未被納入主權信評模型的核心引數，但這些因素卻可能影響實際投資人的長期投資決策。
因此，此次評等調升固然是正面訊號，卻不宜被視為哈薩克經濟結構問題已獲根本解決的定論，而應理解為國際信評機構對其短期至中期總體經濟穩定性的肯定。
後續觀察重點方面，市場將密切關注S&P於未來12個月內是否進一步將展望由「穩定」調整為「正向」，此將取決於哈薩克政府能否持續落實財政紀律、加速結構性改革，以及全球能源市場是否維持相對平穩。此外，另一家主要信評機構穆迪（Moody's）與惠譽（Fitch）是否跟進調升哈薩克評等，亦將是觀察國際信評市場對哈薩克信心是否全面回升的重要指標。
同時，哈薩克政府如何運用此次評等調升所帶來的市場信心，推動國有資產私有化、金融市場深化及中小企業發展等改革議程，將是決定該國能否真正吸引長期資本、實現經濟轉型的關鍵。國際投資人亦將關注哈薩克央行後續的貨幣政策走向，以及政府是否會藉此機會擴大國際債券發行規模，以籌措基礎建設與綠色轉型所需資金。
