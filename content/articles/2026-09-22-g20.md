  # US Extends G20 Invitations to Central Asian Leaders, Elevating Strategic Status of Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan
  
  > U.S. President Donald Trump has officially invited Kazakh President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev and Uzbek President Shavkat Mirziyoyev to attend the 2026 G20 Summit in Miami, highlighting the strategic importance of Central Asian nations in the global geopolitical and economic landscape.
  
  The global geopolitical landscape has seen significant adjustments in 2026. U.S. President Donald Trump recently stated publicly that he has held phone calls with Kazakh President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev and Uzbek President Shavkat Mirziyoyev, formally inviting both leaders to participate in the G20 Summit to be held next year in Miami, Florida. This move is viewed as a concrete action by the United States to strengthen strategic partnerships with Central Asian nations, aiming to integrate these countries—located at the heart of the Eurasian continent—more closely into the global economic and security framework.
  
  According to data provided by Taiwantrade, economic vitality in the Central Asian region is continuing to expand. Taking Uzbekistan as an example, its total foreign trade reached $49.5 billion in the first seven months of 2026. Although the country’s primary trading partners remain China (22.7%), Russia (16.4%), and Kazakhstan (6.8%), the potential of Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan as key suppliers of critical minerals and energy has made them targets for competition among major global powers, driven by the restructuring of global supply chains and shifts in the energy landscape. This diplomatic move by the U.S. reflects Washington's high regard for the Central Asian region regarding energy security and regional stability.
  
  There are currently varying interpretations regarding the specific agenda behind the U.S. invitation for Central Asian leaders to join the G20. Some observers believe the move aims to encourage Central Asian nations to deepen cooperation with the West in renewable energy and critical mineral supply chains to diversify their reliance on traditional trading partners. However, others point out that Central Asian countries have long maintained a balance within the spheres of influence of Russia and China; navigating complex relationships with various powers under the G20 framework remains a major diplomatic challenge for both governments. Information yet to be confirmed includes whether both leaders have formally accepted the invitations and whether specific regional security or economic cooperation memorandums will be signed during the summit.
  
  For the Central Asian region, receiving an invitation to the G20 is not only a major diplomatic breakthrough but will also directly influence future industrial policies and foreign investment attraction. As global underwater infrastructure faces threats from hybrid warfare, the protection of undersea cables and energy pipelines has become an international focus. As a hub for land transport and energy transit in Eurasia, Central Asia's stability is vital to the global economic lifeline. Successfully leveraging this opportunity to strengthen strategic alignment with G20 members would help Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan enhance their influence in global supply chains and alleviate current account pressures caused by rapid import growth.
  
  However, academics and think tanks caution against over-interpreting the impact of a single diplomatic invitation. Development in Central Asia remains constrained by its landlocked geography and its status of high dependency on neighboring major powers. Furthermore, the global economy is moving in a more fragmented and unpredictable direction. The Australian Treasury’s "2026 Intergenerational Report" also points out that while the application of artificial intelligence (AI) technology can boost productivity, the fragmentation of the global governance system may offset the benefits of regional cooperation. For Central Asian leaders, balancing domestic digital transformation needs with geopolitical pressures amidst the trend of AI-enabled services will be a key constraint on their governance over the next decade.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on three key points: First, whether there are concrete signs of a shift toward the West in the foreign policy adjustments of Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan ahead of the Miami summit; second, whether Central Asian nations will reach substantive investment agreements with the U.S. regarding critical mineral exports and energy transition; and finally, as the global infrastructure defense revolution emerges, how the Central Asian region ensures the resilience of its cross-border energy pipelines and digital communication infrastructure will become an important indicator for assessing regional security risks. Observers will closely monitor the public statements and bilateral meeting outcomes of both leaders during the summit.

# 美國力邀中亞領袖參與G20，烏茲別克與哈薩克戰略地位提升

> 美國總統川普正式邀請哈薩克總統託卡葉夫與烏茲別克總統米爾濟約耶夫，出席2026年於邁阿密舉行的G20峰會，凸顯中亞國家在全球地緣政治與經濟版圖中的戰略重要性。


2026年全球地緣政治格局出現顯著調整，美國總統川普近期公開表示，已與哈薩克總統託卡葉夫（Kassym-Jomart Tokayev）及烏茲別克總統米爾濟約耶夫（Shavkat Mirziyoyev）進行通話，並正式邀請兩位領袖參與明年於美國佛羅裡達州邁阿密舉辦的20國集團（G20）峰會。此舉被視為美國試圖加強與中亞國家戰略夥伴關係的具體行動，旨在將這些位於歐亞大陸核心的國家更緊密地納入全球經濟與安全體系。
根據臺灣經貿網提供的資料顯示，中亞地區的經濟活力正持續擴張。以烏茲別克為例，截至2026年前7個月，其外貿總額已達495億美元。儘管該國主要貿易夥伴仍以中國（佔22.7%）、俄羅斯（佔16.4%）及哈薩克（佔6.8%）為主，但隨著全球供應鏈重組及能源版圖變動，哈薩克與烏茲別克作為關鍵礦產與能源供應國的潛力，已成為國際大國競相爭取合作的物件。美國此番外交動作，反映出華府對於中亞地區在能源安全與區域穩定上的高度重視。
目前各界對於美方邀請中亞領袖參與G20的具體議程仍有不同解讀。部分觀察家認為，此舉旨在推動中亞國家在可再生能源與關鍵礦產供應鏈上與西方深化合作，以分散對傳統貿易夥伴的依賴；然而，亦有觀點指出，中亞國家長期以來在俄羅斯與中國的勢力範圍中維持平衡，如何在G20框架下處理與各強權的複雜關係，仍是兩國政府面臨的重大外交挑戰。目前尚待確認的資訊包括兩國領袖是否已正式接受邀請，以及峰會期間是否會簽署具體的區域安全或經貿合作備忘錄。
對中亞地區而言，受邀參與G20不僅是外交層面的重大突破，亦將直接影響其未來的產業政策與外資引進。隨著全球水下基礎設施面臨混合戰威脅，海底電纜與能源管道的防護成為國際焦點，中亞作為歐亞大陸陸路運輸與能源轉運的樞紐，其穩定性對全球經濟命脈至關重要。若能藉此機會強化與G20成員國的戰略對接，將有助於烏茲別克與哈薩克提升其在全球供應鏈中的話語權，並緩解因進口成長過快而帶來的經常帳壓力。
然而，學界與智庫提醒，不應過度解讀單一外交邀請的影響力。中亞地區的發展仍受限於其內陸地理環境及高度依賴周邊大國的現狀。此外，全球經濟正朝向更加分散且不可預測的方向發展，澳洲財政部的《2026年代際報告》亦指出，人工智慧（AI）技術的應用雖可提升生產力，但全球治理體系的碎片化可能抵銷區域合作的效益。對於中亞領袖而言，如何在AI技術賦能服務的趨勢下，平衡國內數位轉型需求與地緣政治壓力，將是未來十年執政的關鍵限制。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三點：首先，哈薩克與烏茲別克在邁阿密峰會前的外交政策調整，是否會出現向西方傾斜的具體跡象；其次，中亞國家在關鍵礦產出口與能源轉型議題上，是否會與美國達成實質性的投資協議；最後，隨著全球基礎設施防禦革命的興起，中亞地區如何確保其跨境能源管道與數位通訊基礎設施的韌性，將成為評估該地區安全風險的重要指標。各界將密切關注兩國領袖在峰會期間的公開表態與雙邊會談成果。
