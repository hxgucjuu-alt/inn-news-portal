根據報導，美國空軍官校（USAFA）於2026年10月3日，在與美國海軍學院（Navy）的年度對抗賽前夕，成功創下了一項新的金氏世界紀錄。這項紀錄是關於最多人同時進行一分鐘伏地挺身，此前該榮譽一直由美國海軍學院保持。此次破紀錄的壯舉在科羅拉多州的美國空軍官校舉行，吸引了廣泛關注。

資料顯示，這項挑戰旨在號召大量參與者在同一時間內完成一分鐘的伏地挺身。雖然多數報導未提及具體參與人數，但有報導指出，此次活動由Pete Hegseth帶領，共有3,068人參與，成功超越了舊紀錄。這項成就不僅展現了空軍官校學員的體能與團結，也為即將到來的對抗賽增添了額外的競爭氣氛。

此次紀錄的打破，標誌著美國空軍官校從其長期競爭對手海軍學院手中奪走了這項殊榮。兩所軍事院校之間的體育對抗歷史悠久，充滿激情，而這次在賽前打破世界紀錄，無疑為這場傳統賽事增添了新的看點和話題。

在紀錄被打破的同一天，空軍與海軍的對抗賽也如期舉行。報導指出，最終海軍以34比31的比數擊敗了空軍，贏得了這場備受矚目的橄欖球賽。儘管比賽結果對空軍官校而言未能盡如人意，但他們在賽前所創造的世界紀錄，已成為當天的另一大焦點。

## English edition

The U.S. Air Force Academy (USAFA) reportedly set a new Guinness World Record on October 3, 2026, ahead of its annual rivalry game against the U.S. Naval Academy (Navy). The record was for the most people simultaneously performing push-ups for one full minute, an accolade previously held by the U.S. Naval Academy. This record-breaking event took place at the U.S. Air Force Academy in Colorado, drawing significant attention.

Data indicates that this challenge aimed to gather a large number of participants to complete one minute of push-ups at the same time. While most reports did not specify the exact number of participants, some sources noted that the event, led by Pete Hegseth, involved 3,068 individuals, successfully surpassing the previous record. This achievement not only showcased the physical prowess and unity of the Air Force cadets but also added an extra layer of competitive spirit to the upcoming rivalry game.

The breaking of this record signifies USAFA taking this prestigious honor from its long-standing rival, the Naval Academy. The athletic rivalry between these two military institutions is rich in history and passion, and setting a world record just before the game undoubtedly added a new highlight and talking point to this traditional fixture.

On the same day the record was broken, the Air Force vs. Navy rivalry game proceeded as scheduled. Reports indicate that Navy ultimately defeated Air Force with a score of 34-31 in the highly anticipated football match. Although the game's outcome might not have been what USAFA hoped for, the world record they set prior to the game became another major highlight of the day.
