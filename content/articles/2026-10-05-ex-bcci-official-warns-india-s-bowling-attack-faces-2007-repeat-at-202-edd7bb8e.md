一名前印度板球控制委員會（BCCI）官員於2026年10月4日發出嚴厲警告，指出如果印度國家板球隊未能有效解決其投球陣容的問題，該隊在2027年板球世界盃（ODI World Cup）上恐將面臨「被痛擊」的命運，重演2007年的慘痛經歷。此番言論引發了對印度隊未來表現的廣泛關注。

資料顯示，這名前官員擔憂印度隊的投球能力可能不足以應對即將到來的國際賽事挑戰。他特別提及2007年世界盃，當時印度隊經歷了令人震驚的淘汰賽，未能晉級，成為該屆賽事的一大意外。這項警告強調了投球陣容對於球隊整體表現的關鍵作用。

據報導，這名前BCCI官員認為，距離2007年印度隊意外出局已近20年，但如果當前的投球問題未能得到根本性解決，歷史可能會再次重演。他呼籲球隊管理層必須在2027年世界盃之前，對投球部門進行徹底的改革和加強，以避免再次遭遇類似的挫敗。

## English edition

A former official of the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) issued a stark warning on October 4, 2026, stating that India's national cricket team risks being "clobbered" at the 2027 ODI World Cup, potentially repeating the shocking exit of 2007, if its bowling attack is not adequately addressed. This caution has drawn significant attention to the team's future prospects.

The official's concern stems from the potential inadequacy of India's current bowling capabilities to meet the demands of the upcoming international tournament. He specifically referenced the 2007 World Cup, where India experienced a surprising early elimination, failing to advance from the group stage, which was a major upset at the time. The warning underscores the critical role of a robust bowling lineup for the team's overall performance.

Reports indicate that the former BCCI official believes that nearly two decades after India's unexpected departure from the 2007 World Cup, history could repeat itself if fundamental issues within the bowling department remain unresolved. He urged the team management to undertake a comprehensive overhaul and strengthening of the bowling unit before the 2027 World Cup to prevent a similar setback.
