威斯康辛州霍華德的灣港高中（Bay Port High School）足球隊高年級生，每年都會參與一項特別的傳統，旨在表彰那些在他們成長過程中產生深遠影響的教師和學校職員。這項傳統因其感人的性質而聞名，報導指出，它常常讓受表彰的教師們感動落淚，凸顯了學生對教育工作者的深切感激。

這項由灣港高中足球隊高年級生發起的傳統，超越了球場上的競技，將重點放在社區與教育的連結。透過此舉，學生們有機會公開表達對老師們辛勤付出的認可，並感謝他們在學業和個人發展上的指導與支持。這不僅是一次簡單的表彰，更是一場充滿情感交流的活動。

與此同時，灣港高中足球隊在2026年10月2日星期五，也成為了一項重要社區活動的焦點。綠灣包裝工隊（Green Bay Packers）將其「Packers vs. Cancer」倡議帶到了高中層級，在灣港高中主場對陣西德佩爾高中（West De Pere High School Phantoms）的比賽中舉行。多位包裝工隊球員，包括Brandon Cisse、Dani Dennis-Sutton、Warren Brinson和Dillon Wade，也親臨現場，與球迷一同為UW Health | Carbone癌症中心募款並提升癌症意識。

資料顯示，灣港高中在2026年10月初展現了其社區參與和回饋的精神。一方面，足球隊高年級生透過感人的傳統向教育工作者致敬；另一方面，學校也與職業體育聯盟合作，共同推動重要的公共衛生倡議。這兩項活動雖然性質不同，但都體現了學校在培養學生品格、促進社區福祉方面的努力。

## English edition

Bay Port High School football seniors in Howard, Wisconsin, participate annually in a special tradition aimed at recognizing teachers and school staff who have profoundly impacted their growth. This tradition is renowned for its touching nature, with reports indicating it often moves honored educators to tears, highlighting the students' deep gratitude for their teachers.

This tradition, initiated by Bay Port High School football seniors, extends beyond athletic competition, focusing on the connection between community and education. Through this gesture, students have the opportunity to publicly acknowledge their teachers' hard work and thank them for their guidance and support in academic and personal development. It is not merely a simple recognition but an event filled with emotional exchange.

Concurrently, Bay Port High School's football team was also the focus of a significant community event on Friday, October 2, 2026. The Green Bay Packers brought their 'Packers vs. Cancer' initiative to the high school level, holding it during a game where Bay Port High School hosted the West De Pere High School Phantoms. Several Packers players, including Brandon Cisse, Dani Dennis-Sutton, Warren Brinson, and Dillon Wade, were present to join fans in raising funds and awareness for the UW Health | Carbone Cancer Center.

Data indicates that Bay Port High School demonstrated its spirit of community engagement and giving back in early October 2026. On one hand, football seniors honored educators through a touching tradition; on the other, the school collaborated with a professional sports league to promote an important public health initiative. While different in nature, both activities reflect the school's efforts in fostering student character and promoting community well-being.
