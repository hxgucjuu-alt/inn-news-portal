  # Industrial Digital Transformation and Global Geopolitics: AI Technology Applications and Progress in International Peace Mediation
  
  > This article summarizes recent global trends in industrial AI applications, the cultivation of net-zero green talent in Taiwan, and U.S.-led peace mediation efforts regarding the Russia-Ukraine war, providing a cross-disciplinary news digest.
  
  Recent global industrial and social issues have shown diverse developments, ranging from corporate digital transformation in South America to environmental talent cultivation and political controversies within Taiwan, as well as the latest diplomatic actions taken by the U.S. government regarding the Russia-Ukraine war. These events reflect a high degree of volatility in global technology application, sustainable transformation, and geopolitics. At the industrial level, AI technology has become key to optimizing corporate operations, while discussions regarding talent cultivation and policy transparency continue to draw attention across all sectors of society.
  
  Regarding progress in industrial digitalization, Reuters reports that the Brazilian company Ci&T is collaborating with Ford Motor Company to utilize artificial intelligence to optimize warehouse management systems in South America. This partnership symbolizes the deep integration of AI solutions into South American logistics and manufacturing, aiming to enhance supply chain efficiency through data analysis. Such technical collaboration between multinational corporations not only demonstrates the active investment in digital transformation within the South American market but also reflects the global industrial trend of AI moving from the experimental stage to substantive operational optimization.
  
  Meanwhile, Taiwan has also made significant progress in the field of sustainable development. The Ministry of Environment held the second national centralized examination for the "Net-Zero Green Talent Cultivation Course" on September 5, 2026. According to statistics from the National Institute of Environmental Research, nearly 1,000 trainees took the exam, with an attendance rate exceeding 92%. Minister of Environment Peng Chi-ming emphasized that net-zero transformation is a challenge that all industries must face, and that companies are in urgent need of talent with interdisciplinary knowledge. Data shows that the average age of examinees was 32, with those under 30 accounting for over 50%, indicating a high level of interest in green careers among the younger generation, with female participation slightly higher than male.
  
  In terms of political and social issues, Taiwan has recently been embroiled in a controversy surrounding public-funded education eligibility. The son of Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an, Chiang De-li, was granted public-funded exchange eligibility by the Taipei City Government, sparking public debate. Yin Wei, Commissioner of the Research, Development and Evaluation Commission of the Taipei City Government, and Legislator Shen Po-yang engaged in a public verbal exchange regarding whether the K-12 Education Administration should intervene in an investigation. Shen criticized the city government for releasing misinformation and questioned the relevant processing procedures. This incident reflects the public's high sensitivity toward the transparency of government resource allocation and the rights of children of political figures.
  
  Furthermore, in international geopolitics, U.S. President Trump announced on September 5 that he would dispatch special envoy Steve Witkoff and his son-in-law, Jared Kushner, to the Russia-Ukraine war zone to mediate. Trump stated that the U.S. has proposed a concrete peace concept aimed at ending the Russia-Ukraine war, which has now entered its fifth year. Although the U.S. side emphasized that this move is intended to facilitate an agreement, Trump did not disclose the specific details of the peace plan. Whether this diplomatic action can alleviate the long-standing military conflict remains to be verified by the results of subsequent negotiations.
  
  The aforementioned events indicate that the world is facing turning points in the fields of technology, the environment, and geopolitics. The application of AI in South American industry and the cultivation of green talent in Taiwan both point to a common need for "professional transformation," while the peace mediation in the Russia-Ukraine war and political infighting within Taiwan highlight the challenges of transparency and coordination in international situations and domestic governance. All parties should note that both AI applications and peace mediation are in the early stages of development or negotiation; the effectiveness of these efforts requires observation of subsequent execution details and the results of interactions between all parties, and it is premature to draw conclusions about their ultimate impact.
  
  Future observation will focus on: first, whether the AI warehouse optimization by Ci&T and Ford in South America can effectively reduce costs and expand the scale of application; second, whether Taiwan's net-zero green talent can successfully align with industrial demand and exert substantive influence after obtaining certification; and third, the progress of the U.S. envoy mission in the Russia-Ukraine region, and whether the "peace concept" can receive a substantive response from the warring parties, which will directly affect the stability of global geopolitics.

# 產業數位轉型與全球地緣政治：AI技術應用與跨國和平斡旋進展

> 本文整理近期全球產業AI應用趨勢、臺灣淨零綠領人才培育，以及美國針對俄烏戰爭發起的和平斡旋行動，提供跨領域時事摘要。


近期全球產業與社會議題呈現多元發展，從南美洲的企業數位轉型，到臺灣內部的環境人才培育及政治爭議，以及美國政府針對俄烏戰爭採取的最新外交行動，均反映出當前全球在技術應用、永續轉型與地緣政治上的高度變動。在產業層面，AI技術已成為企業最佳化營運的關鍵，而針對人才培育與政策透明度的討論，亦持續在社會各界引發關注。
在產業數位化進展方面，根據路透社報導，巴西企業 Ci&T 正與福特汽車（Ford）合作，利用人工智慧技術最佳化南美洲地區的倉儲管理系統。此項合作象徵著 AI 解決方案在南美物流與製造業的深度整合，旨在透過資料分析提升供應鏈效率。此類跨國企業的技術協作，不僅顯示出南美市場在數位轉型上的積極投入，也反映了 AI 在全球工業界從實驗階段邁向實質營運最佳化的趨勢。
與此同時，臺灣在永續發展領域亦有顯著進展。環境部於 2026 年 9 月 5 日舉辦「淨零綠領人才培育課程」第 2 次全國集中測驗。根據國家環境研究院統計，本次共有近千名學員應試，到考率超過 92%。環境部長彭啟明強調，淨零轉型是各行各業必須面對的挑戰，企業亟需具備跨域知能的人才。資料顯示，應試者平均年齡為 32 歲，其中 30 歲以下族群佔比超過五成，顯示年輕世代對於綠色職涯的高度關注，女性參與比例則略高於男性。
在政治與社會議題方面，臺灣近期則圍繞公費教育資格產生爭議。臺北市長蔣萬安之子蔣得立獲得北市府公費交換資格，引發輿論討論。臺北市府研考會主委殷瑋與立法委員沈伯洋針對國教署是否介入調查一事，出現公開言詞交鋒。沈伯洋批評市府發布假訊息，並對相關處理程式提出質疑，此事件反映出公眾對於政府資源分配透明度及政治人物子女權益議題的高度敏感性。
此外，國際地緣政治方面，美國總統川普於 9 月 5 日宣佈，派遣特使威特科夫（Steve Witkoff）與其女婿庫許納（Jared Kushner）前往俄烏戰區進行斡旋。川普表示，美方已提出具體的和平構想，目標在於結束已進入第 5 年的俄烏戰爭。儘管美方強調此舉旨在促成協議，但川普並未公開和平方案的具體細節。此項外交行動能否緩解長期的軍事衝突，仍有待後續談判結果驗證。
上述事件顯示，全球在技術、環境與地緣政治領域均面臨轉折點。AI 在南美產業的應用與臺灣綠領人才的培育，皆指向「專業轉型」的共同需求；而俄烏戰爭的和平斡旋與臺灣內部的政治攻防，則凸顯了國際局勢與國內治理在透明度與協調上的挑戰。各界應注意，AI 應用與和平斡旋均處於初期發展或談判階段，相關成效仍需觀察後續的執行細節與各方博弈結果，不宜過早對其最終影響進行定論。
未來觀察重點將聚焦於：第一，Ci&T 與福特在南美的 AI 倉儲最佳化是否能有效降低成本並擴大應用規模；第二，臺灣淨零綠領人才在取得認證後，能否順利與產業需求對接並發揮實質影響力；第三，美國特使團在俄烏地區的斡旋進展，以及該「和平想法」是否能獲得交戰雙方的實質回應，這將直接影響全球地緣政治的穩定性。
