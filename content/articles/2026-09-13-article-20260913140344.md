  # Trump Welcomes Chinese Automakers to Establish Plants in the U.S., Emphasizing the Need to Hire American Workers
  
  > U.S. President Trump recently addressed the issue of Chinese automakers establishing factories in the United States, stating that he does not oppose Chinese car companies entering the U.S. market as long as they follow the model of Japanese automakers and hire American workers. This stance contrasts with the generally opposing views held by Congress and the automotive industry.
  
  U.S. President Trump expressed a clear position on the issue of the Chinese automotive industry entering the U.S. market during an exclusive interview with Fox News on Friday. Although members of the U.S. Congress and domestic automakers are generally opposed to Chinese car companies, Trump publicly stated that he is open to the idea if Chinese firms are willing to open factories and conduct production within the United States. Trump explicitly pointed out that his core consideration is "job creation," citing the past development model of Japanese automakers in the U.S. as a comparison, and emphasized that as long as foreign companies are willing to hire American workers, they can earn his approval.
  
  According to reports compiled by Reuters and Yahoo Entertainment, Trump emphasized in the interview that the success of Japanese automakers in the U.S. proves that foreign-invested plants can effectively drive local employment; therefore, he believes that if Chinese automakers adopt the same model, it will be beneficial to the U.S. economy. Currently, there have been reports of Chinese electric vehicle giant BYD setting up plants in the Americas and attempting to sell products to the U.S. market. This shift in policy stance has undoubtedly sent shockwaves through the automotive industry, which has long been under the shadow of U.S.-China trade tensions, and has sparked widespread discussion regarding the future restructuring of the U.S. automotive supply chain.
  
  However, there remains significant uncertainty regarding the implementation details and policy boundaries of Trump's remarks. Although Trump himself expressed an attitude of "no objection," there is a notable gap between this and the hardline trade policies toward China held within the U.S. Congress. Furthermore, how the "Japanese model" mentioned by Trump will be specifically applied to Chinese automakers—and whether it will involve technology transfers, subsidy reviews, or national security assessments—has not yet been addressed by the White House with specific supporting regulations or executive orders. Market analysts point out that whether these remarks will translate into actual trade policy remains to be seen, depending on subsequent interactions between Congress and the executive branch, and whether the Trump team will formulate specific entry conditions for Chinese automakers.
  
  If this policy direction is implemented, it will have a profound impact on the North American automotive industry. First, it will directly challenge the market share and competitive advantages of existing U.S. domestic automakers and may alter the labor structure of the American automotive industry. Second, for neighboring countries like Canada, this policy may be linked to recent U.S. trade sanctions against the country. According to economic data, the U.S. imposed a 50% tariff on Canadian imports on August 22, citing reasons including unfair treatment of dairy products, alcoholic beverages, and the automotive industry. If Chinese automakers establish plants in the U.S. in the future, how this will affect industrial chain coordination within the North American Free Trade Area will become a focus of attention for all sectors.
  
  Views from the industry and academia are divided regarding Trump's perspective. Supporters believe that introducing foreign investment to set up factories can stimulate the local economy and create jobs, aligning with the "America First" economic goal. Opponents, however, worry that Chinese automakers might bypass tariff barriers through local production, thereby threatening the long-term survival of the U.S. automotive industry and even bringing concerns regarding data security and technology leakage. Experts caution against over-interpreting Trump's personal remarks, as under the U.S. legal framework, the formulation of trade policy still requires rigorous inter-departmental review and congressional oversight; a single statement does not equate to a finalized policy, and industry investors should remain cautious.
  
  Key points for future observation include whether the Trump administration will issue formal executive guidance on Chinese automakers establishing plants in the U.S., and whether the U.S. Congress will take legislative action to restrict or regulate such investments. Additionally, as U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has recently been actively visiting Latin America and promoting trade agreements with countries such as Colombia, Peru, and Ecuador, whether the U.S.'s overall trade layout in the Western Hemisphere will conflict with the policy of "Chinese automakers establishing plants in the U.S." will be a key variable affecting North American industrial policy and international relations in the coming months.

# 川普表態歡迎中國車企赴美設廠　強調需僱用美國勞工

> 美國總統川普近日針對中國汽車製造商赴美設廠議題表態，稱只要比照日本車企模式並聘用美國勞工，他不反對中國車企進入美國市場。此舉與國會及汽車業普遍反對態度形成對比。


美國總統川普於週五接受《福斯新聞》（Fox News）專訪時，針對中國汽車產業進入美國市場的議題表達了鮮明的立場。儘管目前美國國會議員及國內汽車製造商普遍對中國車企持反對意見，川普卻公開表示，若中國車企有意願在美國境內開設工廠並進行生產，他對此持開放態度。川普明確指出，其核心考量在於「就業機會」，並以日本車企過去在美國的發展模式作為對照，強調只要外資企業願意僱用美國工人，便能獲得其認可。
根據《路透社》與《Yahoo Entertainment》整理的報導，川普在訪談中強調，日本車企在美國的成功經驗證明瞭外資設廠能有效帶動在地就業，因此他認為中國汽車製造商若能採取相同模式，將有助於美國經濟。目前，中國電動車巨頭比亞迪（BYD）已傳出在美洲設廠並嘗試將產品銷往美國市場的動向。此一政策立場的轉變，無疑為長期處於美中貿易緊張局勢下的汽車產業投下震撼彈，也引發了外界對於未來美國汽車市場供應鏈重組的廣泛討論。
然而，關於川普此番言論的執行細節與政策邊界，目前仍存有諸多不確定性。雖然川普本人表達了「沒意見」的態度，但這與美國國會內部強硬的對中貿易政策存在顯著落差。此外，川普所提的「比照日本模式」具體將如何落實於中國車企，是否涉及技術轉移、補貼審查或國家安全評估，目前白宮尚未提出具體的配套法規或行政命令。市場分析師指出，此言論是否會轉化為實際的貿易政策，仍需觀察後續國會與行政部門的互動，以及川普團隊是否會針對中國車企制定專屬的准入條件。
此政策導向若付諸實行，將對北美汽車產業產生深遠影響。首先，這將直接挑戰現有美國本土汽車製造商的市場份額與競爭優勢，並可能改變美國汽車產業的勞動力結構。其次，對於加拿大等鄰國而言，這項政策可能與近期美國對加國實施的貿易制裁產生連動。根據經濟資料顯示，美國於8月22日曾對加拿大進口商品課徵50%關稅，理由包括對乳製品、酒精飲料及汽車產業的不公對待。若未來中國車企在美設廠，將如何影響北美自由貿易區內的產業鏈協調，將成為各界關注的焦點。
針對川普的觀點，產業界與學界看法分歧。支持者認為，引進外資設廠能活絡在地經濟並創造就業，符合「美國優先」的經濟目標；反對者則擔憂，中國車企可能透過設廠繞過關稅壁壘，進而威脅美國汽車產業的長期生存，甚至帶來資料安全與技術外流的隱憂。專家提醒，不應過度解讀川普的個人表態，因為在美國法律架構下，貿易政策的制定仍需經過嚴謹的跨部門審查與國會監督，單一發言並不等同於政策定案，產業投資者應保持觀望。
後續觀察重點在於，川普政府是否會針對中國車企赴美設廠發布正式的行政指導原則，以及美國國會是否會採取立法行動以限制或規範此類投資。此外，隨著美國國務卿魯比歐（Marco Rubio）近期積極訪問拉丁美洲並推動與哥倫比亞、秘魯及厄瓜多等國的貿易協定，美國在西半球的整體貿易佈局是否會與「中國車企在美設廠」的政策產生衝突，將是未來幾個月內影響北美產業政策與國際關係的關鍵變數。
