  # Regional Security and Disaster Resilience in Southeast Asia: The Expansion of Scam Hubs and Disaster Response Assessment
  
  > Southeast Asia is currently facing the severe challenge of rampant cross-border scam crimes. Despite ongoing efforts by various nations to crack down on illegal compounds, these criminal networks continue to expand. Simultaneously, the region has demonstrated high resilience in disaster preparedness and international cooperation mechanisms regarding natural disasters.
  
  Southeast Asia is currently presenting a complex pattern of regional development: on one hand, there is the threat to social security posed by cross-border crime, and on the other, a high level of preparedness for natural disasters. According to an AP News report, although governments across Southeast Asia have launched multiple raids against illegal scam compounds, these criminal hubs continue to expand within the region and have not been completely eradicated despite law enforcement pressure.
  
  Meanwhile, as reported by The Economic Times citing a Gallup survey, while Southeast Asia is a global hotspot for frequent natural disasters, the region performs exceptionally well in disaster preparedness and response mechanisms, becoming a global benchmark in the field of disaster prevention.
  
  Regarding the issue of cross-border scams, sources collectively confirm the necessity and practical difficulties of "transnational law enforcement." The AP News report points out that the illegal industrial chains of scam compounds have penetrated multiple countries in the region. For example, scam compounds in Myanmar have been found to hold foreign nationals, who were subsequently repatriated through hubs such as Mae Sot International Airport in Thailand. These cases reflect how criminal syndicates exploit national borders and law enforcement loopholes to hide their bases in areas with weak regulation, making it difficult for the law enforcement actions of a single country to root them out entirely.
  
  Regarding information discrepancies and areas awaiting confirmation, there remains a gap in information across media reports concerning the specific distribution of scam compounds, the exact nationality ratios of victims, and the actual execution effectiveness of intelligence-sharing mechanisms among governments. The AP News report focuses on the persistence of criminal compounds and the repatriation process, while Asia Times provides broader regional commentary, highlighting the links between these criminal activities and regional political instability as well as border management capabilities. It is currently impossible to confirm whether there are deep-seated political obstacles in the specific cooperation agreements between governments to combat scam compounds, which remains a focus of international attention.
  
  In terms of disaster prevention and industrial impact, the experience of Southeast Asia provides a different perspective. According to the Gallup survey, the reason Southeast Asia can maintain high resilience amidst frequent natural disasters is primarily attributed to the ASEAN-led regional cooperation framework, effective early warning systems, and relatively sound disaster financing channels. This finding offers important insights for policymakers in the region, demonstrating that through the collective action of regional organizations, even developing countries with relatively limited resources can effectively reduce the economic losses and social impacts caused by disasters. This model is becoming a focal point for research in the international disaster prevention academic community.
  
  However, experts warn against over-interpreting the aforementioned survey results. Although Southeast Asia has received positive evaluations for its overall disaster preparedness, this does not mean that all countries in the region possess equal response capabilities. There are vast differences in geography, economic development, and infrastructure construction among these nations; some remote areas or island nations still face severe resource allocation challenges when encountering extreme weather events. Furthermore, the "high score" in disaster preparedness reflects policy-level planning and institutional establishment, but at the practical execution level, it still faces the unpredictability brought about by intensifying climate change.
  
  Future observations will focus on two aspects: first, how Southeast Asian countries can address increasingly complex cross-border scam networks through closer judicial assistance, particularly regarding security management in border areas such as Myanmar and Thailand; and second, whether the successful experience of ASEAN in the field of disaster prevention can be further transformed into a regional governance model for addressing non-traditional security threats (such as criminal networks). The international community will continue to monitor the balance between regional security and disaster resilience in Southeast Asia and evaluate the actual effectiveness of these policy tools under long-term implementation.

# 東南亞區域安全與防災韌性：詐騙園區擴張與災害應變評估

> 東南亞地區近期面臨跨境詐騙犯罪猖獗的嚴峻挑戰，儘管各國持續打擊非法園區，但犯罪網路仍不斷擴張；與此同時，該區域在自然災害的防災準備與國際合作機制上，展現出高度韌性。


東南亞地區近期呈現出複雜的區域發展樣態，一方面是跨境犯罪帶來的社會治安威脅，另一方面則是針對自然災害的高度防災準備能力。根據美聯社（AP News）的報導，儘管東南亞各國政府已針對非法詐騙園區展開多次掃蕩行動，但這些犯罪中心在區域內仍呈現擴張趨勢，並未因執法壓力而徹底根除。
與此同時，根據《經濟時報》（The Economic Times）引述蓋洛普（Gallup）的調查顯示，東南亞地區雖為全球自然災害頻發的熱點，但該區域在防災準備與應變機制上表現優異，成為全球防災領域的參考指標。
在跨境詐騙議題上，各方來源共同確認的進展在於「跨國執法」的必要性與實務困難。美聯社報導指出，詐騙園區的非法產業鏈已深入區域內多國，例如位於緬甸的詐騙園區曾被發現有外籍人士受困，隨後經由泰國美索國際機場（Mae Sot International Airport）等樞紐進行人員遣返。這些案例反映出詐騙集團利用國境邊界與執法漏洞，將犯罪基地隱匿於監管薄弱的地區，使得單一國家的執法行動往往難以斬草除根。
關於資訊的差異與尚待確認之處，目前針對詐騙園區的具體分佈資料、受害者的確切國籍比例，以及各國政府在情報共享機制上的實際執行效能，各媒體報導中仍存在資訊落差。美聯社的報導側重於犯罪園區的持續存在與人員遣返過程，而《亞洲時報》（Asia Times）則提供更廣泛的區域評論，指出這些犯罪活動與區域內政局不穩定性、邊境管理能力之間的連動關係。目前尚無法確認各國政府在打擊詐騙園區的具體合作協議中，是否存在深層的政治阻礙，這也是國際社會持續關注的焦點。
在防災與產業影響方面，東南亞的經驗提供了不同視角。根據蓋洛普的調查，東南亞之所以能在頻繁的自然災害中保持高度韌性，主要歸功於東協（ASEAN）主導的區域合作框架、有效的早期預警系統，以及相對健全的災害金融管道。這項發現對區域內的政策制定者具有重要啟示，顯示透過區域性組織的集體行動，即使是資源相對受限的開發中國家，也能有效降低災害帶來的經濟損失與社會衝擊，此一模式正成為國際防災學界研究的重點。
然而，對於上述調查結果，專家提醒不應過度解讀。雖然東南亞在整體防災準備上獲得正面評價，但這並不代表區域內所有國家皆具備同等的應變能力。各國的地理環境、經濟發展程度與基礎設施建設差異極大，部分偏遠地區或島嶼國家在面臨極端氣候事件時，仍面臨嚴峻的資源配置挑戰。此外，防災準備的「高分」反映的是政策層面的規劃與制度建立，但在實際執行層面，仍需面對氣候變遷加劇所帶來的不可預測性。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於兩個面向：首先是東南亞各國如何透過更緊密的司法互助，應對日益複雜的跨境詐騙網路，特別是針對緬甸、泰國等邊境地區的治安管理；其次是東協在防災領域的成功經驗，是否能進一步轉化為應對非傳統安全威脅（如犯罪網路）的區域治理模式。國際社會將持續監測東南亞在區域安全與防災韌性之間的平衡，並評估這些政策工具在長期執行下的實際成效。
