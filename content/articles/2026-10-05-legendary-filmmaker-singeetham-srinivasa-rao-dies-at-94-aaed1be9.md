印度電影界痛失一位巨匠。資料顯示，傳奇電影製作人辛格瑟姆·斯里尼瓦薩·拉奧於週六在清奈逝世，享耆壽94歲。他的離世標誌著印度電影一個時代的終結，他以其獨特的敘事風格和對非傳統題材的探索，為影壇留下了深遠的影響。

斯里尼瓦薩·拉奧的職業生涯橫跨六十餘載，期間他執導了多部廣受好評的電影，作品類型廣泛且風格多變。他以其對電影藝術的熱情和不斷創新的精神，在印度電影史上佔據了重要地位。

他最為人所知的作品包括無聲喜劇《無聲的婚禮》（Pushpaka Vimanam）、時間旅行冒險片《阿迪亞369》（Aditya 369），以及《孔雀》（Mayur）和《巴伊拉瓦島》（Bhairava Dweepam）等。這些電影不僅展現了他卓越的導演才華，也體現了他勇於挑戰傳統敘事模式的膽識。

斯里尼瓦薩·拉奧的電影作品跨越多種語言和類型，從喜劇到奇幻，從歷史劇到科幻，他總能以獨特的視角講述引人入勝的故事。他的六十年電影遺產將繼續啟發未來的電影製作人，並為全球觀眾所銘記。

## English edition

The Indian film industry mourns the loss of a titan. Legendary filmmaker Singeetham Srinivasa Rao reportedly passed away in Chennai on Saturday at the age of 94. His demise marks the end of an era in Indian cinema, leaving behind a profound legacy through his unique storytelling and exploration of unconventional themes.

Srinivasa Rao's illustrious career spanned over six decades, during which he directed numerous critically acclaimed films across a wide range of genres and styles. His passion for cinematic art and continuous innovation secured his significant place in the history of Indian cinema.

Among his most celebrated works are the wordless comedy "Pushpaka Vimanam," the time-travel adventure "Aditya 369," as well as "Mayur" and "Bhairava Dweepam." These films not only showcased his exceptional directorial talent but also his courage to challenge conventional narrative structures.

Singeetham Srinivasa Rao's filmography traversed multiple languages and genres, from comedy to fantasy, historical drama to science fiction. He consistently captivated audiences with his distinctive perspective and compelling narratives. His six-decade cinematic legacy will continue to inspire future filmmakers and be remembered by audiences worldwide.
