  # EU Strategic Autonomy and Geopolitical Challenges: The Transition from Security Defense to Economic Resilience
  
  > The European Union is deepening defense cooperation through the "Strategic Compass" while facing multiple challenges, including pressure from Trump's policies, economic dependence on China, and extreme weather. The region is actively adjusting its industrial and diplomatic strategies to cope with global uncertainty.
  
  The European Union is currently at a critical crossroads of geopolitical and economic transformation. According to public information, the EU has launched the "Strategic Compass" initiative, which aims to strengthen defense collaboration among member states and enhance Europe's operational capabilities in international security affairs. This move is rooted in the dramatic shifts in the global landscape, including the security threats posed by the Russia-Ukraine war and the potential impact of changes in U.S. politics on transatlantic relations. At the same time, EU nations are attempting to strike a balance between energy security and economic growth amid the challenges of climate change, while ensuring the stability of critical resource supply chains through deepened cooperation with regions such as Greenland.
  
  Multiple sources confirm that the EU is currently actively promoting the "Strategic Compass" plan to integrate the military resources and defense strategies of its member states. This plan is seen as an important milestone for Europe toward military autonomy, aimed at reducing excessive reliance on external forces and enabling more rapid responses to sudden security crises. Furthermore, as Lithuania prepares to assume the rotating presidency of the Council of the EU, its foreign minister has publicly called for the EU to further reduce its economic dependence on China, indicating that the importance placed on supply chain security and economic sovereignty within the EU is rising. At the corporate level, although the outlook for European companies appears slightly dim due to global uncertainty, the market continues to monitor the development of various innovative industries.
  
  However, there are significant differences in how various media outlets interpret and focus on EU policies. Some international media emphasize whether the "Strategic Compass" signifies the formation of an "EU Army." Perspectives on this issue vary among countries, with some member states concerned that such a move might overlap with the functions of NATO or trigger sovereignty disputes. Additionally, regarding the issue of economic dependence on China, although countries like Lithuania have taken a clear stance, there remain conflicting interests among EU member states regarding the specific degree and pace of trade decoupling, and there is currently no unified implementation timetable. Regarding investment cooperation with Greenland, despite the indirect influence of pressure from Trump's policies, the specific scale of investment and details of cooperation remain to be confirmed through subsequent negotiations.
  
  These policy adjustments have had a profound impact on European industry and society. First, in the defense industry, the EU plans to increase investment to enhance autonomous production capabilities, which will drive the development of related technology and manufacturing sectors. Second, in the energy sector, facing heatwaves and abnormal high temperatures caused by extreme weather, European countries are accelerating the adjustment of their energy structures to cope with the public health and livelihood challenges brought about by climate change. Economically, affected by global uncertainty, corporate investment confidence has been suppressed; however, by deepening intra-regional trade and the layout of critical resources, the EU is attempting to build a more resilient economic system to withstand the dramatic fluctuations of external markets.
  
  Nevertheless, the outside world should not over-interpret the EU's policy shift. Although the "Strategic Compass" aims to strengthen collaboration, implementing it into concrete military actions or a unified foreign policy still faces complex legal and political constraints. EU member states have varying defense budgets, strategic interests, and historical backgrounds; achieving the goal of complete military autonomy remains a significant challenge in the short term. Furthermore, while extreme weather caused by climate change is a realistic threat, the contradiction between carbon emission control and economic development in various countries remains a major obstacle to policy implementation, and overly optimistic forecasts may overlook the difficulties of internal coordination.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on the policy execution of Lithuania after it assumes the rotating presidency, particularly regarding specific actions on economic policy toward China and the allocation of defense budgets. Additionally, the interaction between the EU and the U.S. under the influence of Trump's policies, as well as the follow-up response to the Russia-Ukraine war, will be key to determining Europe's future security architecture. The market will also closely monitor the financial performance and capital expenditure adjustments of European companies in the face of global uncertainty, as these data will reflect the actual effectiveness of the EU's economic resilience policies.

# 歐盟戰略自主與地緣政治挑戰：從安全防衛到經濟韌性之轉型

> 歐盟正透過「戰略指南針」深化防衛合作，同時面臨川普政策壓力、對中經濟依賴及極端氣候等多重挑戰，區域內部正積極調整產業與外交策略以應對全球不確定性。


歐盟近期正處於地緣政治與經濟轉型的關鍵十字路口。根據公開資訊顯示，歐盟已啟動「戰略指南針」（Strategic Compass）計畫，旨在強化成員國之間的防衛協作，並提升歐洲在國際安全事務中的行動力。此舉背景源於全球局勢的劇烈變動，包括俄烏戰爭帶來的安全威脅，以及美國政局變動對跨大西洋關係潛在的衝擊。與此同時，歐盟各國亦在氣候變遷的挑戰下，試圖在能源安全與經濟成長之間尋求平衡，並透過深化與格陵蘭等地區的合作，確保關鍵資源的供應鏈穩定。
多項來源共同確認，歐盟目前正積極推動「戰略指南針」計畫，以整合各成員國的軍事資源與防衛戰略。此計畫被視為歐洲邁向軍事自主的重要里程碑，旨在減少對外部勢力的過度依賴，並在應對突發安全危機時能更迅速地做出反應。此外，立陶宛即將接任歐盟輪值主席國，該國外交部長已公開呼籲歐盟應進一步降低對中國的經濟依賴，顯示出歐盟內部對於供應鏈安全與經濟主權的重視程度正日益提高。在企業層面，儘管歐洲企業展望因全球不確定性而略顯黯淡，但市場仍持續關注各項創新產業的發展。
然而，各媒體對於歐盟政策的詮釋與關注焦點存在顯著差異。部分國際媒體強調「戰略指南針」是否意味著「歐盟軍」的成形，針對此議題，各國觀點分歧，部分成員國憂心此舉可能與北約（NATO）的功能重疊，或引發主權爭議。此外，關於對中經濟依賴的議題，雖然立陶宛等國立場明確，但歐盟內部對於具體的貿易脫鉤程度與節奏，仍存在不同成員國之間的利益拉扯，目前尚無統一的執行時間表。對於格陵蘭的投資合作，儘管受到川普政策壓力的間接影響，但具體的投資規模與合作細節仍有待後續協商確認。
這些政策調整對歐洲產業與社會產生了深遠影響。首先，在防衛產業方面，歐盟擬增加投資以提升自主生產能力，這將帶動相關科技與製造業的發展；其次，在能源領域，面對極端氣候帶來的熱浪與異常高溫，歐洲各國正加速調整能源結構，以應對氣候變遷帶來的公共衛生與民生挑戰。經濟方面，受到全球不確定性影響，企業投資信心受到抑制，但透過深化區域內貿易與關鍵資源佈局，歐盟試圖建立更具韌性的經濟體系，以抵禦外部市場的劇烈波動。
然而，外界不應過度解讀歐盟的政策轉向。儘管「戰略指南針」旨在強化協作，但要落實為具體的軍事行動或統一外交政策，仍面臨複雜的法律與政治限制。歐盟各成員國的國防預算、戰略利益與歷史背景各異，要達成完全一致的軍事自主目標，短期內仍有極大挑戰。此外，氣候變遷帶來的極端天氣雖然是現實威脅，但各國在碳排放控制與經濟發展之間的矛盾，依然是政策執行上的重大阻礙，過度樂觀的預測可能忽略了內部協調的困難度。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於立陶宛接任輪值主席國後的政策執行力，特別是在對中經濟政策與防衛預算分配上的具體作為。此外，歐盟與美國在川普政策影響下的互動，以及針對俄烏戰爭的後續應對，將是決定歐洲未來安全架構的關鍵。市場亦將密切關注歐洲企業在面對全球不確定性時的財報表現與資本支出調整，這些資料將反映出歐盟經濟韌性政策的實際成效。
