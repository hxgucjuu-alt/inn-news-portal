伊朗於2026年10月4日表示，具有戰略重要性的荷姆茲海峽將維持關閉，直至美國滿足德黑蘭所提出的條件。此一聲明由伊朗外交部發出，強調重啟該海峽一直是伊朗的主要目標。

根據報導，伊朗國營媒體引述該國議會的說法指出，荷姆茲海峽的重啟取決於美國履行與伊朗在六月簽署的一項臨時協議中所列出的七項條件。伊朗議會議長及該國首席談判代表也曾表示，海峽將持續關閉，直至美國履行其承諾。

儘管伊朗將重啟荷姆茲海峽視為其主要目標，資料顯示美國方面則持續將焦點放在核談判上。此一情勢凸顯了兩國在優先事項上的分歧，可能進一步影響地區穩定。

荷姆茲海峽是全球重要的石油運輸咽喉，連接波斯灣與阿曼灣，其關閉對全球能源市場和國際貿易具有深遠影響。伊朗的此項決定，無疑將加劇與美國之間的緊張關係。

## English edition

Iran announced on October 4, 2026, that the strategically vital Strait of Hormuz will remain closed until the United States meets conditions set by Tehran. This statement, issued by Iran's Foreign Ministry, underscored that reopening the strait has been Iran's main goal.

According to reports, Iranian state media, citing the country's parliament, indicated that the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz hinges on the United States fulfilling seven Iranian conditions outlined in a June interim agreement. Iran's parliament speaker and top negotiator had also previously stated that the strait would remain closed until the US fulfills its commitments.

While Iran has identified the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz as its main objective, data indicates that the United States continues to focus on nuclear talks. This situation highlights a divergence in priorities between the two nations, potentially impacting regional stability.

Connecting the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman, the Strait of Hormuz is a critical global oil transit chokepoint, and its closure has profound implications for global energy markets and international trade. Iran's decision is expected to escalate tensions with the United States.
