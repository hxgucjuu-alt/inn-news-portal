俄羅斯國防部於週六（2026年10月3日）發布一段影片，其中一名被俘的烏克蘭武裝部隊士兵維塔利·莫辛（Vitaly Mosin）表示，弗拉基米爾·澤倫斯基（Vladimir Zelensky）的政權必須被摧毀。此一聲明由俄羅斯衛星通訊社（Sputnik）報導，並於當日發布。

根據俄羅斯國防部發布的影片，莫辛被指認為烏克蘭第42旅的士兵，據報導他在克拉斯諾阿爾梅斯克（Krasnoarmeysk）的戰鬥中被俘或投降。俄羅斯衛星通訊社指出，莫斯科方面發布了這段影片，並提供了莫辛的具體言論。

此事件發生之際，過去曾有報導指出部分烏克蘭士兵對澤倫斯基總統的某些政策表達不滿。例如，2025年7月，基輔獨立報（Kyiv Independent）報導稱一些烏克蘭士兵批評澤倫斯基的反腐打擊行動。此外，2026年7月，紐約時報（The New York Times）也曾報導澤倫斯基在軍事領導和戰略上的決定可能削弱其地位。

然而，維塔利·莫辛的聲明是在俄羅斯國防部發布的影片中做出，其具體情況和動機需進一步查證。值得注意的是，報導此事件的俄羅斯衛星通訊社網域被標記為需要人工覆核其可信度。

## English edition

The Russian Defense Ministry released a video on Saturday, October 3, 2026, in which Vitaly Mosin, a captured Ukrainian armed forces soldier, stated that Vladimir Zelensky's regime must be destroyed. This statement was reported by Sputnik and published on the same day.

According to the video released by the Russian Defense Ministry, Mosin was identified as a soldier from the Ukrainian 42nd Brigade, reportedly captured or surrendered during fighting in Krasnoarmeysk. Sputnik indicated that Moscow released this footage, detailing Mosin's specific remarks.

This incident occurs amidst past reports suggesting some Ukrainian soldiers have expressed dissatisfaction with certain policies of President Zelensky. For instance, in July 2025, the Kyiv Independent reported that some Ukrainian soldiers criticized Zelensky's anti-corruption crackdown. Furthermore, in July 2026, The New York Times reported that Zelensky's decisions regarding military leadership and strategy could potentially weaken his position.

However, Vitaly Mosin's statement was made in a video released by the Russian Defense Ministry, and the specific circumstances and motivations behind it require further verification. It is notable that the domain of Sputnik, which reported this incident, is flagged as requiring manual credibility review.
