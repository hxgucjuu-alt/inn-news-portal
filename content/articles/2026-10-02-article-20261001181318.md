  # EU Expands Mediterranean Naval Mission, Targeting Russia's "Shadow Fleet"
  
  > EU ambassadors have approved the expansion of a military mission in the Mediterranean aimed at cracking down on Russia's "shadow fleet." This move will be formally adopted at the EU Economic and Financial Affairs Council meeting on October 9 and subsequently published in the Official Journal.
  
  By the end of September 2026, several major developments emerged in the global geopolitical and technological spheres. In Europe, the EU has taken concrete action against Russia's efforts to circumvent sanctions. According to a report by Euronews, EU ambassadors reached a consensus on Wednesday to expand the scope of their naval mission in the Mediterranean, with the primary objective of targeting Russia's "shadow fleet." This adjustment to the military mission aims to strengthen the monitoring and interception of Russian maritime transport to counter its evasion of international sanctions.
  
  The plan has received preliminary approval and is expected to be formally adopted at the EU Economic and Financial Affairs Council meeting on October 9. It will then be published in the Official Journal of the European Union, marking a further escalation in the EU's regional security and sanctions enforcement efforts.
  
  Meanwhile, the global technology industry is undergoing drastic changes. Qualcomm is reportedly planning to adjust its foundry strategy for its next-generation flagship processor, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6, in an attempt to end TSMC's status as its exclusive supplier. According to a report by the tech media outlet Techbang, faced with the continuously soaring prices of TSMC's advanced processes, Qualcomm is in deep negotiations with Samsung Electronics, planning to adopt a "product segmentation" dual-foundry model. It is expected that the flagship SoC, set to debut at the end of 2026, will have its high-end version produced by TSMC, while the standard version may shift to Samsung's 2nm process.
  
  This move is seen as a major strategic shift for Qualcomm in balancing cost control and production capacity, and it also highlights the intensifying battle for global advanced semiconductor manufacturing capacity.
  
  In the United States, technology policy and market dynamics are being updated simultaneously. On September 29, U.S. President Trump signed an executive order requiring U.S. government agencies to officially change the term "Artificial Intelligence" (AI) to "Super Intelligence" (SI) in all official documents, reports, and public communications. According to a report by iThome, the Trump administration believes that current frontier systems have moved beyond simple imitation and automation, possessing the ability to amplify human creativity; therefore, "Super Intelligence" more accurately reflects the essence of the technology.
  
  Additionally, the online brokerage Robinhood launched its "Robinhood Agents" service this week. CEO Vlad Tenev pointed out that the service aims to lower the barrier for retail investors to enter automated trading, though the market has also expressed high concern regarding the security challenges brought about by the rise of AI-automated trading.
  
  The aforementioned events have a direct impact on global industries and policies. The EU's decision to expand its naval mission directly impacts Russia's energy exports and logistics chains, potentially triggering rising tensions in regional waters. Regarding the technology supply chain, if Qualcomm implements its dual-foundry strategy, it will affect TSMC's revenue structure and redefine Samsung's competitive position in the advanced process market. Furthermore, while the U.S. government's policy of renaming AI to SI is currently limited to administrative agencies, if it expands to industry standards in the future, it could trigger a global debate within the tech community regarding terminology definitions and regulatory frameworks.
  
  Moreover, the proliferation of AI agent services in financial markets is forcing regulatory agencies to accelerate the establishment of security protection mechanisms for automated trading to prevent potential systemic risks.

# 歐盟擴大地中海海軍任務，目標鎖定俄羅斯影子艦隊

> 歐盟大使已批准擴大在地中海的軍事任務，旨在打擊俄羅斯的「影子艦隊」。此舉將於10月9日經歐盟財長會議正式採納，並於隨後發布於官方公報。


2026年9月底，全球地緣政治與科技領域出現多項重大進展。在歐洲地區，歐盟針對俄羅斯規避制裁的行為採取具體行動。根據歐洲新聞臺（Euronews）報導，歐盟大使已於週三達成共識，同意擴大在地中海的海軍任務範圍，主要目標為針對俄羅斯的「影子艦隊」（shadow fleet）。這項軍事任務的調整，旨在加強對俄羅斯海上運輸的監控與攔截，以應對其規避國際制裁的行為。
該計畫已獲得初步批准，預計在10月9日的歐盟經濟與財政部長會議上進行正式採納，隨後將刊登於歐盟官方公報，標誌著歐盟在區域安全與制裁執行力度的進一步升級。
與此同時，全球科技產業正經歷劇烈變動。高通（Qualcomm）傳出將調整其下一代旗艦處理器 Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 的代工策略，試圖終結臺積電的獨家供應地位。根據科技媒體 Techbang 的報導，面對臺積電先進製程報價持續飆漲，高通正與三星電子進行深度磋商，計畫採取「產品分級」的雙代工模式。預計該款於2026年底亮相的旗艦 SoC，將由臺積電負責高階版本，而標準版則可能轉向三星的 2 奈米製程生產。
此舉被視為高通在成本控制與產能平衡間的重大戰略轉向，亦突顯了全球半導體先進製程產能爭奪戰的白熱化。
在美國方面，科技政策與市場動態同步更新。美國總統川普於9月29日簽署行政命令，要求美國政府機構在所有官方檔案、報告及公開溝通中，將「人工智慧」（Artificial Intelligence，AI）一詞正式改為「超級智慧」（Super Intelligence，SI）。根據 iThome 的報導，川普政府認為現今的前沿系統已超越單純的模仿與自動化，具備放大人類創造力的能力，因此「超級智慧」更能準確反映技術本質。
此外，線上券商羅賓漢（Robinhood）於本週推出「羅賓漢AI代理」（Robinhood Agents）服務，執行長弗拉基米爾·特內夫指出，該服務旨在降低散戶進入自動化交易的門檻，但市場同時也對AI自動交易興起帶來的安全挑戰表示高度關注。
上述事件對全球產業與政策產生直接影響。歐盟擴大海軍任務的決定，直接衝擊俄羅斯的能源出口與物流鏈，可能引發區域海域的緊張局勢升溫。在科技供應鏈方面，高通若落實雙代工策略，將對臺積電的營收結構產生影響，並重新定義三星在先進製程市場的競爭地位。而美國政府將AI更名為SI的政策，雖目前僅限於行政機構，但若未來擴及產業標準，恐將引發全球科技界對於術語定義與監管框架的重新討論。
此外，AI代理服務在金融市場的普及，則迫使監管機構必須加速建立針對自動化交易的安全防護機制，以防範潛在的系統性風險。
然而，各界對上述事件的解讀仍存在差異與限制。關於高通重啟雙代工模式的訊息，目前多源自韓國半導體供應鏈的傳聞，尚未獲得高通或三星官方的正式證實，因此其最終產能分配比例仍具高度不確定性。此外，美國將AI改名為SI的行政命令，雖具備官方強制力，但能否在學術界與國際組織中獲得廣泛認同，仍有待觀察。同時，歐盟擴大海軍任務的具體執行細節，如攔截手段與法律授權範圍，在正式公報發布前仍有變數，不應過度解讀為立即性的軍事衝突升級。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三點：首先，10月9日歐盟財長會議後，關於「影子艦隊」的具體制裁執行細則與各成員國的配合度；其次，高通與三星在2奈米製程上的合作進展，以及該策略是否會引發其他晶片設計大廠的跟進；最後，美國政府將「超級智慧」術語推廣至民間企業的力度，以及此舉是否會影響全球AI產業的標準制定與政策監管走向。各界需持續關註上述領域的後續政策公告與企業財報資料，以掌握全球科技與地緣政治的最新動態。
