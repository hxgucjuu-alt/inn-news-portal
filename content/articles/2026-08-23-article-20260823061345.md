  # US-Canada Trade Talks Collapse, US Imposes 50% Tariffs on Canadian Goods
  
  > After weeks of intensive negotiations failed to produce a trade agreement between the US and Canada, the US began imposing 50% tariffs on approximately CA$28 billion worth of Canadian goods on Saturday local time. Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney announced a suspension of talks and vowed "dollar-for-dollar" retaliation.
  
  Trade negotiations between the US and Canada collapsed on Friday evening local time, with the White House subsequently announcing the imposition of 50% tariffs on certain Canadian imports starting Saturday. The decision marks a serious setback in the economic relationship between these two long-standing allies and poses an unprecedented challenge to the North American free trade system. According to multiple international media reports, Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney held an immediate press conference after the talks broke down, announcing that Canada would take matching retaliatory measures and had ordered Canadian negotiators to return to Ottawa.
  
  Based on reports from multiple media outlets including Chinese state media China.org.cn, Canada's CTV News, Bangladesh's The Business Standard, and US-based CNBC, the two sides had engaged in intensive trade consultations over the past several weeks in an attempt to reach an agreement before the deadline. However, the US government suddenly introduced several new conditions at the tail end of the negotiations, which became the final straw that broke the talks. Prime Minister Carney stated clearly at the press conference that the changes proposed by the US at the last minute were "unfair, uneconomical, and cast doubt on the reliability of any agreement," leading Canada to suspend all trade negotiations.
  
  Regarding the specific scale of the tariff impact, media reports show some discrepancies. CTV News indicated that the total value of goods affected by the new tariffs is approximately CA$28 billion, while CNBC reported that roughly US$20 billion worth of goods are affected. The gap between these two figures may stem from different calculation bases — the former calculated in Canadian dollars and the latter in US dollars — and may also cover different scopes of goods. Despite the numerical differences, all media outlets confirmed that the US has begun implementing 50% tariffs on Canadian goods, and that the Canadian government has activated corresponding retaliatory measures.
  
  This trade dispute has immediate and far-reaching implications for the North American economic landscape. As one of the United States' largest trading partners, Canada's bilateral trade volume with the US exceeds US$700 billion annually. These tariff measures are certain to impact supply chains across multiple industries in both countries. The automotive manufacturing, agricultural products, forestry, and aluminum sectors are hit first and hardest, with related businesses potentially facing the dual pressures of rising costs and heightened market uncertainty. Canadian Prime Minister Carney emphasized that his government will match US tariffs "dollar-for-dollar" to "protect our workers and businesses," signaling that Canada is prepared for a prolonged confrontation.
  
  However, it is worth noting that different media outlets place varying emphasis on who bears responsibility for the collapse of the talks. China.org.cn and The Business Standard present both sides' positions in a relatively neutral manner, reporting only Carney's criticism of US conditions. CTV News provides a more detailed account of Canada's response and retaliation plans. CNBC specifically notes that "both sides accused each other of derailing days of negotiations," suggesting that the US may also be dissatisfied with Canada's negotiating stance. These reporting differences reflect the complex situation in which both parties in this trade dispute hold conflicting accounts, and readers should approach any single source with caution rather than prematurely assigning blame.
  
  Numerous key pieces of information remain to be confirmed. First, the specific list of Canadian goods subject to the 50% US tariffs has not been fully disclosed, with media reports only vaguely referring to "certain goods" or "some products." Second, the scale and implementation timeline of Canada's planned retaliatory tariffs remain unclear — while Prime Minister Carney has said Canada will respond "dollar-for-dollar," specific execution details have yet to be announced. Additionally, whether the two sides might resume negotiations, and when, currently has no clear timetable, and the White House has not issued a public statement on the possibility of further talks.
  
  Key points for future observation will center on several aspects: when the Canadian government will announce specific retaliatory measures and their scope; whether the US and Canada will re-establish contact in the near term; the ripple effects of this trade dispute on global supply chains; and how businesses and labor groups in both countries respond to the tariff measures. It is worth noting that US-Canada trade relations have always been close. If this dispute continues to escalate, it will not only affect bilateral trade but may also have a profound impact on the future direction of the USMCA trade agreement. The international community will closely monitor developments, particularly whether the two countries can find an opportunity to ease tensions in the short term.

# 美加貿易談判破裂 美國對加拿大商品課徵50%關稅

> 美國與加拿大歷經數週密集談判仍未能達成貿易協議，美國於當地時間週六起對價值約280億加幣的加拿大商品課徵50%關稅，加拿大總理卡尼宣佈暫停談判並誓言「一對一」報復。

美國與加拿大之間的貿易談判於當地時間週五晚間宣告破裂，白宮隨後宣佈自週六起對特定加拿大進口商品課徵50%關稅。這項決定標誌著這兩個長期盟友之間的經貿關係出現嚴重倒退，也讓北美自由貿易體系面臨前所未有的挑戰。根據多家國際媒體報導，加拿大總理馬克·卡尼（Mark Carney）在談判破局後立即召開記者會，宣佈加拿大將採取對等報復措施，並已下令加國談判代表返回渥太華。
綜閤中國官方媒體《中國網》、加拿大《CTV新聞》、孟加拉《商業標準報》及美國《CNBC》等多家媒體報導，美加雙方在過去數週進行了密集的貿易磋商，試圖在最後期限前達成協議。然而，美國政府在談判尾聲突然提出多項新增條件，成為壓垮談判的最後一根稻草。卡尼總理在記者會上明確表示，美方在最後一刻提出的變更「不公平、不經濟，且令人質疑任何協議的可靠性」，因此加拿大決定暫停所有貿易談判。
關於關稅影響的具體規模，各媒體報匯出現若干差異。《CTV新聞》指出，新關稅影響的商品總值約為280億加幣，而《CNBC》則報導約為200億美元的商品受到影響。這兩個數字之間的落差可能源於計算基準不同，前者以加幣計算，後者以美元計算，且可能涵蓋不同的商品範圍。儘管數字有所出入，但各媒體均確認美國已開始對加拿大商品實施50%的關稅，且加拿大政府已啟動對應的報復措施。
這項貿易爭端對北美經濟格局產生立即且深遠的影響。加拿大作為美國最大的貿易夥伴之一，雙邊貿易額每年超過7000億美元，此次關稅措施勢必衝擊兩國多個產業供應鏈。汽車製造、農產品、林業及鋁製品等行業首當其衝，相關企業可能面臨成本上升與市場不確定性加劇的雙重壓力。加拿大總理卡尼強調，加國政府將「一對一」匹配美方的關稅措施，「以保護我們的工人和企業」，顯示加拿大政府已做好長期對抗的準備。
然而，值得注意的是，各媒體對談判破裂的責任歸屬呈現不同側重。《中國網》與《商業標準報》較為中立地陳述雙方立場，僅報導卡尼對美方條件的批評；《CTV新聞》則較為詳細地描述了加拿大方面的反應與報復計畫；《CNBC》則特別指出「雙方互相指責對方破壞了數日的談判」，顯示美方可能也對加拿大的談判態度有所不滿。這種報導差異反映出此次貿易爭端中雙方各執一詞的複雜局面，讀者應審慎看待單一來源的說法，不宜過早斷定責任歸屬。
目前仍有諸多關鍵資訊尚待確認。首先，美國對加拿大商品課徵50%關稅的具體商品清單尚未完全公開，各媒體報導僅籠統提及「特定商品」或「部分商品」。其次，加拿大政府計劃實施的報復性關稅規模與實施時間表也未明朗，卡尼總理雖表示將「一對一」回應，但具體執行細節仍待公佈。此外，雙方是否可能重啟談判、以及何時重啟，目前均無明確時間表，白宮方面也未對後續談判可能性發表公開宣告。
後續觀察重點將集中於幾個面向：加拿大政府何時公佈具體報復措施及其涵蓋範圍；美加雙方是否會在近期內恢復接觸；這項貿易爭端對全球供應鏈的連鎖效應；以及兩國企業與勞工群體對關稅措施的反應。值得注意的是，美加貿易關係向來密切，此次爭端若持續升溫，不僅影響雙邊經貿，也可能對北美自由貿易協定（USMCA）的未來走向產生深遠影響。國際社會將密切關注事態發展，尤其是兩國是否能在短期內找到緩和緊張關係的契機。
