前達拉斯獨行俠隊高管喬治·基勒布（George Killebrew）將於2026年10月3日，接任由NBA球星盧卡·唐西奇（Luka Doncic）和前獨行俠隊總經理唐尼·尼爾森（Donnie Nelson）共同擁有的義大利籃球隊的全球業務領導職務。根據《達拉斯晨報》報導，基勒布將把他豐富的體育商業經驗帶到義大利球隊BC羅馬。

唐西奇，目前效力於洛杉磯湖人隊的全明星球員，與尼爾森於2026年5月共同投資了一支義大利職業籃球隊。路透社報導指出，他們收購的是義大利甲級聯賽（Lega Basket Serie A）的瓦諾利克雷莫納（Vanoli Cremona）籃球俱樂部。然而，其他報導，包括ESPN和《體育商業雜誌》，則提及他們投資的是一支位於羅馬的球隊，並稱其為「羅馬隊」。

此次任命基勒布，凸顯了該義大利籃球隊在國際市場拓展的雄心。資料顯示，唐西奇與尼爾森共同擁有的這支球隊，正積極尋求加入正在發展中的NBA歐洲聯賽。ESPN和《體育商業雜誌》均報導了該隊對加入NBA歐洲聯賽的期望或潛力，這將是其全球戰略的重要一環。

基勒布在體育界擁有廣泛的商業經驗，預計將在推動球隊的品牌建設、市場擴張和收入增長方面發揮關鍵作用。他的加入，被視為該義大利籃球隊在全球體育舞台上提升影響力的重要一步。

## English edition

Former Dallas Mavericks executive George Killebrew is set to assume leadership of the global business operations for an Italian basketball team co-owned by NBA star Luka Doncic and former Mavericks general manager Donnie Nelson, effective October 3, 2026. According to The Dallas Morning News, Killebrew will bring his extensive sports business experience to the Italian team, BC Roma.

Doncic, an All-Star guard for the Los Angeles Lakers, teamed up with Nelson in May 2026 to invest in an Italian professional basketball club. Reuters reported that they acquired Vanoli Cremona, a team in Italy's Lega Basket Serie A. However, other reports, including ESPN and Sports Business Journal, referred to their investment as a "Rome team" or a team based in Rome.

Killebrew's appointment underscores the Italian basketball team's ambitions for international market expansion. Reports indicate that the team co-owned by Doncic and Nelson is actively pursuing a bid to join the developing NBA Europe league. Both ESPN and Sports Business Journal highlighted the team's hopes or potential to join NBA Europe, positioning it as a key component of its global strategy.

With extensive experience in the sports business sector, Killebrew is expected to play a crucial role in enhancing the team's brand, market reach, and revenue growth. His arrival is seen as a significant step for the Italian basketball team to elevate its presence on the global sports stage.
