  # Sino-Dutch Semiconductor Control Dispute Escalates: Wang Yi Urges the Netherlands to Improve Business Environment
  
  > Regarding the dispute over the control of Nexperia, Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi met with Dutch Foreign Minister Tom Berendsen in Beijing. The Chinese side urged the Netherlands to provide a fair business environment and strengthen cooperation in the semiconductor sector.
  
  As a key enterprise in the global semiconductor supply chain, Nexperia’s equity structure and control issues have long been a subject of intense international attention. The company is currently a subsidiary of China’s Wingtech Technology and primarily produces power chips widely used in the automotive industry and various consumer electronic products.
  
  Citing national security concerns, the Dutch government imposed restrictive measures on the company’s equity changes and management rights last year, a move that directly triggered friction in trade and economic policies between China and the Netherlands. On November 25, 2026, Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi held a formal meeting with Dutch Foreign Minister Tom Berendsen, who was visiting Beijing. The two sides engaged in in-depth exchanges regarding semiconductor industry chain cooperation and market access, reflecting the tug-of-war between geostrategy and economic security in critical technology supply chains amidst international geopolitics.
  
  According to Reuters and relevant financial media reports, during the talks, Wang Yi clearly expressed China's position to the Dutch side, urging the Dutch government to provide a fair, transparent, and predictable business environment for Chinese enterprises operating in the Netherlands. Wang emphasized that China consistently supports two-way investment and welcomes Dutch companies to develop in the Chinese market, expressing hope that both sides can enhance mutual trust through strengthened communication. In a joint statement issued after the meeting, both sides indicated that China and the Netherlands would maintain close communication regarding cooperation in key industrial sectors such as semiconductors. This development confirms that despite facing geopolitical pressure and national security reviews, both China and the Netherlands are attempting to ease tensions in the semiconductor supply chain through diplomatic channels to ensure the continuity of industrial cooperation.
  
  However, there remains an information gap regarding the specific outcomes of the dialogue. While the Chinese side’s public statement emphasized the demand for "fairness and transparency," the extent of any concessions by the Dutch government regarding national security reviews, as well as its final stance on the future management rights of Nexperia, has not been disclosed in public statements. Furthermore, as the home of the European semiconductor equipment giant ASML, the Netherlands' policy direction is not only influenced by bilateral relations with China but is also heavily constrained by the European Union’s overall trade policy toward China and U.S. export control measures.
  
  Therefore, whether the "close communication" mentioned in the joint statement can be translated into concrete policy easing at a practical level, or if it is merely a transitional gesture to alleviate diplomatic tension, remains to be seen.
  
  The dispute over the control of Nexperia is not merely a commercial conflict involving a single company; it has had a profound impact on the global semiconductor industry landscape. For the automotive manufacturing industry, the chips provided by Nexperia are core components for electric vehicles and autonomous driving technology, and any policy uncertainty could lead to risks of supply chain disruption. For the Netherlands, balancing the protection of national security with maintaining the global competitiveness of its semiconductor industry has become a major challenge in its foreign policy. For China, ensuring the autonomy of critical technology supply chains and the openness of international markets is key to driving industrial upgrading; such disputes directly affect the investment confidence and strategic layout of Chinese enterprises in the European market.
  
  It must be noted that there are clear differences in how international public opinion interprets Sino-Dutch semiconductor cooperation. Some perspectives view this as an attempt by China to break through Western technological blockades through diplomatic pressure, while others argue that the Netherlands also has a need to maintain market diversification in the face of global chip supply chain restructuring. The outside world should not overinterpret this meeting as an immediate solution to all technology export controls or equity restrictions. In fact, in the current global geopolitical environment, the semiconductor industry is no longer just about commercial competition; it is viewed as an extension of national defense security and strategic autonomy, and it is difficult for any single diplomatic meeting to resolve deep-seated structural contradictions in the short term.
  
  Future observations will focus on the specific actions taken by the Dutch government regarding subsequent reviews of Nexperia, and whether the "close communication" between China and the Netherlands can be implemented into concrete technical exchange agreements or investment protection mechanisms. Furthermore, as global sovereign debt pressure and geopolitical risks rise, the protectionist tendencies of various countries in critical industries are likely to persist. Whether China and the Netherlands can find a balance for commercial cooperation while ensuring national security will become an important indicator for observing the future direction of the global semiconductor supply chain. The international community will also continue to monitor whether the Netherlands will further adjust its investment review standards for Chinese enterprises in response to the EU's overall policy.

# 中荷半導體控制權爭議延燒：王毅敦促荷蘭改善營商環境

> 針對安世半導體控制權爭議，中國外交部長王毅與荷蘭外交部長貝倫德森在北京會晤，中方敦促荷蘭提供公平營商環境，並強化半導體領域合作。


安世半導體（Nexperia）作為全球半導體供應鏈中的關鍵企業，其股權結構與控制權問題長期以來受到國際高度關注。該公司目前隸屬於中國聞泰科技旗下，主要生產廣泛應用於汽車工業及各類消費性電子產品的功率晶片。
荷蘭政府基於國家安全考量，去年曾針對該企業的股權變動與經營權採取限制措施，此舉直接引發了中荷兩國在經貿政策上的摩擦。2026年11月25日，中國外交部長王毅與到訪北京的荷蘭外交部長貝倫德森（Tom Berendsen）進行了正式會晤，雙方就半導體產業鏈的合作及市場准入問題進行了深入交流，此事件反映出國際地緣政治下，關鍵技術產業鏈在地緣戰略與經濟安全之間的拉鋸。
根據路透社及相關財經媒體報導，在本次會談中，王毅明確向荷方表達了中方立場，敦促荷蘭政府為在荷營運的中國企業提供公平、透明且具備可預測性的營商環境。王毅強調，中國始終支援雙向投資，並歡迎荷蘭企業赴中國市場發展，期待雙方能透過加強交流來增進互信。雙方在會後發表的聯合宣告中指出，中荷兩國將針對半導體等關鍵產業領域的合作保持密切溝通。這一進展證實了儘管面臨地緣政治壓力與國家安全審查，中荷雙方仍試圖透過外交途徑，緩解半導體供應鏈中的緊張關係，以確保產業合作的延續性。
然而，目前關於雙方對談的具體成果仍存在資訊落差。雖然中方公開宣告強調了「公平與透明」的訴求，但荷蘭政府在國家安全審查上的具體退讓程度，以及對於安世半導體未來經營權的最終態度，尚未見諸於公開宣告中。此外，荷蘭作為歐洲半導體裝置龍頭艾司摩爾（ASML）的所在地，其政策走向不僅受到中荷雙邊關係影響，更深受歐盟整體對華貿易政策以及美國出口管制措施的牽制。
因此，雙方在聯合宣告中提及的「密切溝通」，在實務層面上是否能轉化為具體的放寬政策，或是僅為緩解外交緊張的過渡性表態，目前仍待進一步觀察。
安世半導體控制權爭端，不僅是單一企業的商業糾紛，更對全球半導體產業佈局產生了深遠影響。對於汽車製造業而言，安世半導體提供的晶片是電動車與自動駕駛技術的核心元件，任何政策上的不確定性都可能導致供應鏈中斷風險。對於荷蘭而言，如何在維護國家安全與維持半導體產業全球競爭力之間取得平衡，已成為其外交政策的重大課題。而對於中國而言，確保關鍵技術供應鏈的自主性與國際市場的開放性，則是其推動產業升級的關鍵，此類爭端直接影響了中國企業在歐洲市場的投資信心與佈局策略。
必須指出的是，國際輿論對於中荷半導體合作的解讀存在明顯分歧。部分觀點認為，這是中國試圖透過外交施壓突破西方國家技術封鎖的嘗試；另一部分觀點則認為，荷蘭在面對全球晶片供應鏈重組時，亦有維持市場多元化的需求。外界不應過度解讀此次會晤將立即解決所有技術出口管制或股許可權制問題。事實上，在當前全球地緣政治環境下，半導體產業已不僅是商業競爭，更被視為國防安全與戰略自主的延伸，任何單一外交會晤都難以在短期內化解深層的結構性矛盾。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於荷蘭政府對於安世半導體後續審查的具體動作，以及中荷雙方在半導體領域的「密切溝通」是否能落實為具體的技術交流協議或投資保障機制。此外，隨著全球公債壓力與地緣政治風險的攀升，各國在關鍵產業的保護主義傾向恐將持續。中荷兩國能否在確保國家安全的前提下，找到商業合作的平衡點，將成為觀察全球半導體供應鏈未來走向的重要指標。國際社會亦將持續關注，荷蘭是否會因應歐盟整體政策，進一步調整其對中國企業的投資審查標準。
