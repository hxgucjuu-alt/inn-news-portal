  # Global Climate Change Challenges and Energy Transition: Infrastructure Dilemmas and Adaptation Strategies in Emerging Markets
  
  > As global climate change intensifies, the risks of wildfires triggered by extreme weather and the urgent need for energy transition have become shared challenges for the international community. Recent forums and studies indicate that emerging markets are facing severe difficulties in resource allocation and technology implementation regarding power supply and climate resilience infrastructure.
  
  The 6th Taiwan Climate Action Expo officially opened on October 2, 2026, at the National Science and Technology Museum. Centered on the theme "Towards Net Zero, Creating the Future Together," the event focuses on key issues such as energy transition, climate adaptation, nature-based solutions, and sustainable oceans. The expo aims to translate international climate commitments into concrete pathways and, on the eve of the COP31 summit, help the public understand the connection between climate change and daily life through cross-disciplinary collaboration and interactive exhibits.
  
  Meanwhile, there is a strong call for international participation in climate governance. Ambassador Angel Liu, Taiwan’s Representative to New Zealand, recently made a public appeal, emphasizing that Taiwan's experience in green technology and renewable energy is indispensable to global participation in the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC) system, and argued that Taiwan's experience in climate resilience should be incorporated into the scope of international cooperation.
  
  Regarding the specific impacts of climate change, the scientific community has conducted more in-depth assessments of secondary disasters caused by extreme weather. According to research from the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, the frequency of wildfires has increased significantly due to frequent global heatwaves and droughts, with smoke spreading over thousands of kilometers. The study found that PM2.5 in wildfire smoke poses a higher risk to human health compared to particulate matter from traffic and industrial emissions.
  
  This finding highlights that climate change is not merely an environmental issue but one that directly impacts public health systems. For regions recently threatened by drought and wildfires, including South America, the urgency for climate adaptation has increased significantly.
  
  However, while the world promotes digitalization and the development of artificial intelligence (AI), limitations in energy supply have become a key bottleneck for development in emerging markets. World Bank President Ajay Banga explicitly pointed out during New York Climate Week that emerging markets struggle to build large-scale data centers primarily because electricity resources are extremely precious. AI computing requires vast and stable power infrastructure; for countries with weaker power supply conditions, even with funding and hardware, the stability of electricity supply remains a core obstacle to project implementation.
  
  This perspective contrasts with the "energy transition" path emphasized at the expo, revealing a structural contradiction in resource allocation for developing countries as they pursue both digital and green transformations.
  
  The aforementioned issues have a direct and profound impact on emerging economies such as those in South America. The South American region possesses abundant natural resources but is also deeply affected by droughts and wildfires caused by climate change. If the region must choose between building climate resilience and digital infrastructure, the prioritization of electricity resources will become a dilemma for policymakers. Furthermore, while there is a general consensus on the importance of energy transition, there are divergent views on "how to implement it." Some argue that priority should be given to green technology and renewable energy to strengthen resilience, while others worry that in the absence of sufficient infrastructure, excessive investment in energy-intensive digital industries might crowd out residential electricity demand, leading to social inequality.
  
  Currently, there are still limitations and unknown information in the discussion of climate action. For example, although the academic community has confirmed the harmfulness of wildfire smoke, more empirical data is needed regarding the differences in chemical composition after the combustion of different vegetation types and the long-term health impacts on specific regional populations. Additionally, there is high uncertainty regarding whether the speed of power infrastructure improvement in emerging markets can keep pace with the demands of energy transition. The public should not over-interpret the effectiveness of a single technology or policy; climate adaptation requires cross-border collaboration and localized strategies, rather than simple technology introduction.
  
  Future observations will focus on the concrete commitments from the international community regarding funding and technology transfer for energy transition in emerging markets on the eve of the COP31 summit. At the same time, whether air quality monitoring and public health early warning systems in wildfire-prone areas can be upgraded through international climate cooperation mechanisms will be an important indicator for evaluating the effectiveness of global climate adaptation. How governments balance power supply and sustainable development goals during the digitalization process will also be a core focus of global energy policy in the coming years.

# 全球氣候變遷挑戰與能源轉型：新興市場的基礎設施困境與調適策略

> 隨著全球氣候變遷加劇，極端氣候引發的野火風險及能源轉型需求，成為國際社會共同面臨的課題。近期多項論壇與研究顯示，新興市場在電力供給與氣候韌性建設上，正面臨嚴峻的資源分配與技術落地挑戰。


第六屆臺灣氣候行動博覽會於2026年10月2日在國立科學工藝博物館正式揭幕，本次活動以「邁向淨零，共創未來」為核心，聚焦能源轉型、氣候調適、自然解方及永續海洋等關鍵議題。該博覽會旨在將國際間的氣候承諾轉化為具體的路徑，並於COP31峰會前夕，透過跨域協作與展攤互動，協助公眾理解氣候變遷與日常生活的連結。
與此同時，國際間對於氣候治理的參與亦有高度呼聲，我國駐紐西蘭代表歐江安大使近期公開呼籲，強調臺灣在綠色科技與再生能源方面的經驗，對於全球參與「聯合國氣候變化綱要公約」（UNFCCC）體系具有不可或缺的價值，主張應將臺灣的氣候韌性經驗納入國際合作範疇。
在氣候變遷的具體影響層面上，科學界對於極端氣候導致的次生災害已有更深入的評估。根據美國西奈山伊坎醫學院（Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai）的研究指出，由於全球高溫與乾旱頻發，野火發生頻率大幅增加，其產生的煙霧擴散範圍可達數千公里。研究發現，野火煙霧中的PM2.5與交通、工業排放的懸浮微粒相比，對人體健康造成的損害風險更高。
此一發現凸顯了氣候變遷不僅是環境問題，更直接影響公共衛生體系，對於包括南美洲在內、近期頻受乾旱與野火威脅的區域而言，氣候調適的迫切性已大幅提升。
然而，在全球推動數位化與人工智慧（AI）發展的同時，能源供給的限制成為新興市場發展的關鍵瓶頸。世界銀行總裁班加（Ajay Banga）於紐約氣候週期間明確指出，新興市場難以大規模興建資料中心，主因在於電力資源極為珍貴。AI運算需要龐大且穩定的電力基礎設施，對於供電條件較為薄弱的國家而言，即便具備資金與硬體裝置，電力供給的穩定性仍是專案落地的核心障礙。
此觀點與博覽會中所強調的「能源轉型」路徑形成對比，顯示出開發中國家在追求數位轉型與綠色轉型之間，存在著資源分配的結構性矛盾。
上述議題對南美洲等新興經濟體具有直接且深遠的影響。南美地區擁有豐富的自然資源，但也深受氣候變遷導致的乾旱與野火影響。若該地區需在氣候韌性建設與數位基礎設施之間進行抉擇，電力資源的優先順序配置將成為政策制定者的難題。此外，雖然各界普遍認同能源轉型的重要性，但對於「如何實踐」仍存在觀點分歧。部分觀點強調應優先投入綠色科技與再生能源以強化韌性，另一觀點則擔憂在基礎設施不足的情況下，過度投入高耗能的數位產業可能排擠民生用電需求，造成社會分配不均。
目前各界對於氣候行動的討論，仍存在部分限制與未知資訊。例如，雖然學界已證實野火煙霧的危害性，但針對不同植被型別燃燒後的化學成分差異，以及對特定區域人口的長期健康影響，仍需更多實證資料支援。此外，新興市場在電力基礎設施的改善速度，是否能趕上能源轉型的需求，亦存在高度不確定性。公眾不應過度解讀單一技術或政策的成效，氣候調適需要的是跨國界的協作與因地制宜的策略，而非單純的技術引進。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於COP31峰會前夕，國際社會對於新興市場能源轉型資金與技術轉移的具體承諾。同時，針對野火頻發地區的空氣品質監測與公共衛生預警系統，能否結合國際氣候合作機制進行升級，將是評估全球氣候調適成效的重要指標。各國政府在推動數位化程式中，如何平衡電力供給與永續發展的目標，亦將是未來幾年全球能源政策的觀察核心。
