  The geopolitical landscape of the Middle East has undergone drastic changes over the past three years. As the Gaza war enters its third year, regional conflicts are no longer confined to local skirmishes but have spread to critical strategic shipping lanes and international military deployments. According to The Guardian, this conflict, which erupted three years ago and saw a ceasefire agreement reached one year ago, has completely reshaped the political map of the Middle East, with its influence extending beyond regional borders to become a focal point of global concern.
  
  On the military and diplomatic fronts, U.S. influence in the Middle East is undergoing a transformation, with increasing disputes among regional nations regarding the transparency and control of U.S. military bases. Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi recently publicly questioned reports concerning the status of U.S. military bases in the Middle East, a move stemming from the U.S. refusal to allow Democratic senators to visit relevant facilities, indicating a severe lack of mutual trust regarding military transparency between the two sides.
  
  Synthesizing information from multiple sources, one of the core contradictions in the current Middle Eastern situation lies in the control and shipping security of the Strait of Hormuz. According to information from Project RKT and AsiaOne News, Iran has proposed a "two million dollar per vessel" transit fee as a condition for keeping the Strait of Hormuz open. U.S. Senator Marco Rubio has issued a warning to ASEAN ministers regarding this, emphasizing that if Iran succeeds in controlling the strait, it would set an extremely dangerous precedent, with negative impacts extending far beyond the Middle East to affect global trade and energy supply chains.
  
  Currently, the region is under the threat of military crossfire, with frequent reports of warfare in places like Beirut, indicating that the conflict has spilled over from the simple Gaza battlefield into the broader Levant region and Persian Gulf shipping lanes.
  
  However, there are significant discrepancies in how various parties interpret the current situation and its details. The "fee" narrative regarding the Strait of Hormuz currently appears only in select reports; international official bodies have yet to confirm whether this proposal has entered formal diplomatic negotiation processes or if it is merely a unilateral statement from the Iranian side. Furthermore, regarding the dispute over access to U.S. military bases, the Iranian Foreign Ministry's questioning and the U.S. reasons for denying access present different perspectives of diplomatic maneuvering in various media reports; some media outlets view this as a countermeasure and pressure test by Iran against U.S. military deployment, while another perspective focuses on the power vacuum following the ebb of U.S. military influence in the Middle East in the post-Cold War era.
  
  The true intent behind this information and the diplomatic leverage involved remain to be verified by further diplomatic documents or official announcements.
  
  This ongoing instability has had concrete negative impacts on the regional economy and international policy. First, as the Strait of Hormuz is a hub for global energy transport, any dispute over transit fees or threats of armed conflict will directly lead to global oil price volatility and a surge in shipping insurance costs. Second, the psychological trauma and destruction of social structures caused by the Gaza war to local civilians, especially children, have become the core of a humanitarian crisis.
  
  According to an analysis by Arab News, this is not merely a military confrontation but a restructuring of the "post-American" Middle Eastern order. Regional countries are forced to seek survival space within an increasingly complex power structure, making regional governance and public safety issues extremely fragile, while reconstruction efforts for education and infrastructure remain stagnant due to the ongoing war.
  
  From different perspectives, Western observers largely attribute the current tensions to Iran's attempt to expand its regional influence and use strategic waterways as geopolitical bargaining chips; however, some voices within the region believe this is a backlash against long-standing external intervention in Middle Eastern affairs. It must be noted that information regarding the progress of the conflict is highly susceptible to psychological warfare and propaganda. Readers should not treat reports from a single media outlet about "Beirut in flames" or "Iran proposing specific fee amounts" as a definitive conclusion on the state of the war. These reports reflect the high degree of uncertainty in the current information environment and the attempts by various forces to influence the international community's perception of the situation in the region through public opinion.
  
  Over-interpreting a single military event or diplomatic statement may lead to overlooking the deeper restructuring of regional power structures behind them.

The international community needs to closely monitor the substantive actions of the United Nations and regional organizations in mediating the conflict, as well as the immediate reaction of global energy markets to changes in the region's situation.

# 中東局勢動盪：荷姆茲海峽航運危機與地緣政治角力

> 近期中東地區衝突加劇，伊朗針對荷姆茲海峽提出航運收費要求，並對美方軍事基地資訊提出質疑。區域內戰爭陰影籠罩，國際社會高度關注戰略航道安全及後續外交博弈。


中東地區的地緣政治格局在過去三年內發生劇烈變動，隨著加薩戰爭進入第三個年頭，區域內的衝突不僅限於區域性戰事，更擴散至關鍵戰略航道與國際軍事部署。根據《衛報》報導，這場自三年前爆發、並在一年前達成停火協議的衝突，已徹底重塑中東的政治版圖，其影響力已超越區域界線，成為全球關注的焦點。
在軍事與外交層面上，美國在中東的影響力正經歷轉型，區域各國對於美軍基地的透明度及控制權爭議日益增加，伊朗外交部長阿拉格奇（Abbas Araghchi）近期公開質疑美方關於中東軍事基地狀態的報告，起因於美方拒絕讓民主黨參議員訪問相關設施，顯示雙方在軍事透明度上的嚴重互信缺失。
綜合多方來源確認，當前中東局勢的核心矛盾之一在於荷姆茲海峽的控制權與航運安全。根據《Project RKT》與《AsiaOne News》的資訊，伊朗方面已提出「每艘船隻兩百萬美元」的過路費要求，作為開放荷姆茲海峽的條件。美國參議員盧比歐（Marco Rubio）對此向東協部長們發出警告，強調若伊朗成功控制該海峽，將會開創極其危險的先例，其負面影響將遠超中東區域，波及全球貿易與能源供應鏈。
目前，該地區正處於軍事交叉火力的威脅之下，貝魯特等地頻傳戰火，顯示衝突已從單純的加薩戰場外溢至更廣泛的黎凡特區域與波斯灣航道。
然而，各方對當前局勢的解讀與細節存在顯著差異。關於荷姆茲海峽的「收費」說法，目前僅見於部分報導，國際官方機構尚未證實該項提議是否已進入正式外交談判程式，或僅為伊朗方面的單方面喊話。此外，關於美軍基地訪問權的爭議，伊朗外交部的質疑與美方的拒絕訪問理由，在不同媒體報導中呈現出不同的外交角力視角；部分媒體將此視為伊朗對美方軍事部署的一種反制與壓力測試，而另一派觀點則關注於美國在後冷戰時期，於中東地區軍事影響力退潮後的權力真空問題。
這些資訊的真實意圖與背後的外交籌碼，仍有待後續更多外交檔案或官方宣告的驗證。
這場持續的動盪對區域經濟與國際政策產生了具體的負面影響。首先，荷姆茲海峽作為全球能源運輸的樞紐，任何關於通行費用的爭議或武裝衝突威脅，都會直接導致全球油價波動與航運保險成本飆升。其次，加薩戰爭對當地平民，特別是兒童造成的心理創傷與社會結構破壞，已成為人道主義危機的核心。
根據《阿拉伯新聞》的分析，這不僅是軍事上的對抗，更是一場關於「後美國時代」中東秩序的重組，區域內國家被迫在日益複雜的權力結構中尋求生存空間，這使得區域治理與公共安全議題變得極其脆弱，教育與基礎建設的重建工作亦因戰火持續而陷入停滯。
從不同觀點來看，西方觀察家多將目前的緊張局勢歸因於伊朗試圖擴大其區域影響力，並利用戰略航道作為地緣政治的談判籌碼；然而，區域內部分聲音則認為，這是對長期以來外力介入中東事務的反彈。必須注意的是，目前關於衝突進展的資訊極易受到心理戰與宣傳影響，讀者不應將單一媒體關於「貝魯特在火焰中」或「伊朗提出具體收費金額」的報導視為全面性的戰況定論。這些報導反映了當前資訊環境的高度不確定性，以及各方勢力試圖透過輿論影響國際社會對該地區局勢的認知。
過度解讀單一軍事事件或外交辭令，可能忽略了背後更深層的區域權力結構重組。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於三個面向：第一，荷姆茲海峽的航運安全是否會出現具體的國際幹預機制，以及伊朗是否會將收費提議轉化為實際的封鎖行動；第二，美方對於中東軍事基地透明度爭議的後續回應，這將直接影響美國與區域盟友的互信程度；第三，加薩戰爭三週年後，相關停火協議的執行細節與人道救援進度，是否能有效緩解區域內的緊張氛圍。國際社會需密切關注聯合國與區域組織在調解衝突上的實質作為，以及全球能源市場對該地區局勢變化的即時反應。
