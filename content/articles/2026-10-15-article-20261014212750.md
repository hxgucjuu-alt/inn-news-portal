  # Global Climate Change and Energy Demand Challenges: From Smart Energy Transition to Forest Resilience Building
  
  > As global climate change intensifies, various sectors are actively seeking strategies for energy transition and environmental resilience. Recently, Energy Taiwan has focused on the power demands driven by AI computing power, while the New Taipei City Government, in response to the International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction, has emphasized strengthening forest resilience through afforestation to collectively address the multifaceted challenges posed by climate change.
  
  On October 13, 2026, global climate change issues sparked attention across various levels. In the industrial energy sector, Energy Taiwan is set to kick off at the Nangang Exhibition Center on the 14th, with a three-day exhibition gathering the focus of the global energy industry. Simultaneously, coinciding with the United Nations' "International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction," the Agriculture Department of the New Taipei City Government issued a declaration regarding forest resource protection and disaster reduction, urging the public to increase fire safety awareness during the dry season and to strengthen natural carbon sinks through afforestation efforts to cope with the environmental impacts of climate change. Although these two events occurred in different fields, they both point to the urgent needs faced globally regarding energy transition and ecological resilience.
  
  According to James Huang, Chairman of the Taiwan External Trade Development Council (TAITRA), the level of internationalization at this year's Energy Taiwan has reached a record high, with the number of exhibitors increasing by 16% year-on-year to 115. Huang pointed out that the world is currently facing the dual, intersecting challenges of "massive power demand driven by the explosion of AI computing power" and "intensifying climate change," making energy transition not just an environmental issue, but a core key to industrial development. Meanwhile, the New Taipei City Agriculture Department, starting from the perspective of nature conservation, emphasized the importance of forests as the "Earth's natural green barrier," which not only functions to conserve water, prevent soil erosion, and purify the air, but also mitigates the impacts of climate change by increasing natural carbon sinks.
  
  Regarding information verification, there is a high degree of consensus across sectors on the challenges brought by climate change. In the energy industry, the consensus is that the advancement of AI technology will lead to exponential growth in power demand, which directly makes renewable energy and smart grids a market necessity. In terms of environmental disaster prevention, official statistics show that up to 97% of forest fires are caused by human factors, especially during the dry autumn and winter seasons, when the northeast monsoon can easily spread small sparks, causing large-scale damage. However, there are differences in their approaches: the energy industry tends to resolve supply-demand imbalances through technological innovation and hardware upgrades, while local governments tend to strengthen the environment's self-healing capabilities through public education and ecological conservation.
  
  In terms of specific impacts, the transformation of the energy industry will directly drive business opportunities in related supply chains, including smart grid management, energy storage systems, and green energy generation technologies, with exhibition-related business matchmaking expected to continue growing. For the industry, balancing high-energy AI computing demands with decarbonization commitments has become a major test for corporate operations. On the other hand, for the general public and local governments, the strengthening of forest resilience is directly related to public safety and soil and water conservation. By providing free saplings for afforestation, the New Taipei City Government is attempting to transform disaster reduction actions into a public policy involving the entire population, which not only helps regulate regional microclimates but is also a local implementation of the UN's disaster reduction goals.
  
  Although there is a consensus on the urgency of climate change, limitations remain regarding different perspectives. Energy experts warn that over-reliance on a single energy transition path may bring risks of unstable power supply, and whether the growth rate of power demand brought by AI computing power will exceed the construction speed of renewable energy remains a core focus for market observation. Furthermore, while afforestation is an important means of increasing carbon sinks, without subsequent forest management and fire monitoring, simple tree-planting activities may face fire risks due to dry climate conditions, causing carbon sequestration achievements to be lost. Therefore, the public should not view technological solutions as a panacea, but should seek a more nuanced balance between industrial development and ecological conservation.
  
  Looking ahead, the energy industry will closely monitor the policy signals released during Energy Taiwan, as well as the substantive pressure of AI computing power demand on power infrastructure. At the same time, with the promotion of the International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction, forest fire prevention and resilience building will become key focuses of public governance during the autumn and winter seasons. Subsequent observation will focus on: first, whether the energy industry can achieve green transition goals under the AI wave; second, whether local government afforestation plans can effectively enhance the forest's ability to withstand extreme weather; and third, whether global policy standards for carbon sinks and energy efficiency will be further tightened, thereby affecting the layout strategies of multinational industries. These dynamics will continue to influence the direction of global climate and energy policies.

# 全球氣候變遷與能源需求挑戰：從智慧能源轉型到森林韌性建構

> 隨著全球氣候變遷加劇，各界正積極尋求能源轉型與環境韌性對策。近期臺灣智慧能源週聚焦 AI 算力帶動的電力需求，同時新北市政府響應國際減災日，強調透過植樹造林強化山林韌性，共同應對氣候變遷帶來的多重挑戰。


2026年10月13日，全球氣候變遷議題在不同層面引發關注。在產業能源領域，臺灣智慧能源週即將於14日在南港展覽館盛大登場，為期三天的展會匯聚了全球能源產業焦點。與此同時，適逢聯合國訂定的「國際減災日」，新北市政府農業局亦針對森林資源保護與減災議題發布宣告，呼籲民眾在乾燥季節提高防火警覺，並透過植樹造林行動來強化自然碳匯，以應對氣候變遷帶來的環境衝擊。這兩項事件雖發生於不同領域，卻共同指向了全球在能源轉型與生態韌性上所面臨的迫切需求。
根據外貿協會董事長黃志芳的說明，今年智慧能源週的國際化程度創下歷屆新高，參展廠商數量年增16%，達到115家。黃志芳指出，當前全球面臨「AI算力爆發帶動龐大電力需求」與「氣候變遷加劇」的雙重交錯挑戰，這使得能源轉型不再僅是環境議題，更成為產業發展的核心關鍵。與此同時，新北市農業局則從自然保育的角度出發，強調森林作為「地球天然綠色屏障」的重要性，不僅具備涵養水源、防砂固土與淨化空氣的功能，更能透過增加自然碳匯來緩解氣候變遷的衝擊。
在資訊確認方面，各界對於氣候變遷帶來的挑戰具有高度共識。能源產業方面，產業界一致認為AI技術的進步將導致電力需求呈現指數級成長，這直接促使再生能源與智慧電網成為市場剛需。在環境防災方面，官方統計資料顯示，高達97%的森林火災源於人為因素，特別是在秋冬乾燥季節，東北季風易使微小火星擴散，造成大面積災損。然而，兩者在處理方式上存在差異：能源產業傾向於透過技術創新與硬體升級來解決供需失衡，而地方政府則傾向於透過公眾教育與生態保育來強化環境的自我修復能力。
具體影響而言，能源產業的變革將直接帶動相關供應鏈的商機，包括智慧電網管理、儲能系統及綠能發電技術，預計展會媒合商機將持續成長。對產業界而言，如何在高耗能的AI運算需求與減碳承諾之間取得平衡，成為企業營運的重大考驗。另一方面，對於一般民眾與地方政府，森林韌性的強化則直接關係到公共安全與水土保持。新北市政府透過提供免費造林苗木，嘗試將減災行動轉化為全民參與的公共政策，這不僅有助於調節區域性微氣候，亦是落實聯合國減災目標的在地實踐。
儘管各界對於氣候變遷的急迫性已有共識，但不同觀點仍存在限制。能源專家提醒，過度依賴單一能源轉型路徑可能帶來供電不穩的風險，且AI算力帶來的電力需求增長速度是否會超越再生能源的建置速度，仍是市場觀察的核心。此外，植樹造林雖是增加碳匯的重要手段，但若缺乏後續的森林管理與防火監控，單純的植樹行為亦可能因氣候乾燥而面臨火災風險，導致碳匯成果付諸東流。因此，社會大眾不應將技術解決方案視為萬靈丹，而應在產業發展與生態保育之間尋求更為細緻的平衡。
展望後續，能源產業將密切觀察智慧能源週期間所釋出的政策訊號，以及AI算力需求對電力基礎設施的實質壓力。同時，隨著國際減災日的推廣，森林防火與韌性建構將成為秋冬季節的公共治理重點。後續觀察重點將聚焦於：第一，能源產業能否在AI浪潮下落實綠色轉型目標；第二，地方政府的植樹造林計畫能否有效提升森林對極端氣候的抵禦能力；第三，全球對於碳匯與能源效率的政策標準是否會進一步收緊，進而影響跨國產業的佈局策略。這些動態將持續影響全球氣候與能源政策的走向。
