根據報導，位於倫敦漢普斯特的 Bonoo Indian Tapas 餐廳榮獲 TripAdvisor 2026 年度英國最佳「隱藏瑰寶」餐廳的殊榮。這家位於芬奇利路（Finchley Road）的印度餐廳，因其卓越的服務和獨特的餐飲體驗而受到廣泛認可。

Bonoo Indian Tapas 以提供現代印度小吃（tapas）聞名，自2016年開業以來，已累積了近四千條評論，平均評分高達4.9分（滿分5分）。其在倫敦的餐飲界迅速崛起，成為當地最受歡迎的餐廳之一。

這項由 TripAdvisor 頒發的「旅行者之選最佳獎」（Travellers' Choice Awards Best of the Best）旨在表彰在12個月內獲得大量高評價和意見的頂級旅遊業者。資料顯示，Bonoo Indian Tapas 不僅在英國位居榜首，更在全球「隱藏瑰寶」餐廳類別中名列第13位，同時也是倫敦地區的第一名。

該餐廳的成功也促使其在穆斯韋爾山（Muswell Hill）開設了另一家分店，進一步擴大了其在倫敦的影響力。這項殊榮不僅肯定了 Bonoo Indian Tapas 的品質，也突顯了其作為一個值得探索的餐飲目的地。

## English edition

Bonoo Indian Tapas, an Indian restaurant located in Hampstead, London, has been named TripAdvisor's best 'hidden gem' restaurant in Britain for 2026, according to reports. The establishment, situated on Finchley Road, has garnered significant recognition for its exceptional service and unique dining experience.

Known for serving modern Indian small plates, Bonoo Indian Tapas has achieved an impressive average rating of 4.9 out of 5 from 3,981 reviews since its opening in 2016. Its rapid ascent in London's culinary scene has established it as one of the city's most beloved dining spots.

The accolade comes from TripAdvisor's Travellers' Choice Awards Best of the Best, which celebrates businesses receiving a high volume of outstanding reviews and opinions from the Tripadvisor community over a 12-month period. Data indicates that Bonoo Indian Tapas not only topped the list for the UK but also secured the 13th position globally in the 'hidden gem' restaurant category, and ranked first in London.

The restaurant's success has also led to the opening of another location in Muswell Hill, further expanding its presence across London. This prestigious award not only validates Bonoo Indian Tapas' quality but also highlights its status as a must-visit culinary destination.
