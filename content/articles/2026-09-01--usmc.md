  # North American Trade Deadlock: Trump’s Refusal to Renew USMCA Sparks Economic Turmoil and Industrial Anxiety
  
  > Former U.S. President Donald Trump’s refusal to renew the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA) has left the North American trade system facing a crisis of collapse. Recent negotiations between the U.S. and Canada have broken down, with both sides deeply divided over automotive industry provisions and trade restrictions, impacting the stability of regional supply chains.
  
  The cornerstone of the North American free trade system is facing unprecedented challenges. According to numerous international media reports since July 2026, the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA)—which was personally negotiated and signed by Trump himself—has recently fallen into a legal and political vacuum due to his refusal to renew it. This event is not only a major turning point for the North American regional economy but is also viewed by economists as a significant disaster that could potentially end the current North American trade framework. With the U.S. imposing new tariffs on Canada and the subsequent collapse of negotiations in Washington and Ottawa, the integration and future development of North American supply chains are in a period of high uncertainty and volatility.
  
  According to reports from Reuters and the BBC, the U.S. and Canada were once close to reaching an agreement over the past few weeks; however, the negotiations took a sharp turn for the worse at the final moment. Canadian negotiator Mark Carney publicly criticized the terms proposed by the U.S. as "unfair and inconsistent with economic principles," specifically citing the harsh requirements for the automotive industry and the U.S. attempt to impose "unacceptable" restrictions on trade agreements Canada signs with third-party countries. Although both sides attempted to resume trade talks around July 21, the subsequent implementation of new U.S. tariffs on Canada caused bilateral relations to deteriorate rapidly, leading to the complete collapse of the foundation of mutual trust at the negotiating table.
  
  Regarding the details of the breakdown in negotiations, there are some discrepancies between the accounts of various media outlets and think tanks. The BBC emphasized that the talks collapsed in the final hours due to disputes over specific clauses, with both sides holding completely opposing views on the reliability of the agreement; Reuters focused on the specific timeline and impact of the tariffs imposed by the U.S. on Canada. Furthermore, information regarding the progress of negotiations between the U.S. and Mexico remains relatively vague. It is currently unclear whether the U.S. has developed an independent trade framework for Mexico following its refusal to renew the USMCA, or if trade rules among the three North American nations will revert entirely to a more primitive state of tariff barriers.
  
  This trade deadlock has already had a direct and significant negative impact on North American industrial chains. Jock Finlayson and Steven Globerman, experts at the Fraser Institute in Canada, warned that if Canada adopts retaliatory tariffs, it will lead to a surge in costs for domestic businesses and consumers, further exacerbating the risk of supply chain disruptions in North America and dragging down the economic growth of the entire region.
  
  As the core of U.S.-Canada trade, the automotive industry has been hit particularly hard. Because production processes are highly integrated across borders, any tariff barriers will directly increase production costs and force companies to re-evaluate their investment layouts in North America.
  
  Public opinion is divided regarding the current situation. Some argue that Canada should not fall into the trap of Trump-style protectionism and advocate for Canada to adopt a unilateral free trade policy to reduce its reliance on U.S. protectionism. However, another group fears that without the legal protection of the USMCA, unilateral action may not effectively alleviate industrial pressure. Additionally, experts have cautioned against over-interpreting the long-term impact of a single U.S. policy, as domestic political factors in the U.S. and upcoming policy adjustments may still leave a narrow window for negotiation, though all parties currently remain pessimistic.
  
  Future observations will focus on whether the Canadian government will take concrete countermeasures against U.S. tariff policies and how companies across North America will adjust their cross-border supply chains to avoid high tariffs. Furthermore, whether the recently deepened industrial partnership between Germany and Canada will become an alternative economic strategy for Canada following setbacks in the North American market is also a key indicator for observing the trajectory of North American geo-economics. As the effectiveness of the USMCA gradually fades, the future landscape of the North American free trade zone will depend on the strategic choices countries make between protectionism and open markets in the coming months.

# 北美貿易協定僵局：川普拒絕續約 USMCA 引發經濟震盪與產業焦慮

> 美國前總統川普拒絕續約《美墨加協定》（USMCA），導致北美貿易體系面臨崩解危機。美加談判近日宣告破裂，雙方在汽車產業條款及貿易限制上產生嚴重分歧，衝擊區域供應鏈穩定。


北美自由貿易體系的基石正面臨前所未有的挑戰。根據 2026 年 7 月以來的多項國際媒體報導，曾由川普本人親自談判並簽署的《美墨加協定》（USMCA），近期因川普拒絕續約而陷入法律與政治真空。此事件不僅是北美區域經濟的一大轉折，更被經濟學家視為可能終結現行北美貿易架構的重大災難。隨著美國對加拿大祭出新關稅，加上雙方在華盛頓與渥太華的談判最終宣告破裂，北美供應鏈的整合性與未來發展正處於高度不確定的動盪期。
綜合路透社與 BBC 的報導，美加兩國在過去數週內曾一度接近達成協議，然而談判在最後關頭急轉直下。加拿大談判代表卡尼（Mark Carney）公開指責美方提出的條款「不公平且不符合經濟原則」，特別是針對汽車產業的苛刻要求，以及美方試圖對加拿大與第三方國家簽署貿易協定施加「不可接受」的限制。儘管雙方在 7 月 21 日前後曾嘗試恢復貿易會談，但隨後美方對加拿大實施的新關稅政策，使雙邊關係迅速惡化，導致談判桌上的互信基礎徹底瓦解。
針對談判破裂的細節，各家媒體與智庫的說法存在部分差異。BBC 強調談判是在最後數小時內因條款爭議而崩潰，且雙方對於協議可靠性的認知完全對立；路透社則側重於美方對加國施加關稅的具體時程與影響。此外，關於美墨之間的談判進展，目前資訊仍相對模糊，各界尚不清楚美方在拒絕續約 USMCA 後，是否已針對墨西哥制定了獨立的貿易應對框架，抑或北美三國的貿易規則將全面回歸到更為原始的關稅壁壘狀態。
此一貿易僵局對北美產業鏈已產生直接且顯著的負面影響。加拿大菲沙研究所（Fraser Institute）專家喬克·芬萊森（Jock Finlayson）與史蒂芬·格洛伯曼（Steven Globerman）警告，加拿大若採取報復性關稅，將導致本國企業與消費者的成本激增，進一步加劇北美供應鏈的斷鏈風險，並拖累整體區域的經濟成長。
汽車產業作為美加貿易的核心，受到的衝擊尤為劇烈，由於生產流程高度跨國整合，任何關稅壁壘都將直接墊高生產成本，並迫使企業重新評估在北美的投資佈局。
針對當前局勢，輿論出現了分歧觀點。部分觀點認為，加拿大不應陷入川普式的保護主義泥淖，主張加拿大應採取單方面自由貿易政策，以降低對美方保護主義的依賴；然而，另一派觀點則擔憂，在失去 USMCA 的法律保障後，單邊行動可能無法有效緩解產業壓力。此外，專家亦提醒，目前不應過度解讀美方單一政策的長期影響，因為美國國內政治因素與即將到來的政策調整，仍可能為談判留下極小的轉圜空間，但目前各方對此均持悲觀態度。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於加拿大政府是否會針對美方的關稅政策採取具體反制措施，以及北美各國企業如何調整其跨國供應鏈以規避高額關稅。此外，德國與加拿大近期深化的工業夥伴關係，是否會成為加拿大在北美市場受挫後的替代性經濟戰略，亦是觀察北美地緣經濟走向的關鍵指標。隨著 USMCA 的效力逐漸消退，北美自由貿易區的未來格局，將取決於未來數月內各國在保護主義與開放市場之間的戰略抉擇。
