中佛羅里達大學（UCF）橄欖球隊於2026年9月26日星期六，在對陣德州基督教大學（TCU）的Big 12聯盟賽季開幕戰中，首次佩戴了印有華特迪士尼世界標誌的頭盔。這一引人注目的新標誌被放置在頭盔的後保險槓上，象徵著UCF體育與華特迪士尼世界度假區之間建立的最新贊助夥伴關係。

據UCF Athletics官方網站報導，這項新的頭盔贊助協議於2026年9月25日星期五正式揭幕。此次合作將兩個知名組織連結起來，並在全國電視轉播的賽事中首次亮相，吸引了廣泛關注。此舉被視為大學體育界持續增加商業合作夥伴關係的趨勢之一。

此次合作不僅為UCF橄欖球隊帶來了新的視覺元素，也為華特迪士尼世界度假區提供了在大學體育舞台上的品牌曝光機會。隨著越來越多的大學體育項目尋求與企業建立贊助關係，UCF與迪士尼世界的合作案例，進一步凸顯了體育行銷和品牌聯名的重要性，預計此類夥伴關係將在未來持續增長。

## English edition

The University of Central Florida (UCF) football team debuted helmets adorned with the Walt Disney World logo on Saturday, September 26, 2026, during their Big 12 Conference opener against TCU. This prominent new decal, placed on the rear bumper of the helmets, signifies the latest sponsorship partnership established between UCF Athletics and Walt Disney World Resort.

According to the official UCF Athletics website, the new helmet sponsorship was officially unveiled on Friday, September 25, 2026. This collaboration brings together two prominent organizations and made its debut during a nationally televised game, drawing significant attention. The move is seen as part of a continuing trend within collegiate sports to increase commercial partnerships.

This partnership not only introduces a new visual element for the UCF football team but also provides Walt Disney World Resort with brand exposure on the collegiate sports stage. As more university athletic programs seek to establish sponsorship relationships with corporations, the collaboration between UCF and Disney World further underscores the importance of sports marketing and brand co-branding, with such partnerships expected to continue growing in the future.
