  # KMT Vice Chairman Hsiao Hsu-tsen Attends Dongguan Taiwan Expo, Emphasizes "Peace and Prosperity for Taiwan" Strategy
  
  > KMT Vice Chairman Hsiao Hsu-tsen attended the 17th Taiwan Products Expo in Dongguan, Guangdong, where he publicly called for replacing confrontation with goodwill-based dialogue. He reiterated the KMT's commitment to promoting cross-strait economic and cultural exchanges through the "Peace and Prosperity for Taiwan" strategy, aiming to create a stable environment for Taiwanese businesses and citizens to innovate and live.
  
  On the morning of September 3, 2026, KMT Vice Chairman Hsiao Hsu-tsen officially attended the opening ceremony of the "17th Dongguan Taiwan Products Expo" held in Dongguan City, Guangdong Province, mainland China. This expo has long served as an important platform for cross-strait economic and trade exchanges, aimed at fostering connections between Taiwanese-funded enterprises and the mainland Chinese market. In his address, Hsiao presented a clear discourse on the current development of cross-strait relations, emphasizing that the existing hostile atmosphere between the two sides must be broken through proactive measures. He advocated for "goodwill" as the core principle for handling cross-strait affairs, replacing the current model of confrontation to resolve the cross-strait stalemate through institutionalized dialogue.
  
  According to public information from the event, Hsiao explicitly pointed out that the KMT's future cross-strait policy will continue to focus on the "Peace and Prosperity for Taiwan" strategy. The core objective of this strategy is to deepen connections between the two sides in areas such as economy, culture, youth exchange, and social interaction through multi-layered cooperation. Hsiao emphasized that the KMT will fully promote large-scale exchanges and cooperation in these fields, with the ultimate goal of providing a stable and predictable business environment for the Taiwanese business community. This would allow Taiwanese businesspeople and citizens to innovate without worry and ensure that the people of Taiwan can live and work in peace. This move is seen as a concrete action by the KMT to seek a breakthrough in the current cross-strait situation.
  
  Regarding Hsiao's remarks, current attention is primarily focused on the KMT's interpretation of the interaction model for cross-strait relations. Although Hsiao emphasized the necessity of "Peace and Prosperity for Taiwan" and "goodwill over confrontation," there are still varying interpretations regarding the specific supporting measures for the practical implementation of this strategy and how it will address the current complex international geopolitical environment. At present, it remains to be seen whether this strategy can garner a synchronized response from both official and private sectors on both sides of the strait, and how it will be implemented in specific economic and trade cooperation projects.
  
  Furthermore, opinions are divided regarding the political implications of Hsiao's attendance. Some observers believe this move demonstrates the KMT's proactive attitude toward cross-strait exchanges, while other commentators point out that against the backdrop of weak mutual trust, it remains uncertain whether unilateral appeals for goodwill can be effectively translated into substantive policy dividends.
  
  Hsiao's public remarks hold indicative significance for the Taiwanese business community. As Dongguan has long been a major hub for Taiwanese businesses in mainland China, the stability of its business environment directly affects the investment willingness and industrial layout of these firms. The "Peace and Prosperity for Taiwan" strategy proposed by Hsiao explicitly links the economic interests of Taiwanese businesses with cross-strait peace and stability. This is not only a policy commitment to the business community but also reflects the KMT's strategic thinking of using economic and trade ties as a buffer for cross-strait relations. For relevant industries, maintaining open channels for dialogue would help reduce uncertainties in logistics, supply chains, and investment regulations, thereby stabilizing the role of Taiwanese businesses within the Eurasian supply chain.
  
  However, it must be noted that the complexity of cross-strait relations far exceeds the scope of a single economic and trade expo. Although Hsiao called for replacing confrontation with dialogue, in the current global geopolitical landscape, political and economic ties in the Eurasian region are becoming increasingly tight, and cross-strait relations are significantly influenced by international factors. Therefore, one should not over-interpret a speech at a single venue as representing an immediate and major shift in cross-strait policy. Furthermore, the details and implementation path of the policy framework for the "Peace and Prosperity for Taiwan" strategy proposed by Hsiao still await further clarification and explanation by the KMT.
  
  When evaluating this event, all parties should consider the structural limitations of cross-strait interactions, avoid viewing the position of a single political party as a comprehensive conclusion on cross-strait relations, and continue to monitor the specific responses and subsequent policy adjustments from all sides.
  
  Looking ahead, the dynamics of cross-strait economic and trade exchanges remain a key point of observation. With the holding of the 17th Dongguan Taiwan Products Expo, whether more concrete economic and trade cooperation agreements will be signed, or whether substantive new plans for youth and cultural exchanges will be promoted, will become key indicators for testing the feasibility of the "Peace and Prosperity for Taiwan" strategy. Additionally, how the KMT handles the balance between cross-strait relations and international affairs in subsequent political activities, and how it responds to different voices within Taiwan regarding cross-strait policy, will continue to influence the atmosphere of cross-strait interactions. It is recommended that all parties continue to monitor subsequent interactions between official and private institutions on both sides and pay close attention to the actual impact of relevant policies on the Eurasian economic and trade landscape.

# 國民黨副主席蕭旭岑赴東莞參與臺博會 強調兩岸和平興臺路線

> 國民黨副主席蕭旭岑於廣東東莞出席第17屆臺灣名品博覽會，公開呼籲以善意對話取代對立，並重申國民黨將致力推動兩岸經濟與文化交流，透過「和平興臺」路線，為臺商與臺胞創造安穩的創業與生活環境。


國民黨副主席蕭旭岑於2026年9月3日上午，正式出席在中國大陸廣東省東莞市舉辦的「第17屆東莞臺灣名品博覽會」開幕典禮。此項博覽會長期以來作為兩岸經貿交流的重要平臺，旨在促進臺資企業與中國大陸市場的連結。蕭旭岑在致詞中針對當前兩岸關係發展提出明確論述，強調現階段兩岸之間存在的敵對氛圍必須透過積極作為予以打破，並主張應以「善意」作為處理兩岸事務的核心原則，取代現有的對抗模式，進而以對話機制化解兩岸之間的對立僵局。
根據現場公開資訊，蕭旭岑明確指出國民黨未來的兩岸政策將持續聚焦於「和平興臺」路線。此路線的核心目標在於透過多層次的交流與合作，深化兩岸在經濟、文化、青年交流及社會互動等領域的連結。蕭旭岑強調，國民黨將全力推動上述領域的大交流與大合作，其最終目的在於為臺灣企業界提供一個穩定、可預測的經營環境，讓臺商與臺胞能夠在無後顧之憂的情況下安心創業，確保臺灣人民能夠在和平的環境中安居樂業，此舉被視為國民黨試圖在當前兩岸局勢中尋求突破的具體行動。
針對蕭旭岑的發言，目前各界關注焦點主要集中於國民黨對於兩岸關係互動模式的詮釋。儘管蕭旭岑強調「和平興臺」與「善意對抗」的必要性，但外界對於該路線在實際執行層面的具體配套措施，以及如何對應當前複雜的國際地緣政治環境，仍存在不同解讀。目前公開資訊中，關於此路線是否能獲得兩岸官方與民間同步響應，以及在具體經貿合作專案上將如何落實，尚待後續觀察。
此外，針對蕭旭岑此次出席活動的政治意涵，各方意見亦有分歧，部分觀察家認為此舉顯示國民黨在兩岸交流上採取積極主動的態度，但也有評論指出，在當前兩岸互信基礎薄弱的背景下，單方面的善意呼籲是否能有效轉化為實質的政策紅利，仍存有不確定性。
此次蕭旭岑的公開談話對於臺商群體而言具有指標性意義。長期以來，東莞作為臺商在中國大陸的重要聚落，其營商環境的穩定與否直接影響臺商的投資意願與產業佈局。蕭旭岑提出的「和平興臺」路線，明確將臺商的經濟利益與兩岸和平穩定掛鉤，這不僅是對臺商群體的一種政策承諾，也反映了國民黨試圖透過經貿連結作為兩岸關係緩衝器的策略思維。對於相關產業而言，若兩岸能維持對話管道的暢通，將有利於降低物流、供應鏈及投資法規上的不確定性，進而穩定臺商在歐亞供應鏈中的角色定位。
然而，必須指出的是，兩岸關係的複雜性遠超單一經貿博覽會的範疇。儘管蕭旭岑呼籲以對話取代對立，但在當前全球地緣政治局勢下，歐亞地區的政經連結日益緊密，兩岸關係亦受到國際因素的顯著影響。因此，不應過度解讀單一場域的致詞即代表兩岸政策將出現立即性的重大轉向。此外，蕭旭岑所提出的「和平興臺」路線，其政策框架的細節與執行路徑仍有待國民黨內部進一步釐清與對外說明。
各界在評估此一事件時，應考量兩岸互動的結構性限制，避免將單一政黨的立場視為兩岸關係的全面性定論，並應持續關注各方對於此路線的具體回應與後續政策調整。
展望後續，兩岸經貿交流的動態仍是觀察重點。隨著第17屆東莞臺灣名品博覽會的舉行，後續是否會有更多具體的經貿合作協議簽署，或是兩岸在青年、文化交流層面是否有實質性的新計畫推動，將成為檢驗「和平興臺」路線是否具備執行力的關鍵指標。此外，國民黨在後續的政治活動中，將如何處理兩岸關係與國際事務的平衡，以及如何回應臺灣內部對於兩岸政策的不同聲音，亦將持續影響兩岸互動的氛圍。建議各界持續關注兩岸官方與民間機構的後續互動，並密切留意相關政策在歐亞經貿版圖中的實際影響力。
