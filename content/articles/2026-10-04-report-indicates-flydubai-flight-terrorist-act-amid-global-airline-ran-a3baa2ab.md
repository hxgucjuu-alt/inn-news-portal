根據Livemint報導，阿聯酋政府擁有的Flydubai航空公司一架從杜拜飛往特拉維夫的航班近期遭遇空中緊急事件。報導指出，副機長在飛行途中襲擊了機長Smit Machchhar，導致飛機急速下降。當局已將此事件定性為一宗有預謀的恐怖行為。

此事件發生之際，全球航空業的表現與排名正受到廣泛關注。資料顯示，新加坡航空在2026年的Skytrax世界航空獎中榮獲「全球最佳航空公司」稱號，位居榜首。報導亦指出，美國的航空公司未能進入前20名。

此外，根據維基百科資料，截至2026年，全球最大的航空公司可透過多種方式衡量。例如，聯合航空在可用座位里程、營收座位里程、機隊規模、主線員工數量及服務目的地方面均為最大。而達美航空則在營收、資產、市值和品牌價值方面最具價值。儘管原始新聞標題提及「Smit Machhar的Flydubai排名如何」，但現有資料並未明確指出Flydubai在這些全球頂級或最大航空公司排名中的具體位置。

## English edition

According to Livemint, a recent Dubai-Tel Aviv flight operated by the Emirati government-owned Flydubai airline experienced a mid-air emergency. Reports indicate that the co-pilot attacked Captain Smit Machchhar during the flight, leading to a rapid descent. Authorities have labeled this incident as a planned terrorist act.

This incident occurs amidst widespread attention on the performance and rankings of the global airline industry. Data indicates that Singapore Airlines was named the "World's Best Airline for 2026" at the Skytrax World Airline Awards, securing the top spot. Reports also noted that US carriers failed to crack the top 20.

Furthermore, according to Wikipedia data, as of 2026, the world's largest airlines can be measured in several ways. For instance, United Airlines was the largest in terms of available seat miles, revenue seat miles, mainline fleet size, number of mainline employees, and destinations served. Delta Air Lines, on the other hand, was the most valuable by revenue, assets, market capitalization, and brand value. Although the original news title questioned "Where does Smit Machhar's Flydubai rank," the available data does not specify Flydubai's exact position within these global top or largest airline rankings.
