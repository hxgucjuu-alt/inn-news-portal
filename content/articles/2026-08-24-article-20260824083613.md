  # Asia's AI Market Growth Advances Alongside Medical Applications, Presenting Both Industry Opportunities and Labor Disruptions
  
  > The global AI platform and model market is projected to grow 63% this year, with Asia's tech industry seen as the core of the revolution. Meanwhile, AI applications in healthcare have gained international recognition, though concerns over job displacement remain to be observed.
  
  The global artificial intelligence (AI) market is in a phase of rapid expansion. According to the latest forecast from market research firm Gartner, the global AI platform and model market will grow by 63% this year, indicating that the industry continues to maintain strong growth momentum. At the same time, Asia's technology sector is regarded by asset management firms as the front line of this AI revolution. From cross-border corporate investment strategies to policy initiatives across various countries, AI has clearly evolved from a purely technological topic into a key force driving economic and social transformation.
  
  On the application front, Taiwan's healthcare system has also delivered tangible results. China Medical University Hospital (CMUH) achieved the highest global score in an international digital health assessment, demonstrating that AI technology is gradually being implemented in real-world settings.
  
  According to data cited by the Economic Daily News from Gartner, the rapid growth of the global AI platform and model market reflects strong corporate demand for generative AI, machine learning models, and related infrastructure. This forecast covers a broad spectrum ranging from cloud computing to edge devices, indicating that AI's penetration is no longer confined to a single industry.
  
  Meanwhile, an analysis report released by Allianz Global Investors points out that Asia's technology industry is firmly positioned at the forefront of the global AI revolution. The report emphasizes that although investors need to be mindful of potential risks such as geopolitical uncertainty and currency headwinds, the Asian region still offers a diverse range of promising corporate targets. From semiconductor manufacturing to software services, the far-reaching impact of AI applications is evident.
  
  Cross-referencing the above sources confirms several common facts: First, the global AI market is experiencing significant growth, and Asia is widely recognized as a key player. Second, AI technology applications have advanced from the proof-of-concept stage to actual industrial investment and healthcare settings. However, the focus of different sources varies. Gartner's data emphasizes quantitative analysis of market size and growth rates, while Allianz Global Investors approaches the topic from an asset allocation perspective, highlighting the investment value and risk balance of Asian tech stocks.
  
  Meanwhile, a report from Business Next focuses on specific medical application cases. CMUH achieved the highest global score in the HIMSS Digital Health Indicator (DHI). Although this achievement reflects the performance of a single institution, it also indirectly confirms the tangible benefits of AI in improving healthcare quality and safety.
  
  The impact of this AI wave on the Asian region is multi-faceted. On the industry front, Allianz Global Investors' analysis indicates that Asia's technology supply chain plays an indispensable role in global AI development. From upstream chip design to downstream end-user applications, Asian companies are visibly involved. This undoubtedly represents a significant growth opportunity for Taiwan, South Korea, and Southeast Asian countries whose economies rely on technology manufacturing. On the policy front, governments across the region are actively promoting AI-related infrastructure and talent development, attempting to secure advantageous positions in this round of technological competition. However, the rapid development of AI has also raised concerns about the labor market.
  
  An IBM perspective article republished by MarketersGO directly addresses the core question: Will artificial intelligence take away our jobs? The article cites a consensus among senior executives from 150 global technology companies, who consider AI the foremost application among future forward-looking technologies. It also points out that the combination of the Internet of Things (IoT) with AI will bring about the next wave of major industrial transformation, implying the urgency of redefining job roles and reskilling.
  
  Regarding AI's impact on employment, different sources present divergent views. IBM's perspective tends to view AI as an assistive tool, emphasizing that its combination with IoT will give rise to new industries rather than simply replacing human labor. However, the article does not deny that certain highly repetitive jobs may be automated. In contrast, Allianz Global Investors' report takes an optimistic approach, focusing on the investment opportunities and industry growth potential created by AI, with less attention paid to structural impacts on the labor market. It is worth noting that most of these discussions remain at the level of overarching trends, lacking detailed quantitative analysis of specific occupations or regions, and therefore should not be over-interpreted.
  
  Furthermore, Gartner's market forecast data and HIMSS assessment results respectively represent the views of a market analysis firm and a medical professional organization. Their evaluation methodologies and indicator definitions differ, and the appropriate context for each should be considered when citing them.
  
  Key points for future observation should focus on the following aspects: First, whether the 63% market growth rate forecast by Gartner will continue to materialize in the second half of the year, and Asia's actual share within that growth. Second, whether the geopolitical risks and currency fluctuations highlighted by Allianz Global Investors will affect multinational technology companies' investment decisions in Asia. Third, whether CMUH's HIMSS achievement can drive other Taiwanese medical institutions to accelerate digital transformation, thereby creating a clustering effect. Finally, whether the AI-IoT convergence trend proposed by IBM will generate concrete industry application cases in the coming quarters and produce observable effects on employment structures.
  
  These issues will determine the actual path by which AI transforms from a technological hotspot into long-term economic momentum.

# 亞洲AI市場增速與醫療應用並進，產業機遇與勞動衝擊並存

> 全球AI平臺暨模型市場今年預估增長63%，亞洲科技業被視為革命核心；同時醫療AI應用獲國際肯定，但工作取代疑慮仍待觀察。（79字）

全球人工智慧（AI）市場正處於高速擴張期，市場研究機構Gartner的最新預測指出，今年全球AI平臺暨模型市場的增速將達到63%，顯示整體產業仍維持強勁的成長動能。與此同時，亞洲地區的科技產業被資產管理業者視為這波AI革命的前沿陣地，從跨國企業的投資佈局到各國政策推動，皆可見AI已從單純的技術話題，轉變為驅動經濟與社會變革的關鍵力量。
在應用層面，臺灣的醫療體系也傳出具體成果，中國醫藥大學附設醫院（中國附醫）更在國際數位健康評比中獲得全球最高成績，顯示AI技術正逐步落地於實際場域。
根據經濟日報引述Gartner的資料，全球AI平臺暨模型市場的快速增長，反映了企業對生成式AI、機器學習模型及相關基礎設施的強勁需求。這項預測涵蓋了從雲端運算到邊緣裝置的廣泛範疇，顯示AI技術的滲透已不再侷限於單一產業。
與此同時，安聯投資發布的分析報告指出，亞洲科技產業正穩固地站在全球AI革命的最前線，該報告強調，儘管投資人需留意地緣政治不確定性與貨幣逆風等潛在風險，但亞洲地區仍提供了一系列多元且具前景的企業標的，從半導體製造到軟體服務，皆可見到AI應用的深遠影響。
交叉比對上述來源，可確認幾項共同事實：第一，全球AI市場正經歷顯著成長，且亞洲被普遍認為是重要參與者；第二，AI技術的應用已從概念驗證階段，推進至實際的產業投資與醫療場域。然而，不同來源的關注重點存在差異。Gartner的資料側重於市場規模與增速的量化分析，安聯投資則從資產配置角度切入，強調亞洲科技股的投資價值與風險平衡。
而《數位時代》的報導則聚焦於具體的醫療應用案例，中國附醫在HIMSS「數位健康指標（DHI）」中獲得全球最高成績，此一成就雖屬個別機構表現，但也側面印證了AI在提升醫療品質與安全上的實際效益。
這波AI浪潮對亞洲地區的影響是多層面的。在產業面，安聯投資的分析指出，亞洲科技供應鏈在全球AI發展中扮演不可或缺的角色，從上游的晶片設計到下游的終端應用，皆可見到亞洲企業的參與，這對於以科技製造為經濟支柱的臺灣、韓國及東南亞國家而言，無疑是重要的成長契機。在政策面，各國政府正積極推動AI相關基礎建設與人才培育，試圖在這一輪技術競賽中佔據有利位置。然而，AI的快速發展也引發了對勞動市場的擔憂。
行銷人轉載的IBM觀點文章便直指核心問題：人工智慧是否將搶走我們的工作？該文引述全球150家科技公司高階主管的共識，認為AI是未來前瞻科技的首要應用，但也同時點出物聯網結合AI將帶來下一波產業大變革，這意味著工作內容的重新定義與技能轉型的迫切性。
針對AI對就業的衝擊，不同來源呈現出觀點差異。IBM的觀點傾向於將AI視為輔助工具，強調其與物聯網結合後將催生新興產業，而非單純取代人力；然而，該文並未否認部分重複性高的工作可能被自動化取代。相對地，安聯投資的報告則從樂觀角度出發，聚焦於AI所創造的投資機會與產業成長潛力，對勞動市場的結構性影響著墨較少。值得注意的是，這些討論多數仍停留在總體趨勢層面，缺乏針對特定職業或地區的細緻量化分析，因此在解讀時不宜過度推論。
此外，Gartner的市場預測資料與HIMSS的評比結果，分別代表了市場分析機構與醫療專業組織的看法，其評估方法與指標定義不盡相同，在引用時應注意其各自適用的脈絡。
後續觀察重點應放在以下幾個面向：首先，Gartner所預測的63%市場增速是否能在下半年持續兌現，以及亞洲市場在其中所佔的實際比重；其次，安聯投資所提示的地緣政治風險與貨幣波動，是否會影響跨國科技企業在亞洲的投資決策；再者，中國附醫在HIMSS的成績能否帶動臺灣其他醫療機構加速數位轉型，進而形成群聚效應；最後，IBM所提出的AI與物聯網結合趨勢，是否會在未來數季內催生具體的產業應用案例，並對就業結構產生可觀測的影響。
這些議題將決定AI從技術熱點轉化為長期經濟動能的實際路徑。
