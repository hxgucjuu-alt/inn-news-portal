  # US Rallies 41 Nations to Protest China’s Missile Test, Sparking International Military and Diplomatic Tensions
  
  > The United States recently united 41 countries to lodge a strong protest against China’s intercontinental ballistic missile (ICBM) test, attempting to invoke international norms to constrain Beijing. The move was immediately refuted by Chinese representatives, escalating international diplomatic and military tensions.
  
  The U.S. government recently joined forces with 41 countries at an international military negotiation conference to express strong protest against China’s recent intercontinental ballistic missile test. This move is viewed as a major diplomatic pressure campaign by the U.S. as part of its strategic layout in the Asia-Pacific and globally to counter China’s military expansion. The U.S. intends to cite the "Hague Code of Conduct against Ballistic Missile Proliferation" (HCOC) as a legal basis to demand that China cease such testing activities and has raised questions regarding its military transparency.
  
  This incident occurred in early October 2026 and quickly became the focus of global security governance issues, affecting not only the security and stability of Oceania and the Indo-Pacific region but also intensifying the power struggle between the U.S. and China over the formulation of international military rules.
  
  According to cross-referenced information from multiple sources, the U.S. and its allies clearly took collective diplomatic action to express dissatisfaction with China’s missile test. Confirmed facts include: the U.S. organized a coalition of 41 countries to pressure Beijing at an international conference; in response to this collective protest, Chinese representatives adopted a hardline stance, refuting the U.S. accusations on the spot and responding with strong rhetoric such as "try it and see," indicating that China completely rejects the legitimacy and reasonableness of the U.S. attempt to use international norms as a constraint. This interaction demonstrates that both sides have entered a state of high antagonism regarding military transparency and the power to interpret international norms.
  
  However, there are significant differences in perspectives regarding the specific legal efficacy and subsequent impact of this international protest. The U.S. advocates using the "Hague Code of Conduct against Ballistic Missile Proliferation" to constrain China, but critics such as Li Zheng-jie have pointed out in media commentary that because China is not a signatory to the convention, the U.S. move is questioned as a "double standard" political maneuver. Furthermore, information yet to be confirmed includes: what specific sanctions these 41 countries might impose following the protest, and whether China will take further military countermeasures in response to this diplomatic containment. These details lack clear policy documentation in current public reports and remain in a gray area of public interpretation and political maneuvering.
  
  The impact of this incident on the Asia-Pacific and Oceania regions is particularly profound. As a core area of the Indo-Pacific strategy, Oceania island nations have long been at the forefront of the U.S.-China rivalry. By uniting allies to exert pressure, the U.S. aims to consolidate its security perimeter in the region and convey the importance of military norms to Pacific island nations. However, such high-intensity diplomatic pressure may lead to rising regional tensions, forcing surrounding countries to make difficult choices between the two major powers. For relevant industries, the escalation of military tensions may affect regional shipping safety, the stability of technology supply chains, and adjustments in national defense budgets and foreign policies, increasing uncertainty in regional governance.
  
  There are differing international views regarding the U.S. diplomatic action. Perspectives supporting the U.S. position argue that China’s ICBM test threatens global security and must be constrained through international multilateral mechanisms to maintain the current international order. Perspectives opposing or questioning the U.S. position argue that the U.S. has long promoted military expansion globally while demanding that other countries adhere to specific norms without a legal basis, which is essentially an act to maintain its hegemonic status rather than being based purely on international justice.
  
  Limitations and the need to avoid over-interpretation lie in the fact that a single protest does not equate to a comprehensive consensus within the international community to sanction China, and the motivations of each country in joining this coalition vary; therefore, this should not be viewed as the complete formation of a global anti-China front.
  
  Subsequent observation should focus on several key aspects: first, whether China will propose countermeasures in international forums or demonstrate a tougher stance in military exercises; second, whether these 41 countries will take more substantive restrictive measures in future international conferences rather than merely issuing verbal protests; and finally, the reactions of Asia-Pacific and Oceania countries to this incident, particularly how these nations balance their economic cooperation with China against their security and defense cooperation with the U.S. The international community will continue to monitor the game between the U.S. and China regarding military transparency and rule-making, which will directly affect the stability and evolution of the future global security architecture.

# 美國號召41國抗議中國試射飛彈 引發國際軍事與外交角力

> 美國近期聯合41個國家，針對中國試射洲際彈道飛彈提出強烈抗議，並試圖引用國際準則進行約束，引發中國代表當場駁斥，國際外交與軍事緊張情勢隨之升溫。


美國政府近期在國際軍事談判會議上，聯合全球41個國家，針對中國近期試射洲際彈道飛彈的行為表達強烈抗議。此舉被視為美國在亞太地區及全球戰略佈局中，針對中國軍事擴張採取的一項重大外交施壓行動。美方意圖引用「防彈道飛彈擴散行為準則」（Hague Code of Conduct against Ballistic Missile Proliferation, HCOC）作為法律依據，要求中國停止相關試射活動，並對其軍事透明度提出質疑。
此事件發生於2026年10月上旬，迅速成為全球安全治理議題的焦點，不僅涉及大洋洲及印太區域的安全穩定，更牽動了美中兩大強權在國際軍事規則制定上的角力。
根據多個來源的資訊交叉比對，此次事件中，美國及其盟友明確採取了集體外交行動，針對中國的飛彈試射表達不滿。各方共同確認的事實包括：美方確實組織了一個包含41個國家的聯盟，在國際會議場合對北京當局施壓；而中國代表在面對此一集體抗議時，採取了強硬的對抗姿態，當場駁斥美方的指控，並以「試試看」等強烈語氣回應，顯示中方對於美方引用國際準則進行約束的合法性與合理性持完全否定的態度。這一互動過程顯示，雙方在軍事透明度與國際規範的解釋權上，已進入高度對立的狀態。
然而，關於此次國際抗議行動的具體法律效力及後續影響，各界說法存在顯著差異。美方主張透過「防彈道飛彈擴散行為準則」來約束中國，但批評者如慄正傑在媒體評論中指出，由於中國並未簽署該項公約，美方此舉被質疑為「雙重標準」的政治操弄。此外，目前尚待確認的資訊包括：這41個國家在抗議行動後的具體制裁手段為何？以及中方是否會採取更進一步的軍事反制措施來回應此一外交圍堵。這些細節在現階段的公開報導中尚未有明確的政策檔案支援，仍處於各方輿論解讀與政治角力的模糊地帶。
此次事件對亞太及大洋洲地區的影響尤為深遠。作為印太戰略的核心區域，大洋洲島國長期以來處於美中角力的前沿。美國透過聯合盟友施壓，意在鞏固其在該區域的安全防線，並向太平洋島國傳遞軍事規範的重要性。然而，這種高強度的外交施壓可能導致區域緊張局勢升高，迫使周邊國家在美中兩大強權之間做出更困難的選擇。對於相關產業而言，軍事緊張局勢的升溫可能影響區域內的航運安全、科技供應鏈的穩定性，以及各國在國防預算與外交政策上的調整，增加了區域治理的不確定性。
針對美方的外交行動，國際間存在不同觀點。支援美方立場的觀點認為，中國試射洲際彈道飛彈已威脅全球安全，必須透過國際多邊機制進行約束，以維護現行的國際秩序。反對或質疑美方立場的觀點則認為，美國長期在全球推動軍事擴張，卻在缺乏法律基礎的情況下要求他國遵守特定準則，此舉本質上是為了維護其霸權地位，而非單純基於國際公義。
限制與不應過度解讀之處在於，單一事件的抗議並不等同於國際社會已對中國達成全面的制裁共識，且各國參與此一聯盟的動機各異，不應將此視為全球反中陣線的完全成形。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於幾個關鍵面向：首先是中國是否會在國際場合提出反制提案，或是在軍事演習上展現更強硬的姿態；其次是這41個國家在後續的國際會議中，是否會採取更具實質性的約束措施，而非僅止於口頭抗議；最後則是亞太與大洋洲國家對此事件的反應，特別是這些國家如何平衡與中國的經濟合作關係，以及與美國的安全防衛合作。國際社會將持續關注美中兩國在軍事透明度與規則制定上的博弈，這將直接影響未來全球安全架構的穩定性與演變方向。
