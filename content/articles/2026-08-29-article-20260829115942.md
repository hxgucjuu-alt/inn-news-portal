  > Hong Kong Chief Executive John Lee has concluded his visit to Central Asia, announcing the signing of 96 cooperation agreements with Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan. These agreements span various sectors, including business, finance, technology, and aviation, aimed at deepening regional economic and trade ties.
  
  Hong Kong Chief Executive John Lee recently led a delegation to Central Asia for official visits to Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan, a diplomatic and economic mission aimed at strengthening bilateral relations between Hong Kong and the Central Asian region. As a key node in the "Belt and Road" Initiative, Central Asian countries have shown significant potential in infrastructure development, energy exploration, and digital financial transformation in recent years. The Hong Kong government has positioned this visit as a strategic move to explore emerging markets, seeking to leverage its financial service advantages to assist Central Asian enterprises in accessing international capital markets while creating more cross-border cooperation opportunities for Hong Kong's professional services sector.
  
  According to official releases from the Hong Kong government news portal, the visit yielded significant results, with a total of 96 cooperation agreements and memoranda of understanding (MOUs) reached. The core scope of these agreements covers eight major areas: business, education, social development, economic and trade exchanges, investment cooperation, financial services, technological innovation, and aviation transport. Officials emphasized that these agreements are not merely statements of intent but possess substantive industrial linkage effects, designed to establish long-term, stable cooperation mechanisms to promote deep integration between Hong Kong and Central Asian countries in terms of policy alignment and market access.
  
  Although the government has announced the number of agreements and the sectors covered, specific execution details and the legal binding force of each agreement remain to be clarified. Regional media outlets such as Trend.az have reported that Uzbekistan’s Asia Trans Gas has initiated a tender project for non-ferrous metals, indicating the country's urgent demand for industrial raw materials and infrastructure construction. However, the "eight major achievements" announced by Hong Kong officials have not yet provided a complete list disclosing specific investment amounts or technology transfer details for individual industries, such as energy supply chains and mining development.
  
  Furthermore, whether these various memoranda can be converted into concrete commercial orders remains dependent on subsequent market environment assessments and the coordination of cross-border regulations.
  
  This visit has a direct impact on the industrial development of the Central Asian region. As Hong Kong financial institutions and technology companies strengthen their ties with the region, it is expected that they will provide technical support for the digital transformation of Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan. For instance, the Mongolian fintech application "Mandal Khurimtlal" was recently awarded the Asia Fintech Awards 2026, demonstrating the rapid rise of digital finance in Central Asia and its surrounding areas.

# 香港特首率團出訪中亞：與哈薩克及烏茲別克達成八大領域合作

> 香港行政長官李家超結束中亞訪問行程，宣佈與哈薩克及烏茲別克簽署96份合作協議，涵蓋商務、金融、科技及航空等多個領域，深化區域經貿連結。


香港行政長官李家超近期率領代表團出訪中亞，正式訪問哈薩克與烏茲別克，此次外交與經貿行程旨在強化香港與中亞地區的雙邊關係。作為「一帶一路」倡議的重要節點，中亞國家近年來在基礎建設、能源開發與數位金融轉型上展現高度潛力。香港政府將此次訪問定位為開拓新興市場的戰略舉措，試圖透過金融服務優勢，協助中亞企業進入國際資本市場，同時為香港的專業服務業尋求更多跨國合作機會。
根據香港政府新聞網的官方發布，此次訪問行程取得顯著成果，雙方共計達成96份合作協議與備忘錄。這些協議的核心範疇涵蓋商務、教育、社會發展、經貿往來、投資合作、金融服務、科技創新以及航空運輸等八大領域。官方強調，這些協議不僅是單純的意向書，更具備實質的產業連結效應，旨在建立長期穩定的合作機制，推動香港與中亞國家在政策對接與市場准入方面的深度整合。
儘管官方公佈了協議數量與涵蓋領域，但在具體執行細節與各項協議的法律約束力上，目前仍有待進一步釐清。Trend.az 等區域媒體報導指出，烏茲別克的 Asia Trans Gas 已啟動針對有色金屬的招標專案，顯示該國在工業原料與基礎設施建設上的迫切需求。然而，香港官方公佈的「八大成果」中，對於個別產業如能源供應鏈、礦業開發的具體投資金額與技術轉移細節，尚未向公眾披露完整清單。
此外，各項備忘錄是否能轉化為具體的商業訂單，仍取決於後續的市場環境評估與跨境法規協調。
此次訪問對中亞地區的產業發展具有直接影響。隨著香港金融機構與科技企業加強與當地的連結，預期將為哈薩克與烏茲別克的數位轉型提供技術支援。例如，蒙古的金融科技應用「Mandal Khurimtlal」近期獲頒 Asia Fintech Awards 2026 殊榮，顯示中亞及周邊地區在數位金融領域的快速崛起。
香港作為國際金融中心，其參與將加速中亞地區金融科技生態系的成熟，並透過航空協議的簽署，強化區域間的人員流動與貨物運輸效率，進而提升中亞國家在全球供應鏈中的戰略地位。
針對此次合作，各界觀點存在差異。支持者認為，這是香港落實多元化貿易政策的關鍵一步，能有效降低對傳統市場的依賴；然而，批評者或保守觀察者則提醒，中亞市場的政治風險、法律體系差異以及物流成本，均是企業在進入時必須考量的限制因素。此外，不應過度解讀單次訪問的經濟效應，協議的簽署僅是合作的開端，後續能否克服文化隔閡、語言障礙及行政效率等現實挑戰，才是決定合作成敗的關鍵。公眾與投資者應理性看待官方資料，避免對短期的經濟成長預期過高。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於96份協議的落實進度，特別是金融合作與科技轉移的具體案例。此外，香港與中亞國家是否會針對稅務協定、投資保障條款進行進一步的法律談判，將是評估合作深度的重要指標。同時，隨著 Caspian Post 等區域媒體對中亞動態的持續追蹤，外界亦可觀察中亞國家在與香港合作的同時，如何平衡與其他國際大國的經貿關係。INN新聞編輯部將持續監控相關產業招標進展，並針對後續的經貿資料變化進行追蹤報導。
