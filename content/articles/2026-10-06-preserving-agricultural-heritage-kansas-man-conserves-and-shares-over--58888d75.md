綜合外媒報導，來自美國堪薩斯州的維修工人羅恩·杜馬（Ron Thuma）自1970年代起投身於種子保存。最初他僅保存了家族世代流傳的少數傳家寶種子品種，然而隨著時間推移，這項興趣逐漸發展為規模龐大的蔬菜種子保存工作。

資料顯示，杜馬在加入約翰·威西（John Withee）創立的「Wanigan Associates」組織後，進一步擴展了他的種子收藏範疇。數十年來，他不僅細心培育各類作物，更完整記錄每種作物的生長數據與歷程，將個人農事經驗系統化記錄。

報導指出，杜馬主要透過「種子保存者交流協會」（Seed Savers Exchange）等平台，向廣大園藝愛好者無私分享其收集與繁衍的成果，累計分享品種已超過275種。這項活動不僅維護了農業生物多樣性與傳統作物遺產，同時也作為家族傳統持續傳承。

## English edition

According to multiple reports, Ron Thuma, a maintenance worker from Kansas, began saving seeds in the 1970s. Initially starting with just a handful of heirloom varieties preserved by his own family, his long-term efforts eventually developed into one of the notable heirloom vegetable collections in the United States.

Records indicate that Thuma broadened the scope of his seed conservation after joining Wanigan Associates, an initiative established by John Withee. Over several decades, he meticulously cultivated different crops while maintaining detailed records documenting their growth patterns and characteristics.

Reports state that Thuma has shared more than 275 heirloom seed varieties with fellow gardeners through platforms such as the Seed Savers Exchange. In addition to preserving agricultural biodiversity and heritage crops, gardening and seed stewardship have remained a sustained family tradition that continues to be passed down.
