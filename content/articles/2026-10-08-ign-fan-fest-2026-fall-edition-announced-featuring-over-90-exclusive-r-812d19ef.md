娛樂媒體IGN公布「IGN Fan Fest 2026：秋季版」（IGN Fan Fest 2026: Fall Edition）的相關播映時程與活動細節。根據報導指出，本屆活動將提供超過90項獨家內容展示，範圍涵蓋電子遊戲、電影、電視影集、漫畫及收藏品等多個娛樂領域。

資料顯示，本次秋季活動的線上直播預計於美西時間10月14日上午10點（美東時間下午1點、英國夏令時間下午6點）正式展開，相關活動預熱則自當週週一提前啟動。此系列活動為該展會的延續，過去活動亦著重於預告片、實機遊玩畫面、深入解析以及對創作者的專訪。

在預計亮相的項目方面，相關報導提及將包含《VisionQuest》、《電馭叛客：邊緣行者 2》（Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2）、《虹彩六號：圍攻行動》（Rainbow Six Siege）以及《Whalefall》等作品的最新資訊。主辦方預期將透過跨平台線上直播，向全球玩家及影視愛好者展示未來的重點發行內容。

## English edition

Entertainment media outlet IGN has unveiled details and viewing schedules for IGN Fan Fest 2026: Fall Edition. Reports indicate that the upcoming event will feature more than 90 exclusive reveals spanning video games, movies, television series, comics, and collectibles.

According to coverage across regional editions, the official livestream is scheduled to take place on Wednesday, October 14, at 10:00 AM PT (1:00 PM ET / 6:00 PM BST), with event festivities kicking off earlier that week on Monday. The showcase is part of an ongoing event series that typically brings together trailers, gameplay reveals, deep dives, and creator interviews.

Featured presentations highlighted in the announcements include updates on projects such as VisionQuest, Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2, Rainbow Six Siege, and Whalefall, among others. The showcase will be streamed online to global audiences, highlighting upcoming releases across the digital entertainment spectrum.
