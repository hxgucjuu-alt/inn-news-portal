報導指出，一項針對澳洲投資者的豪華別墅投資項目已然崩潰，數百萬美元的投資付諸東流。該項目目前深陷警方投訴、投資者損失慘重以及前商業夥伴之間的激烈爭鬥。

根據Dailymail.com的報導，一場法庭訴訟揭露了涉事商人阿德里安·詹姆斯·坎貝爾（Adrian James Campbell）隱藏的犯罪歷史，使其過往的商業行為和犯罪記錄再次受到公眾審視。資料顯示，此事件涉及與被禁澳洲商人傑米·麥金太爾（Jamie McIntyre）相關的詐騙指控。

澳洲媒體報導指出，數十名澳洲普通投資者據稱在與傑米·麥金太爾相關的龍目島（Lombok）豪華退休別墅投資騙局中損失了高達700萬美元。巴厘島警方目前正在調查這些指控。

這場災難不僅導致投資者蒙受巨大損失，也引發了前商業夥伴之間就這場數百萬美元的投資災難展開激烈爭奪。

## English edition

A luxury villa investment project, heavily marketed to Australian investors, has reportedly collapsed, with millions of dollars invested now in ruins. The project is currently embroiled in police complaints, devastated investors, and a bitter war between former business partners.

According to Dailymail.com, court proceedings have uncovered the hidden criminal past of businessman Adrian James Campbell, bringing his past business and criminal history back into public scrutiny. Reports indicate the incident involves alleged fraud linked to banned Australian businessman Jamie McIntyre.

Australian media outlets report that dozens of "mum and dad" investors have allegedly lost up to $7 million in a luxury retirement villa investment scam in Lombok, linked to Jamie McIntyre. Bali police are currently investigating these allegations.

This disaster has not only led to significant losses for investors but has also sparked a fierce battle between former business partners over the multimillion-dollar investment catastrophe.
