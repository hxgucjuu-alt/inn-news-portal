  Located at the heart of the Eurasian continent, Central Asia occupies a unique geographical position. It serves as a transportation hub connecting East and West, and has garnered significant international attention due to its proximity to several global geopolitical hotspots. The region is not only rich in natural resources but is also viewed as an emerging area of potential for global economic development. According to an analysis by the German Marshall Fund (GMF), while the five Central Asian nations offer economic opportunities, they simultaneously face long-term challenges regarding security and regional stability.
  
  As the largest economy in the region, Kazakhstan’s economic performance serves as a key indicator. According to The Times of Central Asia, Kazakhstan’s Gross Domestic Product (GDP) reached approximately $306 billion in 2025, accounting for more than half of the total economic scale of the five Central Asian nations, highlighting its central role in regional economic integration.
  
  In recent years, Central Asian countries have shown a significant shift in defense equipment procurement, with Turkish-made Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) becoming a popular choice for regional military modernization. According to Turkish News World, citing a BBC report, interest in Turkish drones among Central Asian nations is growing, a trend that has sparked widespread discussion within the region. Simultaneously, U.S. foreign policy is undergoing a corresponding adjustment, with frequent high-level official visits aimed at expanding its influence in the area. The Diplomat magazine reported that the U.S. Secretary of State recently visited Central Asia and received a warm welcome from local governments.
  
  Analysts believe that through these diplomatic visits, the new U.S. administration can not only avoid certain international crises but also further consolidate its strategic foothold in Central Asia by strengthening bilateral relations.
  
  Although multiple sources confirm that Central Asia is in a critical period of military technology upgrades and the reshaping of diplomatic relations, media interpretations of future trends vary. Regarding the impact of Turkish drones, some perspectives emphasize their role as tools for upgrading regional security, while others express concern that this move could trigger a regional arms race or diplomatic friction. Furthermore, U.S. policy objectives toward Central Asia have been interpreted in various reports as either "strategic alliance" or "diplomatic hedging." Information yet to be confirmed includes the depth of cooperation between the U.S. and Central Asian nations regarding specific defense procurement, and the actual deployment effectiveness of Turkish drones in specific border conflicts; these details require further official data and field investigations for verification.

With the expansion of Kazakhstan’s economy, the country’s influence in regional supply chains has significantly increased. The introduction of Turkish drones has altered the defense budget structures of Central Asian nations, directing more resources toward high-tech defense industries. Additionally, the active visits by U.S. diplomats are expected to drive more Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) into Central Asia, particularly in the fields of energy development, infrastructure, and digital transformation. For neighboring countries that rely on Central Asian energy and logistics corridors, the policy direction of Central Asia amid the intertwined influence of the U.S., Turkey, and other powers will directly affect regional transport costs and the geopolitical security environment.
  
  However, scholars and think tanks caution against over-interpreting a "pro-Western" or "pro-Turkish" shift in Central Asia. Central Asian nations have long adopted a multilateral diplomatic approach aimed at balancing the interests of Russia, China, the United States, and Turkey. Limiting factors include the need for infrastructure upgrades in these countries and the persistent issue of internal economic inequality. Furthermore, the complexity of geopolitics means that any single military procurement or diplomatic declaration could trigger a chain reaction within the region. Therefore, observers suggest that current trends should be viewed as a means for Central Asian nations to maximize their national interests in a multipolar world, rather than a simple choice of sides.

At the same time, whether the role of Turkish drones in the regional security architecture will lead to potential conflicts with other major powers in the region will be a key indicator for monitoring Central Asian geopolitics over the next two years. Additionally, whether the new U.S. administration’s commitments to Central Asia will translate into long-term economic plans, rather than just diplomatic visits, will determine the sustainability of U.S. influence in the region. Stakeholders must continue to monitor the specific actions of the five Central Asian nations regarding energy export policies and defense autonomy strategies to assess the substantive evolution of the region’s status in the global trade system.

# 中亞地緣政治新局：土耳其無人機擴張與美國外交角力

> 中亞地區近期成為國際角力焦點，哈薩克經濟規模穩健成長，土耳其無人機技術輸出引發區域關注，同時美國新政府正透過外交訪問強化與中亞五國的戰略連結。


中亞位於歐亞大陸核心地帶，地理位置特殊，既是連線東西方的交通樞紐，也因接壤多個全球地緣政治熱點而備受國際矚目。該區域不僅蘊藏豐富的自然資源，更被視為全球經濟發展的新興潛力區。根據《德國馬歇爾基金會》（GMF）的分析，中亞五國在提供經濟機會的同時，長期面臨安全與區域穩定挑戰。
哈薩克作為區域內最大經濟體，其經濟表現指標性強，據《中亞時報》（The Times Of Central Asia）指出，哈薩克2025年國內生產毛額（GDP）已達約3060億美元，佔中亞五國總體經濟規模半數以上，顯示其在區域經濟整合中的核心地位。
近年來，中亞國家在國防裝備採購上出現顯著轉向，土耳其製無人機（UAV）成為區域軍事現代化的熱門選項。根據《土耳其新聞世界》（Turkish News World）引述BBC報導，中亞各國對土耳其無人機的興趣日益濃厚，這一趨勢在區域內引發廣泛討論。與此同時，美國的外交政策亦同步調整，透過高層官員頻繁互訪，試圖擴大在該地區的影響力。《外交家》（The Diplomat）雜誌報導指出，美國國務卿近期對中亞進行訪問，並獲得了當地政府的熱烈歡迎。
分析認為，透過外交出訪，美國新政府不僅能避開部分國際危機，更能藉由加強雙邊關係，進一步鞏固在中亞的戰略立足點。
儘管多方來源皆確認中亞正處於軍事技術升級與外交關係重塑的關鍵期，但各媒體對於未來走向的詮釋存在差異。關於土耳其無人機的影響，部分觀點強調其作為區域安保升級的工具，但亦有觀點擔憂此舉可能引發區域軍事競賽或外交摩擦。此外，美國對中亞的政策目標，在不同報導中被解讀為「戰略結盟」或「外交避險」。目前尚待確認的資訊包括：美國與中亞國家在具體國防採購上的合作深度，以及土耳其無人機在特定邊境衝突中的實際部署成效，這些細節仍需更多官方資料與實地調查佐證。
中亞地區的戰略轉向對全球貿易與能源供應鏈具有直接影響。隨著哈薩克經濟規模的擴張，該國在區域供應鏈中的話語權顯著提升。土耳其無人機的引入，則改變了中亞各國的國防預算結構，將更多資源導向高科技防禦產業。此外，美國外交官員的積極造訪，預期將帶動更多外商直接投資（FDI）進入中亞，特別是在能源開發、基礎建設與數位轉型領域。對於依賴中亞能源與物流通道的周邊國家而言，中亞在美、土等國影響力交織下的政策走向，將直接影響區域內的運輸成本與地緣安全環境。
然而，學界與智庫提醒，不應過度解讀中亞地區的「親西方」或「親土耳其」轉向。中亞各國長期採取多邊主義外交，旨在平衡俄羅斯、中國、美國與土耳其等多方勢力。限制因素在於，中亞各國的基礎設施仍有待升級，且區域內部的經濟發展不平衡問題依然存在。此外，地緣政治的複雜性意味著任何單一軍事採購或外交宣示，都可能在區域內引發連鎖反應。因此，觀察家建議，應將目前的趨勢視為中亞國家在多極化世界中尋求最大化國家利益的手段，而非單純的陣營選邊站。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於哈薩克等國如何在維持與傳統夥伴關係的同時，有效整合美國的技術與投資資源。同時，土耳其無人機在區域安保架構中扮演的角色，是否會導致與區域內其他大國的潛在矛盾，將是未來兩年內觀察中亞地緣政治的關鍵指標。此外，美國新政府對於中亞的承諾是否會轉化為長期的經濟計畫，而非僅止於外交訪問，將決定美國在該地區影響力的永續性。各界需持續關注中亞五國在能源出口政策與國防自主策略上的具體行動，以評估該地區在全球貿易體系中地位的實質演變。
