  # Global Sovereign Wealth Funds Shift to ESG Investing: Sustainable Development Becomes the New Paradigm in East Asian Financial Markets
  
  > Global sovereign wealth funds are making large-scale bets on ESG sustainable investing, with the proportion of asset owners adopting sustainable practices climbing from 53% in 2018 to 86% in 2022, indicating that ESG has become a core paradigm in the global financial market.
  
  Global climate change issues and energy transitions have become the strategic core for governments and large investment institutions worldwide. According to the latest survey by the London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG), the importance placed on Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) criteria in global financial markets is experiencing explosive growth. Historical data shows that in 2018, only 53% of global asset owners incorporated sustainable practices into their investment evaluations; however, by 2022, this figure had surged to 86%.
  
  This trend indicates that ESG is no longer merely a corporate social responsibility slogan, but a key indicator substantially influencing the allocation of over $13 trillion in global sovereign wealth funds. This is particularly evident in East Asia, where governments and financial institutions are actively adjusting their asset portfolios to meet the high standards of sustainable development required by international capital markets.
  
  Synthesizing various reports and market data, a high degree of consensus has been reached among global sovereign wealth funds regarding the ESG sector: "sustainable development" has become the new paradigm in the investment market. Governments and enterprises are increasingly directing capital toward initiatives that meet environmental protection, social responsibility, and corporate governance standards. This shift is not only based on moral appeals but also stems from considerations of long-term financial stability in the face of climate risks and energy transitions. In the East Asian market, as regional governments establish clear policies for net-zero emission targets, investors are gradually reducing their reliance on traditional fossil fuel industries and shifting toward renewable energy, green infrastructure, and low-carbon transition sectors.
  
  Despite the rising global consensus on ESG investing, there are slight differences in how various media and research institutions describe the "resistance to progress." Some reports point out that the wave of sustainable investment has not been entirely smooth and has faced shocks from multiple external factors. For instance, the outbreak of the Russia-Ukraine war in 2022 caused global energy prices to soar, forcing some countries to weigh energy security against decarbonization goals, even leading to a short-term resurgence in reliance on fossil fuels. Furthermore, shifts in international political landscapes, such as the attitude of former U.S. President Donald Trump toward environmental policy, have also become sources of uncertainty affecting the flow of global ESG capital.
  
  What remains to be observed is how East Asian countries, given their structural high reliance on imported energy, will balance energy costs with the long-term returns of ESG investments; this remains a focal point of market attention.
  
  For the East Asian region, this global ESG investment wave has profound implications. First, enterprises in the region that fail to meet international ESG standards will face the risk of rising capital costs and the withdrawal of foreign investment; conversely, companies actively transitioning to green energy can gain favor from sovereign wealth funds and secure more competitive financing terms. Second, policymakers in various countries are accelerating the promotion of green finance frameworks to attract international capital. However, this shift also places immense pressure on traditional energy industrial chains, making the balance between maintaining stable energy supplies and promoting industrial upgrading an urgent policy challenge for governments.
  
  Nevertheless, market analysts caution that investor enthusiasm for ESG should not be over-interpreted. Although data shows a significant increase in mainstream investor attention toward ESG, the complexity of ESG rating standards and regulatory differences across countries remain major limitations for investors. Some perspectives suggest that ESG investing is currently in a standardization process, and over-reliance on a single indicator may overlook the specificities of regional markets. Additionally, global geopolitical volatility, such as the suddenness of energy security issues, could disrupt the rhythm of sustainable investment at any time. Therefore, when evaluating ESG assets, investors must consider the linkage between the global macroeconomic environment and regional energy policies, avoiding the view of ESG as a guarantee of profit.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on whether, after 2026, global sovereign wealth funds will continue to expand their ESG asset allocations in the face of inflationary pressures and energy price volatility, or shift toward more conservative investment strategies. In particular, it remains to be seen whether financial regulators in East Asia will further unify ESG information disclosure standards to reduce the risk of "greenwashing." Furthermore, as extreme weather events caused by climate change occur more frequently, whether the speed of energy transition will accelerate due to climate adaptation needs will be a key indicator for the global financial market and energy industry in the coming years.

# 全球13兆美元主權基金轉向ESG投資 永續發展成東亞金融市場新顯學

> 全球主權財富基金正大規模押注ESG永續投資，資產所有人採行永續實踐比例從2018年的53%攀升至2022年的86%，顯示ESG已成為全球金融市場的核心顯學。


全球氣候變遷議題與能源轉型已成為各國政府與大型投資機構的戰略核心。根據倫敦證券交易所集團（LSEG）的最新調查顯示，全球金融市場對於環境、社會與公司治理（ESG）的重視程度正呈現爆發性成長。從歷史資料觀察，2018年時全球僅有53%的資產所有人將永續實踐納入投資評估範圍，然而至2022年，該比例已大幅躍升至86%。
這項趨勢顯示，ESG不再僅是企業的社會責任口號，而是實質影響全球超過13兆美元主權基金配置的關鍵指標，特別是在東亞地區，各國政府與金融機構正積極調整資產組合，以因應國際資本市場對於永續發展的高標準要求。
綜合各方報導與市場資料，全球主權基金在ESG領域的佈局已達成高度共識，即「永續發展」已成為投資市場的新顯學。各國政府與企業紛紛將資金導向符合環境保護、社會責任與公司治理標準的倡議專案。這種轉變不僅是基於道德訴求，更源於對氣候風險與能源轉型所帶來的長期財務穩定性考量。在東亞市場，隨著區域內各國政府針對淨零排放目標制定明確政策，投資人對於傳統化石燃料產業的依賴度正逐漸降低，轉而投入再生能源、綠色基建及低碳轉型產業。
儘管全球對於ESG投資的共識度提升，但各媒體與研究機構對於「推動阻力」的描述存在些許差異。部分報導指出，永續投資浪潮並非一帆風順，曾面臨多重外部因素的衝擊。例如，2022年爆發的俄烏戰爭導致全球能源價格飆升，迫使部分國家在能源安全與減碳目標之間進行權衡，甚至出現短期內重新依賴化石燃料的現象。此外，國際政治局勢的變動，如美國前總統川普對於環境政策的態度，也成為影響全球ESG資金流向的不確定因素。
目前尚待觀察的是，在東亞各國高度依賴進口能源的結構下，如何平衡能源成本與ESG投資的長期回報，仍是市場關注的焦點。
對於東亞地區而言，這波全球性的ESG投資潮具有深遠的影響。首先，區域內的企業若無法符合國際ESG標準，將面臨資本成本上升及外資撤離的風險；反之，積極轉型綠能的企業則能獲得主權基金的青睞，取得更具競爭力的融資條件。其次，各國政策制定者正加速推動綠色金融框架，以吸引國際資金進駐。然而，這種轉變也對傳統能源產業鏈造成巨大壓力，如何在維持能源供應穩定與推動產業升級之間取得平衡，成為各國政府刻不容緩的政策挑戰。
然而，市場分析師提醒，投資人對於ESG的熱度不應過度解讀。儘管資料顯示主流投資者對ESG的關注度大幅提升，但ESG評級標準的複雜性與各國監管差異，仍是投資人面臨的主要限制。部分觀點認為，ESG投資目前仍處於標準化過程，若過度依賴單一指標，恐忽略了區域性市場的特殊性。此外，全球地緣政治的動盪，如能源安全問題的突發性，可能隨時打斷永續投資的節奏。因此，投資人在評估ESG資產時，必須考量全球總體經濟環境與區域能源政策的連動性，避免將ESG視為獲利的保證。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於2026年後，全球主權基金在面對通膨壓力與能源價格波動時，是否會持續擴大ESG資產配置，或是轉向更為保守的投資策略。特別是東亞地區的金融監管機構，是否會進一步統一ESG資訊揭露標準，以減少漂綠（Greenwashing）風險。此外，隨著氣候變遷帶來的極端天氣事件頻發，能源轉型的速度是否會因氣候調適需求而加快，將是未來幾年全球金融市場與能源產業觀察的關鍵指標。
