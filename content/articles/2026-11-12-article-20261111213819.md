  > Canada has announced the reinstatement of sanctions on 11 Iranian entities, covering the energy and financial sectors. Simultaneously, Europe is grappling with the "Iran threat" narrative and internal political crises, while the EU has recently expanded its own sanctions, significantly heightening regional geopolitical security risks.
  
  Canadian Minister of Foreign Affairs Anita Anand officially announced on November 10, 2026, that the Canadian government has decided to reinstate sanctions on 11 Iranian institutions. This policy shift marks a significant change in Canada's diplomatic stance toward Iran; these entities had previously been removed from the sanctions list in 2016 following progress made by Iran in fulfilling the nuclear agreement.
  
  The entities now subject to renewed restrictions include the National Iranian Oil Company, the National Iranian Tanker Company, and two nuclear fuel enterprises, Kala Naft and Naftiran Intertrad, among other key economic entities. Canadian officials explicitly stated that these organizations are involved in assisting Iran with sensitive nuclear activities, ballistic missile programs, or providing financial support for its malicious activities.
  
  Against the backdrop of synchronized international sanctions, the European Union adopted its 22nd sanctions package on October 12, 2026, involving nearly 1,600 individuals and entities—the largest expansion of EU sanctions since 2022. Kaja Kallas, the EU High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy, emphasized at the time that the move was intended to address increasingly complex regional security challenges. However, the European region is currently facing multiple pressures; beyond external security threats, some member states are also mired in internal political turmoil.
  
  According to reports, the Italian government has failed to form a cabinet three months after the conclusion of elections. It is rumored that the designated Prime Minister, Carlo Cottarelli, may abandon efforts to form a technocratic government, leading to volatility in local financial markets and continued political uncertainty.
  
  Regarding the relationship between Iran and Europe, there is currently a divergence in information and rising tension. Online community discussions have pointed out that Tehran has publicly named Italy and Germany, claiming that if the United States uses NATO bases for future military operations, these European nations would become targets for missile attacks. However, European officials remain cautious, with many publicly stating that there is currently no concrete evidence that Iran is targeting the European continent directly. The gap between this "threat narrative" and the "lack of evidence" reflects the dual challenge of psychological defense and practical intelligence assessment currently facing the European region in responding to geopolitical conflicts.
  
  Such geopolitical shifts have had a direct impact on global energy markets and European industrial policy. Canada's reinstatement of sanctions on Iran's state-owned oil and tanker companies is expected to further compress Iran's energy export capacity, exacerbating tensions in the global energy supply chain. For Europe, under the dual pressure of energy transition and security guarantees, any escalation in the Iranian threat would force EU member states to re-evaluate their defense budgets and the priorities of their NATO military cooperation. Meanwhile, political instability in countries like Italy could weaken the EU's collective bargaining power in addressing the Iranian nuclear issue and regional security topics, leading to more internal obstacles for the EU in formulating a consistent foreign policy.
  
  Regarding the current situation, experts and policy observers caution against over-interpreting threat rhetoric from single sources. Although the confrontational stance between Iran and Western nations is evident, equating "named threats" directly with "preparations for military action" still lacks substantive intelligence support. Furthermore, while the EU's sanctions are large in scale, their actual suppressive effect on the Iranian economy remains limited by the supply and demand structure of the global energy market. For European citizens and businesses, the core risk at present lies in market volatility caused by political uncertainty, rather than an immediate military conflict. All parties should closely monitor subsequent EU defense and security meetings, as well as the impact of Italy's cabinet-formation process on the overall stability of the EU.

As uncertainty in the global security environment increases, how nations balance domestic political crises with external security defense will become a key indicator of future regional stability.

# 加拿大重啟伊朗制裁及歐盟局勢動盪：地緣政治風險升溫

> 加拿大宣佈重啟對11家伊朗機構的制裁，涵蓋能源與金融領域。與此同時，歐洲面臨伊朗威脅論與內部政治危機，歐盟近期亦擴大對外製裁規模，地緣安全域性勢顯著升溫。


加拿大外交部長安妮塔．阿南德（Anita Anand）於2026年11月10日正式宣佈，加拿大政府決定恢復對11家伊朗機構的制裁措施。這項政策調整標誌著加拿大對伊朗外交立場的重大轉變，此前這些機構曾因伊朗在核協議履行上取得進展，於2016年被移出制裁名單。
此次重新列管的物件包括伊朗國家石油公司（National Iranian Oil Company）、伊朗國家油輪公司（National Iranian Tanker Company），以及兩家核燃料企業、Kala Naft與Naftiran Intertrad等關鍵經濟實體。加拿大官方明確指出，這些機構涉及協助伊朗進行敏感核活動、彈道飛彈計畫，或為其惡意活動提供資金支援。
在國際制裁行動同步推進的背景下，歐盟於2026年10月12日透過了第22輪制裁方案，涉及近1,600名個人與實體，這是自2022年以來歐盟規模最大的一次制裁擴張。歐盟外交與安全政策高階代表卡拉斯（Kaja Kallas）當時強調，此舉旨在應對日益複雜的區域安全挑戰。然而，歐洲地區目前正面臨多重壓力，除了外部安全威脅，部分成員國亦陷入內部政治動盪。
根據相關報導，義大利政府在選舉結束三個月後仍未能順利組成內閣，總理指定人選卡洛．科塔雷利（Carlo Cottarelli）傳出可能放棄組建技術官僚政府，導致當地金融市場受到波動，政局不確定性持續加劇。
針對伊朗與歐洲的關係，目前出現了資訊上的分歧與緊張局勢。有網路社群討論指出，德黑蘭方面曾公開點名義大利與德國，稱若美國在未來的軍事行動中使用北約（NATO）基地，這些歐洲國家將成為飛彈攻擊目標。然而，歐洲官方對此持保留態度，多名歐洲官員公開表示，目前並無確切證據顯示伊朗正將歐洲大陸列為直接打擊目標。這種「威脅論」與「無證據說」之間的落差，反映出當前歐洲地區在應對地緣衝突時，面臨著心理防禦與實際情報判斷的雙重挑戰。
此類地緣政治變動對全球能源市場與歐洲產業政策產生了直接影響。加拿大恢復對伊朗國營石油與油輪公司的制裁，預計將進一步壓縮伊朗的能源出口空間，加劇全球能源供應鏈的緊張情緒。對於歐洲而言，在能源轉型與安全保障的雙重壓力下，若伊朗威脅升級，將迫使歐盟成員國重新評估其國防預算與北約軍事合作的優先順序。義大利等國的政局不穩，則可能削弱歐盟在處理伊朗核問題與區域安全議題上的集體談判力，導致歐盟在制定一致性外交政策時面臨更多內部阻礙。
針對目前的局勢，專家與政策觀察家提醒，不應過度解讀單一來源的威脅言論。儘管伊朗與西方國家的對抗態勢明顯，但將「點名威脅」直接等同於「軍事行動準備」仍缺乏實質情報支撐。此外，歐盟的制裁行動雖然規模宏大，但其對伊朗經濟的實際壓制效果，仍受限於全球能源市場的供需結構。對於歐洲民眾與企業而言，當前的核心風險在於政治不確定性帶來的市場波動，而非立即性的軍事衝突。各界應密切關注歐盟後續的國防安全會議，以及義大利組閣程式對歐盟整體穩定性的影響。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於三個層面：首先是加拿大重啟制裁後，其他西方盟國是否會跟進加強對伊朗能源與金融實體的管制；其次是歐洲各國針對伊朗威脅論的具體外交回應，特別是德國與義大利在北約框架下的防禦立場；最後則是義大利政府組建的進展，這將直接決定歐盟在未來數月內，能否針對中東局勢採取統一且強硬的對外政策。隨著全球安全環境的不確定性增加，各國如何平衡國內政治危機與外部安全防禦，將成為未來區域穩定與否的關鍵指標。
