  # EU Defense Autonomy and the Shift in China Policy: Geopolitical Challenges Facing Europe
  
  > European Council President António Costa has called for strengthening EU defense, while the German governing coalition is reshaping its China policy, signaling that Europe is entering a major transitional period in regional security and global economic strategy.
  
  Europe is currently facing a complex geopolitical situation. From the enhancement of regional defense capabilities to adjustments in foreign trade policy, these developments reflect the European Union's attempt to seek strategic autonomy within a volatile international environment. According to recent public information, senior EU officials have clearly stated that member states must strengthen defense cooperation to address increasingly severe security challenges. At the same time, Germany, as the core economy of the EU, has seen a structural shift in its China policy, moving from a relatively relaxed model of cooperation toward more cautious risk management, marking a reassessment of global supply chain dependencies and national security within the EU.
  
  Multiple media reports have confirmed that the EU is currently at a critical juncture for policy adjustment. European Council President António Costa recently called on member states at an EU Council meeting in Belgium to take substantive action to strengthen the EU's overall defense capabilities. This stance has been supported by European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen, who, in interactions with regional leaders such as Serbian President Aleksandar Vučić, emphasized the EU's determination to maintain regional stability.
  
  Furthermore, the German media outlet *Handelsblatt* pointed out that Germany and France are working together to promote the establishment of new EU counter-measures. This is not merely a reaction to specific economic and trade issues, but also indicates that the German governing coalition has become alert to its long-standing naive attitude toward China, officially declaring that Berlin's China policy has entered a phase of "major change."
  
  However, there remain different interpretations and observations regarding the specific details and implementation strength of the EU's policy shift. Although Germany has adopted a tougher stance, significant differences persist within the EU regarding the specific scope of application for "counter-measures" and the willingness of various countries to invest resources in defense autonomy. Some sources indicate that concerns within European society regarding political trends are not limited to foreign policy, but also include internal structural social divides.
  
  For example, the experience following Brexit shows that the urban-rural divide and generational fragmentation are core factors leading to political instability. Whether these changes in social structure will affect the implementation of defense and foreign policies by EU countries remains a focus of discussion among scholars and policymakers, with no consensus reached yet.
  
  These policy changes have profound implications for Europe. At the industrial level, the policy shift jointly pushed by Germany and France aims to reduce over-reliance on a single market, which will force multinational corporations to reassess their supply chain layouts in Europe and may lead to increased operating costs. At the social level, the increase in defense budgets is often accompanied by controversies over the crowding-out effect on public spending, especially against the backdrop of inflation and economic weakness in many European countries. Balancing defense spending with public welfare will become a major public issue for governments. Additionally, the defense autonomy capabilities the EU seeks to build will directly affect its interaction models with NATO and other international partners.
  
  Although senior EU officials have released strong policy signals, analysts warn against over-interpreting the immediate effects of these measures. First, there are significant administrative and political obstacles between establishing counter-measures and actually achieving defense integration. Second, European countries are still constrained by their respective domestic economic interests when dealing with China and other major powers, and there remains room for compromise during the policy implementation process. Therefore, viewing these policy adjustments as a one-way "confrontation" may be an oversimplification; in fact, it is more like a complex game between safeguarding economic interests and ensuring national security.
  
  Subsequent observations should focus on the specific commitments made by EU member states regarding defense budget allocations in upcoming Council meetings, as well as how the German governing coalition handles backlash from the domestic industrial sector while implementing its China policy shift. Furthermore, whether concerns within European society regarding the rise of far-right forces will further affect the stability of various governments in promoting EU integration policies is also an indicator worth tracking. As the international situation changes rapidly, how Europe strikes a balance between maintaining economic openness and strengthening security defenses will determine the EU's strategic position in the coming years.

# 歐盟防務自主與對華政策轉向：歐洲面臨的地緣政治挑戰

> 歐洲理事會主席科斯塔呼籲強化歐盟防務，同時德國執政聯盟正重塑對華政策，顯示歐洲在區域安全與全球經濟戰略上正進入重大轉型期。


歐洲近期面臨複雜的地緣政治局勢，從區域防務能力的提升到對外貿易政策的調整，皆反映出歐盟正試圖在動盪的國際環境中尋求戰略自主。根據近期公開資訊，歐盟高層官員已明確表態，要求成員國加強防務合作，以應對日益嚴峻的安全挑戰。與此同時，德國作為歐盟核心經濟體，其對華政策亦出現了結構性的轉變，從過去相對寬鬆的合作模式，轉向更為謹慎的風險控管，這標誌著歐盟內部對於全球供應鏈依賴與國家安全的重新審視。
多方媒體報導共同確認，歐盟目前正處於政策調整的關鍵節點。歐洲理事會主席科斯塔（António Costa）近期在比利時召開的歐盟理事會會議上，公開呼籲成員國必須採取實質行動，加強歐盟整體的防務能力。此一主張獲得了歐盟委員會主席範德賴恩（Ursula von der Leyen）的支援，並在與塞爾維亞總統武契奇（Aleksandar Vučić）等區域領導人的互動中，強調了歐盟在維護區域穩定上的決心。
此外，德國媒體《商報》亦指出，德法兩國正聯手推動歐盟設立新的反制工具，這不僅是針對特定經貿問題的反應，更顯示德國執政聯盟對於長期以來的對華政策天真態度已感到警覺，正式宣告柏林對華政策進入了「重大改變」的階段。
然而，關於歐盟政策轉向的具體細節與執行力道，各界仍存在不同的解讀與觀察。雖然德國採取了更為強硬的立場，但歐盟內部對於「反制工具」的具體應用範圍，以及在防務自主上各國投入資源的意願，仍存在顯著差異。部分來源指出，歐洲社會內部對於政治走向的擔憂，不僅限於對外政策，還包括內部結構性的社會鴻溝。
例如，英國脫歐後的經驗顯示，城鄉差距與世代斷裂是導致政治動盪的核心因素，這些社會結構變遷是否會影響歐盟各國在防務與外交政策上的執行力，目前仍是學界與政策制定者討論的焦點，尚無定論。
這些政策變動對歐洲的具體影響深遠。在產業層面，德法聯手的政策轉向意在降低對單一市場的過度依賴，這將迫使跨國企業重新評估其在歐洲的供應鏈佈局，並可能導致營運成本上升。在社會層面，防務預算的增加往往伴隨著公共支出排擠效應的爭議，尤其是在歐洲多國面臨通貨膨脹與經濟疲軟的背景下，如何平衡國防開支與民生福利，將成為各國政府面臨的重大公共議題。此外，歐盟試圖建立的防務自主能力，也將直接影響其與北約（NATO）及其他國際夥伴的互動模式。
儘管歐盟高層釋出了強硬的政策訊號，但分析家提醒，不應過度解讀這些措施的立即效果。首先，設立反制工具與實際達成防務一體化之間，存在巨大的行政與政治障礙；其次，歐洲各國在處理與中國及其他大國關係時，仍受到各自國內經濟利益的限制，政策執行過程中的妥協空間依然存在。因此，將這些政策調整視為單一方向的「對抗」可能過於簡化，事實上，這更像是一場在維護經濟利益與保障國家安全之間的複雜博弈。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於歐盟各成員國在接下來的理事會會議中，對於防務預算分配的具體承諾，以及德國執政聯盟在落實對華政策轉向時，如何處理國內產業界的反彈。此外，歐洲社會對於極右翼勢力興起的擔憂，是否會進一步影響各國政府在推動歐盟整合政策時的穩定性，亦是值得持續追蹤的指標。隨著國際局勢的快速變化，歐洲如何在保持經濟開放與強化安全防衛之間取得平衡，將決定未來數年歐盟的戰略地位。
