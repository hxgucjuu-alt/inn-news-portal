  # Trend Analysis: Global AI Arms Race Exacerbates Inflation Risks and Cybersecurity Defense Demands
  
  > The global investment boom in artificial intelligence infrastructure continues to drive economic growth, but it has also triggered inflation warnings and cybersecurity threats. Enterprises are strengthening their defenses through AI technology, while global stakeholders are exploring the sustainable balance between industrial development and resource allocation.
  
  The global technology industry is at a critical turning point in the development of artificial intelligence (AI). Large-scale infrastructure investment has not only become a core engine for economic growth but has also brought about complex chain reactions. According to an analysis report by the Congressional Research Service, massive capital expenditures in the AI sector contributed 1 percentage point to U.S. economic growth in the second quarter of 2025, followed by a 0.7 percentage point contribution in the first quarter of 2026. However, as global trade in AI-related products surged by 65% in the first half of 2025 compared to the same period the previous year, the side effects of this "AI arms race" have gradually emerged, particularly regarding the potential impact on macroeconomic inflationary pressures.
  
  Meanwhile, with the proliferation of AI technology, cyberattack methods have evolved into an "AI agent" model, targeting software vulnerabilities such as PHP2Shell, forcing enterprises to adopt more aggressive automated cybersecurity defense strategies.
  
  Multiple sources have confirmed that the rapid expansion of AI technology has evolved from simple research and development into a global competition for industrial resources. A research report by Goldman Sachs has explicitly warned that the wave of AI capital expenditure could trigger supply-demand imbalances in economic transmission mechanisms, estimating that related inflationary pressures could push inflation up by 0.5 percentage points. In the field of cybersecurity, Honling and Cheng Yun Technology, subsidiaries of Nenglu Innovation, have confirmed that in the face of AI-driven cyberattack crises, enterprises can only effectively address increasingly severe defense challenges by adopting an "AI vs. AI" strategy, combined with zero-trust endpoint management and AI-assisted security platforms (AI SOC).
  
  Furthermore, regarding energy and land use, how to balance industrial development with food security has become an issue that cannot be ignored in global technology and industrial policies. Scientific land classification and benefit-sharing mechanisms are seen as necessary means to achieve sustainable development.
  
  Although there is a preliminary consensus on the economic and security impacts brought by AI, differences remain regarding the assessment of its long-term risks. Goldman Sachs' warning focuses on macroeconomic inflation and overheating capital expenditure, while the technology industry's focus is on real-time cybersecurity threat protection and channel integration. Additionally, information regarding specific regions (such as Basking Ridge, New Jersey, in the United States) indicates that the operation of local commercial and social infrastructure still maintains traditional models, forming a sharp contrast with the rapid iteration of the global high-tech industry.
  
  What remains to be confirmed is whether the inflationary pressure brought by AI investment will automatically ease as technology matures, and whether the global supply chain will experience deeper resource crowding-out effects when facing the surge in demand for AI hardware.
  
  For relevant industries and policies, the direct impact of the AI arms race lies in the forced upgrade of corporate cybersecurity budgets. The collaboration case between Honling and Cheng Yun Technology shows that enterprises with vast channels and technical R&D capabilities are attempting to help small and medium-sized enterprises build defense networks through integrated solutions. This is not just a technical upgrade, but a manifestation of industrial structural adjustment. In emerging markets such as South America, although they are not directly participating in the central nodes of AI infrastructure, the energy demand and raw material price fluctuations driven by global AI will indirectly affect local rural economies and land use policies.
  
  How to combine the photovoltaic industry with rural revitalization, and through scientific land management, ensure that food security is not sacrificed while pursuing technological progress, has become a common challenge that governments must face.
  
  However, academics and market analysts remind that the interpretation of AI's economic contribution should not be overly optimistic. Although data shows that AI investment can significantly boost economic growth, the risk of diminishing marginal effects caused by over-investment cannot be ignored. Furthermore, while the "AI vs. AI" strategy for cybersecurity defense can alleviate the current crisis, as the technology of attackers evolves, defenders may fall into an endless arms race, which poses a heavy burden on the long-term operating costs of enterprises.
  
  Therefore, policymakers must consider social adaptability after the popularization of technology when promoting the development of the AI industry, avoiding the neglect of the transformation needs of traditional industries and rural areas due to the digital divide or the excessive concentration of resources in the high-tech industry.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on the following three aspects: First, whether the 0.5 percentage point inflationary pressure warned by Goldman Sachs will become a reality in the second half of 2026, prompting central banks of various countries to adjust monetary policies; second, the penetration rate of global AI cybersecurity defense platforms and whether they can effectively reduce losses caused by vulnerabilities such as PHP2Shell; and finally, the policy balance between green energy demand and rural development in various countries, especially whether the scientific classification standards for the transformation of low-yield agricultural land into photovoltaic use can become a reference model for promoting sustainable technological development globally. These indicators will determine whether the AI industry will continue to serve as an economic engine in the coming years or evolve into a source of risk that triggers market volatility.

# 全球AI軍備競賽加劇通膨風險與資安防禦需求之趨勢分析

> 全球人工智慧基礎建設投資熱潮持續推升經濟成長，但也引發通膨預警與資安威脅。企業正透過AI技術強化防禦，同時全球各界亦在探討產業與資源分配的永續平衡。


全球科技產業正處於人工智慧（AI）發展的關鍵轉折期，大規模的基礎建設投資不僅成為推動經濟成長的核心引擎，亦帶來了複雜的連鎖效應。根據美國國會研究處的分析報告，AI領域的龐大資本支出在2025年第二季為美國經濟成長貢獻了1個百分點，隨後在2026年第一季亦貢獻了0.7個百分點。然而，隨著全球AI相關產品貿易額在2025年上半年較前一年同期大幅攀升65%，這波「AI軍備競賽」的副作用也逐漸浮現，特別是對於總體經濟通膨壓力的潛在影響。
與此同時，隨著AI技術的普及，網路攻擊手法亦進化為「AI代理人」模式，針對如PHP2Shell等軟體漏洞進行攻擊，迫使企業必須採取更為激進的自動化資安防禦策略。
多方來源共同確認的是，AI技術的快速擴張已從單純的技術研發演變為全球性的產業資源競爭。高盛（Goldman Sachs）的研究報告明確示警，AI資本支出浪潮可能引發經濟傳導機制中的供需失衡，預估相關通膨壓力恐將推高0.5個百分點。在資安領域，能率創新旗下宏羚與誠雲科技已證實，面對AI驅動的網路攻擊危機，企業唯有透過「AI對抗AI」的策略，結合零信任端點管理與AI輔助資安平臺（AI SOC），方能有效應對日益嚴峻的防禦挑戰。
此外，在能源與土地利用方面，如何平衡產業發展與糧食安全，已成為全球科技與產業政策中不可忽視的議題，科學化的土地分級與利益回饋機制被視為達成永續發展的必要手段。
儘管各界對於AI帶來的經濟與安全影響已有初步共識，但針對其長期風險的評估仍存在差異。高盛的預警側重於總體經濟層面的通膨與資本支出過熱，而科技產業的焦點則集中於即時的資安威脅防護與通路整合。此外，部分針對特定地區（如美國紐澤西州巴斯金裡奇地區）的資訊顯示，當地商業與社會基礎設施的運作仍維持傳統模式，與全球高科技產業的快速迭代形成強烈對比。
目前尚待確認之處在於，AI投資帶來的通膨壓力是否會隨技術成熟而自動緩解，以及全球供應鏈在面對AI硬體需求暴增時，是否會產生更深層的資源排擠效應。
對於相關產業與政策而言，AI軍備競賽的直接影響在於企業資安預算的被迫升級。宏羚與誠雲科技的合作案例顯示，擁有龐大通路與技術研發能力的企業，正試圖透過整合性方案協助中小企業建構防禦網。這不僅是技術升級，更是產業結構調整的體現。在南美洲等新興市場，雖然未直接參與AI基礎建設的中心節點，但全球AI帶動的能源需求與原物料價格波動，將間接影響當地的農村經濟與土地利用政策。
如何將光電產業與農村再生結合，透過科學化的土地管理，確保在追求科技進步的同時不犧牲糧食安全，已成為各國政府必須面對的共同挑戰。
然而，學界與市場分析師提醒，對於AI經濟貢獻的解讀不應過度樂觀。儘管資料顯示AI投資能顯著拉抬經濟成長，但過度投資（Over-investment）帶來的邊際效應遞減風險不容忽視。此外，資安防禦的「AI對抗AI」策略雖能緩解當前危機，但隨著攻擊者技術的演進，防禦方恐陷入永無止境的軍備競賽，這對企業的長期營運成本構成沉重負擔。
因此，政策制定者在推動AI產業發展時，必須考量技術普及後的社會適應性，避免因數位落差或資源過度集中於高科技產業，而忽略了傳統產業與農村地區的轉型需求。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個面向：首先，高盛所預警的0.5個百分點通膨壓力是否會在2026年下半年成為現實，並促使各國央行調整貨幣政策；其次，全球AI資安防禦平臺的普及率，以及是否能有效降低企業因PHP2Shell等漏洞造成的損失；最後，各國在綠電需求與農村發展間的政策平衡，特別是針對低產值農地轉型光電的科學化分級標準，是否能成為全球推動永續科技發展的參考範本。這些指標將決定AI產業在未來幾年是持續作為經濟引擎，還是演變為引發市場動盪的風險源。
