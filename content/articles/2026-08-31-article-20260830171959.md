  # EU Pushes for Mercosur Free Trade Agreement; Divergent Stances Between Germany and France Draw Attention
  
  > The European Union is currently undergoing the voting process for a free trade agreement with Mercosur, which requires support from the European Parliament and a majority of member states. However, differing attitudes between France and Germany have sparked intense debate within the EU regarding trade policy and agricultural protection.
  
  The European Union is actively pushing for a free trade agreement with Mercosur, aiming to deepen economic and trade ties between the EU and major South American economies. According to the relevant procedures, the agreement must be submitted to the 27 EU member states for a vote. The threshold for passage is stringent, requiring not only formal approval from the European Parliament but also the consent of 15 member states representing at least 65% of the total EU population. Since negotiations began, this free trade agreement has remained at the heart of internal EU political maneuvering due to sensitive issues involving environmental standards, agricultural subsidies, and market access.
  
  According to various media reports, the EU has confirmed that it is facing significant political obstacles in advancing the agreement, primarily due to the divergent stances of Germany and France. The German government, based on its export-oriented economic structure, tends to support the agreement, believing it will help the EU maintain competitiveness in global supply chains and open up emerging markets. Conversely, the French government has long maintained a reserved or even strongly opposed stance, primarily fearing that a surge in imports of low-cost South American agricultural products—such as beef and grains—will impact domestic French agriculture. France also criticizes the agreement for insufficient regulations regarding environmental protection and climate change commitments, arguing it fails to align with the standards of the EU's internal Green Deal.
  
  Although the difference in positions between Germany and France regarding the progress of the agreement is a matter of public record, there are varying interpretations as to whether it will ultimately pass. Some media outlets point out that France's hardline stance could trigger a chain reaction, leading other member states sensitive to agricultural protection to follow suit, thereby increasing the difficulty of reaching the "65% population threshold." Furthermore, details regarding the enforcement mechanisms for environmental regulations within the agreement remain to be clarified between the European Commission and national representatives. It remains to be seen whether the EU will include additional compensation clauses or supplementary environmental protection agreements to appease France and other major agricultural nations, as these variables could influence the final vote.
  
  Whether this agreement proceeds will have a profound impact on the EU's agricultural policy, trade structure, and geopolitics. For the European agricultural sector, if the agreement takes effect, it will face direct competition from low-cost producers in South America, forcing the EU to re-evaluate the subsidy mechanisms of its Common Agricultural Policy (CAP). On the other hand, for industrial powerhouses like Germany, this agreement is key to expanding exports of automobiles, machinery, and other products to the South American market. If the agreement is shelved due to political factors, it will be seen as a symbol of rising trade protectionism within the EU. Moreover, this reflects the ongoing difficulty the EU faces in striking a perfect balance between pursuing globalized trade and maintaining internal industrial protection.
  
  Views on this issue remain clearly divided. Supporters argue that signing a free trade agreement with Mercosur is a necessary means for the EU to achieve market diversification and reduce reliance on specific supply chains. Opponents emphasize that the EU should not sacrifice the livelihoods of farmers and environmental standards in exchange for trade volume. Analysts caution against over-interpreting the opposition of a single country, as the EU's decision-making process often involves complex compromises and negotiations, and the final version may differ significantly from the current draft. Additionally, public sentiment regarding price fluctuations and employment shocks caused by globalization also serves as a critical factor for national governments to consider when voting.
  
  Future observations will focus on how the European Commission coordinates the differences between Germany and France, as well as the voting intentions within the European Parliament. As the voting schedule approaches, domestic political pressure on member states will continue to rise. In particular, how the French government balances protests from its agricultural groups with the EU's overall trade goals will be a key point to watch. Furthermore, the extent to which South American countries accept the additional environmental requirements proposed by the EU will directly affect the final direction of the negotiations. INN will continue to track the progress of the agreement's deliberation and monitor whether the EU proposes specific compromise plans regarding agricultural subsidies or environmental regulations.

# 歐盟推動南方共同市場自貿協定 德法立場分歧引關注

> 歐盟正針對與南方共同市場的自貿協定進行表決程式，該協定需獲歐洲議會及過半會員國支援。然而，法國與德國對此協定持不同態度，引發歐盟內部對於貿易政策與農業保護的激烈辯論。


歐盟近期正積極推動與南方共同市場（Mercosur）之間的自由貿易協定，此項協定旨在深化歐盟與南美洲主要經濟體之間的經貿連結。根據相關程式，該協定必須提交予擁有27個會員國的歐盟進行表決，其透過門檻相當嚴格，不僅需要歐洲議會（European Parliament）的正式批准，還必須獲得代表至少歐盟總人口65%的15個會員國同意。這項自貿協定自談判以來，便因涉及環境標準、農業補貼及市場准入等敏感議題，長期處於歐盟內部的政治角力核心。
綜合多家媒體報導，目前針對該協定的推進，歐盟內部已確認面臨顯著的政治阻礙，核心在於德法兩國的態度分歧。德國政府基於出口導向的經濟結構，傾向支援推動此項自貿協定，認為這有助於歐盟在全球供應鏈中維持競爭力並開拓新興市場。然而，法國政府則長期對此持保留甚至強烈反對立場，主要擔憂南美洲廉價農產品（如牛肉、穀物）大量進口將衝擊法國本土農業，並批評該協定在環境保護與氣候變遷承諾上的規範不足，未能與歐盟內部的綠色協議標準完全接軌。
儘管德法兩國在推動進度上的立場差異已成公開事實，但關於協定最終能否順利過關，各界仍存在不同解讀。部分媒體指出，法國的強硬態度可能引發連鎖反應，導致其他對農業保護敏感的會員國跟進反對，進而影響達到「65%人口門檻」的難度。此外，協定中關於環境監管的執行機制，目前在歐盟委員會與各國代表間仍有細節待釐清。目前尚待確認之處在於，歐盟是否會為了安撫法國及其他農業大國，而在協定內容中加入額外的補償條款或環境保護附加協議，這些變數均可能影響最終的投票結果。
此項協定的推進與否，將對歐盟的農業政策、貿易結構及地緣政治影響深遠。對於歐洲農業產業而言，若協定生效，將面臨來自南美洲低成本生產者的直接競爭，這迫使歐盟必須重新評估其共同農業政策（CAP）的補貼機制。另一方面，對於德國等工業強國而言，此協定是擴大汽車、機械等產品出口至南美市場的關鍵，若協定因政治因素擱置，將被視為歐盟貿易保護主義抬頭的象徵。此外，這也反映出歐盟在追求全球化貿易與維護內部產業保護之間，始終難以取得完美的平衡。
針對此議題，各界觀點呈現明顯分歧。支持者認為，與南方共同市場簽署自貿協定是歐盟實現市場多元化、降低對特定供應鏈依賴的必要手段；反對者則強調，歐盟不應以犧牲農民生計與環境標準為代價來換取貿易額。分析人士提醒，不應過度解讀單一國家的反對立場，因為歐盟內部的決策過程往往涉及複雜的妥協與談判，最終版本可能與現行草案存在顯著差異。此外，民眾對於全球化帶來的物價波動與就業衝擊亦有不同感受，這些社會情緒也成為各國政府在表決時的重要考量因素。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於歐盟委員會如何協調德法兩國的歧見，以及歐洲議會內部的投票意向。隨著表決時程逼近，各會員國的國內政治壓力將持續升高，特別是法國政府如何平衡其農業團體的抗議與歐盟整體的貿易目標，將是觀察重點。此外，南美洲國家對於歐盟提出的環境附加要求之接受程度，亦將直接影響談判的最終走向。INN新聞將持續追蹤此項協定的審議進度，並關注歐盟是否會針對農業補貼或環境規範提出具體的折衷方案。
