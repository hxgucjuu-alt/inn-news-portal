  # Starbucks Terminates AI Inventory Management System in North America, Returns to Manual Counting
  
  > Starbucks recently announced the termination of the AI inventory automation system implemented in its North American stores. The program was halted after nine months, sparking widespread discussion regarding the balance between automation technology and labor management in large retail chains.
  
  Starbucks has recently undergone a significant adjustment in its North American operational strategy. According to internal communications and informed sources, the company has officially terminated an artificial intelligence (AI) project aimed at automating store inventory counts. Since its implementation, the system was intended to use technology to reduce the burden on store employees regarding tedious inventory tasks and to improve the accuracy of supply chain management. However, this automation attempt, which had been in place across North American stores for approximately nine months, ultimately failed to achieve the expected results, leading the company to decide to remove the system entirely and require stores to return to traditional manual counting processes.
  
  This move highlights the severe challenges Starbucks faces in integrating digital transformation with on-site store operations, specifically regarding the gap between technical deployment and practical application.
  
  Cross-referencing multiple media reports and internal sources confirms that Starbucks has indeed terminated the AI inventory system. The system's primary function was to assist employees in automatically calculating inventory levels to optimize ordering and material management. In practice, however, the system did not function as smoothly as expected, forcing store personnel to spend significant time on manual verification and potentially affecting the accuracy of material supply due to system miscalculations.
  
  Currently, Starbucks has not released a detailed technical failure report regarding this decision. However, informed sources indicate that the decision was based on a comprehensive assessment of store operational efficiency, demonstrating that automation software still faces significant technical limitations when handling the complex and frequently changing environment of retail inventory.
  
  Regarding the specific details of this decision, there is a gap in information among sources. While CNBC reported clearly that Starbucks has terminated the program, the report did not specify whether an alternative AI tool would be developed or if the company would shift to other types of digital assistance systems. Furthermore, there is currently a lack of public feedback from frontline employees regarding the system's performance variations across different stores and their specific reactions to it.
  
  Some industry observers believe the system's failure may be related to data input accuracy, the stability of hardware sensing devices, or the transition costs associated with changing store personnel's operational habits. However, these details remain to be clarified by future internal evaluation reports.
  
  This event is significant for the North American retail and food service industries. As a global leader in coffee chains, Starbucks' investment trends in digitalization and automation are often viewed as an industry bellwether. The removal of the AI inventory system reflects the reality that large enterprises, while pursuing "automation" to reduce labor costs, must confront the high complexity of on-site environments. For the North American market, this change will directly impact the daily operational workflows of thousands of stores, requiring store managers and employees to readjust their work schedules to adapt to the labor intensity of returning to manual counting.
  
  Furthermore, this move may prompt other food and beverage operators to re-evaluate their return on investment regarding labor management and automation technology to avoid repeating similar mistakes.
  
  However, outsiders should not over-interpret this single event as a "total failure of artificial intelligence in the food and beverage industry." Some perspectives suggest that the potential for AI in inventory forecasting and supply chain optimization remains immense, and Starbucks' decision may be a correction specific to "store-level applications" rather than an abandonment of its overall digital strategy. The limitation lies in the fact that such technological applications are often constrained by disparities in store hardware infrastructure and the depth of employee training. Overemphasizing automation while ignoring the actual operational logic of the store floor can lead to a regression in operational efficiency.
  
  Therefore, this decision should be viewed as a necessary adjustment by the company regarding cost-effectiveness and practical feasibility when introducing new technology, rather than a rejection of AI technology itself.
  
  The focus for future observation will be on how Starbucks adjusts its digital transformation strategy and whether it will launch an improved solution for inventory management. Additionally, the industry will watch to see if other food and beverage giants adopt similar conservative strategies or continue to push forward with automation programs. This change in the North American market not only affects Starbucks' internal operating costs and labor structure but may also serve as an important reference case for future retail automation projects. It remains to be seen whether the company will conduct a public technical review of this failure and whether it will shift toward a more flexible and resilient hybrid management model for supply chain management.

# 星巴克終止北美人工智慧庫存管理系統，全面回歸人工盤點模式

> 星巴克近日宣佈終止在北美門市推行的 AI 庫存自動化系統。該計畫實施九個月後喊卡，引發外界對於大型連鎖企業匯入自動化科技與勞動力管理平衡的廣泛討論。


星巴克（Starbucks）近期在北美地區的營運策略出現重大調整，根據內部通訊及知情人士透露，該公司已正式終止一項旨在自動化門市庫存檔點的人工智慧（AI）計畫。該系統自推行以來，原意是透過科技手段減輕門市員工在繁瑣庫存檔點作業上的負擔，提升供應鏈管理的精確度。然而，這項在北美門市實施約九個月的自動化嘗試，最終未能達到預期成效，公司決定全面撤除該系統，並要求門市回歸傳統的人工盤點流程。
此舉標誌著星巴克在數位轉型與門市現場作業整合過程中，面臨了技術落地與實際應用場景之間的嚴峻挑戰。
根據多家媒體與內部訊息來源的交叉比對，星巴克終止 AI 庫存系統的事實已獲確認。該系統主要功能為協助員工自動計算庫存量，以最佳化訂貨與物料管理。然而，在實務運作中，該系統並未如預期般順利運作，導致門市人員在處理庫存時仍需花費大量時間進行人工校對，甚至可能因系統誤判而影響物料供應的準確性。
目前，星巴克官方並未針對此決策發布詳細的技術故障報告，但知情人士指出，這項決策是基於對門市營運效率的綜合考量，顯示出自動化軟體在處理複雜且變動頻繁的零售庫存環境時，仍存在顯著的技術侷限。
關於此項決策的具體細節，目前來源間存在資訊落差。雖然 CNBC 報導明確指出星巴克已終止該計畫，但該報導並未詳細說明是否會開發替代性的 AI 工具，或是將轉向其他型態的數位輔助系統。此外，針對該系統在不同門市的表現差異，以及員工對該系統的具體反饋，目前尚缺乏來自基層員工的公開意見彙整。
部分產業觀察家認為，該系統的失敗可能與資料輸入的精確度、硬體感測裝置的穩定性，或是門市人員操作習慣的轉移成本有關，但這些細節仍有待後續更多內部評估報告釋出，方能釐清確切的失敗原因。
此事件對北美零售產業及餐飲服務業具有指標性意義。星巴克作為全球連鎖咖啡龍頭，其在數位化與自動化領域的投資動向，往往被視為產業風向球。此次撤除 AI 庫存系統，反映出大型企業在追求「自動化」以降低人力成本的同時，必須面對現場環境的高度複雜性。對於北美市場而言，這項變動將直接影響數千家門市的日常營運流程，門市經理與員工必須重新調整工作排程，以適應回歸人工盤點後的勞動強度。
此外，此舉亦可能促使其他餐飲業者重新評估其在勞動力管理與自動化科技投入上的投資報酬率，避免重蹈覆轍。
然而，外界不應將此單一事件過度解讀為「人工智慧在餐飲業的全面潰敗」。部分觀點認為，AI 在庫存預測與供應鏈最佳化方面的潛力依然巨大，星巴克的決策可能僅是針對特定「門市端應用」的修正，而非放棄整體的數位化策略。限制在於，這類科技應用往往受限於門市硬體基礎設施的落差，以及員工培訓的深度。若過度強調自動化而忽略了門市現場的實際作業邏輯，反而會造成營運效率的倒退。
因此，這項決策應被視為企業在匯入新技術時，針對成本效益與實務可行性進行的必要調整，而非對 AI 技術本身的否定。
後續觀察重點在於星巴克未來如何調整其數位轉型策略，以及是否會針對庫存管理推出改良版的解決方案。此外，業界亦將關注其他餐飲巨頭是否會採取類似的保守策略，抑或持續推進自動化程式。星巴克在北美市場的此項變動，不僅影響其內部的營運成本與勞動力結構，更可能成為未來零售自動化專案在進行決策時的重要參考案例。後續需持續關注公司是否會針對此次失敗進行公開的技術檢討，以及其在供應鏈管理上是否會轉向更靈活、更具彈性的混合式管理模式。
