  # Conclusion of South American Regional Security Conference and Progress in EU Trade Agreement Negotiations
  
  > The 2025 South American Defense Conference was held in Argentina, focusing on regional security threats and cooperation. Simultaneously, the European Union is actively pushing for a historic trade agreement with South America, urgently seeking support from member states to ensure passage by a majority vote.
  
  From August 20 to 21, 2025, U.S. government and military officials traveled to Argentina to hold the "2025 South American Defense Conference" (SOUTHDEC 25) with defense leaders from various South American nations. The conference primarily focused on in-depth discussions regarding current security challenges within the South American region, aiming to strengthen military cooperation and intelligence sharing among nations to address increasingly complex regional security threats. This move demonstrates that the United States continues to prioritize its strategic ties with South American partners in the realm of national defense and seeks to stabilize the regional security landscape and prevent potential conflicts and instability through multilateral dialogue platforms.
  
  Meanwhile, the issue of economic integration in South America has also made progress on the international stage. According to the latest information from April 2026, the European Union is at a critical juncture in pushing for a historic trade agreement with South America. To ensure the agreement passes through a majority vote, the European Commission is actively seeking support from the Italian government. EU officials pointed out that the agreement holds symbolic significance for bilateral economic development. Danish Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen also publicly called for an end to threatening behavior toward historic allies, emphasizing the importance of international cooperation in promoting regional trade integration.
  
  Currently, information regarding South American regional security and economic cooperation is developing on multiple levels, though some aspects remain to be clarified. Regarding defense cooperation, although the U.S. Southern Command (SOUTHCOM) clearly stated that the conference aimed to discuss regional threats, specific details of defense strategies and the proportions of resource commitments from each country have not been fully disclosed. Regarding the trade agreement, while negotiations between the EU and South America have entered the final sprint, disagreements persist among member states regarding the distribution of benefits, particularly on sensitive issues such as agricultural subsidies and environmental standards. It remains highly uncertain whether sufficient majority support can be secured within the deadline.
  
  These regional events have had a direct impact on the social and policy environment of South America. On the defense front, the convening of the SOUTHDEC 25 conference signals that South American nations will adopt a more unified stance on border security, combating transnational crime, and military modernization, which will help enhance regional defense resilience. On the economic front, if the trade agreement between the EU and South America is successfully implemented, it will help South American countries expand export markets and attract more European capital for infrastructure investment and industrial upgrading, thereby driving regional economic growth and creating job opportunities, which will have a profound impact on the long-term development of South America.
  
  However, the promotion of relevant policies still faces multiple constraints. First, defense cooperation often involves a delicate balance between national sovereignty and diplomatic positions; overemphasizing military intervention may trigger backlash within the region. Second, the negotiation process for trade agreements is often influenced by the internal political landscape of the EU, and shifts in the attitudes of countries like Italy could lead to a stagnation of the agreement process. Analysts warn that these forms of cooperation should not be viewed as a single political alliance, but rather as a complex geopolitical game. While pursuing security and economic interests, countries must still face the dual pressure of domestic public opinion and international regulations. Over-interpreting the influence of a single conference or agreement may ignore the challenges posed by structural differences within the region.
  
  Looking ahead, the development of the situation in South America depends on how countries implement the resolutions of the defense conference and the final voting results of the EU-South America trade negotiations. Key areas to observe include: first, whether South American nations will establish concrete, normalized military coordination mechanisms for transnational threats; second, whether the EU can resolve the concerns of its member states within the deadline to successfully sign the trade agreement; and third, whether the U.S. strategic layout in South America will be adjusted in response to global political and economic changes. These issues will continue to affect the role and status of South America in the international community and are worthy of continued close attention.

# 南美區域安全會議落幕與歐盟貿易協定談判進展

> 2025年南美防衛會議於阿根廷召開，聚焦區域安全威脅與合作；同時，歐盟正積極推動與南美洲的歷史性貿易協定，亟需成員國支援以確保多數決透過。


2025年8月20日至21日，美國政府與軍方官員前往阿根廷，與南美洲多國防衛領導人共同舉行「2025年南美防衛會議」（SOUTHDEC 25）。該會議主要針對當前南美區域內的安全挑戰進行深入研討，旨在強化各國間的軍事合作與情報共享，以應對日益複雜的區域安全威脅。此舉顯示美國持續重視與南美夥伴在國防安全領域的戰略連結，並試圖透過多邊對話平臺，穩定區域內的安全域性勢，防範潛在的衝突與不穩定因素。
與此同時，南美洲的經濟整合議題亦在國際舞臺上取得進展。根據2026年4月的最新資訊顯示，歐盟正處於推動與南美洲達成歷史性貿易協定的關鍵時刻。為了確保該協議能順利透過多數決審議，歐盟執委會正積極爭取義大利政府的支援。歐盟官員指出，該協議對雙邊經濟發展具有指標性意義，丹麥總理梅特·弗雷德裡克森（Mette Frederiksen）亦公開呼籲，應停止針對歷史性盟友的威脅行為，強調國際合作對於推動區域貿易整合的重要性。
目前，關於南美區域安全與經濟合作的資訊呈現多層次發展，但仍存在部分待釐清之處。在防衛合作方面，儘管美國南方司令部（SOUTHCOM）明確指出會議旨在討論區域威脅，但具體的防衛策略細節與各國承諾的資源投入比例尚未完全公開。而在貿易協定方面，歐盟與南美洲的談判雖已進入最後衝刺階段，但各成員國對於協議細節的利益分配仍存有歧見，特別是在農業補貼與環境標準等敏感議題上，各方說法尚未達成完全一致，是否能在期限內取得足夠多數支援仍具高度不確定性。
這些區域性事件對南美洲的社會與政策環境產生了直接影響。在國防層面，SOUTHDEC 25會議的召開，預示著南美各國未來在邊境安全、打擊跨國犯罪及軍事現代化方面，將採取更為一致的立場，有助於提升區域防禦韌性。而在經濟層面，若歐盟與南美洲的貿易協定能順利生效，將有助於南美各國擴大出口市場，吸引更多歐洲資本投入基礎建設與產業升級，進而帶動區域經濟成長並創造就業機會，對南美洲的長期發展具有深遠影響。
然而，相關政策的推動仍面臨多重限制。首先，防衛合作往往涉及各國主權與外交立場的微妙平衡，過度強調軍事介入可能引發區域內反彈。其次，貿易協定的談判過程常受到歐盟內部政治生態的影響，義大利等國的態度轉變可能導致協議程式停滯。分析人士提醒，不應將上述合作視為單一的政治結盟，而應將其視為複雜的國際地緣政治博弈。各國在追求安全與經濟利益的同時，仍需面對國內民意與國際法規的雙重壓力，過度解讀單一會議或協議的影響力，恐忽略了區域內部結構性差異所帶來的挑戰。
展望未來，南美洲的局勢發展重點在於各國如何落實防衛會議的決議，以及歐盟與南美貿易談判的最終投票結果。觀察重點應聚焦於：第一，南美各國是否會針對跨國威脅建立具體的常態化軍事協調機制；第二，歐盟是否能在限期內化解成員國疑慮，成功簽署貿易協定；第三，美國在南美地區的戰略佈局是否會因應全球政經變動而有所調整。這些議題將持續牽動南美洲在國際社會中的角色與地位，值得各界持續密切關注其後續演變。
