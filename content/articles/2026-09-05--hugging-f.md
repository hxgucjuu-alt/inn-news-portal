  # NVIDIA Acquires Hugging Face for $12.9 Billion Amid Major Global Disaster Relief Efforts
  
  > NVIDIA has officially announced the acquisition of the AI open-source platform Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, emphasizing its commitment to maintaining the platform's openness. Meanwhile, following a devastating glacial flood in Nepal that resulted in significant casualties, the Fubon Group has announced a $1 million donation to support international humanitarian relief efforts.
  
  On September 4, 2026, major events occurred in both the global technology and humanitarian sectors. In the tech industry, chip giant NVIDIA officially announced on September 3 that it had reached an agreement to acquire Hugging Face, the world's largest AI open-source community and model platform, for $12.903 billion. This move is seen as a key strategy for NVIDIA to strengthen its AI ecosystem; the deal had been rumored in the industry for some time and is now finalized. Regarding global humanitarian aid, the Himalayan region on the Nepal border recently experienced a devastating flood triggered by a severe glacial collapse, causing significant local casualties.
  
  According to statistics, the disaster has resulted in over 1,000 deaths, with more than 4,000 people reported missing and a large number of residents displaced, marking a catastrophic situation.
  
  Regarding NVIDIA's acquisition of Hugging Face, NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang has personally issued an official statement. Huang explicitly promised that even after the acquisition is completed, NVIDIA will maintain the independence and open spirit of the Hugging Face platform, ensuring that developers can continue to access open models through the platform, thereby driving continuous innovation and progress in global AI technology. This announcement aims to alleviate concerns that the acquisition of an open-source community by a large corporation might lead to technical enclosure or commercial restrictions.
  
  In terms of humanitarian relief, the Fubon Group officially announced today (the 4th) a donation of $1 million to support disaster relief efforts for the Nepal floods. The funds will be donated to the Red Cross Society of the Republic of China (Taiwan) and World Vision Taiwan to support rescue and resettlement plans in the disaster areas, helping local residents overcome these difficulties.
  
  Regarding the details of the NVIDIA and Hugging Face transaction, while the official acquisition amount and the commitment to maintaining openness have been confirmed, the industry remains cautious regarding the specific path for subsequent technical integration and whether there will be potential changes to the usage rights of the existing open-source community. As for the Nepal floods, although the Fubon Group has taken the lead in providing relief funds, there remains a high degree of uncertainty regarding further developments in the disaster, particularly the progress of search and rescue operations for the missing and the extent of damage to infrastructure in the affected areas, which still awaits more detailed assessment reports from international relief organizations and the local government.
  
  These two events have had a direct impact on their respective fields. NVIDIA's acquisition is expected to reshape the industrial structure of AI development tools. For developers and companies that rely on open-source models, this transaction represents a deep integration between a computing power giant and the open-source ecosystem, which may accelerate the commercialization of AI models. The floods in Nepal highlight the environmental safety risks caused by glacier melting due to climate change, posing urgent challenges to public safety and humanitarian relief mechanisms in South Asia. The Fubon Group's donation reflects a trend of corporations fulfilling social responsibilities through transnational humanitarian aid during major international disasters.
  
  Although NVIDIA emphasizes openness, market analysts and the open-source community have cautioned against over-interpreting the definition of "maintaining openness." Operational adjustments following corporate acquisitions often have delayed effects, and whether Hugging Face will prioritize support for NVIDIA's hardware architecture remains a limiting factor of concern. Similarly, regarding the relief efforts in Nepal, while international donations can provide immediate relief, given the scale of the missing persons and the disaster, a single corporate donation is merely a drop in the bucket. Long-term post-disaster reconstruction and climate adaptation policies still require long-term resource investment and policy collaboration from the international community.
  
  Future observation will focus on two areas. First, how NVIDIA implements its commitments to the open-source community and whether the acquisition will trigger scrutiny from global antitrust regulators will be the focus of the tech industry. Second, the progress of rescue and recovery in the Nepal disaster area, as well as the international community's review and investment in climate disaster warning systems for the Himalayan region, will be important indicators for international public issues in the coming months. # 輝達斥資百億美元收購 Hugging Face 與全球重大災難救援進展

> 輝達（NVIDIA）正式宣佈以 129 億美元收購 AI 開源平臺 Hugging Face，強調維持平臺開放性；與此同時，尼泊爾冰川洪災釀成嚴重傷亡，富邦集團宣佈捐贈 100 萬美元投入國際人道救援。


2026 年 9 月 4 日，全球科技與人道領域發生重大事件。在科技產業方面，晶片巨頭輝達（NVIDIA）於 9 月 3 日正式對外宣佈，已達成協議以 129.303 億美元的價格，正式併購全球最大的 AI 開源社群與模型平臺 Hugging Face。此舉被視為輝達強化其 AI 生態系版圖的關鍵策略，該交易案在業界傳聞已久，如今正式定案。而在全球人道救援方面，尼泊爾邊境喜馬拉雅山區近期因嚴重的冰川崩塌引發毀滅性洪災，造成當地重大傷亡。
根據統計，該災害已導致超過千人罹難，另有逾 4,000 人失蹤，大量民眾流離失所，災情極為慘重。
針對輝達收購 Hugging Face 一案，輝達執行長黃仁勳已親自發表官方宣告。黃仁勳明確承諾，即便在併購完成後，輝達仍將維持 Hugging Face 平臺的獨立性與開放精神，確保開發者能持續透過該平臺取得開放模型，進而推動全球 AI 技術的持續創新與進步。此宣告旨在緩解外界對於大型企業併購開源社群後，可能導致技術封閉或商業化限制的擔憂。
在人道救援行動上，富邦集團今日（4 日）正式宣佈捐款 100 萬美元，將資金投入尼泊爾洪災的賑災工作，款項將分別捐助予中華民國紅十字會及臺灣世界展望會，以支援災區的救援與安置計畫，協助當地居民度過難關。
關於輝達與 Hugging Face 的交易細節，目前官方雖已確認收購金額與維持開放的承諾，但針對後續技術整合的具體路徑，以及是否會對現有開源社群的使用許可權產生潛在變動，各界仍持審慎觀察態度。而在尼泊爾洪災方面，雖然富邦集團已率先投入救援資金，但當地災情的進一步發展仍存在高度不確定性，尤其是失蹤人數的搜救進度，以及受災地區基礎建設的毀損程度，目前仍待國際救援組織與當地政府提供更詳細的評估報告。
這兩起事件對相關領域產生了直接影響。輝達的收購案預計將重塑 AI 開發工具的產業結構，對於依賴開源模型的開發者與企業而言，這項交易代表了算力巨頭與開源生態的深度繫結，可能加速 AI 模型的商業化程式。尼泊爾的洪災則凸顯了氣候變遷導致冰川融化所引發的環境安全風險，對南亞地區的公共安全與人道救援機制提出了迫切挑戰。富邦集團的捐助行動，則反映了企業在國際重大災難中，透過跨國人道援助履行社會責任的趨勢。
儘管輝達強調開放性，但市場分析師與開源社群仍提醒，不應過度解讀「維持開放」的定義，企業併購後的營運模式調整往往具有延遲效應，未來 Hugging Face 是否會優先支援輝達的硬體架構，仍是各界關注的限制因素。同樣地，針對尼泊爾的救援行動，雖然國際捐款能緩解燃眉之急，但面對如此大規模的失蹤與災情，單一企業的捐助僅是杯水車薪，災後的長期重建與氣候調適政策，仍需依賴國際社會長期的資源投入與政策協作。
後續觀察重點將集中在兩方面。首先，輝達如何落實其對開源社群的承諾，以及該收購案是否會引發全球反壟斷監管機構的審查，將是科技界觀察的焦點。其次，尼泊爾災區的救援進展與災後復原狀況，以及國際社會對於喜馬拉雅山區氣候災害預警系統的檢討與投資，將是未來幾個月內國際公共議題的重要指標。INN 新聞編輯部將持續追蹤上述事件的最新進展，並為讀者提供即時更新。
