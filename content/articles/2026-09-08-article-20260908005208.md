  # Review of Taiwan's Social Safety Net: "Kai-Kai Case" Verdict Sparks Controversy Over Social Workers' Status as Guarantors
  
  > The first-instance verdict in the 2026 "Kai-Kai case," which ruled that a social worker held the status of a guarantor and imposed a prison sentence, has drawn significant attention from the social welfare and legal sectors in Taiwan. Various parties are calling for the adoption of international practices, urging a review of systemic loopholes rather than merely punishing frontline personnel to strengthen the functionality of the social safety net.
  
  Taiwan's social safety net policy has sparked widespread discussion in recent years due to the "Kai-Kai case." This case not only exposed potential gaps in child protection mechanisms but also pushed the legal responsibilities of social workers into the spotlight of public opinion following the Taipei District Court's first-instance verdict in April 2026. The verdict determined that the social worker involved held the "status of a guarantor" and sentenced them to two years in prison. This result immediately caused an uproar; not only did social welfare groups and the Judicial Reform Foundation publicly support the frontline social worker, but the academic community also engaged in intense debate over whether social workers should be legally viewed as guarantors.
  
  This incident is seen as a major turning point in the implementation of Taiwan's social safety net policy, forcing the government and the private sector to re-examine the positioning, rights, and responsibilities of social workers within the child protection system.
  
  Based on cross-referencing various media reports and public information, the facts currently confirmed by all parties include: the case indeed exposed the dilemma in the current child protection mechanism where social workers lack sufficient public authority support and professional systemic safeguards when facing high-risk cases. Academics and legal experts agree that blaming frontline social workers for all responsibilities may lead to a loss of social work personnel, subsequently causing a structural collapse of the social safety net.
  
  Currently, scholars such as Professor Wang Tseng-yung from the Department of Social Work at National Chengchi University have proposed concrete suggestions, calling on the government to learn from international systems in Europe and the United States. They advocate shifting the focus of reviews toward systemic optimization rather than simple punitive mechanisms, emphasizing that social workers should perform their duties under clear divisions of rights and responsibilities, while requiring stronger public authority intervention as a backup.
  
  However, there are significant differences in perspectives regarding the legal interpretation of whether social workers possess the "status of a guarantor." Legal professionals analyze that the court's determination was primarily based on the social worker's substantive supervisory role within the child protection system, but this view has been strongly opposed by the social work community and judicial reform groups. Opponents argue that social workers are merely providers of social welfare services and are not the legal guardians or sole caregivers of children; they contend that infinitely expanding their legal liability will cause social workers to face extreme legal risks while performing their duties.
  
  Information currently awaiting confirmation includes whether judicial authorities will adjust their legal interpretations regarding such cases in the future, and what specific institutional reform measures the government will take in response to public demands to clarify the scope of social workers' rights and responsibilities.
  
  This case has had a profound impact on Taiwanese society and related industries. First, the social work industry is facing a severe crisis of confidence; if the boundaries of legal liability remain blurred, it may lead to a massive loss of professional social work talent, thereby affecting the quality of overall social welfare services. Second, the government faces immense policy pressure and must strike a balance between "protecting children" and "safeguarding the rights of social workers." Furthermore, this has affected public trust in the social safety net; how to prevent the next tragedy through institutional reform has become the core of public policy discussion.
  
  Internationally, the construction of social safety nets often emphasizes the integration of cross-professional resources and the effective intervention of public authority, rather than concentrating all responsibility on individual social workers. This serves as an important reference indicator for Taiwan's current policy reforms.
  
  Although there is a preliminary consensus on the direction of reform, the risk of over-interpretation must be noted. For example, the verdict of a single case does not mean that all social workers are at the same legal risk, as judicial rulings must still depend on the specific circumstances of each case. Furthermore, when drawing on international experience, one must consider the differences in Taiwan's localized social structure and legal system; foreign models cannot be directly copied. The biggest limitation at present is that institutional reform is often slow and complex, and it is difficult to achieve structural change in the short term through public pressure alone. Moreover, the problems of social worker shortages and unequal resource distribution cannot be solved by a single verdict.
  
  Therefore, various parties are calling for a calm approach to judicial verdicts and for the focus to be shifted back to long-term institutional construction and resource investment.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on several aspects: first, whether the judicial system's legal interpretation of the social worker's status as a guarantor will take a different turn following subsequent appeals; second, specific legislative amendments or administrative guidelines proposed by the Ministry of Health and Welfare and relevant competent authorities regarding the protection of social work practice; and third, whether the advocacy actions of social work professional groups and the Judicial Reform Foundation can effectively promote policy implementation. As 2026 enters the second half, public attention toward child protection mechanisms continues to rise; whether the government can propose concrete improvement plans before the end of the year will be a key indicator of whether the social safety net policy has been effectively revised.
  
  At the same time, Taiwan's performance in international rankings regarding quality of life and social safety-related indicators will also reflect the long-term impact of such social events on the nation's overall image.

# 臺灣社會安全網檢討：剴剴案判決引發社工保證人地位爭議

> 針對2026年「剴剴案」一審判決社工具保證人地位並判處徒刑，引發國內社福界與法界高度關注。各界呼籲借鏡國際經驗，應檢討制度漏洞而非僅懲罰第一線人員，以強化社會安全網功能。


臺灣社會安全網政策近年因「剴剴案」引發廣泛討論，該案不僅揭露了兒少保護機制中的潛在漏接風險，更在2026年4月臺北地方法院一審判決後，將社會工作者的法律責任推向輿論焦點。該判決認定涉案社工具備「保證人地位」，並判處2年有期徒刑。此判決結果隨即引發社會譁然，不僅社福團體與民間司改會公開聲援第一線社工，學界亦針對社工是否應被視為法律上的保證人展開激烈辯論。
這一事件被視為臺灣社會安全網政策執行過程中的重大轉折點，迫使政府與民間重新審視社工在兒少保護體系中的定位與權責邊界。
根據多家媒體報導與公開資訊交叉比對，目前各界共同確認的事實包括：該案確實暴露了現行兒少保護機制中，社工在面對高風險個案時，缺乏足夠公權力支援與專業制度保障的困境。學界與法律專家一致認為，將所有責任歸咎於第一線社工，恐導致社工人力流失，進而造成社會安全網的結構性崩潰。
目前，政大社工系教授王增勇等學者已提出具體建議，呼籲政府應借鏡歐美等國際制度，將檢討重點轉向制度面的最佳化，而非單純的懲罰機制，並強調社工應在明確的權責劃分下執行業務，同時需有更強力的公權力介入作為後盾。
然而，針對社工是否具備「保證人地位」的法律詮釋，目前存在顯著的觀點差異。法界人士分析，法院認定社工具備保證人地位，主要是基於其在兒少保護體系中的實質監督角色，但此觀點遭到了社工界與司改團體的強烈反對。反對者認為，社工僅是社會福利服務的提供者，並非兒少的法定監護人或唯一照顧者，若將其法律責任無限擴張，將導致社工在執行業務時陷入極大的法律風險。
目前尚待確認的資訊包括：司法機關後續是否會針對此類案件的法律見解進行調整，以及政府部門在回應民間訴求時，將採取何種具體的制度性改革措施來釐清社工的權責範圍。
此案對臺灣社會及相關產業的影響深遠。首先，社工產業面臨嚴重的信心危機，若法律責任界線模糊，恐導致社工專業人才大量流失，進而影響整體社會福利服務的品質。其次，政策面臨極大壓力，政府必須在「保護兒少」與「保障社工權益」之間取得平衡。此外，這也影響了民眾對於社會安全網的信任感，如何透過制度改革來防止下一個悲劇發生，已成為公共政策討論的核心。
國際間對於社會安全網的建構，往往強調跨專業的資源整合與公權力的有效介入，而非將所有責任集中於個案社工身上，這也是目前臺灣政策改革的重要參考指標。
儘管各界對於改革方向有初步共識，但仍需注意過度解讀的風險。例如，單一案件的判決結果並不代表所有社工在法律上皆處於相同風險，司法判決仍需視個案具體情節而定。此外，借鏡國際經驗時，必須考量臺灣在地化的社會結構與法律體系差異，不能直接照搬國外模式。目前最大的限制在於，制度的改革往往緩慢且複雜，單靠輿論壓力難以在短期內達成結構性的變革，且社工人力不足與資源分配不均的問題，並非單一判決所能解決。
因此，各界呼籲應冷靜看待司法判決，並將焦點拉回長期性的制度建構與資源投入。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於幾個面向：第一，司法系統對於社工保證人地位的法律見解，是否會隨後續上訴審理而有不同轉折；第二，衛生福利部與相關主管機關針對社工執業保障提出的具體修法或行政指引；第三，社工專業團體與司改會後續的倡議行動，是否能有效推動政策落實。隨著2026年進入下半年，社會大眾對於兒少保護機制的關注度持續升溫，政府能否在年底前提出具體的改善方案，將是檢驗社會安全網政策是否有效修正的關鍵指標。
同時，國際評比中臺灣在生活品質與社會安全相關指標的表現，也將反映出此類社會事件對國家整體形象的長期影響。
