  # Glimmer of Hope in US-China Trade Talks Following Trump-Xi Meeting; Global Capital Market Sentiment Turns Optimistic
  
  > The United States Trade Representative has confirmed progress in US-China trade negotiations, with details expected to be released on September 28. Encouraged by this news, the four major US stock indices closed in the green, signaling that global investors are optimistic about the easing of geopolitical tensions and the macroeconomic outlook.
  
  The global economy and trade landscape have recently reached a major turning point. Following a long period of trade friction and structural competition, the two major economies of the US and China held a "Trump-Xi meeting." On September 25, 2026, United States Trade Representative Katherine Tai informed the media that both sides have reached an agreement on certain goods and will conduct trade under more favorable conditions in the future. This move is seen as a signal that bilateral trade negotiations have made substantive progress.
  
  Meanwhile, global financial markets have responded positively. The four major US stock indices closed in the green on September 25, with the Dow Jones Industrial Average surging 478 points and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rising 176 points, demonstrating the market's strong resilience after digesting geopolitical and macroeconomic data.
  
  According to a cross-analysis of multiple media sources, US-China trade relations are undergoing a significant structural shift. Data shows that the US trade deficit with China has shrunk significantly from a record $418 billion in 2018 to $203 billion in 2025, a decrease of 52%. Analysts point out that the focus of the US trade imbalance has gradually shifted toward other Asian economies and Mexico. Furthermore, the US stock market demonstrated extremely strong capital-attracting power in the second quarter of this year, with foreign investment in stocks and investment funds reaching $426 billion, the highest record since the first quarter of 2020.
  
  This surge in capital is primarily driven by the booming development of the artificial intelligence (AI) industry, indicating that global confidence in the US market has not weakened due to geopolitical tensions; rather, it shows a strong pattern of returning buying interest.
  
  Despite the optimistic market sentiment, there are still discrepancies in the details of the negotiations across various sources. Currently, the Office of the United States Trade Representative has only announced that the details of the negotiations will be officially released on September 28. The public is still awaiting official confirmation regarding the specific list of "certain goods," the extent of tariff adjustments, and whether the agreement covers strategic materials such as critical rare earth resources. Additionally, although the trade deficit data shows a downward trend, energy supply security remains a variable affecting global trade. Reports indicate that due to supply disruptions in the Middle East caused by the war in Iran, China's risk of dependence on imported oil has increased, making the competition between the US and China over critical resources and energy strategy complexly intertwined with the progress of trade negotiations.
  
  The progress of US-China trade negotiations has potential indirect impacts on Central America and the Caribbean. As the US trade focus shifts to Mexico and other Asian economies, Central American countries, as potential beneficiaries of US supply chain restructuring, are receiving significant attention regarding their regional industrial policies and trade status. If US-China trade relations stabilize, the volatility of global supply chains may decrease, which would be conducive to Central American countries attracting more diverse international investment. However, if the struggle between the US and China over rare earths and critical resources intensifies, it could lead to global energy price fluctuations, thereby affecting energy-import-dependent economies in the Caribbean and increasing pressure on their public finances and the cost of living.
  
  It must be noted that the market should remain cautious regarding the optimism following the "Trump-Xi meeting." Analysts remind us that despite the strong performance of US stocks, the recent rise in US Treasury yields continues to put pressure on financial markets. Furthermore, while foreign capital inflows into the US market have hit a record high, this reflects global capital's pursuit of the AI industry rather than purely an expectation of the trade negotiation results. Investors should not over-interpret the short-term stock market rebound as the complete end of the US-China trade dispute, as the structural contradictions in the strategic competition between the two countries—especially in energy supply and the scramble for critical minerals—remain long-term variables, and the short-term agreement can only be viewed as a phased easing.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on the trade negotiation details to be released on September 28, particularly whether the agreement includes specific clauses regarding its impact on Central American and Caribbean supply chains, as well as the follow-up interactions between the US and China on energy security issues. In addition, the market will continue to monitor the volatility of US Treasury yields and the willingness of global foreign capital to continue investing in the US market to determine whether this optimistic trend has long-term support. For the Central American region, observing its industrial upgrading progress under the US "nearshoring" policy will be a key indicator for assessing its position in the new global trade landscape.

# 川習會後美中貿易談判露曙光，全球資本市場情緒轉趨樂觀

> 美國貿易代表證實美中貿易談判取得進展，預計於9月28日公佈細節。受此訊息激勵，美股四大指數同步收紅，顯示全球投資人對地緣政治緩解與宏觀經濟前景持樂觀態度。


近期全球經濟與貿易領域迎來重大轉折，美中兩大經濟體在歷經長期的貿易摩擦與結構性競爭後，於近期召開「川習會」。美國貿易代表葛裡爾（Katherine Tai）於2026年9月25日向媒體表示，美中雙方已就部分商品達成協議，未來將在更有利的條件下進行貿易，此舉被視為雙邊貿易談判取得實質進展的訊號。
與此同時，全球金融市場對此做出正面回應，美股四大指數在9月25日同步收紅，其中道瓊工業指數勁揚478點，費城半導體指數亦上漲176點，展現出市場在消化地緣政治與宏觀經濟資料後的強勁韌性。
根據多個媒體來源的交叉整理，美中貿易關係正經歷顯著的結構性轉變。資料顯示，美國對中國的貿易逆差已從2018年創紀錄的4,180億美元，大幅縮減至2025年的2,030億美元，降幅高達52%。分析指出，美國貿易失衡的重心已逐漸轉向其他亞洲經濟體及墨西哥。此外，美股在今年第二季展現出極強的吸金力道，外資投入股票與投資基金的金額高達4,260億美元，創下自2020年第一季以來的最高紀錄。
這股資金熱潮主要由人工智慧（AI）產業的蓬勃發展所驅動，顯示全球資金對於美國市場的信心並未因地緣政治緊張而減弱，反而呈現買氣回籠的強勢格局。
儘管市場情緒樂觀，各來源對於談判細節的掌握仍存在差異。目前美國貿易代表辦公室僅預告將於9月28日正式對外公佈談判細節，外界對於「部分商品」的具體清單、關稅調整幅度以及是否涉及關鍵稀土資源等戰略物資的協議內容，尚待官方進一步證實。此外，雖然貿易逆差資料呈現下降趨勢，但能源供應安全問題仍是影響全球貿易的變數。報導指出，受伊朗戰爭導致中東供應受阻影響，中國對進口石油的依賴風險增加，這使得美中兩國在關鍵資源與能源戰略上的競爭，與貿易談判的進展呈現複雜的交織狀態。
美中貿易談判的進展對中美洲與加勒比海地區具有潛在的間接影響。隨著美國貿易重心轉向墨西哥及其他亞洲經濟體，中美洲國家作為美國供應鏈重組的潛在受惠者，其區域內的產業政策與貿易地位備受關注。若美中貿易關係趨於穩定，全球供應鏈的波動性可能降低，有利於中美洲國家吸引更多元的國際投資。然而，若美中在稀土與關鍵資源的爭奪加劇，可能導致全球能源價格波動，進而影響加勒比海地區依賴能源進口的經濟體，增加其公共財政與民生物價的壓力。
必須注意的是，市場對於「川習會」後的樂觀情緒，仍應保持審慎。分析師提醒，儘管美股表現強勁，但近期美國公債殖利率攀升仍持續對金融市場帶來壓力。此外，外資流入美國市場雖創下紀錄，但這反映的是全球資金對於AI產業的追逐，而非單純對貿易談判結果的預期。投資人不應將短期的股市反彈過度解讀為美中貿易爭端的全面終結，因為兩國在戰略競爭層面的結構性矛盾，特別是在能源供應與關鍵礦產爭奪上，仍是長期的變數，短期內的協議僅能視為階段性的緩解。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於9月28日公佈的貿易談判細節，特別是協議中是否包含對中美洲與加勒比海供應鏈的具體影響條款，以及美中在能源安全議題上的後續互動。此外，市場將持續監測美國公債殖利率的波動，以及全球外資對於美國市場的持續投入意願，以判斷此波樂觀行情是否具備長期支撐力。對於中美洲地區而言，觀察其在美國「近岸外包」（Nearshoring）政策下的產業升級進度，將是評估其在全球貿易新局勢中地位的關鍵指標。
