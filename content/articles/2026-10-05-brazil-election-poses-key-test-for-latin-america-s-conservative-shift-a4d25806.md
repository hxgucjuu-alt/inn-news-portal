根據2026年10月4日的報導，巴西即將舉行的大選正受到全球密切關注，因為它被視為拉丁美洲保守主義轉向所面臨的最大考驗。資料顯示，候選人路易斯·伊納西奧·盧拉·達席爾瓦在民調中僅以微弱優勢領先弗拉維奧·波索納羅，預示著一場競爭激烈的選舉。

分析人士指出，此次巴西大選的結果可能重塑美洲的保守主義格局。報導強調，巴西是西半球尚未轉向保守陣營的最後一個主要障礙，儘管該半球存在其他左翼政府，但作為全球第11大經濟體，巴西的政治走向具有舉足輕重的影響力。

除了國內政治格局，此次選舉也牽動著國際關係。報導指出，美國與巴西之間的關係、關稅政策以及與中國的貿易往來都可能因選舉結果而受到影響。這場左右翼之間的對決，其影響力顯然超越了單純的國內政治範疇。

## English edition

According to reports from October 4, 2026, Brazil's upcoming election is drawing global attention as it is seen as the biggest test yet for Latin America's conservative shift. Data indicates that candidate Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva holds only a narrow lead over Flavio Bolsonaro in election polls, signaling a tightly contested race.

Analysts suggest that the outcome of this Brazilian election could reshape conservatism across the Americas. Reports emphasize that Brazil stands as the last major stumbling block in the Western Hemisphere that has not yet shifted to the conservative column. Despite the presence of other left-wing governments in the hemisphere, Brazil, as the world's 11th largest economy, holds significant influence over the region's political direction.

Beyond the domestic political landscape, this election also has significant international implications. Reports indicate that U.S. ties with Brazil, tariff policies, and trade relations with China could all be at stake depending on the election's outcome. The confrontation between the left and right in Brazil clearly extends beyond the scope of purely domestic politics.
