  # 60 Nations Issue Joint Declaration Opposing Tolls on International Straits
  
  > In response to recent threats from certain regions to impose additional tolls on international strategic waterways, 60 nations worldwide have issued a joint declaration to firmly defend freedom of navigation and oppose any discriminatory restrictions or illegal charges.
  
  Recently, global shipping security has become a major focus of the international community. A Reuters report on October 12, 2026, indicated that 60 countries, including EU member states, Australia, Japan, and the United Kingdom, issued a joint declaration this Monday. This move aims to respond to recent claims by certain regions regarding the imposition of tolls on international strategic waterways (such as the Strait of Hormuz), as well as frequent shipping disruptions. The declaration clearly expresses a commitment to global freedom of navigation, emphasizing that nations will work through multilateral cooperation to ensure the smooth and safe operation of maritime passages, while sending a strong signal of opposition to any actions that could lead to increased logistics costs or shipping interruptions.
  
  According to the joint declaration, the signatory nations unanimously support the adoption of defensive and non-escalatory multilateral initiatives to uphold navigation rights in international straits. The declaration strictly opposes any measures that hinder legal navigation, specifically including the illegal imposition of tolls, the setting of discriminatory restrictions, mandatory pre-authorization requirements, and other conditional demands. Participating countries emphasized that international strategic straits are global public waterways and should not be used by individual nations as chips for privatization or political maneuvering; any attempt to monetize passage through these waterways or set up artificial barriers is viewed as a challenge to the international trade order.
  
  Currently, there are some discrepancies and uncertainties regarding information surrounding shipping security and toll disputes in the Middle East. Although the 60-nation coalition explicitly opposes the fees, individuals associated with the Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps previously threatened, via the semi-official Fars News Agency, that they might close the southern shipping lanes. Furthermore, shipping attacks in the Middle East have been frequent recently, including an attack on an oil tanker in the waters north of Qatar and Iranian drone surveillance of the Strait of Hormuz. While these security threats overlap with the "prohibition of illegal charges" mentioned in the joint declaration, the conflict points at the practical implementation level remain to be clarified. It is currently impossible to confirm what specific military or diplomatic means countries will take to implement the commitment to "uphold freedom of navigation."
  
  This issue has a direct impact on global energy supply chains and logistics costs. JKO Asset Management pointed out that due to frequent shipping attacks in the Middle East, coupled with U.S. weather factors disrupting energy facilities in the Gulf of Mexico, international oil prices are facing pressure from supply disruptions. If international strategic straits are closed or tolls are forcibly imposed, it will lead to a significant increase in uncertainty for crude oil transportation, thereby driving up global logistics costs. For South American countries that are highly dependent on energy imports, the stability of the global shipping order directly affects the prices of imported energy and domestic inflationary pressure. Therefore, although South American nations did not directly participate in this declaration, their economic stability is closely linked to global maritime security.
  
  Regarding differing perspectives, some analysts caution against over-interpreting the immediate deterrent effect of this 60-nation declaration. Although the declaration demonstrates a strong diplomatic front, there remains high uncertainty as to whether this multilateral statement can be translated into actual escort operations or economic sanctions when faced with asymmetric threats from regional powers (such as drone harassment or regional blockades). Furthermore, there may be disagreements among signatories regarding the definition of "defensive initiatives"; some countries may prefer diplomatic mediation, while others may advocate for strengthening maritime military deployments. Viewing the effects of the declaration with excessive optimism may overlook the risk of regional conflict escalation.
  
  The focus for subsequent observation lies in whether the 60-nation coalition will propose specific joint patrol plans for the Strait of Hormuz or other key waterways, and how Iran will react to this declaration. Additionally, adjustments to oil tanker insurance rates by the International Energy Agency (IEA) and relevant shipping organizations will be an important indicator for assessing whether shipping risks have substantially decreased. The global community must continue to monitor whether geopolitical tensions will further evolve into substantive maritime blockades and track the volatility of international oil prices amidst supply chain uncertainties.

# 全球60國發表聯合宣告 反對國際海峽徵收通行費

> 針對近期部分地區試圖對國際戰略水道加徵通行費的威脅，全球60國發表聯合宣告，堅定捍衛航行自由，反對任何歧視性限制與非法收費。


近期，全球航運安全議題備受國際社會關注。路透社於2026年10月12日報導指出，包括歐盟成員國、澳洲、日本及英國在內的60個國家，於本週一共同發表了一份聯合宣告。此舉旨在回應部分地區近期針對國際戰略水道（如荷姆茲海峽）所提出的通行費徵收主張，以及頻繁的航運幹擾事件。宣告明確表達了對全球航行自由的承諾，強調各國將透過多邊合作，確保海上通道的順暢與安全，並對任何可能導致物流成本上升或航運中斷的行為釋出強烈反對訊號。
根據聯合宣告內容，簽署國一致支援採取防禦性且非升級性的多邊倡議，以維護國際海峽的航行權利。宣告中嚴正反對任何阻礙合法航行的措施，具體包括非法徵收通行費、設定歧視性限制、強制預先授權要求，以及其他附帶的條件。參與國強調，國際戰略海峽屬於全球公共航道，不應被個別國家作為私有化或政治博弈的籌碼，任何試圖將航道通行費用化或設定人為障礙的企圖，均被視為對國際貿易秩序的挑戰。
目前，針對中東地區航運安全與通行費爭議，各界資訊存在部分差異與不確定性。雖然60國聯盟明確反對收費，但伊朗革命衛隊相關人士先前曾透過半官方媒體法爾斯通訊社揚言，可能關閉南側航道。此外，中東地區近期航運攻擊事件頻傳，包括卡達北部海域的油輪遇襲，以及伊朗對荷姆茲海峽的無人機監視行為，這些安全威脅與聯合宣告中提到的「禁止非法收費」議題雖有重疊，但實際執行層面的衝突點仍有待釐清。目前尚無法確認各國將採取何種具體軍事或外交手段來落實「維護航行自由」的承諾。
此議題對全球能源供應鏈與物流成本具有直接影響。街口投信分析指出，由於中東航運攻擊頻傳，加上美國天候因素幹擾墨西哥灣能源設施，導致國際油價面臨供應受阻的壓力。若國際戰略海峽遭到關閉或被強行徵收通行費，將導致原油運輸不確定性大增，進而推升全球物流成本。對於高度依賴能源進口的南美洲國家而言，全球航運秩序的穩定與否，直接影響其進口能源的價格與國內通膨壓力，因此南美各國雖未直接參與此次宣告，但其經濟穩定性與全球海運安全息息相關。
不同觀點方面，部分分析人士提醒，不應過度解讀此次60國宣告的立即威懾效果。雖然宣告展現了強大的外交陣線，但面對區域性強權的非對稱威脅（如無人機騷擾或區域性封鎖），多邊宣告是否能轉化為實際的護航行動或經濟制裁，仍存在高度不確定性。此外，各簽署國對於「防禦性倡議」的定義可能存在分歧，部分國家可能傾向於外交斡旋，而另一部分國家則可能主張加強海上軍事部署，過度樂觀地看待宣告效果可能會忽略區域衝突升級的風險。
後續觀察重點在於，該60國聯盟是否會針對荷姆茲海峽或其他關鍵水道提出具體的聯合巡邏計畫，以及伊朗方面對此宣告的反應。此外，國際能源署（IEA）與相關航運組織對於油輪保險費率的調整，將是評估航運風險是否實質降低的重要指標。全球社會需持續關注地緣政治緊張局勢是否會進一步演變為實質的海上封鎖，以及國際油價在供應鏈不確定性下的波動走勢。
