據報導，衣索比亞政府軍及盟友戰士已於2026年10月4日控制該國北部提格雷地區的首府默克萊。此舉發生在領導該地區反對聯盟的提格雷人民解放陣線（TPLF）撤離之後。資料顯示，聯邦部隊及盟友戰士在佔領主要地區機場後，已控制該市。

提格雷人民解放陣線宣布從默克萊撤退，BusinessDay報導指出該組織在臉書上發布聲明證實其撤離。美聯社進一步指出，提格雷人民解放陣線曾於上個月佔領該市，但為避免破壞而選擇撤出。提格雷人民解放陣線的這項戰略性撤退，使得親政府部隊得以重新控制該市。

政府軍重新奪回默克萊，對提格雷人民解放陣線及其領導的反對聯盟而言，是一次重大挫敗。路透社強調，這是提格雷地區持續衝突中的一項重要進展。當地局勢仍不斷變化，國際觀察員正密切關注該地區的發展。

## English edition

Ethiopian government forces and allied fighters have reportedly seized control of Mekelle, the capital of the country's northern Tigray region, on October 4, 2026. This move follows the withdrawal of the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF), which leads the opposition alliance in the region. Federal troops and allied fighters were seen in control of the city after capturing the main regional airport, according to reports.

The TPLF announced its retreat from Mekelle, with BusinessDay reporting a statement posted on Facebook by the group confirming their withdrawal. AP News further indicated that the TPLF had seized the city last month but chose to pull out to avoid destruction. This strategic withdrawal by the TPLF allowed pro-government forces to regain control.

The recapture of Mekelle by government forces represents a major setback for the TPLF and the opposition alliance it leads. Reuters highlighted this as a significant development in the ongoing conflict within the Tigray region. The situation remains fluid, with international observers closely monitoring developments in the area.
