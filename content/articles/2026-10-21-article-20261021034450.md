  # Trump Administration Considers Easing "Shadow Fleet" Sanctions, Sparking International Geopolitical and Economic Controversy
  
  > U.S. President Trump has signed an executive order exempting certain Russian sanctions and is considering relaxing restrictions on the "shadow fleets" of Russia, Iran, and Venezuela to increase shipping capacity, triggering backlash from Ukraine and members of Congress.
  
  On October 16, 2026, U.S. President Trump signed an executive order implementing significant adjustments to sanctions and tariff measures against Russia, just two weeks before the November 3 U.S. midterm elections. According to Secretary of State Marco Rubio, the administration will initiate a 60-day assessment period to review the specific impact of these measures on the global economy. Against this backdrop, Washington is considering further easing restrictions on "shadow fleets" associated with Russia, Iran, and Venezuela, with the intention of increasing the overall capacity of international merchant vessels to alleviate current global shipping pressures.
  
  According to Reuters and various media reports, the Trump administration is considering allowing oil tankers previously subject to sanctions to return to the legal international transport market. The context for this policy adjustment is linked to the rising shipping risks caused by frequent attacks on oil tankers in the Strait of Hormuz. Currently, the focus of internal discussions within the U.S. government is on how to bridge the gap in the global energy supply chain by lifting bans on these sanctioned vessels. Although officials emphasize that this move is intended to optimize shipping capacity, the decision has already caused political ripples in Washington; some Republican lawmakers have expressed concern, and the Ukrainian government has voiced strong opposition to a policy that could weaken the effectiveness of sanctions.
  
  There is currently an information gap regarding the specific implementation details of the "shadow fleet" unbanning. While the Trump administration has released information about the assessment, there is no definitive conclusion yet on whether all relevant companies will receive exemptions, what the criteria for these exemptions will be, or whether these vessels will be subject to additional international oversight after being unbanned. Furthermore, there is a clear divergence in interpretation between Washington and the international community regarding whether the sanctions exemption should be viewed as a major shift in U.S. policy toward Russia or merely a short-term expedient. These uncertainties have complicated market expectations regarding energy prices and shipping insurance costs.
  
  The potential impact of this policy adjustment on Central America and the Caribbean cannot be ignored. As a vital hub for global energy transport, this region is highly susceptible to shocks from changes in international oil tanker shipping policies. Additionally, the Caribbean region has recently suffered severe damage from Hurricane Melissa, and countries such as Cuba, Jamaica, Haiti, and the Dominican Republic are in the midst of post-disaster reconstruction with severely damaged infrastructure. If changes in international shipping policy lead to fluctuations in energy transport costs, it will directly exacerbate the economic burden on these regions during their post-disaster recovery.
  
  At the same time, as a strategic zone connecting North and South America, the stability of Central America's cross-border trade and logistics chains will be directly affected by adjustments to global shipping capacity.
  
  Opinions on the Trump administration's decision are polarized. Supporters argue that against the backdrop of global supply chain restructuring and intensifying geopolitical turmoil, moderately easing capacity restrictions will help stabilize energy prices and align with current U.S. economic interests. However, critics worry that this move will undermine the integrity of the international sanctions system and potentially provide sanctioned countries with opportunities to obtain illicit funds through shadow fleets. Experts warn that at such a sensitive time before the midterm elections, one should not over-interpret the long-term strategic significance of a single executive order, as the final implementation of the policy will depend on the results of the subsequent 60-day assessment and political maneuvering in Congress.
  
  Future observation will focus on the U.S. Department of State's final decision after the 60-day assessment period and the actual effectiveness of the policy in curbing global energy market prices. In addition, whether the diplomatic reactions from Ukraine and other sanctioned countries will prompt the U.S. to adjust its bargaining chips is also a key indicator for observing the direction of geopolitics. For Central America and the Caribbean, the stability of international shipping policy is directly related to the speed of energy supply and economic recovery following the disaster; changes in logistics costs for related industrial chains will be economic data that requires close attention in the coming months.

# 川普政府研擬鬆綁影子船隊制裁，引發國際地緣政治與經濟爭議

> 美國總統川普簽署行政命令豁免部分俄羅斯制裁，並研擬放寬對俄、伊、委「影子船隊」限制以提升運能，引發烏克蘭及國會議員反彈。


美國總統川普於 2026 年 10 月 16 日簽署行政命令，針對俄羅斯的制裁及關稅措施進行重大調整，此舉正值 11 月 3 日美國期中選舉倒數兩週之際。根據國務卿盧比歐（Marco Rubio）說明，政府將啟動為期最多 60 天的評估期，檢視相關措施對全球經濟的具體影響。在此背景下，華府正研擬進一步放寬對俄羅斯、伊朗及委內瑞拉相關「影子船隊」（shadow fleet）的限制，意圖藉此增加國際商船的整體運能，緩解當前全球航運壓力。
根據路透社與多家媒體報導，川普政府考慮允許原本遭到制裁的油輪重新投入合法國際運輸市場。這一政策調整的背景，與近期荷姆茲海峽油輪頻繁遇襲導致的航運風險上升有關。目前，美國政府內部的討論重點在於如何透過解禁這些被列入制裁名單的船隻，以補充全球能源供應鏈的運送缺口。儘管官方強調此舉旨在最佳化運能，但該決策已在華府引發政治漣漪，部分共和黨議員對此表示擔憂，烏克蘭政府亦對此項可能削弱制裁力度的政策表達強烈反對。
目前各界對於「影子船隊」解禁的具體執行細節仍存在資訊落差。雖然川普政府釋出評估訊息，但相關企業是否能全數獲得豁免、豁免的審核標準為何，以及這些船隻在解禁後是否需接受額外的國際監管，目前尚無確切定論。此外，針對制裁豁免是否會被視為美國對俄羅斯政策的重大轉向，抑或僅是短期內的權宜之計，華府內部與國際社會的解讀存在明顯分歧。這些不確定因素使得市場對於能源價格與航運保險成本的預期心理更加複雜。
此項政策調整對中美洲與加勒比海地區的潛在影響不容忽視。該區域作為全球能源運輸的重要樞紐，極易受到國際油輪航運政策變動的衝擊。此外，近期加勒比海地區甫經歷「梅麗莎」（Melissa）颶風的嚴重侵襲，古巴、牙買加、海地與多明尼加共和國等國正處於災後重建階段，基礎設施受損嚴重。若國際航運政策變動導致能源運輸成本波動，將直接加劇這些地區在災後重建期間的經濟負擔。
同時，中美洲作為連線南北美洲的戰略地帶，其跨境貿易與物流鏈的穩定性，將直接受到全球航運運能調整的連動影響。
針對川普政府的此項決策，各方觀點呈現兩極化。支持者認為，在全球供應鏈重組及地緣政治動盪加劇的背景下，適度放寬運能限制有助於穩定能源價格，並符合美國當前的經濟利益；然而，批評者則擔憂此舉將破壞國際制裁體系的完整性，並可能給予受制裁國家透過影子船隊獲取非法資金的空間。專家提醒，在期中選舉前的敏感時刻，不應過度解讀單一行政命令的長期戰略意義，因為政策的最終落實仍需視後續 60 天的評估結果與國會的政治角力而定。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於美國國務院在 60 天評估期後的最終決策，以及該政策對於全球能源市場價格的實際抑制效果。此外，烏克蘭及其他受制裁影響國家的外交反應，是否會促使美國調整其談判籌碼，亦是觀察地緣政治走向的關鍵指標。對於中美洲與加勒比海地區而言，國際航運政策的穩定性，將直接關係到其災後復甦的能源供應與經濟恢復速度，相關產業鏈的物流成本變化，將是未來數月內需高度關注的經濟資料。
