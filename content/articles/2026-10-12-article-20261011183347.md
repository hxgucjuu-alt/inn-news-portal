  # Analysis of North American City Livability Rankings and Regional Population Growth Trends
  
  > According to the latest survey, Canadian cities have performed exceptionally well in North American livability rankings, with Calgary topping the list with a high score. Meanwhile, the North American region continues to experience population growth, with urbanization and social inclusivity becoming core indicators of regional development.
  
  Urban development and demographic changes in North America have long been a focus of global society and public policy. According to a report by The National News, several cities in Canada have been ranked as the most livable in North America due to their high level of social inclusivity, tolerant social atmosphere, and high quality of life. These cities have shown consistent performance across various evaluation indicators, reflecting Canada's long-term investment in urban planning and social welfare policies, which has successfully translated into high public satisfaction and established these cities as benchmarks for urban governance in the North American region.
  
  Among various evaluation data, Calgary, Alberta, has performed the most prominently, receiving a total score of 96 out of 100 in relevant surveys, making it the highest-scoring city in North America. This data has been jointly confirmed by relevant research institutions, indicating that Calgary possesses high competitiveness in areas such as infrastructure, public safety, medical resources, and the socio-cultural environment.
  
  At the same time, according to population data analysis for North America provided by the World Population Review, the North American continent currently occupies approximately 16.28% of the world's land area, while its total population accounts for 7.49% of the global total, demonstrating the region's uniqueness in terms of geography and population density.
  
  Although various data indicate a positive trend in North American urban development, there are subtle differences in the definitions of "livability" and "growth" among different sources. Some surveys focus on indicators of social integration and quality of life, while demographic reports pay more attention to the expansion speed of "megapolitan areas." What remains to be confirmed is whether, with the third-highest population growth rate in the world, major North American cities can maintain their current high levels of livability regarding infrastructure load capacity, housing supply, and the allocation of public service resources; this remains a focus of ongoing debate among scholars and policymakers.
  
  In terms of industry and policy impact, the results of livability rankings directly affect talent mobility and investment willingness. The high scores of Canadian cities give them an advantage in attracting global technical immigrants and multinational corporations. This trend not only promotes the diversification of local economies but also forces North American governments to strike a balance between "rapid population growth" and "maintaining quality of life." If the rate of population growth exceeds the rate of public infrastructure construction, it may put pressure on future social stability and urban operational efficiency, which is a severe test for the urban governance capabilities of all levels of government in North America.
  
  However, the interpretation of livability rankings should be approached with caution. Different survey institutions use different weighting criteria; for example, some emphasize economic growth indicators, while others emphasize environmental sustainability or social inclusivity. Therefore, a single ranking does not represent the full picture of urban development. Furthermore, although the population in North America is growing rapidly, development between regions is extremely uneven, and the gap between urban and rural areas and the disparity between rich and poor remain significant in some regions. Therefore, these data should not be viewed as evidence that all cities in North America are growing synchronously, but rather as reference indicators for regional development, avoiding the oversimplification of complex social structural issues.
  
  Looking ahead, the focus of subsequent observations in North America will be on the interaction between demographic changes and urban carrying capacity. As population data projections for 2026 continue to be updated, whether governments can formulate more resilient public policies for densely populated areas will be the key to determining whether future livability indicators can be maintained. In addition, the maintenance of policies regarding social inclusivity and cultural diversity is also core to Canada and other countries maintaining their urban competitiveness. Future efforts should closely monitor how cities, when facing population pressure, use digital transformation and green infrastructure to solve increasingly complex social and environmental challenges.

# 北美城市宜居性評比與區域人口成長趨勢分析

> 根據最新調查顯示，加拿大城市在北美宜居性排名中表現卓越，卡加利以高分位居榜首。同時，北美地區人口持續增長，城市化程式與社會包容度成為區域發展的核心關注指標。


北美地區的城市發展與人口結構變化，長期以來是全球社會與公共政策關注的焦點。根據《The National News》的報導，加拿大境內多座城市因其高度的社會包容性、寬容的社會氛圍以及優質的生活品質，被評選為北美最宜居的城市。這些城市在各項評比指標中表現穩定，反映出加拿大在城市規劃與社會福利政策上的長期投入，已成功轉化為民眾的高生活滿意度，成為北美區域內城市治理的標竿。
在多項評比資料中，加拿大亞伯達省的卡加利（Calgary）表現最為突出，在相關調查中獲得了高達 96 分（滿分 100 分）的總分，成為北美所有城市中的最高分得主。這一資料獲得了相關研究機構的共同確認，顯示卡加利在基礎設施、公共安全、醫療資源及社會文化環境等方面，均具備極高的競爭力。
與此同時，根據《World Population Review》提供的北美人口資料分析，北美洲目前佔據全球約 16.28% 的土地面積，人口總數則佔全球的 7.49%，顯示出該地區在地理與人口密度上的獨特性。
儘管各項資料顯示北美城市發展呈現正向趨勢，但不同來源對於「宜居」與「成長」的定義存在細微差異。部分調查側重於城市的社會融合度與生活品質指標，而人口統計類報告則更關注於「巨型城市」（Megapolitan areas）的擴張速度。目前尚待確認的是，隨著人口成長率位居全球第三高，北美各大城市在基礎設施負荷能力、住房供應以及公共服務資源分配上，是否能持續維持現有的高宜居水準，這仍是學界與政策制定者持續爭論的焦點。
從產業與政策影響層面來看，宜居城市的評比結果直接影響了人才流動與投資意願。加拿大城市的高評分，使其在吸引全球技術移民與跨國企業進駐時更具優勢。這種趨勢不僅推動了地方經濟的多元化，也迫使北美各國政府必須在「人口快速成長」與「維持生活品質」之間取得平衡。若人口增長速度超過公共基礎設施的建設速度，恐將對未來的社會穩定與城市運作效率造成壓力，這對於北美各級政府的城市治理能力是一項嚴峻的考驗。
然而，對於宜居性排名的解讀應保持審慎。不同調查機構採用的加權標準不同，例如部分強調經濟成長指標，部分則強調環境永續或社會包容度，因此單一排名並不代表城市發展的全貌。此外，北美地區人口成長雖快，但各區域間的發展極不平衡，城鄉差距與貧富懸殊問題在部分地區依然顯著。因此，不應將這些資料視為北美所有城市皆處於同步成長的狀態，而應視其為區域性發展的參考指標，避免過度簡化複雜的社會結構問題。
展望未來，北美地區的後續觀察重點將集中在人口結構變動與城市承載力的互動關係。隨著 2026 年預測人口資料的持續更新，各國政府是否能針對人口密集區域制定更具韌性的公共政策，將是決定未來宜居性指標能否維持的關鍵。此外，針對社會包容度與文化多元性的政策維護，亦是加拿大等國維持其城市競爭力的核心。後續應密切關注各城市在面對人口壓力時，如何透過數位轉型與綠色基礎建設，解決日益複雜的社會與環境挑戰。
