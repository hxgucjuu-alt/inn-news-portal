根據俄羅斯副總理德米特里·切爾內申科（Dmitry Chernyshenko）辦公室發布的聲明，俄羅斯與中國之間的雙邊貿易額預計將在今年底達到創紀錄的2,700億至2,750億美元。相關預測是在雙邊官員舉行工作會談後正式對外公布。

報導指出，切爾內申科於週四與中國駐俄羅斯大使張漢暉舉行了工作會議，雙方重點討論了兩國在貿易、交通運輸以及旅遊等多個領域的雙邊合作情況。會談評估了雙邊經濟交流的進展，並對全年的經貿數據提出了樂觀預期。

切爾內申科辦公室在聲明中進一步表示，兩國預期將持續落實雙方領導人所設定的目標，力爭在2030年使雙邊貿易額達到3,000億美元。這項表態反映出莫斯科與北京當局計畫進一步加強各項產業與基礎建設的經濟聯結。

## English edition

Bilateral trade turnover between Russia and China is projected to reach a record high of $270 billion to $275 billion by the end of the year, according to a statement released by the office of Russian Deputy Prime Minister Dmitry Chernyshenko.

Reports indicate that the forecast follows a working meeting held on Thursday between Chernyshenko and Chinese Ambassador to Russia Zhang Hanhui. During the discussions, both officials reviewed bilateral cooperation across several key sectors, including trade, transportation, and tourism.

In addition to the year-end projections, the deputy prime minister's office noted that both sides expect to fulfill the directive established by their leaders to achieve an annual bilateral trade volume of $300 billion by 2030, underscoring ongoing economic ties between Moscow and Beijing.
