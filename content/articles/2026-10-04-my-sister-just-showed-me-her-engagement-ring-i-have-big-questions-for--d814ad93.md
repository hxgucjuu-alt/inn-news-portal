INN全球新聞報導，Slate Magazine 於 2026 年 10 月 3 日發布了一篇名為「姊妹展示訂婚戒指，我對她的未婚夫有大疑問」的建議專欄文章。這篇文章是其廣受歡迎的「親愛的普魯登斯」（Dear Prudence）系列的一部分，由 Jenée D 撰寫，旨在為讀者提供個人困境的建議。

該專欄文章的核心內容圍繞著一位讀者所面臨的家庭關係挑戰。資料顯示，這位讀者的姊妹展示了她的訂婚戒指，但讀者本人對未婚夫抱有「大疑問」。文章探討了在親人即將步入婚姻時，家人可能產生的疑慮和擔憂。

「親愛的普魯登斯」專欄以其處理複雜人際關係和社會倫理問題而聞名。這篇文章再次證明了該專欄在提供個人生活指導方面的作用，特別是在涉及家庭、愛情和承諾等敏感議題時。

此類建議專欄反映了社會中普遍存在的個人困境，並為讀者提供了一個平台，以尋求外部視角和潛在的解決方案。這篇文章的發布，再次引發了關於家庭成員在婚禮前對伴侶的看法和擔憂的討論。

## English edition

INN Global News reports that Slate Magazine published an advice column titled "My Sister Just Showed Me Her Engagement Ring. I Have Big Questions for Her Fiancé." on October 3, 2026. The article is part of its popular "Dear Prudence" series, authored by Jenée D, and aims to provide advice to readers facing personal dilemmas.

The core content of the column revolves around a family relationship challenge faced by a reader. Data indicates that the reader's sister displayed her engagement ring, but the reader herself harbors "big questions" about the fiancé. The article explores the doubts and concerns family members might have when a loved one is about to enter marriage.

The "Dear Prudence" column is renowned for addressing complex interpersonal relationships and social ethical issues. This article once again demonstrates the column's role in offering personal life guidance, especially when dealing with sensitive topics such as family, love, and commitment.

Such advice columns reflect common personal dilemmas prevalent in society and provide a platform for readers to seek external perspectives and potential solutions. The publication of this article once again sparks discussion about family members' views and concerns regarding partners before marriage.
