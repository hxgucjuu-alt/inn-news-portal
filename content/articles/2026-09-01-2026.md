According to long-term monitoring by the International Crisis Group and the Atlantic Council, the region faces multiple systemic challenges in 2026, including intense turf wars between criminal organizations in Sinaloa, Mexico, which have led to thousands of displacements and casualties.
  
  Meanwhile, research from Chatham House indicates that the region's international relations, political-economic structures, and migration issues are being profoundly affected by the restructuring of global supply chains and great-power competition, leaving governments increasingly strained as they address domestic crises and diplomatic pressures.
  
  Multiple authoritative sources confirm that the region is currently in a highly unstable transitional period. In terms of security, the conflict between criminal organizations within Mexico is no longer merely a local law-and-order issue; it has evolved into a humanitarian crisis that threatens regional stability. The U.S. government continues to exert pressure on this front, demanding that the Mexican government adopt tougher measures to curb the spread of organized crime.
  
  On the geoeconomic front, China's influence in Latin America continues to expand. According to the Inter-American Dialogue, China has utilized its strong narrative capabilities and economic cooperation models to fill the void left by regional countries' disappointment with traditional Western foreign policy. Particularly against the backdrop of concerns raised by the "America First" policy, China's appeal in the region has shown a significant upward trend.
  
  However, there are clear divergences and unresolved issues among sources regarding the path to resolving these crises. The International Crisis Group emphasizes that the Mexican government should leverage the pressure exerted by the U.S. to catalyze efforts against official collusion and corruption, arguing that this is the only way to eradicate organized crime. Yet, in terms of practical implementation, how the government can balance maintaining sovereignty with meeting U.S. security demands remains fraught with variables.

This information is interpreted differently across various sources regarding the strength of U.S. military deterrence.
  
  These geopolitical and security challenges have profound effects within the region. Criminal violence not only causes social unrest but also directly hinders foreign investment and infrastructure development, further exacerbating the fragility of economic structures. Migration, as a chain reaction to crime and economic distress, has become a core issue in diplomatic negotiations between Central American nations and the United States. As the ten key issues defining the region in 2026 gradually emerge, governments are forced to make extremely difficult policy choices within a complex game of protecting domestic industries, managing cross-border migration flows, and seeking survival space between the two major powers of the U.S. and China.

Analyses from Chatham House and the Atlantic Council both caution that the political systems, levels of economic development, and socio-cultural backgrounds of countries in the region vary greatly; the experience of a single country cannot be applied to the entire region. Furthermore, the discourse surrounding "increasing Chinese influence" and "failed U.S. policy" varies significantly in terms of domestic acceptance and actual policy impact within different countries. Over-interpreting a single trend may overlook the agency of regional nations in actively seeking diverse diplomatic strategies.

# 2026年拉丁美洲與加勒比海地緣政治挑戰與安全域性勢分析

> 隨著2026年局勢演變，中美洲與加勒比海地區面臨犯罪組織暴力、國際地緣博弈及地緣政治重塑的多重壓力，區域穩定成為全球關注焦點。


中美洲與加勒比海地區長期處於地緣政治的十字路口，不僅是美洲大陸安全防線的關鍵，更是全球大國影響力角逐的戰場。根據國際危機組織（International Crisis Group）與大西洋理事會（Atlantic Council）的長期監測，該地區在2026年面臨多重系統性挑戰，包括墨西哥錫那羅亞州（Sinaloa）境內激烈的犯罪地盤爭奪戰，導致數千人流離失所與傷亡。
同時，查塔姆研究所（Chatham House）的研究指出，該區域的國際關係、政治經濟結構及移民問題，正受到全球供應鏈重組與大國競爭的深刻影響，使得各國政府在應對內政危機與外交壓力時，顯得愈發捉襟見肘。
多個權威來源共同確認，該地區目前正處於高度不穩定的轉型期。在安全層面，墨西哥境內的犯罪組織衝突已不僅是地方治安問題，更演變為威脅區域穩定的人道危機。美國政府對此持續施壓，要求墨西哥政府採取更強硬的打擊手段，以遏止有組織犯罪的蔓延。
而在地緣經濟層面，中國在拉丁美洲的影響力持續擴大，根據美洲對話組織（Inter-American Dialogue）的觀點，中國透過強大的論述能力與經濟合作模式，填補了部分割槽域國家對傳統西方外交政策的失望感，特別是在「美國優先」政策引發區域疑慮的背景下，中國的吸引力在該地區呈現顯著增長趨勢。
然而，各來源對於危機的解決路徑存在明顯分歧與尚待釐清之處。國際危機組織強調，墨西哥政府應利用美國施加的壓力，轉化為打擊官員勾結與腐敗的動力，認為這是根除有組織犯罪的唯一途徑；然而，實際執行層面上，政府如何在維持主權與滿足美國安全需求之間取得平衡，仍充滿變數。
此外，關於美國軍事力量在區域外的部署與行動，如近期在中東地區的軍事動態（如伊朗相關攻擊事件），雖未直接發生於中美洲，但其對美國全球軍事資源分配的影響，間接牽動了各國對美國在拉美地區安全承諾的信心評估，這部分資訊在不同來源中呈現出對美國軍事威懾力度的不同解讀。
這些地緣政治與安全挑戰對區域內部的影響極為深遠。犯罪暴力不僅造成社會動盪，更直接阻礙了外資投入與基礎建設的發展，進一步加劇了經濟結構的脆弱性。移民問題作為犯罪與經濟困境的連鎖反應，已成為中美洲國家與美國外交談判的核心議題。隨著2026年定義該區域的十大關鍵問題逐漸浮現，各國政府被迫在保護本國產業、管理跨境移民流動，以及在美中兩大強權之間尋求生存空間的複雜棋局中，進行極其艱難的政策選擇。
必須指出的是，外界對中美洲與加勒比海的分析不應過度簡化為單一的「大國角力」或「犯罪失控」。查塔姆研究所與大西洋理事會的分析均提醒，該地區各國的政治體制、經濟發展水平與社會文化背景差異甚大，不能將單一國家的經驗套用於整個區域。此外，所謂「中國影響力增加」與「美國政策失效」的論述，在不同國家內部的接受度與實際政策影響力亦大相徑庭，過度解讀單一趨勢可能忽略了區域內國家主動尋求多元外交策略的自主性。
展望未來，觀察重點將聚焦於墨西哥政府是否能有效清理內部腐敗體系，以及中美洲各國在面對2026年全球經濟不確定性時，如何調整其國際合作框架。此外，美國在拉丁美洲的政策調整是否能從單純的「安全施壓」轉向更具建設性的經濟與治理支援，將是決定該地區未來幾年穩定與否的關鍵。國際社會需持續關注這些跨境公共議題的演變，並審慎評估各項地緣政治變數對區域和平的長期影響。
