Thrustmaster 近日宣布推出其最新款官方授權《跑車浪漫旅 7》T-GT III 賽車方向盤，專為 PlayStation 4 和 PlayStation 5 遊戲機設計。此舉標誌著 Thrustmaster 與 Gran Turismo 長達 16 年合作夥伴關係的「新紀元」，並重申其對遊戲玩家的承諾。T-GT III 不僅是 Thrustmaster 為 Gran Turismo 系列打造的第五款官方授權方向盤，也同時宣布 Thrustmaster 成為 Gran Turismo 世界系列賽的官方合作夥伴。

根據報導，T-GT III 是為 Gran Turismo 設計的第一款「Direct Axial Drive」套裝，也是首款原生整合「load-cell」踏板組的產品。資料顯示，它結合了 T598 伺服底座與 T-GT II 輪圈，並配備了「load-cell」煞車。這款新產品的售價為 749.99 美元，預計將於 12 月 3 日正式上市。

此次發布恰逢 Gran Turismo 世界系列賽在新加坡舉行，Polyphony Digital 也計劃在賽事轉播期間公布《跑車浪漫旅 7》Spec IV 的更多細節。Thrustmaster 強調，T-GT III 旨在提供更真實、更沉浸的賽車體驗，以滿足 Gran Turismo 系列創始人山內一典所啟發的原始願景。

## English edition

Thrustmaster has recently announced the unveiling of its latest officially licensed Gran Turismo 7 T-GT III steering wheel, designed for PlayStation 4 and PlayStation 5 consoles. This launch marks a "new era" in Thrustmaster's 16-year-long partnership with Gran Turismo and reaffirms its commitment to gamers. The T-GT III is not only Thrustmaster's fifth officially licensed wheel for the Gran Turismo series but also coincides with the announcement of Thrustmaster becoming an official partner of the Gran Turismo World Series.

Reports indicate that the T-GT III is the very first "Direct Axial Drive" bundle designed for Gran Turismo, and also the first to natively integrate a "load-cell" pedal set. Data suggests it pairs a T598 Servo Base with the T-GT II rim and features a load-cell brake. This new product is priced at $749.99 and is expected to go on sale on December 3.

The reveal of the T-GT III coincides with the Gran Turismo World Series taking place in Singapore, where Polyphony Digital also plans to disclose more details on Gran Turismo 7's Spec IV during the broadcast. Thrustmaster emphasizes that the T-GT III aims to deliver a more realistic and immersive racing experience, staying true to the original vision inspired by Gran Turismo series creator Kazunori Yamauchi.
