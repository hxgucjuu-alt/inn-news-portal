  # Taipei City Councilor Questions the Suitability and Controversy of Social Welfare Department Commissioner Serving as Fubon Financial Board Director
  
  > The Taipei City Government has recently become embroiled in a political battle over personnel appointments and public governance. On September 1, 2026, Taipei City Councilor Lin Yen-feng held a press conference to publicly disclose that Yao Shu-wen, Commissioner of the Taipei City Department of Social Welfare, is currently serving concurrently as a director of Fubon Financial Holding Co. Combined with recent controversies regarding the Department of Social Welfare's handling of child protection cases, Lin strongly questioned the legitimacy of Yao's professional background and the appointment. This incident has not only sparked public discussion regarding the city government's appointment standards but has also touched upon the boundaries of public officials' responsibilities in managing social safety nets versus corporate governance.
  
  ### Handling of Child Protection Cases Sparks Public Concern
  
  The trigger for this controversy stems from a serious child protection case. According to Councilor Lin Yen-feng, a ten-year-old girl was repeatedly sexually molested at home. Following the report of the case, the city's Department of Social Welfare failed to provide proper placement and protection measures for 240 days, leaving the victim in a high-risk environment for an extended period. Lin criticized Yao Shu-wen for her rigid attitude when facing related inquiries in the city council, noting that she even engaged in verbal altercations with councilors. This response has triggered strong dissatisfaction among the public and media. Critics argue that as the primary official responsible for child protection, the Commissioner of the Department of Social Welfare should demonstrate high levels of professionalism and empathy, rather than adopting a confrontational stance toward democratic oversight.
  
  ### Concerns Regarding the Professionalism of Government-Appointed Director Standards
  
  Beyond the controversy surrounding the child protection case, Lin Yen-feng further disclosed the fact that Yao Shu-wen serves as a director of Fubon Financial and raised serious questions about it. According to public information, the Taipei City Government, as the largest single shareholder of Fubon Financial, is entitled to appoint three directors by convention. Lin pointed out that in the past, the criteria for government-appointed directors typically favored department heads with professional backgrounds in finance or law, such as the Director-General of Budget, Accounting and Statistics, the Commissioner of the Department of Legal Affairs, or the Commissioner of the Department of Finance. The core purpose of these appointments is to ensure the city government's equity interests and to conduct effective risk management for the financial holding company.
  
  However, Yao Shu-wen's background is considered to lack relevant financial or legal professional experience, and this appointment has sparked external questioning regarding the city government's appointment standards. Lin questioned what functions Yao could possibly perform on the Fubon Financial board of directors without a relevant professional background. There is widespread concern that this move may fail to effectively exercise the risk management and supervisory duties expected of a government-appointed director, with some even suggesting political motivations related to "rewarding loyalty." Lin demanded that Mayor Chiang Wan-an come forward to explain the specific standards and considerations behind the appointment.
  
  ### Deep Impacts of Political Conflict and Public Governance
  
  Currently, there are significant differences in focus regarding this incident. Councilor Lin Yen-feng’s position centers on the "mismatch of expertise" and the political motives behind the personnel appointment. On the other hand, the city government has yet to provide a detailed official explanation regarding the specific considerations for this appointment or Yao Shu-wen's actual functions on the Fubon Financial board. Furthermore, regarding the progress of the ten-year-old girl's case, there remains a massive gap between the Department of Social Welfare and the City Council regarding the determination of whether the "placement was timely," a matter that still requires subsequent investigation to clarify.
  
  This personnel controversy has had a potential impact on public governance in Taipei City. If the professionalism of the directors appointed by the city government is questioned, it may affect public trust in the city's asset management and risk control. At the same time, as the primary official responsible for child protection, the Social Welfare Commissioner's attitude in the council and policy execution capability are directly related to the effectiveness of social welfare policy implementation. For Fubon Financial, the professional background of board members is also an important indicator for investors to evaluate corporate governance; the city government's personnel changes may indirectly affect external perceptions of the financial holding company's governance structure.
  
  ### Follow-up Observations and Institutional Review
  
  From different perspectives, those who support the city government's personnel arrangements may argue that department heads participating in corporate boards can strengthen the connection between the public and private sectors. However, opponents emphasize that public officials should focus on their primary duties, especially when social safety net issues such as child protection have yet to be perfected. The public should not simplify this incident into a mere political struggle, but should instead deeply examine the institutional standards for the city government's appointment of government directors, as well as the procedural justice and execution efficiency of the Department of Social Welfare in handling major child protection cases. Over-interpreting Yao Shu-wen's personal motives may obscure substantive discussions on the city government's administrative efficiency and the professionalism of corporate governance.
  
  Follow-up observations will focus on three aspects: First, whether the Taipei City Government will publicly explain the appointment standards for government-appointed directors or adjust relevant personnel configurations; second, whether the City Council's tracking of the Department of Social Welfare's handling of child protection cases will further trigger internal review mechanisms within the city government; and third, whether Fubon Financial's shareholders and market investors will express concerns regarding this personnel arrangement. These developments will continue to affect the Taipei City Government's administrative satisfaction and public evaluation of its governance capabilities. Related progress still requires close attention to council inquiries and the city government's formal responses.

# 臺北市議員質疑社會局長任富邦金董事適任性與爭議

> 臺北市政府近期陷入一場關於人事派任與公共治理的政治攻防。臺北市議員林延鳳於 2026 年 9 月 1 日召開記者會，公開揭露臺北市社會局長姚淑文目前兼任富邦金融控股公司董事一職，並結合近期社會局處理兒少保護案件的爭議表現，強烈質疑其專業背景與職務派任的正當性。此事件不僅引發輿論對於市府人事任命標準的討論，更進一步觸及了公職人員在處理社會安全網與企業治理職責之間的界線問題。


### 兒少保護案件處理引發社會關注
此次爭議的導火線源於一起嚴重的兒少保護案件。據林延鳳議員指出，一名十歲女童在家中多次遭受性猥褻，案件在報案後長達 240 天的時間內，市府社會局卻未能提供妥善的安置與保護措施，導致受害女童長期處於高風險環境中。林延鳳批評，姚淑文在議會面對相關質詢時，態度不僅強硬，甚至多次與議員發生口角，這種應對方式引發了社會大眾與輿論的強烈不滿。外界認為，作為兒少保護的第一線負責人，社會局長應展現高度的專業與同理心，而非以對抗姿態面對民意監督。
### 官派董事派任標準的專業性疑慮
在兒少保護案件的爭議之外，林延鳳進一步披露姚淑文兼任富邦金控董事的事實，並對此提出嚴正質疑。根據公開資訊，臺北市政府作為富邦金控的最大單一股東，依慣例可派任三席董事。林延鳳指出，過去官派董事的派任標準，多由具備財金、法律專業背景的局處首長擔任，例如主計處長、法務局長或財政局長，其核心目的在於確保市府股權權益，並對金控進行有效的風險控管。
然而，姚淑文的背景被認為缺乏相關的財經或法律專業經歷，這項人事任命引發了外界對於市府派任標準的質疑。林延鳳質疑，姚淑文在缺乏相關專業背景的情況下，進入富邦金控董事會究竟能發揮何種職能？外界普遍擔憂，此舉恐無法有效發揮官派董事應有的風險控管與監督職責，甚至有「護主有功」的政治聯想，要求市長蔣萬安必須出面說明具體的派任標準與考量。
### 政治攻防與公共治理的深層影響
目前各界對於此事件的關注點存在顯著分歧。林延鳳議員的立場集中於「專業不對口」以及人事任命背後的政治動機；另一方面，市府對於該項人事任命的具體考量，以及姚淑文在富邦金董事會中的實際職能，目前尚未有官方進一步的詳細說明。此外，針對該名十歲女童案件的處理進度，社會局與議會之間對於「安置是否及時」的認定仍存在巨大落差，這部分仍待後續調查釐清。
此項人事爭議對於臺北市的公共治理產生了潛在影響。若市府派任董事的專業性受到質疑，可能影響公眾對於市府資產管理與風險控管的信任度。同時，社會局長作為兒少保護的第一線負責人，其在議會的應對態度與政策執行力，直接關係到社會福利政策的推動成效。對於富邦金控而言，董事會成員的專業背景亦是投資人評估公司治理的重要指標，市府的人事變動可能間接影響外界對該金控治理結構的觀感。
### 後續觀察與制度化檢視
從不同觀點來看，支援市府人事安排者可能認為，局處首長參與企業董事會可強化公私部門的連結，但反對者則強調公職人員應專注於本職工作，特別是在兒少保護等社會安全網議題尚未完善之際。外界不應僅將此事件簡化為單純的政治鬥爭，而應深入檢視市府派任官派董事的制度化標準，以及社會局在處理重大兒少案件時的程序正義與執行效率。過度解讀姚淑文的個人動機，恐將模糊了對於市府行政效能與公司治理專業性的實質討論。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個面向：第一，臺北市政府是否會針對官派董事的派任標準進行公開說明，或調整相關人事配置；第二，議會對於社會局處理兒少保護案件的追蹤力道，是否會進一步引發市府內部的檢討機制；第三，富邦金控的股東與市場投資人是否會對此人事安排表達疑慮。這些發展將持續影響臺北市政府的施政滿意度及公眾對其治理能力的評價，相關後續進展仍需密切關注議會質詢與市府的正式回應。
