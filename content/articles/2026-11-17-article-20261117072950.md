  # India Strengthens Ties with East Asia and Neighbors as Regional Geopolitical Landscape Continues to Shift
  
  > The Indian government is actively promoting infrastructure and diplomatic connectivity with its neighbors and East Asian nations, aiming to expand its regional influence. Meanwhile, China’s peace principles and foreign policy in Africa have sparked international concern, as the geopolitical and global strategic landscape in Northeast Asia undergoes a complex period of adjustment.
  
  The Indian government has recently announced through official channels that it will continue to expand various forms of connectivity with its neighbors and East Asian countries. According to a report by *Sarkaritel*, the Indian government is committed to strengthening regional infrastructure and policy coordination. This move is seen as part of India's strategic layout in the Indo-Pacific, intended to deepen cooperation with East Asian nations in the fields of economy, diplomacy, and public policy. The report indicates that various Indian government ministries are actively promoting relevant projects, establishing closer ties with East Asian countries to address rapidly changing geopolitical challenges within the region and to further implement its "Act East" policy.
  
  Regarding regional security and foreign policy, the international community remains highly attentive to the intervention strategies of major powers abroad. According to a report by *Asia Times*, the implementation of China's proposed peace principles in Africa is facing a test. The report specifically mentions that the West African nation of Mali abolished the Algiers Peace Agreement signed in 2015 in January 2024, a move that led to the resumption of armed conflict in the northern region. The report analyzes that China's peace initiatives and foreign policy in Africa often struggle to provide substantive stability when faced with complex local military rule and armed conflict, which indirectly influences the international community's assessment of China's geopolitical influence in Northeast Asia and globally.
  
  Regarding the progress of India's connectivity with East Asia, the current focus of various sectors is on the extent of the Indian government's policy implementation and the specific responses from East Asian countries to such connectivity plans. By comparison, regarding the impact of China's policy in Africa, *Asia Times* clearly points out its potential link to the resumption of armed conflict, but public information remains relatively limited concerning subsequent specific responses and mediation plans from the Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Furthermore, whether India's efforts to promote connectivity with East Asia will overlap or conflict with existing regional security frameworks remains a key issue for international political observers to clarify.
  
  Such geopolitical shifts have had a profound impact on Northeast Asia and surrounding regions. India's expanded connectivity with East Asia is expected to promote the integration of industrial supply chains and infrastructure investment within the region, which is an important variable for Northeast Asian economies that rely on regional stability and smooth trade. At the same time, if China's policy dilemmas in Africa lead to a diversion of its diplomatic resources, it could indirectly affect the intensity of its engagement in Northeast Asian regional affairs. Policymakers need to closely observe these cross-regional interactions to assess their potential impact on regional security and economic cooperation frameworks.
  
  Although there are many analyses of the aforementioned geopolitical trends, it must be emphasized that the evolution of international relations is extremely complex, and the impact of a single policy or a single regional event should not be over-interpreted. For example, India's connectivity plans with East Asia are still in the policy promotion and preliminary implementation stage, and their actual effectiveness is limited by the economic conditions and internal political stability of the countries in the region. Similarly, while China's policy setbacks in Africa have sparked international skepticism, this does not mean that its strategic layout in Northeast Asia will immediately undergo a fundamental shift. Analysts should avoid simplifying geopolitical events into black-and-white zero-sum games and must consider the constraints of multilateralism in the current international system.
  
  Subsequent observation should focus on three aspects: First, whether the Indian government will release a more specific list of East Asian cooperation projects in the coming months, and whether East Asian countries will provide formal diplomatic responses to India's initiatives; second, whether the conflict situation in Mali and surrounding areas will further deteriorate, thereby forcing China to adjust its African foreign policy; and finally, the official attitudes of Northeast Asian countries toward the aforementioned geopolitical shifts, particularly regarding shifts in their positions during regional security dialogues. These indicators will help clarify the future development path of regional geopolitics and provide more precise references for relevant industries and policy planning.

# 印度強化與東亞及鄰國連結，區域地緣政治格局持續變動

> 印度政府近期積極推動與鄰國及東亞國家的基礎建設與外交連結，旨在擴大區域影響力。與此同時，中國在非洲的和平原則與外交政策引發國際關注，東北亞地緣政治與全球戰略佈局正處於複雜的調整期。


印度政府近期透過官方管道對外宣示，將持續擴大與鄰國及東亞國家的各項連結。根據《Sarkaritel》的報導，印度政府正致力於強化區域間的基礎設施與政策協調，此舉被視為印度在印太地區戰略佈局的一環，意在提升與東亞國家在經濟、外交及公共政策領域的合作深度。該報導指出，印度政府各部會正積極推動相關計畫，透過與東亞各國建立更緊密的聯絡，以應對區域內快速變動的地緣政治挑戰，並進一步落實其「東向行動」政策。
在區域安全與外交政策的層面上，國際社會對於大國在海外的介入策略保持高度關注。根據《Asia Times》的報導，中國提出的和平原則在非洲地區的實踐面臨考驗。報導特別提到，西非國家馬利在二零二四年一月廢除了二零一五年簽署的《阿爾及爾和平協議》，此舉導致北部地區武裝衝突重啟。該報導分析認為，中國在非洲的和平倡議與外交政策，在面對當地複雜的軍政府統治與武裝衝突時，往往難以提供實質的穩定保障，這也間接影響了國際社會對於中國在東北亞及全球地緣政治影響力的評估。
關於印度與東亞的連結進展，目前各界關注點在於印度政府具體的政策落實程度，以及東亞各國對此類連結計畫的具體回應。相較之下，關於中國在非洲的政策影響，《Asia Times》明確指出其與武裝衝突重啟的潛在關聯性，但對於中國外交部後續的具體回應及調解方案，目前公開資訊仍相對有限。此外，印度政府在推動與東亞連結的過程中，是否會與現有的區域安全架構產生重疊或衝突，仍是國際政治觀察家尚待釐清的關鍵議題。
此類地緣政治的變動對東北亞及周邊地區產生了深遠的影響。印度擴大與東亞的連結，預計將促進區域內的產業供應鏈整合與基礎建設投資，這對於依賴區域穩定與貿易暢通的東北亞經濟體而言，是一個重要的變數。同時，中國在非洲的政策困境，若導致其外交資源分散，可能會間接影響其在東北亞區域事務上的投入力度。政策制定者需密切觀察這些跨區域的互動，以評估其對區域安全與經濟合作架構的潛在衝擊。
儘管各界對上述地緣政治趨勢有諸多分析，但必須強調，國際關係的演變極為複雜，不應過度解讀單一政策或單一地區事件的影響。例如，印度與東亞的連結計畫仍處於政策推動與初步執行階段，其實際成效受限於區域內各國的經濟條件與內部政治穩定性。同樣地，中國在非洲的政策失利，雖引發國際輿論質疑，但並不代表其在東北亞的戰略佈局會立即出現根本性轉變。分析者應避免將地緣政治事件簡化為非黑即白的零和博弈，並需考量多邊主義在當前國際體系中的制約因素。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於三個面向：首先，印度政府在未來幾個月內是否會公佈更具體的東亞合作專案清單，以及各東亞國家是否會針對印度的倡議做出正式外交回應；其次，馬利及周邊地區的衝突局勢是否會進一步惡化，進而迫使中國調整其非洲外交政策；最後，東北亞各國對於上述地緣政治變動的官方態度，特別是在區域安全對話中的立場轉變。這些指標將有助於釐清未來區域地緣政治的發展路徑，並為相關產業與政策規劃提供更精確的參考依據。
