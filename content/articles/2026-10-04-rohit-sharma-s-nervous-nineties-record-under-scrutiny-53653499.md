印度板球運動員羅希特·沙瑪在「緊張九十」分數區間被淘汰的紀錄近期受到審視，報導詳細說明了他將90至99分之間的得分轉化為世紀得分所面臨的挑戰。資料顯示，沙瑪在國際板球所有賽制和印度超級聯賽（IPL）中，共有八次在90分區間被淘汰。這個分數區間被指出是他歷史上最難超越的關卡之一。

對沙瑪完整「緊張九十」紀錄的審查，是由Cricketnews.com於2026年10月3日發布的一篇文章所強調。這項分析特別關注他在國際比賽和競爭激烈的IPL中的表現，在這些比賽中，達到世紀得分對任何擊球手來說都是一個重要的里程碑。

儘管一份報導指出他在國際賽和IPL中共有八次在90分區間被淘汰，但另一份資料則提供了更廣泛的視角。swagcricket.com的資料顯示，羅希特·沙瑪在國際板球中，於80分和90分區間合計被淘汰了15次。這個更廣泛的數字突顯了他國際生涯中多次與三位數得分擦肩而過的情況。

沙瑪以其一旦進入狀態便能打出「大滿貫世紀得分」的能力而聞名，然而他卻多次面臨這個特定的障礙。他在「緊張九十」的紀錄，提供了頂級擊球手在接近重要里程碑時所面臨的壓力和挑戰的見解，說明即使是高產的得分手也可能多次與世紀得分失之交臂。

## English edition

Indian cricketer Rohit Sharma's record of dismissals in the "nervous nineties" has recently come under scrutiny, with reports detailing his challenges in converting scores between 90 and 99 into centuries. According to data, Sharma has been dismissed eight times in the 90s across all formats of international cricket and the Indian Premier League (IPL). This scoreline is noted as one he has historically found hardest to surpass.

The examination of Sharma's complete 'nervous nineties' record was highlighted in an article published by Cricketnews.com on October 3, 2026. This analysis specifically focuses on his performance in both international matches and the highly competitive IPL, where reaching a century is a significant milestone for any batsman.

While one report indicates eight dismissals in the 90s across international and IPL matches, another source presents a broader perspective. Data from swagcricket.com suggests that Rohit Sharma has been dismissed 15 times in the 80s and 90s combined in international cricket alone. This broader figure underscores the numerous occasions he has narrowly missed the three-figure mark in his international career.

Sharma, often celebrated for his ability to score "daddy hundreds" once he gets going, has nonetheless faced this particular hurdle multiple times. His record in the "nervous nineties" offers insight into the pressures and challenges faced by top-tier batsmen as they approach significant milestones, illustrating how even prolific scorers can closely miss out on achieving centuries.
