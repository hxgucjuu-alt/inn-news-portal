  # Global AI Infrastructure Expansion and Cybersecurity Threats: Industry Dynamics and Market Challenges
  
  > As AI technology evolves, global tech giants are actively deploying data centers and cross-system standardization, while simultaneously facing cybersecurity challenges posed by the weaponization of AI and concerns regarding market return on investment.
  
  The artificial intelligence (AI) industry has recently shown a highly active development trend on a global scale. In terms of infrastructure construction, JERA, Japan's largest power generator, is actively negotiating with U.S. tech giants such as Anthropic, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, and Google to build AI data centers near power plants around Tokyo, aiming to address the massive electricity demand required for AI computing.
  
  At the same time, the tech industry is promoting the standardization of cross-system interoperability. For instance, the Model Context Protocol (MCP) proposed by Anthropic has been widely adopted, and giants like Google and Meta are also committed to promoting open standards such as A2UI and PAP to resolve technical barriers in cross-platform communication and data integration.
  
  Upstream in the industry chain, the semiconductor market is also benefiting from the growth in AI demand. In a recent report, U.S.-based foreign investor Citi pointed out that with the increasing demand for AI server power management, coupled with the recovery of the automotive and industrial markets, Vanguard International Semiconductor Corporation's (VIS) interposer and power management IC businesses are expected to become major growth engines. Analysts expect VIS's capacity utilization rate to rise to between 90% and 95% by 2027. These dynamics indicate that despite concerns over interest rate hikes and uncertainties in tariff policies facing the global economy, the expansion of AI infrastructure and hardware supply chains has not ceased.
  
  However, technological progress is accompanied by significant risks. Cybersecurity expert Ed O'Neill recently issued a warning, pointing out that after AI technology is "weaponized," hackers have used automated attack tools to significantly lower the threshold for advanced threats. Currently, multiple U.S. government agencies and private enterprises have suffered large-scale system outages, even affecting Microsoft Office tools, and intelligence agencies such as the National Security Agency (NSA) and the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) are also facing severe challenges. Furthermore, market concerns regarding AI return on investment (ROI) persist, which, combined with interest rate hike expectations and the shadow of tariffs, have put pressure on global stock markets, leading to recent sell-offs in European tech stocks.
  
  Regarding the nature and development of AI, there are differing perspectives in academia and industry. Research in the field of neuroscience continues to explore the essential differences between human consciousness and AI, arguing for the uniqueness of human perception and emphasizing that Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) is still difficult to fully replace human intuition and consciousness at this stage. In terms of technical application, although open standardization helps improve interoperability, the differences in AI deployment strategies among companies and the complexity of cross-border data security management remain key obstacles that must be overcome for future industry development.
  
  Currently, regarding the details of the cooperation between JERA and U.S. tech giants, no specific investment terms or formal agreements have been announced by any party, and the relevant companies have not immediately responded to media reports. At the same time, the cybersecurity threat posed by AI weaponization has evolved into a national-level security issue. How governments will formulate defense policies in the future, and how companies will balance the efficiency and security brought by AI integration, will be key points to observe. Additionally, whether global financial market concerns about an AI bubble will affect long-term capital expenditure will also influence the subsequent layout of the semiconductor and infrastructure industries.
  
  In summary, the global AI industry is at a crossroads of "infrastructure expansion" and "intensifying cybersecurity threats." Future observations should focus on: 1. The balance between large-scale data center power supply and AI computing efficiency; 2. The evolution of government regulations and defense technologies in response to AI-weaponized attacks; 3. The trade-off between open standardization and data privacy protection by tech giants; 4. The verification of the profitability of the AI industry by global capital markets, especially whether investor confidence in the AI hardware supply chain can continue to support capacity expansion plans in a high-interest-rate environment.

# 全球AI基礎設施擴張與資安威脅並行：產業動態與市場挑戰

> 隨AI技術演進，全球科技巨頭積極佈局資料中心與跨系統標準化，同時面臨AI武器化帶來的資安挑戰及市場投資回報的疑慮。


人工智慧（AI）產業近期在全球範圍內呈現高度活躍的發展態勢。從基礎設施建設來看，日本最大發電商JERA正積極與美國科技巨頭如Anthropic、Meta、亞馬遜（Amazon）、微軟（Microsoft）及Google展開洽談，計畫在東京周邊發電廠附近興建AI資料中心，旨在解決AI運算所需的龐大電力需求。
與此同時，科技產業正推動跨系統協同運作的標準化，例如Anthropic提出的模型上下文協定（MCP）已獲廣泛應用，Google、Meta等巨頭亦致力於推動A2UI與PAP開放標準，以解決跨平臺溝通與資料串接的技術門檻。
在產業鏈上游，半導體市場同樣受惠於AI需求的成長。美系外資花旗在最新報告中指出，隨著AI伺服器電源管理需求增加，加上汽車與工業市場復甦，世界先進（VIS）的中介層與電源管理IC業務預計將成為主要成長引擎。分析預期，世界先進的產能利用率可望在2027年提升至90%至95%的水準。這些動態顯示，儘管全球經濟面臨升息疑慮與關稅政策的不確定性，AI基礎設施與硬體供應鏈的擴張腳步並未停歇。
然而，技術進步伴隨著顯著的風險。資安專家Ed O'Neill近期發出警告，指出AI技術遭「武器化」後，駭客利用自動化攻擊工具大幅降低了進階威脅的門檻。目前，美國多個政府機構與民間企業已遭受大規模系統癱瘓，甚至波及微軟Office工具，連國家安全域性（NSA）與中央情報局（CIA）等情報單位亦面臨嚴峻挑戰。此外，市場對於AI投資回報率（ROI）的疑慮持續存在，這與升息預期及關稅陰霾共同對全球股市造成壓力，導致歐洲科技類股近期出現賣壓。
針對AI的本質與發展，學界與產業界亦有不同觀點。神經科學領域的研究持續探討人類意識與AI的本質差異，論證人類感知的獨特性，強調AGI（通用人工智慧）在現階段仍難以完全取代人類的直覺與意識。在技術應用層面，雖然開放標準化有助於提升互通性，但各家企業在AI部署策略上的差異，以及跨國資料安全管理的複雜性，仍是未來產業發展必須克服的關鍵障礙。
目前，關於JERA與美國科技巨頭的合作細節，各方尚未公佈具體的投資條件或正式協議，相關企業亦未針對媒體報導立即回應。同時，AI武器化帶來的資安威脅已演變為國家級安全議題，後續政府將如何制定防禦政策，以及企業如何平衡AI匯入的效率與安全性，將是觀察重點。此外，全球金融市場對AI泡沫化的擔憂是否會影響長期的資本支出，亦將牽動半導體與基礎設施產業的後續佈局。
綜上所述，全球AI產業正處於「基礎建設擴張」與「資安威脅加劇」的十字路口。未來觀察重點應聚焦於：一、大型資料中心電力供應與AI運算效率的平衡；二、各國政府針對AI武器化攻擊的應對法規與防禦技術演進；三、科技巨頭在開放標準化與資料隱私保護之間的權衡；四、全球資本市場對AI產業獲利能力的驗證，特別是在高利率環境下，投資人對於AI硬體供應鏈的信心是否能持續支撐產能擴張計畫。
