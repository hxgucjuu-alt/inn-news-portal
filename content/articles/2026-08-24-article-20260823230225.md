  # Cross-Comparison of Korean Job Platform Data: New Trends Emerge in Seoul Metropolitan Area Job Supply Structure
  
  > Major Korean job platforms and Seoul's official channels have recently released job postings simultaneously, showing that the Seoul metropolitan area labor market is undergoing structural adjustment. The information gap between private platforms and the public employment service system deserves attention.
  
  A noteworthy phenomenon of information integration has recently emerged in the Korean job market. According to cross-comparable public data, including Korea's representative job platform JobKorea (잡코리아), the country's earliest-established recruitment website Incruit (인크루트), Seoul's official employment portal, and the Seoul job listings page on the international business social platform LinkedIn, all have updated a large volume of Seoul metropolitan area employment information within the same time frame. These platforms belong to private enterprises, local governments, and multinational internet services respectively, and their simultaneously released job posting data provides multiple perspectives for observing Korea's current job market.
  
  From the information structure disclosed by each platform, Seoul's employment portal (job.seoul.go.kr) places particular emphasis on the "디딤돌 일자리" (Stepping Stone Jobs) program, which combines professional education with work experience and aims to help job seekers transition into employment at private companies. This public employment service design reflects local governments' proactive transformation in their role as labor market intermediaries—no longer limited to job postings, but further intervening in the complete process of pre-employment training and job matching.
  
  Meanwhile, the JobKorea platform focuses on real-time public recruitment announcements from large corporations, startup evaluations, salary information, and interview reviews, showing that private platforms are attracting job seekers to stay and deepen their usage through more detailed corporate intelligence services.
  
  Cross-comparing the various sources reveals that information supply in the Korean job market has formed a multi-layered system. Seoul's official channels focus on small and medium-sized enterprises (강소기업, meaning excellent SMEs) and public employment support programs; private platforms such as JobKorea and Incruit cover a wide range of positions from large corporations to startups, with added value-added services such as salary comparison and corporate evaluations; LinkedIn's international perspective provides an overview of multinational companies' talent demand in Seoul.
  
  This multi-track information ecosystem, on the one hand, expands the range of choices for job seekers; on the other hand, because each platform's job screening criteria, update frequency, and industry focus differ, the total number and structure of job postings at any given point in time are difficult to compare directly.
  
  Notably, there is a clear information gap among the various sources. LinkedIn indicates more than 7,000 job postings in the Seoul area and emphasizes daily additions; domestic Korean platforms, however, do not provide specific totals, instead attracting users with real-time updates and recommendation features. Although Seoul's official portal integrates resources from district-level employment information centers (such as the Yongsan District Employment Information Center), the degree of overlap between its job types and those on private platforms cannot be confirmed from existing data. Furthermore, each platform's definition of "employment" also differs—official channels may include government-subsidized temporary positions, while private platforms focus on direct corporate hiring—and this discrepancy may lead to significant gaps in statistical figures.
  
  This information structure has concrete implications for both job seekers and policymakers. For job seekers, searching for positions on a single platform may cause them to miss opportunities on other channels; in particular, the linkage mechanism between official employment support programs and private sector job openings often requires additional research to fully grasp. For companies, posting positions across multiple platforms can expand reach but also increases recruitment costs and the burden of resume screening. For the Seoul Metropolitan Government, how to integrate private platform data to grasp the true employment situation and avoid overlap between public resources and private services will be a key challenge for subsequent policy adjustments.
  
  However, the existing data also has clear limitations and should not be over-interpreted. First, the content displayed on each platform's homepage may not reflect the complete job database, and actual job numbers and industry distribution may differ significantly from what is shown on the homepage. Second, the candidate materials do not provide key data such as registered user numbers, active job posting counts, or matching success rates for each platform, making it impossible to assess each platform's actual influence and efficiency. Third, some platforms (such as Clearly Canada) have no direct connection to the Korean job market and only appeared in the candidate materials by coincidence; they should not be included in employment trend analysis.
  
  Finally, any assessment of the overall health of the Korean job market still requires reference to official data such as the employment trends published by Statistics Korea, rather than relying solely on platform information.
  
  Future observation should focus on the following aspects: first, the actual number of people matched through Seoul's "Stepping Stone Jobs" program and the employment retention rate, which are key indicators for evaluating the effectiveness of public employment policy; second, whether domestic platforms such as JobKorea and Incruit will further disclose total job posting volumes and industry distribution data to enhance information transparency; third, whether changes in the penetration rate of international platforms such as LinkedIn in the Korean job-seeking market reflect increases or decreases in multinational companies' talent demand in Seoul; fourth, whether information integration or strategic alliances will emerge among platforms to shorten job seekers' information search time. The above observations will help more accurately grasp the dynamic changes in the Seoul metropolitan area job market.

# 韓國就業平臺資料交叉比對：首都圈職缺供給結構浮現新趨勢

> 韓國主要就業平臺與首爾市官方管道近期同步釋出職缺訊息，顯示首都圈勞動市場正經歷結構性調整，民間平臺與公共就業服務體系之間的資訊落差值得關注。

韓國就業市場近期出現值得注意的訊息整合現象。根據可交叉比對的公開資料，包括韓國代表性求職平臺JobKorea（잡코리아）、國內最早成立的招聘網站Incruit（인크루트）、首爾市官方就業入口網站、以及國際商業社交平臺LinkedIn的首爾職缺頁面，均在同一時間區間內更新了大量首都圈就業資訊。這些平臺分屬民間企業、地方政府與跨國網路服務，其同步釋出的職缺資料，為觀察韓國當前就業市場提供了多角度的切入視野。
從各平臺揭露的資訊結構來看，首爾市就業入口網站（job.seoul.go.kr）特別強調「디딤돌 일자리」（墊腳石就業）計畫，該方案結合專業教育與工作經驗，旨在協助求職者轉入民間企業就業。此一公共就業服務設計，反映出地方政府在勞動力仲介角色上的積極轉型，不再僅止於職缺公告，而是進一步介入職前訓練與就業媒合的完整流程。
與此同時，JobKorea平臺則主打大企業即時公開招聘訊息、新創企業評價、薪資資訊與面試後記等內容，顯示民間平臺正以更細緻的企業情報服務，吸引求職者停留並深化使用。
交叉比對各來源可以發現，韓國就業市場的資訊供給已形成多層次體系。首爾市官方管道側重於中小企業（강소기업，意指優秀中小企業）與公共就業支援方案；JobKorea與Incruit等民間平臺則涵蓋大企業至新創的廣泛職缺，並附加薪資比較與企業評價等加值服務；LinkedIn的國際視角則提供了跨國企業在首爾的人才需求概況。
此一多軌並行的資訊生態，一方面擴大了求職者的選擇範圍，另一方面也因各平臺的職缺篩選標準、更新頻率與產業側重不同，導致同一時間點的職缺總數與結構難以直接互相比較。
值得注意的是，各來源之間存在明顯的資訊落差。LinkedIn標示首爾地區職缺超過七千筆，且強調每日新增；而韓國本土平臺則未提供具體總數，僅以即時更新與推薦功能吸引使用者。首爾市官方入口網站雖整合了區級就業資訊中心（如龍山區就業資訊中心）的資源，但其職缺型別與民間平臺的重疊程度尚無法從現有資料中確認。此外，各平臺對「就業」的定義亦不盡相同，官方管道可能納入政府補助的階段性工作，民間平臺則聚焦於企業直接僱用，此差異可能導致統計數字上的顯著落差。
此一資訊結構對求職者與政策制定者均產生具體影響。對求職者而言，單一平臺的職缺搜尋可能遺漏其他管道的機會，尤其官方就業支援方案與民間企業職缺之間的銜接機制，往往需要額外查詢才能掌握。對企業而言，多平臺刊登職缺雖能擴大觸及面，但也增加招聘成本與履歷篩選負擔。對首爾市政府而言，如何整合民間平臺資料以掌握真實就業狀況，並避免公共資源與民間服務重疊，將是後續政策調整的關鍵課題。
然而，現有資料亦存在明顯限制，不宜過度解讀。首先，各平臺的首頁呈現內容未必反映完整職缺資料庫，實際職缺數量與產業分佈可能與首頁展示有顯著差異。其次，候選素材中未提供各平臺的註冊使用者數、活躍職缺數或媒合成功率等關鍵資料，因此無法評估各平臺的實際影響力與效率。再者，部分平臺（如Clearly Canada）與韓國就業市場無直接關聯，僅因同時出現在候選素材中，不應納入就業趨勢分析。
最後，韓國就業市場的整體景氣判斷，仍需參照韓國統計廳的僱傭動向等官方資料，而非僅依賴平臺資訊。
後續觀察重點應放在以下幾個面向：第一，首爾市「墊腳石就業」計畫的實際媒合人數與就業維持率，此為評估公共就業政策成效的關鍵指標；第二，JobKorea與Incruit等本土平臺是否會進一步公開職缺總量與產業分佈資料，以提升資訊透明度；第三，LinkedIn等國際平臺在韓國求職市場的滲透率變化，是否反映跨國企業在首爾的人才需求增減；第四，各平臺之間是否出現資訊整合或策略聯盟，以縮短求職者的資訊搜尋時間。上述觀察將有助於更準確掌握韓國首都圈就業市場的動態變化。
