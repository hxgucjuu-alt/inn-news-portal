  # Booking Information Chaos at Rio's Ibiza Barra Hotel: International Travelers Should Note Source Discrepancies
  
  > Rio de Janeiro's Ibiza Barra Hotel has recently shown different versions of hotel information across multiple booking platforms and travel websites, including inconsistent descriptions of distance, facilities, and booking offers, raising concerns among international travelers about actual accommodation conditions.
  
  Located in the Barra da Tijuca district of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, Ibiza Barra Hotel is a small hotel primarily marketed as an affordable option. According to information disclosed on platforms such as MapQuest and Booking.com, the hotel is situated at Estrada da Barra da Tijuca 395, facing the Marapendi Canal, approximately 1.9 to 3 kilometers from Praia do Pepe Beach. Basic hotel facilities include air conditioning, a minibar, television, and a private bathroom, with some rooms offering canal views, as well as a 24-hour front desk and a daily breakfast buffet.
  
  Tripadvisor's guest review page emphasizes that the hotel offers free Wi-Fi and a refrigerator, positioning it as a budget accommodation option.
  
  However, there are notable discrepancies in information across platforms. Regarding distance descriptions, MapQuest indicates the hotel is 1.9 miles (approximately 3 kilometers) from Praia do Pepe Beach, while Booking.com directly lists it as 3 kilometers—the two figures are close after conversion, but presented differently. More notably, the hotel's official website, hotelibizabarra.com, claims that booking directly through the official site offers a 10% discount on the first night, with installment payments of up to 10 installments starting from the second night, yet this offer is not mentioned on third-party platforms such as Booking.com, MapQuest, or Tripadvisor.
  
  Additionally, the official website is in Portuguese, which does not present any obvious conflict with the English or Portuguese content on other platforms, but the uniqueness of the promotional information may influence travelers' booking decisions.
  
  The chaotic information situation at this hotel reflects a common problem of information asymmetry in international bookings. For international travelers planning to visit Rio de Janeiro, discrepancies in price, distance, and facility descriptions across different platforms may lead to actual stay experiences not matching expectations. In particular, the discounts and installment payment plans offered on the official website may cause travelers to miss out on deals if they do not confirm directly with the hotel, or conversely, to question prices on third-party platforms. Furthermore, the hotel is located across from the Marapendi Canal, at a certain distance from major beaches and tourist attractions, so transportation convenience and the surrounding environment should also be taken into consideration.
  
  From different perspectives, the information gap between the hotel's official website and third-party booking platforms may stem from marketing strategies or inconsistent platform update schedules. The official website emphasizes direct booking incentives, aiming to reduce reliance on third-party platforms and lower commission costs; while platforms such as Booking.com and MapQuest provide standardized hotel information that may not promptly update all details. Tripadvisor guest reviews offer actual stay experiences, but the number and time distribution of reviews are unclear, making it impossible to confirm their representativeness. Therefore, when travelers refer to this information, they should be aware that a single source may not fully present the actual situation and should not make judgments based solely on one platform's description.
  
  Information still pending confirmation includes: whether the hotel actually provides the discounts and installment payment plans described on the official website, the actual walking time to the beach, the connection quality and coverage of the free Wi-Fi, the content and serving hours of the breakfast buffet, and whether the actual configuration of the in-room refrigerator and minibar matches the description. Additionally, whether the prices displayed on each platform include taxes or breakfast is not consistently stated. These unresolved details may affect travelers' budget planning and accommodation experience.
  
  Future observation should focus on whether the hotel unifies its information presentation across platforms and whether the official website's promotional offers remain valid. For travelers planning to stay, it is recommended to confirm the latest room rates and facility status directly through the hotel's official channels, and to cross-reference multiple platforms to reduce the risk of information gaps. At the same time, monitoring whether new guest feedback appears on review platforms such as Tripadvisor will help verify whether the hotel's actual service quality matches its descriptions. As international travel gradually recovers, the information transparency of small hotels like this will become an important reference indicator for travelers when choosing accommodations.

# 裡約Ibiza Barra Hotel訂房資訊混亂 跨國旅客需注意來源差異

> 巴西里約熱內盧Ibiza Barra Hotel近期在多家訂房平臺與旅遊網站出現不同版本的飯店資訊，包括距離、設施與訂房優惠等描述不一，引發跨國旅客對實際住宿條件的疑慮。


位於巴西里約熱內盧巴拉達蒂茹卡區的Ibiza Barra Hotel，是一間以經濟實惠為主要賣點的小型飯店。根據MapQuest與Booking.com等平臺揭露的資訊，該飯店坐落於Estrada da Barra da Tijuca 395號，正對著Marapendi運河，距離Praia do Pepe海灘約1.9至3公里不等。飯店基本設施包括空調、迷你吧、電視與私人衛浴，部分房間可欣賞運河景觀，並提供24小時接待櫃臺與每日早餐自助餐。
Tripadvisor的旅客評價頁面則強調該飯店提供免費Wi-Fi與冰箱，定位為經濟型住宿選擇。
然而，各平臺之間存在明顯的資訊差異。距離描述方面，MapQuest標示飯店距離Praia do Pepe海灘1.9英里（約3公里），而Booking.com則直接標示為3公里，兩者雖換算後接近，但呈現方式不同。更值得留意的是，飯店官方網站hotelibizabarra.com宣稱透過官網直接訂房可享首晚10%折扣，且第二晚起可分期付款最多10期，但此優惠方案並未在Booking.com、MapQuest或Tripadvisor等第三方平臺上提及。
此外，官方網站使用的語言為葡萄牙語，與其他平臺的英文或葡萄牙語內容並無明顯衝突，但優惠資訊的獨特性可能影響旅客的訂房決策。
這間飯店的資訊混亂情況，反映了跨國訂房時常見的資訊不對稱問題。對於計畫前往裡約熱內盧旅遊的國際旅客而言，不同平臺之間的價格、距離與設施描述差異，可能導致實際入住體驗與預期不符。特別是官方網站提供的折扣與分期付款方案，若旅客未直接向飯店確認，可能錯失優惠，或反過來對第三方平臺上的價格產生質疑。此外，飯店位於Marapendi運河對面，距離主要海灘與觀光景點有一定距離，交通便利性與周邊環境也應納入考量。
從不同觀點來看，飯店官方網站與第三方訂房平臺之間的資訊落差，可能源於行銷策略或平臺更新時程不一。官方網站強調直接訂房的優惠，旨在減少對第三方平臺的依賴，降低佣金成本；而Booking.com與MapQuest等平臺則提供標準化的飯店資訊，可能未即時更新所有細節。Tripadvisor的旅客評價則提供實際入住經驗，但評價數量與時間分佈不明，無法確認其代表性。因此，旅客在參考這些資訊時，應注意單一來源可能無法完整呈現真實情況，不宜僅憑單一平臺描述即做出判斷。
尚待確認的資訊包括：飯店是否確實提供官方網站所述的折扣與分期付款方案、距離海灘的實際步行時間、免費Wi-Fi的連線品質與覆蓋範圍、早餐自助餐的內容與供應時間，以及房間內冰箱與迷你吧的實際配置是否與描述一致。此外，各平臺顯示的價格是否含稅、是否包含早餐，也未有一致說明。這些未明朗的細節，可能影響旅客的預算規劃與住宿體驗。
後續觀察重點應放在飯店是否統一各平臺的資訊呈現，以及官方網站的優惠方案是否持續有效。對於計畫入住的旅客，建議直接透過飯店官方管道確認最新房價與設施狀態，並參考多家平臺的交叉比對，以降低資訊落差帶來的風險。同時，觀察Tripadvisor等評價平臺是否出現新的旅客反饋，有助於驗證飯店實際服務品質與描述是否相符。在跨國旅遊逐漸恢復之際，此類小型飯店的資訊透明度，將成為旅客選擇住宿時的重要參考指標。
