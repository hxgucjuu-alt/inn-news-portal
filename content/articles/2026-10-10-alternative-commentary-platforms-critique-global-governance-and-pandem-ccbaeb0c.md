根據 Activist Post 及多家轉載網站發布的評論文章，部分獨立評論者正針對國際組織與全球治理機構的作用提出質疑。文章提及世界經濟論壇（WEF）發布的相關白皮書，批評該機構主張由慈善機構和基金會擔任自然環境守護者的觀點，並將此類全球治理倡議解讀為非經選舉產生的機構試圖擴大對土地、海洋與公共資源的影響力。

報導同時指出，評論內容回顧了自2020年爆發的公共衛生危機，聲稱當時各國政府與機構的防疫訊息傳播啟動了大眾的恐懼本能與應激心理機制。評論作者對當局與傳統機構的公信力提出質疑，認為公眾在面對政府與金融系統的多起爭議後，不應盲目相信官方敘事。

資料顯示，此類針對公共衛生措施與全球治理的評論被 Freedoms Phoenix 及 SGT Report 等多個平台轉載。相關內容反映出部分社群對國際組織倡議以及國家層面危機管理仍抱持高度懷疑態度，呼籲公眾重新審視自身對既有體制的信任。

## English edition

According to commentary pieces published by Activist Post and syndicated across several websites, alternative commentators are raising sharp criticisms against international organizations and their governance models. The commentary points to a recent World Economic Forum (WEF) white paper, taking issue with its proposition that philanthropies and foundations should act as stewards of nature, and arguing that such initiatives represent an overreach of influence by unelected global institutions.

The reports also reflect on the public health crisis that began in 2020, alleging that the messaging surrounding the pandemic leveraged fear to trigger survival instincts in the general public. The author questions the continued institutional credibility of governments and traditional systems, arguing against uncritical trust in official narratives following past controversies and policy decisions.

According to available records, these viewpoints were republished across platforms such as Freedoms Phoenix and SGT Report. The commentaries highlight an ongoing skepticism within certain communities toward both international organizational initiatives and state-level crisis management mechanisms.
