莫斯科（Sputnik）報導指出，一名涉嫌在FlyDubai航班上襲擊同事的飛行員，已承認他意圖將飛機墜毀在以色列。這項消息引述了消息來源，揭示了這起發生在2026年10月1日左右的航空事件背後的駭人意圖。該航班原定從杜拜飛往以色列，卻因機上衝突而被迫緊急迫降。

根據其他媒體報導，這起事件涉及一名阿曼籍副駕駛，他在飛行途中持刀襲擊了機長，導致飛機一度急劇下降。隨後，機組人員與乘客合力制服了襲擊者，並成功將飛機轉降至沙烏地阿拉伯。調查人員正努力釐清這名飛行員的確切動機。

以色列總理班傑明·納坦雅胡（Benjamin Netanyahu）對此事件發表評論，報導指出他聲稱該名副駕駛曾接受「激進灌輸」，並有自殺傾向。這些說法為這起航空安全事件增添了複雜的背景，引發了國際社會對飛行員心理健康和潛在激進化風險的廣泛關注。

事件發生後，FlyDubai航空公司已宣布暫停所有飛往以色列的航班，以應對這起嚴重的安全危機。各國當局正密切合作，對此事件進行全面調查，以確保未來航空旅行的安全，並防止類似事件再次發生。

## English edition

MOSCOW (Sputnik) reports indicate that a pilot suspected of assaulting a colleague on a FlyDubai flight has admitted his intent to crash the plane in Israel. This information, citing sources, reveals a chilling motive behind the aviation incident that occurred around October 1, 2026. The flight, originally bound from Dubai to Israel, was forced to make an emergency landing due to the onboard conflict.

According to other media outlets, the incident involved an Omani co-pilot who reportedly stabbed the captain during the flight, causing the aircraft to plummet dangerously. Crew members and passengers subsequently subdued the assailant, and the plane was successfully diverted to an emergency landing in Saudi Arabia. Investigators are working to determine the precise motives of the pilot.

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu commented on the incident, with reports indicating he claimed the co-pilot had undergone "radical indoctrination" and was suicidal. These statements add a complex layer to the aviation security event, sparking widespread international concern over pilot mental health and the risks of potential radicalization.

Following the incident, FlyDubai airline announced the suspension of all flights to Israel in response to this severe security crisis. Authorities from various nations are collaborating closely to conduct a comprehensive investigation into the event, aiming to ensure the safety of future air travel and prevent similar occurrences.
