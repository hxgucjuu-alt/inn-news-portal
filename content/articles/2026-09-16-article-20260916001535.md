  # The Controversy Over the Politicization of Global Public Health: China Accused of Using "Dollar Diplomacy" to Block Taiwan’s Participation in the WHA
  
  > Recent political maneuvering within the global public health system has surfaced, drawing significant international attention. According to media reports, internal documents allegedly from the Chinese diplomatic system were exposed on September 7, 2026. The contents reveal that prior to this year's World Health Assembly (WHA), China employed highly organized diplomatic tactics aimed at obstructing Taiwan’s participation in international medical and health cooperation. The core controversy of this incident lies in whether global public health issues have been politicized, and how China's diplomatic apparatus has used specific methods to compress Taiwan's international space.
  
  According to cross-referenced reports from multiple media outlets, the leaked documents indicate that before the opening of the WHA, China’s diplomatic system prepared arguments and draft joint letters opposing Taiwan’s participation, and mobilized allies to sign them. This operational model is described as a complete "chain": China would issue the demand to block Taiwan, followed by allies signing and submitting the joint letters, ultimately achieving the goal of excluding Taiwan from the WHA. Reports point out that this process was accompanied by China providing substantial donations to the relevant allies, sparking strong public skepticism regarding "dollar diplomacy" and "political manipulation."
  
  Currently, information regarding this incident is primarily concentrated on the leaked documents disclosed by the media and comments from political figures. DPP Legislator Chen Kuan-ting publicly stated that if the contents of the diplomatic files are true, this is not only a suppression of Taiwan’s international participation but also a serious "international scandal" that politicizes global public health issues and severely hinders the fairness of international medical cooperation. However, as of now, Chinese officials have not responded to the authenticity or content of these documents, and the details of the diplomatic operations and the flow of funds remain subject to further international verification.
  
  This incident has profound implications for the global public health sector. As an important part of the international medical system, if Taiwan is excluded from the WHA due to political factors, it will create gaps in regional epidemic information exchange, infectious disease monitoring, and the coordination of public health policies. Particularly in regions like South America, which have faced diverse public health challenges in recent years, if international medical cooperation mechanisms are obstructed by the political maneuvering of major powers, it could weaken global resilience in responding to public health emergencies and affect international exchanges and scientific research cooperation in the medical industry.
  
  There are divergent views in academia and political circles regarding such incidents. Perspectives supporting Taiwan’s participation argue that public health knows no borders and that any political blockade violates the World Health Organization’s (WHO) mission to ensure health for all. However, some international relations observers caution that in the current complex geopolitical environment, the sources and political motivations of such diplomatic documents must be carefully evaluated to avoid over-interpreting the impact of a single event on the global health governance structure. They suggest that the boundaries between decision-making mechanisms within international organizations and diplomatic maneuvering should be clarified to prevent information from being used in a one-sided manner.
  
  Future observation will focus on the international community’s reaction to China’s diplomatic tactics and whether the Taiwanese government will take further international lobbying or legal action against such diplomatic blockades. Additionally, whether subsequent WHA meeting decisions will be affected by this incident, leading to adjustments in participation mechanisms for non-member states, is also a key point of concern for global public health observers. As global demands for information transparency and the impartiality of international organizations continue to rise, this type of controversy involving the politicization of public health is expected to continue fermenting in international forums.
  
  Beyond public health governance, discussions on information transparency and technical regulation are also heating up globally. For example, tech giant Meta recently pushed a firmware update for its AI smart glasses aimed at blocking a bypass vulnerability that allowed users to "secretly record" by covering the privacy indicator light. Such technical privacy protections and the issue of diplomatic transparency in international politics together form the complex landscape of current global governance. When public health cooperation faces the challenge of political blockades while simultaneously needing to address the privacy risks posed by emerging technologies, the international community’s need for fair, transparent, and resilient collaborative mechanisms becomes increasingly urgent.
  
  Furthermore, despite the challenging international political environment, cross-border cooperation in medical research continues to advance. For instance, the anti-cancer drug Pidnarulex (CX-5461) from Senhwa Biosciences, supported by the U.S. National Cancer Institute (NCI) program, has completed its first clinical enrollment in combination with a major international immune checkpoint inhibitor. Such scientific achievements demonstrate that even under the shadow of political maneuvering, the global medical industry remains committed to seeking breakthroughs in treating intractable diseases through multinational clinical validation. These developments contrast with the political controversy of the WHA, highlighting the ongoing tug-of-war between scientific cooperation and political blockades.

# 全球公衛政治化爭議：中國遭指控透過金錢外交封鎖臺灣參與WHA

> 近期關於全球公共衛生體系的政治角力浮上檯面，引發國際社會高度關注。根據媒體報導，疑似中國外交體系的內部密件於2026年9月7日曝光，內容揭露中國在今年世界衛生大會（WHA）開幕前，採取了高度組織化的外交手段，旨在阻撓臺灣參與國際醫療衛生合作。此事件的核心爭議在於全球公共衛生議題是否遭到政治化，以及中國外交體系如何透過特定手段壓縮臺灣的國際空間。


根據多方媒體交叉報導，該份密件內容顯示，中國外交體系在WHA開幕前，預先準備了反對臺灣參與的論述與聯名信草稿，並動員友邦進行簽署。這一運作模式被描述為一套完整的「鏈條」：由中方提出封鎖臺灣的要求，隨後由友邦簽署聯名信並送出，最終達成在WHA封殺臺灣的目標。報導指出，此過程伴隨著中方對相關友邦提供高額捐款的運作，引發輿論對於「金錢外交」與「政治操弄」的強烈質疑。
目前關於此事件的資訊，主要集中於媒體披露的密件內容與政界人士的評論。民進黨立委陳冠廷公開表示，若相關外交檔案內容屬實，這不僅是對臺灣國際參與的打壓，更是一起嚴重的「國際醜聞」，將全球公共衛生議題政治化，嚴重阻礙了國際醫療合作的公平性。然而，截至目前為止，中國官方尚未針對此份密件的真實性或內容做出回應，相關外交運作的細節與資金流向仍有待進一步的國際查證。
此事件對全球公共衛生領域的影響深遠。臺灣作為國際醫療體系的重要一環，若因政治因素被排除在WHA之外，將導致區域防疫資訊交換、傳染病監控及公共衛生政策協調出現缺口。特別是南美等地區，近年來面臨多元的公共衛生挑戰，若國際間的醫療合作機制因大國政治博弈而受阻，將可能削弱全球應對突發公共衛生事件的韌性，並影響醫療產業的國際交流與科研合作。
針對此類事件，學界與政界觀點存在分歧。支援臺灣參與的觀點認為，公共衛生無國界，任何政治封鎖均違反世界衛生組織（WHO）保障全民健康的宗旨；然而，部分國際關係觀察家則提醒，在當前複雜的地緣政治環境下，需審慎評估此類外交密件的來源與政治動機，避免過度解讀單一事件對全球衛生治理結構的衝擊，並應釐清國際組織內部決策機制與外交角力之間的界線，以防資訊被片面利用。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於國際社會對於中國外交手段的反應，以及臺灣政府是否會針對此類外交封鎖採取進一步的國際遊說或法律行動。此外，WHA後續的會議決策是否會受到此次事件影響，進而調整對非成員國參與機制，亦是全球公衛觀察家關注的重點。隨著全球對資訊透明度與國際組織公正性的要求日益提高，這類涉及公共衛生政治化的爭議，預計將持續在國際論壇中發酵。
在公共衛生治理之外，全球對於資訊透明與技術監管的討論亦同步升溫。例如，科技巨頭Meta近期針對AI智慧眼鏡推送韌體更新，旨在防堵使用者透過遮蔽隱私提示燈進行「偷拍」的旁路漏洞。此類技術層面的隱私防護與國際政治中的外交透明度議題，共同構成了當前全球治理的複雜圖景。當公共衛生合作面臨政治封鎖的挑戰，同時又需應對新興科技帶來的隱私風險時，國際社會對於建立公平、透明且具備韌性的協作機制，顯得愈發迫切。
此外，儘管國際政治環境充滿挑戰，醫療科研的跨國合作仍持續推進。例如，生華科旗下抗癌新藥Pidnarulex（CX-5461）在美國國家癌症研究所（NCI）計畫支援下，已與國際重磅免疫檢查點抑制劑完成首例臨床收案。這類科研成果顯示，即便在政治博弈的陰影下，全球醫療產業仍致力於透過跨國臨床驗證，尋求突破難治性疾病的治療瓶頸。這些發展與WHA的政治爭議形成對比，凸顯了科學合作與政治封鎖之間持續拉鋸的現狀。
