  # Acer Deepens Global Talent Strategy and MediaTek Achieves Breakthrough in Flagship Chip Technology
  
  > Acer is cultivating interdisciplinary young talent through its summer internship program to strengthen global competitiveness, while MediaTek has announced the Dimensity 9600 Pro flagship platform, expected to debut in OPPO's new smartphone, highlighting Taiwan's dual focus on talent and hardware technology in the tech industry.
  
  The global technology industry continues to advance in the fields of talent cultivation and chip hardware technology. Recently, iconic Taiwanese companies Acer and MediaTek have made progress in human resources development and semiconductor technology applications, respectively. On September 17, 2026, Acer announced the completion of this year's summer internship program, which aims to enhance the company's global competitiveness by introducing talent from diverse backgrounds. Simultaneously, MediaTek recently announced its next-generation flagship mobile platform, the "Dimensity 9600 Pro," and confirmed that it will be featured in the new OPPO flagship model, the "Find X10 Pro Max," which is scheduled for an official launch on September 22.
  
  According to data released by Acer, the summer internship program attracted 2,508 applications, with 11 interns ultimately selected. The backgrounds of the selected students span eight major fields, including music and arts, politics and international relations, business administration, health services management, agricultural economics, industrial design, information engineering, and business analytics, demonstrating that Acer is actively moving toward interdisciplinary and diverse professional recruitment strategies. Acer stated that the program includes a comprehensive development mechanism, featuring department project participation, interaction with senior executives, and international learning opportunities, aimed at helping students connect with the industry early, cultivate practical skills and an international perspective, and inject innovative energy into the company.
  
  In terms of hardware technology, MediaTek's official release of the Dimensity 9600 Pro flagship platform has become a focus of industry attention. According to reports, the chip utilizes advanced 2nm process technology and features 33 billion transistors, demonstrating powerful computing capabilities and performance potential. OPPO Vice President Wu Henggang has publicly announced that the company's flagship model, the OPPO Find X10 Pro Max, will be among the first devices to be equipped with this platform. Although Acer's internship program and MediaTek's chip release belong to different industrial categories, both reflect the high importance Taiwanese tech companies place on technology R&D and talent reserves when facing global market competition, attempting to consolidate their market positions through a dual approach of software and hardware.
  
  However, there are still some discrepancies and details awaiting confirmation regarding the aforementioned information. In Acer's internship program, although it is clearly stated that 11 interns were admitted, there is currently no further public information regarding the retention rate or specific project outcomes for these 11 interns after the completion of their internships.
  
  Regarding the collaboration between MediaTek and OPPO, while it is confirmed that the Dimensity 9600 Pro will debut in the OPPO Find X10 Pro Max, details such as the specific launch dates and pricing strategies for the phone in different regional markets, as well as the chip's energy efficiency performance in real-world application scenarios, still await the test data and official explanations following the September 22 launch event. One should not over-extrapolate its market performance based solely on existing specifications.
  
  For relevant industries and regions, Acer's interdisciplinary talent cultivation strategy reflects the increasingly urgent need for talent with diverse thinking in the digital transformation process of global technology companies, which will indirectly influence the talent cultivation direction of higher education. On the other hand, MediaTek's advancement in 2nm process chips directly impacts the competitive landscape of the global flagship smartphone market. With the increasing demand for artificial intelligence and high-performance computing, the miniaturization of chip processes and the increase in transistor counts will become key factors in determining the future performance and market competitiveness of mobile devices. These developments are not limited to individual companies but reflect the trend of Taiwan's deep integration from hardware manufacturing to technology R&D within the global supply chain.
  
  Future observations will focus on several aspects: First, whether the talent cultivated by Acer through its interdisciplinary internship program can effectively translate into an internal driving force for innovation and increase its market share in the global PC and related industries; second, whether the performance of the MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro in actual end-user products can create a gap with competitors in the high-end mobile phone market and drive sales growth for partners like OPPO. In addition, whether the global tech industry's demand for cross-disciplinary talent will become the norm, and the yield and supply stability of 2nm process chips after mass production, are all key indicators worth tracking over the coming months.

# 宏碁深化全球人才佈局與聯發科旗艦晶片技術突破進展

> 宏碁透過暑期實習計畫培育跨領域青年人才以強化全球競爭力，同時聯發科發布天璣9600 Pro旗艦平臺，預計由OPPO新機首發搭載，展現臺灣科技產業在人才與硬體技術的雙重佈局。


全球科技產業在人才培育與晶片硬體技術領域持續推進，近期臺灣指標性企業宏碁（Acer）與聯發科（MediaTek）分別在人力資源發展與半導體技術應用上有所進展。宏碁於2026年9月17日宣佈完成本年度暑期實習計畫，該計畫旨在透過多元背景人才的引入，提升企業的全球競爭力；與此同時，聯發科則於近期發布新一代旗艦級行動平臺「天璣9600 Pro」，並確認將由OPPO新款旗艦機型「Find X10 Pro Max」首批搭載，預計於9月22日正式發布。
根據宏碁官方公佈的資料，本次暑期實習計畫共吸引2,508人次投遞履歷，最終錄取11位實習生。獲選學生背景涵蓋音樂藝術、政治與國際關係、企業管理、醫務管理、農業經濟、工業設計、資訊工程及商業分析等八大領域，顯示宏碁在人才招募策略上，正積極向跨領域與多元專業靠攏。宏碁表示，該計畫規劃了完整的培育機制，包含部門專案參與、高階主管交流及跨國學習機會，旨在讓學生提前接軌產業，培養實務能力與國際視野，進而為企業注入創新能量。
在硬體技術方面，聯發科正式發布的天璣9600 Pro旗艦平臺，成為產業關注焦點。根據報導，該晶片採用先進的2nm製程技術，並具備330億個電晶體，展現出強大的算力與效能潛力。OPPO副總裁吳恆剛已公開宣佈，旗下旗艦機型OPPO Find X10 Pro Max將成為首批搭載此平臺的裝置。儘管宏碁的實習計畫與聯發科的晶片發布屬於不同產業範疇，但兩者皆體現了臺灣科技業者在面對全球市場競爭時，對於技術研發與人才儲備的高度重視，試圖透過軟硬體雙管齊下鞏固市場地位。
然而，目前關於上述資訊仍存在部分細節差異與待確認之處。在宏碁的實習計畫中，雖然明確指出錄取人數為11人，但針對這11位實習生在實習結束後的留任率或具體專案成效，目前尚未有進一步的公開資料。
而在聯發科與OPPO的合作案中，雖然確認了天璣9600 Pro將由OPPO Find X10 Pro Max首發，但關於該款手機在不同區域市場的具體上市時間、售價策略，以及該晶片在實際應用場景中的能耗比表現，仍需等待9月22日發布會後的實測資料與官方說明，不應僅憑現有規格資料過度推論其市場表現。
對於相關產業與地區而言，宏碁的跨領域人才培育策略，反映了全球科技企業在數位轉型過程中，對於具備多元思維人才的需求日益迫切，這將對高等教育的人才培育方向產生間接影響。另一方面，聯發科在2nm製程晶片上的推進，則直接影響全球智慧型手機市場的旗艦機競爭格局。隨著人工智慧與高效能運算需求增加，晶片製程的微縮與電晶體數量的提升，將成為決定未來行動裝置效能與市場競爭力的關鍵。這些發展不僅限於單一企業，更反映出臺灣在全球供應鏈中從硬體製造向技術研發深度整合的趨勢。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於幾個面向：首先，宏碁透過跨領域實習計畫所培育的人才，未來是否能有效轉化為企業內部的創新驅動力，並提升其在全球PC及相關產業的市佔率；其次，聯發科天璣9600 Pro在實際終端產品上的效能表現，是否能在高階手機市場中與競爭對手拉開差距，並帶動OPPO等合作夥伴的銷售成長。此外，全球科技產業對於人才的跨界需求是否會形成常態，以及2nm製程晶片在量產後的良率與供應穩定性，皆是未來幾個月內值得持續追蹤的關鍵指標。
