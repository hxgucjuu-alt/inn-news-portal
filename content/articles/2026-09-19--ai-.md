  # Silicon Valley Venture Capital Shifts Toward Deep Tech and AI Hardware; Musk Predicts Grok 5 Will Achieve AGI
  
  > Global venture capital is flooding into deep tech sectors such as semiconductors and robotics to support AI infrastructure demands. Meanwhile, Elon Musk has announced that Grok 5 will become the realization of AGI, marking a new phase in the AI computing power arms race.
  
  In mid-September 2026, the global artificial intelligence (AI) industry reached a dual turning point in both technology and capital deployment. Regarding the capital market, Silicon Valley venture capital firms are shifting their focus from pure software applications to "Deep Tech" sectors, such as semiconductors and robotics, involving billions of dollars. According to market observations, these hardware technologies, which require long-term research and development and are capital-intensive, are now viewed as the core infrastructure supporting the operation of large language models (LLMs) and future AI development.
  
  On the industrial application side, xAI founder Elon Musk publicly released the latest technical roadmap via social media on September 17, officially predicting that Grok 5 will feature 2.5 trillion parameters and claiming that the model will serve as the final destination for achieving Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).
  
  Facts confirmed by multiple sources indicate that the momentum of AI industry development has shifted entirely from simple software development to an extreme pursuit of computing power and hardware infrastructure. Silicon Valley venture capital data shows that hardware companies have accounted for nearly one-third of total U.S. venture capital allocation this year, primarily because the training and inference processes of LLMs have led to an exponential increase in demand for advanced chips and data center hardware. At the same time, Musk revealed xAI's technical evolution strategy, emphasizing that Grok 5 will fully transition its underlying architecture to a C++ software stack, aiming to squeeze out hardware performance to meet massive computing demands—a move that aligns with the trend of Silicon Valley capital flowing into hardware infrastructure.
  
  However, there remain significant differences in the processes and definitions of AI development across various sectors. Although Musk has publicly claimed that Grok 5 will be the completed form of AGI and has admitted that the current version, Grok 4.7, still has technical flaws and room for improvement, academia and the industry have yet to reach a consensus on the specific definition and timeline for achieving "AGI." Furthermore, despite the billions of dollars Silicon Valley venture capital has invested in deep tech, these investments are largely long-term R&D in nature, and whether they can be converted into commercially profitable products in the short term remains to be verified by the market.
  
  Meanwhile, reports from the Taiwanese financial sector indicate that AI-driven exports and semiconductor demand have become key drivers of economic growth. However, whether these hardware advantages can continue to translate into profit momentum for the banking industry remains influenced by both global geopolitics and expectations of moderate economic growth.
  
  This wave of AI-driven hardware resurgence and technological competition has had a profound impact on North American and global supply chains. In North America, as large tech companies see a surge in demand for computing power, energy supply and data center construction have become the focus of policy and industry. For instance, energy companies such as Engie North America are playing an increasingly critical role in power infrastructure to meet the massive demand for stable electricity from AI computing centers. For Taiwan, the launch of investment attraction projects like the Taoyuan Asia Silicon Valley Research and Development Center indicates that the government is attempting to extend Taiwan's advantages in semiconductor front-end manufacturing into an AI application innovation hub to cope with the reconfiguration of global supply chains.
  
  Despite high market sentiment, analysts warn against over-interpreting the technical declarations of a single company. While Musk’s expectations for Grok 5 have sparked significant market attention, the realization of AGI involves complex cognitive science and computational limits, which cannot be achieved solely by increasing parameter scale. Furthermore, although venture capital is flooding into the hardware sector, deep tech startups face extremely high technical barriers and long investment recovery periods. If global economic growth falls short of expectations or geopolitical issues lead to supply chain disruptions, the construction progress of related hardware facilities may be affected. Investors should pay attention to the gap between technical indicators and actual commercial implementation to avoid blindly chasing market trends.
  
  Future observations will focus on three areas: First, whether xAI can complete the training of Grok 5 as scheduled and verify its AGI capabilities, which will directly affect the pace of the global AI arms race; second, whether the billions of dollars in venture capital invested by Silicon Valley in the semiconductor and robotics sectors can spawn next-generation hardware architectures with market competitiveness; and finally, the progress of North American energy policy and data center construction, particularly whether power supply will become a bottleneck limiting the expansion of AI computing power. These factors will collectively determine the development trajectory and market landscape of the global AI industry in the second half of 2026.

# 矽谷創投轉向深科技與 AI 硬體，馬斯克預告 Grok 5 將實現 AGI

> 全球創投資金正大規模湧入半導體與機器人等深科技領域，以支撐 AI 基礎設施需求。同時，馬斯克宣佈 Grok 5 將成為 AGI 完成體，標誌著 AI 發展進入算力軍備競賽新階段。


2026 年 9 月中旬，全球人工智慧（AI）產業迎來技術與資本佈局的雙重轉折點。在資本市場方面，矽谷創投機構正將數十億美元的資金重心，從純軟體應用轉向半導體、機器人等「深科技」（Deep Tech）領域。根據市場觀察，這些需要長期研發與資本密集的硬體技術，已被視為支撐大型語言模型（LLM）運作與未來 AI 發展的核心基礎建設。
在產業應用端，xAI 創辦人馬斯克（Elon Musk）於 9 月 17 日透過社群平臺公開發表最新技術藍圖，正式預告 Grok 5 將具備 2.5 兆引數，並宣稱該模型將成為實現通用人工智慧（AGI）的終點站。
多方來源共同確認的事實顯示，AI 產業的發展動能已從單純的軟體開發，全面轉向對算力與硬體基礎設施的極致追求。矽谷創投資料指出，今年以來硬體公司在美國創投總資金分配中佔比已接近三分之一，主要原因在於 LLM 的訓練與推理過程對先進晶片及資料中心硬體的需求呈指數級增長。與此同時，馬斯克揭露了 xAI 的技術演進策略，強調 Grok 5 將底層架構全面改用 C++ 軟體棧，旨在榨乾硬體效能以應對龐大的算力需求，此舉與矽谷資本流向硬體基礎建設的趨勢不謀而合。
然而，各界對於 AI 發展的程式與定義仍存在顯著差異。馬斯克雖公開宣稱 Grok 5 將是 AGI 的完成體，並坦承現行版本 Grok 4.7 仍存在技術缺陷與改進空間，但關於「AGI」的具體定義與實現時間表，學界與業界尚未達成共識。此外，儘管矽谷創投對深科技投入百億美元，但這些投資多屬於長期研發性質，短期內是否能轉化為具備商業獲利能力的產品，仍有待市場驗證。
與此同時，臺灣金融業報告指出，AI 帶動的出口與半導體需求已成為經濟成長的關鍵，但這些硬體優勢能否持續轉化為銀行業的獲利動能，仍受全球地緣政治與經濟溫和成長預期的雙重影響。
這波由 AI 驅動的硬體回歸與技術競賽，對北美及全球產業鏈產生了深遠影響。在北美，隨著大型科技公司對算力需求激增，能源供應與資料中心建設已成為政策與產業的焦點。例如，能源企業如 Engie North America 等在電力基礎設施領域的角色日益吃重，以滿足 AI 運算中心對穩定電力的龐大需求。對臺灣而言，桃園亞矽創研中心等招商計畫的啟動，顯示出政府正試圖將臺灣在半導體前端製造的優勢，進一步延伸至 AI 應用創新樞紐，以應對全球供應鏈的重新配置。
儘管市場情緒高漲，但分析師提醒，不應過度解讀單一企業的技術宣言。馬斯克對 Grok 5 的預期雖引發市場高度關注，但 AGI 的實現涉及複雜的認知科學與運算極限，並非僅靠引數規模提升即可達成。此外，創投資金雖湧入硬體領域，但深科技新創公司面臨的技術門檻極高，投資回收期長，若全球經濟成長不如預期，或地緣政治導致供應鏈斷鏈，可能影響相關硬體設施的建置進度。投資人應關注技術指標與實際商業化落地之間的落差，避免盲目追逐市場熱點。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三方面：首先，xAI 是否能如期完成 Grok 5 的訓練並驗證其 AGI 能力，這將直接影響全球 AI 軍備競賽的節奏；其次，矽谷創投對半導體與機器人領域的百億美元投入，是否能催生出具備市場競爭力的下一代硬體架構；最後，觀察北美能源政策與資料中心建設的進展，特別是電力供應是否會成為限制 AI 算力擴張的瓶頸。這些因素將共同決定全球 AI 產業在 2026 年下半年的發展走向與市場格局。
