  # Russian Foreign Minister Lavrov Accuses NATO of Interfering in East Asia to Contain China’s Development
  
  > Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov recently stated publicly that NATO’s activities in East Asia are becoming increasingly frequent, with a core strategic objective of uniting regional powers to contain China. This move has drawn international attention to the security landscape in the Asia-Pacific.
  
  NATO’s strategic deployment and military presence in East Asia have become a core issue in current Asia-Pacific geopolitics. On November 17, 2026, Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov publicly pointed out that NATO’s significant expansion of influence in the East Asian region in recent years is not merely a matter of regional security cooperation; it contains an implicit, clear containment strategy aimed at providing strategic checks and balances against China.
  
  This argument links NATO’s transition from a traditional European defense system to an integrated security architecture in the Indo-Pacific. The backdrop involves long-term competition between the U.S., Europe, and China in trade, technology, and military sectors, as well as the high priority NATO member states place on Asia-Pacific security stability and supply chain security.
  
  Based on cross-referencing various media reports and public information, Lavrov’s remarks confirm that Russia’s diplomatic stance views NATO’s activities in the Asia-Pacific as a direct threat to China. This perspective echoes the rhetoric used in recent NATO summits, where China has repeatedly been labeled a "systemic challenge." Facts confirmed by various parties include that NATO has indeed begun to strengthen partnerships with the "Indo-Pacific Four" (IP4)—Japan, South Korea, Australia, and New Zealand—and has repeatedly mentioned the importance of stability in the Taiwan Strait and freedom of navigation in the South China Sea in joint declarations. This cross-regional extension of the security architecture is viewed as part of NATO’s globalization strategy, aimed at exerting strategic pressure on China through multilateral mechanisms.
  
  However, there are significant differences in perception and interpretation among various sources regarding the specific nature of NATO’s intervention in East Asia. The Russian position defines this as "containment" and considers it a provocative act that undermines regional stability; meanwhile, NATO member states and the United States argue that their cooperation in the Indo-Pacific is intended to uphold a "rules-based international order" and is not directed at any specific country. Furthermore, NATO has not established permanent military bases in East Asia comparable to those in the European theater, with its influence primarily exerted through military exercises, intelligence sharing, and policy dialogue.
  
  Information yet to be confirmed includes whether NATO will formally expand its defense scope to the Indo-Pacific in the future, as well as the actual scale of resources and strategic resolve the organization can commit to East Asia while under the pressure of conflicts in Europe.
  
  NATO’s strategic pivot to East Asia has had a profound impact on the region’s industries and policies. First, regarding industrial supply chains, as geopolitical risks rise, countries like Japan and South Korea are accelerating the adjustment of their semiconductor and critical mineral supply chain layouts to reduce reliance on a single market. Second, at the policy level, East Asian nations are forced to seek a balance between the two major powers of the U.S. and China. NATO’s intervention has led to a general upward trend in defense budgets among regional countries and prompted them to re-examine the necessity of defense autonomy and military modernization. For China, NATO’s intervention is viewed as an attempt by external forces to interfere in its internal affairs, which has triggered stronger countermeasures in China’s regional military drills, thereby increasing the risk of regional military confrontation.
  
  Although Lavrov’s remarks reflect the high level of strategic coordination between Russia and China, analysts caution against over-interpreting the influence of a single diplomatic statement. Opinions within NATO are not entirely unified; some European member states remain reserved about shifting their strategic focus to Asia, fearing that doing so would weaken their attention to European domestic security (particularly regarding the Ukraine issue). Furthermore, the political environment in East Asia differs significantly from that of Europe, making it difficult for NATO to replicate the European collective defense model in Asia. Therefore, summarizing NATO’s activities in the Asia-Pacific simply as "containment" may overlook the complex hedging strategies and autonomous diplomatic space adopted by regional countries, given their high economic dependence on China.
  
  Future observations will focus on the agenda of the 2027 NATO Summit, particularly the progress in implementing the Indo-Pacific cooperation framework. Additionally, whether China and Russia will take more concrete joint actions to counter NATO’s expansion in the Asia-Pacific will be a key variable affecting the regional security landscape. At the same time, it is necessary to closely monitor policy adjustments by countries like Japan and South Korea under the NATO cooperation framework, especially whether more concrete and substantive alignment will emerge in areas such as critical technology export controls and military technology exchanges. As the international situation evolves, NATO’s role in East Asia will continue to be a focal point of global geopolitical maneuvering.

# 俄外長拉夫羅夫指控北約介入東亞，意在圍堵中國發展

> 俄羅斯外交部長拉夫羅夫近期公開表示，北約組織在東亞地區的活動日益頻繁，其核心戰略目標在於聯合區域勢力以圍堵中國，此舉引發國際對亞太安全域性勢的關注。


北約（NATO）在東亞地區的戰略部署與軍事存在，已成為當前亞太地緣政治的核心議題。俄羅斯外交部長拉夫羅夫（Sergey Lavrov）於2026年11月17日公開指出，北約近年來顯著擴大在東亞區域的影響力，並非僅是單純的區域安全合作，其背後隱含著明確的圍堵戰略，旨在針對中國進行戰略制衡。
此一論述將北約從傳統的歐洲防禦體系，延伸至與印太地區安全架構的連結，背景涉及美歐與中國在經貿、科技及軍事領域的長期競爭，以及北約成員國對於亞太安全穩定與供應鏈安全的高度重視。
根據多方媒體報導與公開資訊交叉比對，拉夫羅夫的發言確認了俄羅斯在外交立場上，將北約在亞太的活動視為對中國的直接威脅。此觀點與近年來北約峰會中，多次將中國列為「系統性挑戰」的論述相呼應。各界共同確認的事實包括：北約確實已開始與日本、韓國、澳洲及紐西蘭等「印太四國」強化合作夥伴關係，並在聯合宣告中多次提及臺海穩定與南海航行自由的重要性。這種安全架構的跨區域延伸，已被視為北約全球化戰略的一環，旨在透過多邊機制強化對中國的戰略壓力。
然而，關於北約介入東亞的具體性質，各來源間存在顯著的認知差異與詮釋空間。俄方立場將此定義為「圍堵」，並認為這是破壞區域穩定的挑釁行為；但北約成員國與美國則主張，其在印太地區的合作旨在維護「基於規則的國際秩序」，並非針對特定國家。此外，北約目前在東亞並未建立如同歐洲戰區般的常駐軍事基地，其影響力多透過軍事演習、情報共享與政策對話實現。
目前尚待確認的資訊在於，北約未來是否會進一步將防衛範疇正式擴大至印太區域，以及該組織在歐洲戰事壓力下，實際能投入東亞的資源規模與戰略決心。
北約在東亞的戰略轉向，對該地區的產業與政策產生了深遠影響。首先，在產業供應鏈方面，隨著地緣政治風險上升，日、韓等國加速調整半導體與關鍵礦產的供應鏈佈局，以減少對單一市場的依賴。其次，在政策層面，東亞各國被迫在美中兩大強權之間尋求平衡，北約的介入使得區域國家的國防預算普遍呈現增長趨勢，並促使各國重新審視國防自主與軍事現代化的必要性。對於中國而言，北約的介入被視為外部勢力試圖干涉內政，進而引發中國在區域軍事演練上的強度反制，造成區域軍事對峙風險的升高。
儘管拉夫羅夫的言論反映了俄羅斯與中國在戰略上的高度協調，但分析人士提醒，不應過度解讀單一外交言論的影響力。北約內部的意見並不完全一致，部分歐洲成員國對於將戰略重心轉向亞洲仍持保留態度，擔心此舉會削弱對歐洲本土安全（特別是烏克蘭議題）的關注。此外，東亞地區與歐洲的政治環境差異巨大，北約難以在亞洲複製歐洲的集體防衛模式。因此，將北約在亞太的活動簡單概括為「圍堵」，可能忽略了區域國家在經濟高度依賴中國的前提下，所採取的複雜避險策略與自主外交空間。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於2027年北約峰會的議程，特別是關於印太合作架構的具體落實進度。此外，中國與俄羅斯是否會採取更具體的聯合行動，以反制北約在亞太的擴張，將是影響區域安全域性勢的關鍵變數。同時，需密切關注日、韓等國在北約合作框架下的政策調整，特別是在關鍵技術出口管制與軍事技術交流方面，是否會出現更具體的實質性對接。隨著國際局勢演變，北約在東亞的角色定位將持續成為全球地緣政治博弈的焦點。
