  # East Asia’s Economic Resilience: 2026 Growth Forecast Reaches 4.5% Amid Continued Trade Openness
  
  > A recent World Bank report indicates that the East Asia and Pacific region is projected to grow by 4.5% in 2026, demonstrating resilience against global uncertainty. Economies within the region continue to seek stable development in a shifting geopolitical landscape through diversified trade and investment cooperation.
  
  As a vital engine of global economic growth, the East Asia and Pacific (EAP) region has continuously faced complex international challenges in recent years. According to the latest economic outlook report released by the World Bank on October 6, 2026, despite the numerous uncertainties and headwinds facing the global economy, the EAP region continues to show significant resilience, with a stable growth rate of 4.5% projected for 2026.
  
  This data reflects the region's continued industrial adaptability and market demand support, even while navigating global supply chain restructuring and the pressures of energy transition, providing an important macroeconomic backdrop for regional public policy formulation and industrial development.
  
  Multiple sources confirm that East Asia's economic prosperity is largely attributed to its highly open trade system. According to analysis by the East Asia Forum, the region contains a large number of "late-developing" small open economies that have long successfully benefited from bilateral trade with both the United States and China. By actively participating in the globalization process, these countries and regions have successfully attracted diversified foreign direct investment (FDI), thereby driving their own industrialization and modernization.
  
  Reports from both the World Bank and the East Asia Forum indicate that the region's economic growth is not driven by a single factor, but is instead built upon the foundation of deep integration into global value chains and the maintenance of transnational trade networks.
  
  However, regarding the drivers and risks of future growth, there are differing focal points among the sources. The World Bank emphasizes that the "application of artificial intelligence (AI)" will be a key variable in regional economic development, suggesting that countries should enhance productivity through technological transformation. In contrast, the East Asia Forum focuses more on the impact of geopolitics on trade policy, noting that the current political environment in East Asia still tends to favor preserving the economic dividends brought by globalization.
  
  Furthermore, regarding the regional presence of certain multinational corporations, international brands such as Louis Vuitton list markets like mainland China and Japan side-by-side on their official websites. This reflects a highly segmented and localized operational strategy when facing the East Asian market, which differs from the perspective of macroeconomic reports that view East Asia as a single growth bloc, highlighting the complexity of business operations within the region.
  
  For relevant industries and policies, a 4.5% growth forecast implies that the East Asia region still has massive investment needs in infrastructure, digital transformation, and energy transition. Governments are working to balance reliance on foreign trade with the expansion of domestic demand to mitigate the shocks caused by geopolitical friction. Particularly for small open economies dependent on exports, maintaining good trade relations with the two major economies of the U.S. and China while simultaneously enhancing their own technological autonomy has become the core of policy planning for countries in the region. This structural economic adjustment directly impacts the transformation needs of the regional labor market, as well as long-term capital allocation for green energy and digital infrastructure.
  
  Although the data shows steady growth, analysts caution against over-interpreting a single growth indicator. The risks of extreme weather brought by global climate change, as well as cost fluctuations during the regional energy transition process, could pose challenges to future economic resilience. Furthermore, while the current geopolitical environment remains favorable to trade openness, the development model of the East Asia region—which relies on diversified investment—would face severe tests if global protectionism rises or if technological competition between major economies intensifies. Therefore, it is unrealistic to view economic growth forecasts as absolute, and policymakers must consider the risks of structural unemployment and industrial relocation that may result from the restructuring of international supply chains.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on how East Asian countries implement the industrial application of AI technology, as well as their negotiation strategies in the face of changing global trade rules. Additionally, as climate issues become a focal point of global public policy, the progress of energy transition in East Asia will directly determine whether it can maintain a growth momentum of around 4.5% over the next decade. Observers will continue to monitor policy coordination among major regional economies as they push for a dual transition toward a green and digital economy, as well as the supply chain adjustments and capital allocation strategies implemented by enterprises in response to market changes in the region.

# 東亞經濟展現韌性：2026年預測成長率達4.5%並維持貿易開放

> 世界銀行最新報告指出，東亞與太平洋地區2026年預計成長4.5%，展現抵禦全球不確定性的韌性。區域內經濟體持續透過多元化貿易與投資合作，在變動的地緣政治環境中尋求穩定發展。


東亞與太平洋地區（East Asia and Pacific, EAP）作為全球經濟成長的重要引擎，近年來持續面對複雜的國際環境挑戰。根據世界銀行（World Bank）於2026年10月6日發布的最新經濟展望報告，儘管全球經濟面臨諸多不確定性與逆風，東亞與太平洋地區的經濟發展仍顯示出顯著的韌性，預計在2026年將維持4.5%的穩定成長率。
這一資料反映了該區域在經歷全球供應鏈重組與能源轉型壓力下，仍具備一定的產業適應力與市場需求支撐，為區域內的公共政策制定與產業發展提供了重要的宏觀背景。
多方來源共同確認，東亞地區的經濟繁榮在很大程度上歸功於其高度開放的貿易體系。根據《東亞論壇》（East Asia Forum）的分析，東亞區域記憶體在大量「後發型」的小型開放經濟體，這些經濟體長期以來成功地從與美國及中國的雙邊貿易中獲益。這些國家與地區透過積極參與全球化程式，成功吸引了多元化的外國直接投資（FDI），進而推動了自身的工業化與現代化發展。
世界銀行與《東亞論壇》的報告均顯示，該區域的經濟成長並非單一因素驅動，而是建立在深度參與全球價值鏈與維持跨國貿易網路的基礎之上。
然而，關於未來成長的動力與風險，各來源之間存在不同的觀察重點。世界銀行強調「人工智慧（AI）的應用」將是區域內經濟發展的關鍵變數，建議各國應透過技術轉型來進一步提升生產力；相較之下，《東亞論壇》則更側重於地緣政治對貿易政策的影響，指出東亞目前的政治環境依然傾向於維護全球化帶來的經濟紅利。
此外，關於部分跨國企業在區域內的佈局，如路易威登（Louis Vuitton）等國際品牌在官網呈現的區域劃分中，將中國大陸與日本等市場並列，反映出企業在面對東亞市場時，採取了高度細分且在地化的經營策略，這與宏觀經濟報告中將東亞視為單一增長板塊的視角有所差異，顯示出區域內在商業運作層面的複雜性。
對於相關產業與政策而言，4.5%的成長預測意味著東亞地區在基礎建設、數位轉型及能源轉型方面仍有龐大的投資需求。各國政府正致力於平衡對外貿易依賴與內需市場擴張，以降低地緣政治摩擦帶來的衝擊。特別是對於依賴出口的小型開放經濟體，如何維持與中美兩大經濟體的良好貿易關係，同時提升自身的技術自主性，已成為區域內各國政策規劃的核心。這種經濟結構的調整，直接影響了區域內勞動力市場的轉型需求，以及對於綠色能源與數位基礎設施的長期資本配置。
儘管資料顯示成長穩健，但分析師提醒不應過度解讀單一成長指標。全球氣候變遷帶來的極端天氣風險，以及區域內能源轉型過程中的成本波動，均可能對未來的經濟韌性構成挑戰。此外，雖然目前地緣政治環境仍有利於貿易開放，但若全球保護主義抬頭，或主要經濟體間的技術競爭加劇，東亞地區依賴多元化投資的發展模式將面臨嚴峻考驗。因此，將經濟成長預測視為絕對定論是不切實際的，政策制定者必須考慮到國際供應鏈重組可能帶來的結構性失業與產業外移風險。
後續觀察重點將集中在東亞各國如何落實AI技術的產業化應用，以及各國在面對全球貿易規則變動時的談判策略。此外，隨著氣候議題成為全球公共政策的焦點，東亞地區在能源轉型上的進度，將直接決定其能否在未來十年內維持4.5%左右的成長動能。觀察家將持續關注區域內主要經濟體在推動綠色經濟與數位經濟雙轉型過程中的政策協調，以及企業在該區域內針對市場變動所進行的供應鏈調整與資本配置策略。
