根據多方報導，索尼影業（Sony Pictures）目前正積極洽談收購知名電玩遊戲《戰地風雲》（Battlefield）的電影改編權。此一備受矚目的項目，據悉將由麥可·B·喬丹（Michael B. Jordan）主演，並由克里斯多福·麥奎里（Christopher McQuarrie）負責編劇、執導及製作。

此次洽談是在一場多方工作室的激烈競標戰之後浮出水面。資料顯示，索尼影業在眾多競爭者中脫穎而出，有望將這部軍事電玩改編電影納入旗下，進一步擴展其影視內容版圖。

業界觀察指出，索尼影業近年來對電玩遊戲改編電影展現出濃厚興趣，並取得不錯的成績。例如，報導指出最近的《惡靈古堡》電影在票房和整體評價上表現良好，這可能促使索尼對《戰地風雲》這類大型遊戲IP的改編抱持樂觀態度。

## English edition

According to multiple reports, Sony Pictures is currently in advanced discussions to acquire the film adaptation rights for the popular video game "Battlefield." This highly anticipated project is reportedly set to star Michael B. Jordan, with Christopher McQuarrie attached to write, direct, and produce.

The discussions come after a multi-studio bidding war for the rights to the military video game adaptation. Reports indicate that Sony Pictures has emerged as a frontrunner among several competitors to secure the project, aiming to expand its film and television content portfolio.

Industry observers note that Sony Pictures has shown a strong interest and achieved considerable success with video game adaptations in recent years. For instance, the latest "Resident Evil" film reportedly performed well at the box office and received positive overall reception, which may fuel Sony's optimism for adapting major gaming IPs like "Battlefield."
