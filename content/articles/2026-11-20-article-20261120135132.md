  # Shifts in the Global AI Supply Chain: South Korea’s Record Exports and Tech Giants’ Energy Strategies
  
  > As demand for artificial intelligence surges, South Korea’s export value has surpassed the $1 trillion mark for the first time, with SK Hynix reporting significant growth in revenue and profit. Meanwhile, tech giant Google is turning to ultra-deep geothermal energy to power AI computing, while Apple has emerged as a safe-haven asset amidst market volatility.
  
  The global technology industry demonstrated strong momentum and structural transformation in 2026. According to data released by South Korea’s Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy on November 18, 2026, driven by the continuous investment in artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure by global tech firms, demand for semiconductors and computer products has remained robust. South Korea’s cumulative annual export value has officially exceeded $1 trillion, making it the fourth country in the world to reach this milestone. This data reflects South Korea’s core position in the AI hardware supply chain as a major hub for global memory chip production.
  
  Concurrently, South Korean memory giant SK Hynix delivered impressive results for the third quarter of 2026, with quarterly revenue reaching $79 billion—a 328% increase compared to the same period last year—and operating profit surging by 632%, demonstrating the strong market competitiveness of its High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) technology.
  
  At the other end of the supply chain, tech giants are facing immense power challenges brought on by AI computing. On November 18, 2026, Google announced the signing of the world’s first commercial-scale "ultra-deep geothermal" energy procurement agreement, planning to purchase 50 megawatts (MW) of power from Project Obsidian, a subsidiary of the startup Quaise Energy. This project aims to overcome the geographical limitations of traditional geothermal energy; through advanced geothermal systems, it is expected to provide 24/7 power to Google’s data center in Oregon, USA, as early as 2030.
  
  This move highlights the urgent need for energy stability and green power within the AI industry, prompting tech companies to proactively intervene in the development and investment of energy infrastructure.
  
  Despite the promising industry outlook, significant volatility and uncertainty remain in the market. According to reports, large-cap tech stocks have recently experienced drastic fluctuations; the results of the U.S. midterm elections and the sudden removal of NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang have both impacted market confidence. While share prices for chip manufacturers and cloud service providers have generally been under pressure, Apple has demonstrated high resilience, with its stock price rising over 29% this year and its market capitalization reaching $5.1 trillion, making it the world’s most valuable company. Analysts point out that investors view Apple as a safe-haven choice during periods of AI-related turbulence, forming a sharp contrast to the aggressive expansion of the semiconductor supply chain.
  
  Furthermore, the political and geopolitical situation in the Oceania region has also become a focus of global attention. According to information compiled by The News Lens, the Pacific Islands Forum (PIF) has recently faced pressure from Beijing, with cross-national policies and diplomatic maneuvering in the region continuing to intensify. Although the development of the tech industry and regional geopolitics may seem separate, as digital infrastructure (such as undersea cables and data centers) becomes a strategic asset for competition among nations, the role of Pacific Island nations in the future global tech landscape and their interactions with major economies will become a key point of observation.
  
  It is worth noting that multiple perspectives currently exist regarding the development of the AI industry. On one hand, market forecasts for semiconductor and memory demand are extremely optimistic, with companies like SK Hynix targeting market share expansion through 2027; on the other hand, whether the investments made by tech giants in energy transition can alleviate the power supply pressure on AI data centers as scheduled remains dependent on the progress of technological development. Additionally, whether global tech stock valuations are already too high, and the potential interference of geopolitical risks on the supply chain, are risk factors that investors and industry analysts must carefully evaluate; the economic dividends brought by AI should not be oversimplified.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on: first, whether South Korea’s semiconductor exports can maintain this growth momentum through the end of the year and what impact this will have on global memory prices; second, the actual output of Google’s ultra-deep geothermal project during the technical verification phase; and third, the policy direction regarding digital infrastructure within the Oceania region and how it aligns with major global tech economies. These factors will collectively shape the global tech industry landscape in 2027 and beyond.

# 全球AI產業鏈變局：韓國出口創高與科技巨頭能源佈局

> 隨著人工智慧需求激增，韓國出口額首度突破1兆美元大關，SK海力士營收與獲利大幅成長。同時，科技巨頭Google轉向超高溫岩石地熱以支撐AI運算電力，蘋果則在市場動盪中成為避險資產。


2026年全球科技產業展現強勁的動能與結構性轉變。根據韓國產業通商資源部於2026年11月18日發布的資料，受惠於全球科技企業對人工智慧（AI）基礎設施的持續投入，半導體與電腦產品需求暢旺，韓國年度累計出口額已正式突破1兆美元，成為全球第四個達成此里程碑的國家。此一資料反映了韓國作為全球記憶體晶片供應重鎮，在AI硬體供應鏈中佔據的核心地位。
與此同時，南韓記憶體大廠SK海力士在2026年第三季繳出亮眼成績，單季營收達790億美元，較去年同期成長328%，營業利益增幅更達632%，顯示其高頻寬記憶體（HBM）技術在市場上的強勢競爭力。
在產業鏈的另一端，科技巨頭面臨AI運算帶來的巨大電力挑戰。Google於2026年11月18日宣佈簽署全球首筆商業規模的「超高溫岩石地熱」能源採購協議，預計向新創公司Quaise Energy旗下的Project Obsidian採購50百萬瓦（MW）電力。此計畫旨在解決傳統地熱受限於地理位置的痛點，透過進階地熱系統，預計最快於2030年為Google位於美國俄勒岡州的資料中心提供全天候電力。
此舉凸顯AI產業對能源穩定性與綠色電力的迫切需求，已促使科技公司主動介入能源基礎設施的開發與投資。
儘管產業前景看好，但市場仍存在顯著的波動與不確定性。根據報導，近期大型科技股經歷劇烈震盪，包括美國中期選舉結果以及輝達（NVIDIA）執行長黃仁勳的突發罷免事件，均對市場信心造成衝擊。在晶片製造商與雲端服務商股價普遍受壓的情況下，蘋果公司（Apple）卻展現了極高的抗跌性，今年以來股價漲幅逾29%，市值達到5.1兆美元，成為全球市值最高的企業。分析指出，投資者將蘋果視為AI動盪時期的避險選擇，這與半導體供應鏈的激進擴張形成了鮮明對比。
此外，大洋洲地區的政治與地緣局勢亦是全球關注焦點。根據《關鍵評論網》整理的資訊，太平洋島國論壇（PIF）近期面臨來自北京的施壓，區域內的跨國政策與外交博弈持續升溫。雖然科技產業的發展與區域地緣政治看似分離，但隨著數位基礎設施（如海底電纜與資料中心）成為各國競逐的戰略資產，太平洋島國在未來全球科技版圖中的角色，以及其與主要經濟體之間的互動，將成為觀察重點。
值得注意的是，目前關於AI產業的發展仍存在多重觀點。一方面，市場對半導體與記憶體需求的預測極為樂觀，如SK海力士已將目標鎖定至2027年的市佔擴張；另一方面，科技巨頭在能源轉型上的投入是否能如期緩解AI資料中心的供電壓力，仍需視技術開發進度而定。此外，全球科技股的估值是否已過高，以及地緣政治風險對供應鏈的潛在幹擾，皆是投資人與產業分析師必須審慎評估的風險因子，不宜過度簡化AI帶來的經濟紅利。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於：第一，韓國半導體出口是否能維持此一成長動能至年底，並對全球記憶體價格產生何種影響；第二，Google的超高溫岩石地熱計畫在技術驗證階段的實際產出；第三，大洋洲區域內針對數位基礎建設的政策走向，以及其如何與全球主要科技經濟體進行對接。這些因素將共同形塑2027年及往後的全球科技產業格局。
