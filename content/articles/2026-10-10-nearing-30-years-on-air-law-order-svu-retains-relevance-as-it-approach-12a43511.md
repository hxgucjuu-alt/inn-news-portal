美國國家廣播公司（NBC）旗下長壽影集《法網遊龍：特案組》（Law & Order: Special Victims Unit）開播至今已近30年，持續引發觀眾與影評關注。外媒報導指出，該劇在最新一季（第28季）首播中，延續了「取材自真實頭條新聞」的傳統敘事風格，藉由描繪貼近現實社會的案件，證明其在當代影視環境中依舊保有高度的社會關聯性。

根據影視媒體報導，自1999年開播以來，由瑪莉絲卡·哈吉塔（Mariska Hargitay）領銜主演的《法網遊龍：特案組》歷經27年長跑，即將在第28季迎來第600集的罕見里程碑。除了日間肥皂劇之外，極少有黃金檔虛構劇集能夠達到此一播出規模，該劇的持久度已可比肩《荒野大鏢客》（Gunsmoke）與《辛普森家庭》（The Simpsons）等經典作品。

報導同時指出，NBC作為諸多電視長壽劇集的發源地，其體系內的《法網遊龍》系列原創母劇自1990年首播以來已逾35年，而衍生劇《特案組》則憑藉其成熟的敘事模式與對現實社會案件的即時反思，持續在全球流行文化與影視產業中佔有一席之地。

## English edition

NBC's flagship police procedural 'Law & Order: Special Victims Unit' (SVU) continues to maintain strong cultural relevance after nearly three decades on the air. Media reports highlight that the premiere of its 28th season once again leans into narratives 'ripped from the headlines,' reflecting contemporary real-world cases and underscoring the show's persistent social resonance.

Helmed by Mariska Hargitay since its debut in 1999, 'Law & Order: SVU' is poised to cross a rare television milestone during its 28th season by nearing 600 episodes. Excluding daytime soap operas, very few primetime scripted series have achieved such longevity, placing SVU in an elite cohort of broadcast television alongside historic runs like 'Gunsmoke' and 'The Simpsons.'

NBC has long served as a hub for network television's most enduring franchises. While the original 'Law & Order' series debuted in 1990, SVU has established its own sustained success over 27 years, demonstrating an enduring narrative formula that continues to captivate audiences and prompt reflections on modern justice.
