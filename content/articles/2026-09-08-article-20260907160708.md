  # Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an’s Eldest Son Embroiled in Privilege Controversy; Online Defense Accounts Suspected to Originate from Overseas
  
  > Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an’s eldest son, Chiang De-li, has recently been embroiled in controversies regarding public-funded study abroad programs and school admission privileges, drawing significant public attention. Subsequently, a large number of social media accounts, suspected to be using overseas IP addresses, appeared to defend him. Due to repetitive content and the misspelling of his name, these accounts have been widely questioned as being part of a coordinated "internet army" operation, and related discussions continue to intensify.
  
  Chiang De-li, the eldest son of Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an, has recently faced intense scrutiny from the public and the media regarding his application for public-funded study abroad programs, the legitimacy of his receiving a "Mayor's Award," and his admission process to the junior high division of the Affiliated Senior High School of National Taiwan Normal University. Critics have questioned whether he enjoyed privileges beyond those available to ordinary citizens in obtaining educational resources, further raising concerns regarding potential conflicts of interest. This incident not only involves personal educational rights but, due to his status as a family member of a political figure, has also sparked a broad discussion on the fairness of public resource allocation. As the controversy continues to escalate, Mayor Chiang Wan-an's responses and handling of the situation have also become a focal point of observation.
  
  According to public information on the social media platform Threads, shortly after the aforementioned controversy erupted, a large number of accounts claiming to be Chiang De-li’s "junior high classmates" emerged. These accounts densely posted highly repetitive content in his defense. Following cross-referencing and technical tracking by netizens, it was discovered that the IP addresses of these accounts were located in Indonesia. Some posts contained simplified Chinese characters, and in some instances, the subject's name was incorrectly written as "Chiang Li-de." Furthermore, some accounts exhibited clear characteristics of AI-automated responses when interacting with other users, a phenomenon that quickly triggered suspicions of an "internet army" intervening in public opinion.
  
  Regarding this phenomenon, Chang Yu-meng, Chairman of the Taiwan Youth Association for Democracy, stated publicly on September 6 that these "defense posts" have instead caused Chiang Wan-an to become a subject of ridicule in the public sphere, even turning into an internet meme. However, there is currently no definitive evidence linking these accounts to specific operators or their motives. While public opinion generally assumes this is an organized internet army operation, Mayor Chiang Wan-an’s stance on these accounts and whether they have any connection to his city government team remain matters of speculation and are yet to be clarified. Media coverage of this event has focused primarily on the detection of abnormal online behavior rather than confirming the direct involvement of any specific political entity.
  
  The specific impact of this incident on Taiwanese society lies in the heightened sensitivity of the public toward the issue of privileges enjoyed by the families of political figures. This concerns not only personal integrity but also public trust in educational fairness and administrative neutrality. When the family affairs of political figures become linked to public policy resources, it often triggers skepticism regarding institutional transparency. Furthermore, the vulnerability of social media as a forum for public opinion has been re-examined; the phenomenon of overseas IP addresses intervening in Taiwan's internal political discussions has become a critical issue in public safety and information cognitive warfare, affecting the quality of policy debate and the formation of social consensus.
  
  From different perspectives, supporters of Chiang Wan-an might argue that attacks on his family members constitute political smearing, and that the online defense may be a spontaneous reaction from supporters, which should not be over-interpreted as being officially sanctioned. However, critics argue that the formatted nature of the defense content and the abnormal IP addresses indicate a high degree of organization, which goes beyond the scope of free speech and constitutes an attempt to manipulate public opinion. Experts suggest that in the absence of clear evidence, one should avoid labeling all online discourse as the work of a specific camp's internet army to avoid falling into the trap of information warfare, and should instead focus more on the systemic issues regarding the review of public-funded study abroad and admission qualifications.
  
  Future observations will focus on whether the Taipei City Government will provide a formal response regarding the "internet army" allegations, and whether relevant educational authorities will launch an investigation into the controversies surrounding Chiang De-li’s admission and awards. At the same time, with the evolution of digital technology, how to prevent overseas forces from using AI and automated accounts to interfere in Taiwan's social issues will be a major challenge for future public policy and internet governance. The public should continue to pay attention to the transparency of information sources and maintain critical thinking regarding abnormal operations on social media to avoid losing sight of the essence of events amidst fragmented information.

# 蔣萬安長子蔣得立捲入特權疑雲 網路護航帳號遭質疑來自境外

> 臺北市長蔣萬安長子蔣得立近期身陷公費留學與入學特權爭議，引發輿論關注。社群媒體隨後出現大量疑似境外IP的帳號進行護航，因內容重複且出現名稱誤植，遭各界質疑為網軍操作，相關討論持續發酵。


臺北市長蔣萬安之長子蔣得立，近期因涉及公費留學申請、獲頒市長獎之合理性，以及師大附中國中部入學程式等議題，遭到社會大眾與輿論的高度檢視。外界質疑其在教育資源獲取過程中，是否享有超越一般市民的特權，並進一步探討是否存在利益迴避之疑慮。此事件不僅牽涉個人教育權益，更因其身為政治人物家屬的特殊身份，引發公眾對於公共資源分配公平性的廣泛討論。隨著爭議持續延燒，蔣萬安市長的相關回應與處理態度，也成為各界觀察的焦點。
根據社群平臺Threads上的公開資訊，在上述爭議爆發後，短時間內湧現大量自稱為蔣得立「國中同學」的帳號。這些帳號在平臺上密集發布內容高度雷同的護航貼文，試圖為蔣得立的爭議進行辯護。經網友交叉比對與技術追蹤後發現，這些帳號的IP位址顯示位於印尼，且部分貼文內容出現簡體字，甚至將當事人姓名誤植為「蔣立得」。此外，部分帳號在與網友互動時，展現出明顯的AI自動化回覆特徵，此現象迅速引起網路上對於「網軍」介入輿論操作的質疑。
針對此一現象，臺灣青年世代共好協會理事長張育萌於9月6日公開表示，此類「護航文」的操作手法，反而使得蔣萬安在輿論場中陷入被嘲諷的境地，甚至成為網路迷因。然而，目前針對這些帳號背後的實際操作者及其動機，仍缺乏確切的證據連結。雖然輿論普遍認為這是有組織的網軍行為，但蔣萬安本人對於這些帳號的立場，以及其是否與市府團隊有關聯，目前仍處於各界推測與尚待釐清的階段。各媒體對於此事件的報導重點，主要集中在網路異常行為的偵測，而非確認特定政治實體的直接參與。
此事件對臺灣社會的具體影響，在於公眾對於政治人物家屬特權議題的敏感度再次提升。這不僅關乎個人誠信，更涉及公眾對於教育公平與行政中立的信任。若政治人物的家庭事務與公共政策資源產生連結，往往會引發對於制度透明度的質疑。此外，社群媒體作為輿論場域的脆弱性也再次受到檢視，境外IP介入臺灣內部政治討論的現象，已成為公共安全與資訊認知作戰中的重要課題，影響著政策討論的品質與社會共識的形成。
從不同觀點來看，支援蔣萬安的陣營可能認為，針對家屬的攻擊屬於政治抹黑，網路上的護航行為或許是支持者的自發性反應，不應過度解讀為官方授意。然而，批評者則認為，護航內容的格式化與IP異常，顯示出極高的組織化程度，這已非單純的言論自由範疇，而是試圖操弄輿論的行為。專家建議，在缺乏明確證據前，應避免將所有網路言論直接歸類為特定陣營的網軍，以免陷入資訊戰的陷阱，並應更關注公費留學與入學資格審查的制度性問題。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於臺北市政府是否會針對「網軍疑雲」進行正式回應，以及相關教育主管機關是否會就蔣得立的入學與獎項爭議啟動調查。同時，隨著數位科技的演進，如何防範境外勢力利用AI與自動化帳號幹預臺灣社會議題，將是未來公共政策與網路治理的重要挑戰。公眾應持續關注資訊來源的透明度，並對於社群媒體上的異常操作保持批判性思考，避免在碎片化的資訊中喪失對事件本質的判斷。
