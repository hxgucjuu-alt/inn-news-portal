  # California Passes Amendment to "Digital Age Assurance Act," Exempting Open Source Operating Systems
  
  > The California State Legislature recently passed an amendment to the "Digital Age Assurance Act" (DAAA), explicitly excluding open source operating systems from age verification requirements, aiming to alleviate compliance burdens on the open source community.
  
  The California State Legislature officially passed an amendment (Bill No. AB-1856) to the "Digital Age Assurance Act" (DAAA) on August 26, 2026, local time. The core background of this legislation stems from the original bill (AB 1043) signed by California Governor Gavin Newsom in October 2025, which was originally scheduled to take effect in January 2027.
  
  The legislative intent was to bridge regulatory gaps in online child safety by implementing a mandatory "age signal" mechanism, requiring digital platforms to verify user age to protect minors from online risks.
  
  According to confirmed reports from various media outlets, the most significant change in the passed amendment, AB-1856, is the explicit exclusion of "Open Source Operating Systems" from the scope of the act. This adjustment directly addresses long-standing concerns from the tech industry and the open source community that mandatory age verification mechanisms could impose severe technical and administrative compliance pressures on decentralized, transparent, and collaborative open source software development models. Through this exemption, the open source community can continue to drive software innovation without altering its core development architecture.
  
  However, information regarding the execution details and potential impact of the act remains to be clarified. While the amendment resolves compliance issues for open source operating systems, specific "age signal" technical standards and privacy protection boundaries for other types of digital platforms—particularly those involving social media, interactive entertainment software, and ad-tracking technologies—still await more detailed implementation rules from relevant regulatory agencies.
  
  Furthermore, the California government has previously attempted to restrict platform content or verify identities through mandatory measures, but these efforts have repeatedly been rejected or blocked by federal courts due to concerns regarding constitutionally protected freedom of speech. Whether AB-1856 will face similar legal challenges during its future implementation remains a focus of industry attention.
  
  This policy change has a direct impact on the global technology industry, especially for software developers and cloud service providers that rely on open source technology. As a hub for global technological innovation, California's legislative direction often serves as a benchmark for other states and the international community. For the open source community, obtaining an exemption means that developers are not forced to embed verification modules—which could affect privacy or performance—into the underlying layers of operating systems to comply with California's specific regulations, which is crucial for maintaining the openness of the software ecosystem. However, this has also raised concerns among some child safety advocacy groups, who argue that excessive exemptions could weaken the act's protection for minors.
  
  There is significant disagreement regarding the "Digital Age Assurance Act." Supporters argue that in the absence of progress at the federal level, California's adoption of an "age signal" mechanism is a pragmatic compromise that protects children while avoiding the pitfalls of being overturned by courts for infringing on freedom of speech. Conversely, opponents or those with reservations point out that technical age verification is often difficult to achieve with complete precision and may lead platforms to over-collect private personal data, thereby creating new cybersecurity risks. Therefore, this amendment should not be over-interpreted as a "complete relaxation of regulation"; it is merely a precise adjustment for a specific technical field, rather than an abandonment of governance over the online environment.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on the transition period before the act officially takes effect in January 2027. First, how California regulators define the technical boundaries of "open source operating systems" to prevent platforms from circumventing regulation by disguising their open source status will be key to enforcement. Second, as the legal environment in California shifts, whether other regions will follow suit with similar exemption clauses or seek different balances between digital security and technological innovation will be an important indicator for global internet governance over the next two years. All parties in the industry chain need to closely monitor updates to relevant legal interpretations and assess their impact on global compliance configurations.

# 加州透過《數位年齡保障法》修正案，開源作業系統獲豁免

> 美國加州議會近期透過《數位年齡保障法》（DAAA）修正案，明確將開源作業系統排除在年齡驗證要求之外，旨在減輕開源社群合規負擔。


美國加州議會於當地時間 2026 年 8 月 26 日正式透過《數位年齡保障法》（Digital Age Assurance Act，簡稱 DAAA）的修正案（法案編號 AB-1856）。該法案的核心背景源於加州州長紐森（Gavin Newsom）於 2025 年 10 月簽署的原始法案（AB 1043），該法案原定於 2027 年 1 月正式實施。
其立法初衷是為了填補兒童網路安全的監管缺口，透過強制性的「年齡訊號」機制，要求數位平臺確認使用者年齡，以保護未成年人免受網路風險威脅。
根據各媒體報導的共同確認事項，此次透過的修正案 AB-1856 最重要的變革，在於明確將「開源作業系統」（Open Source Operating Systems）排除在法案的適用範圍之外。這項調整直接回應了科技界與開源社群長期以來的擔憂，即強制性的年齡驗證機制可能對去中心化、透明且協作的開源軟體開發模式造成嚴重的技術與行政合規壓力。透過此項豁免，開源社群得以在無需改變其核心開發架構的前提下，繼續推動軟體創新。
然而，關於該法案的執行細節與潛在影響，目前仍存在部分尚待釐清的資訊。儘管修正案解決了開源作業系統的合規問題，但對於其他型別的數位平臺，特別是涉及社交媒體、互動式娛樂軟體及廣告追蹤技術的平臺，其具體的「年齡訊號」技術標準與隱私保護邊界，仍需等待相關監管機構發布更詳細的執行細則。
此外，過去加州政府曾多次嘗試以強制手段限制平臺內容或驗證身分，但屢次因涉及憲法保障的言論自由問題，遭到聯邦法院駁回或阻止執行，這使得 AB-1856 在未來實施過程中，是否會面臨類似的法律挑戰，仍是產業關注的焦點。
此項政策變動對全球科技產業，尤其是依賴開源技術的軟體開發商與雲端服務供應商具有直接影響。加州作為全球科技創新的重鎮，其立法走向往往會成為其他州甚至國際社會的參考基準。對於開源社群而言，獲得豁免意味著開發者無需為了配合加州的特定法規，而被迫在作業系統底層植入可能影響隱私或效能的驗證模組，這對於維持軟體生態系統的開放性至關重要。但同時，這也引發了部分兒童安全倡議團體的疑慮，認為過多的豁免條款可能會削弱法案對未成年人的保護力度。
各界對於《數位年齡保障法》的觀點存在顯著分歧。支持者認為，在聯邦層級立法進度緩慢的情況下，加州採取「年齡訊號」機制是務實的折衷方案，既能保護兒童，又能避開過往因侵犯言論自由而遭法院撤銷的陷阱。然而，反對者或持保留態度者則指出，技術性的年齡驗證往往難以完全精準，且可能導致平臺過度收集個人隱私資料，反而造成新的資安風險。因此，不應過度解讀此次修正案為「完全放寬監管」，它僅是針對特定技術領域的精確調整，而非放棄對網路環境的治理。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於 2027 年 1 月法案正式實施前的過渡期。首先，加州監管機構如何定義「開源作業系統」的技術邊界，以防止平臺透過偽裝開源身分來規避監管，將是執法關鍵。其次，隨著加州法律環境的變動，其他地區是否會跟進類似的豁免條款，或是在數位安全與技術創新之間尋求不同的平衡點，將是未來兩年全球網路治理的重要觀察指標。產業鏈各方需密切監控相關法律解釋的更新，並評估其對全球合規性配置的影響。
