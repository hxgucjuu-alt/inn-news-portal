  # U.S. Expands Military Presence in Panama and Strengthens Economic Ties: Regional Dynamics Amid Global Supply Chain Restructuring
  
  > The United States has recently expanded its military deployment in Central America, dispatching 600 troops to Panama, while simultaneously deepening supply chain linkages with allies such as Taiwan through economic and trade activities to address the challenges of the politicization of global trade.
  
  In October 2026, the global geopolitical and economic landscape underwent significant shifts. In the security domain, the U.S. Department of Defense, in response to strategic requirements for "combating narco-terrorism," officially expanded its military presence in Latin America and the Caribbean. According to the Center for Economic and Policy Research (CEPR), citing a report from The New York Times, the U.S. Army's 10th Mountain Division has deployed a battalion-sized unit of 600 soldiers to a new jungle training facility in Panama. This move marks an escalation of U.S. military deployment in the region and has drawn international attention to regional security dynamics.
  
  Concurrently, the U.S. Department of State has continued to promote policy dialogues with Central American nations. On October 2, Juan Pablo Segura, Assistant Secretary of State for Western Hemisphere Affairs, hosted a briefing in Miami to emphasize the U.S. policy focus and diplomatic layout regarding the region.
  
  On the economic front, the United States is actively reshaping supply chain security through economic and trade cooperation. On October 13, 2026, Taiwan’s Ministry of Economic Affairs and the Taiwan External Trade Development Council (TAITRA), in conjunction with the American Institute in Taiwan (AIT) and the American State Offices Association (ASOA), held the "U.S. Business Day" in Taipei. The event invited 13 major U.S. buyers, including those from Texas, Illinois, and Montana, to conduct one-on-one procurement negotiations with Taiwanese firms in the semiconductor, ICT, machinery, and electronic components sectors.
  
  William Liu, Director General of the International Trade Administration at the Ministry of Economic Affairs, pointed out that this initiative aims to deepen Taiwan-U.S. economic and trade cooperation, leveraging Taiwan's comprehensive industrial supply chain and the vast market demand in the United States to establish a more robust and mutually beneficial relationship to cope with global supply chain uncertainties.
  
  However, the global economic and trade environment is facing severe challenges. Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi recently warned publicly that global interconnected systems—such as supply chains, trade, and technology—have increasingly been transformed into tools for coercion, leading to bottlenecks in global trade routes. Addressing this trend, former Premier Chen Chun also noted that trade confrontations between traditional allies like the United States and Canada have not only shaken the foundation of trust among allies but have also sounded an alarm for global bloc realignment and multilateral cooperation. Although the U.S. continues to court allies through economic and trade activities, concerns regarding the instrumentalization of trade for political maneuvering are deepening, which, combined with the assertive U.S. military deployment in Latin America, constitutes the complex image of current global geopolitics.
  
  Currently, beyond the official claim of "combating narco-terrorism," whether there are other strategic intentions behind the U.S. military presence in Panama remains a focus of discussion among international observers. Furthermore, the actual impact of U.S.-Canada trade friction on global supply chains, and how the U.S. balances its regional influence between "military expansion" and "economic reciprocity," remain to be seen. Whether there is a policy contradiction between the concerns over the "weaponization" of supply chains and the U.S. government's stated goal of "building trust and ensuring uninterrupted supply of materials" is also a key variable for assessing future global trade stability.
  
  Regarding these developments, economists and policy analysts caution against over-interpreting the impact of any single military deployment or economic activity. While the U.S. military presence in the Western Hemisphere has increased and it is actively strengthening supply chains with countries like Taiwan, these actions reflect a trend toward bloc formation driven by a lack of trust in the globalization process. For Central American and Caribbean nations, maintaining autonomous development and reducing the risk of regional conflict under the assertive military and economic policies of the U.S. will be an important challenge in the coming years. Additionally, when planning global operations, companies must consider trade route bottlenecks and political risks to avoid relying solely on traditional supply chain models.
  
  Future observations will focus on: first, the operational scale and frequency of the U.S. military's jungle training facility in Panama, and whether this facility will become a base for broader U.S. military operations in Latin America; second, whether Taiwan and the U.S. can effectively implement long-term supply contracts for semiconductors and critical components following the "U.S. Business Day" event, and mitigate the risk of supply chain weaponization; third, whether trade disputes between the U.S. and traditional allies like Canada will further deteriorate, triggering a chain reaction in global supply chains. Governments and multinational corporations need to closely monitor these regional dynamics to respond to potential trade and security shifts.

# 美國擴大駐軍巴拿馬與強化經貿鏈結：全球供應鏈重組下的區域動態

> 美國近期於中美洲擴大軍事部署，派遣六百名士兵進駐巴拿馬，同時透過經貿活動深化與臺灣等盟友的供應鏈鏈結，應對全球貿易政治化挑戰。


二○二六年十月，全球地緣政治與經濟版圖出現顯著變動。在安全領域，美國國防部因應「打擊毒品恐怖主義」之戰略需求，正式擴大在拉丁美洲與加勒比海地區的軍事存在。根據經濟政策研究中心（CEPR）引述《紐約時報》報導，美軍第十山地師已派遣六百名士兵組成的營級部隊，進駐位於巴拿馬的全新叢林訓練設施。此舉標誌著美國在該地區軍事部署的升級，並引發國際社會對於區域安全域性勢的關注。
與此同時，美國國務院亦持續推動與中美洲各國的政策對話，於十月二日由西半球事務局助理國務卿胡安·帕布羅·塞古拉（Juan Pablo Segura）在邁阿密主持簡報會，強調美國對該區域的政策關注與外交佈局。
在經濟層面，美國正積極透過經貿合作重塑供應鏈安全。二○二六年十月十三日，臺灣經濟部與外貿協會聯合美國在臺協會（AIT）及美國州政府辦事處協會（ASOA），在臺北舉辦「全球商機鏈結總動員－美國場」（U.S. Business Day）。活動邀請包括德州、伊利諾州及蒙大拿州在內的十三家美國大型買主，與臺灣半導體、資通訊、機械及電子零元件業者進行一對一採購洽談。
經濟部國際貿易署署長劉威廉指出，此舉旨在深化臺美經貿合作，利用臺灣完整的產業供應鏈與美國龐大的市場需求，建立更為穩健的互惠關係，以應對全球供應鏈不確定性。
然而，全球經貿環境正面臨嚴峻挑戰。印度總理莫迪近期公開警告，供應鏈、貿易與技術等全球相互關聯系統，已逐漸被轉化為實施脅迫的工具，導致全球貿易路線出現阻塞點。針對此趨勢，前行政院長陳衝亦指出，傳統盟邦如美國與加拿大之間出現貿易對抗，不僅動搖了盟邦間的信任基礎，更為全球陣營重組與多邊合作敲響警鐘。雖然美方持續透過經貿活動拉攏盟友，但各界對於貿易被政治角力工具化的擔憂日益加深，這與美國在拉丁美洲擴大軍事部署的強勢作風，共同構成了當前全球地緣政治的複雜影象。
目前，關於美軍進駐巴拿馬的具體任務細節，除官方宣稱的「打擊毒品恐怖主義」外，是否存在其他戰略意圖，仍是國際觀察家的討論焦點。此外，美加貿易摩擦對全球供應鏈的實際衝擊程度，以及美國如何在「軍事擴張」與「經貿互惠」之間平衡其區域影響力，尚待後續觀察。各方對於供應鏈「武器化」的擔憂，與美國政府強調的「建立信任與保障物資不斷鏈」目標之間，是否存在政策執行上的矛盾，亦是各界評估未來全球貿易穩定性的關鍵變數。
針對上述發展，經濟學界與政策分析師提醒，不應過度解讀單一軍事部署或經貿活動的影響。雖然美國在西半球的軍事存在感增強，且積極與臺灣等國強化供應鏈，但這些行動背後反映的是全球化程式中，因信任缺失而導致的陣營化趨勢。對於中美洲與加勒比海國家而言，如何在美方強勢的軍事與經濟政策下，維持自主發展並降低區域衝突風險，將是未來數年內的重要課題。此外，企業在進行全球佈局時，需考量貿易路線阻塞點與政治風險，避免單純依賴傳統供應鏈模式。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於：第一，美軍在巴拿馬叢林訓練設施的運作規模與頻率，以及該設施是否會成為美國在拉丁美洲進行更廣泛軍事行動的基地；第二，臺灣與美國在「全球商機鏈結」活動後，能否實際落實半導體與關鍵零元件的長期供應合約，並緩解供應鏈被武器化的風險；第三，美國與加拿大等傳統盟邦的貿易爭端是否會進一步惡化，進而引發全球供應鏈的連鎖反應。各國政府與跨國企業需密切監控上述區域動態，以應對可能出現的貿易與安全變局。
