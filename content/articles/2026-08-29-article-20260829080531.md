  > The security landscape in Northeast Asia has recently intensified. The foreign ministers of South Korea, the United States, and Japan have reaffirmed their commitment to denuclearization and stability in the Taiwan Strait. Meanwhile, the China-Japan-ROK trilateral diplomatic framework faces challenges of marginalization, even as Japan and South Korea deepen their security and economic cooperation in response to regional dynamics.
  
  The Northeast Asian region is currently facing complex security and diplomatic challenges, with intensified great power competition and geopolitical tensions. According to various public sources, the regional situation involves not only the denuclearization of the Korean Peninsula but also peace and stability in the Taiwan Strait, the security of industrial supply chains, and policies regarding emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence. Since 2026, the strategic environment in Northeast Asia has undergone significant changes: on one hand, the strengthening of the ROK-U.S.-Japan trilateral cooperation mechanism, and on the other, the substantive hollowing out of the traditional China-Japan-ROK diplomatic framework. These shifts reflect the readjustment and divergence of national diplomatic strategies in the face of North Korean nuclear threats and great power competition.
  
  Regarding ROK-U.S.-Japan trilateral cooperation, the foreign ministers of South Korea, the United States, and Japan recently held a trilateral meeting in Manila. Following the meeting, they issued a joint statement reaffirming the principle of denuclearization on the Korean Peninsula and emphasized their commitment to strengthening trilateral cooperation to address regional challenges. Furthermore, the parties explicitly included peace and stability in the Taiwan Strait on their agenda, demonstrating a high degree of consensus among the three nations regarding security in the Indo-Pacific region. Simultaneously, bilateral relations between Japan and South Korea have seen significant progress. In their meeting this January, Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi and South Korean President Lee Jae-myung reached a consensus to deepen security and economic ties. Their discussions covered industrial supply chains, artificial intelligence cooperation, and coordination with their common ally, the United States, aimed at addressing the increasingly tense situation in Northeast Asia.
  
  However, in contrast to the active ROK-U.S.-Japan cooperation, the China-Japan-ROK trilateral diplomatic framework is facing a crisis of marginalization. Although the ninth summit was held in May 2024 with Japan assuming the role of coordinating chair, preparations for the tenth summit have stalled. According to analysis, severe friction between China and Japan is the primary reason for the hollowing out of the framework's functionality. While the three parties have not officially canceled or postponed the summit, the substantive influence of the diplomatic framework has been significantly weakened.
  
  Furthermore, the regional energy market has been affected by climate and seasonal factors. Recent high temperatures in Northeast Asia have led to fluctuations in liquefied natural gas (LNG) prices, indicating that beyond security issues, the economic pressures brought about by energy supply chains and climate change cannot be ignored.
  
  The aforementioned adjustments in diplomatic and security policies have had multiple impacts on Northeast Asia. First, the ROK-U.S.-Japan trilateral mechanism has become a primary stabilizer for regional security, playing a key role in addressing North Korea's nuclear development and regional military deterrence. Second, the improvement in Japan-South Korea relations has not only eased historical tensions but also established closer industrial ties in high-tech fields such as semiconductors and artificial intelligence, which is crucial for responding to global supply chain restructuring. However, the marginalization of the China-Japan-ROK framework may lead to the loss of communication channels within the region, increasing the risk of miscalculation. Especially against the backdrop of an intensifying arms race in Northeast Asia, the lack of an effective multilateral dialogue mechanism will make the regional situation more fragile.
  
  Regarding the current situation, there are significant differences in perspectives. Some observers believe that strengthening the ROK-U.S.-Japan alliance is a necessary means to counter Chinese expansion and North Korean provocations, possessing high strategic legitimacy. However, other views suggest that excessive reliance on military alliances may further stimulate confrontational sentiments in the region, leading to an intensified security dilemma. Additionally, the failure of the China-Japan-ROK framework reflects that geopolitical contradictions have transcended the scope of economic cooperation, causing regional economic integration to face the challenge of politicization.
  
  Scholars caution that when interpreting these diplomatic dynamics, one should not oversimplify the strategic intentions of each country. In particular, the delicate balance that countries strike between pursuing national security and economic interests is often constrained by both domestic politics and relations with international allies.
  
  Looking ahead, the development of the situation in Northeast Asia will focus on several key indicators. First, whether the ROK-U.S.-Japan trilateral group can continue to implement the consensus reached in their meetings and expand the scope of cooperation to broader areas of economic security. Second, whether the China-Japan-ROK trilateral framework can overcome friction through diplomatic mediation and restart high-level dialogue will be an important bellwether for observing regional stability. Finally, North Korea's nuclear policy and Russia's movements in the Far East will continue to affect the military balance in Northeast Asia. How countries strike a balance between an arms race and diplomatic dialogue will determine the peace and prosperity of Northeast Asia in the coming years; subsequent developments require close attention to the substantive actions and policy shifts of these countries in regional security forums.

# 東北亞安全域性勢與外交架構變動：韓美日合作與中日韓框架之消長

> 近期東北亞安全域性勢持續升溫，韓美日三方外長重申無核化與臺海穩定，與此同時，中日韓三邊外交架構面臨邊緣化挑戰，日韓兩國則在區域局勢下深化安全與經濟合作。


東北亞地區近期面臨複雜的安全與外交挑戰，區域內大國競爭與地緣政治緊張態勢加劇。根據多方公開資訊，該地區不僅涉及朝鮮半島的無核化議題，亦包含臺海和平穩定、工業供應鏈安全及人工智慧等新興科技政策。自2026年以來，東北亞的戰略環境發生顯著變化，一方面是韓美日三方合作機制的強化，另一方面則是傳統中日韓外交架構的實質性空洞化。這些變動反映了該地區在面對北韓核威脅與強權競爭時，各國外交策略的重新調整與分化。
在韓美日三方合作方面，近期南韓、美國及日本外交首長於馬尼拉舉行三方會談，會後共同宣告重申朝鮮半島無核化原則，並強調將持續強化三邊合作以應對區域挑戰。此外，各方亦明確將臺海和平穩定納入討論議程，顯示出韓美日三國對印太區域安全的高度共識。與此同時，日本與南韓的雙邊關係亦有顯著進展，日本首相高市早苗與南韓總統李在明在今年1月的會晤中，達成深化安全與經濟關係的共識，雙方議題涵蓋工業供應鏈、人工智慧合作，以及與共同盟友美國的協調，旨在應對日益緊張的東北亞局勢。
然而，與韓美日合作熱絡形成對比的是，中日韓三邊外交架構正處於邊緣化危機。儘管在2024年5月舉行了第九次峰會，且由日本接任協調主席國，但目前第十次峰會的籌備進度停滯。根據分析，中日兩國之間的嚴重摩擦是導致該架構功能空洞化的主要原因。雖然三方並未正式取消或延後峰會，但外交框架的實質影響力已大幅削弱。
此外，區域內能源市場亦受到氣候與季節性因素影響，東北亞近期高溫導致液化天然氣（LNG）價格波動，顯示出該地區在安全議題之外，能源供應鏈與氣候變遷帶來的經濟壓力同樣不容忽視。
上述外交與安全政策的調整，對東北亞產生了多重影響。首先，韓美日三邊機制已成為區域安全的主要穩定器，對於北韓的核武發展與區域軍事威懾具有關鍵作用。其次，日韓關係的改善不僅緩解了歷史遺留的緊張情緒，更在半導體與人工智慧等高科技領域建立了更緊密的產業連結，這對於應對全球供應鏈重組至關重要。然而，中日韓框架的邊緣化，則可能導致區域內溝通管道的喪失，增加誤判風險，尤其在當前東北亞軍備競賽加劇的背景下，缺乏有效的多邊對話機制將使區域局勢更加脆弱。
針對當前局勢，各界觀點存在顯著差異。部分觀察家認為，韓美日同盟的強化是應對中國擴張與北韓挑釁的必要手段，具有高度的戰略正當性；然而，亦有觀點指出，過度依賴軍事同盟可能進一步刺激區域內的對抗情緒，導致安全困境加劇。此外，中日韓框架的失靈，反映了地緣政治矛盾已超越經濟合作的範疇，使得區域經濟整合面臨政治化的挑戰。
學界提醒，在解讀這些外交動態時，不應過度簡化各國的戰略意圖，特別是各國在追求國家安全與經濟利益之間的微妙平衡，往往受到國內政治與國際盟友關係的共同制約。
展望未來，東北亞的局勢發展將聚焦於幾個關鍵指標。首先，韓美日三方是否能持續落實會談共識，並將合作範圍擴充套件至更廣泛的經濟安全領域。其次，中日韓三邊架構是否能透過外交斡旋克服摩擦，重啟高層對話，將是觀察區域穩定性的重要風向球。最後，北韓的核武政策與俄羅斯在遠東地區的動向，亦將持續牽動東北亞的軍事平衡。各國如何在軍備競賽與外交對話之間取得平衡，將決定未來數年東北亞的和平與繁榮，後續需密切關注各國在區域安全論壇上的實質行動與政策轉向。
