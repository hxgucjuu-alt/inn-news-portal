  # Houthi Rebels Claim Attack on Key Saudi Pipeline, Global Energy Supply Faces Potential Risk
  
  > Yemen's Houthi rebels have claimed attacks on Saudi Arabia's East-West Pipeline and oil facilities in Yanbu, sparking widespread concern over the security of energy transport and the stability of global supplies.
  
  This incident occurred on November 12, 2026, when Yemen's Houthi rebels issued a statement claiming they had launched military attacks against a pumping station on Saudi Arabia's strategically vital "East-West Pipeline," as well as oil facilities in Yanbu on the Red Sea coast. As a critical piece of infrastructure for Saudi crude oil exports, the East-West Pipeline’s primary function is to allow Saudi oil to bypass the Strait of Hormuz and be transported directly to the Red Sea coast for export.
  
  Because the Strait of Hormuz is the world's most important chokepoint for crude oil transport, the strategic status of this pipeline has long been a focus of the international energy market. Statistics indicate that the infrastructure under threat accounts for approximately 4% of the global crude oil supply; any disruption in operations could cause a significant shock to the global energy market.
  
  According to reports compiled by Reuters and Yahoo Entertainment, the Houthi rebels explicitly mentioned the aforementioned attacks in a statement released on Thursday, targeting key energy facilities within Saudi Arabia. The group has long maintained close ties with Iran and, against the backdrop of the Yemeni civil war, has repeatedly conducted cross-border attacks on infrastructure within Saudi Arabia. This incident once again highlights the vulnerability of global supply chains to geopolitical tensions in the Middle East. For energy markets that rely on Middle Eastern crude imports, any news regarding damage to oil pipelines is quickly reflected in the volatility of international oil prices.
  
  There remain several uncertainties regarding the details of this incident, primarily because the statement released by the Houthi rebels did not specify the exact date and time of the attacks. Furthermore, as of the time of the Reuters report, the Saudi Arabian government had not yet issued a public response to the Houthi statement, nor had it confirmed whether the relevant facilities were indeed attacked or if any substantive damage had occurred. Due to the lack of on-site assessment reports from Saudi officials or international monitoring agencies, it is currently impossible to confirm whether pipeline operations have been interrupted or if there have been any casualties or equipment damage. The scale of the attack and its actual impact remain in a state of high speculation.
  
  If the attack is confirmed and has caused damage to the facilities, the direct impact would not be limited to Saudi Arabia's energy export capacity; it could lead to a panic-driven reaction in the global energy market. The operational stability of the East-West Pipeline serves as an important buffer mechanism in the global energy supply chain, especially given the heightened geopolitical risks in the Middle East; the security of this pipeline is directly linked to the stability of global oil prices. For industries, transportation, and public energy policies worldwide that rely on oil, any disruption could trigger a chain reaction, leading to soaring energy prices, exacerbating global inflationary pressures, and imposing a heavy fiscal burden on energy-importing economies.
  
  Regarding this event, there are significant differences in perspectives, and over-interpretation should be avoided. The Houthi statement may carry political signaling, aimed at expanding their geopolitical influence by threatening energy infrastructure, and does not necessarily mean that their actual military actions have caused the intended destruction. International analysts warn that in the absence of third-party verification, the unilateral claims of armed groups should be viewed with caution and should not be taken as established fact. Furthermore, as a major global oil producer, Saudi Arabia possesses the necessary repair capabilities and response mechanisms; even if the pipeline were damaged, it could potentially mitigate the impact in the short term through other export routes or inventory scheduling.
  
  Therefore, market observers suggest continuing to monitor official statements from Saudi Arabia and subsequent assessment reports from agencies such as the International Energy Agency (IEA).
  
  Future observation will focus on whether Saudi officials issue a formal clarification or statement regarding this claim, and whether international monitoring agencies can verify the condition of the facilities through satellite imagery or communication channels. At the same time, the intensity of the international community's condemnation of such Houthi attacks on civilian and critical energy infrastructure, and whether further diplomatic or security measures will be taken, will be key indicators in assessing whether the situation in the Middle East will further deteriorate. Energy market participants should closely monitor oil price fluctuations and pay attention to whether the global energy supply chain experiences short-term supply tightness or logistics schedule adjustments due to the escalation of geopolitical risks.

# 胡塞武裝聲稱襲擊沙國關鍵輸油管線，全球能源供應面臨潛在風險

> 葉門胡塞武裝宣稱攻擊沙烏地阿拉伯東西輸油管線及延布石油設施，此舉引發全球對能源運輸安全與供應穩定性的高度擔憂。


本起事件發生於2026年11月12日，葉門胡塞武裝（Houthis）對外發表宣告，宣稱已針對沙烏地阿拉伯境內極具戰略意義的「東西輸油管線」（East-West Pipeline）其中一座泵站，以及位於紅海沿岸的延布（Yanbu）石油設施發動軍事攻擊。東西輸油管線作為沙國原油出口的重要基礎設施，主要功能在於協助沙國原油繞過荷姆茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz），直接輸送至紅海沿岸進行出口。
由於荷姆茲海峽為全球最重要的原油運輸咽喉，該管線的戰略地位長期受到國際能源市場的高度關注。據統計，此次受威脅的基礎設施涉及全球約4%的原油供應量，一旦營運中斷，恐對全球能源市場造成顯著衝擊。
根據路透社與Yahoo Entertainment整理的報導，胡塞武裝在週四發布的宣告中明確提及了上述攻擊行動，並將矛頭指向沙國境內的關鍵能源設施。該組織長期以來與伊朗保持密切關係，並在葉門內戰背景下，多次針對沙烏地阿拉伯境內的基礎設施進行跨境襲擊。此次事件再次凸顯了中東地區地緣政治緊張局勢對全球供應鏈的脆弱性，特別是對於依賴中東原油進口的能源市場而言，任何關於輸油管線受損的訊息，皆會迅速反映在國際油價的波動上。
目前關於此事件的細節仍存在多項不確定因素，主要在於胡塞武裝發布的宣告中，並未明確說明攻擊發生的確切日期與時間點。此外，截至路透社發稿時，沙烏地阿拉伯政府官方尚未針對胡塞武裝的宣告做出公開回應，也未證實相關設施是否確實遭受攻擊，或是否出現實質性的損害。由於缺乏來自沙國官方或國際監測機構的現場評估報告，目前無法確認輸油管線的作業是否已經中斷，或者是否存在任何人員傷亡與裝置毀損的具體情況。外界對於攻擊的規模與實際造成的影響程度，仍處於高度推測階段。
若此次攻擊屬實且造成設施損壞，其直接影響將不僅限於沙烏地阿拉伯的能源出口能力，更可能導致全球能源市場的恐慌性反應。東西輸油管線的運作穩定性，是全球能源供應鏈中的重要緩衝機制，特別是在中東地區地緣政治風險升高的情況下，該管線的安全性直接關聯到全球油價的穩定。對於全球依賴石油的產業、交通運輸及公共能源政策而言，任何中斷都可能引發連鎖反應，導致能源價格飆升，進而加劇全球通膨壓力，並對依賴能源進口的經濟體造成沈重的財政負擔。
針對此事件，各界觀點存在顯著差異，且應避免過度解讀。胡塞武裝的宣告可能帶有政治宣示意味，旨在透過威脅能源基礎設施來擴大其地緣政治影響力，並不一定代表其實際軍事行動已造成預期破壞。國際分析人士提醒，在缺乏第三方驗證的情況下，應謹慎看待武裝組織的單方面說法，不應將其視為既定事實。此外，沙國作為全球主要的產油國，具備一定的修復能力與應對機制，即便管線受損，短期內亦可能透過其他出口路徑或庫存排程來減緩衝擊。
因此，市場觀察者建議應持續關注沙國官方的正式宣告，以及國際能源總署（IEA）等機構的後續評估報告。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於沙烏地阿拉伯官方是否會針對此宣告發布正式澄清或說明，以及國際監測機構是否能透過衛星影像或通訊管道，驗證相關設施的受損狀況。同時，國際社會對於胡塞武裝此類針對民用與關鍵能源設施攻擊行為的譴責力度，以及是否會採取進一步的外交或安全措施，將是評估中東局勢是否進一步惡化的關鍵指標。能源市場參與者應密切留意油價變動，並關注全球能源供應鏈是否會因地緣政治風險的升級，而出現短期的供應吃緊或物流排程調整。
