電商平台亞馬遜（Amazon）秋季促銷活動「Prime Big Deal Days」進入最後階段。根據《PCMag》等科技媒體報導，儘管活動時限即將截止或部分促銷檔期進入尾聲，市場上仍有大量經專業評測推薦的消費性科技產品維持折扣。

報導指出，持續降價的產品類別涵蓋筆記型電腦、平板電腦、智慧型手機、電視、耳機及智慧家庭裝置。《PCMag》列出超過150項精選科技優惠，而《WIRED》等媒體亦指出，即使促銷活動接近終點，部分精選設備的限時折扣仍持續供消費者選購。

資料顯示，各家媒體在促銷後期持續追蹤降價動態。這類秋季促銷被視為年末購物季前的重要採購節點，但相關報導亦提醒，具體商品的優惠幅度與存貨狀態仍將隨著活動正式落幕及庫存變動而調整。

## English edition

E-commerce platform Amazon's fall shopping event, Prime Big Deal Days, has entered its closing hours. According to reports from technology publications such as PCMag, a substantial volume of expert-vetted consumer tech discounts remains accessible even as the primary promotional window draws to a close.

Media coverage indicates that lingering markdowns span multiple hardware categories, including laptops, tablets, smartphones, televisions, headphones, and smart home devices. PCMag highlighted more than 150 vetted deals, while WIRED similarly reported that dozens of recommended products retained promotional pricing.

Reports show that tech outlets closely tracked pricing shifts throughout the final stretch of the event. While these fall sales serve as an early shopping window ahead of the year-end holiday season, coverage notes that product availability and discounts remain subject to inventory changes as the event wraps up.
