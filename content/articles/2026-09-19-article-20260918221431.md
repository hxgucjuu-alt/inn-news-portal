  # O-Bank Partners with BitoGroup to Launch Corporate Stablecoin Cross-Border Payment Pilot Program
  
  > O-Bank has announced a partnership with BitoGroup to launch a corporate stablecoin cross-border payment service on September 15, becoming the first bank in Taiwan to integrate stablecoins into its corporate online banking platform, aiming to optimize cross-border trade payment processes for businesses.
  
  O-Bank officially announced on September 13, 2026, that it will enter into a strategic partnership with the domestic virtual asset service provider BitoGroup to jointly promote a "Corporate Stablecoin Cross-Border Payment Service" pilot program. This service is scheduled to go live on September 15, with its core objective being to address the growing demand for blockchain payment tools among enterprises amid the rapid expansion of the global digital economy. As digital assets increasingly enter the mainstream financial landscape, how traditional banking institutions can leverage technological integration to resolve issues regarding settlement efficiency and costs in international trade has become a key development indicator in the fintech sector.
  
  By embedding the stablecoin payment function directly into its corporate online banking system, O-Bank marks a substantive leap in blockchain application scenarios for Taiwanese financial institutions, attempting to build a legal and compliant bridge between traditional foreign exchange and cryptocurrency payments.
  
  According to information confirmed by both O-Bank and BitoGroup, the operational mechanism of this pilot program has been clearly defined. Corporate clients wishing to use this service must meet dual account-opening requirements: first, the enterprise must open a corporate account with O-Bank and activate its corporate online banking system; second, the enterprise must also complete the account opening application and relevant review procedures at the Bito exchange. Once these processes are completed, the enterprise can conduct cross-border stablecoin collection through the integrated platform.
  
  This move not only simplifies the administrative processes for enterprises handling crypto assets but also adds financial regulatory transparency to what were previously relatively decentralized stablecoin transactions through the bank's risk control mechanisms, ensuring that businesses have a more stable and traceable flow of funds when conducting cross-border trade settlements.
  
  Although O-Bank has clearly announced the service launch date and application process, there remain areas regarding the practical operation of this service that are yet to be confirmed or observed. For instance, details such as the range of supported stablecoins, the real-time exchange rate conversion mechanism between the exchange and the bank, and specific complaint-handling procedures in the event of blockchain network congestion or abnormal transactions have not been fully detailed in the public information.
  
  Furthermore, as this is a "Pilot Program," it remains to be seen whether there will be a cap on the scale of the service or if it will be limited to specific industries. Its potential for future large-scale adoption can only be assessed after observing its actual operation and market feedback following the official launch on September 15.
  
  The launch of this service has profound implications for Taiwan's financial and trade industries. For enterprises engaged in long-term cross-border trade that require high payment speeds, stablecoins offer the advantages of 24/7 operation and rapid cross-border settlement. If seamlessly integrated with traditional bank accounts, this could significantly reduce the time costs and intermediary bank fees associated with traditional cross-border remittances. At the same time, this compels Taiwanese financial institutions to accelerate digital transformation, re-examine the positioning of virtual assets in trade finance, and incorporate blockchain technology into existing financial service systems under a compliant framework to maintain competitiveness in global digital trade.
  
  However, the market should maintain a neutral and cautious attitude toward such innovative services. While the partnership between O-Bank and BitoGroup provides convenience for businesses, the crypto asset market itself is highly volatile. Although stablecoins are named "stable," the transparency of their pegged assets, the audit status of the issuers' reserves, and the continuously changing regulatory policies on virtual assets worldwide are all risk factors that enterprises must consider when adopting this service. Furthermore, this should not be over-interpreted as a "complete replacement for traditional foreign exchange." Currently, the service is positioned as a supplementary tool aimed at resolving payment needs in specific scenarios; businesses should still comprehensively evaluate regulatory compliance, asset security, and transaction costs when making decisions.
  
  Future observations will focus on the execution effectiveness of this pilot program. First, market acceptance will be a key indicator, specifically how many enterprises are willing to incorporate stablecoins into their financial settlement systems. Second, the subsequent regulatory stance of financial authorities toward this "bank-virtual asset provider partnership" model will determine whether such services can be further expanded into broader financial operations. Additionally, as the application of stablecoin payments in cross-border trade increases, how banks establish robust Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) mechanisms to address the anonymity challenges of blockchain transactions will be a top priority for the financial industry to monitor.

# 王道銀行攜手幣託集團啟動企業穩定幣跨境收款先導計畫

> 王道銀行宣佈與幣託集團合作，將於9月15日推出企業穩定幣跨境收款服務，成為國內首家整合穩定幣至企網銀平臺的銀行，旨在最佳化企業跨境貿易支付流程。


王道銀行（O-Bank）於2026年9月13日正式對外宣佈，將與國內虛擬資產服務業者幣託集團（BitoGroup）展開策略合作，共同推動「企業穩定幣跨境收款服務」先導計畫。此項服務預計於9月15日正式上線，其核心目標在於應對全球數位經濟快速擴張下，企業對區塊鏈支付工具日益增長的需求。隨著數位資產逐漸進入主流金融視野，傳統銀行業如何透過技術整合，解決企業在國際貿易中面臨的資金結算效率與成本問題，已成為金融科技領域的重要發展指標。
王道銀行此次將穩定幣收款功能直接嵌入企業網路銀行系統，標誌著臺灣金融機構在區塊鏈應用場景的實質性跨越，試圖為傳統外匯與加密貨幣支付之間搭建一座合法合規的橋樑。
根據王道銀行與幣託集團共同確認的資訊顯示，該項先導計畫的運作機制已明確規範。企業客戶若欲使用此服務，必須滿足雙重開戶條件：首先，企業需在王道銀行開立企業帳戶，並啟用其企業網路銀行系統；其次，該企業亦須在幣託交易所完成開戶申請與相關審查程式。一旦完成上述流程，企業即可透過整合後的平臺進行穩定幣的跨境收款作業。
此舉不僅簡化了企業處理加密資產的行政流程，亦透過銀行端的風控機制，為原本相對去中心化的穩定幣交易增加了金融監管的透明度，確保企業在進行跨境貿易結算時，能擁有更為穩定且可追蹤的資金流向。
儘管王道銀行已明確公佈服務啟動時間與申請流程，但關於此項服務在實際操作中的細節，目前仍存在部分尚待確認與觀察的空間。例如，針對不同幣別穩定幣的支援範圍、交易所與銀行間的即時匯率轉換機制，以及在遭遇區塊鏈網路壅塞或異常交易時的具體客訴處理流程，目前公開資訊中尚未詳盡說明。
此外，由於此為「先導計畫」（Pilot Program），其服務規模是否設有上限，或是僅針對特定產業型別的企業開放，仍有待9月15日正式上線後，觀察其實際運作狀況與市場反饋，方能評估該服務在未來大規模普及的可能性。
此項服務的推出，對臺灣的金融貿易產業具有深遠影響。對於長期從事跨境貿易、且對支付速度有高度要求的企業而言，穩定幣具備全天候運作與跨境結算速度快的優勢，若能與傳統銀行帳戶無縫接軌，將能顯著降低企業在傳統銀行體系下進行跨境匯款所需的時間成本與中間銀行費用。同時，這也促使臺灣的金融機構必須加速數位轉型，重新審視虛擬資產在貿易金融中的定位，並在合規前提下，將區塊鏈技術納入現有的金融服務體系，以維持在全球數位貿易中的競爭力。
然而，市場對於此類創新服務仍需保持中性且審慎的態度。雖然王道銀行與幣託集團的合作為企業提供了便利，但加密資產市場本身具備高度波動性，穩定幣雖名為「穩定」，但其掛鉤資產的透明度、發行商的儲備金審計狀況，以及全球各國對虛擬資產監管政策的持續變動，均是企業在採用此項服務時必須納入考量的風險因子。此外，不應過度解讀此舉為「全面取代傳統外匯」，目前該服務定位仍屬於補充性質，旨在解決特定場景下的收款需求，企業在決策時仍需綜合評估法規遵循、資產安全與交易成本等多重面向。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於該先導計畫的執行成效。首先，市場接受度將是關鍵指標，即有多少企業願意將穩定幣納入其財務結算系統；其次，金融監管機構對此類「銀行與虛擬資產業者合作」模式的後續監管態度，將決定此類服務能否進一步擴充套件至更廣泛的金融業務。此外，隨著穩定幣支付在跨境貿易中的應用增加，銀行端如何建立完善的防洗錢（AML）與認識你的客戶（KYC）機制，以應對區塊鏈交易的匿名性挑戰，將是金融產業觀察的重中之重。
