  > Australia and New Zealand have recently taken frequent actions in their foreign policies. Beyond actively seeking a seat on the UN Security Council and adjusting their positions on Palestinian statehood, they have called for increased U.S. engagement in the Pacific to address the challenges of an increasingly volatile global landscape.
  
  As the core nations of Oceania, Australia and New Zealand have long played pivotal roles in regional security and international affairs. Recently, both countries have demonstrated greater ambition in their diplomatic strategies. The Australian government has officially announced its bid for a seat on the United Nations Security Council for 2029, intending to exert greater influence on the international stage. Meanwhile, New Zealand has maintained its stance on military operations, emphasizing that the transit of its naval vessels through sensitive waters, such as the Taiwan Strait, is a routine passage based on international law, demonstrating its determination to uphold freedom of navigation. These diplomatic and military moves are rooted in both nations' profound concerns regarding the current shifts in the global landscape.
  
  According to joint reports, New Zealand’s Minister of Foreign Affairs, Winston Peters, has recently issued public warnings on multiple occasions, clearly stating that the global situation is becoming increasingly volatile. Peters emphasized that the Pacific region is currently facing "unwelcome geopolitical maneuvering," which poses a potential threat to regional stability. Based on this assessment, the New Zealand government has strongly urged the United States to engage more actively in Pacific affairs to balance the increasingly complex power structure within the region. Australia and New Zealand have shown a high degree of consistency in their positions on maintaining regional security and international order, with both countries leaning toward strengthening ties with traditional allies to counter the infiltration and interference of external forces.
  
  However, there remain subtle differences and points requiring confirmation regarding the specific implementation of certain foreign policies. The news concerning Australia and New Zealand’s potential recognition of Palestinian statehood has triggered a strong backlash from Israel, even leading to tensions in bilateral diplomatic relations. Although this issue has been widely mentioned in media reports, public information has yet to provide a clear timeline or execution details regarding the specific schedule for recognition, supporting diplomatic measures, or the internal decision-making processes of the two governments. Furthermore, the specific frequency of New Zealand naval vessels transiting the Taiwan Strait and the subsequent diplomatic effects remain within the realm of constantly shifting international politics, leaving room for different interpretations regarding whether this move will trigger a chain reaction in the regional situation.
  
  These shifts in foreign policy have had a profound impact on the industries, education, and public policies of the Oceania region. First, at the policy level, both governments are attempting to elevate Pacific issues to a global strategic height by participating in international organizations and strengthening military diplomacy, which serves as a benchmark for the development strategies of other island nations in the region. Second, in terms of industry and education, as geopolitical risks rise, Australia and New Zealand are re-evaluating their connections to global supply chains and may increase investment in the cultivation of defense and diplomatic talent, as well as relevant think tanks, to cope with the increasingly severe international environment.

# 澳洲與紐西蘭在地緣政治與外交政策上的多重挑戰與轉向

> 澳洲與紐西蘭近期在外交政策上動作頻頻，除積極爭取聯合國安理會席次與調整巴勒斯坦國家地位立場外，更針對太平洋地區的地緣政治局勢，呼籲美國加強參與，以應對全球日益動盪的挑戰。


澳洲與紐西蘭作為大洋洲的核心國家，長期在區域安全與國際事務中扮演關鍵角色。近期，兩國在外交戰略上展現出更為積極的企圖心。澳洲政府已正式宣佈將角逐2029年聯合國安理會席次，意圖在國際舞臺上發揮更大的影響力。與此同時，紐西蘭在軍事行動上亦維持其立場，強調其軍艦航經臺海等敏感水域是基於國際法所進行的例行性透過，展現出捍衛航行自由的決心。這些外交與軍事舉措，背景均源於兩國對於當前全球局勢變遷的深刻擔憂。
根據聯合報導，紐西蘭外交部長皮特斯（Winston Peters）近期多次公開示警，明確指出全球局勢正變得愈來愈動盪不安。皮特斯強調，太平洋地區目前正面臨「不受歡迎的地緣政治操弄」，這對區域穩定構成了潛在威脅。基於此一判斷，紐西蘭政府已強烈呼籲美國必須更加積極地參與太平洋事務，以平衡區域內日益複雜的權力結構。澳洲與紐西蘭在維護區域安全與國際秩序的立場上，展現出高度的一致性，兩國均傾向透過強化與傳統盟友的聯絡，來應對外部勢力的滲透與幹擾。
然而，在部分外交政策的具體落實上，各方資訊仍存在細微差異與待確認之處。關於澳洲與紐西蘭擬承認巴勒斯坦國家地位的訊息，已引發以色列方面的強烈反彈，甚至導致雙邊外交關係緊張。儘管此議題在媒體報導中被廣泛提及，但針對具體承認的時間表、外交配套措施以及兩國政府內部的決策細節，目前公開資訊並未給出明確的時程與執行細節。此外，紐西蘭軍艦航經臺海的具體頻率與後續外交效應，亦屬於持續變動的國際政治範疇，各界對於此舉是否會引發區域局勢的連鎖反應，仍存有不同解讀空間。
這些外交政策的轉向，對大洋洲地區的產業、教育與公共政策產生了深遠影響。首先，在政策層面，兩國政府正試圖透過參與國際組織與強化軍事外交，將太平洋地區的議題提升至全球戰略高度，這對於區域內其他島國的發展策略具有指標意義。其次，在產業與教育方面，隨著地緣政治風險的升高，澳洲與紐西蘭正重新評估其與全球供應鏈的連結，並可能在未來增加針對國防、外交人才的培養與相關智庫的投入，以應對日益嚴峻的國際環境。
這些調整不僅是外交上的宣示，更是兩國在經濟與社會穩定上尋求避險的必要手段。
儘管澳洲與紐西蘭展現了強硬的外交姿態，但各界專家與觀察家仍提醒，不應過度解讀這些政策轉向的即時效應。首先，承認巴勒斯坦國家地位的議題涉及極為複雜的國際法與中東地緣政治，兩國政府在推動此政策時，勢必面臨來自國內民意與國際盟友的多重壓力，其政策的可持續性仍有待觀察。其次，呼籲美國加強參與太平洋事務，雖然符合兩國的戰略利益，但美國自身的內部政治變數以及對全球戰略的優先順序調整，亦可能成為影響該訴求成效的限制因素。
因此，將單一的外交宣告視為政策定論，恐會低估國際政治運作的複雜度與不確定性。
展望未來，澳洲與紐西蘭在國際事務中的表現，將成為觀察大洋洲地緣政治走向的關鍵指標。後續觀察重點包括：第一，澳洲能否成功爭取到2029年聯合國安理會席次，以及其上任後將如何具體落實其區域影響力；第二，紐西蘭在面對全球動盪時，如何平衡與美國的戰略協作及與其他區域國家的經貿關係；第三，兩國對於巴勒斯坦地位問題的最終決策，是否會引發更廣泛的外交連鎖反應。這些議題不僅關乎兩國自身的國家利益，更將深遠影響未來數年太平洋地區的和平與穩定。
