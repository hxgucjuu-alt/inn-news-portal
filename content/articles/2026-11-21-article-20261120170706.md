  # US-China Shenzhen Summit Maintains Trade Truce; Tech and Soybean Trade Rivalry Continues
  
  > U.S. President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping held talks in Shenzhen, where both sides reaffirmed their willingness to maintain economic and trade stability and continue the trade truce, though the ban on advanced chips remains unresolved. Meanwhile, China faces a backlog of soybean inventories due to weak demand in the livestock sector and delays in port customs clearance, as the global economy faces dual challenges from climate change and geopolitics.
  
  On November 19, 2026, U.S. President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping held bilateral talks in Shenzhen, a move seen as a key indicator of the world's two largest economies seeking stability in their economic and trade relations amidst turbulent times. The summit took place against a complex backdrop, involving not only the technological competition between the U.S. and China but also international pressures from global climate anomalies—specifically the potential record-breaking intensity of the El Niño phenomenon—and humanitarian crises such as those in Sudan. According to World Meteorological Organization forecasts, tropical Pacific sea surface temperatures could be 3.8 degrees Celsius above average from November to January next year, a climatic variable that poses a long-term threat to the global economy and agricultural production.
  
  During the summit, both the U.S. and China confirmed their commitment to maintaining the existing trade truce and expressed a willingness to promote tariff reductions. Reuters reported that the two countries are using existing mechanisms to discuss lowering tariffs on certain goods, involving approximately $60 billion in trade. Citigroup CEO Jane Fraser emphasized on the eve of the talks that maintaining contact between the U.S. and China is crucial for economic security, noting that ensuring the continued operation of multinational corporate financial activities through U.S. financial institutions is a core strategy for safeguarding U.S. economic security.
  
  However, there is a significant gap in the summit's outcomes across different levels. While both sides reached a preliminary consensus on traditional economic and trade issues, the export restrictions on advanced chips, which are critical to the global semiconductor and artificial intelligence industries, did not see any breakthroughs in these talks. Furthermore, the Chinese domestic market is facing structural challenges; it is reported that due to weak demand in the livestock industry, China's domestic soybean inventories are severely backlogged, causing customs clearance times for imported soybeans at some ports to extend to over 20 days, far exceeding the normal 15 days. It remains to be confirmed what the final details of the U.S.-China tariff reduction agreement will be, and whether the Chinese government will take more concrete measures to adjust import restrictions to alleviate the soybean backlog at ports.
  
  The economic and trade interaction between the U.S. and China has a profound impact on the global supply chain. In the technology sector, the ongoing chip ban has restricted the development path of the global AI industry, forcing relevant companies to seek a balance between the two major economies. In the agricultural sector, although Chinese state-owned importers continue to fulfill their commitment to purchase 25 million tons of U.S. soybeans annually, private crushing companies have turned to Brazil for procurement due to tariff and cost considerations, creating logistical pressure at Chinese ports. This contradiction between "state-owned procurement" and "private market demand" reflects the difficult trade-offs China faces between fulfilling international economic and trade commitments and meeting domestic commercial needs.
  
  Views on U.S.-China relations remain divided. Some in the financial sector, such as Citigroup, believe that maintaining financial and commercial contact is a necessary means to ensure economic security; however, the continued confrontation in the tech war shows that U.S.-China relations are presenting a contradictory pattern of "economic and trade easing" alongside "high-tech containment." Experts warn against over-interpreting the expectation of a relaxation of tech bans from a single summit, as the essence of the confrontation between the two sides has not changed under the framework of national security and technological hegemony. Furthermore, the El Niño phenomenon brought about by climate change will further exacerbate the uncertainty of the global food supply chain, which is undoubtedly another layer of potential economic pressure for the Chinese market, which relies on soybean imports.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on three key points: First, whether the U.S. and China can translate the willingness to "lower tariffs" into concrete legal agreements and implement them in the $60 billion trade of goods; second, whether the issue of soybean backlogs at Chinese ports will trigger larger-scale defaults on import contracts or order-switching effects, thereby affecting the global agricultural futures market; and finally, whether the U.S.-China tech rivalry will see new regulatory measures or room for negotiation in specific technical fields (such as quantum computing or advanced packaging) in subsequent bilateral consultations. The global market will continue to observe how these two major powers maintain a fragile dynamic balance between competition and cooperation.

# 美中深圳峰會維持貿易休兵，科技與大豆貿易角力持續

> 美國總統川普與中國國家主席習近平於深圳舉行會談，雙方重申經貿穩定意願並維持貿易休兵，惟先進晶片禁令未解。同時，中國因畜牧需求疲軟導致大豆庫存積壓，港口通關延遲，全球經濟面臨氣候變遷與地緣政治雙重挑戰。


2026年11月19日，美國總統川普與中國國家主席習近平在深圳進行雙邊會談，此舉被視為全球兩大經濟體在動盪局勢下，尋求經貿關係穩定的重要指標。此次峰會背景複雜，不僅涉及美中兩國的科技競爭，更在全球氣候異常——特別是聖嬰現象強度恐破紀錄，以及蘇丹等人道危機的國際壓力下進行。根據世界氣象組織預報，熱帶太平洋海面溫度於11月至明年1月可能高出平均值攝氏3.8度，此氣候變數對全球經濟與農業生產構成長期威脅。
在本次峰會中，美中雙方共同確認維持現有的貿易休兵安排，並表達推動關稅調降的意願。路透社報導指出，兩國正透過既有機制討論降低部分商品關稅，涉及貿易額約600億美元。花旗集團執行長Jane Fraser在會談前夕強調，美國與中國保持接觸對於經濟安全至關重要，特別是確保跨國企業的金融活動持續透過美國金融機構運作，是維護美方經濟安全的核心策略之一。
然而，峰會成果在不同層面存在顯著落差。雖然雙方在傳統經貿議題上達成初步共識，但攸關全球半導體與人工智慧產業的先進晶片出口限制，並未在本次會談中取得任何突破。此外，中國國內市場亦面臨結構性挑戰，據悉因畜牧業需求疲軟，中國國內大豆庫存嚴重積壓，導致部分港口對進口大豆的通關時間延長至20天以上，遠超常態的15天。目前尚待確認的是，美中關稅調降協議的最終細節，以及中國政府為緩解港口大豆積壓問題，是否會採取更具體的進口限制調整措施。
美中兩國的經貿互動對全球產業鏈影響深遠。在科技領域，持續的晶片禁令限制了全球AI產業的發展路徑，迫使相關企業在兩大經濟體間尋求平衡。在農業領域，中國國有進口商雖持續履行每年採購2,500萬噸美國大豆的承諾，但民營壓榨企業因關稅與成本考量，已轉向巴西採購，造成中國港口物流壓力。這種「國有採購」與「民營市場需求」的矛盾，反映出中國在履行國際經貿承諾與國內商業需求之間的艱難取捨。
針對美中關係，各界觀點不一。部分金融界人士如花旗集團認為，保持金融與商業接觸是確保經濟安全的必要手段；然而，科技戰的持續對抗顯示，美中關係正呈現「經貿緩解」與「高科技封鎖」並行的矛盾格局。專家提醒，不應過度解讀單一峰會對科技禁令的鬆綁預期，因為在國家安全與科技霸權的框架下，雙方的對抗本質並未改變。此外，氣候變遷帶來的聖嬰現象，將進一步加劇全球糧食供應鏈的不確定性，這對於依賴大豆進口的中國市場而言，無疑是另一層潛在的經濟壓力。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三點：首先，美中雙方是否能將「降低關稅」的意願轉化為具體的法律協議，並落實於600億美元的商品貿易中；其次，中國港口大豆積壓問題是否會引發更大規模的進口合約違約或轉單效應，進而影響全球農產品期貨市場；最後，美中科技角力在後續的雙邊磋商中，是否會針對特定技術領域（如量子運算或先進封裝）出現新的管制措施或談判空間。全球市場將持續觀察這兩大強權如何在競爭與合作之間，維持脆弱的動態平衡。
