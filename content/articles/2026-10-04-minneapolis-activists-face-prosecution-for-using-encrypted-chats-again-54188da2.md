明尼亞波利斯社運人士因涉嫌使用加密群組聊天應用程式來阻撓美國移民及海關執法局（ICE）的行動，正遭到聯邦政府的起訴。據報導，這些社運人士利用Signal等加密平台，成功地在ICE的「都會區突擊行動」（Operation Metro Surge）中佔得上風，但現在卻面臨法律追究。

這項調查可追溯至2026年1月，當時聯邦調查局（FBI）局長卡什·帕特爾（Kash Patel）宣布，將針對明尼亞波利斯抗議者使用的Signal群組聊天啟動刑事調查，此舉是基於一項「極右翼聲明」。社運人士在川普政府的驅逐行動期間，曾依賴Signal進行安全通訊，以組織反ICE的集會活動。

隨著調查的深入，明尼蘇達州聯邦檢察官丹尼爾·羅森（Daniel Rosen）於2026年6月宣布，已對15名涉案人員提出指控。資料顯示，聯邦調查人員在構建案件時，依賴了這些Signal通訊內容，這引發了外界對政府如何獲取加密訊息的疑問。

然而，這項起訴的廣泛性已引起法律界的擔憂。一位明尼蘇達州律師向《Reason》雜誌表示，他認為法院將對此案的廣泛程度「非常懷疑」。這項發展不僅挑戰了社運人士的通訊自由，也對加密通訊在公民行動中的合法使用劃下了問號，其後續影響備受關注。

## English edition

Minneapolis activists are facing federal prosecution for allegedly using encrypted group chat applications to outmaneuver operations by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). Reports indicate that these activists utilized encrypted platforms like Signal to successfully counter ICE's "Operation Metro Surge," but are now facing legal repercussions.

The investigation dates back to January 2026, when FBI Director Kash Patel announced a criminal investigation into Signal group chats used by Minneapolis protesters, based on a "far-right claim." Activists had relied on Signal for secure communications during anti-deportation campaigns under the Trump administration, organizing rallies against ICE.

As the investigation progressed, Minnesota U.S. Attorney Daniel Rosen announced in June 2026 that charges had been filed against 15 individuals involved. Data suggests that federal investigators leaned on these Signal communications to build their case, raising questions about how the government accessed encrypted messages.

The broad scope of this prosecution has, however, sparked concerns within the legal community. One Minnesota lawyer told Reason magazine that he believes courts will be "very suspicious of how broad this is." This development not only challenges activists' freedom of communication but also casts a shadow over the legitimate use of encrypted communications in civic action, with its long-term implications remaining a subject of close scrutiny.
