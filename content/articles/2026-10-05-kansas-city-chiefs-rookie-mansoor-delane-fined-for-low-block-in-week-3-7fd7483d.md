堪薩斯城酋長隊的新秀角衛曼蘇爾·德萊恩（Mansoor Delane）因在國家美式足球聯盟（NFL）第三週對陣邁阿密海豚隊的比賽中進行低位阻擋，被處以罰款。多個報導指出，這項罰款是針對其在比賽中「不必要的粗暴行為」或「非法動作」。

資料顯示，德萊恩被罰款12,537美元。這是這位來自路易斯安那州立大學（LSU）的新秀球員本賽季首次收到聯盟的罰款通知。此次罰款凸顯了NFL對球員安全和比賽規則執行的重視。

此次事件發生在酋長隊與海豚隊的第三週比賽中。儘管報導未詳細說明該次阻擋對比賽結果的具體影響，但聯盟對此類犯規行為的迅速處理，重申了其維護比賽公平性和球員健康的立場。

## English edition

Kansas City Chiefs rookie cornerback Mansoor Delane has been fined by the National Football League (NFL) for a low block executed during the Week 3 game against the Miami Dolphins. Multiple reports indicate that the fine was issued for "unnecessary roughness" or an "illegal play" committed during the match.

Data shows that Delane was assessed a fine of $12,537. This marks the first time the rookie player, an LSU product, has received a fine from the league this season. The penalty underscores the NFL's commitment to player safety and the enforcement of game rules.

The incident occurred during the Chiefs' Week 3 matchup against the Dolphins. While reports did not detail the specific impact of the block on the game's outcome, the league's swift action on such infractions reaffirms its stance on maintaining fair play and player well-being.
