  # The Interplay and Challenges of US-China Economic Relations and Global Geopolitics
  
  > As the relationship between the world's two largest economies enters a new phase, executives from Citi and JPMorgan Chase emphasize economic security and the status of the US dollar. Meanwhile, obstacles in China's soybean imports and the growth of trade along the Mekong River reflect the adjustment pressures and opportunities within regional supply chains amidst complex geopolitics.
  
  Recent global economic and political focus has centered on the interaction between the two major powers, the US and China, and its profound impact on the global trade system. As US President Trump prepares for a meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping, international attention toward such high-level dialogues continues to rise. Some observers have likened these potential trilateral or bilateral talks to "Yalta 2.0," suggesting that major powers such as the US, China, and Russia might be reallocating the global order. However, unlike the historical context of 1945, the current global power structure is far more complex, with nations exhibiting high tension between economic interdependence and geopolitical competition.
  
  In the financial sector, executives from major US banks have expressed key perspectives on US-China economic and trade relations. Citi CEO Jane Fraser clearly stated at an event in Washington that although the US and China are in fierce competition across multiple fields, maintaining engagement with China is crucial for safeguarding economic security. She emphasized that Citi serves numerous Fortune 500 companies in China, and ensuring that the financial activities of these firms—such as cash management, foreign exchange, and payroll—continue to be conducted through US financial institutions is a core means of maintaining US economic influence.
  
  Meanwhile, JPMorgan Chase CEO Jamie Dimon addressed the internationally discussed topic of "de-dollarization," emphasizing that as long as the United States maintains strong military and economic power and a robust rule of law, the status of the US dollar as the global reserve currency will be difficult to shake in the short term.
  
  However, a significant gap remains between macroeconomic financial policy and microeconomic trade practices. According to market information, weak demand in China's livestock industry has led to a severe backlog of soybean inventories, which in turn has affected port logistics. It is reported that for some private crushing enterprises, customs clearance time for soybean shipments imported from Brazil has extended from the usual 15 days to over 20 days, with some ports even experiencing delays in clearance, aimed at easing inventory pressure at ports and crushing plants.
  
  It is worth noting that China continues to fulfill its commitments to purchase US soybeans, handled by state-owned importers, while private traders tend to purchase Brazilian soybeans due to tariff considerations. This strategic divergence between "state-owned and private" entities regarding import sources reflects China's dilemma in balancing international commitments with weak domestic market conditions.
  
  In the Southeast Asian region, cross-border trade along the Mekong River presents a different picture. Relying on the Lancang-Mekong "Golden Waterway," cross-border fruit trade at the Guanlei port has seen significant growth in recent years. Relevant data shows that in the first nine months of this year, the import volume of fruit has surpassed other types of cargo, becoming the top trade category at the port. This phenomenon indicates that despite the tariff and inventory pressures facing global commodity trade, regional cross-border agricultural trade still demonstrates strong resilience and has become an important pillar of economic exchange for countries surrounding the Mekong.
  
  For relevant industries and policies, the aforementioned events reveal the multiple limitations of the current global trade system. First, although financial institutions call for continued engagement to maintain economic security, physical trade is deeply constrained by tariffs and weak market demand. Second, while the political game between the US and China attracts much attention, its impact on global supply chains is not linear but exhibits regional differences. For example, the strategic adjustments of Chinese soybean importers have directly impacted the export shares of Brazil and the US, while the trade growth in the Mekong region proves that regional supply chains can maintain stable growth in specific areas.
  
  The outside world should not over-interpret US-China talks as a singular "Yalta-style" power reorganization, because the current level of global economic interdependence is far higher than during the Cold War, and unilateral trade restrictions or geopolitical decisions often produce complex spillover effects.
  
  Future observation should focus on the specific direction of economic and trade policies following high-level US-China meetings, particularly whether China will adjust its import strategy regarding agricultural product inventories such as soybeans, and whether the operations of US financial institutions in the Chinese market will face stricter compliance reviews due to geopolitical tensions. Furthermore, whether the growth trend of cross-border trade in the Mekong region can continue to offset the shocks caused by global commodity trade fluctuations is also an important indicator for assessing the economic resilience of the Southeast Asian region. As uncertainty in the global economic environment increases, businesses and policymakers need to seek a more precise balance between maintaining economic security and pursuing market efficiency.

# 美中經貿互動與全球地緣政治局勢的連動與挑戰

> 隨著全球兩大經濟體美中關係進入新階段，花旗與摩根大通高層強調經濟安全與美元地位。同時，中國大豆進口受阻與湄公河貿易增長，反映出區域供應鏈在複雜地緣政治下的調整壓力與機遇。


近期全球經濟與政治焦點集中於美中兩大強權的互動，以及其對全球貿易體系的深遠影響。隨著美國總統川普準備與中國國家主席習近平進行會晤，國際社會對此類高層對話的關注度持續攀升。部分觀察家將此類潛在的三方或雙邊會談比擬為「雅爾達2.0」，意指美、中、俄等大國可能就全球秩序進行重新分配。然而，與1945年歷史背景不同的是，當前全球權力結構更為複雜，各國在經濟相互依賴與地緣政治競爭之間呈現高度緊張的態勢。
在金融領域，美國大型銀行高層針對美中經貿關係發表了關鍵觀點。花旗集團首席執行長珍·佛瑞澤（Jane Fraser）在華盛頓的活動中明確指出，儘管美中兩國在多個領域存在激烈競爭，但美國持續與中國保持接觸對於維護經濟安全至關重要。她強調，花旗在中國服務了眾多《財富》500強企業，確保這些企業的金融活動，如現金管理、外匯與薪資支付等，持續透過美國金融機構進行，是維繫美國經濟影響力的核心手段。
與此同時，摩根大通執行長傑米·戴蒙（Jamie Dimon）則針對國際間討論的「去美元化」議題表態，強調只要美國維持強大的軍事、經濟實力與法治環境，美元作為全球儲備貨幣的地位短期內難以動搖。
然而，在宏觀金融政策與微觀貿易實務之間，仍存在顯著的落差。根據市場訊息，中國國內因畜牧業需求疲軟，導致大豆庫存嚴重積壓，進而影響了港口的物流運作。據悉，部分民營壓榨企業從巴西進口的大豆貨物，通關時間已從通常的15天延長至超過20天，部分口岸甚至出現延遲通關情況，目的在於舒緩港口與壓榨廠的庫存壓力。
值得注意的是，中國目前仍持續履行對美採購大豆的承諾，由國有進口商負責執行，但民營貿易商則因關稅考量傾向採購巴西大豆，這種「國有與民營」在進口來源上的策略差異，反映出中國在履行國際承諾與應對國內市場疲軟之間的兩難。
在東南亞區域，湄公河沿岸的跨境貿易則呈現出另一番景象。依託瀾滄江-湄公河這條「黃金水道」，關累港口岸的跨境水果貿易近年來實現了顯著增長。相關資料顯示，今年前九個月，水果進口貨運量已超越其他貨物種類，成為該口岸貿易的首位。這一現象顯示，儘管全球大宗商品貿易面臨關稅與庫存壓力，但區域性的跨境農產品貿易仍展現出強勁的韌性，並成為湄公河周邊國家經濟交流的重要支柱。
對於相關產業與政策而言，上述事件揭示了當前全球貿易體系的多重限制。首先，金融機構雖然呼籲保持接觸以維護經濟安全，但實體貿易層面卻深受關稅與市場需求疲軟的制約。其次，美中兩國的政治博弈雖然備受矚目，但其對全球供應鏈的影響並非單一線性，而是呈現出區域性差異。例如，中國大豆進口商的策略調整，直接衝擊了巴西與美國的出口份額，而湄公河區域的貿易增長則證明瞭區域供應鏈在特定領域仍能維持穩定增長。
外界不應過度將美中會談解讀為單一的「雅爾達式」權力重組，因為當前全球經濟的相互依賴程度，遠高於冷戰時期，單邊的貿易限制或地緣政治決策，往往會產生複雜的溢位效應。
後續觀察重點應放在美中高層會晤後的具體經貿政策走向，特別是中國是否會針對大豆等農產品庫存問題調整進口策略，以及美國金融機構在中國市場的業務是否會因地緣政治緊張而面臨更嚴格的合規審查。此外，湄公河區域的跨境貿易增長趨勢，是否能持續抵銷全球大宗商品貿易波動帶來的衝擊，亦是評估東南亞區域經濟韌性的重要指標。隨著全球經濟環境的不確定性增加，企業與政策制定者需在維護經濟安全與追求市場效率之間，尋求更為精準的平衡點。
