根據Sputnikglobe.com和Nampa.org於2026年10月3日的報導，俄羅斯G20特使丹尼斯·阿加福諾夫（Denis Agafonov）表示，二十國集團（G20）可能成為討論人工智慧（AI）監管的合適平台。此言論是在華盛頓舉行的一場記者會上提出的。

阿加福諾夫指出，G20作為一個匯集不同觀點和立場的國際組織，將是一個討論AI監管的理想場所。他認為，透過G20平台，各成員國能夠就AI的潛在風險與治理框架進行深入對話。

然而，資料顯示，對於AI監管的途徑，G20成員國之間存在不同看法。路透社在2026年9月1日的一篇報導中指出，美國將敦促G20成員國不要設立新的監管機構來監督AI發展。這表明美國傾向於採取「不干預」的監管態度，與俄羅斯提出的討論監管平台建議形成對比。

此外，discoverinai.com的報導也指出，G20國家目前正努力平衡人工智慧的巨大經濟增長潛力與建立有效治理以減輕風險的需求。創新與監管之間的這種張力，定義了當前全球在G20人工智慧政策上的討論。這凸顯了在尋求統一AI治理方法時，各國國家利益的多元性與複雜性。

## English edition

According to reports from Sputnikglobe.com and Nampa.org on October 3, 2026, Russia's G20 Sherpa Denis Agafonov stated that the Group of Twenty (G20) could become a suitable platform for discussing artificial intelligence (AI) regulation. This statement was made during a press conference in Washington.

Agafonov highlighted that the G20, as an international organization that consolidates diverse views and points of view, would be an ideal venue for discussing AI regulation. He suggested that through the G20 platform, member states could engage in in-depth dialogue regarding the potential risks and governance frameworks of AI.

However, data indicates differing approaches to AI regulation among G20 member countries. A Reuters report on September 1, 2026, noted that the United States would urge G20 member countries not to establish new regulatory organizations to oversee AI development. This suggests a preference from the U.S. for a "hands-off" regulatory approach, contrasting with Russia's suggestion for a platform to discuss regulation.

Furthermore, a report from discoverinai.com points out that G20 nations are currently grappling with balancing AI's immense potential for economic growth with the need to establish effective governance to mitigate risks. This tension between innovation and regulation defines the current global discourse on G20 AI policy, underscoring the diversity and complexity of national interests in seeking a unified approach to AI governance.
