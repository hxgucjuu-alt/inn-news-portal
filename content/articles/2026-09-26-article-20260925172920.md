  # Global Technology and Medical Governance: AI Applications, Semiconductor Expansion, and Challenges in Rare Disease Care
  
  > Recent global developments in technology and healthcare—ranging from the application of AI in cultural preservation and the strategic layout of advanced semiconductor manufacturing to reforms in medical reimbursement systems for rare diseases—highlight the importance of cross-disciplinary collaboration and technological innovation.
  
  Global issues in technology and medical governance have recently shown diverse developments, encompassing innovative applications of artificial intelligence (AI) in cultural heritage protection, the large-scale expansion of the semiconductor industry to meet AI computing demands, and international cooperation and reimbursement improvements for rare disease medical systems.
  
  In the cultural sector, the integration of technology and art is changing how the public interacts with history. On the industrial front, TSMC is actively expanding its 2nm and 3nm advanced process capacity to meet the global thirst for generative AI computing power and flagship chips. Meanwhile, in the field of public health, the Taiwan Society for Pharmacoeconomics and Outcomes Research (TaSPOR) is focusing on early diagnosis and reimbursement mechanisms for rare disease treatments, emphasizing the sustainability of medical resources and patient participation.
  
  According to public information, progress in technology and industry has entered a stage of substantial expansion. HONOR, in collaboration with the Hong Kong Maritime Museum, will utilize AI imaging technology to animate century-old paintings and other collections from September 21 to 28, 2026, aiming to increase public interest in historical artifacts through technological means. At the same time, semiconductor manufacturing leader TSMC announced that its 2026 capital expenditure is expected to reach $64 billion, fully accelerating the expansion of 2nm and 3nm wafer capacity. It is projected that capacity will see a significant increase of approximately 20% by mid-2027, with plans to commence 3nm production at its second plant in Arizona, USA, in the second half of 2027 to ensure its leading position in angstrom-node technology.
  
  Despite significant progress in technology applications and industrial expansion, different levels of challenges and uncertainties remain in these fields. At the international political level, although high-level exchanges between the US and China continue, discussions regarding urgent issues such as security, technology, and "superintelligence" (AI)—while viewed as critical to peace and prosperity for the coming decades—show limited actual progress according to public information. Furthermore, in the medical field, the treatment of rare diseases is not merely about "having drugs available." Experts point out that ensuring medical accessibility and systemic sustainability is a core challenge that requires early diagnosis, the establishment of reasonable value assessment mechanisms, and the improvement of health insurance reimbursement systems—issues that are difficult for any single country or group to solve independently.
  
  The aforementioned events have had a profound impact on global industrial chains and social policies. TSMC's capacity expansion directly reflects the strong competition in the global generative AI computing power demand and flagship mobile chip market; the deployment of next-generation photomask technology by partners such as ASML demonstrates the close collaboration within the semiconductor ecosystem to address the challenges of advanced processes. On a social level, the allocation of medical resources and the improvement of rare disease care systems reflect the need for countries to strike a balance between "drug accessibility" and "systemic sustainability" when facing high medical costs.
  
  At the same time, the application of AI technology in cultural storage has provided new pathways for digital transformation in museums and history education, allowing static artifacts to engage in a dialogue with modern audiences in a dynamic form.
  
  Regarding these developments, perspectives and limitations from all sectors must be interpreted with caution. Experts generally believe that the issue of rare diseases requires the joint participation of government, medical, academic, industrial, and patient groups to form a consensus, and cannot be solved by a single strategy. In terms of geopolitics and technology governance, although world leaders emphasize the importance of AI technology for future peace and prosperity, the setting of technical standards, cross-border regulation, and security boundaries remains a long and complex negotiation process. While the public focuses on the convenience and innovation brought by AI technology, they should not ignore the structural challenges behind these applications, such as ethics, data security, and resource allocation.
  
  Future observations will focus on three aspects: First, the execution progress of TSMC's 2027 advanced process capacity expansion plan and the actual performance of its Arizona plant; second, whether international negotiations on "superintelligence" and AI governance can translate verbal commitments into concrete policy frameworks; and finally, whether reforms to rare disease medical reimbursement systems in Taiwan and internationally can effectively implement multi-dimensional assessment mechanisms while maintaining the long-term stability of health insurance systems while ensuring patients' rights to medication. The subsequent development of these issues will continue to influence the global technology competition landscape and social welfare.

# 全球科技與醫療治理：AI應用、半導體擴張及罕病照護挑戰

> 近期全球科技與醫療領域動態頻繁，從AI技術應用於文化儲存、半導體產業的先進製程佈局，到罕見疾病的醫療給付制度改革，展現了跨領域合作與技術創新的重要性。


全球科技與醫療治理議題近期呈現多元發展，涵蓋了人工智慧（AI）在文化資產保護的創新應用、半導體產業針對AI算力需求的大規模擴張，以及罕見疾病醫療制度的國際合作與給付精進。
在文化領域，科技與藝術的結合正改變大眾與歷史的互動模式；在產業端，臺積電（TSMC）為應對全球對生成式AI算力與旗艦晶片的渴求，正積極擴充2奈米與3奈米先進製程產能；而在公共衛生領域，臺灣藥物經濟暨效果研究學會（TaSPOR）則聚焦罕病治療的早期診斷與給付機制，強調醫療資源永續性與病人參與。
根據公開資訊，科技與產業的進展已進入實質擴張階段。HONOR與香港海事博物館合作，於2026年9月21日至28日期間，利用AI影像技術將百年古畫等館藏進行動態化展示，旨在透過科技手段提升公眾對歷史文物的興趣。與此同時，半導體製造龍頭臺積電宣佈2026年資本支出上看640億美元，全面加速2奈米與3奈米晶圓產能擴充，預計2027年中產能將迎來約兩成的大幅提升，並計畫於2027年下半年在美國亞利桑那州二廠開出3奈米產能，以確保在埃米節點技術的領先地位。
儘管科技應用與產業擴張進展顯著，但相關領域仍存在不同層次的挑戰與不確定性。在國際政治層面，儘管美中高層交流持續進行，針對涉及安全、科技及「超級智慧」（AI）等迫切問題的討論，雖被視為未來數十年和平與繁榮的關鍵，但目前公開資訊顯示實際進展仍相當有限。此外，在醫療領域，罕見疾病的治療並非僅止於「有藥可用」，專家指出，如何透過早期診斷、建立合理的價值評估機制以及完善健保給付制度，是確保醫療可近性與制度永續性的核心難題，且此類議題難以由單一國家或群體獨立解決。
上述事件對全球產業鏈與社會政策產生了深遠影響。臺積電的產能擴張動作，直接反映了全球生成式AI算力需求與旗艦手機晶片市場的強勁競爭；ASML等合作夥伴的次世代光罩技術部署，則顯示了半導體生態系為應對先進製程挑戰的緊密協作。在社會層面，醫療資源的分配與罕病照護制度的精進，反映了各國在面對高昂醫療成本時，必須在「用藥可近性」與「制度永續性」之間尋求平衡。
同時，AI技術在文化儲存的應用，也為博物館與歷史教育提供了新的數位轉型路徑，使靜態文物能以動態形式與現代觀眾對話。
針對上述發展，各界觀點與限制亦需審慎解讀。專家普遍認為，罕病議題須透過政府、醫療、學術、產業及病友團體的共同參與，形成共識，不能僅憑單一策略解決。而在地緣政治與科技治理方面，雖然各國領袖強調AI技術對未來和平與繁榮的重要性，但技術標準、跨國監管及安全邊界的設定，仍是長期且複雜的談判過程。公眾在關注AI技術帶來的便利與創新時，亦不應忽視技術應用背後的倫理、資料安全與資源分配等結構性挑戰。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個面向：首先，臺積電2027年先進製程產能擴張計畫的執行進度，以及其在美國亞利桑那州產能開出的實際效能；其次，全球針對「超級智慧」與AI治理的國際談判是否能從口頭承諾轉化為具體的政策框架；最後，臺灣及國際間對於罕見疾病醫療給付制度的改革，能否有效落實多元評估機制，並在確保病人用藥權益的同時，維持健保體系的長期穩健運作。這些議題的後續發展，將持續影響全球科技競爭格局與社會福祉。
