  # The Global AI Arms Race Intensifies: SoftBank Expands Financing Amid Supply Chain Shocks and South American Tech Outlook
  
  > As global demand for AI surges, SoftBank Group has expanded its margin loan facility by pledging Arm shares to raise $25 billion, doubling down on AI infrastructure. Meanwhile, global memory shortages and energy resilience challenges are exerting profound impacts on the development of the technology sector in emerging markets like South America.
  
  The rapid iteration of artificial intelligence (AI) technology is profoundly altering the operational models of global capital markets and industrial supply chains. According to recent financial reports, SoftBank Group completed a renegotiation with creditor banks in September 2026, significantly increasing its margin loan facility—backed by shares of its chip design subsidiary, Arm—to $25 billion. This move provides SoftBank with an additional $5 billion in liquidity, aimed at further investment in AI infrastructure, chip research and development, and model companies.
  
  Founder Masayoshi Son’s recent investment commitment to OpenAI has reached as high as $65 billion, demonstrating SoftBank’s high-leverage expansion strategy in the AI sector. Simultaneously, optical and materials science giant Coherent Corp. (COHR) has benefited from robust AI hardware demand, with its stock price soaring 182.4% over the past year, far outperforming the S&P 500 index, reflecting the high concentration of market capital in the AI hardware supply chain.
  
  On the industrial supply chain front, the global memory chip shortage is continuing to worsen, with supply constraints expected to persist through 2027 and potentially into 2028. Data from market research firms TrendForce and Counterpoint Research indicate that memory prices surged by 93% to 98% in the first quarter of this year, with a further increase of 13% to 18% projected for the current quarter. This supply chain crisis is forcing small electronic device manufacturers to redesign products and face pressure from rising terminal retail prices; global smartphone shipments are estimated to decline by 13.9% to approximately 1.08 billion units, marking the largest drop in history.
  
  Furthermore, the proliferation of AI technology has brought about massive energy demands. The American Institute in Taiwan (AIT) recently convened experts from 28 countries to discuss "strengthening energy resilience and supply chain security," with a focus on the grid pressure and governance challenges accompanying AI development.
  
  Regarding the South American region, although there is a gap in direct investment in AI infrastructure compared to the U.S. and Asian regions, its role as a vital supplier of global critical minerals and energy is being indirectly affected by the aforementioned global shifts. According to reporting from the *Latin Herald* and the Associated Press (AP News) concerning South American nations such as Ecuador, the region faces challenges regarding insufficient infrastructure resilience while navigating global supply chain restructuring and digital transformation.
  
  Currently, there are differing assessments among media outlets regarding the specific economic impact of AI on South America. Some perspectives suggest that the demand for memory and semiconductors driven by AI will further exacerbate the digital divide in developing countries, while others emphasize that if energy supply security can be improved through international cooperation, South America may find a new strategic position in the global supply chain of the AI era.
  
  There remains uncertainty regarding the subsequent impact of SoftBank’s expanded financing. While SoftBank has secured funds by pledging Arm shares, high leverage could pose financial risks if the return cycle on AI investments does not meet expectations. Additionally, it remains to be seen whether the impact of memory shortages on the end-consumer market will lead to a structural recession in the global electronics industry. Experts warn against viewing the stock performance of a single company or the supply-demand imbalance of a specific industry as the sole indicator of the global economy. Particularly in emerging markets like South America, policymakers need to carefully evaluate the capital flows of global tech giants to avoid neglecting the autonomy of local energy and digital infrastructure due to over-reliance on external supply chains.
  
  Regarding the specific impact on related industries, the explosion in AI demand is no longer limited to the software layer but has extended to the energy and basic materials sectors. For technology policymakers in South America, balancing participation in the global AI supply chain with ensuring domestic energy supply resilience will be a core challenge in the coming years. As cost pressures from global memory shortages spill over, the import costs for electronic devices in South America are expected to rise significantly, which may prompt local governments to accelerate the R&D and transformation of localized supply chains to mitigate the shocks caused by external market volatility.
  
  Subsequent observations should focus on two main aspects: first, whether SoftBank Group’s massive investments in AI can translate into concrete technological breakthroughs, thereby easing the current tensions in the global hardware supply chain; and second, the restructuring process of the global energy supply chain driven by AI demand, particularly the progress of international cooperation in energy transition and digital infrastructure construction in South America. As the 2027 supply-demand equilibrium point approaches, the flow of capital and technology in the global tech industry will continue to reshape the competitive landscape of regional economies, making it worth continuing to track changes in relevant policies and industry data.

# 全球AI軍備競賽加劇：軟銀擴大融資與供應鏈衝擊下的南美科技展望

> 隨著全球AI需求激增，軟銀集團擴大抵押Arm股份以籌措250億美元資金，加碼佈局AI基礎設施。與此同時，全球記憶體短缺與能源韌性挑戰，亦對南美等新興市場的科技產業發展構成深遠影響。


人工智慧（AI）技術的快速迭代，正深刻改變全球資本市場與產業供應鏈的運作模式。根據最新財經報導，軟銀集團（SoftBank Group）於2026年9月完成與債權銀行的重新協商，將以旗下晶片設計大廠安謀（Arm）股份作為抵押的保證金貸款額度，大幅提升至250億美元。此舉意味著軟銀額外增加了50億美元的流動資金，旨在進一步投入AI基礎設施、晶片研發及模型公司。
創辦人孫正義近期對OpenAI的投資承諾已高達650億美元，顯示出軟銀在AI領域採取的高槓桿擴張策略。與此同時，光學與材料科學巨頭Coherent Corp.（COHR）受惠於強勁的AI硬體需求，過去一年股價飆升182.4%，遠超標準普爾500指數的表現，反映出市場資本正高度集中於AI硬體供應鏈。
在產業供應鏈端，全球記憶體晶片短缺問題正持續惡化，預計供應緊張態勢將延續至2027年，甚至可能持續至2028年。市場研究機構集邦科技（TrendForce）與Counterpoint Research資料顯示，記憶體價格在今年第一季已暴漲93%至98%，且本季預計將再上揚13%至18%。此一供應鏈危機正迫使小型電子裝置製造商重新設計產品，甚至面臨終端售價上漲的壓力，全球智慧型手機出貨量預估將下滑13.9%，降至約10.8億支，創下史上最大跌幅。
此外，AI技術的普及亦帶來龐大的能源需求，美國在臺協會（AIT）近期邀集28國專家研議「強化能源韌性與供應鏈安全」，重點討論AI發展隨之而來的電網壓力與治理挑戰。
針對南美洲地區而言，儘管該地區在AI基礎設施的直接投入上與美、亞兩大區域存在落差，但其作為全球關鍵礦產與能源供應的重要角色，正受到上述全球變局的間接影響。根據《Latin Herald》與美聯社（AP News）針對厄瓜多等南美國家的報導脈絡，該地區在應對全球供應鏈重組與數位轉型時，面臨著基礎設施韌性不足的挑戰。
目前各媒體對於AI對南美具體經濟影響的評估尚存差異，部分觀點認為，AI帶動的記憶體與半導體需求，將進一步加劇開發中國家的數位鴻溝，而另一觀點則強調，若能透過國際合作提升能源供應安全，南美洲有望在AI時代的全球供應鏈中尋求新的戰略定位。
目前市場對於軟銀擴大融資的後續影響仍存在不確定性。雖然軟銀透過抵押Arm股份獲得資金，但若AI投資回報週期不如預期，高額槓桿可能帶來財務風險。此外，記憶體短缺對終端消費市場的衝擊程度，是否會導致全球電子產業出現結構性衰退，仍有待觀察。各界專家提醒，不應將單一企業的股價表現或特定產業的供需失衡，視為全球經濟的唯一指標。特別是在南美等新興市場，政策制定者需謹慎評估全球科技巨頭的資本流向，避免因過度依賴外部供應鏈而忽視本地能源與數位基礎設施的自主性。
針對相關產業的具體影響，AI需求的爆發已不僅限於軟體層面，更延伸至能源與基礎材料領域。對於南美地區的科技政策制定者而言，如何在參與全球AI供應鏈與保障國內能源供應韌性之間取得平衡，將是未來幾年的核心課題。隨著全球記憶體短缺帶來的成本壓力外溢，南美地區的電子裝置進口成本預計將顯著攀升，這可能促使當地政府加速推動在地化供應鏈的研發與轉型，以減緩外部市場波動帶來的衝擊。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於兩大面向：首先是軟銀集團在AI領域的鉅額投資，是否能轉化為具體的技術突破，進而緩解目前全球硬體供應鏈的緊張局勢；其次是全球能源供應鏈在AI需求驅動下的重組程式，特別是南美地區在能源轉型與數位基礎設施建設上的國際合作進展。隨著2027年供需平衡點的臨近，全球科技產業的資本與技術流向，將持續重塑區域經濟的競爭格局，值得持續追蹤相關政策與產業資料的變動。
