  # Political Turmoil in Iran and Regional Security Crisis: Analysis of the Major Situation in February 2026
  
  > On February 28, 2026, Iran erupted into its most profound political crisis since the 1979 Revolution. With the international community closely monitoring the rapid deterioration of regional security and geopolitical shocks, concerns are mounting regarding U.S.-Israeli strikes, Iran’s nuclear program, and the stability of the regime.
  
  The Islamic Republic of Iran fell into its most severe political crisis since the 1979 Islamic Revolution on February 28, 2026. According to comprehensive reports from various international media outlets and monitoring platforms, the country recently suffered a series of external military strikes targeting the core facilities and leadership of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), triggering violent upheaval in Iran's domestic political landscape. The backdrop of this crisis stems from long-standing, deep-seated conflicts between Iran, the United States, and Israel regarding nuclear programs, regional proxy wars, and sanctions policies.
  
  As regional conflicts escalated, large-scale protests emerged within Iran. The government immediately implemented harsh information blockade measures, including a nationwide internet shutdown, in an attempt to curb the spread of anti-regime forces.
  
  Information gathered from various sources confirms that the core of Iran's current crisis lies in the dual pressure of military strikes and internal political collapse. Reports from the Associated Press (AP) and the news aggregation platform NewsNow indicate that Iran has recently been subjected to targeted military operations. These attacks not only directly damaged Iran's defense infrastructure but also resulted in casualties among the IRGC high command, including reports that Supreme Leader Khamenei may have been killed or is currently unaccounted for. This development has directly impacted Iran's power structure, leading to unprecedented chaos in the country's military and political chain of command.
  
  Simultaneously, the progress of Iran's nuclear program is facing pressure for interruption or adjustment due to the military strikes. International concerns regarding Iran's nuclear weapons development and demands for regional security guarantees have become the focus of current diplomatic mediation.
  
  Although multiple sources have confirmed the objective fact that Iran is in crisis, there remain significant discrepancies in media reports regarding the details and scope of the events. For instance, regarding the specific status of Khamenei, there has been no definitive confirmation from official Iranian channels. While some international media outlets cite intelligence sources claiming he has been killed, other analyses suggest this could be psychological warfare disseminated by opposition groups.
  
  Furthermore, regarding the specific scale and tactical details of the joint U.S.-Israeli strikes, public information from the U.S. Department of State’s Bureau of Near Eastern Affairs merely emphasizes a commitment to resolving regional conflicts and addressing security threats; it does not directly confirm participation in military operations at a specific time. This creates a narrative gap with reports such as those from NewsNow that explicitly point to "U.S.-Israeli attacks," highlighting the gray area between the international community's public stance and actual military operations.
  
  The political turmoil in Iran has profound implications for the Middle East, not only directly impacting energy security and shipping stability in the Persian Gulf but also potentially triggering a new wave of refugee crises and regional conflicts. The U.S. Department of State has clearly stated that it will continue to monitor regional instability and is committed to ensuring that immigration and border security comply with U.S. laws, reflecting Washington's deep concern that the Iranian crisis could spill over into neighboring countries and subsequently affect the security of the U.S. homeland.
  
  For industries in the Middle East, as Iran is a key member of the Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (OPEC), its political instability has already directly led to global oil price fluctuations and exacerbated geopolitical tensions between the Levant and Persian Gulf nations, causing stagnation in regional education, cultural exchange, and economic cooperation.
  
  Regarding the current situation, there are significant differences in perspectives. Some analysts believe this is the culmination of long-standing internal contradictions within the Iranian regime, which could lead to regime change. However, other viewpoints warn that over-interpreting the impact of a single military event may overlook the resilience of the Iranian regime in the face of internal protests. Additionally, the lack of truth caused by information blockades makes it difficult for outsiders to assess the true scale of the protests and whether the IRGC still possesses the control to maintain domestic order after losing its core leadership.
  
  Therefore, in the absence of transparent information from Iranian officials, any predictions regarding the imminent collapse of the regime should be treated with high caution to avoid misjudging regional military friction as the final disintegration of the regime.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on three levels: first, the process of filling the power vacuum within Iran, observing whether power struggles will emerge within the IRGC or if hardline military factions will take full control; second, adjustments in the subsequent foreign policies of the United States and Israel, particularly whether containment measures against Iran's nuclear program will shift from military strikes to stricter economic sanctions or diplomatic isolation; and finally, the reactions of other countries in the region, such as Saudi Arabia, Iraq, and Egypt. How these countries maintain their own security amidst the Iranian crisis and seek balance between the U.S. and Iranian powers will determine the future direction of Middle Eastern geopolitics.
  
  The international community must continue to closely monitor the real information released from within Iran once the internet blockade is lifted to clarify the ultimate impact of this crisis.

# 伊朗政局動盪與區域安全危機：2026年2月重大情勢分析

> 2026年2月28日，伊朗爆發自1979年革命以來最深重的政治危機。針對美以攻擊、伊朗核計畫及政權穩定性，國際社會正密切關注區域安全域性勢的急劇惡化與地緣政治震盪。


伊朗伊斯蘭共和國於2026年2月28日陷入自1979年伊斯蘭革命以來最為嚴峻的政治危機。根據多家國際媒體與監測平臺綜合報導，該國近期遭受了一系列外部軍事攻擊，目標直指伊朗革命衛隊（IRGC）的核心設施及相關領導層，引發了伊朗國內政局的劇烈動盪。此次危機的背景，源於長期以來伊朗與美國、以色列之間在核計畫、區域代理人戰爭及制裁政策上的深層矛盾。
隨著區域衝突升級，伊朗境內出現了大規模抗議活動，政府隨即採取了嚴厲的資訊封鎖措施，包括全國性的網際網路中斷，試圖遏止政權反對勢力的擴散。
綜合各方資訊來源確認，伊朗目前的危機核心在於軍事打擊與內部政治崩潰的雙重壓力。美聯社（AP）與新聞聚合平臺NewsNow的報導指出，伊朗境內近期遭受了針對性的軍事行動，這些攻擊不僅直接損害了伊朗的國防基礎設施，更導致了伊朗革命衛隊高層的傷亡，其中包括最高領袖哈米尼（Khamenei）可能遭到殺害或失聯的訊息。此一事態發展直接衝擊了伊朗的權力架構，導致該國軍事與政治指揮鏈出現前所未有的混亂。
同時，伊朗的核計畫進度也因軍事打擊而面臨中斷或調整的壓力，國際社會對於伊朗核武開發的擔憂與區域安全保障的訴求，成為當前外交斡旋的焦點。
儘管多方來源確認了伊朗陷入危機的客觀事實，但在關於事件細節與影響範圍上，不同媒體報導仍存在顯著差異。例如，關於哈米尼的具體狀況，目前尚無伊朗官方管道的確切證實，部分國際媒體引述情報來源稱其已遇害，但亦有分析指出，這可能是反對派釋放的心理戰資訊。
此外，關於美以聯合攻擊的具體規模與戰術細節，美國國務院（State Department）近東事務局的公開資訊中，僅強調致力於解決區域衝突與應對安全威脅，並未直接證實參與了特定時間點的軍事行動，這與NewsNow等報導中明確指出的「美以攻擊」存在敘事上的落差，顯示出國際社會在公開立場與實際軍事行動之間的模糊地帶。
此次伊朗政局動盪對中東地區的影響極為深遠，不僅直接衝擊了波斯灣地區的能源安全與航運穩定，更可能引發新一波的難民危機與區域性衝突。美國國務院已明確表示，將持續關注區域不穩定因素，並致力於確保移民與邊境安全符合美國法律規範，這反映出華府對於伊朗危機可能外溢至周邊國家、進而影響美國本土安全的深層擔憂。
對於中東地區的產業而言，伊朗作為石油輸出國組織（OPEC）的重要成員，其政局不穩已直接導致全球油價波動，並加劇了黎凡特地區與波斯灣國家之間的地緣政治緊張，使得區域內的教育、文化交流與經濟合作陷入停滯。
針對當前的局勢，各界觀點存在顯著分歧。部分分析人士認為，這是伊朗政權內部長期矛盾累積後的總爆發，可能導致伊朗走向政權更迭；然而，也有觀點警告，過度解讀單一軍事事件的影響力可能忽略了伊朗政權在面對內部抗議時的韌性。此外，資訊封鎖導致的真相缺失，使得外界難以評估抗議活動的真實規模，以及伊朗革命衛隊在失去核心領導後，是否仍具備維持國內秩序的控制力。
因此，在缺乏伊朗官方透明資訊的情況下，任何關於該國政權即將崩潰的預測，均應保持高度謹慎，避免將區域性的軍事摩擦誤判為最終的政權解體。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於三個層面：首先是伊朗境內權力真空的填補過程，觀察革命衛隊內部是否會出現權力鬥爭，或由軍方強硬派全面接管；其次是美國與以色列在後續外交政策上的調整，特別是針對伊朗核計畫的遏制手段是否會從軍事打擊轉向更嚴厲的經濟制裁或外交孤立；最後則是區域內其他國家，如沙烏地阿拉伯、伊拉克與埃及等國的反應。這些國家如何在伊朗危機中維護自身安全，並在美、伊兩大勢力之間尋求平衡，將決定未來中東地緣政治的走向。
國際社會目前仍需密切留意網路封鎖解除後，伊朗內部釋出的真實資訊，以釐清此次危機的最終影響。
