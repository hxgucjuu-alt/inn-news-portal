  # Trump Signs Executive Order Excluding U.S.-China Artificial Intelligence Technical Cooperation
  
  > Former U.S. President Donald Trump recently signed an executive order explicitly excluding the possibility of technical cooperation with China in the field of artificial intelligence. This move marks a new phase in U.S.-China technological competition and has sparked significant global attention regarding technology governance and industrial policy.
  
  Former U.S. President Donald Trump recently attended an event at the Andrew W. Mellon Auditorium in Washington, where he officially signed and unveiled an executive order concerning the development of artificial intelligence (AI). The core of this policy aims to strengthen U.S. autonomy and national security in the AI sector, explicitly excluding any form of technical cooperation or resource sharing with China. The background of this move stems from the intense competition between the U.S. and China in recent years across key technological fields such as semiconductors, cloud computing, and generative AI.
  
  As the world's two largest economies, the U.S. government has long viewed artificial intelligence as a strategic high ground that determines future national competitiveness. The signing of this executive order is not only a major declaration of U.S. domestic technology policy but also has profound implications for the global technology supply chain and the landscape of transnational research and development cooperation.
  
  According to cross-referenced reports from multiple media outlets, the confirmed facts are as follows: Trump has indeed signed the executive order, and the specific provisions of the order explicitly set strict limitations on interactions between the U.S. and China in the AI field. This policy shift is consistent with the context of previous U.S. government policies regarding export controls and chip bans, demonstrating that the U.S. has adopted a tougher protectionist stance on AI governance.
  
  Media outlets have reported on the timing and implementation details of the order, confirming that the policy has officially entered the execution phase. It was publicly announced via the official U.S. website, America.gov, indicating that this is the highest-level policy guidance currently adopted by the U.S. regarding AI development.
  
  However, there are significant information gaps and areas requiring verification regarding the details and scope of impact reported by the media. Some reports focus on specific termination clauses for international cooperation agreements within the executive order, but there is currently no unified official explanation as to whether the order will be retroactive, affecting ongoing transnational academic exchanges or corporate cooperation projects. Furthermore, interpretations differ among media outlets regarding how the order specifically defines the scope of "cooperation," such as whether it includes basic research, open-source code sharing, or personnel exchanges. Due to the lack of detailed regulations, the industry remains in a wait-and-see and evaluation phase regarding how this policy will affect the existing global technology ecosystem, and a consensus market expectation has yet to form.
  
  This policy has had a direct and concrete impact on the technology industry and public governance in North America. For U.S.-based AI R&D companies, this means they must re-examine their supply chains and R&D partners to comply with government requirements. At the same time, this may lead to a redistribution of global tech talent, as some multinational corporations may adjust their R&D footprints in North America in response to policy risks. At the public policy level, this move may force other countries to take sides in the U.S.-China tech competition, thereby affecting the formulation of global AI standards and potentially leading to the risk of fragmentation in the global technology governance system.
  
  Although the policy has received some support within the U.S. on the grounds that it helps safeguard national security and technological leadership, different perspectives have also raised warnings. Some industry experts point out that AI development is highly dependent on the flow of global talent and data, and excessive restrictions on international cooperation could lead to a slowdown in technological innovation or even trigger a vicious cycle of technological barriers. In addition, the academic community is concerned that this move will severely hit transnational scientific research cooperation and limit breakthroughs in basic science. Therefore, the outside world should not over-interpret the order as a simple technological blockade, but rather view it as an attempt by the U.S. to seek a balance between national security and global technological competition; its long-term economic benefits and technological impacts still need to be tested by the market and time.
  
  Future observation will focus on several key areas: first, the specific implementation details of the executive order, particularly when compliance guidelines for companies and academic institutions will be released; second, the Chinese government's response to this policy and whether it will take countermeasures, which could affect the stability of the global semiconductor and AI industry supply chains; and finally, how technology companies in North America will adjust their globalization strategies to cope with the increasingly severe international political environment. All sectors will closely monitor the long-term evolution of this policy regarding future global AI technical standards, models of transnational R&D cooperation, and geopolitical games, while continuing to assess its potential impact on global economic growth.

# 特朗普簽署行政命令排除美中人工智慧技術合作

> 美國前總統特朗普近期簽署行政命令，明確排除與中國在人工智慧領域進行技術合作的可能性，此舉標誌著美中科技競爭進入新階段，引發全球科技治理與產業政策的高度關注。


美國前總統特朗普近期於華盛頓安德魯·W·梅隆禮堂出席活動時，正式簽署並展示了一份關於人工智慧（AI）發展的行政命令。該政策核心旨在強化美國在人工智慧領域的自主權與國家安全，明確排除與中國進行任何形式的技術合作或資源共享。此舉背景源於近年來美中兩國在半導體、雲端運算及生成式AI等關鍵技術領域的激烈博弈。
作為全球最大的兩大經濟體，美國政府長期以來將人工智慧視為決定未來國家競爭力的戰略制高點，此次行政命令的簽署，不僅是美國國內科技政策的重大宣示，更對全球科技供應鏈與跨國研發合作格局產生了深遠影響。
根據多家媒體報導的交叉比對，目前已確認的事實包括：特朗普確實已簽署該份行政命令，且該命令的具體條文內容明確針對美中兩國在AI領域的互動設下嚴格限制。此項政策變動與先前美國政府在出口管制、晶片禁令等領域的政策脈絡相符，顯示出美國在AI治理方面採取了更為強硬的保護主義立場。
各界媒體對於該命令的簽署時間點與執行細節均有報導，確認了該項政策已正式進入執行階段，並透過美國官方網站 America.gov 進行公開宣示，顯示此舉為美國當前針對人工智慧發展所採取的最高層級政策指導。
然而，在媒體報導的細節與影響範圍上，不同來源之間存在顯著的資訊落差與待確認之處。部分報導聚焦於該行政命令對於國際合作協議的具體廢止條款，但對於該命令是否會溯及既往，影響已經展開的跨國學術交流或企業合作專案，目前尚無統一的官方解釋。此外，針對該命令如何具體定義「合作」的範疇，例如是否包含基礎研究、開原始碼共享或人員交流，不同媒體的解讀存在差異。由於缺乏細則說明，產業界對於該政策將如何影響現有的全球科技生態鏈，仍處於觀望與評估階段，尚未形成一致的市場預期。
此項政策對北美地區的科技產業與公共治理產生了直接且具體的影響。對於美國本土的AI研發企業而言，這意味著必須重新檢視其供應鏈與研發合作夥伴，以符合政府的合規要求。同時，這也可能導致全球科技人才流動的重新分配，部分跨國企業可能因應政策風險，調整其在北美的研釋出局。在公共政策層面，此舉可能迫使其他國家在美中科技競爭中進行選邊站，進而影響全球人工智慧標準的制定過程，並可能導致全球科技治理體系出現碎片化的風險。
儘管該政策在美國國內獲得部分支援，認為其有助於保障國家安全與技術領先地位，但不同觀點亦提出警示。部分產業專家指出，人工智慧發展高度依賴全球人才與資料的流動，過度限制國際合作可能導致技術創新速度放緩，甚至引發技術壁壘的惡性迴圈。此外，學術界擔憂此舉將嚴重打擊跨國科研合作，限制基礎科學的突破。因此，外界不應過度解讀該命令為單純的技術封鎖，而應將其視為美國在國家安全與全球科技競爭之間尋求平衡的嘗試，其長期的經濟效益與技術影響仍需經受市場與時間的檢驗。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下幾個方面：首先是該行政命令的具體執行細則，特別是針對企業與學術機構的合規指引何時公佈；其次是中國政府對此政策的反應，以及是否會採取反制措施，進而影響全球半導體與AI產業的供應鏈穩定；最後則是北美地區的科技企業如何調整其全球化策略，以應對日益嚴峻的國際政治環境。各界將密切關注該政策對未來全球人工智慧技術標準、跨國研發合作模式以及地緣政治博弈帶來的長期演變，並持續評估其對全球經濟成長的潛在衝擊。
