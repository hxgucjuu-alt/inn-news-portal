  # Surging Global AI Computing Demand and Labor Unrest in South Asia: A Dual Challenge for Industry and Society
  
  > Digital Realty, a leader in global data centers, has reached record-high performance due to AI computing demand, accelerating its global expansion. Simultaneously, severe social unrest triggered by labor disputes in Bangladesh highlights the challenges regarding labor rights and infrastructure behind the global technology supply chain.
  
  Global data center operator Digital Realty Trust Inc. released its latest quarterly financial report on November 6, 2026. Data shows that the company’s quarterly leasing and signed contracts pending commencement both hit record highs, leading to an upward revision of its full-year core earnings outlook. As one of the world's largest data center Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs), Digital Realty's strong performance directly reflects the urgent global demand for computing power in the technology industry.
  
  With the rapid iteration of artificial intelligence (AI) technology, the thirst for computing power has shown explosive growth, even leading to bottlenecks in some regions where power supply, land resources, and infrastructure construction speeds struggle to keep pace with demand.
  
  During the same period, the social situation in South Asia has undergone significant changes. According to a report by The Independent, large-scale labor protests erupted in the Mirpur area of Dhaka, Bangladesh, on November 4, 2026. Garment factory workers clashed fiercely with security forces over wage issues. The tragedy of a worker being attacked and killed while filming the protest highlights the severe challenges regarding labor rights and supply chain stability in the region.
  
  Cross-referencing reports from multiple media outlets, the surge in AI computing demand has become the core engine driving investment in global digital infrastructure. By owning, operating, and managing real estate, Digital Realty has successfully captured the massive demand for data center space required for AI large model training and inference. These data centers are not merely hardware storage sites; they are "computing factories" that support the operation of the modern digital economy.
  
  Meanwhile, progress in technology hardware has not ceased. According to a report by Techbang, Apple is expected to incorporate TSMC’s 2nm process A20 chip into the standard iPhone 18 models and improve battery life by increasing battery capacity, although this may lead to an increase of approximately $100 in the final retail price due to soaring memory costs. These pieces of information outline a multinational technology ecosystem: upstream chip manufacturing continues to advance, midstream data center space demand is exploding, and downstream terminal products are facing cost pressures and adjustments to release schedules.
  
  However, behind the booming development of AI and technology hardware, there are significant differences in how various sources assess the stability and risks of the global supply chain. Digital Realty’s reporting focuses on the optimism of capital markets and the necessity of infrastructure expansion, emphasizing an industry logic where "supply chases demand." In contrast, sources such as The Independent and the Times of India focus on social unrest in South Asia. As an important link in the global supply chain, the garment industry in Bangladesh and its labor protests reflect the wage and human rights dilemmas faced by traditional manufacturing workers amidst digital transformation and global competition.
  
  It remains to be confirmed whether such regional labor protests will spread to other South Asian countries or cause long-term chain reactions for local technology hardware assembly and supply chain logistics. Furthermore, there is currently a lack of direct official evidence linking Apple's decision to postpone the release of the iPhone 18 series to the first half of 2027 with global supply chain turmoil or chip capacity allocation.
  
  For relevant regions, the surge in AI computing demand means that data center site selection will become a key factor in the competition for digital sovereignty. As an important global manufacturing and service outsourcing base, South Asia could potentially benefit from this wave of global digital transformation if it can properly manage labor relations and improve infrastructure construction. However, if social unrest continues, it will not only affect local garment exports but may also shake the confidence of multinational corporations in the region as a long-term node in the technology supply chain. For industrial policymakers, how to balance labor rights and social stability while promoting AI infrastructure construction has become an unavoidable issue.
  
  Although the market is full of expectations for the future of AI, investors and observers should note that over-interpreting the growth potential of a single industry may overlook macroeconomic and geopolitical risks. While Digital Realty's earnings growth is impressive, its operations are highly dependent on power supply and land development policies; if the global energy crisis intensifies or land policies change, its expansion plans will be directly impacted. Similarly, while the exposure of iPhone 18 specifications has sparked market discussion, there remains great uncertainty regarding consumer acceptance of high-priced mobile phones under global inflationary pressure and fluctuating memory costs.
  
  These factors remind market participants that the prosperity of the technology industry is not a linear development but is constrained by multiple factors, including the global supply chain, labor policies, and the macroeconomic environment.
  
  Future observations should focus on three aspects: First, whether Digital Realty’s global expansion map will include South Asia as a key investment area; second, whether the subsequent development of labor protests in Bangladesh and surrounding South Asian countries will have a substantial impact on the stability of the global supply chain; and finally, how TSMC’s 2nm process capacity allocation between AI and consumer electronics will affect the price trends and market competition landscape of technology hardware over the coming year. The INN editorial department will continue to track the above issues and provide in-depth reports on the interaction between global digital infrastructure and regional economies.

# 全球AI算力需求激增與南亞勞工動盪：產業與社會的雙重挑戰

> 全球資料中心龍頭裡瑞通因AI算力需求創下業績新高，同步加速全球佈局；與此同時，南亞地區孟加拉因勞資爭議引發嚴重社會動盪，顯示全球科技供應鏈背後的勞權與基礎設施挑戰。


全球資料中心營運商裡瑞通（Digital Realty Trust Inc.）於2026年11月6日公佈最新季度財報，資料顯示該公司季度租賃與簽約待生效合約量均創下歷史新高，並隨之上修全年核心盈餘展望。作為全球最大的資料中心房地產投資信託（REITs）之一，裡瑞通的強勁表現直接反映了全球科技產業對運算能力的迫切需求。
隨著人工智慧（AI）技術的快速迭代，各界對於算力的渴求已呈現爆發性增長，甚至在部分地區出現電力供應、土地資源與基礎設施建設速度難以跟上需求的瓶頸。
在同一時期，南亞地區的社會情勢亦出現重大變化，根據《獨立報》（The Independent）報導，孟加拉達卡米爾普爾（Mirpur）地區於2026年11月4日爆發大規模勞工抗爭，成衣廠工人因薪資待遇問題與安全部隊發生激烈衝突，事件中更發生勞工在拍攝抗議過程時遭襲擊致死的悲劇，凸顯了區域內勞動權益與供應鏈穩定性的嚴峻挑戰。
根據多家媒體報導的交叉比對，AI算力需求的激增已成為推動全球數位基礎設施投資的核心引擎。裡瑞通透過持有、營運與管理不動產，成功捕捉到AI大模型訓練與推論對資料中心空間的龐大需求。這類資料中心不僅是硬體存放地，更是支撐現代數位經濟運作的「算力工廠」。
與此同時，科技硬體端的進展亦未停歇，根據《Techbang》報導，蘋果公司預計在iPhone 18標準版中匯入臺積電2奈米製程的A20晶片，並透過加大電池容量來提升續航表現，儘管此舉可能因記憶體成本飆漲導致終端售價上漲約100美元。這些資訊共同勾勒出一個跨國科技生態系：上游晶片製程持續推進，中游資料中心空間需求爆發，而下游終端產品則面臨成本壓力與發布節奏的調整。
然而，在AI與科技硬體蓬勃發展的背後，不同來源對於全球供應鏈的穩定性與風險評估存在顯著差異。裡瑞通的報導聚焦於資本市場的樂觀情緒與基礎設施擴張的必要性，強調的是「供給追趕需求」的產業邏輯。相對地，《獨立報》與《印度時報》（Times of India）等來源則將焦點置於南亞地區的社會動盪。孟加拉成衣產業作為全球供應鏈的重要環節，其勞工抗爭事件反映了在數位轉型與全球化競爭中，傳統製造業勞工面臨的薪資與人權困境。
目前尚待確認的是，這類區域性勞工抗爭是否會進一步擴散至其他南亞國家，或對當地科技硬體組裝與供應鏈物流造成長期的連鎖反應。此外，蘋果將iPhone 18系列產品發布延後至2027年上半年的決策，是否與全球供應鏈的動盪或晶片產能分配有關，目前仍缺乏直接的官方連結證據。
對相關地區而言，AI算力需求的飆升意味著資料中心選址將成為各國爭奪數位主權的關鍵。南亞地區作為全球重要的製造與服務外包基地，若能妥善處理勞資關係並提升基礎設施建設，將有機會在這一波全球數位轉型中分得一杯羹。然而，若社會動盪持續，不僅會影響當地成衣出口，亦可能動搖跨國企業對於該區域作為長期科技供應鏈節點的信心。對於產業政策制定者而言，如何在推動AI基礎設施建設的同時，兼顧勞工權益與社會穩定，已成為無法迴避的課題。
儘管市場對於AI前景充滿期待，但投資者與觀察家仍需注意，過度解讀單一產業的成長潛力可能忽略了宏觀經濟與地緣政治的風險。裡瑞通的業績成長雖然亮眼，但其營運高度依賴電力供給與土地開發政策，若全球能源危機加劇或土地政策變動，將直接衝擊其擴張計畫。同樣地，iPhone 18規格的曝光雖引發市場討論，但在全球通膨壓力與記憶體成本波動下，消費者對高價手機的接受度仍存在極大不確定性。
這些因素提醒市場參與者，科技產業的繁榮並非線性發展，而是受到全球供應鏈、勞工政策與宏觀經濟環境的多重製約。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於三個面向：首先，裡瑞通在全球擴張版圖的具體地點，是否會將南亞地區納入重點投資範圍；其次，孟加拉及周邊南亞國家的勞工抗爭後續發展，是否會對全球供應鏈的穩定性造成實質性衝擊；最後，臺積電2奈米製程在AI與消費電子產品間的產能分配，將如何影響未來一年科技硬體的價格走勢與市場競爭格局。INN新聞編輯部將持續追蹤上述議題，並針對全球數位基礎設施與區域經濟的互動進行深入報導。
