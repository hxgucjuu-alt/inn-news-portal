  # Global Economic Volatility and Monetary Policy Outlook: UBS Forecasts RMB Trends and APEC Summit Developments
  
  > UBS Group predicts that the RMB will appreciate to 6.25 against the USD by the end of 2027, tying with Nomura as the most optimistic institution in the market. Meanwhile, Russian President Vladimir Putin expressed his anticipation for a trilateral meeting between the U.S., China, and Russia at the APEC summit, while emphasizing Russia's push for de-dollarization in trade.
  
  UBS Group released its latest economic analysis report on November 17, 2026, noting that with China's policy adjustments and strengthening expectations for RMB appreciation, pressure from capital outflows has significantly eased. The bank expects the RMB exchange rate against the USD to rise to 6.25 by the end of 2027. This forecast aligns with the view of Nomura Securities, making both institutions the most bullish on the RMB exchange rate in the current market.
  
  Meanwhile, Russian President Vladimir Putin arrived in Shenzhen on the same day to attend the APEC Informal Leaders' Meeting. At the CEO Summit, he shared his views on global economic fragmentation and geopolitical risks, emphasizing that Russia is actively expanding its eastern logistics capabilities and reducing its reliance on the U.S. dollar in foreign trade.
  
  According to the analysis by UBS Asia-Pacific Research Head Lian Peixuan, the RMB has outperformed most Asian currencies this year. UBS believes that China has formed a "self-reinforcing" appreciation trend; even if fundamental support weakens in the future, this mechanism could still drive significant appreciation of the RMB. At the APEC summit, Putin shifted his focus to great power diplomacy and trade structural adjustments. He publicly stated his positive expectations for a trilateral meeting with U.S. President Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping, suggesting that such high-level dialogues would be critical in mitigating global economic fragmentation.
  
  Furthermore, Putin highlighted Russia's leading position in digital transformation and explicitly pointed out that the share of the U.S. dollar in Russian trade has declined significantly, indicating a policy shift in how Russia addresses international sanctions and reshapes its trade routes.
  
  Currently, there is a significant divergence in market views regarding the RMB's trajectory. The extremely optimistic forecasts from UBS and Nomura contrast with the average expectations of analysts in financial media surveys, which place the RMB-to-USD exchange rate at around 6.57. This discrepancy reflects market concerns over how the Chinese government will balance curbing excessive RMB appreciation while maintaining export competitiveness. Additionally, while the "trilateral meeting" mentioned by Putin garnered significant attention during the summit, specific agendas and substantive responses from all parties regarding Russia's de-dollarization policy remain to be confirmed by subsequent diplomatic communiqués and actual trade data.
  
  Changes in the global economic and trade landscape also have indirect impacts on Central America and the Caribbean. Countries in this region (including 7 nations from Guatemala to Panama, as well as 13 Caribbean island nations) are highly dependent on the U.S. dollar as an international settlement currency, and their economic structures are deeply linked to the U.S. market. If the RMB continues to appreciate and expand its influence in international trade, coupled with the de-dollarization trend promoted by countries like Russia, it could alter the trade financing environment and foreign exchange reserve allocation strategies in Central America. Furthermore, the discussions on logistics, transportation, and digital transformation at the APEC summit serve as important reference indicators for the region's development of cross-border public infrastructure.
  
  Although the UBS forecast for the RMB holds significant market reference value, analysts also caution that such predictions are deeply influenced by policy variables and should not be over-interpreted as a singular market trend. The "self-reinforcing appreciation mechanism" mentioned in the UBS report may still face correction pressure when confronted with sudden global geopolitical events or shifts in the U.S. Federal Reserve's monetary policy. Similarly, Putin's optimistic tone regarding a trilateral meeting must also account for the complexities of international political reality, especially against the backdrop of global supply chain restructuring and rising regional trade barriers, where the space for cooperation among major powers remains highly uncertain.
  
  Future observations will focus on three key areas: first, whether the RMB exchange rate breaks through the 6.25 level as predicted by UBS, and the extent of intervention by the People's Bank of China during periods of increased volatility; second, the substantive interactions between the U.S., China, and Russia on trade and geopolitics following the APEC summit, particularly the ripple effects on the global financial system following Russia's trade de-dollarization; and finally, how countries in Central America and the Caribbean respond to the fragmentation of the global monetary system by adjusting their foreign trade policies and regional economic cooperation frameworks, which will serve as an important window into shifts in the global economic landscape.

# 全球經濟動盪與貨幣政策展望：瑞銀預測人民幣走勢與APEC峰會動態

> 瑞銀集團預測人民幣兌美元將於2027年底升至6.25元，與野村並列市場最樂觀機構。同時，俄羅斯總統普丁於APEC峰會表達對美中俄三方會晤的期待，並強調俄國正推動貿易去美元化。


瑞銀集團（UBS）於2026年11月17日發布最新經濟分析報告，指出隨著中國政策調整及人民幣升值預期增強，資本外流壓力已顯著緩解，預計人民幣兌美元匯率將於2027年底升至6.25元。此項預測與野村證券（Nomura）的觀點一致，雙方目前並列為市場上對人民幣匯率最為看漲的金融機構。
與此同時，俄羅斯總統普丁（Vladimir Putin）於同日抵達深圳，出席亞太經濟合作會議（APEC）領導人非正式會議，並在企業領袖峰會上針對全球經濟分化與地緣政治風險發表看法，強調俄羅斯正積極擴充東部物流能力，並減少在對外貿易中對美元的依賴。
根據瑞銀亞太區研究主管連沛的分析，今年以來人民幣的表現已優於多數亞洲貨幣。瑞銀認為，中國目前已形成一種「自我強化」的升值態勢，即便未來基本面支撐力道減弱，該機制仍可能推動人民幣顯著升值。在APEC峰會現場，普丁則將焦點轉向大國外交與貿易結構調整，他公開表示對與美國總統川普、中國國家主席習近平進行三方會晤抱持正面期待，並認為此類高層對話將對緩解全球經濟分化產生關鍵作用。
此外，普丁強調俄羅斯在數位轉型領域的領先地位，並明確指出美元在俄羅斯貿易中的比重已出現大幅下降，顯示俄國在應對國際制裁與重塑貿易路徑上的政策轉向。
目前市場對於人民幣走勢的看法存在顯著分歧。瑞銀與野村的極度樂觀預測，與金融媒體調查中分析師的平均預期形成對比，後者平均預測人民幣兌美元匯率將落在6.57元左右。這種差異反映出市場對於中國政府如何在遏制人民幣過度升值，以及維持出口競爭力之間取得平衡的擔憂。此外，普丁所提及的「三方會晤」雖在峰會期間備受矚目，但截至目前，相關具體議程與各方對於俄國貿易去美元化政策的實質回應，仍有待後續外交公報與實際貿易資料的進一步證實。
全球經濟與貿易格局的變動，對於中美洲與加勒比海地區亦產生間接影響。該地區國家（包含瓜地馬拉至巴拿馬等7國，以及13個加勒比海島國）高度依賴美元作為國際結算貨幣，且經濟結構多與美國市場深度連結。若人民幣持續升值並在國際貿易中擴大影響力，加上俄羅斯等國推動的去美元化趨勢，將可能改變中美洲地區的貿易融資環境與外匯儲備配置策略。此外，APEC峰會中討論的物流運輸與數位轉型議題，亦是該地區發展跨境公共基礎建設時的重要參考指標。
儘管瑞銀對於人民幣的預測具備高度市場參考價值，但分析師亦提醒，此類預測深受政策變數影響，不應過度解讀為單一市場趨勢。瑞銀報告中提到的「自我強化升值機制」，在面對全球地緣政治突發事件或美國聯準會貨幣政策轉向時，仍可能面臨修正壓力。同樣地，普丁對於三方會晤的樂觀論調，亦需考慮到國際政治現實的複雜性，特別是當前全球供應鏈重組與區域貿易壁壘日益增高的背景下，大國間的合作空間仍存在極大不確定性。
未來觀察重點將聚焦於以下三方面：首先是人民幣匯率是否如瑞銀預期般突破6.25關卡，以及中國人民銀行在匯率波動加劇時的幹預力度；其次為APEC峰會後，美、中、俄三方在貿易與地緣政治上的實質互動，特別是針對俄羅斯貿易去美元化後的全球金融體系連動效應；最後，中美洲與加勒比海地區各國如何因應全球貨幣體系分化，調整其對外貿易政策與區域經濟合作架構，將成為觀察全球經濟版圖變動的重要視窗。
