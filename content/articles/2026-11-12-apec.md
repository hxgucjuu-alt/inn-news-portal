  # China Plans to Promote Smart Port Technology at APEC Summit, Targeting 90 Automated Terminals by 2030
  
  > China will showcase its smart port technology at the APEC summit in Shenzhen this November, aiming to increase the number of automated terminals to 90 by 2030 and striving to lead standards for electronic bills of lading and customs databases to strengthen its global maritime influence.
  
  Global maritime trade accounts for over 80% of total merchandise trade, making port operational efficiency and digitalization key areas for countries competing for supply chain dominance. The Chinese Ministry of Transport recently disclosed its port development strategy, planning to use the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit, to be held in Shenzhen from November 18 to 19, 2026, as a platform to showcase its technical prowess in the field of smart ports.
  
  The Chinese government has clearly stated that it will actively seek a leading role in the formulation of electronic bill of lading standards and in working groups for customs databases built using artificial intelligence (AI) and big data, attempting to comprehensively expand China's standard-setting power and influence in the international maritime industry, from terminal hardware equipment to the digitalization of shipping documentation processes.
  
  According to various media reports and official data, China has currently built 60 automated terminals, the highest number in the world. In terms of technical application, the Mawan Terminal in Shenzhen has fully integrated driverless trucks, remote-controlled cranes, and AI management systems; the Qianwan Container Terminal in Qingdao has demonstrated remarkable operational efficiency, with a single crane capable of handling 61 containers per hour, more than double the efficiency of traditional terminals. The specific goal set by Chinese officials is to further expand the total number of automated terminals nationwide to 90 by 2030. This move is not only an upgrade of hardware facilities but also symbolizes China's attempt to promote its smart port ecosystem to the international market.
  
  Although China has shown strong progress in the construction of automated terminals, the global shipping market still faces the challenge of supply and demand imbalance. According to the latest forecast for the 2026 shipping market by the French maritime consultancy Alphaliner, global supply growth is estimated at 4.8%, while demand growth is only 2.5%, indicating that the market remains under pressure from overcapacity.
  
  Furthermore, shipping industry revenue performance is deeply affected by geopolitics and regional holidays. For instance, data recently released by Yang Ming Marine Transport Corporation shows that due to the slowdown in market demand following China's "Golden Week" holiday, revenue for October fell by 3.02% compared to September. Although the year-on-year growth rate remains at 82.45%, it indicates that market demand is fluctuating violently in the short term, and the complexity of the international environment continues to interfere with the stability of the shipping industry.
  
  The promotion of smart port technology has a profound impact on regional economies and industrial chains. If China succeeds in promoting and leading the standards for electronic bills of lading and customs databases at APEC, it could significantly simplify trade clearance efficiency within the region and reduce logistics costs, but it may also trigger concerns among other economies regarding data security and technological dependence. For shipping companies, while the popularization of automated terminals can improve operational efficiency and reduce labor costs, balancing capital expenditure with overcapacity will be an operational challenge that shipping enterprises must face in the coming years against the backdrop of slowing global demand growth.
  
  Currently, there are various interpretations regarding China's intention to promote the standardization of smart ports. Supporters believe this will help the digital transformation of the global supply chain and improve overall trade efficiency; however, critics focus on the issue of data sovereignty behind technical standardization, fearing that if a single country leads the customs database, it could lead to compromised transparency and fairness in trade information. In addition, the construction of automated terminals is highly dependent on AI and big data technology, which also intersects with the current global discussion on AI technology regulation and safety; whether relevant technical standards can reach a consensus among all parties under the APEC framework remains to be seen.
  
  The focus for follow-up observation will be the specific responses of member states to the smart port technology standards and customs database cooperation proposals put forward by China during the Shenzhen APEC summit in November. In addition, as shipping companies gradually arrange for the resumption of key routes such as the Red Sea route, the dynamic adjustments of global shipping logistics, and whether the manufacturing PMI index can maintain an expansion trend will become important indicators for judging whether the shipping market demand is warming up. How countries coordinate technical standards and data security while pursuing port digitalization will be an important agenda for future Asia-Pacific economic cooperation.

# 中國擬藉APEC峰會推廣智慧港口技術，目標2030年擴充至90座自動化碼頭

> 中國將於11月深圳APEC峰會展示智慧港口技術，目標2030年將自動化碼頭增至90座，並爭取主導電子提單與海關資料庫標準，以強化全球海運影響力。


全球海運貿易量佔總商品貿易逾八成，港口營運效率與數位化程度已成為各國爭奪供應鏈主導權的關鍵領域。中國交通運輸部近期公開其港口發展戰略，計畫利用即將於2026年11月18日至19日在深圳舉辦的亞太經濟合作會議（APEC）峰會作為平臺，向亞太地區展示其在智慧港口領域的技術實力。
中國政府明確表示，將積極爭取在電子提單標準制定，以及運用人工智慧（AI）與大資料構建的海關資料庫工作小組中發揮主導作用，試圖從碼頭硬體裝置至航運檔案數位化流程，全面擴大中國在國際海運產業的標準制定權與話語權。
根據多家媒體報導與官方資料顯示，中國目前已建成60座自動化碼頭，數量居全球之首。在技術應用層面，深圳媽灣碼頭已全面匯入無人駕駛卡車、遠端操控起重機及AI管理系統；青島前灣貨櫃碼頭則展現出顯著的作業效率，其單臺起重機每小時處理貨櫃量可達61個，效率為傳統碼頭的兩倍以上。中國官方設定的具體目標為在2030年前，將全國自動化碼頭總數進一步擴充至90座。此舉不僅是硬體設施的升級，更象徵中國正試圖將其智慧港口生態系統推廣至國際市場。
儘管中國在自動化碼頭建設上展現強勢進度，但全球航運市場仍面臨供需失衡的挑戰。根據法國海運諮詢機構Alphaliner針對2026年航運市場供需的最新預測，全球供給成長率預估為4.8%，而需求成長率僅為2.5%，顯示市場仍存在產能過剩的壓力。
此外，航運業營收表現深受地緣政治與區域假期影響，如陽明海運近期發布的資料顯示，受中國「十一」長假後市場需求放緩影響，10月單月營收較9月下滑3.02%，儘管年增率仍達82.45%，顯示短期內市場需求波動劇烈，且國際環境的複雜性對航運業的穩定性構成持續性幹擾。
智慧港口技術的推廣對區域經濟與產業鏈具有深遠影響。若中國成功在APEC推動並主導電子提單及海關資料庫標準，將可能大幅簡化區域內的貿易通關效率，進而降低物流成本，但也可能引發其他經濟體對於資料安全與技術依賴的擔憂。對於航運業者而言，自動化碼頭的普及雖然能提升作業效率、減少人力成本，但在全球需求成長放緩的背景下，如何平衡資本支出與運能過剩問題，將是未來幾年航運企業必須面對的營運課題。
目前外界對於中國推動智慧港口標準化的意圖存在多種解讀。支持者認為，這將有助於全球供應鏈的數位化轉型，提升整體貿易效率；然而，批評者則關注技術標準化背後的資料主權問題，擔心若由單一國家主導海關資料庫，可能導致貿易資訊的透明度與公平性受損。此外，自動化碼頭的建設高度依賴AI與大資料技術，這也與當前全球對於AI技術監管與安全性的討論產生交集，相關技術標準在APEC框架下是否能獲得各方共識，仍有待觀察。
後續觀察重點在於11月深圳APEC峰會期間，各成員國對於中國提出的智慧港口技術標準與海關資料庫合作案的具體回應。此外，隨著航運業者陸續安排如紅海航線等關鍵航線復航，全球航運物流的動態調整，以及製造業PMI指數是否能維持擴張趨勢，將成為判斷航運市場需求是否回暖的重要指標。各國在追求港口數位化的同時，如何協調技術標準與資料安全，將是未來亞太經濟合作的重要議程。
