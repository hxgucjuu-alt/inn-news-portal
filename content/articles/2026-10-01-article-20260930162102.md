  # Pokémon Joins Forces with European Space Agency to Promote Science Education; Taiwan Tech Deepens International Co-creation for USR
  
  > Pokémon and the European Space Agency (ESA) have partnered to promote World Space Week, with a special Pikachu doll orbiting the Earth over a thousand times. Simultaneously, Taiwan Tech hosted an international USR empowerment event to explore sustainable practices in regional revitalization and cross-border educational cooperation.
  
  Two significant developments have recently emerged in the global fields of education and society. First, in the realm of science education and cross-industry marketing, the global IP "Pokémon" and the European Space Agency (ESA) have announced a deep partnership aimed at warming up for the upcoming 2026 "World Space Week." This collaboration transcends traditional brand licensing frameworks; a specially made Pikachu doll has actually been sent into space, orbiting the Earth more than a thousand times, becoming a symbol connecting the public with space science.
  
  Second, in the field of higher education social responsibility, the National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (Taiwan Tech) held the Ministry of Education’s 2026 University Social Responsibility (USR) joint empowerment event in late September. The theme focused on "New Horizons for Sustainability: Innovation, Diffusion, and International Cooperation," bringing together scholars and practitioners from Taiwan, Japan, the United States, and Indonesia to engage in cross-border exchanges on issues such as regional revitalization, smart governance, and community resilience.
  
  According to a report by *Techbang*, the collaboration between Pokémon and the European Space Agency is not limited to marketing, but rather uses a high-profile IP to attract global public interest in space science. The project will guide players to explore space knowledge through game interfaces such as *Pokémon GO* during World Space Week, which runs from October 4 to 10. Meanwhile, *Yahoo Entertainment* noted that Taiwan Tech’s USR empowerment event has established a development trend for higher education to move from "local practice" to "international co-creation."
  
  President Jia-Yush Yen emphasized that USR is a core development priority for the university. The goal is to enable students to give back to society through what they have learned and to integrate Taiwan's local social innovation experiences with global sustainable development issues through cross-national course collaborations and group workshops.
  
  Although both events demonstrate a trend of cross-boundary cooperation, there are significant differences in their goals and nature. The collaboration between Pokémon and the ESA belongs to the intersection of popular science and the entertainment industry, focusing on increasing public participation in Space Week through digital games. In contrast, Taiwan Tech’s USR empowerment event belongs to the academic and social policy level, aiming to address the dilemma faced by higher education in promoting social responsibility, where projects struggle to form long-term, scalable impacts. Information currently awaiting confirmation includes whether the partnership between Pokémon and the ESA will be long-term, and how Taiwan Tech, under the international co-creation model, will specifically measure social impact assessment indicators for various USR projects across different countries.
  
  These two developments have had a concrete impact on the global society and education industries. At the educational level, the Taiwan Tech case shows that universities are no longer limited to social services in a single region, but are shifting toward building cross-national knowledge networks, which is indicative for global colleges and universities in promoting the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). At the level of popular science and mass communication, the Pokémon and ESA collaboration provides a new paradigm for promoting science education by leveraging the penetration of cultural IP to lower the threshold for understanding science.
  
  Furthermore, the Global Risk Report recently released by the United Nations also points out that understanding and preparing for complex risks requires broad, cross-border participation, which coincides with the aforementioned international cooperation trends in education and science popularization.
  
  However, both academia and industry warn against over-interpreting the effectiveness of single events. Regarding international cooperation in USR, experts point out that without a foundation of deep local engagement, cross-border cooperation can easily become superficial and fail to achieve substantial improvements in community resilience. Similarly, while the Pokémon and ESA collaboration can attract significant traffic, how to translate "gaming experience" into "scientific literacy" still depends on subsequent educational design and content integration. The success of these activities depends on whether they can strike a balance between short-term public attention and long-term social value creation, rather than merely stopping at the level of brand exposure or short-term academic exchange.
  
  Future observations will focus on two points: First, whether the collaboration between Pokémon and the ESA can effectively increase public awareness of space issues during the 2026 World Space Week, and whether the ESA will expand the scale of its cooperation with other gaming IPs. Second, how Taiwanese colleges and universities can overcome the challenges of cross-border regulations, cultural differences, and resource integration in the process of promoting USR internationalization, and translate these cases into replicable international education models. Relevant units and research institutions will continue to track the long-term impact of these cross-boundary collaborations on global education policy and social participation models.

# 寶可夢聯手歐洲太空總署推廣科普，臺科大深化USR國際共創

> 寶可夢與歐洲太空總署合作推廣世界太空週，特製皮卡丘繞行地球逾千圈；同時，臺科大舉辦USR國際培力活動，探討地方創生與跨國教育合作之永續實踐。


全球教育與社會領域近期出現兩項顯著進展。首先，在科普教育與跨界行銷方面，全球知名 IP《寶可夢》（Pokémon）官方與歐洲太空總署（ESA）宣佈建立深度合作夥伴關係，旨在為即將到來的 2026 年「世界太空週（World Space Week）」暖身。此次合作突破傳統品牌授權框架，一隻特製的皮卡丘玩偶已實際升空，並繞行地球超過一千圈，成為連結大眾與太空科學的象徵。
其次，在高等教育社會責任領域，國立臺灣科技大學於 2026 年 9 月下旬舉辦教育部一一五年度大學社會責任（USR）共同培力活動，主題聚焦於「永續新視野：創新、擴散與國際合作」，匯聚來自臺灣、日本、美國及印尼的學者與實務工作者，針對地方創生、智慧治理及社群韌性等議題進行跨國交流。
根據《Techbang》報導，寶可夢與歐洲太空總署的合作並非僅止於行銷層面，而是透過高人氣 IP 吸引全球大眾對太空科學的興趣。該合作計畫將於 10 月 4 日至 10 日的世界太空週期間，透過《Pokémon GO》等遊戲介面，引導玩家深入探索太空知識。而《Yahoo Entertainment》則指出，臺科大的 USR 培力活動確立了高等教育從「在地實踐」跨越至「國際共創」的發展趨勢。
校長顏家鈺強調，USR 乃該校核心發展重點，目標在於讓學生透過所學回饋社會，並將臺灣在地的社會創新經驗，透過跨國課程合作與分組工作坊，與全球永續發展議題接軌。
儘管兩項事件均展現了跨界合作的趨勢，但其目標與性質存在顯著差異。寶可夢與 ESA 的合作屬於大眾科普與娛樂產業的跨界結合，重點在於透過數位遊戲提升大眾對太空週的參與度；相較之下，臺科大的 USR 培力活動則屬於學術與社會政策層面，旨在解決高等教育在推動社會責任時，面臨計畫難以形成長期規模化影響的困境。目前尚待確認的資訊包括：寶可夢與 ESA 的合作是否會長期化，以及臺科大在國際共創模式下，具體如何衡量各項 USR 計畫在不同國家的社會影響力評估指標。
這兩項動態對全球社會與教育產業產生了具體影響。在教育層面，臺科大的案例顯示，大學已不再侷限於單一區域的社會服務，而是轉向建立跨國知識網路，這對全球大專院校推動永續發展目標（SDGs）具有指標意義。而在科普與大眾傳播層面，寶可夢與太空總署的合作案，則為推廣科學教育提供了新典範，即利用文化 IP 的滲透力來降低科學的理解門檻。
此外，聯合國近期發布的全球風險報告（Global Risk Report）亦指出，理解並準備複雜風險需要跨國界的廣泛參與，這與上述教育與科普領域的國際合作趨勢不謀而合。
然而，學界與產業界均提醒，不應過度解讀單一活動的成效。針對 USR 的國際合作，專家指出，若缺乏在地深耕的基礎，跨國合作容易流於形式，難以達成實質的社群韌性提升；同樣地，寶可夢與 ESA 的合作雖能吸引大量流量，但如何將「遊戲體驗」轉化為「科學素養」，仍有賴後續的教育設計與內容銜接。這些活動的成功與否，取決於其能否在短期的公眾關注與長期的社會價值創造之間取得平衡，而非僅僅停留在品牌曝光或短期學術交流的層次。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下兩點：第一，2026 年世界太空週期間，寶可夢與 ESA 的合作是否能有效提升大眾對太空議題的認知度，以及 ESA 是否會擴大與其他遊戲 IP 的合作規模；第二，臺灣大專院校在推動 USR 國際化過程中，如何克服跨國法規、文化差異與資源整合的挑戰，並將這些案例轉化為可複製的國際教育模式。相關單位與研究機構將持續追蹤這些跨界合作對全球教育政策與社會參與模式的長期影響。
