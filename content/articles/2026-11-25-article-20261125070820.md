  # NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang Ousted by Board: A Crisis of Integrity and Corporate Governance
  
  > NVIDIA founder Jensen Huang has reportedly been ousted by the company's board of directors. Insiders indicate that the core reason for the dismissal was not merely an extramarital affair, but rather his choice to "lie" when confronted with related integrity issues. This action, deemed a severe violation of corporate governance principles, has drawn intense global attention from the technology sector.
  
  As the leader of the global AI chip industry, the departure of NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang has sent shockwaves through global financial and technology markets. According to a report by *Techbang* on November 23, 2026, Huang’s departure was not a sudden, isolated event, but a decision reached after careful deliberation by the NVIDIA board of directors. The report cites insiders stating that the board was dissatisfied with how Huang handled his personal conduct, particularly regarding communication surrounding his private life and integrity issues; Huang was accused of failing to remain candid, which served as the catalyst for the board's final decision to remove him.
  
  This incident is not merely a change in individual career paths; it is viewed as a significant case study on how major technology companies define the boundaries between "integrity" and "corporate governance" when facing executive moral risks.
  
  According to currently available information, the NVIDIA board remains firm in its stance regarding Huang's dismissal. Multiple sources have consistently confirmed that despite efforts by industry heavyweights, including Elon Musk, and some NVIDIA employees to advocate for his return, the board has maintained its original decision, characterizing his actions as a "severe violation of corporate governance and institutional standards." This demonstrates that the NVIDIA board has adopted strict ethical scrutiny standards when dealing with the company's core leader.
  
  Insiders emphasize that board members believe that as the helmsman of a global technology giant, integrity is a non-negotiable bottom line. Any attempt to conceal the truth causes irreparable damage to the company's brand reputation and shareholder trust.
  
  However, there remain some discrepancies and unresolved aspects regarding the details of this incident. Although the *Techbang* report clearly points to an "extramarital affair" as the starting point for the board's concern, there is currently no detailed official announcement or third-party investigative report to substantiate the specific timeline of how this evolved from a private matter into a corporate governance crisis, or whether there were factional struggles within the board. Furthermore, information regarding the specific content and extent of the "lying" is currently limited to one-sided accounts from insiders. NVIDIA has yet to issue a statement regarding the detailed decision-making process for the dismissal, leaving the market in a wait-and-see mode regarding the company's future strategic direction and operational stability.
  
  This incident has had a profound impact on the global semiconductor industry and the AI supply chain. As the absolute leader in the AI chip market, every move NVIDIA makes affects the nerves of the global tech industry. Huang's departure may not only lead to a restructuring of NVIDIA's internal management but has also triggered investor concerns regarding the company's future product development roadmap, relationships with partners, and whether its leading position in the AI market will be shaken. Moreover, this storm once again highlights the importance companies place on the "ethical standards" and "corporate governance" of senior executives. In the current environment of rising global ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) awareness, any flaw involving integrity can become a fatal blow to a corporate leader's career.
  
  Public opinion on this incident is polarized. Perspectives supporting the board's decision argue that technology giants must be built on absolute transparency and integrity, and that any act of concealment should be strictly sanctioned to maintain the soundness of corporate governance. However, some market analysts and supporters argue that as the soul of NVIDIA, Huang's private life should be appropriately separated from his professional management capabilities, and that overemphasizing moral judgment could lead the company to lose its most strategically visionary leader, thereby affecting overall industrial competitiveness. Until information becomes fully transparent, the outside world should not dismiss his professional capabilities based solely on one-sided reports and should await the company's subsequent governance reports and public explanations.
  
  The focus for future observation lies in how the NVIDIA board will proceed with succession planning and whether the new leadership can stabilize investor confidence. Additionally, whether NVIDIA will release a formal internal investigation report to clarify the specific scope of the integrity issues will be key to stabilizing market sentiment. At the same time, the global tech community will continue to monitor whether this incident triggers follow-up reforms in internal governance standards at other large technology companies. This storm is not only a challenge for NVIDIA but also an important test for the global technology industry in facing the moral risks of its leaders.

# 輝達執行長黃仁勳遭董事會罷免：誠信危機與公司治理風暴

> 輝達（NVIDIA）創辦人黃仁勳日前驚傳遭董事會罷免，知情人士指出，罷免核心主因並非單純的婚外情醜聞，而是其在面對相關誠信問題時選擇「說謊」，此舉嚴重違反公司治理原則，引發全球科技界高度關注。


輝達（NVIDIA）作為全球AI晶片產業的龍頭，其執行長黃仁勳的去職事件震撼全球金融與科技市場。根據《Techbang》於2026年11月23日的報導，黃仁勳離職並非突發性的單一事件，而是經過輝達董事會內部審慎評估後的決定。該報導引述知情人士說法，指出輝達董事會對於黃仁勳個人行為的處理方式感到不滿，特別是在涉及私生活與誠信問題的溝通上，黃仁勳被指控未能保持坦誠，這成為董事會最終決定罷免的導火線。
此事件不僅是個人職涯的變動，更被視為大型科技公司在面臨高層道德風險時，如何界定「誠信」與「公司治理」界線的重大案例。
根據目前公開的資訊，輝達董事會對於罷免黃仁勳的立場相當堅定。多方訊息來源一致確認，儘管有包括馬斯克（Elon Musk）在內的業界重量級人物，以及部分輝達內部員工極力爭取其回歸，但董事會仍維持原議，將其行為定調為「嚴重違反公司治理與體制」。這顯示出輝達董事會在面對公司核心領袖時，採取了嚴格的道德審視標準。
知情人士強調，董事會成員認為，作為一家全球性科技巨頭的掌舵者，誠信是不可妥協的底線，任何試圖掩蓋事實的行為，都會對公司的品牌聲譽與股東信任造成無法彌補的傷害。
然而，關於此事件的細節仍存在部分分歧與尚待釐清之處。雖然《Techbang》報導明確指出「婚外情」是引發董事會關注的起點，但針對該事件如何從私人領域演變為公司治理危機的具體時間軸，以及董事會內部是否存在不同派系的角力，目前尚無更詳盡的官方宣告或第三方調查報告佐證。此外，外界對於「說謊」的具體內容與程度，目前僅能透過知情人士的片面說法得知，輝達官方至今尚未針對罷免的詳細決策過程發表說明，這也使得市場對於未來輝達的戰略走向與經營穩定性，仍抱持觀望態度。
此事件對全球半導體產業及AI供應鏈產生了深遠的影響。作為AI晶片市場的絕對主導者，輝達的一舉一動牽動著全球科技產業的神經。黃仁勳的離職不僅可能導致輝達內部管理層的重組，更引發了投資人對於輝達未來產品開發路徑、與合作夥伴關係以及AI市場領先地位是否會動搖的擔憂。此外，這場風暴也再次凸顯了企業對於高層主管「道德標準」與「公司治理」的重視程度，在當前全球ESG（環境、社會與公司治理）意識抬頭的環境下，任何涉及誠信的瑕疵都可能成為企業領袖職涯的致命傷。
針對此事件，外界觀點呈現兩極化。支援董事會決策的觀點認為，科技巨頭必須建立在絕對的透明與誠信之上，任何隱瞞行為都應受到嚴格制裁，以維護公司治理的健全；然而，也有部分市場分析師與支持者認為，黃仁勳作為輝達的靈魂人物，其個人私生活與公司專業經營能力應適度切割，過度強調道德審判可能導致公司失去最具戰略眼光的領袖，進而影響整體產業競爭力。在資訊尚未完全透明化之前，外界不應僅憑單一面向的報導對其專業能力進行全面否定，應靜待公司後續的治理報告與公開說明。
後續觀察重點在於輝達董事會將如何進行接班佈局，以及新任領導層是否能穩住投資人的信心。此外，輝達是否會針對此次事件發布正式的內部調查報告，以釐清誠信問題的具體範疇，將是穩定市場情緒的關鍵。同時，全球科技界也將持續關注此事件是否會引發其他大型科技公司在內部治理規範上的跟進與改革。這場風暴不僅是輝達的挑戰，更是全球科技產業在面對領導者道德風險時的一場重要測試。
