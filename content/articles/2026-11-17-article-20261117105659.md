  # A Ten-Year Retrospective on Brexit: An Analysis of Economic Stagnation and the Shifting Geopolitical Landscape of the EU
  
  > Ten years have passed since the Brexit referendum. Assessments indicate that the UK's GDP has suffered significantly, with the economy mired in long-term stagnation. This article integrates geographical and economic perspectives to analyze the profound impact of Brexit on the UK and the broader geopolitical and economic development of Europe.
  
  Ten years have passed since the Brexit referendum, a major event that altered the geopolitical landscape of Europe. At the time, supporters viewed it as a critical turning point to break free from EU regulatory constraints and regain sovereignty over national laws and borders. From a geographical perspective, Europe, defined as a peninsula on the northwestern part of the Eurasian continent, has long maintained a high degree of economic integration and open borders through the EU system. However, with the UK's formal departure from this system, the mode of interaction between the UK and the European continent has undergone structural changes. Brexit was not merely a political decision by a single nation; it profoundly affected the overall economic stability and regional governance models of the European continent, becoming an important case study in contemporary international political research.
  
  According to currently available economic data and analytical reports, multiple sources have confirmed the negative economic effects following Brexit. Data shows that in the decade since Brexit, the UK's Gross Domestic Product (GDP) has experienced a significant decline, reaching as high as 8%. This figure reflects the chain reactions caused by trade barriers, declining investment confidence, and obstacles to labor market mobility. Although Brexit supporters had expected to reap economic dividends after shedding the EU's complex regulations, the reality has presented a state of economic stagnation, indicating that the UK failed to successfully compensate for the economic gap through globalized layouts after losing access to the European Single Market.
  
  Regarding the specific impact of Brexit, there are differences in perspectives and assessments. Some viewpoints emphasize the shock of Brexit on the UK's financial services and manufacturing supply chains, arguing that this is the primary cause of economic stagnation; however, other analyses point out that external factors such as changes in the global economic environment, the impact of the pandemic, and energy price fluctuations have also placed undeniable pressure on the UK economy. What remains to be confirmed is whether the UK government can effectively offset the long-term structural damage caused by Brexit through the signing of independent bilateral trade agreements in the next decade, and how the EU will adjust its foreign trade policy to maintain competitiveness following the UK's departure.
  
  Analyzing from the perspective of geopolitics and regional policy, Brexit has had a profound impact on European industries and society. As an important economy in northwestern Europe, the UK's withdrawal has led to a restructuring of the power dynamics within the EU when dealing with transnational public issues. For industries, companies must re-adapt to the newly added tariffs and administrative costs between the UK and the EU, which directly increases the complexity of cross-border operations. Furthermore, regarding education and talent exchange, Brexit has restricted the free movement of academic and professional labor, posing negative challenges to Europe's overall innovation capacity and knowledge economy development, prompting European nations to re-examine the value of regional integration.
  
  There are significant differences in views regarding the impact of Brexit, and it should not be oversimplified. Brexit supporters still argue that, in the long run, the UK can build a more flexible economic model through an autonomous legal system, and that breaking away from the EU bureaucracy helps improve national governance efficiency. However, the economic community generally believes that the short-to-medium-term economic costs far outweigh the expected dividends. The limitation is that measuring the success or failure of Brexit solely by GDP data may overlook non-economic factors such as political sovereignty and social identity. Therefore, when evaluating the impact of Brexit, it is necessary to carefully distinguish between the objective decline in economic data and the long-term value of political decisions, avoiding the reduction of complex international governance issues to a simple economic profit-and-loss analysis.
  
  The focus of subsequent observation lies in how the UK handles the revision of its trade relationship with the EU, and whether more variables in regional integration will emerge within the EU due to the UK's precedent. In addition, whether the voices of review regarding Brexit policy within the UK will translate into concrete policy revisions is also a key point to watch. As the ten-year transition period after Brexit concludes, the UK's industrial policy, foreign trade strategy, and neighborhood relations with the European continent will enter a new stage of adjustment. The international community will continue to monitor whether the UK can find new growth points amidst the predicament of economic stagnation, and how the European Union will consolidate its position as a major global economy in the face of geopolitical challenges.

# 英國脫歐十年回顧：經濟停滯與歐盟地緣版圖的變遷分析

> 英國脫歐公投屆滿十年，相關評估顯示英國GDP受創嚴重，經濟陷入長期停滯。本文綜合地理與經濟視角，分析脫歐對英國及歐洲整體地緣政治與經濟發展的深遠影響。


英國脫歐（Brexit）公投至今已屆滿十年，這場改變歐洲地緣政治版圖的重大事件，在當時被支持者視為擺脫歐盟法規束縛、重新掌握國家法律與邊界主權的關鍵轉折。從地理學定義來看，歐洲作為歐亞大陸西北部的半島，長期以來透過歐盟體系維持高度的經濟整合與邊界開放。然而，隨著英國正式脫離該體系，其與歐洲大陸的互動模式發生了結構性的改變。英國脫歐不僅是單一國家的政治決策，更深刻影響了歐洲大陸整體的經濟穩定與區域治理模式，成為當代國際政治研究的重要案例。
根據目前公開的經濟資料與分析報告，多個來源共同確認了英國脫歐後的經濟負面效應。資料顯示，英國在脫歐後的十年內，國內生產毛額（GDP）出現了顯著的衰退，幅度高達8%。這項資料反映了脫歐帶來的貿易壁壘、投資信心下降以及勞動力市場流動受阻等連鎖反應。儘管脫歐支持者曾預期擺脫歐盟繁複的法規後能迎來經濟紅利，但現實情況卻呈現出經濟反停滯的狀態，顯示出英國在失去歐洲單一市場准入權後，未能成功透過全球化佈局彌補經濟缺口。
關於脫歐的具體影響，各界說法與評估存在差異。部分觀點強調脫歐對英國金融服務業與製造業供應鏈的衝擊，認為這是導致經濟停滯的主因；然而，亦有分析指出，全球經濟環境的變動、疫情影響以及能源價格波動等外部因素，同樣對英國經濟造成了不可忽視的壓力。目前尚待確認的是，英國政府在未來十年內，是否能透過簽署獨立的雙邊貿易協定，有效抵銷脫歐帶來的長期結構性損害，以及歐盟在面對英國脫離後，如何調整其對外貿易政策以維持競爭力。
從地緣政治與區域政策的角度分析，英國脫歐對歐洲產業與社會產生了深遠影響。英國作為歐洲西北部的重要經濟體，其退出導致了歐盟在處理跨國公共議題時的權力結構重組。對於產業而言，企業必須重新適應英國與歐盟之間新增的關稅與行政成本，這直接增加了跨國經營的複雜度。此外，對於教育與人才交流，脫歐也限制了學術與專業勞動力的自由流動，這對歐洲整體的創新能力與知識經濟發展帶來了負面挑戰，促使歐洲各國重新審視區域整合的價值。
針對脫歐的影響，各界觀點存在顯著分歧，不應過度簡化解讀。支援脫歐者仍主張，長期而言，英國能透過自主的法律體系建立更具彈性的經濟模式，且擺脫歐盟官僚體繫有助於提升國家治理效率。然而，經濟學界普遍認為，短期至中期的經濟成本遠高於預期紅利。限制在於，單純以GDP資料衡量脫歐成敗可能忽略了政治主權與社會認同等非經濟因素。因此，在評估脫歐影響時，必須謹慎區分經濟資料的客觀衰退與政治決策的長期價值，避免將複雜的國際治理議題簡化為單一的經濟損益分析。
後續觀察重點在於英國如何處理與歐盟的貿易關係修訂，以及歐盟內部是否會因英國的先例而出現更多區域整合的變數。此外，英國國內對於脫歐政策的檢討聲浪是否會轉化為具體的政策修正，亦是觀察重點。隨著脫歐後的十年過渡期結束，英國的產業政策、對外貿易戰略以及與歐洲大陸的鄰裡關係將進入新的調整階段。國際社會將持續關注英國能否在經濟停滯的困境中找到新的增長點，以及歐洲聯盟在面對地緣政治挑戰時，如何鞏固其作為全球重要經濟體的地位。
