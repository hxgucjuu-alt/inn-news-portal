知名摔角選手 CM Punk 預計將於 2026 年 10 月 9 日回歸世界摔角娛樂（WWE）的 SmackDown 節目。根據 Sacnilk.com 等多家媒體報導，WWE 已於近期節目中正式宣布了這位巨星的歸來，引發了摔角界的廣泛關注。

儘管回歸消息已獲證實，但資料顯示，WWE 尚未公布 CM Punk 在 10 月 9 日 SmackDown 節目中的具體比賽或環節。WrestlingNoticias 報導指出，該集節目將在路易斯安那州拉法葉的 Cajundome 舉行，並且是「Money in the Bank」大賽前的最後一集 SmackDown。

此外，該集 SmackDown 節目也將有其他重要看點。Ringside News 報導指出，夏洛特·福萊爾（Charlotte Flair）和塔圖姆·帕克斯利（Tatum Paxley）將爭奪 WWE 女子雙打冠軍，對陣 Fatal Influence 組合。CM Punk 的回歸無疑為這場賽事增添了更多星光和期待。

## English edition

Renowned professional wrestler CM Punk is slated to make his return to World Wrestling Entertainment's (WWE) SmackDown program on October 9, 2026. Multiple media outlets, including Sacnilk.com, have reported that WWE officially announced the superstar's comeback during a recent broadcast, generating significant buzz within the wrestling community.

While the return has been confirmed, available data indicates that WWE has not yet revealed a specific match or segment for CM Punk's appearance on the October 9 SmackDown episode. WrestlingNoticias reports that this particular show is scheduled to take place at the Cajundome in Lafayette, Louisiana, and will serve as the final SmackDown before the "Money in the Bank" premium live event.

Furthermore, the October 9 SmackDown episode is set to feature other significant attractions. Ringside News reports that Charlotte Flair and Tatum Paxley will challenge the Fatal Influence duo for the WWE Women’s Tag Team Championships. CM Punk's return is expected to add considerable star power and anticipation to the event.
