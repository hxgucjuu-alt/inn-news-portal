  > Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang recently admitted to an extramarital affair with a woman accused of being a Chinese spy, sparking significant concern in U.S. political circles. Multiple members of Congress, citing national security concerns, have publicly demanded his resignation and are preparing to mandate his attendance at hearings.
  
  As Nvidia stands as the leader of the global artificial intelligence (AI) industry, the personal affairs of its CEO, Jensen Huang, have recently become a focal point in Washington politics. According to a report by *Technews.tw* citing *CNBC*, Huang recently publicly admitted to maintaining a long-term extramarital affair with a woman named Alice Huang (transliteration), who has been identified by U.S. intelligence agencies as a Chinese spy. This news immediately triggered a strong shockwave in the U.S. Congress. Because Nvidia holds core technologies essential to the global AI chip supply chain and is involved in highly sensitive national security issues, Huang’s personal conduct is viewed by some lawmakers as a potential national security breach, leading to growing calls for his resignation.
  
  According to currently available public information, multiple bipartisan U.S. lawmakers have taken action regarding this incident. Republican Senator Kevin Cramer of the Senate Banking Committee was the first to publicly call for Huang to resign voluntarily, citing personal conscience and moral responsibility. Cramer emphasized that given the woman's alleged status as a Chinese spy, Huang’s interactions with her could lead to the leakage of sensitive technology, posing a severe threat to U.S. technological sovereignty. Congress is currently considering exercising its subpoena power to mandate Huang’s attendance at a hearing to conduct a comprehensive investigation into the details of his relationship with the woman, whether any company secrets were compromised, and the internal security control mechanisms at Nvidia regarding its top executives.
  
  However, there are still discrepancies and areas requiring clarification among the various reports. Although Huang has admitted to the affair, there is no official investigative report confirming whether the woman is indeed a Chinese spy, whether Huang was aware of her identity during their relationship, or whether any technical data was leaked through this channel. The details regarding the identity of "Alice Huang" and her specific links to Chinese intelligence units are currently based primarily on public accusations by lawmakers and intelligence agency findings cited by the media; the matter has not yet entered judicial proceedings or public hearings, and therefore awaits further investigation to determine if it constitutes substantive legal liability or a national security violation.
  
  This incident has already had a tangible impact on the global technology industry and the geopolitical landscape. As a monopolistic enterprise in the AI chip market, Nvidia’s stability is directly linked to the security of global digital infrastructure. If Huang is forced to resign or if Nvidia faces strict regulation by the U.S. Congress, it could trigger severe volatility in the global technology supply chain and potentially influence the policy direction of various countries regarding AI technology procurement. Furthermore, this incident has intensified the atmosphere of U.S.-China technological confrontation, and senior executives at multinational technology firms may face more rigorous background checks and personal conduct standards in the future to prevent similar national security risks.
  
  It must be emphasized that while the calls from members of Congress are strong, they represent political pressure and do not equate to a legal verdict. At this stage, one should not overinterpret this incident as one that will immediately lead to a shutdown of Nvidia’s operations or that Huang will inevitably face criminal charges. Observers should distinguish between "calls for resignation on moral grounds" and "national security investigations on legal grounds." Additionally, the allegations of being a "Chinese spy" are currently in the stage of intelligence analysis and political maneuvering; until evidence is proven in court, a neutral stance should be maintained, avoiding the treatment of statements by individual politicians as final rulings, while continuing to monitor subsequent responses from Nvidia and the U.S. Department of Justice.

This storm is not only a management crisis for Nvidia but also an important indicator of how the global technology industry will handle executive security and corporate transparency while caught in the crossfire of U.S.-China geopolitics.

# 輝達執行長黃仁勳陷國安風暴 美國會跨黨派要求辭職並出席聽證

> 輝達（Nvidia）執行長黃仁勳日前承認與遭指控為中國間諜的女性維持婚外情，引發美國政壇高度關注。多位國會議員以國家安全為由，公開要求其辭職並研擬強製出席聽證會。


輝達（Nvidia）作為全球人工智慧（AI）產業的領頭羊，其執行長黃仁勳的個人動態近期成為華府政壇焦點。根據《Technews.tw》引述《CNBC》報導，黃仁勳於日前公開承認與一名經美國情報機構認定為中國間諜的女性，名為黃樂（Alice，音譯）的人士，長期維持婚外情關係。此訊息一出，隨即在美國國會引發強烈震動，由於輝達掌握全球AI晶片供應鏈的核心技術，且涉及高度敏感的國安議題，黃仁勳的私人行為被部分議員視為潛在的國安破口，要求其引咎辭職的聲浪日益高漲。
根據目前公開資訊，多位美國跨黨派國會議員已針對此事件採取行動。參議院銀行委員會共和黨籍成員凱文·克拉默（Kevin Cramer）率先公開呼籲，基於個人良知與道德責任，黃仁勳應主動辭職。克拉默強調，鑑於該名女性被情報機構指控為中國間諜，黃仁勳與其往來恐導致敏感技術外洩，對美國科技主權構成嚴重威脅。國會內部目前正研擬動用傳票權，計畫強制要求黃仁勳出席聽證會，針對其與該名女性的往來細節、是否涉及公司機密洩露，以及輝達內部對於高層安全控管的機制進行全面調查。
然而，目前各方資訊仍存在部分差異與待釐清之處。儘管黃仁勳已承認婚外情關係，但針對該女性是否確為中國間諜，以及黃仁勳在交往過程中是否知曉其身份、是否有任何技術資料透過此管道外流，目前尚未有官方調查報告給予定論。報導中提到的「黃樂」身份細節，以及其與中國情報單位的具體連結，目前多數僅見於國會議員的公開指控與媒體引述之情報機構認定，尚未進入司法程式或公開聽證階段，因此尚待後續調查結果確認是否構成實質的法律責任或國安違規。
此事件對全球科技產業與地緣政治局勢已產生具體影響。作為AI晶片市場的壟斷性企業，輝達的穩定性直接關乎全球數位基礎設施的安全。若黃仁勳被迫辭職或輝達面臨美國國會的嚴格監管，恐將引發全球科技供應鏈的劇烈震盪，並可能影響各國對於AI技術採購的政策方向。此外，這起事件也加劇了美中在科技領域的對抗氛圍，未來跨國科技企業的高層管理人員，恐將面臨更為嚴格的背景審查與個人行為規範，以防範類似的國安風險再次發生。
需要強調的是，雖然國會議員的呼籲聲浪強烈，但這屬於政治性施壓，並不等同於法律判決。目前不應過度解讀此事件將立即導致輝達的營運停擺，或黃仁勳必然會面臨刑事指控。各界應區分「道德層面的辭職呼籲」與「法律層面的國安調查」兩者之差異。此外，針對「中國間諜」的指控，目前仍處於情報分析與政治攻防階段，在法庭證據確鑿前，應保持中立態度，避免將單一政治人物的言論視為最終裁定，並持續關注輝達官方與美國司法部的後續回應。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個面向：首先是黃仁勳是否會出席國會聽證會，以及其在聽證會上的證詞內容；其次是美國政府是否會針對輝達啟動更深度的國安審查，甚至限制其特定產品的出口或經營權；最後則是輝達董事會是否會因應輿論壓力，對執行長職位做出調整。這場風暴不僅是輝達的經營危機，更是全球科技產業在美中地緣政治夾縫中，如何處理高層人員安全與企業透明度的重要指標。
