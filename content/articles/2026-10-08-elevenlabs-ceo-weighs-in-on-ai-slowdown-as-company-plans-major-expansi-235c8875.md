人工智慧語音技術新創公司ElevenLabs共同創辦人兼執行長Mati Staniszewski近期針對當前業界的「AI放緩」爭議發表看法。據相關外媒報導，Staniszewski主張在確保安全的前提下持續推進與部署人工智慧，反對在缺乏具體安全隱患的情況下全面暫停技術開發。相關討論也反映出科技產業對AI研發步伐與風險控管之間權衡的關注。

在此背景下，路透社報導指出，這家總部位於倫敦的語音AI企業正積極布局南亞市場，執行長向路透社透露，公司計劃在印度投資「數億美元」。報導進一步提及，印度豐富的語言多樣性正在深刻影響ElevenLabs語音模型的研發路線圖，該市場的多元語言挑戰為其技術研發提供了重要測試場域，公司亦正著眼於建立一支約百人的團隊。

此外，根據《The Times of India》等外媒早報資訊，除ElevenLabs的發展動向外，全球投資機構Prosus也正擴大其金融科技布局，據報正探索投資Kiwi及Oolka等印度金融科技新創企業。相關動態顯示跨國科技企業與創投資本目前正將印度視為核心拓展板塊。

## English edition

Mati Staniszewski, co-founder and CEO of AI voice technology company ElevenLabs, has weighed in on the ongoing debate regarding an artificial intelligence slowdown. According to industry reports, Staniszewski advocates for the continued deployment of AI where it remains safe, opposing a general halt to AI progress unless specific safety concerns arise. The remarks highlight continuing industry discourse over the speed of innovation versus risk management.

Amid these developments, Reuters reported that the London-based AI voice firm plans to invest "hundreds of millions of dollars" in India, Staniszewski confirmed. The country's linguistic diversity is increasingly shaping the development of ElevenLabs' voice AI models, presenting unique challenges that inform its technical roadmap. Additional reports indicate the company is eyeing a 100-member team in the region.

Alongside ElevenLabs' strategic pivot toward India, global investor Prosus has also been dialing up its fintech bets. Reports indicate Prosus is exploring investments in Indian fintech startups including Kiwi and Oolka, reflecting sustained international corporate and venture interest across India's technology sectors.
