  # Global Trade Turmoil: Analysis of U.S.-China Negotiation Progress and Geopolitical Power Struggles in Oceania
  
  > The global economy has recently been affected by structural shifts in U.S.-China trade and progress in negotiations. Meanwhile, the Oceania region is experiencing divergence in regional security and diplomatic stances due to geopolitical maneuvering, with various nations actively adjusting their response strategies.
  
  The global economic and trade landscape has undergone significant changes recently, as the competition between the U.S. and China—the world's two largest economies—in trade, energy, and critical resources enters a new phase. According to public data, the U.S.-China trade deficit has shrunk significantly from its 2018 peak of $418 billion to $203 billion in 2025, a decrease of 52%. This structural shift indicates that the focus of U.S. trade imbalances has gradually shifted toward Mexico and other Asian economies.
  
  Meanwhile, U.S. Trade Representative Katherine Tai confirmed on September 25, 2026, that the U.S. and China have reached an agreement on more favorable trade terms for certain goods. The details are expected to be officially announced on September 28, a move viewed as significant progress in bilateral trade negotiations.
  
  In capital markets, the U.S. stock market has demonstrated immense power in attracting capital. According to an *Axios* report, the total amount of foreign capital flowing into the U.S. in the second quarter of 2026 exceeded $1 trillion, with $426 billion invested in stocks and investment funds, marking a new high since the first quarter of 2020. Market analysts generally believe that this surge in capital is primarily driven by the booming artificial intelligence (AI) industry, which has successfully offset concerns regarding the waning attractiveness of the U.S. market.
  
  However, the chain reaction of the trade war has also placed pressure on regional economies. The Ministry of Finance of Ontario, Canada, has issued a public warning that, due to tariffs and barriers triggered by U.S. trade disputes, the province will be forced to continue adopting deficit spending policies to maintain economic stability.
  
  The geopolitical situation in the Oceania region presents a complex power struggle. According to a *Paral* report, during the Pacific Islands Forum (PIF) foreign ministers' meeting held in Fiji in August 2026, a consensus to issue a joint condemnation regarding China's ballistic missile test in July could not be reached, primarily due to the joint obstruction by Nauru and Kiribati. New Zealand Foreign Minister Winston Peters hinted that China's infiltration of regional influence has interfered with the collective diplomatic decision-making of Pacific Island nations.
  
  This incident highlights that Pacific Island nations do not hold a unified stance when facing competition between major powers, with some countries leaning toward maintaining cooperative relations with China in their diplomatic and economic choices.
  
  This geopolitical uncertainty directly impacts the strategic layout of countries like Australia in the Pacific region. The Australian government has long been committed to expanding its influence among Pacific Island nations to address the increasingly intense international competition in the region. According to data from the INN Interstellar Federation News Network, Australia signed a security agreement with Vanuatu in December 2022, aiming to strengthen bilateral cooperation and consolidate its regional security perimeter. However, as the confrontation between the U.S. and China over rare earth elements, oil supplies, and trade rules intensifies, Oceania island nations face even more severe pressure to choose sides while seeking economic development and maintaining national security.
  
  There is a clear discrepancy in how various parties interpret the current situation. The U.S. perspective tends to emphasize the progress of trade negotiations and market resilience, viewing capital inflows as a vote of confidence in the U.S. economic outlook; however, the side effects of trade protectionism have already sparked concerns over fiscal deficits in countries like Canada. Furthermore, the divisions within the Pacific Islands Forum serve as a reminder to the international community not to oversimplify the China policies of Oceania nations. Countries have different priorities between security agreements and economic aid, and the view that the Pacific region is a single diplomatic bloc clearly fails to explain the current diplomatic impasse.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on the disclosure of the details of the U.S.-China trade negotiations on September 28, and whether these agreements will further affect the reallocation of global supply chains. At the same time, whether Oceania nations can restore consensus in upcoming international forums when facing regional security issues such as China's ballistic missile tests, or whether they will remain divided, will be a key indicator for assessing regional geopolitical stability. Additionally, it is worth tracking whether Ontario's deficit policy will trigger further fiscal chain reactions in the North American region.

# 全球貿易局勢動盪：美中談判進展與大洋洲地緣博弈分析

> 近期全球經濟受美中貿易結構性轉變與談判進展影響，大洋洲地區則因地緣政治角力，在區域安全與外交立場上呈現分歧，各國正積極調整因應策略。


全球經濟與貿易格局近期出現顯著變動，美中兩大經濟體在貿易、能源及關鍵資源上的競爭進入新階段。根據公開資料顯示，美中貿易逆差已從 2018 年高峰時的 4,180 億美元，大幅縮減至 2025 年的 2,030 億美元，降幅達 52%。此結構性轉變顯示美國貿易失衡重心已逐漸轉移至墨西哥及其他亞洲經濟體。
與此同時，美國貿易代表葛裡爾（Katherine Tai）於 2026 年 9 月 25 日對外證實，美中雙方已就部分商品達成更有利的貿易條件協議，詳細內容預計於 9 月 28 日正式公佈，此舉被視為雙邊貿易談判的重要進展。
在資本市場方面，美國股市展現極強的吸金力道。據《Axios》報導，2026 年第二季流入美國的外資總額超過 1 兆美元，其中外資投入股票及投資基金的金額高達 4,260 億美元，創下自 2020 年第一季以來的新高紀錄。市場分析普遍認為，此波資金熱潮主要受惠於人工智慧（AI）產業的蓬勃發展，成功抵銷了外界對於美國市場吸引力減弱的擔憂。
然而，貿易戰的連鎖反應亦對區域經濟造成壓力，加拿大安大略省財政部門即公開預警，受美國貿易爭端引發的關稅與壁壘影響，該省將被迫持續採取赤字支出政策以維持經濟穩定。
大洋洲地區的地緣政治局勢則呈現複雜的角力態勢。根據《Paral》報導，2026 年 8 月，太平洋島國論壇（PIF）外長會議在斐濟召開期間，針對中國七月進行的彈道飛彈試射議題，未能達成發布聯合譴責宣告的共識，主因在於諾魯與吉里巴斯兩國聯手封阻。紐西蘭外長溫斯頓·彼得斯（Winston Peters）對此暗示，中國在區域內的影響力滲透已對太平洋島國的集體外交決策造成幹擾。
此事件凸顯了太平洋島國在面對大國競爭時，內部立場並不一致，部分國家在外交與經濟選擇上更傾向維持與中國的合作關係。
這種地緣政治的不確定性，直接影響了澳洲等國在太平洋區域的戰略佈局。澳洲政府長期致力於擴大在太平洋島國的影響力，以因應區域內日益激烈的國際競爭。根據 INN 星際聯邦官方新聞網資料，澳洲於 2022 年 12 月與萬那杜簽署安全協議，旨在強化雙邊合作，鞏固其區域安全防線。然而，隨著美中在稀土爭奪、石油供應及貿易規則上的對抗升溫，大洋洲島國在尋求經濟發展與維護國家安全之間，面臨更為嚴峻的選邊站壓力。
各方觀點對於當前局勢的解讀存在明顯落差。美方觀點傾向強調貿易談判的進展與市場韌性，將資金流入視為對美國經濟前景的信心投票；然而，貿易保護主義的副作用已在加拿大等國引發財政赤字隱憂。此外，太平洋島國論壇內部的分歧，也提醒國際社會不應過度簡化大洋洲國家對華政策。各國在安全協議與經濟援助之間有著不同的優先順序，將太平洋地區視為單一外交陣營的觀點，顯然已無法解釋當前的外交僵局。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於 9 月 28 日美中貿易談判細節的揭露，以及這些協議是否會進一步影響全球供應鏈的重新分配。同時，大洋洲各國在面對中國彈道飛彈試射等區域安全議題時，能否在即將到來的國際論壇中修復共識，或將持續分裂，將成為檢視區域地緣政治穩定性的關鍵指標。此外，安大略省的赤字政策是否會引發更多北美地區的財政連鎖反應，亦值得持續追蹤。
