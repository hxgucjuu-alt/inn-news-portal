  # Climate Change and Energy Transition: APEC Best Practice Awards Announced and Regional Disaster Prevention Strategies
  
  > The Energy Administration of the Ministry of Economic Affairs hosted the APEC Energy Smart Communities Initiative workshop to recognize Taiwan's energy innovation cases; meanwhile, the New Taipei City Government responded to the International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction by promoting afforestation to strengthen natural carbon sinks and disaster resilience.
  
  Against the backdrop of intensifying global climate change and the urgent need for energy transition, international cooperation and local ecological governance have become a dual-track strategy for addressing these challenges. From October 20 to 21, 2026, the Energy Administration of Taiwan’s Ministry of Economic Affairs hosted the "2026 APEC Energy Smart Communities Initiative (ESCI) Best Practices Awards and Energy Solutions Workshop" in Taipei. This event aimed to address the growing electricity demand driven by the development of artificial intelligence (AI) and digital technology, while facilitating technical exchanges on global energy transition and the risks posed by extreme weather.
  
  Simultaneously, around the time of the United Nations' "International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction" on October 13, local governments in Taiwan took concrete actions regarding forest fire prevention and the enhancement of natural carbon sinks, emphasizing the construction of a more resilient environmental foundation through afforestation and public participation.
  
  According to a report by Technews.tw, this APEC Energy Smart Communities Initiative workshop brought together representatives from various APEC economies, including the United States, Japan, Thailand, and Vietnam. The core topics of the meeting focused on the construction of smart grids, the application of energy-saving technologies, and the development of low-carbon energy. Taiwan demonstrated significant energy innovation achievements at this meeting, with two cases winning APEC Best Practice Awards. These awards not only symbolize international recognition of Taiwan's technical prowess on the path of energy transition but also reflect that in a digital era where energy supply and demand are increasingly strained, regional technical sharing and policy coordination are key pathways to enhancing overall energy resilience.
  
  At the level of local governance, the Agriculture Department of the New Taipei City Government has focused on the protection and enhancement of forest ecosystems. According to a report by Yahoo Entertainment, the New Taipei City Government took the opportunity of the "International Day for Disaster Risk Reduction" to urge the public to pay attention to the risk of forest fires during the dry season. Forestry statistics indicate that up to 97% of forest fires are caused by human factors, especially during the autumn and winter seasons, when the northeast monsoon can easily cause small sparks in areas where dead branches and leaves have accumulated to spread, subsequently triggering large-scale disasters.
  
  To this end, the New Taipei City Government has adopted a "prevention through planting" strategy, providing free saplings and encouraging citizens to participate in tree planting. This is not only intended to strengthen soil and water conservation and prevent erosion, but also aims to mitigate the impact of global climate change on the local microclimate by increasing natural carbon sinks.
  
  There is currently a high degree of consensus across all sectors regarding climate governance: energy transition and ecological protection must proceed simultaneously. However, there are differences in the measures taken to address climate challenges in different regions. The APEC workshop focuses on energy intelligence at the technical level, emphasizing the resolution of energy supply and demand contradictions through digital governance and infrastructure upgrades; meanwhile, local government tree-planting initiatives focus on ecosystem self-repair and disaster prevention. Although the two differ in execution levels and technical means, their ultimate goals are both directed toward enhancing social adaptability to climate change.
  
  What remains to be confirmed is how these fragmented technical innovations and local tree-planting actions can be more effectively integrated into a comprehensive national climate resilience strategy in the future, particularly regarding resource allocation and inter-ministerial coordination, which still require further evaluation.
  
  From the perspective of industry and public policy impact, energy transition has become a core engine for driving economic development and environmental sustainability. As the high energy-consumption characteristics of the AI and digital industries become increasingly prominent, the smart grid and energy-saving programs promoted by the Energy Administration will directly affect the electricity costs and energy efficiency of related industries in the future. On the other hand, the afforestation and fire prevention advocacy promoted by New Taipei City embody the integration of public safety and ecological conservation, which has direct benefits for reducing the depletion of disaster relief resources.
  
  However, all parties must remain cautious and should not over-interpret the immediate contribution of a single tree-planting activity to global carbon neutrality. The effectiveness of tree planting requires long-term monitoring, and the practical application of energy technologies must also consider the infrastructure differences between different economies to avoid creating an energy divide between regions due to technical thresholds.
  
  Looking ahead, the focus for subsequent observation should be on the progress of technology transfer among APEC economies and the effectiveness of the promotion and application of Taiwan's award-winning cases internationally. Regarding local disaster prevention, the survival rate of the saplings provided by the New Taipei City Government and their actual contribution to local ecosystem carbon sinks will be key indicators for evaluating the long-term benefits of this policy. Furthermore, as the frequency of extreme weather caused by climate change increases, how to more closely link the seemingly independent issues of "smart energy" and "forest disaster prevention" in policy planning will be an important subject for future climate adaptation policies. All sectors should continue to monitor the implementation data of relevant policies and evaluate their resilience under different climate scenarios.

# 氣候變遷與能源轉型：APEC 最佳案例獎揭曉與區域防災策略

> 經濟部能源署舉辦 APEC 能源智慧社群倡議研討會，表彰臺灣能源創新案例；同時，新北市政府響應國際減災日，推動植樹造林以強化自然碳匯及防災韌性。


在面對全球氣候變遷加劇與能源轉型迫切需求的背景下，國際間的合作與地方性的生態治理成為應對挑戰的雙軌策略。2026 年 10 月 20 日至 21 日，臺灣經濟部能源署於臺北舉辦「2026 年 APEC 能源智慧社群倡議（ESCI）最佳案例獎暨能源解決方案研討會」。此活動旨在因應人工智慧（AI）與數位科技發展所帶來的用電需求增長，並針對全球能源轉型與極端氣候帶來的風險進行技術交流。
與此同時，臺灣地方政府亦於 10 月 13 日聯合國「國際減災日」前後，針對森林火災防治與自然碳匯提升採取具體行動，強調透過植樹造林與公眾參與，構建更具韌性的環境基礎。
根據 Technews.tw 的報導，本次 APEC 能源智慧社群倡議研討會匯集了來自美國、日本、泰國及越南等多個 APEC 經濟體的代表。會議核心議題聚焦於智慧電網的建置、節能技術的應用以及低碳能源的開發。臺灣在本次會議中展現了顯著的能源創新成果，共有兩項案例獲得 APEC 最佳案例獎。這些案例的獲獎不僅象徵臺灣在能源轉型路徑上的技術實力獲得國際肯定，也反映了在能源供需日益緊張的數位時代，透過區域間的技術共享與政策協調，是提升整體能源韌性的關鍵路徑。
在地方治理層面，新北市政府農業局則將焦點置於森林生態的保護與增進。根據 Yahoo Entertainment 的報導，新北市政府藉由「國際減災日」的契機，呼籲民眾重視乾燥季節的森林火災風險。林業統計資料顯示，高達 97% 的森林火災源於人為因素，特別是在秋冬季節，東北季風易使枯枝落葉堆積處的微小火星擴散，進而引發大面積災情。
為此，新北市政府採取了「以植代防」的策略，免費提供造林苗木，鼓勵市民參與植樹，不僅是為了強化水土保持與防砂固土，更期望透過增加自然碳匯，緩解全球氣候變遷對在地微氣候的衝擊。
目前各界對於氣候治理的認知已達成高度共識，即能源轉型與生態保護必須同步進行。然而，針對不同區域的氣候挑戰，各界採取的應對手段存在差異。APEC 的研討會側重於技術層面的能源智慧化，強調透過數位治理與基礎設施升級來解決能源供需矛盾；而地方政府的植樹行動則側重於生態系統的自我修復與災害預防。儘管兩者在執行層級與技術手段上有所區別，但其最終目標皆指向提升社會對氣候變遷的適應能力。
目前尚待確認的是，這些分散的技術創新與地方植樹行動，在未來如何能更有效地整合為一套完整的國家級氣候韌性策略，特別是在資源分配與跨部會協調方面，仍有待進一步的評估。
從產業與公共政策的影響來看，能源轉型已成為推動經濟發展與環境永續的核心引擎。隨著 AI 與數位產業的高耗能特性日益顯著，能源署推動的智慧電網與節能方案，將直接影響未來相關產業的用電成本與能源效率。另一方面，新北市推動的植樹造林與防火宣導，則體現了公共安全與生態保育的結合，對於減少救災資源的耗損具有直接效益。
然而，各界亦需保持審慎，不應過度解讀單一植樹活動對全球碳中和的立即性貢獻，植樹的成效需長期監測，且能源技術的落地應用亦需考量不同經濟體之間的基礎建設差異，避免因技術門檻而造成區域間的能源鴻溝。
展望未來，後續觀察重點應放在 APEC 經濟體間的技術轉移進度，以及臺灣獲獎案例在國際間的推廣應用成效。在地方防災方面，新北市政府提供的苗木存活率及其對當地生態系統碳匯的實際貢獻，將是評估該政策長期效益的關鍵指標。此外，隨著氣候變遷帶來的極端天氣頻率增加，如何將「智慧能源」與「森林防災」這兩項看似獨立的議題，在政策規劃中進行更緊密的連結，將是未來氣候調適政策的重要課題。各界應持續關注相關政策的執行資料，並評估其在不同氣候情境下的應變彈性。
