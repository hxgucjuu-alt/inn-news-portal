根據報導，西雅圖海鷹隊的新老闆維諾德·科斯拉及其所有權集團，在以創紀錄的96.12億美元收購該球隊後，其公開形象和媒體互動成為焦點。NFL老闆們在三天前才批准了這項收購案，但科斯拉已透過其社群媒體展現出獨特的公開存在感。

多家媒體指出，科斯拉似乎迫切需要一位優秀的公關人員。報導稱，他與體育界的溝通方式，與他過去在科技界的溝通模式有所不同，這導致了一些誤解或爭議。

例如，有報導提到科斯拉曾因承認自己是鋼人隊球迷而「回擊媒體」，這成為一個有趣的引述和討論話題。他的坦率線上存在感被視為球隊的一個獨特變化，但也引發了關於其溝通策略的討論。

儘管這些互動為媒體提供了話題，但資料顯示，海鷹隊球迷不應過於擔心球隊未來的表現。然而，科斯拉在體育界與媒體打交道的方式，與他過去的領域有所不同，這凸顯了專業公關在管理公眾形象方面的重要性。

## English edition

According to reports, Seattle Seahawks' new owner, Vinod Khosla, and his ownership group have become a focal point for their public image and media interactions after acquiring the team for a record $9.612 billion. NFL owners had approved the purchase just three days prior, but Khosla has already demonstrated a unique candid online presence via social media.

Multiple media outlets suggest that Khosla appears to be in desperate need of a good PR person. Reports indicate that his communication style with the sports world differs from his past approach in the tech world, leading to some misunderstandings or controversies.

For instance, reports mention Khosla "snapping back at media over Steelers fandom admission," which became a notable pull-quote and aggregation topic. His candid online presence is seen as a unique change for the franchise, but it has also sparked discussions regarding his communication strategy.

While these interactions have provided fodder for the media, data suggests that Seahawks fans should not be overly concerned about the team's future performance. Nevertheless, Khosla's approach to dealing with media in the sports world, distinct from his previous field, underscores the importance of professional public relations in managing public image.
