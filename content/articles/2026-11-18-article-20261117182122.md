  # Global AI Industry in Turmoil: Jensen Huang’s Departure from Nvidia and Deepfake Scams Shatter Trust
  
  > The global technology sector is currently in upheaval. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s resignation amid scandal has shocked the market, while AI-driven deepfake scams have caused massive financial losses. Simultaneously, the publishing industry is gripped by an "AI witch hunt" anxiety. These multiple crises have triggered a profound global reflection on AI ethics and industry trust.
  
  The global artificial intelligence (AI) sector is currently facing an unprecedented crisis of trust and governance. Since mid-November 2026, the negative effects of AI technology have been impacting societal operations across the board, ranging from high-level personnel changes and transnational fraud to controversies over creative ethics in the publishing industry. The most high-profile event was the sudden resignation of Nvidia founder Jensen Huang on November 12, ending his 33-year tenure as president and CEO. Simultaneously, the U.S. Department of Justice unsealed a major criminal case involving the use of AI deepfake technology to target large global institutions over a two-year period, resulting in a total of $129 million (approximately NT$4.1 billion) in fraudulent losses.
  
  Furthermore, the "witch hunt" trend in the publishing industry triggered by erroneous AI detection tools, along with Microsoft’s recent global system outage, has highlighted the fragility of current digital infrastructure and content authentication.
  
  Multiple sources have confirmed that Huang’s departure is linked to a personal conduct scandal involving Chinese intelligence personnel. This is not merely an internal corporate management change but is viewed as a watershed moment for the development of the AI industry. Tech media outlets have pointed out that the frenzy driven by the chip arms race and computing power expansion over the past three years is now facing a cost backlash, forcing the industry to shift toward evaluating practical value. On the criminal front, the U.S. Department of Justice and the U.S. Attorney’s Office for the Southern District of New York confirmed that three foreign prime suspects have been arrested in Europe; they had executed social engineering scams by using deepfakes of CEOs and lawyers.
  
  In the publishing industry, the Hachette Book Group removed the novel "Shy Girl" from shelves under public pressure after the AI detection firm Pangram alleged that 78% of the text was machine-generated. This demonstrates that, in the absence of standards, AI detection tools have become a threat to the rights of creators.
  
  Although various media outlets have reported on these events, interpretations regarding their impact vary. Regarding the aftermath of Huang’s departure, some tech media emphasized that this would accelerate the "formal decoupling" of U.S. and Chinese technology, elevating a single corporate personnel matter to a geopolitical level. However, other analyses suggest that the core of this event lies in the economic return to rationality, as the industry shifts from blind expansion to cost control. Additionally, regarding the global Microsoft outage, although it affected over 70 countries, Microsoft officially denied that the failure was related to AI systems or cyberattacks, emphasizing that it was caused by technical operations to expand network capacity, in an attempt to distance itself from the recent turmoil in the AI industry.
  
  This information indicates a cognitive gap between public panic regarding AI technology and actual technical failures.
  
  These events have had concrete impacts on global industries and society. In terms of corporate governance, the leadership change at Nvidia signals that the chip industry is entering a period of strict scrutiny and adjustment. In terms of financial security, the weaponization of AI deepfake technology has forced large global institutions to re-examine the security of video conferencing and identity verification to guard against highly realistic fraud. The publishing industry is facing a severe crisis of trust, with authors forced to adopt extreme measures, such as recording videos of themselves to prove the authenticity of their work, reflecting the serious lack of digital content authentication mechanisms in the AI era. These shocks are not limited to the tech circle but directly affect the operational stability of global government agencies, financial institutions, and the education and cultural sectors.
  
  However, experts warn against over-interpreting individual events. Regarding the accuracy of AI detection tools, there is currently no unified international technical standard; equating "detection results" directly with "plagiarism" or "non-human creation" carries a high risk of misjudgment, and excessive witch-hunting could stifle the diversity of literary creation. Similarly, attributing all technological failures or geopolitical fluctuations entirely to the development of AI technology may obscure the importance of traditional corporate management and infrastructure maintenance. The public should be vigilant about the risks posed by AI technology, but also needs to distinguish the boundary between "technology abuse" and "the technology itself," avoiding the neglect of the necessity of digital transformation in the midst of panic.
  
  Key areas for future observation include: first, the adjustment of the global AI chip supply chain and U.S.-China technology policy following Nvidia's loss of its long-term leader; second, whether transnational judicial assistance mechanisms for AI deepfake crimes will accelerate legislation among various governments; and finally, whether the publishing and technology sectors can establish a credible set of ethical standards for AI creation and detection. These developments will determine whether AI technology will continue to be a source of social unrest in the coming years or if it can return to its role of assisting humanity through governance frameworks.

# 全球AI產業震盪：輝達黃仁勳去職與深偽詐騙重創信任

> 近期全球科技界動盪，輝達執行長黃仁勳因醜聞辭職震撼市場，AI深偽詐騙造成鉅額損失，出版界亦陷入AI獵巫焦慮，多重危機引發全球對AI技術倫理與產業信任的深層反思。


近期全球人工智慧（AI）領域面臨前所未有的信任與治理危機。自2026年11月中旬以來，從高層人事異動、跨國詐騙犯罪到出版界的創作倫理爭議，AI技術的負面效應正全面衝擊社會運作。最受矚目的事件為輝達（Nvidia）創辦人黃仁勳於11月12日閃電辭去總裁兼執行長職務，結束長達33年的領導生涯。與此同時，美國司法部解密了一起利用AI深偽（Deepfake）技術，針對全球大型機構進行長達兩年、詐騙總額達1.29億美元（約新臺幣41億元）的重大犯罪案件。
此外，出版界因AI檢測工具誤判引發的「獵巫」風潮，以及微軟近期發生的全球性系統當機，均凸顯了當前數位基礎設施與內容認證的脆弱性。
多項來源共同確認，黃仁勳的去職與其捲入涉及中國情報人員的私德風波有關，這不僅是企業內部的管理變動，更被視為AI產業發展的分水嶺。科技媒體指出，過去三年由晶片軍備競賽與算力擴建推動的狂熱模式已面臨成本反噬，產業界正被迫轉向實用價值評估。在犯罪層面，美國司法部與紐約南區聯邦檢察官辦公室證實，三名外籍主嫌已在歐洲落網，他們透過深度偽造執行長與律師的影音進行社交工程詐騙。
而在出版界，阿歇特圖書集團因AI檢測公司Pangram指稱小說《害羞女孩》有78%文字為機器生成，迫於輿論壓力將其下架，顯示出AI檢測工具在缺乏標準下，已對創作者權益構成威脅。
儘管多方媒體均報導了上述事件，但針對影響層面的解讀存在差異。關於黃仁勳去職的後續影響，部分科技媒體強調這將加速美中科技的「正式脫鉤」，將單一企業人事案提升至地緣政治高度；然而，亦有分析認為此事件的核心在於產業從盲目擴張轉向成本控管的經濟理性回歸。此外，針對微軟全球大當機事件，儘管波及70多個國家，微軟官方明確否認該故障與AI系統或網路攻擊有關，強調是擴充網路容量的技術作業所致，試圖與近期AI產業的動盪進行切割。
這些資訊顯示，公眾對AI技術的恐慌與實際技術故障間，存在著認知上的落差。
這些事件對全球產業與社會產生了具體影響。在企業治理方面，輝達的領導層更迭預示著晶片產業將進入嚴格審查與調整期；在金融安全方面，AI深偽技術的武器化迫使全球大型機構必須重新審視視訊會議與身份驗證的安全性，以防範高擬真的詐騙手段。出版產業則面臨嚴峻的信任危機，作家被迫採取錄影自清等極端手段證明創作真實性，反映出數位內容認證機制在AI時代的嚴重缺失。這些衝擊不僅限於科技圈，更直接影響了全球政府機關、金融機構及教育文化產業的運作穩定性。
然而，專家提醒不應過度解讀單一事件。針對AI檢測工具的準確度，目前並無國際統一的技術標準，將「檢測結果」直接等同於「抄襲」或「非真人創作」存在極高誤判風險，過度的獵巫行為可能扼殺文學創作的多元性。同樣地，將所有科技故障或地緣政治波動全數歸因於AI技術的發展，亦可能模糊了傳統企業管理與基礎設施維護的重要性。公眾應警惕AI技術帶來的風險，但也需區分「技術濫用」與「技術本身」的界線，避免在恐慌中忽視了數位轉型的必要性。
後續觀察重點在於：首先，輝達在失去長期領導者後，其全球AI晶片供應鏈與美中科技政策的調整走向；其次，各國政府針對AI深偽犯罪的跨國司法互助機制是否會加速立法；最後，出版界與科技界是否能針對AI創作與檢測建立一套具備公信力的倫理規範。這些發展將決定AI技術在未來幾年內，是持續作為社會動盪的根源，還是能透過治理框架回歸輔助人類的角色。
