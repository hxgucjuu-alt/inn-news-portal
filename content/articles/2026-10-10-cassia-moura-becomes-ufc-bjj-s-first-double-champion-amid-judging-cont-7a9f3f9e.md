在於內華達州舉行的 UFC BJJ 12 賽事中，卡西亞·莫拉（Cassia Moura）在錦標賽決賽中以分歧判定擊敗阿黛爾·福納里諾（Adele Fornarino），奪得首屆 UFC BJJ 蠅量級冠軍頭銜。這場勝利使莫拉同時擁有雛量級與蠅量級腰帶，正式成為該賽事歷史上首位雙級別冠軍。

根據體育媒體報導，這場高水準的無道袍降伏式摔跤對決節奏緊湊且極具技術性。賽制採用綜合格鬥常見的三回合、每回合五分鐘架構，由裁判進行計分。福納里諾在首回合曾以足跟絞（heel hook）施加降伏威脅，莫拉則在第二回合展開反擊，成功完成摔技並取得背部控制（back control）。此外，賽事同場亦傳出伯恩斯（Burns）遭到降伏。

儘管比賽過程精采，裁決結果公布後卻隨即引發爭議。報導指出，兩位選手在各自壓制前幾輪對手後會師決賽，雙方表現旗鼓相當，但裁判的計分卡與判決標準在賽後立刻引起現場與業界討論。這場勝利雖讓莫拉載入賽事史冊，卻也促使各界再度聚焦於降伏式格鬥賽事中採用裁判計分機制的評分標準問題。

## English edition

At UFC BJJ 12, Cassia Moura defeated Adele Fornarino via split decision in the tournament finals to claim the inaugural UFC BJJ flyweight championship. With this victory, Moura, who already held the bantamweight title, became the promotion's first two-division champion.

Media reports indicate that the highly anticipated no-gi submission grappling match featured high-level technical exchanges. The promotion's championship bout utilized a familiar MMA time structure of three five-minute rounds evaluated by judges. Fornarino threatened a finish with a deep heel hook in the opening round, while Moura rebounded in the second round by securing a takedown and establishing back control. Elsewhere on the card, Burns was also reported to have been submitted.

Despite the intense action between the two elite grapplers, the split decision outcome sparked immediate controversy regarding judges' scorecards. Analysts and observers noted that the closely contested matchup reignited discussions over the criteria and mechanics of judging in professional submission grappling events.
