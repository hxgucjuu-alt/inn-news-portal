根據迪士尼相關消息與多家主題樂園主題媒體報導，迪士尼近期公開了即將在華特迪士尼世界度假區、加州迪士尼樂園度假區以及線上平台「Disney Store」推出的節慶限定周邊商品陣容。此次提前曝光旨在配合年中舉辦的「距離節慶過半」（Halfway to the Holidays）宣傳活動，提前炒熱年末氣氛。

報導指出，本次公布的節慶商品系列種類豐富，包括舒適服飾、具收藏價值的掛飾、節慶隨身配件以及居家裝飾品。此外，相關消息也預告了專門為迪士尼世界聖誕派對活動所設計的獨家限定紀念商品，吸引許多喜愛樂園購物的遊客關注。

資料顯示，華特迪士尼世界的年度節慶季涵蓋了多項大型活動，包括「米奇的歡樂聖誕派對」（Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party）、「艾波卡特國際節慶藝術節」（EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays）以及「Jollywood Nights」等。迪士尼透過提前釋出周邊商品訊息，持續帶動節慶旅遊與消費市場的討論熱度。

## English edition

Disney and theme park news outlets have shared an advance preview of upcoming holiday merchandise scheduled to arrive at Walt Disney World Resort, Disneyland Resort, and the official Disney Store later this year. The reveal coincides with mid-year promotional efforts, such as the "Halfway to the Holidays" announcements, designed to build anticipation ahead of the winter holiday season.

Reports indicate that the previewed collection encompasses a wide variety of items, ranging from cozy seasonal apparel and collectible ornaments to festive accessories and home décor. Disney has also teased exclusive merchandise specifically curated for its popular Christmas parties at Walt Disney World, targeting visitors and collectors alike.

Documentation highlights that the upcoming holiday period across Walt Disney World will feature staple seasonal offerings, such as Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party, the EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays, and Jollywood Nights. By offering early glimpses of park-exclusive souvenirs, Disney continues to drive consumer interest and engagement for its end-of-year seasonal events.
