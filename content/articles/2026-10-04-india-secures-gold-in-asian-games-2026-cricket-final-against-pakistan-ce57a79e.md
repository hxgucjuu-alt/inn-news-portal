印度於2026年10月3日在日本日進舉行的亞洲運動會男子板球決賽中，以19分之差擊敗巴基斯坦，成功奪得金牌。這場備受矚目的金牌戰，根據ESPN和Cricbuzz的報導，印度隊最終以211/6的成績領先巴基斯坦的192/6（20局），贏得了勝利。

這場在日進舉行的賽事吸引了全球目光，特別是印度與巴基斯坦之間的對決，向來是板球界的一大焦點。Sporting News指出，賽前各界高度關注印度新星瓦伊巴夫·蘇里亞萬希（Vaibhav Sooryavanshi）的表現。

根據News18的報導，瓦伊巴夫·蘇里亞萬希在這場決賽中為印度隊開局，並在9個球中貢獻了15分。儘管他被Sporting News稱為「神童」，但這場勝利是全隊努力的成果，最終幫助印度隊在亞洲運動會上贏得了這枚重要的金牌。

亞洲運動會男子板球賽事是區域內體育盛事之一，各國頂尖隊伍齊聚一堂爭奪榮譽。印度隊此次的勝利不僅為國家增添了一枚金牌，也再次證明了其在國際板球舞台上的強勁實力。

## English edition

India clinched the gold medal in the Men's Cricket Final at the Asian Games 2026, defeating arch-rivals Pakistan by 19 runs. The highly anticipated gold medal match, held in Nisshin, Japan, on October 3, saw India post a score of 211/6 against Pakistan's 192/6 (20 overs), as reported by ESPN and Cricbuzz.

The final, a significant event in the Asian Games, drew considerable attention, especially given the historic rivalry between India and Pakistan in cricket. Sporting News highlighted the anticipation surrounding the performance of India's young talent, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, prior to the match.

According to News18, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi opened the innings for India in the final, contributing 15 runs off nine balls. While referred to as a "prodigy" by Sporting News, the victory was a collective effort that ultimately secured India's triumph and the coveted gold medal at the Asian Games.

The Asian Games men's cricket competition serves as a major regional sporting spectacle, bringing together top teams to compete for honors. India's victory not only added another gold medal to the nation's tally but also reaffirmed its strong presence on the international cricket stage.
