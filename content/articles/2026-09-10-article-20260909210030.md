  # Social Safety Nets and Protection for the Vulnerable: Exploring Issues from Child Protection to Elderly Care for the Disabled
  
  > The construction of social safety nets and the protection of vulnerable groups have long been core issues in public policy. Recent events in Taiwanese society have drawn significant attention, including advocacy for child protection policies in Kaohsiung City, the long-term elderly care dilemmas faced by families of individuals with intellectual disabilities, and controversies regarding social perception and privilege sparked by the behavior of elected representatives in medical settings. These incidents reflect that social safety nets encompass not only government policy-making but also the equitable use of medical resources, the improvement of working environments for social workers, and the practice of social responsibility by public figures. As we examine these issues, we must look beyond the tragedies behind the data and explore how systems can shift from passive remediation to proactive prevention.
  
  According to relevant media reports, Kuomintang Kaohsiung mayoral candidate Ko Chih-en proposed a child protection policy on September 8, 2026. She emphasized that the total number of child protection cases in Kaohsiung has exceeded 45,000 over the past three years, with reports from medical personnel increasing from 1,526 to 2,440. Last year saw an increase of 467 cases compared to the previous year, the highest growth rate among the six special municipalities. Furthermore, there have been 14 child abuse-related deaths in the last three years, the highest among the six municipalities. Ko proposed three main directions: "strengthening child protection, improving social worker compensation, and safeguarding the mental health of social workers," emphasizing that children's safety cannot rely on luck, and the protection provided by social workers cannot rely solely on a sense of mission; the government must weave a tighter and more stable safety net.
  
  These data indicate that there is still significant room for improvement in the child protection system regarding preventive mechanisms and the working conditions of frontline social workers.
  
  At the same time, the issue of elderly care for individuals with intellectual disabilities has also received high social attention. As Taiwan enters a super-aged society, individuals with intellectual disabilities face the challenges of aging and increased care needs. Celebrity Ruby Lin has collaborated with the Maria Social Welfare Foundation to assist in meeting the care needs of 127 families that have persisted for a decade, building homes for the elderly with intellectual disabilities through a relay of love. This move highlights the critical role of private forces in filling service gaps beyond government long-term care resources. However, while private charitable activities can alleviate immediate needs, long-term elderly care policies still require systematic investment and planning of national resources to ensure that vulnerable families do not fall into isolation when facing the pressures of long-term care.
  
  Regarding the boundaries of power in medical settings, Taipei City Councilor Li Bo-yi was reported to have scolded medical staff in an emergency room and photographed a doctor's identification card while threatening to file a complaint, sparking questions about "elected officials abusing their power." This incident, linked to the previous "privilege" controversy involving Chiang Wan-an's son participating in a Taipei City international exchange student program, has triggered widespread discussion in public opinion regarding the boundaries of power for elected representatives and respect for the medical environment. Polling experts point out that such incidents may have a ripple effect on the Blue camp's election prospects, with the specific degree of impact awaiting verification from subsequent polling data. These types of incidents remind the public that clear behavioral norms must be established between the power of elected representatives and the professional dignity of medical personnel to maintain the stable operation of the medical system and prevent a culture of privilege from eroding the fairness of public services.
  
  Furthermore, the scope of protection for the vulnerable also extends to the right to life and dignity in extreme cases. Colombian woman María Consuelo Córdoba was disfigured 26 years ago when her ex-husband forced acid down her throat. After enduring 87 surgeries and long-term poverty, she once applied for euthanasia out of despair. After this case was exposed by the news, it attracted international attention, and a large number of kind-hearted people and medical teams reached out to help, leading her to decide to abandon euthanasia at the last moment and bravely return to the operating table.
  
  Although this case occurred overseas, the issues of "dignity of life" and "social support systems" it touches upon coincide with the discussion in Taiwanese society on how vulnerable individuals can regain hope for survival through external support, and also reflect the importance of psychological support and resource injection within the social safety net during extreme adversity.
  
  Synthesizing the above events, the public should avoid oversimplification when interpreting information. For example, the increase in child protection cases may stem from the popularization of reporting mechanisms and increased social awareness, rather than simply a deterioration in public safety; controversies involving elected representatives are easily magnified into overall image issues for a single political party during political maneuvering, but their core remains the boundary between individual behavior and public etiquette. Subsequent observation will focus on: the Kaohsiung City Government's official response to child protection data and subsequent policy adjustments; the follow-up investigation and public reaction to the Taipei City Councilor's medical controversy; and whether the issue of elderly care for individuals with intellectual disabilities can further promote the reallocation of government resources in long-term care policies.
  
  The stability of the social safety net will depend on whether the government, the private sector, and public figures can implement substantive protection for vulnerable groups within their respective scopes of responsibility, and maintain professionalism and respect in the public sphere.

# 社會安全網與弱勢保障：從兒少保護到身障安老議題探討

> 社會安全網的建構與弱勢族群的保障，長期以來是公共政策的核心議題。近期臺灣社會發生多起引發關注的事件，包括高雄市兒少保護政策的倡議、心智障礙者家庭面臨的長期安老困境，以及民意代表在醫療場域的行為引發的社會觀感與特權爭議。這些事件反映出，社會安全網不僅包含政府的政策制定，亦涉及醫療資源的公平使用、社工勞動環境的改善，以及公眾人物對社會責任的實踐。當我們審視這些議題時，不僅要看到資料背後的悲劇，更需探討制度如何從被動補救轉向主動預防。


根據相關媒體報導，國民黨高雄市長候選人柯志恩於2026年9月8日提出兒少保護政策，強調高雄近三年兒少保護總案量超過4萬5千件，且醫事人員通報件數從1526件增加至2440件，去年比前年增加467件，增幅居六都之首。此外，近三年兒虐致死人數達14人，居六都之冠。柯志恩提出「強化兒少保護、提升社工待遇、守護社工心理健康」三大方向，強調孩子的安全不能靠運氣，社工的守護不能只靠使命感，政府必須把安全網織得更密、更穩。
這些資料顯示，兒少保護體系在預防機制與第一線社工的勞動條件上，仍有顯著的補強空間。
與此同時，心智障礙者的安老問題亦受到社會高度關注。隨著臺灣邁入超高齡社會，心智障礙者面臨老化與照顧需求增加的挑戰。藝人林心如與瑪利亞基金會合作，協助解決127個家庭長達十年的照護需求，透過愛心接力為心智障礙者築家安老。此舉凸顯了在政府長照資源之外，民間力量在填補服務缺口上的關鍵角色。然而，民間公益活動雖能緩解燃眉之急，但長期的安老政策仍需仰賴國家資源的系統性投入與規劃，以確保弱勢家庭在面對長期照護壓力時，不至於陷入孤立無援的困境。
在醫療場域的權力邊界方面，臺北市議員李柏毅遭爆在急診室斥責醫護人員，並拍攝醫師證件揚言投訴，引發「民代耍官威」的質疑。此事件與先前蔣萬安兒子參與北市國際交換學生計畫的「特權」爭議連結，引發輿論對於民代權力邊界與醫療環境尊重的廣泛討論。民調專家指出，此類事件可能對藍營選情造成連環衝擊，具體影響程度仍待後續民調資料驗證。這類事件提醒公眾，在民代權力與醫護專業尊嚴之間，必須建立明確的行為規範，以維持醫療系統的穩定運作，避免特權文化侵蝕公共服務的公平性。
此外，弱勢保障的範疇亦延伸至極端個案的生存權與尊嚴。哥倫比亞女子瑪莉亞（María Consuelo Córdoba）26年前遭前夫強灌潑酸毀容，歷經87次手術與長期的貧困煎熬，曾因絕望申請安樂死。此案經新聞曝光後，引發國際關注，大批善心人士與醫療團隊伸出援手，使其在最後一刻決定放棄安樂死，勇敢重返手術臺。
此案例雖發生於海外，但其所觸及的「生命尊嚴」與「社會支援系統」議題，與臺灣社會對於弱勢者如何透過外部支援重拾生存希望的討論不謀而合，亦反映出社會安全網在極端困境下，心理支援與資源挹注的重要性。
綜合上述事件，公眾在解讀資訊時應避免過度簡化。例如，兒少保護案件的增加，可能源於通報機制的普及與社會意識提升，而非僅是單純的治安惡化；民代爭議事件在政治攻防中，容易被放大為單一政黨的整體形象問題，但其核心仍為個人行為與公共禮儀之界線。後續觀察重點將聚焦於：高雄市府對兒少保護資料的官方回應與後續政策調整；臺北市議員醫療爭議事件的後續調查與民意反應；以及心智障礙者安老議題是否能進一步推動政府在長照政策上的資源重新分配。
社會安全網的穩固程度，將取決於政府、民間與公眾人物能否在各自的職責範圍內，落實對弱勢群體的實質保障，並維護公共場域的專業與尊重。
