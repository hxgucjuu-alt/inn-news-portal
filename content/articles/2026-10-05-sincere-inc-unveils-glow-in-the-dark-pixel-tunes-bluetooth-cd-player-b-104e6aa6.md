Sincere Inc. 近日發布了其 Pixel Tunes 便攜式藍牙 CD 播放器的全新限量版，命名為「After Hours」。這款新產品以其引人注目的夜光透明綠色外觀脫穎而出，旨在將經典的 CD 播放體驗與現代科技功能相結合，為使用者帶來獨特的視聽享受。

「After Hours」版本配備了一系列現代功能，包括 USB-C 連接埠、藍牙 5.3 技術以及可充電鋰離子電池。資料顯示，這款限量版是四種半透明顏色之一，但由於使用了磷光材料，它是唯一一款能在黑暗中發光的款式，確保其在光線昏暗的環境中也能輕易被找到。

目前，這款獨特的 CD 播放器在日本市場的售價為 12,980 日圓，約合 83 美元。然而，報導指出，對於美國和英國的買家而言，直接購買可能存在困難，通常需要透過進口服務或第三方經銷商，這可能會導致額外的費用和顯著的價格上漲。

## English edition

Sincere Inc. has recently released a new limited edition of its Pixel Tunes portable Bluetooth CD player, dubbed 'After Hours'. This new product distinguishes itself with an alluring glow-in-the-dark transparent green finish, aiming to blend the classic CD listening experience with modern technological features, offering users a unique audio-visual delight.

The 'After Hours' edition is equipped with a range of contemporary functionalities, including a USB-C port, Bluetooth 5.3 technology, and a rechargeable Li-ion battery. Data indicates that this limited edition is one of four translucent colors available, but it is the only one that glows in the dark due to the use of phosphorescent materials, ensuring it won't get lost in dimly lit spaces.

Currently, this distinctive CD player is available in the Japanese market for ¥12,980, which is approximately $83 USD. However, reports suggest that buyers in the United States and the United Kingdom may face difficulties in direct purchase, often requiring the use of import services or third-party resellers, which could lead to additional costs and a significant price markup.
