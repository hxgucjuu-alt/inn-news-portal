  # 2026 Taiwan Innotech Expo: NCNU and Its Affiliated High School Showcase Educational Innovation Through Inter-school Collaboration
  
  > A team of teachers and students from the National Chi Nan University (NCNU) and the NCNU Affiliated High School secured corporate special awards and multiple medals at the "2026 Taiwan Innotech Expo" with practical projects addressing daily life and social issues, demonstrating the abundant energy of educational innovation in Central Taiwan.
  
  As Taiwan's most iconic exchange platform for innovative inventions, the "2026 Taiwan Innotech Expo" attracts numerous academic units, enterprises, and research institutions every year, aiming to promote the integration of technological innovation, market application value, and social impact. At this year's expo, the National Chi Nan University (hereinafter referred to as NCNU) and its affiliated high school (NCNU Affiliated High School) formed a joint inter-school team through the College of Education's USR (University Social Responsibility) project. The project has long been committed to linking local needs with higher education resources; this participation not only served as a review of academic achievements but also embodied the deep cooperation between universities and the secondary education system in cultivating innovative talent.
  
  According to multiple public sources, the team composed of NCNU and NCNU Affiliated High School faculty and students performed impressively at the expo, winning a total of 9 honors. Specific winning projects include "Corporate Special Awards" presented by three well-known companies: Corning, MSI, and Goodbaby (Mingmen Industry). In addition, the team won 3 silver medals, 1 bronze medal, and 2 "Future Star Awards." The winning achievements focused primarily on practical designs that address public life needs and social issues. The jury gave high marks to the team's performance in technological innovation and market application potential, noting that it demonstrated the concrete effectiveness of integrating educational resources in the Central Taiwan region.
  
  Regarding the specific technical details of the winning projects and the individual inventions corresponding to each award, there is currently no detailed technical specification or patent disclosure in the public domain. Although media reports consistently confirm a total of 9 awards and clearly identify the names of the awarding companies and categories, information regarding the research and development cycle of each project, the distribution of participating faculty and students, and the specific path for future commercialization remains undisclosed. Furthermore, regarding the selection criteria for certain awards, the specific definition of the "Future Star Award" in terms of the weighting between technical indicators and social impact remains to be further clarified by the organizers.
  
  These awards have a positive impact on education and industrial development in Central Taiwan. First, through the promotion of the USR project, NCNU has successfully rooted higher education resources downward, allowing high school students to gain early exposure to the field of innovative invention and enhance their problem-solving abilities. Second, the receipt of corporate special awards indicates that the connection between academic R&D achievements and industrial needs is becoming increasingly close, which is significant for promoting regional industrial upgrading and cultivating innovative talent with practical capabilities. This type of cross-school, cross-stage cooperation model provides a feasible practice template for Taiwan's education sector: using social responsibility projects as a medium to transform educational settings into innovation laboratories, thereby driving the overall development of local society.
  
  However, when interpreting the results of this expo, all parties should remain neutral and avoid over-interpretation. Although the number of awards reflects the team's strength, the market application value of innovative technology often requires long-term market verification rather than being determined solely by awards from an invention exhibition. Furthermore, there remains a technological gap between innovation within the education system and industrial mass production technology. Whether the winning works can be successfully transformed into economically viable products is still limited by external factors such as capital injection, supply chain integration, and marketing. Therefore, awards from a single expo should not be viewed as the sole measure of the university's or the team's technical R&D capabilities, but rather as a milestone in the process of educational innovation.
  
  Subsequent observation will focus on the progress of the winning technologies and how the NCNU USR project will further deepen these innovative achievements within the local community. First, it is necessary to monitor whether the winning teams can sign further industry-academia cooperation agreements with the corporate sector to transform prototypes into practical application products. Second, it remains to be seen whether the model of NCNU Affiliated High School participating in such high-intensity innovation competitions will become a normalized mechanism for promoting STEM education in secondary education. Finally, the level of policy support from relevant educational authorities for this type of inter-school cooperation model, and how to promote this successful experience to other regions, will be important indicators for observing the future development of educational innovation in Taiwan.

# 2026臺灣創新技術博覽會：暨大與附中跨校合作展現教育創新實力

> 國立暨南國際大學與暨大附中師生團隊，於「2026臺灣創新技術博覽會」中以貼近生活與社會議題的實作成果，斬獲企業特別獎與多項獎牌，展現中臺灣教育深耕創新的豐沛能量。


「2026臺灣創新技術博覽會」作為臺灣國內最具指標性的創新發明交流平臺，每年吸引眾多學術單位、企業與研究機構參與，旨在推動技術創新、市場應用價值與社會影響力之結合。在本次博覽會中，國立暨南國際大學（以下簡稱暨大）與其附屬高階中學（暨大附中）透過教育學院USR（大學社會責任實踐）計畫，共同組成跨校師生團隊參展。該計畫長期致力於鏈結地方需求與高教資源，此次參展不僅是學術成果的檢視，更體現了大學與中等教育體系在創新人才培育上的深度合作。
根據多方公開資訊確認，暨大與暨大附中組成的師生團隊在本次博覽會中表現亮眼，累計奪下9項殊榮。具體獲獎專案包括：獲得康寧、微星科技、明門實業等三間知名企業頒發的「企業特別獎」，此外還榮獲3面銀牌、1面銅牌，以及2座「未來創星獎」。此次獲獎成果主要聚焦於貼近民眾生活需求與解決社會議題的實作設計，評審團對該團隊在技術創新性與市場應用潛力上的表現給予高度評價，認為其展現了中臺灣區域教育資源整合的具體成效。
關於此次獲獎的具體技術細節與各獎項對應的單一發明內容，目前公開資訊中尚無詳細的技術規格說明或專利細節披露。雖然各媒體報導一致肯定獲獎總數為9項，且明確指出獲獎企業名稱與獎項類別，但針對每一項獲獎作品的研發週期、參與師生人數分配，以及該技術在未來商業化程式中的具體路徑，目前仍處於資訊未完全公開的階段。此外，針對部分獎項的評選標準，各界對於「未來創星獎」在技術指標與社會影響力權重上的具體定義，仍有待主辦單位進一步說明。
此次獲獎對中臺灣的教育與產業發展具有正面影響。首先，透過USR計畫的推動，暨大成功將高教資源向下紮根，讓高中職階段的學生能提早接觸創新發明領域，提升解決問題的能力。其次，企業特別獎的獲得，顯示學界研發成果與產業需求之間的連結日益緊密，這對於促進區域產業升級、培育具備實務能力的創新人才具有指標性意義。此類跨校、跨階段的合作模式，為臺灣教育界提供了一種可行的實踐範本，即透過社會責任計畫作為媒介，將教育現場轉化為創新實驗室，進而帶動地方社會的整體發展。
然而，對於此次博覽會的成果，各界在解讀時應保持中立並避免過度詮釋。雖然獲獎數量反映了團隊的實力，但創新技術的市場應用價值往往需要經過長時間的市場驗證，而非僅憑發明展的獎項即可定論。此外，教育體系內的創新成果與產業量產技術之間仍存在技術斷層，獲獎作品是否能順利轉化為具備經濟效益的商品，仍受限於資金挹注、供應鏈整合及市場行銷等外部因素。因此，不應將單一博覽會的獎項視為該校或該團隊在技術研發上的唯一衡量標準，而應將其視為教育創新過程中的一個階段性里程碑。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於獲獎技術的後續開發進度，以及暨大USR計畫如何將這些創新成果進一步深化至地方社群。首先，需關注獲獎團隊是否能與企業界進一步簽訂產學合作協議，將原型機轉化為實際應用產品。其次，觀察暨大附中參與此類高強度創新賽事的模式，是否會成為未來中等教育推動STEM教育的常態化機制。最後，相關教育主管機關對於此類跨校合作模式的政策支援力度，以及如何將此成功經驗推廣至其他區域，將是未來觀察臺灣教育創新發展的重要指標。
