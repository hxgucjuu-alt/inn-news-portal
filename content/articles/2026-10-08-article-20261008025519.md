  # The Strategic Challenges of U.S. Interstellar Ambitions and Reliance on Commercial Spaceflight
  
  > The U.S. interstellar exploration program has recently drawn attention due to NASA’s deepening reliance on SpaceX. This has sparked discussions regarding whether national space strategy is being constrained by the decision-making of private enterprises, even as the scientific community continues to conduct in-depth observations of interstellar objects.
  
  As the cornerstone of multinational space cooperation, the International Space Station (ISS) has undergone decades of expansion since the docking of Russia’s Zarya module and the U.S. Unity node in 1998. With the addition of Russia’s Nauka laboratory and the Prichal node in 2021, the ISS has become a complex structure integrating technologies from multiple nations, including NASA, Roscosmos, ESA, and JAXA.
  
  However, as the ISS faces pressure regarding its decommissioning, U.S. interstellar exploration ambitions have gradually shifted toward deep cooperation with private enterprises. In particular, SpaceX’s critical role in federal government contracts has become a core issue in current global aerospace policy and strategic planning.
  
  According to various media reports, the U.S. government currently demonstrates a high level of reliance on Elon Musk’s SpaceX for space transportation and interstellar exploration missions. Information compiled by the Associated Press and *inews* indicates that NASA has signed multiple federal government contracts with SpaceX, which cover not only logistics resupply for low Earth orbit but also extend to future crewed lunar landings and deep-space exploration missions.
  
  It is a widely acknowledged fact that SpaceX has become an indispensable commercial partner for the United States to maintain its space superiority. The launch capabilities and vehicle technologies it provides directly influence the scheduling and execution efficiency of NASA’s interstellar exploration efforts.
  
  Although commercial cooperation has become an established reality, there is a clear divergence in media assessments of this model. Some reports suggest that U.S. interstellar ambitions are at risk of being "at the mercy of Elon Musk’s personal will." Citing a *Wall Street Journal* review of SpaceX’s federal contracts, some observers worry that the decision-making patterns of private enterprises may conflict with national long-term space strategic goals. Conversely, another perspective emphasizes that the rise of commercial spaceflight is an inevitable choice for reducing the costs of space exploration and accelerating technological iteration.
  
  What remains to be confirmed is whether the U.S. government has sufficient contingency mechanisms in place to ensure that interstellar exploration missions will not be interrupted should SpaceX undergo significant operational adjustments or policy shifts in the future.
  
  This trend has had a profound impact on the global space industry and policy. First, it has altered the traditional "government-led, contractor-executed" aerospace supply chain model, granting a single private enterprise unprecedented influence over national security and scientific exploration. For international partners, this means that future participation in NASA-led interstellar programs will require adapting to this highly commercialized operational environment. Furthermore, this places pressure on national space budget allocations; governments must strike a balance between maintaining autonomous space capabilities and purchasing commercial services to avoid over-reliance on a single supplier for critical technologies, which could otherwise weaken national strategic autonomy in space.
  
  Regarding U.S. interstellar ambitions, academia and the industry have raised several limitations and warnings. First, overly binding national space policy to the commercial interests of a single entrepreneur may lead to uncertainty in policy implementation—this is not merely a technical issue, but a political risk. Second, current research in the scientific community regarding interstellar objects (such as the interstellar comet 3I/Atlas) still relies heavily on the open sharing of international data, rather than solely on the technical capabilities of a single corporation. Therefore, over-interpreting the dominant position of commercial spaceflight in interstellar exploration may overlook the core value of basic scientific research and multinational scientific cooperation in driving human exploration of the universe.
  
  All parties should view the efficiency gains brought by commercialization with caution, while remaining vigilant about the potential strategic vulnerabilities it may introduce.
  
  Looking ahead, future observation will focus on how NASA manages contract risks with SpaceX and whether the U.S. government will adopt a more diversified supplier strategy to mitigate risk. Additionally, as the decommissioning of the ISS approaches, international planning for the next generation of space stations and the role of private enterprises in deep-space exploration will become key indicators of whether this commercial model possesses long-term stability. The scientific community will also continue to monitor celestial bodies such as interstellar comets; these studies will not only reveal information about environments outside the solar system but will also continue to test the limits of human deep-space exploration technology and multinational collaborative capabilities.

# 美國星際探索雄心與商業航太依賴的戰略挑戰

> 美國星際探索計畫近期引發關注，NASA對SpaceX的商業依賴程度加深，引發外界對於國家太空戰略是否受限於私人企業決策的討論，同時科學界持續針對星際物體進行深度觀測。


國際太空站（ISS）作為人類跨國太空合作的基石，自1998年由俄羅斯建造的曙光號（Zarya）模組與美國團結號（Unity）節點艙對接以來，已歷經數十年的擴建。隨著2021年俄羅斯科學號（Nauka）實驗室與碼頭號（Prichal）節點艙的加入，ISS已成為結合NASA、Roscosmos、ESA與JAXA等多國技術的複雜結構。
然而，隨著ISS面臨除役壓力，美國的星際探索雄心已逐漸轉向與私人企業的深度合作，特別是SpaceX在聯邦政府合約中的關鍵角色，成為當前全球航太政策與戰略佈局的核心議題。
根據多方媒體報導，美國政府目前在太空運輸與星際探索任務上，對伊隆·馬斯克（Elon Musk）旗下的SpaceX展現了極高的依賴性。美聯社與《inews》整理的資訊顯示，NASA與SpaceX簽署了多項聯邦政府合約，這些合約不僅涵蓋近地軌道的物資運補，更延伸至未來的載人登月與深空探測任務。
各界共同確認的事實是，SpaceX已成為美國維持太空優勢不可或缺的商業夥伴，其提供的發射能力與載具技術，直接影響了美國國家航空暨太空總署（NASA）在星際探索領域的時程規劃與執行效率。
儘管商業合作已成定局，但針對此模式的評估在媒體間存在明顯分歧。部分報導指出，美國的星際雄心正處於「受制於馬斯克個人意志」的風險之中，引述《華爾街日報》對SpaceX聯邦合約的審查，部分觀察家擔憂私人企業的決策模式可能與國家長遠的太空戰略目標產生衝突。然而，另一種觀點則強調，商業航太的崛起是降低太空探索成本、加速技術迭代的必然選擇。
目前尚待確認的是，若SpaceX在未來出現重大的營運調整或政策轉向，美國政府是否有足夠的備案機制，以確保星際探索任務不會因此中斷。
此一趨勢對全球太空產業與政策產生了深遠影響。首先，這改變了傳統「政府主導、承包商執行」的航太供應鏈模式，使得單一私人企業在國家安全與科學探索中擁有前所未有的話語權。對於國際合作夥伴而言，這意味著未來參與NASA主導的星際計畫時，必須適應這種高度商業化的運作環境。此外，這也對各國的太空預算分配造成壓力，各國政府必須在維持自主太空能力與購買商業服務之間取得平衡，以免在關鍵技術上過度依賴單一供應商，進而削弱國家在太空領域的戰略自主性。
針對美國的星際野心，學界與產業界亦提出多項限制與警示。首先，過度將國家太空政策與單一企業家的商業利益繫結，可能導致政策執行上的不確定性，這不僅是技術問題，更是政治風險。其次，科學界目前對於星際物體（如星際彗星3I/Atlas）的研究，仍高度仰賴國際間的公開資料共享，而非僅憑藉單一企業的技術能力。因此，過度解讀商業航太在星際探索中的主導地位，可能會忽略基礎科學研究與跨國科研合作在推動人類探索宇宙過程中的核心價值。
各界應謹慎看待商業化帶來的效率提升，同時警惕其可能帶來的戰略脆弱性。
展望未來，後續觀察重點將集中於NASA如何管理與SpaceX的合約風險，以及美國政府是否會採取更多元化的供應商策略以分散風險。此外，隨著ISS除役時程的逼近，國際間對於下一代空間站的規劃，以及私人企業在深空探索中扮演的角色，將成為檢驗此一商業模式是否具備長期穩定性的關鍵指標。科學界亦將持續監測星際彗星等天體，這些研究不僅能揭示太陽系外的環境資訊，也將持續考驗人類在深空探測技術上的極限與跨國協作能力。
