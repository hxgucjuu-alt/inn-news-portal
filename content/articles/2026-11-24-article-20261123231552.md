  # JPMorgan CEO Dimon Reaffirms Stability of Dollar Hegemony and Outlook on Global Economic Situation
  
  > JPMorgan Chase CEO Jamie Dimon recently emphasized that the U.S. dollar’s status as a reserve currency remains solid, attributing this to America's military and economic strength. Meanwhile, Russia is investing heavily to strengthen logistics infrastructure in Kaliningrad, reflecting regional challenges in global geopolitics and economic supply chains.
  
  JPMorgan Chase CEO Jamie Dimon recently delivered a speech at the Economic Club of Chicago, offering a clear perspective on the "de-dollarization" trend that has been widely discussed internationally in recent years. Dimon pointed out that the U.S. dollar's leading position as the global reserve currency is unlikely to be shaken in the short term. A reserve currency is a key foreign currency held in large quantities by central banks to support international trade, stabilize domestic exchange rates, and settle foreign debt. Dimon analyzed that the stability of dollar hegemony is rooted in four core pillars: America's powerful military strength, a stable political system, a sound economic structure, and the rule of law.
  
  These factors collectively foster high levels of trust among global users regarding the liquidity and legal stability of the U.S. financial system, allowing it to maintain an irreplaceable position in the international financial architecture.
  
  At the same time, adjustments to economic supply chains driven by global geopolitics are ongoing. According to official Russian information, Prime Minister Mikhail Mishustin announced on Monday that the Russian government will allocate 47 billion rubles (approximately $576 million) for the construction of two large rail ferries. This decision aims to ensure that the supply chain for the Russian exclave of Kaliningrad remains uninterrupted amidst rising regional tensions.
  
  This infrastructure investment highlights that in the current environment of international sanctions and geopolitical confrontation, certain countries are actively strengthening logistics resilience to cope with potential blockade risks and challenges of economic isolation, forming a sharp contrast to the global financial system stability emphasized by Dimon.
  
  There are significant differences in perspectives regarding the future status of the dollar. Dimon's assessment focuses on the stability of America's internal structure and its military deterrent, arguing that as long as these foundations remain unshaken, the dollar's global influence will remain strong. However, international discussions on de-dollarization have not ceased, as some emerging market countries, based on hedging considerations, are attempting to reduce their reliance on the dollar in bilateral trade and increase the proportion of gold or other currency reserves.
  
  Furthermore, while Russia's rail ferry investment plan for Kaliningrad belongs to regional logistics construction, the economic logic behind it reflects a close link between national security and economic autonomy, which presents a different development path in terms of operational logic compared to the dollar settlement mechanism under the globalized market system.
  
  The aforementioned events have had a profound impact on the global economy and trade. Dimon's remarks provide a confidence indicator for global investors regarding the stability of dollar assets, helping to alleviate market concerns about the collapse of the reserve currency system. However, Russia's move to strengthen logistics in Kaliningrad reminds global supply chain participants that geopolitical risk remains a key factor affecting regional trade stability. When the international trade environment is influenced by political confrontation, rising logistics costs and the fragmentation of supply chains may lead to reduced global trade efficiency, thereby affecting inflation expectations and economic growth momentum in various countries.
  
  Policymakers need to seek a more nuanced balance between maintaining the stability of the international financial system and addressing regional logistics challenges.
  
  Regarding Dimon's views, market analysts caution against over-interpreting his remarks as a "dollar omnipotence theory." Although the dollar still possesses strong institutional advantages in the short term, the evolution of the global financial system is extremely complex. In the long run, it remains influenced by global trade flows, the development of digital currency technology, and shifts in central bank policies worldwide. Similarly, while Russia's heavy investment in rail ferries addresses current logistics bottlenecks, its long-term economic benefits still depend on the evolution of the regional situation and the duration of international sanctions.
  
  These pieces of information indicate that the global economy is in a transitional phase where it is both dependent on the existing financial order and must cope with regional geopolitical shocks; any change in a single factor could trigger a chain reaction.
  
  Subsequent observations should focus on two major directions: first, whether the monetary policy moves of the U.S. Federal Reserve and major central banks will further consolidate or weaken the dollar's liquidity advantage; second, the progress of logistics infrastructure construction in geopolitical hotspots, particularly transportation links in the border regions between Russia and Europe, which will be an important indicator for measuring regional economic resilience and the level of geopolitical tension. Investors and policymakers should continue to monitor liquidity data in international financial markets and the adjustment strategies of global supply chains in the face of geopolitical risks to grasp the pulse of structural changes in the global economic system.

# 摩根大通執行長戴蒙重申美元霸權穩定性與全球經濟局勢展望

> 摩根大通執行長戴蒙近期強調美元儲備貨幣地位穩固，歸功於美國軍事與經濟實力；與此同時，俄羅斯則投入巨資強化加里寧格勒物流基礎建設，反映全球地緣政治與經濟供應鏈的區域性挑戰。


摩根大通執行長傑米·戴蒙（Jamie Dimon）於近期在芝加哥經濟俱樂部發表演說，針對近年國際間熱議的「去美元化」趨勢提出明確觀點。戴蒙指出，美元作為全球儲備貨幣的龍頭地位，短期內難以被撼動。所謂儲備貨幣，係指各國央行大量持有以支應國際貿易、穩定本幣匯率及清償外債的關鍵外幣。戴蒙分析，美元霸權的穩固根植於四大核心支柱：美國強大的軍事力量、穩定的政體、健全的經濟結構以及法治環境。
這些因素共同構成全球使用者對美國金融體系流動性與法治穩定性的高度信任，使其在國際金融體系中維持不可取代的地位。
與此同時，全球地緣政治帶來的經濟供應鏈調整亦在持續進行。根據俄羅斯官方資訊，總理米哈伊爾·米舒斯京（Mikhail Mishustin）於週一宣佈，俄國政府將撥款470億盧布（約合5.76億美元），用於建造兩艘大型鐵路渡輪。此項決策旨在確保在區域緊張局勢升溫的背景下，俄羅斯飛地加里寧格勒（Kaliningrad）的物資供應鏈能維持不中斷。
這項基礎設施投資凸顯了在當前國際制裁與地緣政治對峙的環境下，特定國家正積極透過強化物流韌性，以應對潛在的封鎖風險與經濟孤立挑戰，與戴蒙所強調的全球金融體系穩定性形成鮮明對比。
關於美元的未來地位，各界觀點存在顯著差異。戴蒙的評估側重於美國內部結構的穩定性與軍事威懾力，認為只要這些基礎不動搖，美元的全球影響力將維持強勢。然而，國際間關於去美元化的討論並未止息，部分新興市場國家基於避險考量，正嘗試在雙邊貿易中減少對美元的依賴，並增加黃金或其他貨幣的儲備比重。
此外，俄羅斯針對加里寧格勒的鐵路渡輪投資計畫，雖然屬於區域性物流建設，但其背後的經濟邏輯反映了國家安全與經濟自主之間的緊密連結，這與全球化市場體系下的美元結算機制，在運作邏輯上呈現出不同的發展路徑。
上述事件對全球經濟與貿易產生了深遠影響。戴蒙的言論為全球投資者提供了關於美元資產穩定性的信心指標，有助於緩解市場對於儲備貨幣體系崩潰的擔憂。然而，俄羅斯加強加里寧格勒物流建設的舉措，則提醒全球供應鏈參與者，地緣政治風險仍是影響區域貿易穩定性的關鍵因素。當國際貿易環境受到政治對立影響時，物流成本的上升與供應鏈的碎片化，將可能導致全球貿易效率降低，進而影響各國的通膨預期與經濟成長動能。
政策制定者需在維護國際金融體系穩定與應對區域性物流挑戰之間，尋求更為細緻的平衡。
針對戴蒙的觀點，市場分析師提醒，不應過度解讀其言論為「美元萬能論」。儘管美元在短期內仍具備強大的制度性優勢，但全球金融體系的演變極為複雜，長期而言，依然受到全球貿易流向、數位貨幣技術發展以及各國央行政策轉向的影響。同樣地，俄羅斯投入巨資建設鐵路渡輪，雖然解決了當前的物流瓶頸，但其長期經濟效益仍取決於區域局勢的演變與國際制裁的持續程度。
這些資訊均顯示，全球經濟正處於一個既依賴現有金融秩序，又必須應對區域性地緣政治衝擊的過渡階段，任何單一因素的變動都可能引發連鎖反應。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於兩大方向：首先是美國聯準會與主要央行在貨幣政策上的動向，是否會進一步鞏固或削弱美元的流動性優勢；其次是地緣政治熱點地區的物流基礎設施建設進度，特別是俄羅斯與歐洲邊境地區的交通連結，這將是衡量區域經濟韌性與地緣政治緊張程度的重要指標。投資人與決策者應持續關注國際金融市場的流動性資料，以及全球供應鏈在面對地緣政治風險時的調整策略，以掌握全球經濟體系結構性變化的脈動。
