  # Geopolitical and Economic Landscape in the Asia-Pacific: From Trans-Pacific Cooperation to the Evolution of the Indo-Pacific Strategy
  
  > This article examines the development of the Asia-Pacific region from the early Trans-Pacific Strategic Economic Partnership Agreement (TPSEP) to the current shift in Indo-Pacific geopolitical and economic landscapes, while exploring the profound impacts of infrastructure, shipping logistics, and regional security on global supply chains.
  
  As a core region for global trade and geopolitics, the development of the Asia-Pacific began with the establishment of multilateral trade frameworks. According to historical data compiled by KPMG, the Trans-Pacific Strategic Economic Partnership Agreement (TPSEP) was signed in June 2005 by Singapore, New Zealand, Brunei, and Chile. The agreement was initially aimed at promoting trade liberalization among member states; although the economies of the member countries were relatively small at the time, this framework laid the foundation for broader Asia-Pacific economic integration. Concurrently, infrastructure and environmental governance in the region also developed, such as environmental research on red tides and harmful algal blooms in the Asia-Pacific, reflecting the diversity of public issues and the urgent need for transnational cooperation within the region.
  
  In terms of trade practices and logistics operations, shipping indicators in the Asia-Pacific have become an important window for observing global supply chains. The World Container Index (WCI) provided by Drewry shows that freight rates on trans-Pacific and Asia-Europe routes are deeply affected by market demand fluctuations. For instance, at specific junctures such as Golden Week, a decline in demand directly leads to freight rate volatility, demonstrating that logistics efficiency and maritime costs in the Asia-Pacific are closely linked to the stability of global supply chains. Furthermore, large-scale infrastructure projects, such as the construction of China's Three Gorges Dam, were also regarded as milestones in regional public works, with their construction progress and influence attracting widespread international attention at the time.
  
  However, there are significant differences in interpretation regarding the definition and geopolitical influence of the Asia-Pacific region. Research by the German Institute for International and Security Affairs (SWP) points out that the conceptual shift from "Asia-Pacific" to "Indo-Pacific" reflects an expansion of geopolitical and geoeconomic space. Official documents position the Indo-Pacific as a core zone for defending U.S. global interests, yet its geographical boundaries remain imprecisely defined to this day. This ambiguity leads countries to face different interpretive challenges in strategic execution and policy implementation, making the region's role in international political maneuvering increasingly complex.
  
  Regarding regional security and the media environment, information flow and diplomatic relations in the Asia-Pacific are also facing tests. According to i24NEWS, international media reporters have faced legal proceedings and threats from authorities when covering regional issues, highlighting profound divisions within the region regarding information transparency, freedom of speech, and ideological stances. Such conflicts not only affect the media reporting environment but also reflect the friction in political systems and values among countries in the Asia-Pacific as they pursue economic integration; these frictions directly impact the operational security of multinational corporations and the formulation of diplomatic policies.
  
  There is a significant gap in perspectives regarding the aforementioned issues. Supporters argue that through the promotion of agreements like the TPSEP and the Indo-Pacific strategy, the Asia-Pacific region can effectively integrate resources and enhance global competitiveness; however, critics point out that an excessive emphasis on geopolitical confrontation may sacrifice space for regional economic cooperation. Furthermore, the balance between infrastructure and environmental protection, as well as the resilience of shipping logistics in the face of sudden demand fluctuations, are limiting factors that policymakers in all countries must face. The influence of a single strategic document should not be over-interpreted, as substantive economic and trade exchanges within the region are often constrained by both market mechanisms and local regulations.
  
  Looking ahead, the development focus of the Asia-Pacific will center on the restructuring of supply chains and the stability of the regional security architecture. As the "Indo-Pacific" concept gradually replaces the traditional "Asia-Pacific" framework, how countries maintain economic autonomy amidst the U.S.-China rivalry, while addressing transnational public issues such as environmental governance, logistics efficiency, and information flow, will become a key point of observation. Future efforts should continue to track the expansion progress of regional trade agreements, as well as changes in infrastructure investment and shipping logistics data, to assess their long-term impact on the global economic landscape.

# 亞太地區地緣政治與經貿格局：從跨太平洋合作到印太戰略的演變

> 本文梳理亞太地區從早期的跨太平洋戰略經濟夥伴協定（TPSEP）發展，至當前印太地緣政治與經濟版圖的轉變，並探討基礎建設、航運物流及區域安全對全球供應鏈的深遠影響。


亞太地區作為全球經貿與地緣政治的核心區域，其發展歷程始於多邊貿易架構的建立。根據KPMG整理的歷史資料，跨太平洋戰略經濟夥伴協定（TPSEP）於2005年6月由新加坡、紐西蘭、汶萊及智利四國共同簽署。該協定最初旨在促進成員國間的貿易自由化，儘管當時成員國經濟規模相對較小，但此架構為後續更廣泛的亞太經貿整合奠定了基礎。與此同時，該區域的基礎建設與環境治理亦同步發展，例如針對亞太地區赤潮與有害藻華的環境研究，反映了區域內公共議題的多樣性與跨國合作的迫切需求。
在經貿實務與物流運作方面，亞太地區的航運指標成為觀察全球供應鏈的重要視窗。德路里（Drewry）提供的世界貨櫃運價指數（WCI）顯示，跨太平洋航線與亞歐航線的運價表現，深受市場需求波動影響。例如在黃金週等特定節點，需求下降直接導致運價波動，顯示亞太地區的物流效率與海運成本，與全球供應鏈的穩定性緊密掛鉤。此外，大型基礎建設如中國長江三峽大壩的建設工程，亦被視為區域內重大公共工程的里程碑，其建設進度與影響力在當時引發了國際社會的廣泛關注。
然而，關於亞太地區的定義與地緣政治影響，各界存在顯著的詮釋差異。德國國際與安全事務研究所（SWP）的研究指出，從「亞太」到「印太」的概念轉變，反映了地緣政治與地緣經濟空間的擴大。官方檔案中將印太地區定位為捍衛美國全球利益的核心地帶，但其地理邊界至今仍未有精確定義。這種模糊性導致各國在戰略執行與政策落實上，面臨不同的詮釋挑戰，也使得該區域在國際政治角力中扮演的角色愈發複雜。
在區域安全與媒體環境方面，亞太地區的資訊流通與外交關係亦面臨考驗。根據i24NEWS報導，國際媒體記者在報導區域議題時，曾面臨來自當局的法律訴訟與威脅，這凸顯了區域內在資訊透明度、言論自由與意識形態立場上的深刻分歧。此類衝突不僅影響媒體的報導環境，更反映了亞太地區在追求經濟整合的同時，各國在政治體制與價值觀上的摩擦，這些摩擦直接影響了跨國企業的營運安全與外交政策的制定。
針對上述議題，各界觀點存在顯著落差。支持者認為，透過TPSEP等協定及印太戰略的推動，亞太地區能有效整合資源、提升全球競爭力；然而，批評者則指出，過度強調地緣政治對抗，恐將犧牲區域內的經濟合作空間。此外，基礎建設與環境保護之間的平衡，以及航運物流在面對突發需求波動時的韌性，均是各國政策制定者必須正視的限制因素。不應過度解讀單一戰略檔案的影響力，因為區域內的實質經貿往來，往往受到市場機制與在地法規的雙重製約。
展望未來，亞太地區的發展重點將聚焦於供應鏈的重組與區域安全架構的穩定。隨著「印太」概念逐漸取代傳統的「亞太」框架，各國如何在美中博弈的夾縫中維持經貿自主，並解決如環境治理、物流效率及資訊流通等跨國公共議題，將成為觀察重點。後續應持續追蹤區域內貿易協定的擴充套件進度，以及各國在基礎建設投資與航運物流資料上的變化，以評估其對全球經濟格局的長期影響。
