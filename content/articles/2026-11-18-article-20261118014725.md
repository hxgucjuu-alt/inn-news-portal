  # NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang Resigns Amidst Geopolitical Tensions in the Strait of Hormuz and the Asia-Pacific Political and Economic Landscape
  
  > The Asia-Pacific region is facing significant upheaval. The resignation of NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang has sent shockwaves through the tech industry, while a tanker mine incident in the Strait of Hormuz has exacerbated risks to global energy and food supply chains. APEC members are closely monitoring the subsequent economic and trade impacts.
  
  Recent global geopolitical and technological sectors have experienced major tremors. First, the news of NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang’s formal resignation has drawn intense attention during the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit, with the move viewed as a critical turning point in the transformation of Silicon Valley’s corporate governance model. Simultaneously, shipping safety in the Middle East’s Strait of Hormuz has once again signaled a red alert, as the Iranian Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy confirmed that two oil tankers caught fire after striking mines, leading to a disruption in regional shipping. These events, intertwined, pose severe challenges to the stability of the Asia-Pacific technology supply chain and energy security, with the United Nations issuing a strong warning regarding the global food crisis triggered by the related conflicts.
  
  According to multiple media reports, the resignation of NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang has been confirmed, an event seen as the end of an era where founder-led personal will dominated tech giants. Analysts point out that this change is inseparable from U.S. board accountability mechanisms, differences in U.S.-China technology governance, and Washington’s strict export control policies on high-end chips. Meanwhile, the conflict in the Strait of Hormuz has also been confirmed by multiple parties; the Iranian Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy claimed that two tankers attempting to pass through the strait secretly exploded after striking mines, while two other tankers turned back after receiving warnings. United Nations data indicates that the hostilities have caused global fertilizer prices to rise by approximately 30%, and if shipping remains obstructed, it will threaten the food security of tens of millions of people worldwide.
  
  Currently, there are varying interpretations regarding the specific impact of the leadership change at NVIDIA. Some perspectives suggest that Huang’s departure symbolizes the exit of the Silicon Valley strongman leadership model under the pressures of national security red lines and transnational regulation. However, internal details regarding strategic adjustments following the succession have not yet been fully disclosed, and the market remains in a wait-and-see mode regarding whether there will be a shift in its chip export control strategy. Regarding the Strait of Hormuz incident, descriptions of the tanker mine strikes are consistent between Iranian state media and external reports, but claims regarding whether the tankers deliberately turned off their Automatic Identification System (AIS) to evade regulation remain to be further verified. Whether shipping risks in the region will escalate further remains in a state of high uncertainty.
  
  These geopolitical and technological shifts have profound implications for the Asia-Pacific region. As a global hub for technology manufacturing, the leadership change at NVIDIA may affect the layout of semiconductor supply chains and policy adjustments toward the U.S. across Asia-Pacific nations. In terms of energy and agriculture, the Strait of Hormuz serves as a vital global energy transport artery, and its security directly impacts the energy costs and food imports of Asia-Pacific countries. The United Nations warns that if supply chain disruptions persist, 45 million people globally could face severe food insecurity, and 30 million could fall into poverty. For emerging Asia-Pacific markets that are highly dependent on imported resources, this undoubtedly represents immense economic pressure, which may trigger regional inflation and social instability.
  
  Regarding the aforementioned events, there are significant differences in perspectives. Some observers believe that the changes at NVIDIA are internal corporate governance adjustments in the U.S. and should not be over-interpreted as a direct result of the U.S.-China tech war. However, other viewpoints emphasize that under multilateral mechanisms like APEC, countries should conduct economic and trade exchanges on an equal footing to counter the supply chain fragmentation brought about by unilateralism. Furthermore, regarding the crisis in the Strait of Hormuz, the United Nations has called on all parties to decouple the transport of essential goods, such as fertilizer, from military conflicts to alleviate the humanitarian disaster.
  
  The limitation lies in the fact that the international community currently lacks a binding multilateral coordination mechanism for effectively intervening in strait security and ensuring food transport channels, which leaves risk management in the relevant areas fragile.

Additionally, whether APEC member states will propose more concrete cooperation frameworks regarding technology governance and regional security in subsequent meetings will be an important indicator for assessing the future political and economic stability of the Asia-Pacific region. All parties should continue to monitor official announcements and real-time updates on shipping data to grasp the latest developments in the situation.

# 輝達執行長黃仁勳辭任與荷姆茲海峽地緣衝突之亞太政經局勢

> 亞太地區近期面臨重大變局，輝達執行長黃仁勳辭職引發科技界震撼，同時荷姆茲海峽油輪觸雷事件加劇全球能源與糧食供應鏈風險，亞太經合組織（APEC）成員正密切關注後續經貿衝擊。


近期全球地緣政治與科技產業發生重大震盪。首先，科技巨頭輝達（NVIDIA）執行長黃仁勳正式辭任的訊息，在亞太經合組織（APEC）峰會期間引發高度關注，此舉被視為矽谷企業治理模式轉型的關鍵節點。與此同時，中東荷姆茲海峽航運安全再度亮起紅燈，伊朗伊斯蘭革命衛隊海軍證實兩艘油輪因觸雷起火，導致區域航運中斷。這些事件交織在一起，對亞太地區的科技供應鏈穩定及能源安全構成嚴峻挑戰，聯合國更針對相關衝突引發的全球糧食危機發出強烈警告。
根據多方媒體報導，輝達執行長黃仁勳的辭職已獲證實，此事件被視為創辦人個人意志主導科技巨頭時代的終結。分析指出，此變動與美國董事會問責機制、中美科技治理差異以及華府對高階晶片的嚴格出口管制政策密不可分。與此同時，荷姆茲海峽的衝突亦獲得多方確認，伊朗伊斯蘭革命衛隊海軍聲稱，兩艘試圖秘密透過海峽的油輪觸雷後爆炸，另有兩艘油輪在接獲警告後折返。聯合國資料顯示，戰事已導致全球化肥價格上漲約三成，若航運持續受阻，將威脅全球數千萬人的糧食安全。
目前各界對於輝達高層異動的具體影響仍存在不同解讀。部分觀點認為，黃仁勳的離去象徵著矽谷強人領導模式在國安紅線與跨國監管壓力下的退場；然而，輝達內部對於接班後的戰略調整細節尚未完全對外揭露，市場對於其晶片出口管制策略是否會出現轉向仍持觀望態度。在荷姆茲海峽事件中，伊朗官方媒體與外部報導對於油輪觸雷的細節描述一致，但對於油輪是否刻意關閉船舶自動識別系統（AIS）以規避監管，各方說法仍有待進一步核實，該區域的航運風險是否會進一步升級，目前仍處於高度不確定狀態。
這些地緣與科技變局對亞太地區的影響極為深遠。作為全球科技製造中心，輝達的領導層異動可能牽動亞太各國半導體產業鏈的佈局與對美政策的調整。而在能源與農業層面，荷姆茲海峽作為全球能源運輸要道，其安全與否直接影響亞太國家的能源成本與糧食進口。聯合國警告，若供應鏈中斷持續，全球恐有四千五百萬人面臨嚴重糧食不安全，三千萬人陷入貧困，這對高度依賴進口資源的亞太新興市場而言，無疑是巨大的經濟壓力，恐將引發區域內的通膨與社會不穩定。
針對上述事件，各界觀點存在顯著差異。部分觀察家認為，輝達的變動是美國企業內部的治理調整，不應過度解讀為中美科技戰的直接結果；然而，另有觀點強調，在APEC等多邊機制下，各國應在平等基礎上進行經貿交流，以對抗單邊主義帶來的供應鏈斷裂。此外，針對荷姆茲海峽的危機，聯合國呼籲各方應將化肥等民生物資的運輸與軍事衝突脫鉤，以緩解人道災難。
限制在於，目前國際社會對於如何有效介入海峽安全、保障糧食運輸通道，尚缺乏具備約束力的多邊協調機制，這使得相關地區的風險管理依然脆弱。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於幾個維度：首先是輝達新任領導層對華府出口管制政策的具體回應，以及其對亞太供應鏈合作夥伴的政策調整；其次是荷姆茲海峽的局勢演變，特別是聯合國的呼籲是否能促使相關國家達成臨時航運協議，以緩解化肥與能源供應緊張。此外，APEC成員國在後續會議中，是否會針對科技治理與區域安全議題提出更具體的合作框架，將是評估亞太地區未來政經穩定度的重要指標。各界應持續關注相關官方宣告與航運資料的即時更新，以掌握局勢的最新動態。
