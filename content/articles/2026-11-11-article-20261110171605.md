  # Lisbon Web Summit Focuses on AI Embodiment; Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Caught in National Security Investigation
  
  > The global tech event "Web Summit" has kicked off in Lisbon, exploring physical AI applications and European strategy. Meanwhile, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang is facing an independent investigation by the board of directors due to an extramarital affair involving national security risks.
  
  On November 9, 2026, the Web Summit, a premier global technology event, officially opened in Lisbon, the capital of Portugal. The four-day event is expected to attract over 70,000 tech leaders, entrepreneurs, investors, and policymakers. The core themes of the conference focus on the physical application of artificial intelligence (AI), including the rise of humanoid robots and autonomous AI agents. It also delves into the impact of automation on the global job market, as well as Europe’s digital sovereignty and strategic positioning in the global tech race amid the dominance of the U.S. and China.
  
  At the same time, the tech industry is facing a crisis of public trust. Recent polls indicate that a majority of Americans hold a negative view of AI development and express strong resistance to the expansion of data centers.
  
  Regarding industry progress, AI technology is accelerating its transition from cloud computing to edge AI and physical environments. Taiwanese supply chain companies, such as Atech Technology, have demonstrated explosive operational growth and are actively expanding into overseas markets. However, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has recently become embroiled in a major controversy. Huang has sent a letter to the company admitting to an extramarital affair with Alice Huang, a former executive in the Singaporean server and cloud industry.
  
  Because Alice Huang has been identified by U.S. intelligence agencies as a spy recruited by China’s Ministry of State Security (MSS), the Nvidia board of directors has officially launched an independent investigation to assess whether Jensen Huang’s personal relationship has exposed the company to risks of being targeted by foreign intelligence circles.
  
  Currently, there are significant discrepancies in the details provided by various sources regarding the incident, particularly concerning the connection between Jensen Huang’s personal whereabouts and national security risks, which remains subject to further verification. According to media reports, Jensen Huang and Alice Huang have met privately in Taipei multiple times in recent years, including interactions at nightclubs and private dining rooms. These details have only appeared in certain media reports and have not been publicly confirmed by Nvidia regarding specific meeting locations. Furthermore, the specific evidence regarding the allegations that Alice Huang is a Chinese spy is currently at the stage of being cited by various parties from intelligence agency claims and has not yet entered judicial proceedings.
  
  This incident has a direct impact on the global tech industry and geopolitics. As a leader in AI chips, the national security investigation into Nvidia's CEO may trigger market concerns regarding supply chain security and data privacy, subsequently affecting the commercial viability assessments of global AI investments. Meanwhile, the negative public perception of AI in the U.S. and the backlash against data centers (with 72% of respondents opposing the construction of data centers in their communities) are creating significant public resistance to the expansion of tech infrastructure. These social pressures and the integrity crisis of corporate leadership may force tech giants to re-examine their corporate governance and social communication strategies while pushing for innovation.
  
  Although the Web Summit has showcased breakthroughs in AI technology, such as in the field of humanoid robots, one should avoid over-interpreting the linear relationship between technological development and social acceptance. Current public concerns regarding AI stem primarily from resource consumption and security risks rather than the technology itself. Furthermore, the Jensen Huang case is still in the board investigation stage; the public should not equate his personal behavior with the safety of Nvidia’s technical products before the investigation report is released. The development of the tech industry benefits from technological innovation but is also constrained by geopolitics and social trust; both are indispensable.
  
  Future observations will focus on three areas: first, the results of the Nvidia board’s investigation into the Jensen Huang case and its potential impact on corporate governance and stock price; second, the policy recommendations released following the Lisbon Web Summit, particularly Europe’s specific actions regarding AI sovereignty; and finally, whether the backlash from the American public against data centers will evolve into stricter land-use regulations, thereby limiting the construction speed of global AI computing infrastructure.

# 里斯本網路峰會聚焦AI實體化，輝達執行長黃仁勳陷國安調查風波

> 全球科技盛會「網路峰會」於里斯本揭幕，探討AI實體應用與歐洲戰略；與此同時，輝達執行長黃仁勳因婚外情物件涉及國安風險，遭董事會啟動獨立調查。


2026年11月9日，全球科技指標盛會「網路峰會（Web Summit）」於葡萄牙首都里斯本正式開幕。本次活動預計為期四天，吸引超過7萬名科技領袖、企業家、投資者及政策制定者參與。會議核心議題聚焦於人工智慧（AI）的實體化應用，包含人形機器人與自主AI代理（Agent）的崛起，並深入探討自動化對全球就業市場的衝擊，以及歐洲在全球科技競賽中，面對美中主導局面下的數位主權與戰略定位。
與此同時，科技產業亦面臨公眾信任危機，最新民調顯示多數美國民眾對AI發展持負面態度，且對資料中心擴建抱持強烈抗拒。
在產業進展方面，AI技術正加速從雲端運算走向邊緣端（Edge AI）與實體場域，臺灣相關供應鏈如和椿科技等已展現營運爆發力，積極拓展海外市場。然而，科技巨擘輝達（Nvidia）執行長黃仁勳近期卻陷入重大爭議。黃仁勳已向公司內部發出信函，坦承與新加坡伺服器與雲端業前高層黃樂（Alice Huang）存在婚外情。
由於黃樂被美國情報機構點名為中國國家安全部（MSS）吸收的間諜，輝達董事會已正式啟動獨立調查，評估黃仁勳是否因私人關係而使公司暴露於外國情報圈的鎖定風險中。
目前各來源對於事件的細節描述存在顯著差異，特別是關於黃仁勳的私人行蹤與國安風險的關聯性，仍待進一步查證。根據媒體報導，黃仁勳與黃樂近年曾多次在臺北進行私人會面，包括在夜店小酌及私廚包廂互動，這些行蹤細節目前僅見於部分媒體披露，尚未獲得輝達官方針對具體約會地點的公開證實。此外，關於黃樂被指控為中國間諜的具體證據，目前亦處於各界引述情報機構說法的階段，尚未進入司法審判程式。
此事件對全球科技產業與地緣政治產生直接影響。輝達作為AI晶片龍頭，其執行長的國安調查恐引發市場對供應鏈安全性與資料隱私的擔憂，進而影響全球AI投資的商業可行性評估。同時，美國民眾對AI的負面觀感與對資料中心的反對聲浪（高達72%受訪者反對在社群興建資料中心），正為科技基礎設施的擴張帶來巨大的民意阻力。這些社會壓力與企業高層的誠信危機，可能迫使科技巨頭在推動創新時，必須重新審視企業治理與社會溝通策略。
儘管網路峰會展現了AI技術在人形機器人等領域的突破，但應避免過度解讀技術發展與社會接納度之間的線性關係。目前公眾對AI的疑慮主要源於資源消耗與安全風險，而非技術本身。此外，黃仁勳案目前仍處於董事會調查階段，外界不應在調查報告出爐前，將其私人行為與輝達的技術產品安全性直接劃上等號。科技產業的發展既受惠於技術創新，也受制於地緣政治與社會信任，兩者缺一不可。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於三方面：首先是輝達董事會針對黃仁勳案的調查結果，及其對公司治理與股價的潛在影響；其次為里斯本網路峰會後續發布的政策建議，特別是歐洲針對AI主權的具體行動；最後則是觀察美國民眾對資料中心的反彈，是否會演變為更嚴格的土地使用法規，進而限制全球AI算力基礎設施的建設速度。
