澳洲右翼政黨「一國黨」（One Nation）近期對特定新聞機構實施採訪禁令，引發各界對政治干預與新聞自由的關注。相關報導顯示，該黨黨魁寶琳·韓森（Pauline Hanson）與黨部主管詹姆士·艾許比（James Ashby）的相關措施引發爭議，促使澳洲廣播公司（ABC）及《衛報》（The Guardian）公開譴責艾許比的言論，並呼籲該黨撤銷這項針對媒體的限制措施。

根據澳洲獨立媒體《Crikey》的深入調查與訪談，報導引述了多位澳洲資深政治記者、媒體高層以及《紐約客》（The New Yorker）記者蘇珊·格拉瑟（Susan Glasser）的觀點，探討政客對新聞自由的施壓行為，並質疑澳洲主流媒體對此類打壓為何缺乏集體的集體抵抗與反制行動。

業界內部人士在報導中透露，部分媒體過度依賴政治圈的「放話」（drops）或獨家消息來源，可能是導致媒體同業在面對政客單方面封鎖同行時保持沉默的原因之一。相關爭議持續引發澳洲社會對於政治權力限縮報導自由與新聞審查風險的討論。

## English edition

The Australian right-wing political party One Nation has sparked controversy over press freedom after implementing a media ban targeting specific news organizations. Reports indicate that party leader Pauline Hanson and party director James Ashby have faced criticism, with both the Australian Broadcasting Corporation (ABC) and The Guardian condemning Ashby's comments and officially calling on the party to reverse the ban.

Australian independent media outlet Crikey conducted an investigation featuring perspectives from senior political journalists, media executives, and The New Yorker's Susan Glasser. The reporting explores politicians' mounting pressure on the free press and examines why the Australian media landscape appears to lack a collective resistance against such restrictions.

According to industry insiders cited in the reports, an ongoing addiction to political leaks and background drops may explain why some media outlets remain largely silent when peer organizations are blacklisted. The development continues to fuel debates in Australia over political interference and censorship concerns targeting independent journalism.
