  # AI Technological Evolution and Geopolitical Security: Challenges in Mathematics and Regional Defense Strategies
  
  > Two major focal points have recently emerged in the global technology sector: OpenAI's release of numerous mathematical manuscripts has sparked academic skepticism regarding AI's comprehension capabilities, while the threat of AI-agent-driven cyberattacks has prompted Taiwanese enterprises to accelerate the deployment of "AI-versus-AI" defense mechanisms, reflecting a crisis of trust and cybersecurity challenges amidst rapid technological iteration.
  
  Since entering 2026, artificial intelligence technology has demonstrated vastly different impacts across academic research and industrial applications. In academia, OpenAI recently released 722 mathematical manuscripts, claiming to have solved Millennium Prize problems, a move that has caused significant upheaval in the mathematics community. However, mathematician Terence Tao remains reserved, emphasizing that while AI can provide precise answers, it fails to offer the deep understanding required by humans.
  
  Simultaneously, the global cybersecurity environment has become strained due to the "PHP2Shell" vulnerability and automated cyberattacks launched by AI agents. In response to this wave of threats, the Taiwanese business sector, led by Able Innovation’s subsidiary Hong-Ling and Cheng-Yun Technology, is promoting a zero-trust endpoint management solution integrated with an "AI SOC" (Security Operations Center) platform, attempting to gain an advantage in the digital defense war.
  
  Multiple media outlets and industry reports have confirmed that the rapid evolution of AI technology has officially entered an "adversarial" phase. In the field of cybersecurity, the collaboration between Hong-Ling and Cheng-Yun Technology clearly indicates that AI agents have become the primary tools for cyberattacks, forcing enterprises to adopt automated proactive defense networks. In the field of mathematics, the 722 manuscripts released by OpenAI have indeed triggered panic in the academic community; not only are graduate students worried about future career prospects, but senior mathematicians have also begun to adopt a closed-research stance out of fear that their findings might be misappropriated or misled by AI.
  
  These events point to a core fact: while AI has reached a critical threshold in its efficiency for handling complex logic and automated tasks, the social and academic trust crises it brings are expanding in tandem.
  
  Regarding the OpenAI manuscript incident, there are significant discrepancies in various accounts. According to reports, OpenAI retracted three of the manuscripts the day after they were published, a move that deepened academic skepticism regarding the reliability of its technology. The "Mathematics 2.0" concept proposed by Terence Tao in an interview stands in stark contrast to the "AI problem-solving efficiency" pursued by OpenAI. Meanwhile, in the cybersecurity sector, although Hong-Ling and Cheng-Yun Technology emphasize that their "AI SOC" platform can effectively defend against AI cyberattacks, there remains technical uncertainty in the market regarding whether such defense solutions can fully withstand the constantly evolving "PHP2Shell" attacks.
  
  Furthermore, geopolitical factors in the Eurasian region—such as the situation in Afghanistan affecting stability in Central Asia, and China's strategic involvement in the Arctic—while following different paths from technological development, have indirectly influenced the allocation of resources for global technology supply chains and cybersecurity defense.
  
  For Taiwanese enterprises, the direct impact of this cybersecurity crisis lies in the urgency of budget allocation. As Hong-Ling and Cheng-Yun Technology integrate their defense solutions into over 40,000 enterprises, Taiwan's cybersecurity industry chain is accelerating its transformation into an "AI-versus-AI" model. This model is not merely a technical upgrade, but a survival strategy. At the academic level, Terence Tao's concerns reflect the need for the global education system to rethink the essence of mathematics education—shifting from simple "problem-solving" to "understanding and innovation"—which has profound implications for the cultivation of top-tier scientific research talent in the Eurasian region.
  
  However, the public and relevant industries should not oversimplify the progress and threats of AI. Terence Tao's perspective reminds us that AI's "answers" are not equivalent to "knowledge." Over-reliance on AI-generated mathematical proofs could lead to the degradation of human mathematical research capabilities. Similarly, in the cybersecurity field, while "AI-versus-AI" is the current popular solution, it does not mean that a single technical solution can resolve all structural vulnerabilities.
  
  Regarding geopolitical analysis of the Eurasian region, such as the regional conflicts and Arctic competition mentioned by the BBC and Eurasia Review, these issues have no direct causal link to AI technological development. However, they collectively constitute an unstable global environment, necessitating that technology deployment take into account more geopolitical risk variables.
  
  Subsequent observations should focus on three aspects: First, whether OpenAI can provide specific technical explanations regarding the retracted manuscripts to restore academic trust; second, whether the defense effectiveness of Taiwanese enterprises after deploying the "AI SOC" can effectively reduce the actual damage caused by the "PHP2Shell" vulnerability; and finally, whether geopolitical shifts in the Eurasian region—including disputes over Arctic affairs and security dynamics in Central Asia—will further affect the stability of the global technology hardware supply chain. These developments will determine the future balance point for AI applications in terms of academic depth and industrial security.

# AI技術演進與地緣安全：數學界挑戰與區域防禦策略

> 近期全球科技領域出現兩大焦點：OpenAI公佈大量數學手稿引發學界對AI理解能力的質疑，同時，AI代理人網攻威脅促使臺灣企業加速部署「AI對抗AI」的防禦機制，反映出技術迭代下的信任危機與資安挑戰。


人工智慧技術在進入2026年後，於學術研究與產業應用層面展現出截然不同的影響力。在學術界，OpenAI近期釋出722篇數學手稿，宣稱已解出千禧年難題，此舉在數學界引發劇烈震盪。然而，數學家陶哲軒對此持保留態度，強調AI雖能提供精確答案，卻無法提供人類所需的深度理解。
與此同時，全球資安環境因「PHP2Shell」漏洞及AI代理人發起的自動化網攻而陷入緊張，臺灣企業界亦針對此波威脅，由能率創新旗下宏羚與誠雲科技聯手，推動結合「AI SOC」人工智慧輔助平臺的零信任端點管理方案，試圖在數位防禦戰中取得先機。
多家媒體與產業報告共同確認，AI技術的快速演進已正式進入「對抗性」階段。在資安領域，宏羚與誠雲科技的合作案明確指出，AI代理人已成為網攻的主要工具，迫使企業必須採取自動化主動防禦網來應對。在數學領域，OpenAI釋出的722篇手稿確實引發了學術界的恐慌，不僅研究生對未來的教職前景感到擔憂，資深數學家亦因擔心研究成果遭AI挪用或誤導，開始採取封閉研究的態度。
這些事件共同指向一個核心事實：AI在處理複雜邏輯與自動化任務的效率已達臨界點，但其帶來的社會與學術信任危機正同步擴大。
關於OpenAI的手稿事件，各界說法存在顯著差異。根據報導，OpenAI在公佈手稿的隔天便撤回了其中三篇，這一舉動加深了學界對其技術可靠性的懷疑。陶哲軒在專訪中提出的「數學2.0」概念，與OpenAI追求的「AI解題效率」形成強烈對比。而在資安領域，儘管宏羚與誠雲科技強調其「AI SOC」平臺能有效防禦AI網攻，但市場對於此類防禦方案能否完全抵禦不斷演變的「PHP2Shell」攻擊，仍存在技術評估上的不確定性。
此外，歐亞地區的地緣政治因素——如阿富汗局勢對中亞穩定、以及中國在北極地區的戰略參與——雖與科技發展路徑不同，但這些區域性的動盪與擴張，亦間接影響了全球科技供應鏈與資安防禦的資源分配。
對於臺灣企業而言，此次資安危機的直接影響在於預算分配的急迫性。隨著宏羚與誠雲科技將防禦方案匯入逾4萬家企業，臺灣的資安產業鏈正加速轉型為「以AI對抗AI」的模式。這種模式不僅是技術升級，更是一種生存策略。而在學術層面，陶哲軒的擔憂反映出全球教育體系必須重新思考數學教育的本質，即如何從單純的「解題」轉向「理解與創新」，這對未來歐亞地區的頂尖科研人才培養具有深遠的影響。
然而，公眾與相關產業不應將AI的進展與威脅過度簡化。陶哲軒的觀點提醒我們，AI的「答案」不等於「知識」，若過度依賴AI生成的數學論證，可能導致人類數學研究能力的退化。同樣地，在資安領域，雖然「AI對抗AI」是當前的熱門解方，但這並不代表單一技術方案能解決所有結構性漏洞。
對於歐亞地區的地緣政治分析，如BBC與Eurasia Review所提及的區域衝突與北極競爭，這些議題與AI科技發展雖無直接因果，但它們共同構成了一個不穩定的全球環境，使得科技部署必須考慮到更多地緣政治的風險變數。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於三個面向：首先，OpenAI是否能針對撤回手稿事件提出具體的技術說明，以恢復學界信任；其次，臺灣企業在部署「AI SOC」後的防禦成效，是否能有效降低「PHP2Shell」漏洞帶來的實際損害；最後，歐亞地區的地緣政治變動——包括北極事務的爭議與中亞安全域性勢——是否會進一步影響全球科技硬體供應鏈的穩定性。這些發展將決定未來AI應用在學術深度與產業安全性上的平衡點。
