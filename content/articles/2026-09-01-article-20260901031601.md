  # Ma On Shan Tai Po Memorial School Secures Funding to Transition to Private Institution; Teacher Shortages in Hualien Spark Educational Concerns
  
  > The Wu Clan Association Tai Po Memorial School in Ma On Shan, which faced a "zero-class" allocation due to insufficient enrollment, has secured HK$12 million in funding to transition into a private primary school. Simultaneously, Hualien County is facing a shortage of nearly 50 substitute teachers, sparking public discussion regarding the retention of educators in rural areas.
  
  The Wu Clan Association Tai Po Memorial School in Ma On Shan previously faced a crisis of potential closure after being assigned "zero classes" by the Education Bureau due to insufficient enrollment. However, on August 31, 2026, the school officially announced that it had received HK$12 million in funding from a "white knight," allowing it to transition into a private primary school and attempt to continue its educational mission through an extra-institutional operating model.
  
  At the same time, structural issues regarding teaching staff have emerged in Taiwan's education sector. As the new semester began in Hualien County, statistics indicated a shortage of nearly 50 substitute teachers. Independent Hualien County Magistrate candidate Chang Chun publicly called on the county government to address the issue of teacher recruitment and proposed more attractive retention incentives for rural areas, highlighting that both regions are facing severe resource and human capital challenges under their respective educational systems.
  
  According to various reports and public information from the school, the transition plan for the Wu Clan Association Tai Po Memorial School is now in the implementation phase. Principal Hou Tat-sun stated in a media interview that the school remains positive and optimistic about its private operations and has set clear enrollment targets. Although the current number of enrolled students has not yet reached the target of 16, the school emphasized that the current focus is on using reforms and community communication to ensure parents and the public understand the school's future operating model. The school views this transition as an opportunity for long-term development and is focusing on its 2027 enrollment plan, hoping to establish a stable teaching environment through continuous reform.
  
  However, regarding the details and implementation of information, there are still differing interpretations across various sectors concerning the allocation and demand for educational resources. Taking the Tai Po Memorial School in Hong Kong as an example, despite receiving HK$12 million in private funding, its long-term viability under a private system remains dependent on subsequent enrollment figures and financial transparency. In the case of Hualien, Taiwan, the shortage of substitute teachers reflects structural deficiencies in the public sector's teacher planning. Chang Chun pointed out that the Hualien County government should prioritize addressing the current difficulties in recruiting teachers for rural areas and suggested starting with housing, transportation subsidies, and living support to increase teacher retention incentives, rather than merely focusing on filling vacancies.
  
  Although the two cases involve different subjects, both highlight the survival pressures and resource integration challenges faced by educational institutions when confronted with declining birth rates or urban-rural disparities.
  
  This incident has had a direct impact on the relevant education industries and regional policies. In Hong Kong, privatization may become an alternative for schools facing "school killing" crises, but it may also increase the financial burden on parents. In Taiwan, if the teacher shortage in Hualien is not effectively resolved, it will directly affect the educational rights of rural students and the stability of instruction. Chang Chun's appeal is not only aimed at the current teacher shortage but also demands that the county government conduct a comprehensive review of educational resources. For rural areas, high teacher turnover has always been a key factor affecting learning outcomes; if policy measures cannot be used to improve retention incentives, short-term recruitment measures may struggle to solve long-term human resource gaps.
  
  Furthermore, there are significant differences in perspectives regarding educational reform. Some education experts warn against over-interpreting the success or failure of a single school's transition, as private operating models are highly uncertain and may exclude families who cannot afford tuition. Similarly, regarding the teacher issue in Hualien, although there are calls to increase retention incentives, how to implement specific housing and transportation subsidies remains subject to local fiscal and geographical constraints, requiring concrete policy planning and budget support. Over-simplifying the problem or relying solely on external funding is unlikely to fundamentally improve educational quality; the socio-economic background and demographic changes of different regions must be considered.
  
  Future observations will focus on two indicators: first, the enrollment progress of the Ma On Shan Tai Po Memorial School after its transition to a private institution and whether it can meet its teaching goals for the 2027 academic year, which will serve as an important reference for other schools in Hong Kong facing enrollment crises; second, the follow-up remedial measures taken by the Hualien County government regarding the shortage of substitute teachers and whether it can provide a concrete policy response to the rural retention incentives proposed by Chang Chun. These educational issues reflect deep-seated social anxiety regarding the fairness and sustainability of teaching resources; how to strike a balance between institutional reform and resource allocation will remain a focus for all sectors.

# 馬鞍山泰伯紀念學校獲注資轉型私立，花蓮師資短缺引發教育界關注

> 因收生不足遭派「0班」的馬鞍山吳氏宗親總會泰伯紀念學校，獲1200萬元資金挹注轉型私立小一；與此同時，花蓮縣面臨近50名代理教師缺額，偏鄉師資留任問題引發社會討論。


馬鞍山吳氏宗親總會泰伯紀念學校此前因面臨收生不足的困境，遭教育局派發「0班」，面臨停辦危機。然而在2026年8月31日，該校正式對外宣佈獲得一名「白武士」注資1200萬元，將轉型開辦私立小一課程，試圖透過體制外的營運模式延續教學使命。
與此同時，臺灣教育現場亦傳出師資結構性問題，花蓮縣新學期開學之際，統計顯示有近50名代理教師缺額，無黨籍花蓮縣長參選人張峻公開呼籲縣府應正視師資補實問題，並針對偏鄉地區提出更具吸引力的留任配套措施，顯示兩地在不同教育體制下，均面臨嚴峻的資源與人力挑戰。
根據多方報導與校方公開資訊，馬鞍山吳氏宗親總會泰伯紀念學校的轉型計畫已進入執行階段。校長侯達燊在接受媒體訪問時表示，校方對於轉型後的私立營運抱持積極樂觀態度，並已設定明確的收生目標。雖然目前尚未達到預期的16名學生入學人數，但校方強調，目前的重點在於透過改革與社群溝通，讓家長與社會大眾瞭解學校未來的運作模式。校方將此次轉型視為長遠發展的契機，並將目光鎖定在2027年度的收生計畫，期望透過持續的改革，建立穩定的教學環境。
然而，在資訊細節與執行層面上，各界對於教育資源的分配與需求仍存在不同解讀。以香港的泰伯紀念學校為例，儘管獲得了1200萬元的私人資金支援，但其能否在私立體制下長期維持營運，仍取決於後續的收生狀況與財務透明度；而在臺灣花蓮的案例中，代理教師缺額問題則反映了公部門在師資規劃上的結構性缺失。張峻指出，花蓮縣政府目前應優先處理偏鄉師資招募困難的現狀，並建議從住宿、交通補助及生活支援等面向著手，提升教師留任誘因，而非僅止於補足缺額。
兩者雖然針對的物件不同，但皆凸顯了教育機構在面臨少子化或城鄉差距時，所面臨的生存壓力與資源整合挑戰。
此次事件對相關教育產業及地區政策產生了直接的影響。在香港，私立化轉型可能成為面臨「殺校」危機學校的一種替代方案，但也可能加劇家長在教育支出上的負擔；在臺灣，花蓮地區的師資短缺若未能有效解決，將直接影響偏鄉學童的受教權與教學穩定性。張峻的呼籲，不僅是針對當前的師資缺額問題，更進一步要求縣府進行整體性的教育資源檢視。對於偏鄉地區而言，師資的流動率過高一直是影響學習成效的關鍵因素，若無法透過政策手段提升留任誘因，短期內的補實措施恐難以解決長期的人力缺口問題。
此外，各界對於教育改革的觀點亦存在顯著差異。部分教育專家提醒，不應過度解讀單一學校的轉型成功與否，因為私立化營運模式具有高度不確定性，且可能排擠到無法負擔學費的家庭。同樣地，對於花蓮的師資問題，儘管各界呼籲應增加留任誘因，但受限於地方財政與地理條件，如何落實具體的住宿與交通補貼，仍需具體的政策規劃與預算支援。過度簡化問題或單純依賴外部資金，恐難以從根本上改善教育品質，必須考量到不同地區的社會經濟背景與人口結構變化。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於兩項指標：首先是馬鞍山泰伯紀念學校在轉型私立後的收生進度，以及其能否在2027年度達到設定的教學目標，這將成為香港其他面臨收生危機學校的重要參考指標；其次是花蓮縣政府針對代理教師缺額所採取的後續補救措施，以及是否能針對張峻提出的偏鄉留任誘因進行具體的政策回應。這些教育議題反映了當前社會對於教學資源公平性與永續性的深層焦慮，如何在體制改革與資源分配之間取得平衡，將是未來各界持續關注的焦點。
