  # Political Turmoil in Eurasia and Technological Industry Transformation: Amazon Layoffs and Escalating Cybersecurity Threats
  
  > The Eurasian region is currently facing multiple challenges, with protests erupting in Germany demanding the resignation of Chancellor Merz, and concerns rising over the internal power structure of the Turkmen regime. Simultaneously, the tech industry is grappling with massive layoffs at Amazon and a warning from Microsoft regarding a new "ASCII smuggling" fraud, as industrial resources accelerate their shift toward artificial intelligence computing power.
  
  Germany is currently facing severe domestic political pressure. According to relevant information, large-scale demonstrations have broken out locally, with citizens publicly demanding the immediate resignation of the government led by Chancellor Friedrich Merz and calling for early parliamentary elections. Protesters are also advocating for peaceful resolutions to various international conflicts, including the situation in Ukraine.
  
  Meanwhile, political concerns have emerged in Turkmenistan in Central Asia. Analysis from the Carnegie Russia Eurasia Center indicates that a "father-son faction" power struggle has surfaced within the country's ruling class. As many high-ranking officials have served across the administrations of former leader Gurbanguly and current President Serdar, the stability and power distribution within the regime are becoming a key focus for observing Eurasian geopolitics.
  
  In the technology sector, Amazon initiated a new round of layoffs on October 7, 2026. According to various media reports, the cumulative number of layoffs at Amazon over the past year has exceeded 30,000, with middle-management positions accounting for 70% of the total. The scope of these layoffs is extensive, covering customer service, platform support, and retail engineering departments, with affected regions including teams in India and the UK. An official Amazon spokesperson has confirmed these workforce reduction measures to Business Insider.
  
  Simultaneously, the Microsoft security research team has issued an urgent warning, noting that hackers are extensively utilizing "ASCII Smuggling" techniques to hide malicious payloads within invisible Unicode tags. The number of attack emails using this method has exceeded 2.37 million per day. Due to its high level of stealth, traditional antivirus software finds it difficult to detect.
  
  Regarding the specific details of Amazon's layoffs, media reports have confirmed the scale and departmental distribution; however, regarding the total number of people affected, some reports indicate that this round of layoffs involves fewer than 1,000 people. Nevertheless, when compared with the cumulative data of 30,000 over the past year, it indicates that the company is undergoing long-term resource restructuring. Regarding the protests in Germany, although public images and information show high levels of public dissatisfaction with the government, specific statistics on the scale of the demonstrations and the government's subsequent formal response are still in the stage of information gathering and await confirmation from more official sources.
  
  Furthermore, whether the power struggle in Turkmenistan will directly affect the country's energy exports and Eurasian logistics infrastructure remains limited to think-tank analysis, with no signs of concrete policy changes yet.
  
  These events have had a profound impact on the global industry and society. Amazon is redirecting the budget saved from layoffs toward AI computing infrastructure, with capital expenditures reaching an all-time high, signaling that tech giants are reshaping their competitiveness with AI at the core. Meanwhile, to meet developers' demand for computing power, Google has officially integrated Colab benefits into the Google AI subscription plan, supporting background computing and cumulative computing power, reflecting that the cloud computing industry is entering a full-scale "AI computing power race."
  
  However, the ASCII smuggling fraud revealed by Microsoft highlights that during the development of AI and digital transformation, cybersecurity defense systems are facing unprecedented challenges. Invisible character technology can not only paralyze email systems but is also being used for AI prompt injection attacks, posing a major threat to global corporate information security.
  
  Despite the clear trends of tech industry layoffs and AI transformation, market analysts warn that layoffs should not be simply interpreted as corporate decline, but rather as a structural adjustment of resource allocation. Regarding the political movements in Germany and Turkmenistan, it is also inappropriate to over-interpret a single protest or think-tank report, as the geopolitical situation in the Eurasian region is extremely complex, involving the linkage of energy supply chains and foreign policy. Any change within a regime could have long-term impacts on cross-border logistics and energy security; therefore, it is necessary to maintain a neutral observation and avoid inflating sporadic events into omens of regional collapse.

# 歐亞政局動盪與科技產業變革：亞馬遜裁員與資安威脅加劇

> 近期歐亞地區面臨多重挑戰，德國爆發要求總理梅爾茨下臺的示威，土庫曼政權內部權力結構引發關注；同時，科技業面臨亞馬遜大規模裁員與微軟示警新型 ASCII 走私詐騙，產業資源正加速向人工智慧算力傾斜。


德國近期面臨嚴峻的國內政治壓力，根據相關資訊顯示，當地爆發大規模示威活動，民眾公開要求總理弗里德里希·梅爾茨（Friedrich Merz）領導的政府立即總辭，並主張提前舉行國會大選。示威者同時訴求和平解決包含烏克蘭在內的各項國際衝突。
與此同時，中亞地區的土庫曼也傳出政治隱憂，卡內基俄羅斯歐亞中心分析指出，該國統治階層內部出現「父子派系」權力傾軋，由於許多高階官員的職涯跨越了前任領導人庫爾班古力與現任總統謝爾達爾的執政期，政權內部的穩定性與權力分配正成為歐亞區域地緣政治的觀察重點。
在科技產業方面，亞馬遜（Amazon）於 2026 年 10 月 7 日啟動了新一輪裁員計畫。綜合多方媒體報導，亞馬遜在過去一年內累計裁員人數已突破三萬人，其中中階主管職位佔比高達七成。此次裁員波及範圍廣泛，涵蓋客服、平臺支援及零售工程部門，受影響區域包括印度與英國團隊。亞馬遜官方發言人已向 Business Insider 證實此項精簡人力措施。
與此同時，微軟安全研究團隊亦發布緊急警示，指出駭客正大規模利用「ASCII 走私」（ASCII Smuggling）技術，將惡意載荷隱藏於不可見的 Unicode 標籤中，該手法單日攻擊信件已突破 237 萬封，由於其隱匿性極高，傳統防毒軟體難以偵測。
針對亞馬遜裁員的具體細節，各媒體報導均確認裁員規模與部門分佈，但在受影響的具體總人數上，部分報導指出本輪裁員人數不到 1000 人，然而與過去一年累積三萬人的資料對照，顯示該公司正進行長期的資源重整。關於德國示威活動，雖然公開影像與資訊顯示民眾對政府的不滿情緒高漲，但對於示威規模的具體統計與政府後續的正式回應，目前仍處於各方資訊彙整階段，尚待更多官方資料確認。
此外，土庫曼的權力鬥爭是否會直接影響該國的能源出口與歐亞物流基建，目前僅止於智庫分析階段，尚未有具體政策變動的跡象。
這些事件對全球產業與社會產生了深遠影響。亞馬遜將裁員省下的預算轉向 AI 算力基礎設施，資本支出已達歷史新高，顯示科技巨頭正以 AI 為核心重塑競爭力。與此同時，Google 為了滿足開發者對算力的需求，正式將 Colab 權益併入 Google AI 訂閱方案，支援背景運算與算力累加，反映出雲端運算產業正全面進入「AI 算力競賽」階段。
然而，微軟揭露的 ASCII 走私詐騙，突顯了在 AI 發展與數位轉型過程中，資安防禦體系正遭受前所未有的挑戰，隱形字元技術不僅能癱瘓郵件系統，更被用於 AI 提示詞注入攻擊，對全球企業資訊安全構成重大威脅。
儘管科技業裁員與 AI 轉型趨勢明顯，但市場分析師提醒，不應將裁員簡單解讀為企業衰退，而應視為資源配置的結構性調整。對於德國與土庫曼的政治動向，外界亦不宜過度解讀單一示威或智庫報告，因為歐亞地區的地緣政治局勢極為複雜，涉及能源供應鏈與外交政策的連動，任何政權內部的變動都可能對跨境物流與能源安全產生長遠影響，需保持中性觀察，避免將零星事件擴大為區域性崩潰的預兆。
後續觀察重點將聚焦於以下三個面向：首先是亞馬遜在 AI 算力投入後的實質營收回饋，以及裁員後對組織運作的長期影響；其次是微軟與資安界針對 ASCII 走私攻擊的防禦技術更新進度，以及該技術是否會引發更大規模的全球數位基礎設施癱瘓；最後則是歐亞地區的政治穩定性，特別是德國政府對民意反彈的處理方式，以及土庫曼權力結構演變對中亞能源輸歐路徑的潛在衝擊。
