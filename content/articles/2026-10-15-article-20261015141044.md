  # Geopolitical Shifts and Regional Security: From the Situation in Ukraine to East Asian Defense Polls
  
  > As global geopolitics enter a new normal, the shift in U.S. policy toward Ukraine has sparked concerns regarding regional security commitments. Simultaneously, domestic Japanese polling data on the defense of the Taiwan Strait has become a key indicator for Beijing to interpret regional military deployments and political expectations.
  
  The global geopolitical landscape has shifted dramatically in recent times. U.S. President Donald Trump has publicly questioned the political future of Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, arguing that Ukraine should elect a leader better positioned to reach an agreement with Russia. This move has not only rewritten the international community's traditional understanding of "U.S. support" but has also triggered widespread discussion in Taiwan regarding the reliability of "U.S. commitments." Meanwhile, the latest poll released by the Japan Press Research Institute shows that as many as 80% of Japanese citizens oppose the Self-Defense Forces directly participating in a conflict in the Taiwan Strait. This data was immediately cited by the Chinese state-run media outlet *Global Times* to criticize the military expansion expectations held by certain Japanese politicians and Taiwan's Democratic Progressive Party government regarding the notion that "a Taiwan contingency is a Japan contingency."
  
  According to reports and analyses from various media outlets, the adjustment of U.S. policy toward Ukraine has become a focal point of international political observation. Professor Weng Li-chung, based in the U.S., pointed out that Trump's remarks reveal that in U.S. foreign policy, considerations of realistic interests often take precedence over traditional alliance commitments, which serves as a major warning for Taiwan. Taiwanese society and policymakers are currently re-examining whether Taiwan possesses sufficient autonomous defense capabilities in the event of a shift in U.S. interests. At the same time, public opinion trends within Japan indicate that although the government emphasizes Taiwan Strait security at a strategic level, the public remains highly reserved about direct intervention in military conflicts, adding uncertainty to the practical implementation of regional security cooperation.
  
  In terms of information presentation, there are significant differences in how various sources interpret these events. China's *Global Times* views the Japanese poll as a powerful rebuttal to the "Taiwan contingency" narrative, using it to question the lack of a realistic basis for the Taiwan government's expectations of Japanese defense assistance. However, the Japanese poll itself only reflects the public's willingness to "directly participate in war" and does not entirely negate Japan's other roles in regional security. Furthermore, whether the U.S. policy shift toward Ukraine will be directly replicated in East Asia remains in the realm of political analysis; there are no specific policy documents or actions confirming that the U.S. will fully withdraw support for Taiwan. Related discussions are mostly focused on the levels of strategic autonomy and risk management.
  
  These geopolitical fluctuations are directly impacting global financial markets and regional investment layouts. As geopolitical risks rise, international investment institutions such as BlackRock, PIMCO, and J.P. Morgan will gather in Taipei on October 30 to explore how to build a robust new investment paradigm in a changing situation. In addition, trans-Eurasian logistics and infrastructure development, such as the promotion of infrastructure between Russia and Mongolia, are seen as key to changing the Eurasian freight landscape. The establishment of such land transport networks aims to reduce excessive reliance on maritime routes and provide more cost-effective trade paths for landlocked countries, which coincides with the current trend of supply chain restructuring caused by geopolitical tensions.
  
  Although there is high concern regarding the regional situation, analysts remind that one should not over-interpret a single poll or the remarks of a single politician. Japanese polls reflect current social sentiment and do not necessarily equate to the future decision-making of the Japanese government in extreme scenarios. Similarly, while Trump's remarks on Ukraine are indicative, the policy framework of the U.S. Congress and executive branch in the Indo-Pacific region still maintains continuity. The current greatest limitation is that the speed of change in the international situation is extremely fast, and the policies of various countries are deeply influenced by domestic elections and economic factors. The occurrence of a single event is often accompanied by multiple variables, and it is inappropriate to simplify it into a single cause-and-effect relationship.
  
  Follow-up observations should focus on three aspects: first, the linkage of U.S. government policy between Ukraine and the Indo-Pacific region, especially regarding specific adjustments to alliance commitments; second, how the Japanese government responds to domestic anti-war public opinion and strikes a balance between expanding military capabilities and maintaining public support; and finally, the progress of infrastructure in the Eurasian region, especially how changes in logistics networks can mitigate the economic impact caused by geopolitical conflicts. These developments will continue to affect the global political and economic landscape and influence the strategic choices of various countries in future international governance.

# 地緣政治變局與區域安全：從烏克蘭局勢到東亞防衛民調

> 隨著全球地緣政治進入新常態，美國對烏克蘭政策的轉向引發各界對區域安全承諾的疑慮。同時，日本國內對於臺海防衛的民調資料，亦成為北京方面解讀區域軍事部署與政治期待的重要指標。


近期全球地緣政治板塊劇烈變動，美國總統川普公開對烏克蘭總統澤倫斯基的政治去留提出質疑，主張烏克蘭應選出更能與俄羅斯達成協議的領導人，此舉不僅改寫了國際社會對「美國支援」的傳統認知，更在臺灣引發對於「美國承諾」可靠性的廣泛討論。與此同時，日本新聞通訊調查會發布的最新民調顯示，高達八成日本民眾反對自衛隊直接參與臺海衝突，該資料隨即被中國官方媒體《環球時報》引用，用於批評日本部分政治人物及臺灣民進黨政府對「臺海有事即日本有事」的軍事擴張期待。
根據多家媒體報導與分析，美國對烏政策的調整已成為國際政治觀察的焦點。旅美教授翁履中指出，川普的言論揭示了美國外交政策中，現實利益考量往往優先於傳統盟友承諾，這對臺灣而言是一項重大警訊。臺灣社會與政策制定者目前正重新檢視在美國利益轉向時，臺灣是否具備足夠的自主防衛能力。與此同時，日本內部的輿論趨勢也顯示，儘管官方在戰略層面強調臺海安全，但民間對於直接介入軍事衝突仍抱持高度保留態度，這為區域安全合作的實際執行力增添了不確定性。
在資訊呈現上，各來源對於事件的解讀存在顯著差異。中國《環球時報》將日本民調視為對「臺海有事」論述的有力反駁，並以此質疑臺灣政府對日本協防的期待缺乏現實基礎；然而，日本民調本身僅反映民眾對於「直接參戰」的意願，並未完全否定日本在區域安全中的其他角色。此外，美國對烏克蘭的政策轉向是否會直接複製於東亞，目前仍屬於政治分析範疇，尚未有具體的政策檔案或行動證實美國將全面撤回對臺支援，相關討論多集中在戰略自主與風險控管的層面。
這些地緣政治的波動正直接影響全球金融市場與區域投資佈局。隨著地緣政治風險上升，國際投資機構如貝萊德（BlackRock）、PIMCO及摩根大通等，將於10月30日齊聚臺北，探討在變動局勢下如何打造穩健的投資新典範。此外，跨歐亞的物流與基礎建設發展，如俄羅斯與蒙古之間的基建推動，被視為改變歐亞貨運格局的關鍵，這類陸路運輸網路的建立，旨在降低對海上航線的過度依賴，為內陸國家提供更具成本效益的貿易路徑，這與當前地緣政治緊張導致的供應鏈重組趨勢不謀而合。
儘管各界對區域局勢高度關注，但分析人士提醒，不應過度解讀單一民調或單一政治人物的言論。日本民調反映的是當下社會情緒，未必等同於未來日本政府在極端情境下的決策；同樣地，川普對烏克蘭的言論雖具指標性，但美國國會與行政部門在印太地區的政策架構仍具有延續性。目前最大的限制在於，國際局勢的變動速度極快，且各國政策深受國內選舉與經濟因素影響，單一事件的發生往往伴隨著多重變數，不宜將其簡化為單一的因果關係。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於三方面：首先是美國政府在烏克蘭與印太地區政策的連動性，特別是針對盟友承諾的具體調整；其次是日本政府如何回應國內反戰民意，並在擴大軍事能力與維持國民支援之間取得平衡；最後則是歐亞地區基礎建設的進展，特別是物流網路的變革如何緩解地緣政治衝突帶來的經濟衝擊。這些發展將持續牽動全球政經格局，並影響各國在未來國際治理中的戰略選擇。
