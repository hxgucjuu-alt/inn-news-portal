洛杉磯道奇隊內野工具人米格爾·羅哈斯（Miguel Rojas）近期公開分享了其參加美國公民入籍測驗的經歷與結果。報導指出，羅哈斯目前距離正式成為美國公民僅一步之遙，其公開坦承入籍歷程的發言隨後引起輿論關注。

針對羅哈斯的入籍歷程，保守派政治評論員兼 OutKick 節目聯合主持人托米·拉倫（Tomi Lahren）公開表達了肯定與尊重。拉倫強調，羅哈斯循正規管道爭取入籍並坦承分享測驗過程，展現了對美國法律與體系的真正尊重。

資料顯示，羅哈斯長年在大聯盟賽場奮戰，此次入籍事件跨越了單純的體育競技領域，成為公眾討論移民法規與公民歸化程序的焦點之一。相關媒體報導進一步指出，公眾人物循合法程序入籍的經歷，引發了體育與社會輿論間的廣泛迴響。

## English edition

Los Angeles Dodgers utilityman Miguel Rojas recently shared details and results concerning his U.S. citizenship test. Reports indicate that Rojas is close to officially becoming an American citizen after publicly opening up about his journey through the naturalization process.

Following Rojas' public remarks, political commentator and OutKick co-host Tomi Lahren expressed her appreciation and respect for his path. Lahren noted that his honest sharing and adherence to the formal immigration process demonstrated true respect for the nation's laws.

According to published reports, Rojas' experience has resonated beyond professional baseball, drawing public and media interest regarding legal immigration and civic integration processes.
