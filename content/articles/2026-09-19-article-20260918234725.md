  # King of the United Kingdom Convenes Tech Giants to Discuss AI Risks; Taiwan Simultaneously Deepens Industrial Intelligent Transformation
  
  > King Charles III of the United Kingdom convened technology leaders, including those from NVIDIA and OpenAI, in Scotland to focus on AI safety risks and control. Meanwhile, Taiwan's steel industry is optimizing manufacturing processes through artificial intelligence platforms, demonstrating concrete applications and investment momentum for AI technology in the manufacturing sector.
  
  The global development trend of artificial intelligence (AI) has recently become an international public issue. King Charles III of the United Kingdom convened a summit in Scotland with several technology industry leaders to hold in-depth dialogues regarding the increasingly severe risks posed by AI. Attendees included NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang and senior executives from OpenAI. The core issue of the summit was how to ensure human control over AI technology and implement safety standards during the technical development process.
  
  Regarding industrial trends in the Northeast Asian region, Taiwan is also actively promoting the implementation of AI technology. On the 18th of this month, the Ministry of Economic Affairs' "InvesTaiwan" office approved an expansion investment project by Dragon Steel, a subsidiary of China Steel Corporation, totaling over NT$17.8 billion, aimed at enhancing manufacturing efficiency and operational safety through the integration of AI platforms.
  
  In developments confirmed by multiple sources, NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang explicitly stated at the Scotland summit that AI developers must conduct rigorous testing on systems and adopt a cautious approach—including delaying product releases—if product safety is insufficient. This stance echoes the high level of attention paid by global capital markets to the AI supply chain. During the third-quarter industry summit forum held by SinoPac Securities on the 16th of this month, it was also emphasized that AI has expanded from a single technological issue to the core of the global supply chain and capital markets. The forum expressed optimism that Co-Packaged Optics (CPO), high-speed transmission interfaces, and the memory industry will become the mainstream for investment in the fourth quarter.
  
  However, there are still differing views regarding the pace and execution details of AI development. Although senior executives at the UK summit discussed safety risks, no specific policy changes or technical restrictions have been publicly committed to at this time; the discussions served only as a direct response to safety concerns. On the other hand, the AI application in Taiwan's steel industry focuses on "process digitalization." The investment project by Dragon Steel involves not only the integration of AI platforms but also incorporates carbon reduction measures such as reclaimed water and solar power generation. This indicates that there are significant differences in AI application scenarios across various industrial sectors, ranging from safety controls in software development to process optimization in heavy industry, with AI's influence spreading in diverse forms.
  
  For the Northeast Asian region and the global industry, the evolution of AI technology has already had a substantive impact. At the policy level, governments are actively guiding enterprises to apply AI technology to green supply chains and smart manufacturing to align with international carbon reduction trends. By investing large amounts of capital, Taiwanese enterprises are using AI as a key tool to improve production efficiency and operational safety, demonstrating that AI has become a core indicator of industrial competitiveness. At the same time, the financial market's layout regarding the AI supply chain also reflects investor confidence in the long-term industrial upgrades driven by AI technology, particularly in key hardware areas such as high-speed transmission and memory.
  
  Despite the broad prospects for AI development, experts and business leaders have cautioned against over-interpreting the impact of any single forum or investment project. The UK summit emphasized "technical control" and "risk prevention," which requires a balance in execution with the corporate goal of pursuing "production efficiency." Furthermore, the development and application of artificial intelligence still face challenges regarding technical maturity, safety testing standards, and the international regulatory environment. For investors and the industry, the focus should be on the robustness of technological development rather than merely pursuing short-term market heat, to avoid excessive investment before technology is fully mature, which could lead to an imbalance in resource allocation.
  
  Subsequent observations will focus on three aspects: First, whether the companies participating in the tech summit convened by the British Royal Family will release specific self-regulatory guidelines or technical specifications regarding AI safety standards; second, the actual operational efficiency of the AI platforms integrated into Taiwan's steel industry and their progress toward carbon reduction goals will serve as a benchmark case for the transformation of traditional manufacturing; finally, whether the investment frenzy in the global capital market for the AI supply chain will undergo structural adjustments following the release of fourth-quarter industry data—particularly regarding demand changes for key components such as memory and high-speed transmission interfaces—will be an important indicator for observing the global technology industry's economic climate.

# 英國國王召集科技巨頭共商AI風險，臺灣同步深化產業智慧轉型

> 英國國王查爾斯三世於蘇格蘭召集輝達、OpenAI等科技領袖，聚焦AI安全風險與控管。與此同時，臺灣鋼鐵產業透過人工智慧平臺最佳化製程，展現AI技術在製造業的具體應用與投資動能。


近期全球人工智慧（AI）發展趨勢成為國際公共議題，英國國王查爾斯三世於蘇格蘭召集多位科技業領袖舉行峰會，針對人工智慧日益加劇的風險進行深度對話。與會者包含輝達（NVIDIA）執行長黃仁勳及OpenAI高層主管。此次峰會的核心議題在於如何確保人類對AI技術的控制權，並在技術開發過程中落實安全標準。
在東北亞區域產業脈動方面，臺灣亦積極推動AI技術落地，經濟部投資臺灣事務所於本月18日透過中鋼子公司中龍鋼鐵的擴大投資案，總金額逾新臺幣178億元，旨在透過匯入AI平臺提升製造效率與作業安全。
在多方來源確認的進展中，輝達執行長黃仁勳於蘇格蘭峰會上明確表示，AI開發者必須對系統進行嚴格測試，若產品安全性不足，應採取暫緩發布的審慎態度。此立場與全球資本市場對於AI供應鏈的高度關注相呼應。永豐金證券於本月16日舉辦的第三季產業高峰論壇中，亦強調AI已從單一科技議題擴充套件至全球供應鏈與資本市場的核心，並看好共同封裝光學元件（CPO）、高速傳輸介面及記憶體產業將成為第四季的投資主流。
然而，各界對於AI發展的步調與執行細節仍存在不同看法。雖然英國峰會與會高層討論了安全風險，但目前並未對外公開承諾任何具體的政策改變或技術限制，僅針對安全擔憂做出直接回應。另一方面，臺灣鋼鐵產業的AI應用則聚焦於「製程數位化」，中龍鋼鐵的投資案不僅涉及AI平臺匯入，更結合了再生水與太陽能發電等減碳措施，顯示AI在不同產業領域的應用場景存在顯著差異，從軟體開發的安全控管到重工業的製程最佳化，AI的影響力正以多元形式擴散。
對於東北亞區域及全球產業而言，AI技術的演進已產生實質影響。在政策層面，各國政府正積極引導企業將AI技術應用於綠色供應鏈與智慧製造，以符合國際減碳趨勢。臺灣企業透過大規模資本投入，將AI作為提升生產效率與作業安全的關鍵工具，顯示AI已成為產業競爭力的核心指標。同時，金融市場對於AI供應鏈的佈局，亦反映出投資人對於AI技術長期帶動產業升級的信心，特別是在高速傳輸與記憶體等關鍵硬體領域。
儘管AI發展前景廣闊，但各界專家與企業領袖均提醒，不應過度解讀單一論壇或投資案的影響。英國峰會強調的是「技術控管」與「風險防範」，這與企業追求「生產效率」的目標在執行上存在平衡需求。此外，人工智慧的開發與應用仍面臨技術成熟度、安全性測試標準及國際監管環境的挑戰。對於投資者與產業界而言，應關注技術發展的穩健性，而非僅追求短期市場熱度，避免在技術尚未完全成熟前過度投入，導致資源分配失衡。
後續觀察重點將集中在三個面向：首先，英國王室召集的科技峰會後，參與企業是否會針對AI安全標準發布具體的自律準則或技術規範；其次，臺灣鋼鐵產業匯入AI平臺的實際運作效率，以及其在減碳目標上的達成進度，將成為傳統製造業轉型的指標案例；最後，全球資本市場對於AI供應鏈的投資熱潮，是否會隨著第四季的產業資料公佈而出現結構性調整，特別是針對記憶體與高速傳輸介面等關鍵零元件的需求變化，將是觀察全球科技產業景氣的重要風向球。
