  # The Tug-of-War for Influence Between the U.S. and China in East and Southeast Asia: Latest Research Data and Geopolitical Analysis
  
  > Recent research indicates that China maintains a slight lead over the U.S. in terms of influence in Southeast Asia; however, with adjustments in U.S. policy and frequent regional military exercises, the geopolitical landscape of East Asia is currently in a highly sensitive period of transition.
  
  The East Asian region is currently exhibiting high levels of tension regarding geopolitics and military deployment. According to a report by the South China Morning Post (SCMP), U.S. military armored vehicles gathered at a training ground in Yeoncheon County, Gyeonggi Province, South Korea, this Monday to conduct river-crossing military exercises. This move is viewed as a concrete demonstration of the U.S.-South Korea alliance continuously strengthening its military readiness and joint operational capabilities in the face of regional security challenges. Meanwhile, according to the latest research cited by the Australian Broadcasting Corporation (ABC), despite global instability, China currently retains a narrow advantage over the U.S. in terms of influence in Southeast Asia, though this landscape is facing reorganization as competition between global powers intensifies.
  
  Multiple media outlets and public sources have confirmed that the East Asian region is currently at the heart of great power competition. On one hand, the U.S. is conveying security commitments to regional allies through military training within South Korea; on the other hand, China’s economic and diplomatic influence in Southeast Asia remains solid. Based on a cross-reference between the SCMP and the ABC, the movements of the U.S. military on the Korean Peninsula and the assessment of China’s influence in Southeast Asia constitute the two main axes of current East Asian regional strategy. Observers generally agree that the situations in East Asia and Southeast Asia can no longer be viewed in isolation, as the policy interactions between the U.S. and China directly determine the stability of the region.
  
  However, there remain significant discrepancies and uncertainties regarding the future ebb and flow of influence. According to expert analysis cited by the ABC, China’s current advantage over the U.S. is extremely marginal, and as the effects of policies related to the Trump administration gradually emerge, it remains uncertain whether China can maintain its lead. Some analyses suggest that U.S. policy adjustments could create a "bite" effect on the strategic choices of Southeast Asian nations, thereby impacting the balance of power within the region.
  
  Furthermore, there are differing interpretations regarding the specific strategic objectives of the U.S. military exercises in Yeoncheon County; some perspectives interpret them as a direct deterrent against the situation on the Korean Peninsula, while others view them as part of the U.S. military's normalized deployment in the Indo-Pacific region.
  
  This shift in the geopolitical landscape has had a profound impact on supply chain security, educational exchanges, and public policy formulation in the affected regions. In terms of industry, as East Asia serves as a global hub for semiconductors and high-tech manufacturing, the geopolitical risks brought about by U.S.-China competition are forcing countries to reassess the resilience of their supply chains. At the policy level, Southeast Asian nations are adopting "hedging" strategies between the U.S. and China, attempting to maintain economic cooperation while ensuring that national security is not overly disrupted by great power competition. This strategic balance has a direct and long-term impact on regional trade agreements and diplomatic relations, requiring governments to make delicate trade-offs between U.S. and Chinese influence when formulating policies.
  
  Regarding the aforementioned research data and military developments, experts caution against over-interpreting the impact of any single event. For instance, while China’s influence in Southeast Asia holds an advantage, this metric is subject to multiple variables, including regional infrastructure investment, trade dependency, and the domestic political climate of each country. At the same time, U.S. military exercises are part of routine defensive training, and linking them excessively to specific contingencies could lead to misjudgment. A limitation of the information is that there is currently a lack of comprehensive assessment regarding the specific implementation details of new U.S. policies, and the true attitudes of Southeast Asian nations toward U.S.-China competition often differ from their public diplomatic rhetoric, which leaves a margin of error in influence assessment models.
  
  Future observations should focus on three aspects: First, whether the frequency and scale of U.S. military exercises in South Korea and surrounding areas continue to escalate, and the specific responses to the situation on the Korean Peninsula; second, whether China’s economic policies in Southeast Asia will undergo structural adjustments in response to changes in U.S. policy, such as strengthening the depth of implementation of the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP); and finally, the strategic choices made by middle powers in the region (such as Vietnam, Indonesia, and South Korea) over the next one to two years, which will be key indicators in determining the shift in U.S. and Chinese influence. # 美中在東亞與東南亞影響力角力：最新研究資料與地緣政治現況分析

> 近期研究顯示，中國在東南亞的影響力仍以些微差距領先美國，但隨著美國政策調整及區域軍事演習頻繁，東亞地緣政治格局正處於高度敏感的變動期。


東亞地區近期在地緣政治與軍事部署上呈現高度緊張態勢。根據《南華早報》（SCMP）報導，美軍裝甲車輛於本週一在韓國京畿道漣川郡（Yeoncheon）的訓練場集結，並進行跨越河流的軍事演習。此舉被視為美韓同盟在面對區域安全挑戰時，持續強化軍事準備與聯合作戰能力的具體展現。與此同時，根據澳洲廣播公司（ABC）引用的最新研究指出，儘管國際情勢動盪，中國在東南亞地區的影響力目前仍保有對美國的微弱優勢，但此一格局正隨著全球大國競爭的加劇而面臨重組。
多家媒體與公開來源共同確認，東亞地區目前正處於大國博弈的核心地帶。一方面，美國透過在韓國境內的軍事訓練，向區域盟友傳遞安全承諾；另一方面，中國在東南亞的經濟與外交影響力依然穩固。根據《南華早報》與《澳洲廣播公司》的交叉比對，美軍在朝鮮半島的動態與中國在東南亞的影響力評估，構成了當前東亞區域戰略的兩大主軸。各界觀察家一致認為，東亞與東南亞的局勢已無法單獨切割，美中兩國的政策互動直接決定了該區域的穩定性。
然而，關於未來影響力的消長，各方資訊仍存在顯著差異與未定論。根據《澳洲廣播公司》引述的專家分析，中國目前對美國的優勢極為微弱，且隨著美國川普政府（Trump administration）相關政策的效應逐漸顯現，未來中國是否能持續保持領先地位仍有變數。部分分析認為，美國的政策調整可能對東南亞國家的戰略選擇產生「咬合效應」（bite），進而影響區域內的勢力均衡。
此外，對於美軍在漣川郡演習的具體戰略目標，各界解讀亦有不同，部分觀點將其解讀為針對朝鮮半島局勢的直接威懾，亦有觀點認為這是美軍在印太地區常態化部署的一環。
此一地緣政治格局的變動，對相關地區的產業鏈安全、教育交流及公共政策制定產生了深遠影響。在產業方面，東亞作為全球半導體與高科技製造中心，美中競爭帶來的地緣風險，迫使各國重新評估供應鏈的韌性。在政策層面，東南亞國家在美中之間採取「避險」策略，試圖在維持經濟合作的同時，確保國家安全不受大國博弈的過度幹擾。這種戰略平衡對於區域內的貿易協定與外交關係具有直接且長期的影響，各國政府在制定政策時，必須在美中的影響力之間進行細膩的權衡。
針對上述研究資料與軍事動態，專家提醒不應過度解讀單一事件的影響。例如，中國在東南亞的影響力雖具優勢，但該指標受到多重變數影響，包括區域內的基礎建設投資、貿易依賴度以及各國國內政治氛圍。同時，美軍的軍事演習屬於常態性防禦訓練，將其過度連結至特定突發事件可能導致誤判。限制資訊之處在於，目前尚缺乏關於美國新政策具體實施細節的全面評估，且東南亞各國對於美中競爭的真實態度，往往與公開的外交辭令存在落差，這使得影響力評估模型存在一定的誤差空間。
後續觀察重點應聚焦於三個面向：首先，美軍在韓國及周邊區域的軍事演習頻率與規模是否持續升級，以及朝鮮半島局勢的具體回應；其次，中國在東南亞的經濟政策是否會因應美國政策的變化而進行結構性調整，例如加強區域全面經濟夥伴協定（RCEP）的落實深度；最後，區域內中型國家（如越南、印尼、韓國）在未來一至兩年內的戰略選擇，將是決定美中影響力消長的關鍵指標。INN新聞編輯部將持續追蹤相關政策檔案與軍事動態，為讀者提供即時且中性的資訊更新。
